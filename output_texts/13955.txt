Title: US Retail Sales Lower than Expected
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Lower than ExpectedUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the US rose a meager 0.1 percent in October from September of 2015, after showing no growth in the previous two months. Excluding autos, retail sales rose 0.2 percent, rebounding from an upwardly revised 0.4 percent fall in September. Seven of 13 major categories showed gains, led by building-material stores, restaurants and non-store merchants.2015-11-13T13:35:39
