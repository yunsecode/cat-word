US Factory Growth Beats Forecasts in January: ISMUnited States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management’s Manufacturing PMI in the US edged down to 59.1 in January of 2018 from a downwardly revised 59.3 in December, beating market expectations of 58.8. Slowdowns were seen in new orders and production despite maintaining high levels of expansion. Employment also eased.2018-02-01T15:04:41
