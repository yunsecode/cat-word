Title: US Labour Productivity Falls Slightly Less than Initially Expected
Tag: Productivity

US Labour Productivity Falls Slightly Less than Initially ExpectedUnited States ProductivityNon-farm labor productivity in the US fell an annualized 4.1 percent in the second quarter of 2022, slightly less than initial estimates of a 4.6 percent drop. Output decreased 1.4 percent and hours worked 2.7 percent. Compared to the second quarter of 2021, productivity decreased 2.4 percent, the largest decline sine the first quarter of 1948.2022-09-01T12:40:01.183
