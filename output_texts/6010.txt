Title: Dollar Fails to Keep Momentum
Tag: Currency

Dollar Fails to Keep Momentum United States CurrencyThe dollar index bottomed around 89.80 on Thursday, hovering near yearly lows, as better-than-expected weekly jobless claims numbers drove investors into riskier assets. The most pronounced selling activity was against commodity-linked currencies such as the Australian and New Zealand dollars. The upbeat outlook for the job market came in the wake of somewhat hawkish FOMC minutes, which showed that some policymakers are already comfortable discussing a tapering plan if the US economy continues to pick up strongly and inflationary pressures persist. The greenback also seemed to be pressured by lower Treasury yields, with the benchmark 10-year rate retreating to an over one-week low of 1.62%2021-05-20T16:29:00
