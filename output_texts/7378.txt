Title: Dollar Index Hits 4-week High
Tag: currency

Dollar Index Hits 4-week HighUnited States currencyThe dollar appreciated to a 4-week high of 94.102 against the other major currencies on Monday, as traders rushed to the safety of the greenback amid rising coronavirus infections and a potentially contested election result in the US. Renewed lockdowns in France, Germany and more recently in England and a dovish ECB spooked investor away from the euro and sterling.2020-11-02T00:00:00
