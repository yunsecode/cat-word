Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately in the green on Tuesday 9 January 2017, as oil climbed to a 32-month high nudging energy shares higher and as investors awaited upcoming earnings season with optimistic expectations. All three major indices booked fresh records. The Dow Jones gained 103 points or 0.4% to 25386. The S&P 500 advanced 3 points or 0.1% to 2751. The Nasdaq edged up 6 points or 0.1% to 7164.2018-01-09T21:07:02.57
