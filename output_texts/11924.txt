Title: US Stocks End Mixed As Tech Rout Extends
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed As Tech Rout ExtendsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Monday, as technology shares slipped anew and energy stocks continued to rise on the back of higher crude oil prices, with the WTI trading around 2 percent higher.  The Dow Jones gained 130 points or 0.6 percent to 21479. The S&P 500 edged up 6 points or 0.2 percent to 2429. Contrastingly, the Nasdaq lost 30 points or 0.5 percent to 6110.2017-07-03T20:04:51.153
