Title: US Building Permits Fall 0.1% MoM in July
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Fall 0.1% MoM in July United States Building PermitsBuilding permits in the United States declined by 0.1 percent to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1152 thousand in July 2016 from 1153 thousand in the previous month and worse than market consensus of 1160 thousand. Single-family authorizations fell 3.7 percent to 711 thousand while multi-family building permits advanced 6.5 percent to 411 thousand. Among regions, permits decreased in the Northeast (-10.2 percent) and in the West (-8 percent) while rose in the South (+2.6 percent) and the Midwest (+10.5 percent). Compared with July 2015, however, building permits increased 0.9 percent.2016-08-16T13:41:15
