Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Hits Another Record Low
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Hits Another Record Lowunited states Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year note dropped more than 3.5bps to a fresh all-time low of 1.2656% on Thursday, amid a spike in demand for safe-haven as investors worry about the spread of the coronavirus. More cases have been reported all over the world including in Switzerland, Kuwait, Spain, South Korea and Austria. The yield on the 30-year bond also hit a new low, and that on the two-year note, which typically moves in step with interest rate expectations, was down to close to its lowest since late 2016.2020-02-27T14:23:00
