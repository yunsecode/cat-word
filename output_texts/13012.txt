Title: Foreigners Bought $140.6B of US Assets in July
Tag: Capital Flows

Foreigners Bought $140.6B of US Assets in JulyUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought $140.6 billion of US assets in July of 2016, including short-dated instruments after selling a downwardly revised $194.5 billion in June. Foreign investors sold $13.1 billion of US Treasuries, after selling $32.9 billion in June. Foreigners also bought $103.9 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after selling a downwardly $0.5 billion in the previous month.2016-09-16T20:16:31.427
