Title: US Retail Sales Rise 0.2% in November
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Rise 0.2% in NovemberUnited States Retail Sales MoMUS retail trade rose by 0.2 percent from a month earlier in November 2018, following a revised 1.1 percent growth in October and matching market expectations. Sales of furniture, electronics and a range of other goods was partially offset by a decline in trade at gasoline stations on the back of cheaper fuel prices. Retail sales excluding automobiles, gasoline, building materials and food services surged 0.9 percent in November after an upwardly revised 0.7 percent increase in October.2018-12-14T13:35:00
