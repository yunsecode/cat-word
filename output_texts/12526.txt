Title: Foreign Investors Buy $23.7 Billion Of US Assets
Tag: Capital Flows

Foreign Investors Buy $23.7 Billion Of US AssetsUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought $23.7 billion of US assets in November 2016, including short-dated instruments after buying an upwardly revised $20.6 billion in October. Meanwhile, foreigners bought $12.1 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after selling a downwardly revised $12.6 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors sold $0.2 billion of US treasuries, after selling $63.5 billion in October.2017-01-18T21:10:13
