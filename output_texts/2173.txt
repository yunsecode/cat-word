Title: Dollar Approaches 112
Tag: Currency

Dollar Approaches 112United States CurrencyThe dollar index extended gains and strengthened almost 1.5% to approach 112 on Wednesday, rebounding from a 1.5% loss the day before which was its biggest one-day drop in more than two years. The expectations the Fed will start slowing the pace of monetary tightening were pushed back after the ISM Services PMI beat forecasts and the ADP report pointed to another robust job growth. The next big catalyst for markets will be the nonfarm payrolls report on Friday.2022-10-05T14:24:00
