Title: U.S. Trade Deficit Narrows in February
Tag: Balance of Trade

U.S. Trade Deficit Narrows in FebruaryUnited States Balance of TradeTotal February exports of $181.2 billion and imports of $227.2 billion resulted in a goods and services deficit of $46.0 billion, down from $52.5 billion in January, revised.  February exports were $0.2 billion more than January exports of $180.9 billion. February imports were $6.3 billion less than January imports of $233.4 billion.2012-04-12T13:37:02.29
