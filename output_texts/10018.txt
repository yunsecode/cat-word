Title: US Composite PMI Revised Higher in October: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Composite PMI Revised Higher in October: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit Composite PMI for the US was revised slightly higher to 54.9 in October of 2018 from a preliminary reading of 54.8 and higher than 53.9 in September. The reading pointed to the strongest growth in the US private sector activity in three months as both services (54.8 from 53.5) and manufacturing (55.7 from 55.6) rose faster. 2018-11-05T14:51:41.873
