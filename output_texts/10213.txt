Title: US Stocks Extend Rise
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend RiseUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Friday, with the Dow and the S&P touching new record highs. On Thursday, the Dow notched its first record close since January and the S&P also finished at a record high as fears of a US-China trade war continue to ease after tariffs announced early in the week were lower than expected. The Dow Jones went up 0.3%, the S&P 500 gained 0.3% and the Nasdaq traded 0.2% higher around 5 minutes after the opening bell.2018-09-21T13:30:00
