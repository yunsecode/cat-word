Title: US Stock Futures Trade Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Trade HigherUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures were up on Friday, pointing to the second session of gains, although volatility is expected to continue. Investors sentiment was lifted after California ordered its residents to stay at home, crude oil prices rebounded and the US dollar deprecated slightly.  On Thursday, the Dow Jones gained 189 points or 1% to 20088. The S&P 500 climbed 11 points or 0.5% to 2409. The Nasdaq jumped 161 points or 2.3% to 7151.2020-03-20T08:54:00
