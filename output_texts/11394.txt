Title: US Stocks Jump After Tax Bill Vote
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Jump After Tax Bill VoteUnited States Stock MarketWall Street soared on Monday with the Dow and the S&P reaching record highs after the US Senate passed a tax cut bill over the weekend. The Dow Jones jumped 225 points or 0.9%; the S&P 500 added 21 points or 0.8% and the Nasdaq traded 48 points or 0.7% higher around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2017-12-04T14:30:03
