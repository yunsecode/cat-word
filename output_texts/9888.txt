Title: US Consumer Sentiment Above Forecasts
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Above ForecastsUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US was steady at 97.5 in December of 2018, the same as in the previous month and above market expectations of 97, preliminary estimates showed. In the last 2 years, consumer sentiment has been above 90, a pattern not seen since 1997 to 2000.2018-12-07T15:10:33.81
