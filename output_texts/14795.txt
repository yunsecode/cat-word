U.S. Annual Inflation Rate Rises to 3.8% in AugustUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.4 percent in August on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on September 15.
