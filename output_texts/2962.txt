US Books Capital Inflow in MayUnited States Capital FlowsThe United States recorded a capital and financial account surplus of USD 182.5 billion in May of 2021, reversing a downwardly revised USD 2.7 billion deficit in the previous month. Foreign investors acquired USD 99.9 billion in Treasuries in May, compared with an outflow of USD 1.2 billion the previous month.
