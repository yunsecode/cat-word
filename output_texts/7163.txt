Title: US Average Hourly Earnings Rise More than Expected
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Average Hourly Earnings Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls rose by 9 cents, or 0.3 percent over the month to USD 29.58 in November of 2020, after increasing 0.1% in October and above market expectations of a 0.1 percent gain. Year-on-year, average hourly earnings have increased by 4.4 percent, the same pace as in the prior month and slightly above market consensus of 4.3 percent. Average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees increased by 7 cents to USD 24.87.
