Title: US Services Sector Rises the Most Since 2005: PMI
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Sector Rises the Most Since 2005: PMIUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States increased to 60.1 in October of 2017 from 59.8 in September, beating market expectations of 58.5. It is a new high reading since August of 2005, as production, employment and inventories continued to rise and the outlook for business conditions remained positive. It is the 4th time in the whole series the number is above 60.2017-11-03T14:09:28
