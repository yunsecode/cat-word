Title: Dollar Hits 1-Month Low
Tag: currency

Dollar Hits 1-Month LowUnited States currencyThe dollar index touched a four-week low of 90.08 on Monday as Treasury yields continue to soar amid expectations of high inflation while traders await a raft of economic data to assess the strength of the US economic recovery from the coronavirus-driven economic slump. A disappointing jobless claims report released last week sent a gloomy signal that the labor market continues to struggle to gain traction in 2021. Aside from how the US recovery is faring, investors await developments over another round of coronavirus-related fiscal stimulus in Washington. Biden and his Democrat-controlled Congress are due to roll out massive spending plans that include extra $1,400 checks for most Americans.2021-02-22T15:13:00
