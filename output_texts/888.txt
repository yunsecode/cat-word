Title: US Wholesale Inventories Rebound
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories ReboundUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US increased 0.2% month-over-month in February of 2023, rebounding from an upwardly revised 0.5% drop in January, preliminary estimates showed. Inventories were up 0.6% for durables, after a 0.2% drop while those for nondurables decreased 0.4%, following a 1.1% decline in January. Compared to a year earlier, wholesale inventories jumped 12.2%.2023-03-28T12:34:00
