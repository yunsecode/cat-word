Title: US Corporate Profits Fall in Q1
Tag: Corporate Profits

US Corporate Profits Fall in Q1United States Corporate ProfitsCorporate profits in the United States decreased 0.8 percent to USD 1.94 trillion in the first quarter of 2021, following a 3.3 percent drop in the previous period, a preliminary estimate showed. Net dividends declined 2.8 percent to USD 1.34 trillion. Meantime, net cash flow with inventory valuation adjustment, the internal funds available to corporations for investment, rose 1.8 percent to USD 2.59 trillion and undistributed profits climbed 4 percent to USD 0.60 trillion. 2021-05-27T12:34:00
