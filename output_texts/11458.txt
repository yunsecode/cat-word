Title: US Mortgage Applications Rebound: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rebound: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States increased 3.1 percent in the week ending November 10th 2017, after stalling in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the first rise in four weeks as refinance applications went up 6.3 percent and applications to purchase a home edged up 0.4 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was unchanged at 4.18 percent.2017-11-15T12:04:56.217
