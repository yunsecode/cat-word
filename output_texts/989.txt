US Crude Oil Inventories Beat Estimates: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 1.555 million barrels in the week ended March 10th, 2023, well above market expectation of a 0.555 million barrels rise and following a 3.835 million barrels fall in the previous week, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed.
