Title: US Food Inflation at Near 1-Year Low
Tag: Food Inflation

US Food Inflation at Near 1-Year LowUnited States Food InflationFood prices in the United States increased at a slower 9.5% from a year earlier in February 2023, decelerating from a 10.1% rise in January and a peak of 11.4% in last August. It was the lowest reading since April of 2022, as prices slowed down further for food at home (10.1% vs 11.3% in January), while accelerating for food away from home (8.4% vs 8.2%).2023-03-14T12:39:00
