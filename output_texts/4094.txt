Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Retreat on Ukraine DevelopmentsUnited States Government Bond 10YThe benchmark 10-year Treasury yield fell to below 1.8% on Friday amid a general flight to safety as tensions in Ukraine intensify after Russian forces attacked and took over Europe’s largest nuclear power plant in Ukraine, briefly igniting a fire, and raising concerns over an environmental disaster.
