Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Friday after the jobs report for November showed the economy added more jobs than expected while earnings declined for the first time in two years. The Dow Jones fell 22 points or 0.1%, the S&P 500 closed nearly flat and the Nasdaq added 5 points or less than 0.1%. On the week, the Dow Jones rose less than 0.1%, marking the fourth straight week of gains. In contrast, the S&P 500 lost 1% and the Nasdaq fell 2.7%, ending a 3-week winning streak.2016-12-02T21:26:02.557
