Title: US Equities Resume Sell Off
Tag: Stock Market

US Equities Resume Sell OffUnited States Stock MarketUS Stock futures extended the sell off on Monday, following the worst week since the 2008 financial crisis as recession worries resurfaced after latest Markit PMI data showed the coronavirus hurt new export orders in China and disrupted supply chains in the Euro Area, Germany and France. This comes despite hopes that major central banks could introduce additional monetary stimulus to reduce the impact of coronavirus. On Friday, Fed Chair Powell said the fundamentals of the US economy remain strong and the Fed will use its tools to support the economy. On Monday, Bank of Japan Governor also issued a statement saying it will strive to provide ample liquidity and ensure stability in financial markets. Dow Jones futures were down 250 points, S&P 500 37 points and Nasdaq 70 points around 7:00 AM NY time.2020-03-02T12:10:00
