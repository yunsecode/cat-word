Title: US Retail Sales Unchanged in July
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Unchanged in JulyUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States showed no growth in July from June 2016, following a upwardly revised 0.8 percent gain in the previous period and below market expectations of 0.4 percent rise.  Automobile, furniture and online sales were bright spots in July. Sales at auto dealerships increased 1.1 percent in July after rising 0.5 percent in June. Online retail sales jumped 1.3 percent. Sales fell for: clothing (-0.5 percent), sporting goods and hobby (-2.2 percent), building materials and garden equipment (-0.5 percent), electronics and appliance outlets and service stations, restaurants and bars.2016-08-12T13:32:34
