Title: Dollar Strengthens after Comments from Fed Officials
Tag: Currency

Dollar Strengthens after Comments from Fed OfficialsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index edged slightly higher to 93 on Thursday after the President of the St. Louis Fed James Bullard said he thinks the Fed should begin tapering of asset purchases in the fall and finish the process by the end of Q1 2022 calling for a first-rate hike in late 2022. Meanwhile, the Kansas City Federal Reserve President George said the Fed should start trimming its monthly bond purchases "sooner rather than later" and even though the delta variant poses a risk to the US economic outlook. The dollar has been in a tight range this week as investors await the Fed Powell speech tomorrow for more clarity on the Fed's plans for tapering.2021-08-26T13:02:00
