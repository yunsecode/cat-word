Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States slumped 9.2 percent in the week ended November 29th 2019, after a 1.5 percent rise in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went down 15.6 percent while applications to purchase a home increased 0.9 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was unchanged at 3.37 percent.2019-12-04T12:04:00
