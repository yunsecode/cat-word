Title: US Jobless Claims Fall to 246K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall to 246KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 14 thousand to 246 thousand in the week ended January 28th 2017, below market expectations of 250 thousand. Claims have been below 300,000, the level associated with a healthy labor market, for 100 straight weeks. That’s the longest streak since 1970.2017-02-02T13:34:13
