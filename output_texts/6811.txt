Title: US Stocks Rally
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally United States Stock MarketWall Street rallied on Tuesday with the Dow Jones jumping more than 500 points and both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq adding around 1.5% as investors await earnings from Amazon, Alphabet and Amgen after the opening bell and the Reddit boom seems to be cooling with shares of GameStop retreating alongside silver. Meanwhile, results from Exxon Mobil surprised on the upside while Pfizer disappointed. Also, President Biden reiterated he will not slow down work on his new stimulus bill and will not settle for a package that fails to meet the moment after the meeting with 10 Republican senators who counter-proposed a smaller package. 2021-02-02T15:34:00
