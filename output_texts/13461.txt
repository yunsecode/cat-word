Title: US Jobless Claims Highest in 5-Weeks
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Highest in 5-WeeksUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 17,000 to 274,000 in the week ended April 30th. Figures came above market expectations of 260,000, reaching the highest in five weeks. However, it is the 61st consecutive week initial claims are below 300,000, the level associated with a healthy jobs market.2016-05-05T13:35:13
