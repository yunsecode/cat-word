Title: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index Below Estimates
Tag: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index

Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index Below Estimates United States Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index came in at -2.9 in July of 2016 from 4.7 in June, way below market expectations of 5. It is the lowest reading since January as inventories continued to fall (-4.3 from -9.9) and employment was nearly flat (-1.6 from -10.9) while new orders (11.8 from -3) and shipments (6.3 from -2.1) increased. 2016-07-21T14:04:44.887
