Title: US Jobless Claims at 7-Week High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims at 7-Week HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits increased by 12 thousand to 214 thousand in the week ended September 22 from the previous week's revised level of 202 thousand, above market expectations of 210 thousand. Jobless claims hit the highest level since the week ended August 4, mainly reflecting temporary rises in states affected by Hurricane Florence.  According to unadjusted data, the biggest rises in claims were seen in North Carolina (+8,035), Kentucky (+4,461), South Carolina (+1,899) and California (+1,396) while the largest decreases were reported in Georgia (-1,377), New Jersey (-965) and Texas (-930).
