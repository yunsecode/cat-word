Title: US Existing Home Sales At Near 10-Year High
Tag: Existing Home Sales

US Existing Home Sales At Near 10-Year HighUnited States Existing Home SalesSales of previously owned houses in the United States rose 0.7 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 5610 thousand in November of 2016. It is the highest figure since February of 2007, beating market expectations of a 1 percent fall. Sales of condos jumped 10 percent to 660 thousand while those of single family homes fell 0.4 percent to 4950 thousand. The average price increased 0.5 percent and the months’ worth of supply went down to 4 from 4.3. The October figure was revised down to 5570 thousand from 5600 thousand.2016-12-21T15:09:34.683
