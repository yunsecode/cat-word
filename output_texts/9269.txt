Title: US Stocks Rebound on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Tuesday 21 May 2019, as the US decided to allow Huawei Technologies to buy some U.S. products until August 19th in order to maintain its network and provide software updates to its customers. The Dow Jones jumped 197 points or 0.8%. The S&P 500 climbed 24 points or 0.9%. The Nasdaq 83 points or 1.1%.2019-05-21T20:13:00
