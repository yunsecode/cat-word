Title: 10-Year Treasury Yield Consolidates Around 3.7%
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

10-Year Treasury Yield Consolidates Around 3.7%United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note, seen as a proxy for global borrowing costs, consolidated around 3.7%, a level not seen since October 4th, as the narrative started to change from inflation and tightening to recession and the likelihood of a policy pivot. Minutes from the last Federal Reserve meeting showed officials see the case for a slower pace of interest rate rises. The November meeting minutes also showed that policymakers were growing concerned about the economy's health, with officials noting that a recession is now almost as likely as their baseline projection of weak growth. This outlook for monetary policy is decoupling from the one seen for Europe, in which the ECB reassured markets that its tightening cycle is far over despite the continent heading for a recession in the last quarter of 2022. Germany's 10-year Bund yield, the European benchmark, rebounded from a two-month low to around 1.9%. 2022-11-25T09:13:00
