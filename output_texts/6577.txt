Title: Dollar Gains as Treasury Yields Rise
Tag: Currency

Dollar Gains as Treasury Yields RiseUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index edged higher to 91 on Wednesday as Treasury yields increased amid prospects of a strong economic rebound supported by fiscal stimulus and a faster coronavirus vaccine rollout. President Biden recently said the US will have enough vaccines for every American by the end of May, earlier than July. Furthermore, the House of Representatives approved President Joe Biden's $1.9 trillion pandemic aid package that includes extra $1,400 checks for most Americans. The Senate is now expected to open debate on it as soon as Wednesday afternoon.2021-03-03T14:45:00
