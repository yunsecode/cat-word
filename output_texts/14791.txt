Title: U.S. Trade Deficit Unchanged in August
Tag: Balance of Trade

U.S. Trade Deficit Unchanged in AugustUnited States Balance of TradeTotal August exports of $177.6 billion and imports of $223.2 billion resulted in a goods and services deficit of $45.6 billion, virtually unchanged from July, revised.
