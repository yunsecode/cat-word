Title: Dollar Little Changed after Jobs Report
Tag: Currency

Dollar Little Changed after Jobs ReportUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was little changed at 99.8 against a basket of currencies on Friday after data showed the US economy lost a record 20.5 million jobs in April, less than market expectations of a 22 million cut, while the unemployment rate hit an all-time high of 14.7%, below forecasts of 16%. Risk appetite was broadly higher on the day as more countries plan to reopen their economies and trade tensions between Washington and Beijing eased after representatives from both countries held a phone call and discussed the Phase 1 trade deal.2020-05-08T21:13:00
