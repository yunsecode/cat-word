US Manufacturing PMI Down To 5-Month Low: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe Markit flash US manufacturing PMI fell to 53.4 in March of 2017 from 54.2 in February and well below expectations of 54.8. It is the lowest reading since October of 2016, mainly due to a slowdown in new orders and lower stocks while input cost inflation picked up.2017-03-24T13:51:37.903
