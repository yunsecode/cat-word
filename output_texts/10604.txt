Title: US Jobless Claims Lower than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Lower than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits fell by 4 thousand to 218 thousand in the week ending June 9th, below market expectations of 224 thousand. It is the lowest reading in five weeks. According to unadjusted data, the biggest declines in jobless claims were seen in Missouri (-919), Mississippi (-711) and Arkansas (-667) while the highest increases were recorded in Pennsylvania (5,974), California (5,107) and Texas (2,299). Claims fell in the Virgin Islands (-3) but rose in Puerto Rico (780). 2018-06-14T12:33:00
