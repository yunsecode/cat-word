Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down In March
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down In MarchUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe final reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States fell to 96.9 in March of 2017 from a preliminary reading of 97.6 but was higher than 96.3 in February. Both current conditions and futures expectations were revised slightly down.2017-03-31T14:09:50
