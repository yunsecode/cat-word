Title: US Stocks Rise, Investors Await Tax Reform

US Stocks Rise, Investors Await Tax ReformUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Wednesday amid a rising dollar and better-than-expected data for durable goods while investors await President's Trump speech in the afternoon for further details on tax reform.
