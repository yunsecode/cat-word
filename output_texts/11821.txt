Title: US Factory Activity Growth Slows in July: ISM
Tag: Business Confidence

US Factory Activity Growth Slows in July: ISMUnited States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management’s Manufacturing PMI in the US fell to 56.3 in July of 2017 from 57.8 in June which was the highest since August of 2014. Figures came slightly below market expectations of 56.5 due to a slowdown in new orders, production and employment.
