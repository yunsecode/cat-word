Title: US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised Slightly Up
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised Slightly UpUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe final Markit US Manufacturing PMI came in at 50.7 in May of 2016, slightly up from 50.5 in the preliminary estimate but down from 50.8 in the previous month. It is the lowest figure since September of 2009 as output fell for the first time in more than 6-1/2-years, new work expanded at the slowest pace since December last year while job growth picked up and input cost inflation accelerated to a nine-month high.2016-06-01T14:58:13
