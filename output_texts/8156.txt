Title: US Food Inflation at Over 8-Year High
Tag: Food Inflation

US Food Inflation at Over 8-Year HighUnited States Food InflationCost of food in the United States increased 4.0 percent from a year earlier in May 2020, the most since January 2012. Food at home prices jumped 4.8 percent (vs 4.1 percent in April) amid coronavirus-related lockdown restrictions, while food away from home cost advanced 2.9 percent (vs 2.8 percent in April).2020-06-10T12:39:00
