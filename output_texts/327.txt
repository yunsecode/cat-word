Title: Nasdaq Gets a Boost from Nvidia
Tag: Stock Market

Nasdaq Gets a Boost from NvidiaUnited States Stock MarketThe Nasdaq opened 1.4% higher and the S&P 500 was up 0.6% on Thursday, as the tech sector got a boost from a 25% rally in Nvidia shares. Nvidia's earnings and revenue beat and the company also reported a stronger-than-expected revenue guidance driven by AI chip demand. On the other hand, the Dow Jones fell over 100 points as the debt ceiling impasse continued to weigh on investors' mood with Fitch Ratings putting the US on credit watch for a possible downgrade. House Speaker McCarthy said today that negotiators have made some progress and that he instructed his team to work 24 hours to get the deal. Meanwhile, fresh data showed US economy grew more than initially estimated in Q1 and jobless claims rose less than expected, reinforcing the need for more hikes from the Fed. 2023-05-25T13:37:00
