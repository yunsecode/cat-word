Title: US Stocks Attempt to Rebound
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Attempt to ReboundUnited States Stock MarketWall Street attempted to recover on Tuesday as the Fed monetary policy meeting kicks off and oil prices jump more than 2%. On Monday, stocks fell sharply after a plunge in Facebook shares dragged the tech sector down. The Dow Jones went up 159 points or 0.6%; the S&P 500 increased 5 points or 0.2%; and the Nasdaq added 13 points or 0.2% around 11:40 AM NY time. Meanwhile, Facebook shares lost 5.2%.2018-03-20T15:46:47
