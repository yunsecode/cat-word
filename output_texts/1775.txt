Title: US Industrial Production Rises 3.3% YoY
Tag: Industrial Production

US Industrial Production Rises 3.3% YoYUnited States Industrial ProductionIndustrial Production in the United States increased 3.3% year-on-year in October of 2022, following a downwardly revised 5% rise in September. It is the smallest annual increase since January. Manufacturing went up 2.4%, utilities 2.5% and mining  6.9%.2022-11-16T14:23:33.333
