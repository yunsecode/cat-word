Title: US Stocks Extend 5-Day Rally
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend 5-Day RallyUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately in the green on Thursday 10 January 2018 to a extend a 5-day rally, as an expected positive resolution over US-China trade talks buoyed sentiment in a session with early losses dragged by negative newsflow from traditional retailers. The Dow Jones gained 123 points or 0.5% to 24002. The S&P 500 climbed 12 points or 0.5% to 2597, booking its first 5-day winning streak since September. The Nasdaq rose 29 points or 0.4% to 6986.2019-01-10T21:09:00
