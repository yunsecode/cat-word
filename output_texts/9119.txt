Title: US Labour Productivity Beats Forecasts in Q2
Tag: Productivity

US Labour Productivity Beats Forecasts in Q2United States ProductivityLabor productivity in the US non-farm business sector increased by an annualized 2.3 percent during the second quarter of 2019, matching a preliminary estimate and following a 3.5 percent gain in the previous three-month period. Figures came above market expectations of 2.2 percent. Output rose 1.9 percent and hours worked dropped 0.4 percent, the biggest decline since the third quarter of 2009. Year-on-year, productivity went up 1.8 percent, reflecting a 2.6 percent rise in output and a 0.9 percent increase in hours worked.      2019-09-05T12:32:00
