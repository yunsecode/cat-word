Title: US Stocks Extend Gains After Brainard Comments
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend Gains After Brainard CommentsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded higher on Monday, rebounding from a mixed open and partially recovering from Friday’s sell-off after Fed official Brainard suggested caution in raising interest rates. The Dow Jones was up 84 points or 0.5%, the S&P 500 gained 12 points or 0.6% and the Nasdaq traded 38 points or 0.7% higher around 1:30 PM NY time.2016-09-12T17:38:22
