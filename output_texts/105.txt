Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Holds Around 3.7%
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Holds Around 3.7%United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note hovered around 3.7% in the last week of June, as investors weigh fresh economic data pointing to a resilient economy and prospects that interest rates will continue to march higher. Fed Chair Powell reiterated at the ECB Forum on Central Banking that interest rates will rise further and that he wouldn’t take moving in consecutive meetings off the table at all, but noted that a recession in the US is not the most likely case. Market participants are currently assigning a nearly 84% chance the Fed will deliver a 25bps increase in the fed funds rate in July. The focus now shifts to fresh PCE inflation due Friday.19 days ago
