Dollar Gains Traction Ahead of FOMC MinutesUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index strengthened to 90 on Wednesday amid rising Treasury yields as investors await the FOMC minutes release later in the day. Traders will be looking for any clues on the Fed's view over rising inflationary pressures and policymakers are expected to confirm that a rate hike is still in the distance.
