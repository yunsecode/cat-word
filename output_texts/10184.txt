Title: US Stocks Close Mostly Flat on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mostly Flat on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mostly flat on Friday 28 September 2018, as the Kavanaugh confirmation drama continued and Tesla shares plummeted 14 percent amid news that the SEC is suing CEO Elon Musk on fraud allegations. The Dow Jones gained 18 points or 0.1% to 26458. The S&P 500 closed flat at 2914. The Nasdaq edged up 4 points or less than 0.1% to 8046.
