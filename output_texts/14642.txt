Title: US Annual Inflation Rate Slows to 1.5% in August
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Annual Inflation Rate Slows to 1.5% in AugustUnited States Inflation RateIn August of 2013, U.S. Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 1.5 percent over the last 12 months, from 2 percent recorded in July. For the month, on a seasonally adjusted basis the index increased 0.1 percent.2013-09-17T13:43:14.957
