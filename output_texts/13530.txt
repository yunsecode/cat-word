Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Rise the Most in 3 Weeks
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Rise the Most in 3 WeeksUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil stocks in the United States rose by 6.63 million barrels in the week ended April 8th, 2016, much higher than market expectations of a 2.6 million increase. It is the biggest gain in three weeks. In the previous week, stocks fell by 4.9 million barrels. Gasoline stocks declined by 4.2 million barrels, compared to market expectations of a 1.5 million drop.2016-04-13T16:19:36
