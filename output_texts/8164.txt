Title: US Stock Futures Drop
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures DropUnited States Stock MarketMajor US stock index futures edged lower and pointed to modest losses at the open on Tuesday, as investors digested a strong rally in the previous session, which pushed the S&P 500 into the green for the year and led the Nasdaq to a record high close. On top of hopes for an economic recovery, the Federal Reserve moved to expand the scope of its Main Street Lending Program by lowering the minimum loan size to $250,000 and extending the loan terms to five years. On Monday, the Dow Jones surged 462 points or 1.7% to 27,572. The S&P 500 climbed 39 points or 1.2% to 3232. The Nasdaq jumped 111 points or 1.1% to 9925.2020-06-09T01:17:27.993
