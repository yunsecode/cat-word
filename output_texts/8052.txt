Title: US Jobless Rate Falls More than Expected
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Falls More than ExpectedUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate dropped to 11.1 percent in June 2020, easing further from an all-time high of 14.7 percent reached in April and remaining below market expectations of 12.3 percent, as many people returned to the labor market following weeks of coronavirus-induced restrictions. The number of unemployed persons fell by 3.2 million to 17.8 million, while employment rose by 4.9 million to 142.2 million. Although unemployment fell in May and June, the jobless rate and the number of unemployed are up by 7.6 percentage points and 12.0 million, respectively, since February.2020-07-02T12:31:00
