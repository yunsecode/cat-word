Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States edged down 0.4 percent in the week ended May 4th 2018, after declining 2.5 percent in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications declined 0.6 percent and applications to purchase a home fell 0.2 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went down by 2bps to 4.78 percent.2018-05-09T11:02:36.227
