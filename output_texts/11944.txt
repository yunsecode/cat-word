Title: US Stocks Rebound Sharply On Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound Sharply On WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed sharply in the green on Wednesday, as technology stocks rebounded after global cyberattacks triggered a selloff on Tuesday, and as optimistic readings from central banks lifted risky assets.  The Dow Jones gained 144 points or 0.7 percent to 21455. The S&P 500 advanced 21 points or 0.9 percent to 2441. The Nasdaq surged 88 points or 1.4 percent to 6234.2017-06-28T20:05:24
