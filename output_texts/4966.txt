Title: US Private Sector Activity Growth Quickens to 3-Month High: Markit
Tag: Composite PMI

US Private Sector Activity Growth Quickens to 3-Month High: MarkitUnited States Composite PMIThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI was revised higher to 57.6 in October of 2021, from a preliminary estimate of 57.3. The latest reading signaled the fastest pace of expansion in private sector business activity since July. Although manufacturing production continued to be constrained by supply issues, the overall expansion was supported by a sharper service sector upturn. Contributing to the overall expansion was a faster increase in new business, supported by sharp upticks in new orders at services providers and manufacturers. Foreign client demand fared less well, however, as goods producers registered only a fractional expansion and service sector firms saw a further contraction. Employment increased solidly despite ongoing concerns regarding labor shortages. The rate of cost inflation slowed to a six-month low, but remained historically elevated amid supply shortages.
