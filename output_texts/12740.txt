Title: US New Home Sales at 4-Month Low
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales at 4-Month LowUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States declined 1.9 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 563,000 in October of 2016, compared to market expectations of a 0.3 percent rise. Figures for the previous month were revised down by 19,000 to 574,000. 2016-11-23T15:05:54
