Title: US Q3 Nonfarm Labour Cost Growth Revised Up To 0.7%
Tag: Labour Costs

US Q3 Nonfarm Labour Cost Growth Revised Up To 0.7%United States Labour CostsNonfarm business sector unit labor costs in the United States increased by an annualized 0.7 percent on quarter in the third quarter of 2016, compared to an upwardly revised 6.2 percent growth in the previous period and above the preliminary estimate of 0.3 percent gain. Hourly compensation went up by 3.8 percent and productivity advanced by 3.1 percent. Year-on-year, unit labor costs increased by 3 percent.2016-12-06T13:46:42.537
