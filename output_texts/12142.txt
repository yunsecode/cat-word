Title: US Stocks Edge Higher As Nasdaq Hits New High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Higher As Nasdaq Hits New HighUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed slightly in the green on Tuesday, with technology shares leading gains ahead of Apple’s earnings report and the Fed’s policy review. The Dow Jones closed 36 points higher, or 0.2 percent, to 20950. The S&P 500 edged up 3 points, or 0.1 percent, to 2391. The Nasdaq hit a new record, advancing 4 points, or 0.1 percent, to 6095.2017-05-02T20:07:30
