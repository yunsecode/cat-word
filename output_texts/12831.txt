Title: US Nonfarm Business Productivity Rises 3.1% in Q3
Tag: Productivity

US Nonfarm Business Productivity Rises 3.1% in Q3United States ProductivityLabor productivity in the nonfarm business sector in the United States increased at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 3.1 percent during the third quarter of 2016, more than market expectations of a 2 percent gain and following a 0.2 percent decline in the previous period, preliminary figures showed. It was the first increase after three consecutive declines, as output increased 3.4 percent and hours worked increased 0.3 percent. Year-on-year, productivity was unchanged.2016-11-03T12:36:06
