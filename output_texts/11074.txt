Title: US Existing Home Sales Fall for 2nd Month
Tag: Existing Home Sales

US Existing Home Sales Fall for 2nd MonthUnited States Existing Home SalesSales of previously owned houses in the US shrank 3.2 percent mom to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 5.38 million in January of 2018 from a downwardly revised 5.56 million rate in December. It compares with market expectations of a 0.9 percent rise to 5.6 million. Sales of single family houses went down 3.8 percent to 4.76 million, following a 2 percent drop in December while sales of condos rose 1.6 percent to 0.620 million after a 9 percent slump. The median house price decreased to $240,500 from $246,500 in December and the months' worth of supply rose to 3.4 from 3.2. In addition, the number of houses available in the market increased to 1.520 million. Year-on-year, existing home sales shrank 4.8 percent, the biggest annual drop since August of 2014.2018-02-21T15:08:31.383
