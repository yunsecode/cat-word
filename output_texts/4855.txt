Title: US Crude  Inventories Unexpectedly Fall: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude  Inventories Unexpectedly Fall: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories declined 2.101 million barrels in the week ending November 12th, after a 1.001 million increase in the previous period and compared with market forecasts of 1.398 million rise, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. It was the biggest fall in nationwide holdings since the week ended September 17th. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories fell 0.708 million barrels, more than an expected 0.575 million drop. It was the sixth consecutive period of a higher than expected drop in gasoline stocks.2021-11-17T15:34:00
