Title: FHFA House Prices Rise More Than Expected
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

FHFA House Prices Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States rose 0.5 percent month-over-month in November of 2016, following a downwardly revised 0.3 percent increase in October and higher than market expectations of 0.4 percent. Year-on-year, the FHFA house price index went up 6.1 percent compared to a 6.2 percent increase the previous month. 2017-01-25T14:13:16.15
