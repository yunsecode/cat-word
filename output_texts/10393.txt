Title: US Imports Rise in June
Tag: Imports

US Imports Rise in JuneUnited States ImportsImports from the United States were USD260.2 billion, USD1.6 billion or 0.6 percent more than May imports. Petroleum imports (USD19.6 billion) were the highest since December 2014. Shipments also grow for: consumer goods including pharmaceutical preparations. In contrast, imports of capital goods decreased including computers and telecommunications equipment. . 2018-08-03T12:38:00
