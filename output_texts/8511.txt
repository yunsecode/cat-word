Title: US Crude Stocks Post Biggest Weekly Rise on Record
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Post Biggest Weekly Rise on RecordUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude inventories in the US jumped by 15.177 million barrels in the week ended April 3rd 2020, the biggest one-week rise in crude inventories since the series began in 1982 and compared with market expectations of a 9.271 million advance, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went up by 10.497 million in the week, well above market consensus of a 4.333 million increase.2020-04-08T14:36:00
