Title: US Crude Stocks Fall More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil stocks fell by 4.689 million barrels in the week ended August 21st, 2020, the fifth consecutive period of decrease and compared to market expectations of a 3.694 million drop, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories were down by 4.584 million barrels, while markets had forecast a smaller 1.533 million decline.2020-08-26T14:31:00
