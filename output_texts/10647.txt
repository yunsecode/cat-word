Title: Dow Jones Jumps After Strong Jobs Report
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Jones Jumps After Strong Jobs ReportUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones climbed 193 points, or 0.8%, to 24,609 right after the market opened on Friday despite trade war concerns, as a stronger-than-expected jobs report for May boosted sentiment. The S&P 500 gained 20 points, or 0.7%, to 2,725, and the Nasdaq rose 61 points, or 0.8%, to 7,504.2018-06-01T13:40:00
