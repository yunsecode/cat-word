Title: US ISM New York Index Falls from 2006-High
Tag: Ism New York Index

US ISM New York Index Falls from 2006-HighUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions in the United States fell to 54.5 in February of 2018 from 72.5 in January which was the highest reading since November of 2006. Employment contracted (46.7 from 58.4) and prices paid accelerated (61.1 from 60.5). On the other hand, purchases rose faster (55.6 from 50) and revenues increased for both current (61.8 from 47.4) and expected (72.2 from 68.4). The Six-Month Outlook fell to 64.7 from 76.1 in December.2018-03-02T18:59:22
