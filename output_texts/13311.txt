Title: Wall Street Opens Lower As Brexit Concerns Rise
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Lower As Brexit Concerns RiseUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US continued to fall on Monday led by losses in financials and extending Friday’s plunge as the UK's decision to leave the EU continued to hurt investors' mood. The Dow Jones lost 1.3% or 227 points, the S&P 500 fell 1.3% or 26 points and the Nasdaq slid 1.37% or 64 points around 25 minutes after the opening bell.2016-06-27T15:11:47
