Title: US Stocks Fall on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded lower on Monday despite better-than-expected manufacturing data for September as investors awaited more news surrounding the US Department of Justice’s probe into Deutsche Bank. The Dow Jones decreased 71 points or 0.4%; the S&P 500 was down 8 points or 0.4%; and the Nasdaq retreated 12 points or 0.2% around 11:00 AM NY time. 2016-10-03T15:11:40.403
