Title: US Stocks Rise for Second Day
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise for Second DayUnited States Stock MarketThe three major US stock indexes gained further ground on Wednesday, led by gains in energy and financials, as upbeat quarterly results from companies including Coca-Cola and Verizon boosted confidence about the pace of the economic recovery. Still, investors remained concerned over the threats posed by the rapid spread of the Covid-19 delta variant on business activity. The blue-chip Dow added 286 points, or 0.8%, to 34,798, led by a rally in Chevron. The S&P 500 rose 0.8% to 4,359, with those economically sensitive sectors leading gains. The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite went up 0.9% to 14,632.2021-07-21T20:01:00
