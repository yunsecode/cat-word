Title: US Stocks Book Longest Record Run in 25 Years
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Book Longest Record Run in 25 YearsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed on new highs on Wednesday and booked the longest streak of record closing highs in 25 years. An uptick in consumer prices was observed at the start of the year, with annual inflation reaching in January the highest level in almost 5 years. Following the release, the odds of a Fed rate rise in March jumped to 42 percent from 32 percent on Tuesday. All major indices reached record highs for a fifth straight day, with the Dow Jones increasing 107 points or 0.5 percent to 20,611.45; the S&P rising 12 points or 0.5 percent to 2,349.24; and the Nasdaq climbing 37 points of 0.6 percent to 5,819.44.
