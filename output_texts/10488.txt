Title: US June Budget Deficit Smaller than Expected
Tag: Government Budget Value

US June Budget Deficit Smaller than ExpectedUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US budget deficit narrowed to USD 75.0 billion in June 2018 from USD 90.2 billion in the same month of the previous year, and below market expectations of USD 98.2 billion. Outlays fell 9 percent to USD 391.0 billion and receipts dropped 7 percent to USD 316.0 billion. The gap for the fiscal year, which began last October, was USD 607 billion, compared to a deficit of USD 523 billion in the same period of the previous fiscal year.2018-07-12T18:06:00
