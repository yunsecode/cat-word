US Crude Oil Inventories Fall UnexpectedlyUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 6.805 million barrels in the week ended August 10th 2018, following a 1.351 million decline in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.499 million decrease.
