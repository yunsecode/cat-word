Title: Dollar Gains on US Retail Trade Report
Tag: Currency

Dollar Gains on US Retail Trade ReportUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rallied to above 91 on Wednesday, making a dramatic reversal after testing a key support level at 90.10 in the previous session, after economic data showed the US retail trade rose 5.3% in January, recovering from three consecutive months of falls and easily beating market expectations. Also, rising real yields made the greenback a more attractive investment relative to other currencies. Still, signs of an economic recovery, fueled by large stimulus packages globally and a gradual rollout of vaccines continued to cap the upside in the USD.2021-02-17T13:47:00
