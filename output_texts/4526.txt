Title: US Treasury Yields Ease from 2-Year Highs
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Ease from 2-Year HighsUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury note edged down for a second day to 1.84% on Thursday after touching a fresh pandemic high of 1.9% early in the week. The bond selloff apparently eased as investors digest prospects of a bigger than 25 basis points hike in the fed funds rate by March and focus on the economic recovery and earnings. 2022-01-20T08:51:00
