Title: US Exports Fall 0.9% In March
Tag: Exports

US Exports Fall 0.9% In MarchUnited States ExportsTotal exports from the United States fell 0.9 percent month-over-month to USD 191.0 billion in March 2017, as sales of automobiles and parts declined 6.2 percent. Meanwhile, exports of capital goods rose 1.6 percent and those of civilian aircraft went up 6.7 percent. On a non-seasonally adjusted basis, exports to China fell 1.8 percent while those to Mexico and Canada rose 15.8 percent and 17.9 percent, respectively.2017-05-04T12:31:35
