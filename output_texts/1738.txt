Title: API Reports Another Large US Crude Draw
Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

API Reports Another Large US Crude DrawUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeCrude oil inventories sagged by another 4.2 million barrels, American Petroleum Institute (API) data showed on Tuesday, after dropping 5.8 million barrels in the week prior. Analysts anticipated a smaller 2.2 million barrel draw. The API reported a draw in gasoline inventories this week of 400,000 barrels for the week ending November 18, after the previous week’s 1.690 million-barrel build. Distillate stocks saw a build this week of 1.1 million barrels, on top of last week’s 850,000-barrel increase. Cushing inventories fell 1.4 million barrels in the week to Nov 18, on top of last week’s reported decrease of 842,000 barrels.2022-11-22T22:24:00
