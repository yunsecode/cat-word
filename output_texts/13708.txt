Title: US Export Prices Drop 0.8% in January
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Drop 0.8% in JanuaryUnited States Export Prices MoMExport prices in the United States decreased by 0.8 percent month-on-month in January of 2016 compared to a 1.1 percent drop in December, as both agricultural (-1.1 percent) and non-agricultural prices (-0.8 percent) contributed to the overall decline. Analysts were expecting a 0.9 percent fall. Compared to January of 2015, export prices fell 5.7 percent.2016-02-12T13:40:47
