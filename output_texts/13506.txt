Title: Wall Street Opens Mixed
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens MixedUnited States Stock MarketUS equities opened mixed on Friday after several earnings reports for technological firms disappointed while McDonald's, American Airlines, Honeywell International and Kimberly-Clark, released better than expected results earlier in the day. The Dow Jones added 0.23% or 40 points, the S&P 500 traded near the flat line, up 0.05% or 1 point and the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.6% or 30 points around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2016-04-22T15:08:09
