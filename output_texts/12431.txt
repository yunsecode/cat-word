Title: Stocks in United States Close At All-Time Highs
Tag: Stock Market

Stocks in United States Close At All-Time HighsUnited States Stock MarketThe three major stock indexes in the US finished at record highs on Thursday after President Trump said he plans to make an announcement on a much-anticipated tax reform in the next few weeks. The Dow Jones added 118 points or 0.6% to 20,172.4; the S&P 500 gained 13 points or 0.6% to 2,307.87 and the Nasdaq increased 33 points or 0.6% to 5,715.18. 2017-02-09T21:06:23
