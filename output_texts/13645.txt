Title: US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI at Fresh 2-Year Low
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI at Fresh 2-Year LowUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index declined 0.1 percentage points to 53.4 percent in February of 2016 from 53.5 in the previous month and compared to market expectations of 53.2. It is the lowest reading since February of 2014 as new orders and prices decreased and employment contracted for the first time in two years. 2016-03-03T15:09:42.947
