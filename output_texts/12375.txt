Tag: Stock Market

Dow Extends Wining Streak United States Stock MarketWall Street turned positive in the final minutes of Friday after staying in the red during most of the session on weaker than expected new home sales and consumer sentiment. The Dow closed 11 points higher, or 0.1 percent, to 20,821.76. The S&P rose 4 points, or 0.2 percent, to 2,367.34. The Nasdaq increased 10 points, or 0.2 percent, to 5,845.31. All major indices booked weekly gains, with the Dow rising 1.0 percent, the S&P 0.7 percent, and the Nasdaq 0.1 percent.2017-02-24T21:18:02
