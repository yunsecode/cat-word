Title: Wall Street Hits New Record High
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Hits New Record HighUnited States Stock MarketU.S. stocks closed sharply higher on Friday, with the S&P and the Nasdaq closing at the record high after better than expected jobs report lifted investors sentiment. The Dow Jones surged 191.48 points, or 1%, to finish at 18,543.53; the S&P 500 was up 18.62 points, or 0.9%, to 2,182.87; and the Nasdaq Composite finsihed up 54.87 points, or 1.1%, to close at 5,221.12. 2016-08-06T15:36:23.063
