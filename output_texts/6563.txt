Title: US Exports at 11-Month High
Tag: Exports

US Exports at 11-Month HighUnited States ExportsExports from the United States increased 1.0 percent from the previous month to USD 191.9 billion in January of 2021, the highest level since February and the eighth straight month of rises, as global demand continued to recover from the coronavirus pandemic shock. Goods exports were up by USD 2.1 billion to USD 133.7 billion, boosted by sales of industrial supplies and materials (USD 2.5 billion), such as other petroleum products (USD 0.6 billion), and plastic materials (USD 0.4 billion). Meanwhile, services exports decreased by USD 0.3 billion to USD 56.3 billion, dragged by transport (USD 0.1 billion) and travel (USD 0.1 billion). 2021-03-05T13:34:00
