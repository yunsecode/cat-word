Title: US Consumer Sentiment Falters in January
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Falters in JanuaryUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe Thomson Reuters/University of Michigan's consumer sentiment index fell in January to a final reading of 81.2, from 82.5 in December, but went up from a preliminary reading of 80.4. The sentiment index was hurt by lower expectations among lower and middle-income households.2014-01-31T15:30:21.57
