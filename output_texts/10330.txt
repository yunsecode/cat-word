Title: US Existing Home Sales Lowest since 2016
Tag: Existing Home Sales

US Existing Home Sales Lowest since 2016United States Existing Home SalesSales of previously owned houses in the US declined 0.7 percent month-over-month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 5.34 million in July of 2018. It compares with market expectations of a 0.6 percent rise to 5.4 million. It is the fourth straight fall and the lowest rate since February of 2016. The median house price fell to $269,600 from $273,800 in June and the months' worth of supply was flat at 4.3. In addition, the number of houses available in the market declined to 1.92 million from 1.93 million in June. Year-on-year, existing home sales fell 1.5 percent.2018-08-22T14:07:50.413
