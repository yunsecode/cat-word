Title: Dollar Little Changed on Stimulus Talks
Tag: Currency

Dollar Little Changed on Stimulus TalksUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded nearly flat around 93.7 on Monday as investors await more developments on a new stimulus plan in the US. House Speaker Nancy Pelosi set a Tuesday deadline for the White House to agree on further fiscal stimulus and said she was optimistic about negotiations after speaking with Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin during the weekend. Meanwhile, trade-exposed currencies such as the yuan and the Australian dollar appreciated after growth figures for China showed the economy continues to robustly recover from the pandemic. On the other hand, rising coronavirus cases in Europe and lack of progress on Brexit talks continue to push the dollar up. 2020-10-19T07:16:00
