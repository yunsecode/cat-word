Title: US Markit Services PMI Revised Up
Tag: Services PMI

US Markit Services PMI Revised UpUnited States Services PMIThe final Markit US Services PMI came in at 51.3 in March of 2016, higher than a preliminary figure of 51 and 49.7 in the previous month. Job creation was robust while new work expanded at its slowest pace on record due to uncertainty about the economic outlook and cautious spending patterns. Input cost inflation remained subdued and output prices increased only marginally.2016-04-05T14:53:08.013
