Title: US Producer Prices Flat In May
Tag: Producer Price Inflation MoM

US Producer Prices Flat In MayUnited States Producer Price Inflation MoMProducer prices for final demand in the United States were unchanged in May of 2017 from the previous month, following a 0.5 percent rise in April, in line with market expectations. A 0.3 percent rise in cost of services was offset by 0.5 percent drop in prices of goods. Energy cost slumped 3 percent, the biggest drop since February of 2016. Excluding food and energy, prices went up 0.3 percent, below 0.4 percent in April. Year-on-year, producer prices rose at a slower 2.4 percent (2.5 percent in April) while the core index went up 2.1 percent (1.9 percent). 2017-06-13T12:40:00
