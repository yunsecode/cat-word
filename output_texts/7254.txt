Title: Initial Claims Seen Little Changed
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

Initial Claims Seen Little Changed United States Initial Jobless ClaimsInitial jobless claims are seen edging down to 707 thousand in the week ended November 13th, from 709 thousand in the previous week. That would be the fifth consecutive week with claims remaining below 800 thousand, the first time it happens since the effects of the coronavirus pandemic started to being felt back in March. Still, initial claims remain well above pre-pandemic levels. At the same time, continuing claims are forecast to drop to 6.47 million from 6.78 million in the November 6th week, also the lowest level since March. However, rising coronavirus infections and new restrictions introduced in many states are likely to negatively affect the labour market in the coming weeks.2020-11-19T11:38:00
