Title: US Stocks Rise on Friday, Gain on the Week
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on Friday, Gain on the WeekUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed higher on Friday after a worse-than-expected US jobs report for August lowered expectations for a Fed rate hike this month.The Dow Jones added 73 points or 0.4%, the S&P 500 increased 9 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq closed 23 points or 0.4% higher. On the week, the Dow and the S&P 500 gained 0.5% each and the Nasdaq rose 0.6%. 2016-09-02T21:25:10.77
