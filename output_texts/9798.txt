Title: US ISM New York Index at 6-Month Low in December
Tag: Ism New York Index

US ISM New York Index at 6-Month Low in DecemberUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions index in the United States fell to 65.4 in December of 2018 from 67.8 in November, pointing to the slowest business growth in the New York area since June. Employment (64.5 from 72.7), purchases (54.2 from 59.4), and expected revenues (62.5 from 78.1) increased less and price pressures softened (63.6 from 71.9). On the other hand, current revenues advanced at a faster pace, reaching a 3-month high of 75.0 (from 65.6 in November). Meantime, the Six-Month Outlook deteriorated to 57.1 from 73.4.2019-01-03T14:51:00
