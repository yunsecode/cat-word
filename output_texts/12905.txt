Title: US Industrial Production Edges Up 0.1% in September
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Production Edges Up 0.1% in SeptemberUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial output in the United States increased 0.1 percent month-over-month in September of 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.5 percent fall in August and below market expectations of a 0.2 percent gain. Manufacturing and mining rebounded while utilities fell. For the third quarter as a whole, industrial production rose at an annual rate of 1.8 percent for its first quarterly increase since the third quarter of 2015.2016-10-17T13:19:32
