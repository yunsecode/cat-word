Title: US Stocks Plunge on Trade Concerns
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Plunge on Trade ConcernsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on Monday 25 June 2018 amid rising trade concerns after news that President Trump will impose restrictions on Chinese investment in US companies and start-ups. The Dow Jones lost 328 points or 1.3% to 24253. The S&P 500 declined 38 points or 1.4% to 2717. The Nasdaq plummeted 161 points or 2.1% to 7532. 2018-06-25T20:06:29.817
