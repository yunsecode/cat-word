Title: US Jobless Rate Drops To 16-Year Low of 4.3% In May
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Drops To 16-Year Low of 4.3% In MayUnited States Unemployment RateUS unemployment rate fell to 4.3 percent in May 2017 from 4.4 percent in the previous month and below market expectations of 4.4 percent. It was the lowest jobless rate since May 2001, as the number of unemployed persons was little changed at 6.9 million and the labor force participation rate fell to 62.7 percent.2017-06-02T12:32:27
