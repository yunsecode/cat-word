Title: US Inflation Rate Higher than Expected
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Higher than ExpectedUnited States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States increased 2.1 percent year-on-year in January of 2018, the same as in December and above market expectations of 1.9 percent. A slowdown in gasoline and electricity prices was offset by higher cost for food and medical care services. The monthly inflation rate went up to 0.5 percent from 0.2 percent amid broad-based cost increases.2018-02-14T13:33:10
