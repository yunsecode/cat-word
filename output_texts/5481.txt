Title: US Consumer Inflation Expectations Hold at 4.8%
Tag: Inflation Expectations

US Consumer Inflation Expectations Hold at 4.8%United States Inflation ExpectationsMedian year-ahead inflation expectations in the US stayed at a series high of 4.8 percent in July, following a substantial jump in June. Meantime, expectations for inflation over the next three years rose to 3.7 percent from 3.6 percent in the prior month, reaching the highest level since August 2013. The survey also showed that consumers' expectations for how much home prices will rise over the next year dropped to a median of 6.0 percent in July from 6.2 percent in June.2021-08-09T15:23:01.207
