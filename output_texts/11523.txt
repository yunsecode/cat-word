Title: US Core PCE Prices Rise 0.1% in September
Tag: Core PCE Price Index MoM

US Core PCE Prices Rise 0.1% in SeptemberUnited States Core PCE Price Index MoMThe so-called core PCE price index in the United States, which excludes food and energy, went up 0.1 percent month-over-month in September 2017, the same pace as in the previous period and in line with market expectations. Year-on-year, the core PCE price index rose 1.3 percent, the same pace as in the previous month and also in line with market consensus. The core PCE is the Fed's preferred inflation measure. The central bank has a 2 percent target.2017-10-30T12:36:33.09
