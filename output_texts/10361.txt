US Mortgage Applications Fall for 5th Straight Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States declined 2 percent in the week ended August 10th 2018, after falling 3 percent in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home dropped 3.3 percent while refinance applications were unchanged. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 3bps to 4.81 percent.2018-08-15T11:06:54.853
