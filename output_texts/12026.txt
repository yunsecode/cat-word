Title: US Imports Rise 0.8% To 3-Month High In April
Tag: Imports

US Imports Rise 0.8% To 3-Month High In AprilUnited States ImportsTotal imports to the United States increased 0.8 percent month-over-month to USD 238.6 billion in April 2017, due to higher imports of consumer goods while purchases of industrial supplies fell sharply. On a non-seasonally adjusted basis, imports of goods from China jumped 9.6 percent, while purchases declined from Japan (-14.3 percent), Mexico (-10.3 percent), Canada (-7.5 percent) and Germany (-4.1 percent).2017-06-02T12:41:36
