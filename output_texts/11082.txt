Title: US Stocks End Mixed on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on 16 February 2018, as stocks recovered from the worst week in two years amid concerns over rising inflation and interest rates. The Dow Jones gained 19 points or 0.1% to 25219. The S&P 500 edged up 1 point or less than 0.1% to 2732. In contrast, the Nasdaq retreated 17 points or 0.2% to 7239. During the week, equities booked the strongest weekly gains since 2013, with the Dow Jones jumping 4.3%; the S&P 500 4.3%; and the Nasdaq 5.3%. 2018-02-16T21:08:12
