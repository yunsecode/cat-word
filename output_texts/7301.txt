Title: Dollar at 1-Week High
Tag: Currency

Dollar at 1-Week HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index strengthened to 93.1 on Wednesday, the highest on a closing basis in a week, amid hopes that central banks will not have to inject more money to support the economies if a coronavirus vaccine is available soon. At the same time,  prospects of a divided Congress that will prevent the new Biden administration from raising taxes and regulate businesses also supported the greenback. On the other hand, worries about vaccine deployment and how it will be delivered and rising infections in the US and Europe capped gains.2020-11-11T10:55:00
