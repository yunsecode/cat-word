Title: US ISM New York Index At Near 1-Year Low
Tag: Ism New York Index

US ISM New York Index At Near 1-Year LowUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions in the United States decreased to 46.7 in May of 2017 from 55.8 in the previous month, reaching the lowest value since June of 2016. Decreases were seen in employment (40.3 from 45.1 in April) and current revenues (50 from 55). On the positive side, quantity of purchases increased (55.3 from 48.4), prices paid slowed (50 from 54.7) and expected revenues went up (66.7 from 65.5). The six-month outlook also fell to 70.6 from 73.2.2017-06-02T13:58:42
