Title: US Exports Fall 1% MoM in July
Tag: Exports

US Exports Fall 1% MoM in JulyUnited States ExportsExports from the United States were USD 211.1 billion in July of 2018, 1 percent or USD 2.1 billion less than in June. Sales declined mainly due to capital goods (USD 0.9 billion); civilian aircraft (USD 1.6 billion); foods, feeds, and beverages (USD 0.9 billion), namely soybeans (USD 0.7 billion) and other goods (USD 0.5 billion). Meanwhile, exports of services increased by USD 0.2 billion to USD 70.3 billion, mostly due to charges for the use of intellectual property (USD 0.1 billion) and other business services, which includes research and development services; professional and management services; and technical, trade-related, and other services (USD 0.1 billion).2018-09-05T12:35:00
