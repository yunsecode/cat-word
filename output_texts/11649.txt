Title: US House Prices Rise 0.2% in July: FHFA
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US House Prices Rise 0.2% in July: FHFAUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States increased 0.2 percent month-over-month in July of 2017, following a 0.1 percent rise in June and missing market expectations of 0.4 percent. Year-on-year, prices went up 6.3 percent after rising 6.5 percent in June.2017-09-21T13:17:18.48
