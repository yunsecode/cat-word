Title: US Stocks Rise Ahead Of Earnings Season
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise Ahead Of Earnings SeasonUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately up on Monday with financial stocks among the best performers, as faster economic growth in China and optimism ahead of earnings season momentarily cooled geopolitical risk. The Dow Jones gained 184 points, or 0.9%, to 20,637. The S&P 500 increased by 20 points, or 0.9%, to 2,349. The Nasdaq closed 52 points higher, or 0.9%, to 5,857.2017-04-17T20:05:59
