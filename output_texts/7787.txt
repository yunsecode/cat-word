Title: US Stocks Trade Slightly Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Slightly HigherUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones and the S&P traded slightly higher on Wednesday while the Nasdaq failed to hold gains, following a rally in the previous day which saw both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq closing at record highs. House Speaker Nancy Pelosi said the Democrats are willing to cut new stimulus proposal. Meanwhile, minutes from the last FOMC meeting due later in the day may provide some more detail on the Fed's monetary policy. On the earnings front, Target and Lowe's beat forecasts. Nvidia and JCPenney are also due to report today. Investors remain cautious as tensions with China mount after President Trump said that he has postponed trade talks with China and does not want to speak with China right now. 2020-08-19T13:34:00
