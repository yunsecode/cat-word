Title: US Import Prices Unchanged in July
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Unchanged in JulyUnited States Import Prices MoMUS import prices were unchanged from a month earlier in July 2018, following a downwardly revised 0.1 percent fall in June and missing market consensus of a 0.1 percent gain. Prices for import fuel advanced 1.6 percent in July following a 1.3 percent increase in June, boosted by petroleum (0.9 percent vs 1.4 percent) and natural gas (36.7 percent vs -8.3 percent). By contrast, non-fuel import prices fell 0.3 percent for the second consecutive month in July, due to non-fuel industrial supplies and materials; foods, feeds, and beverages; and capital goods. Year-on-year, import prices increased 4.8 percent in July, the sharpest annual gain since February 2012.2018-08-14T12:36:00
