Dollar Appreciates for 2nd DayUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index strengthened for the second straight session to around 93.7 on Thursday, trading at near two-week highs, as traders rush to the safety of the greenback amid rising coronavirus infections and a potentially contested election result in the US. New Covid-19 cases continue to rise at record pace in the US and in Europe, leading France to impose a new 1-month lockdown while Germany will see a 'light' one.
