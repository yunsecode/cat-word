Title: US Job Openings Rise More than Expected
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Job OffersThe number of job openings in the US rose to 7.488 million in March 2019 from a nine-month low of 7.142 million in the previous month, easily beating market expectations of 7.240 million. Job openings increased in a number of industries, with the largest gains in transportation, warehousing, and utilities (+87,000), construction (+73,000), and real estate and rental and leasing (+57,000). By contrast, a decline was seen in federal government openings (-15,000).2019-05-07T14:04:00
