Title: US Futures Volatile on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Volatile on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketUS futures pointed to a negative start in a volatile session on Tuesday, as concerns about the impact of further lockdown and economic shutdown on US growth continue to weigh on investors' mood. California Governor ordered all counties in the state to close indoor operations for several businesses including restaurants, bars, movie theaters and museums, amid surging Covid-19 cases and hospitalizations. Also, schools in LA and San Diego announced they will conduct only online classes this fall. Meanwhile, stronger than expected earnings results from JPMorgan and Citigroup helped to curb the losses, while Delta Air Lines and Wells Fargo came in worse than expected. On Monday, the Dow Jones gained 11 points or less than 0.1% to 26,086. In contrast, the S&P 500 lost 30 points or 0.9% to 3155, while the Nasdaq plunged 227 points or 2.1% to 10,391.2020-07-14T11:04:00
