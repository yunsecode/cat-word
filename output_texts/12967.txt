Title: US Personal Spending Unchanged in August
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Unchanged in AugustUnited States Personal SpendingConsumer spending in the United States was flat in August from July of 2016, after rising an upwardly revised 0.4 percent in the previous period. It is the worst performance in five months and worse than market expectations of a 0.1 percent gain. Spending declined for both durable (-1.3 percent) and nondurable (-0.2 percent) goods but rose 0.3 percent for services.2016-09-30T12:42:10
