Title: US Economic Optimism Index at Over 15-Year High in May
Tag: Economic Optimism Index

US Economic Optimism Index at Over 15-Year High in MayUnited States Economic Optimism IndexThe IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index in the United States rose to 58.6 in May 2019 from 54.2 in the prior month, reaching its highest level since February 2004. The Personal Financial Outlook, a measure of how Americans feel about their own finances in the next six months gained 2 points to 64.4, and the Six-Month Economic Outlook, a measure of how consumers feel about the economy’s prospects in the next six months, increased 7 points to 55.8. Also, the Confidence in Federal Economic Policies, a proprietary IBD/TIPP measure of views on how government economic policies are working, jumped by 4.1 points to 55.6.
