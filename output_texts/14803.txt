Title: U.S. Economy Grows Just 1.3% in Q2
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

U.S. Economy Grows Just 1.3% in Q2United States GDP Growth RateUnited States real gross domestic product increased at an annual rate of 1.3 percent in the second quarter of 2011, (that is, from the first quarter to the second quarter), according to the "advance" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. First-quarter growth was revised down sharply to a 0.4% rate from the earlier estimate of a 1.9% gain. 2011-07-29T13:44:53.433
