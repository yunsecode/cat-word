Title: Wall Street Set to Open Lower
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Set to Open LowerUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures tracking the broader market were down roughly 1% on Thursday, with investors fretting about the economy's health while reassessing the outlook for monetary policy. Mixed signs from the US economy, with inflation showing some evidence that it is abating but retail sales numbers indicating an unexpected resilience among US consumers, added to uncertainty about the Fed's next step. While policymakers hinted that soon should be appropriate to slow the pace of interest rate increases, there is yet to be consensus about the terminal level. On the corporate side, Bath & Body Works skyrocketed almost 20% in premarket trading after the retailer beat Wall Street forecasts for revenue. Cisco added over 4% after topping both earnings and revenue estimates.2022-11-17T13:01:00
