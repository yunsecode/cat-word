Title: US IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index Below Expectations
Tag: Economic Optimism Index

US IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index Below ExpectationsUnited States Economic Optimism IndexThe IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index declined to 51.3 in May 2017 from 51.7 in April and below market expectations of 52.3. Although it was the lowest figure since October 2016, Americans are encouraged by a number of President Trump’s domestic initiatives, such as his plans for tax reform and his focus on job creation. The personal financial outlook sub-index fell 4.2 points to 57.1 and the six-month economic outlook declined 1.5 points to 49.6. Meanwhile, the measure of confidence in federal economic policies rose 4.5 points to 47.1.
