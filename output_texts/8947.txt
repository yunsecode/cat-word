Title: US Export Prices Rise More than Expected in November
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise More than Expected in NovemberUnited States Export Prices MoMExport prices in the US increased 0.2 percent month-over-month in November 2019, after a 0.1 percent drop in the prior month and above market expectations of a 0.1 percent rise. Prices of agricultural exports went up 2.2 percent, after rising 1.7 percent in October, while those of non agricultural products showed no growth after decreasing 0.2 percent in the previous month. Year-on-year, export prices declined 1.3 percent, following a 2.2 percent fall in October and against market consensus of a 2.0 percent drop.
