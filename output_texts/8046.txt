Title: US 10-Year Bond Yields Higher after Jobs Report
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Bond Yields Higher after Jobs ReportUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on US 10-Year Treasury rose to 0.70% on Thursday, after US payroll report showed the economy added 4.8 million jobs in June, beating market forecasts of a 3.0 million increase, while the jobless rate fell to 11.1%, easing from an all-time high of 14.7% reached in April.2020-07-02T12:49:00
