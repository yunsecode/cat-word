Title: US Stocks Rebound, Nasdaq At Record High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound, Nasdaq At Record HighUnited States Stock MarketWall Street recovered on Wednesday, shrugging off falls in the health care sector after President-Elect Trump said there’s little bidding on drugs, suggesting the US government would start negotiating more aggressively drug prices. The Dow Jones added 99 points or 0.5%, the S&P 500 gained 6 points or 0.3% and the Nasdaq rose 12 points or 0.2% to close at a fresh high of 5563.65, 5th straight record close.2017-01-11T21:18:49.573
