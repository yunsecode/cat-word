Title: US Stocks Fall
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks FallUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded lower on Monday, reversing earlier gains after data showed construction spending unexpectedly fell 0.6% in December, compared to expectations of a 0.2% gain. Investors also await further developments on US-China trade negotiations: if a deal can be reached by the end of the month and if tariffs are removed. The Dow Jones was down 1.1%, the S&P 500 fell 0.8% and the Nasdaq traded 0.8% lower around 12:10 PM NY time.2019-03-04T17:21:03.71
