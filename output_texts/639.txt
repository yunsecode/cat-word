Title: Texas Service Sector Outlook Remains Weak
Tag: Dallas Fed Services Index

Texas Service Sector Outlook Remains WeakUnited States Dallas Fed Services IndexThe Dallas Fed general business activity index for Texas' service sector increased to -14.4 in April of 2023 from -18 in March, suggesting perceptions of broader business conditions continued to worsen, though pessimism waned slightly. The company outlook index also improved to -9.4 from -11.3 while the outlook uncertainty index fell to 16.1. At the same time, the future general business activity index remained negative but largely unchanged at -13.0. In April, improvements were seen for revenues (6.9 vs 5.5), a key measure of state service sector conditions, employment (5.5 vs -0.4), input prices (35.5 vs 38.3), wage pressures (18.4 vs 19.8) and capital expenditures (10.3 vs 7.4).2023-04-25T14:42:42.727
