Title: US Stocks Rebound Sharply
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound Sharply United States Stock MarketWall Street closed deep in the green on 14 February 2018 in spite of US inflation report showing consumer prices increased more than expected (2.1 percent vs 1.9 percent expected) fanning concerns over the pace of US interest rate rises. The Dow Jones jumped 253 points or 1.0% to 24893. The S&P 500 gained 36 points or 1.3% to 2699. The Nasdaq soared 130 points or 1.9% to 7144.
