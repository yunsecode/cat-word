Title: Disney Pushes Dow Up, S&P and Nasdaq Subdued
Tag: Stock Market

Disney Pushes Dow Up, S&P and Nasdaq SubduedUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones added nearly 100 points to start a shortened holiday week, pushed by an 8% surge in Disney shares after the company announced that Bob Iger will return as CEO. On the other hand, the S&P 500 traded around the flatline while the Nasdaq lost nearly 0.3%, amid a drop in risk appetite on concerns of further curbs in China after the country reported covid deaths for the first time in six months during the weekend. Meanwhile, traders remain cautious ahead of the FOMC minutes release on Wednesday which could provide further clues on the pace of the Fed's rate hikes. The US bond market will be closed on Thursday for the Thanksgiving holiday and will close early on Friday.2022-11-21T14:34:00
