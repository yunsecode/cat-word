Title: United States Mortgage Applications Rise 0.6%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

United States Mortgage Applications Rise 0.6%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States went up 0.6 percent in the week ended June 16th 2017, following a 2.8 percent increase in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications rose 2.1 percent while applications to purchase a home shrank 1 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rate were steady at 4.13 percent, the lowest since November of 2016.2017-06-21T11:00:42.03
