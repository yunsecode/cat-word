Title: US Jobless Claims at 1-Month Low in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims at 1-Month Low in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment fell by 16 thousand to 212 thousand in the week ending May 11th 2019 from the previous week's unrevised level of 228 thousand while markets had expected a smaller drop to 220 thousand. This is the lowest level for initial claims since the week ended April 13th of 2019, as seasonal effects from the timing of Easter have faded. According to unadjusted data, the largest declines were seen in New York (-15,255), Pennsylvania (-1,029) and Arizona (-631) while the biggest rises were registered in California (+1,848), Texas (+394) and Minnesota (+315).
