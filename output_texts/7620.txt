Title: Dollar Gains on Safe-Haven Bids
Tag: Currency

Dollar Gains on Safe-Haven BidsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar firmed against major currencies for a second straight session on Tuesday, as investors took shelter in the US currency on remarks by Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell, who reiterated the central bank will use its full range of tools to support the economy. Earlier this session, the dollar index rallied above 94 for the first time in two months after Fed's Evans said the central bank could raise rates before inflation averages 2%. 2020-09-22T17:41:00
