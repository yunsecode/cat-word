Title: Dow Extends Gains on Upbeat Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Extends Gains on Upbeat EarningsUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones and the S&P 500 rose on Tuesday helped by positive earnings reports from companies such as IBM, Coca-Cola, Lockheed Martin, Philip Morris, Novartis and ProLogis, as well as promising early data from trials of three potential vaccines. Investors also cheered news that EU leaders had agreed on a massive coronavirus relief program, while awaited further details about a fresh aid package in the US. US lawmakers have less than two weeks to agree on stimulus before assistance runs out for millions of Americans hit by the coronavirus crisis.2020-07-21T13:33:00
