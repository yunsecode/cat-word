Title: US Export Prices Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Unexpectedly FallUnited States Export Prices MoMPrices for US exports decreased 1.8 percent from a month earlier in December of 2021, the most since a 3.5 percent fall in April 2020, after a downwardly revised 0.8 percent rise in November and compared to market expectations of a 0.3 percent increase. Nonagricultural export charges declined 2.1 percent, the most since April 2020 as lower prices for nonagricultural industrial supplies and materials and nonagricultural food more than offset higher prices for capital goods and consumer goods.  Meanwhile, prices for agricultural exports increased 0.8 percent as higher prices for soybeans, fruit, cotton, dairy products, and corn more than offset lower prices for nuts and meat. Year-on-year, export prices rose 14.7 percent, the least since March and easing from an 18.2 percent advance in the previous month. 2022-01-14T13:35:00
