Title: US Housing Starts Highest Since 2016
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Highest Since 2016United States Housing StartsHousing starts in the US jumped 9.7 percent month-over-month to an annualized rate of 1,326 thousand in January of 2018, following a downwardly revised 6.9 percent fall in December and beating market expectations of a 3.4 percent rise. It is the highest rate since October of 2016.2018-02-16T13:36:19
