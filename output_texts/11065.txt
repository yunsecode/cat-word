Title: US Stocks Gain on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Gain on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded in the green on Friday after closing mixed in the previous session as Treasury yields continue to fall and the dollar strengthened slightly. Investors also await speeches from Federal Reserve officials and the Fed release of a monetary policy report during the day. The Dow Jones added 160 points or 0.6%; the S&P 500 went up 16 points or 0.6%; and the Nasdaq gained 46 points or 0.6% around 25 minutes after the opening bell.2018-02-23T15:01:27.257
