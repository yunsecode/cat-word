Title: US Economy Adds 20K Jobs in February, Well Below Forecasts
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds 20K Jobs in February, Well Below ForecastsUnited States Non Farm PayrollsNonfarm payrolls in the US increased by 20 thousand in February of 2019, following an upwardly revised 311 thousand rise in January and well below market expectations of 180 thousand. It is the lowest reading since February of 2017, mainly due to falls in construction employment while jobs were added in professional and business services, health care, and wholesale trade. 2019-03-08T13:31:00
