Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Remains Near 2-Week Low
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Remains Near 2-Week LowUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note edged higher to 1.255% on Friday but it still remained way below 1.28% hit at the end of last week and 1.78% in late March amid fears that the global economic recovery already under stress from rising covid infections may be further delayed by the early tapering of quantitative easing by the Fed. Federal Reserve meeting minutes on Wednesday showed the central bank will likely start to reduce stimulus this year as the standard of "substantial further progress" toward inflation has been achieved while the maximum-employment goal had not yet been met. All eyes now turn to the Jackson Hole Symposium next week when Fed officials are expected to announce some details on the exact tapering timeline.2021-08-20T14:00:00
