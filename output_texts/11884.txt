Title: US Industrial Output Rises More Than Expected In June
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Output Rises More Than Expected In JuneUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial production in the United States increased by 0.4 percent month-over-month in June 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.1 percent rise in May and beating market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. Manufacturing output rebounded from the previous month, while mining rose at a slower pace and utilities showed no growth.2017-07-14T13:15:29
