Title: US Treasury Yields Remain Elevated
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Remain ElevatedUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note was at 1.6% on Tuesday, remaining slightly below 1.63% hit in the previous session but still close to the highest level since June. Investors continue to anticipate that the Fed would start tapering next month amid rising inflationary pressures and soaring energy prices but still bet the American economy is in better shape to recover from the coronavirus hit. Strong corporate results so far also helped to lift investors' mood. The Treasury will make several auctions this week, including $24 billion of 20-year bonds, $19 billion of 5-year TIPS and $60 billion of 40-day bills.2021-10-19T08:28:00
