Title: US Stocks Open Nearly Flat
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Nearly FlatUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened little changed on Friday amid a fall in oil prices and mixed economic data for the US. In addition, investors await earnings reports for a few companies later in the day and remain cautious ahead of oil producers meeting on Sunday where a deal to freeze output may be reached. The Dow Jones and the S&P 500 traded near the flatline while the Nasdaq lost 0.19% around ten minutes after the open.2016-04-15T14:46:16
