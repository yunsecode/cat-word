Title: Fed May Raise Rates Soon
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed May Raise Rates Soon United States Interest RateMany Fed officials said it might be appropriate to raise rates again fairly soon, depending on incoming data for labour market and inflation, minutes from FOMC meeting held on January 31-February 1 showed. However, policymakers emphasized uncertainty regarding fiscal policies and showed concerns over the dollar appreciation.
