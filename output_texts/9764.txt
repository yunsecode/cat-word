Title: US Inflation Rate Lowest in 16 Months
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Lowest in 16 MonthsUnited States Inflation RateNovember, matching market expectations. It is the lowest inflation rate since August of 2017, mainly due to a decline in gasoline cost. On a monthly basis, consumer prices edged down 0.1 percent after a flat reading in the previous month and also in line with forecasts. It is the first monthly decrease in consumer prices in nine months, due to a 7.5 percent slump in gasoline prices.2019-01-11T13:34:00
