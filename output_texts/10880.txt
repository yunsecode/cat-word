Title: US Stocks Fall
Tag: stock market

US Stocks FallUnited States stock marketWall Street opened in the red on Friday, ending a three-day winning streak amid renewed concerns of a trade war between the US and China after President Trump proposed an additional $100 billion tariff on Chinese products. Also, the March jobs report showed the economy added 103 thousand jobs, well below forecasts of 193 thousand. The Dow Jones fell 176 points or 0.7%, the S&P 500 declined 13 points or 0.5% and the Nasdaq traded 28 points or 0.4% lower around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2018-04-06T13:30:04
