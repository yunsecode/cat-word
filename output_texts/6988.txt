Title: US Wholesale Inventories Revised to a Flat Reading
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Revised to a Flat ReadingUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US were flat month-over-month in November of 2020, compared to initial estimates of a 0.1 percent drop, and breaking a 3-month winning streak. Inventories of durable goods increased 0.9 percent while those for non-durables sank 1.2 percent. Year-on-year, wholesale inventories dropped 2.1 percent.2021-01-08T15:06:24.273
