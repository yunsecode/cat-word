Title: Chicago Business Barometer Falls in February
Tag: Chicago PMI

Chicago Business Barometer Falls in FebruaryUnited States Chicago PMIThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer in the US decreased by 4.3 points to 59.5 in February 2021 from the previous month's 2-1/2-year high of 63.8, missing market expectations of 61.1. Among the main five indicators, New Orders saw the largest monthly decline (down 11 points), followed by Production (down 9.3 points). Employment recorded the biggest gain (up 5.7 points to a sixteen-month high).2021-02-26T14:59:00
