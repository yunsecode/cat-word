Title: United States Consumer Credit Beats Expectations
Tag: Consumer Credit

United States Consumer Credit Beats ExpectationsUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States rose by USD 20.52 billion in October of 2017, following a downwardly revised USD 19.21 billion gain in September and above market expectations of USD 17.50 billion. Revolving credit increased by USD 8.3 billion after rising by 6.0 billion in the previous month. Non-revolving credit went up by USD 12.20 billion, compared with a USD 13.2 billion gain in September.2017-12-07T20:09:26.7
