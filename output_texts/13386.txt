Title: US Planned Layoffs at 5-Month Low
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Planned Layoffs at 5-Month LowUnited States Challenger Job CutsThe number of announced corporate layoffs in the United States fell 53 percent month over month to 30,157 in May of 2016, the lowest figure since December last year. Most of the cuts occurred again in the energy sector (7,572) although lower than 18,759 in the previous month. Job cuts plunged 83 percent to 2,836 in computer industry; declined 68 percent to 901 in the financial sector and were 75 percent lower at 1,287 among retailers. Year-on-year, layoffs fell 27 percent. Considering the first five months the year, 275,218 planned job cuts were announced, up 13 percent from the same period a year ago.
