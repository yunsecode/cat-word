Title: US GDP Growth Revised Up to 3.2% in Q3
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Revised Up to 3.2% in Q3United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy advanced an annualized 3.2 percent on quarter in the three months to September of 2016, up from 1.4 percent growth in the previous period and better than a 2.9 percent expansion in the advance estimate. It is the highest growth rate in two years, as consumer spending, exports and investment in structures rose faster than anticipated while fixed investment fell more, according to the second estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.2016-11-29T13:33:07
