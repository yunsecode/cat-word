Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Friday 22 June 2018, amid trade uncertainty between the US and China and news that OPEC reached a deal to increase oil supply, with crude oil prices rising as much as 5% during the trading session. The Dow Jones gained 119 points or 0.5% to 24581. The S&P 500 edged up 5 points or 0.2% to 2755. In contrast, the Nasdaq lost 20 points or 0.3% to 7693.2018-06-22T20:08:16.587
