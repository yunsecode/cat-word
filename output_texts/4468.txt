Title: US Stocks Rebound Strongly Led by Microsoft
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound Strongly Led by MicrosoftUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks rebounded on Wednesday with the Dow Jones rising more than 200 points before parings some gains, the S&P 500 adding around 1% and the Nasdaq soaring nearly 2%. Microsoft was among the top gainers rising 4% after the company's earnings, revenues and sales forecasts topped estimates. Results from AT&T and Abbott also beat on the upside but stocks failed to rise and Boeing shares declined more than 3% after the company reported a much bigger-than-expected loss of $7.69 per share for the fourth quarter. Tesla and Intel are due to report quarterly results after markets close. Meanwhile, investors look ahead to the Fed decision later in the day for more clarity on how much and how quickly interest rates will be raised.2022-01-26T14:37:00
