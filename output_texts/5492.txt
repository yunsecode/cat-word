Title: US Jobless Rate Falls More than Expected
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Falls More than ExpectedUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate declined by 0.5 percentage points to 5.4 percent in July of 2021, the lowest level since March 2020 and below market expectations of 5.7 percent. The number of unemployed persons fell by 782,000 to 8.7 million. These measures are down considerably from their highs at the end of the February-April 2020 recession. However, they remain well above their levels prior to the coronavirus pandemic (3.5 percent and 5.7 million, respectively, in February 2020). The labor force participation rate was little changed at 61.7 percent in July and has remained within a narrow range of 61.4 percent to 61.7 percent since June 2020. 2021-08-06T12:32:00
