Title: Dollar Falls Despite Trade Optimism
Tag: Currency

Dollar Falls Despite Trade OptimismUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index plunged 0.8% to 96.7 on Thursday despite renewed trade optimism, after an exit poll suggested that Prime Minister Boris Johnson's Conservative Party will win a comfortable majority in Britain's parliament and deliver Brexit on January 31st. Earlier, the dollar had erased some losses after President Trump tweeted that China and the US were getting very close to a big deal.2019-12-12T22:52:00
