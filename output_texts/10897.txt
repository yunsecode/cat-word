Title: US Private Sector Growth Remains Solid: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Growth Remains Solid: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI stood at 54.2 in March 2018, little-changed from a preliminary estimate of 54.3 and below the previous month's final reading of 55.8. Services output rose at a softer pace (PMI at 54 vs 55.9 in February) while manufacturing grew the most since March 2015 (PMI at 55.6 vs 55.3 in February). The average rise in new orders over the first three months of 2018 was the strongest since the third quarter of 2014.2018-04-04T13:48:23
