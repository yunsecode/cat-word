Title: US Stocks Close Lower as Turkey Crisis Extends
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Lower as Turkey Crisis ExtendsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Monday 1 August 2018, as investors fear that the financial crisis in Turkey could hurt other markets.
