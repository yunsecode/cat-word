Title: US Stocks Open Higher On Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Higher On FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Friday as investors await further developments on the health care bill vote which is supposed to take place this Friday morning after being delayed on Thursday. The Dow Jones went up 20 points or 0.1%, the S&P 500 gained 5 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq added 26 points or 0.5% around 11 minutes after the opening bell. 2017-03-24T13:46:05
