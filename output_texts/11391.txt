Title: US Stocks End Mixed after Bullish Opening
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed after Bullish OpeningUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Monday December 4th of 2017, with the Dow Jones climbing to a new record but tech stocks falling sharply after the US Senate passed a tax cut bill over the weekend. The Dow Jones jumped 58 points or 0.2% to 24290. In contrast, the S&P 500 lost 2 points or 0.1% to 2640. The Nasdaq plunged 72 points or 1.1% to 6775.2017-12-04T21:10:11
