Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise 4.1% in Latest Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise 4.1% in Latest Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 4.1 percent in the week ended January 12th 2018, following an 8.3 percent jump in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications advanced 4.4 percent and applications to purchase a home went up 2.7 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate increased by 10bps to 4.33 percent.2018-01-17T12:06:52.88
