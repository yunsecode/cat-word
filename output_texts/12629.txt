Title: US Stocks Fall Slightly on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall Slightly on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened slightly lower on Wednesday, ahead of existing home sales release. In the previous session, the Dow and the Nasdaq closed at record highs and investors await the blue-chip index to top 20,000. The Dow Jones lost 12 points or less than 0.1%, the S&P 500 went down 3 points or 0.1% and the Nasdaq traded 6 points or 0.1% lower around 11 minutes after the opening bell.2016-12-21T14:50:59.727
