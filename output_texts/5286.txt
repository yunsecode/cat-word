Title: US Stocks Sink, Fed and Evergrande in Focus
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Sink, Fed and Evergrande in FocusUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks extended losses in afternoon trading on Monday, after falling sharply in the previous week with the Dow Jones falling more than 700 points to a fresh 3-month low and both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq losing more than 2% as investors' sentiment was hurt by a potential default from Chinese property giant Evergrande and rising Covid-19 cases ahead of the FOMC meeting later in the week. The Fed has made it already clear that tapering could occur this year but investors are waiting for more details, particularly after mixed economic data was released last week. The delta variant of the coronavirus pushed up infections to levels last seen in January, wiping out months of progress despite vaccination efforts as North America heads to colder weather.
