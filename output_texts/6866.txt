Title: Dollar Enjoys Wave of Momentum
Tag: Currency

Dollar Enjoys Wave of MomentumUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index steadied around 90.70 on Thursday, slightly below its weekly tops of 90.90 touched in the previous session as risk appetite ebbed amid persistent worries about surging virus cases and potential disruption to vaccine rollouts. The dollar's rally was particularly pronounced against riskier currencies, such as the Australian and New Zealand dollars. Meanwhile, the Federal Reserve has left policy unchanged and made no change to its monthly bond purchases. While the outlook over the next few months has darkened, the new Biden stimulus plans remains a positive backdrop for markets. 2021-01-27T19:20:00
