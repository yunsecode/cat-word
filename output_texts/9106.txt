Title: US Stocks Extend Gains on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend Gains on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed higher on Thursday, as optimism was boosted by news of progress and goodwill in trade negotiations between the US and China and by the ECB’s efforts to fight recession risks with a rate cut and more bond-buying. Domestic news were not so positive for stimulus bets, as US core inflation accelerated by more than forecast on rising health-care bills ahead of next week’s Fed monetary policy decision. On the commodities front, oil prices declined 1.5% on OPEC+ talks. The Dow Jones gained 45 points or 0.2%. The S&P 500 gained 9 points or 0.3%. The Nasdaq added 25 points or 0.3%.2019-09-12T20:05:00
