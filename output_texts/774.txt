Title: US Energy Prices Fall for 1st Time in Over 2 Years
Tag: Energy Inflation

US Energy Prices Fall for 1st Time in Over 2 YearsUnited States Energy InflationThe cost of energy in the US declined by 6.4% year-on-year in March 2023, compared with a 5.2% rise in the prior month. It was the first drop in energy prices since January 2021, amid a sharp fall in prices of gasoline (-17.4% vs -2% in February) and fuel oil (-14.2% vs -9.2%). At the same time, prices softened for natural gas (5.5% vs 14.3%) and electricity (10.2% vs 12.9%).2023-04-12T12:48:00
