Title: US Stocks Rise Despite Disappointing Jobs Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise Despite Disappointing Jobs DataUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Friday after US jobs report for August came well below expectations, sending the dollar down and lowering expectations of a Fed rate hike this month. The Dow Jones added 88 points or 0.5%, the S&P 500 gained 10 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq traded 19 points or 0.4% higher around 8 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-09-02T14:47:49
