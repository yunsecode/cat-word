US Crude Oil Stocks Unexpectedly Fall United States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil inventories in the United States fell by 0.754 million in the week ended February 5th, 2016, following a 7.792 million barrels rise in the previous week. It is the first decline in five weeks, compared to market expectations of a 3.5 million rise. Stocks of gasoline went up by 1.258 million barrels, much higher than market expectations of a 0.417 million gain.2016-02-10T15:40:43.38
