Title: US Stocks Rise on Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on EarningsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Wednesday, after better-than-expected earnings report from IBM, United Technologies and Procter & Gamble lifted investors' mood. The Dow Jones went up 1.1%, the S&P 500 rose 0.6% and the Nasdaq traded 0.7% higher around 10 minutes after the opening bell.
