Title: US Services PMI Revised Higher: S&P Global
Tag: Services PMI

US Services PMI Revised Higher: S&P GlobalUnited States Services PMIThe S&P Global US Services PMI was revised higher to 52.7 in June of 2022 from a preliminary of 51.6, and down from 53.4 in May pointing to the weakest rise in activity since January. New orders decrease for the first time in almost two years as sustained price pressures and economic uncertainty hit demand. On the price front, a further substantial rise in input prices was recorded in June, although inflation did ease from May's survey peak. Rising wages played a key role in higher input costs, with increased fuel charges also widely mentioned. Firms often passed on higher costs to their customers, but efforts to stimulate demand led to a further slowdown in charge inflation. Finally, business confidence regarding the year ahead outlook dropped to a 21-month low.2022-07-06T13:59:54.263
