Title: US Stocks Plunge As North Korea Tensions Escalate

US Stocks Plunge As North Korea Tensions EscalateUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on Thursday, as escalating rhetorical exchanges between the United States and North Korea sparked volatility. A selloff was seen across sectors and markets. The Dow Jones plunged 205 points or 0.9% to 21844. The S&P 500 retreated 35 points or 1.5% to 2438. The Nasdaq plummeted 135 points or 2.1% to 6217. 2017-08-10T20:07:52
