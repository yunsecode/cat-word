Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Lower United States Stock MarketWall Street opened the week sharply lower, with the Dow Jones losing almost 300 points while both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq fell nearly 0.8%. Investors continue to worry over high interest rates after hawkish remarks from Fed Chair at the Jackson Hole Symposium on Friday cut hopes the central bank would slow monetary tightening. Health, materials and utilities were among the worst S&P performers. Tech shares were also under pressure, amid a rise in Treasury yields.
