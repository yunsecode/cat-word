Title: US Small Business Optimism Nearly 9-1/2-Year Low
Tag: NFIB Business Optimism Index

US Small Business Optimism Nearly 9-1/2-Year LowUnited States NFIB Business Optimism IndexThe NFIB Small Business Optimism Index in the United States decreased to 89.5 in June of 2022, the lowest since January 2013, and compared to 93.1 in May. A net negative 61% of small business owners expect better business conditions over the next six month, the lowest level recorded in the 48-year survey.2022-07-12T10:12:11.46
