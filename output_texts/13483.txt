Title: Wall Street Opens Lower
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens LowerUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened lower on Friday after worse than expected data for Chicago PMI, personal spending, PCE price index and disappointing earnings reports from Chevron weighted on investors’ sentiment. In contrast, earnings from Amazon and LinkedIn came better than expected and the energy sector gained after oil prices hit fresh 2016 highs. The Dow Jones lost 0.7% or 123 points, the S&P 500 slid 0.65% or 13 points and the Nasdaq fell 0.6% or 30 points around 35 minutes after the opening bell.2016-04-29T15:21:41.313
