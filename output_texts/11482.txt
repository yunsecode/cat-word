Title: US Services PMI Revised Down: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services PMI Revised Down: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services PMI came in at 55.3 in October of 2017, the same as in September but below an initial estimate of 55.9. Yet, the reading pointed to a slightly faster expansion of the services sector than the long-run average. New business eased to a six-month low in October while employment levels expanded at a solid rate; backlogs also rose and input inflation eased to the lowest in seven months. In addition, business expectations remained robust.
