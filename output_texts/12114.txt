Title: US Stocks Close Mixed As Nasdaq Hits New High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed As Nasdaq Hits New HighUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Tuesday, as tech stocks, led by Apple breaking the $800 billion barrier, continue to drive the Nasdaq to new record highs, while investors begin to weigh the odds of a Fed tightening next month. The Dow Jones closed 37 points lower, or 0.2 percent, to 20976. The S&P edged down 2 points, or 0.1 percent, to 2397. Contrastingly, the Nasdaq climbed 18 points, or 0.3 percent, to a new high of 6121. 2017-05-09T20:06:14
