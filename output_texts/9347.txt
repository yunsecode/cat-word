Title: US Factory Activity Growth at 2-1/2-Year Low: ISM
Tag: Business Confidence

US Factory Activity Growth at 2-1/2-Year Low: ISMUnited States Business ConfidenceThe ISM Manufacturing PMI in the US fell to 52.8 in April 2019 from 55.3 in March, below market expectations of 55. The latest reading pointed to the weakest growth in factory activity since October 2016, amid slower increases in new orders, production and employment.2019-05-01T14:01:00
