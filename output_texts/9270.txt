Title: US Stocks Rebound on Huawei Relief
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound on Huawei ReliefUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks traded higher on Tuesday, led by a tech rally after the Commerce Department said it would temporarily ease restrictions on Huawei, allowing US companies to continue to do business with Chinese telecom giant until at least August 19th. the Dow Jones rose 143 points, or 0.6%, to 25,823; the S&P 500 gained 20 points, or 0.7%, to 2,861; and the Nasdaq climbed 72 points, or 0.9%, to 7,774 around 10:30 AM NY time.2019-05-21T14:35:01.957
