Title: US Goods Deficit Narrows in December
Tag: Goods Trade Balance

US Goods Deficit Narrows in DecemberUnited States Goods Trade BalanceThe goods gap in the US narrowed to USD 82.5 billion in December of 2020 from a revised USD 85.5 billion in the previous month, the advance estimate showed. Exports increased 4.6 percent, mainly boosted by sales of industrial supplies (4.0 percent), capital goods (3.2 percent) and consumer goods (2.5 percent). Imports rose at a slower 1.4 percent, due to purchases of capital goods (1.2 percent), industrial supplies (6.6 percent) and vehicles (6.2 percent). 2021-01-28T13:33:00
