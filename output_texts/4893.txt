US Government Budget Deficit Narrows More than ExpectedUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US budget deficit narrowed to USD 165 billion in October 2021, compared with a USD 284 billion gap in the same period last year and market expectations of a USD 179 billion gap. Outlays dropped 13.9 percent to USD 448.9 billion, while receipts soared 19.4 percent to USD 283.9 billion
