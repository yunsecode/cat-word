Title: US Initial Jobless Claims to Fall for 3rd Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Initial Jobless Claims to Fall for 3rd WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsInitial jobless claims likely dropped last week to 375 thousand, a third straight period of decline to levels not seen since a pandemic low of 368 thousand was reached at the end of June, adding to signs of a solid recovery in the US labor market despite the lingering threat of the Delta variant. The total number of claimants is likely to decline further in the coming weeks as more states roll off federal enhanced unemployment benefits ahead of their official September expiration date at the national level. Continuing claims likely fell to a fresh pandemic-era low of 2.88 million.2021-08-12T10:03:00
