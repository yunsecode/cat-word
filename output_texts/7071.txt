Dollar Struggles to Maintain MomentumUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rallied to weekly tops at 91 on Monday as investors turned to safe-haven assets amid a resurgence of the new more infectious COVID-19. However, this flight to safety failed to provide support, and the US currency bottomed around 90.
