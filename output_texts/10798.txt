Title: US Wholesale Inventories Rise Less than Forecasts
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Rise Less than ForecastsUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the United States increased 0.5 percent month-over-month to USD 628.8 billion in March of 2018, following a 1 percent gain in February and slightly below market expectations of 0.6 percent, preliminary estimates showed. Stocks of nondurables declined 0.1 percent after a 0.8 percent rise and those of durables rose at a slower 0.8 percent after 1.1 percent in February. Year-on-year, wholesale stocks went up 5.8 percent. 2018-04-26T12:45:14.86
