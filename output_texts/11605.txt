Title: US Services PMI Revised Slightly Higher: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services PMI Revised Slightly Higher: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services PMI was revised slightly higher to 55.3 in September of 2017 from a preliminary reading of 55.1 and compared to 56 in August. New business growth eased from August’s 25-month high but remained robust amid new product development and high client demand. Job creation eased to a three-month low and price pressures intensified as the hurricanes pushed cost of energy, fuel and raw materials up. Business optimism was the lowest in seven months with positive sentiment linked to new business growth although uncertainty surrounding future economic conditions weighed. 2017-10-04T13:57:14.39
