Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States declined 2.6 percent in the week ended May 15th 2020, after rising 0.3 percent in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications dropped 6.3 percent while applications to purchase a home went up 6.4 percent. Meantime, the average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell 2bps to 3.41 percent.2020-05-20T11:04:00
