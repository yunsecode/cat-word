Title: US Durable Goods Orders Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods went up 1.7 percent month-over-month in February of 2017, following an upwardly revised 2.3 percent jump in January and beating market expectations of a 1.2 percent rise. Civilian aircraft orders surged 47.6 percent and were the main driver of the increase. Non-defense capital goods orders excluding aircraft, a closely proxy for business spending plans, fell 0.1 percent after edging up 0.1 percent in the previous month.2017-03-24T12:33:18
