Title: US Exports Rise for 1st Time in 4 Months
Tag: Exports

US Exports Rise for 1st Time in 4 MonthsUnited States ExportsExports from the United States rose USD 13.6 billion from the previous month to USD 158.3 billion in June of 2020. It was the first gain in exports since February, suggesting an improvement in global demand after being depressed by the COVID-19 pandemic. Exports of goods increased USD 13.0 billion, led by automotive vehicles, parts, and engines (up USD 4.9 billion), capital goods (up USD 3.8 billion) and Industrial supplies and materials  (up USD 2.8 billion). Also, exports of services increased USD 0.6 billion.2020-08-05T12:35:00
