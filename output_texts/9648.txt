Title: US Job Openings Hit Record High
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Hit Record HighUnited States Job OffersThe number of job openings in the US reached an all-time high of 7.335 million in December 2018 from an upwardly revised 7.166 million in the previous month and above market expectations of 6.9 million. The number of job openings rose for total private (+198,000) and was little changed for government. Across industries, the largest increases in the job openings level were recorded in construction (+88,000), accommodation & food services (+84,000), and health care & social assistance (+79,000). Meanwhile, job openings declined in non-durable goods manufacturing (-37,000), federal government (-32,000), and real estate & rental and leasing (-31,000).2019-02-12T15:08:00
