Title: US Stocks Give Up Gains
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Give Up GainsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street's main indexes pared some gains in a volatile session on Friday, and were on track for their worst week in nearly three months, as investors continued to digest the probability of a second wave of coronavirus infections and a downbeat economic outlook for the economy delivered by the Fed. Some states including Texas, California and Florida reported a resurgence in new cases and Houston-area officials are “getting close” to reimpose stay-at-home orders.2020-06-12T17:55:00
