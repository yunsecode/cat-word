Title: US Initial Jobless Claims Lower than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Initial Jobless Claims Lower than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits fell by 1 thousand to 222 thousand in the week ending June 2 from the previous week's revised level of 223 thousand and below market expectations of 225 thousand. According to unadjusted data, the biggest declines in claims were recorded in Michigan (-3,774); New Jersey (-1,747), California (-1,448) and New York (-1,332).2018-06-07T12:32:00
