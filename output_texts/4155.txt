Title: Dollar Eases from 20-Month High
Tag: Currency

Dollar Eases from 20-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index retreated below 97 on Friday after hitting a 20-month high of 97.7 in the previous session, as safe-haven demand for the currency eased while traders took stock of geopolitical developments. The dollar index jumped 1.6% to its highest level since June 2020 on Thursday after Russia launched an attack on Ukraine, with president Putin warning other countries against interfering. However, the greenback pared some of its gains after US president Joe Biden unveiled new sanctions on Russia, but made it clear that Western powers were not willing to sacrifice their own economies to penalize Moscow for invading Ukraine. Meanwhile, the dollar remained on solid footing on inflation concerns and ensuing rate hikes from the Federal Reserve. Fed governor Michelle Bowman said Monday that she will assess incoming data in deciding whether a half percentage point rate increase at the March meeting is needed.2022-02-25T06:22:36.597
