Title: Dollar Continues to Appreciate

Dollar Continues to AppreciateUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar extended gains for the 3rd session on Friday amid a rebound on Wall Street and in European major stock markets. Investors also welcomed Nancy Pelosi announcement that US lawmakers and the administration were close to a deal on economic support. The DXY topped an intraday high of 98.297. On Thursday, the greenback rose as much as 2% to the highest level since the end of February as investors rush for liquidity as stock markets plunged the most since 1987.2020-03-13T14:19:00
