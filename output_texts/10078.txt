Title: US Services Sector Gains Steam in October: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Sector Gains Steam in October: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services PMI increased to 54.7 in October of 2018 from 53.5 in September, beating market expectations of 54.0, preliminary figures showed. New orders grew faster and output rebounded from September's eight-month low affected in part by adverse weather. Meanwhile, job creation increased at the slowest pace since June 2017. Also, backlogs of work continued to accumulate. In terms of prices, input cost inflation accelerated to its sharpest level since September 2013, amid reports of the pass through of tariffs, alongside rising fuel bills and higher borrowing costs.
