Title: US Jobless Rate Unchanged at 4.9% in August
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Unchanged at 4.9% in AugustUnited States Unemployment RateUS unemployment rate was recorded at 4.9 percent in August 2016, unchanged from the previous two months while missing market expectations of 4.8 percent. The number of unemployed persons was flat at 7.8 million and the labor force participation rate stood at 62.8 percent.2016-09-02T13:32:23
