Title: Dollar Starts Off 2022 on Positive Note
Tag: Currency

Dollar Starts Off 2022 on Positive NoteUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rose toward 96 on Monday, recouping some losses after declining for two straight weeks, as traders anticipate a faster pace of monetary policy tightening. The Federal Reserve is expected to begin raising interest rates as early as March amid a robust US economic rebound and surging inflation, with the Fed-preferred PCE index accelerating at its fastest pace since 1982 at 5.7% in November. The Fed announced that it would end its pandemic-era asset purchases in March and signaled three quarter point interest rate hikes this year. The dollar gained nearly 7% in 2021, its best annual performance since 2015.2022-01-03T07:31:42.32
