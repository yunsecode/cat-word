Title: US Stock Futures Inch Higher Ahead of GDP Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Inch Higher Ahead of GDP DataUnited States Stock MarketUS equity futures rose on Friday morning, led by Alphabet and Intel shares after both companies reported stronger-than-expected earnings results, while Amazon stocks fell after its results came in below forecasts. Investors await the advance estimate of second-quarter GDP growth later in the day and look ahead to Federal Reserve's monetary policy meeting next week. The Dow Jones advanced 0.2%, the S&P 500 gained 0.4% and the Nasdaq 100 rose 0.9% around 7AM EDT.2019-07-26T10:50:00
