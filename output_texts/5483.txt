Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Rises
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield RisesUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note rose above 1.35% during the second week of August, its highest level since mid-July and recovering from an over five-month low of 1.127% hit last week. Strong labor market data from the US released on Friday raised expectations of an early reduction of the US Federal Reserve's pandemic-era stimulus. On Monday, Federal Reserve officials Raphael Bostic and Eric Rosengren said the start of a bond-purchase taper should happen in the fourth quarter of the year. Investors will now be waiting for the US consumer price report on Wednesday.2021-08-09T07:43:00
