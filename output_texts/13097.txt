Title: US Stocks Fall Despite Upbeat Claims, Durable Goods
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall Despite Upbeat Claims, Durable Goods United States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Thursday despite better-than-expected data for jobless claims and durable goods orders as investors remain cautious ahead of Yellen's speech tomorrow where further clues on the timing of Fed's next rate-hike are expected. The Dow Jones was down 23 points or 0.1%, the S&P 500 fell 3 points or 0.1% and the Nasdaq traded 4 points or 0.1% lower around 12 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-08-25T14:44:30
