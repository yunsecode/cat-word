Title: US Stocks Rally Set to Pause
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally Set to PauseUnited States Stock MarketUS futures traded in the red on Thursday, following a 1.5% rally on Wednesday, as investors try to assess whether the US economy will be able to avoid a recession next year. Contracts on the three benchmark index futures were down nearly 0.2%. On the corporate front, shares of Micron Technology fell nearly 3% in premarket trading after the company forecasted a bigger-than-expected loss for the current period. Traders now will keep an eye on the claims report for an update on the labour market. The final estimate for Q3 GDP growth is also due. In regular trading on Wednesday, the three main indexes booked their best day so far in December, prompted by upbeat quarterly results from Nike and FedEx and a rise in consumer sentiment.2022-12-22T11:58:00
