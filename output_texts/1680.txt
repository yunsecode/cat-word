Title: US Crude Stocks Fall the Most since 2019
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Fall the Most since 2019United States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 12.58 million barrels in the week ended November 25th, the most since June of 2019 and compared with market expectations of a much smaller 2.758-million-barrel decrease, the latest US Energy Information Administration report showed. Crude stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, went down by 415 thousand barrels, following an 887 thousand draw. On the other hand, gasoline stocks rose by 2.769 million, more than market expectations of a 1.625 million build, and distillate stockpiles, including diesel and heating oil, went up by 3.547 million, versus forecasts for a 1.457 million increase.  2022-11-30T15:37:00
