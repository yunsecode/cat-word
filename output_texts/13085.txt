Title: US Stocks Rise After Income and Spending Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise After Income and Spending DataUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Monday despite earlier losses in most European and Asian shares and a rising dollar, after data for consumer spending and income came in line with expectations. The Dow Jones gained 79 points or 0.4%, the S&P 500 went up 8 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq traded 12 points or 0.2% higher around 10 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-08-29T14:45:47
