Title: US Treasury Yields Rise on Hopes Global Economy will Reopen Soon
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Rise on Hopes Global Economy will Reopen SoonUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note increased to 0.627% on Monday, the highest in 6 sessions as investors' risk appetite improved amid hopes the spread of the coronavirus is slowing down and the global economy could reopen soon. Meanwhile, the yield on the 30-year Treasury bond also went up to 1.202%.2020-04-27T12:49:00
