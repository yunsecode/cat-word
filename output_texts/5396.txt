Title: US 10Y Treasury Yields at 2-Week High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Treasury Yields at 2-Week HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note remained at 2-week highs of 1.35% on Thursday, as investors await a speech from Fed Chair at the Jackson Hole symposium tomorrow for more details about Fed's plans for stimulus. Investors are getting more confident the Federal Reserve will delay taper plans amid weak economic data and worries over the spread of the coronavirus delta variant. 2021-08-26T08:18:00
