US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 2nd Week: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States dropped for the second straight time by 0.55 million barrels in the week ended October 21st, 2016, following a 5.20 million decline the previous week and compared to market expectations of 1.0 million rise.
