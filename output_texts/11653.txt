Title: Fed Leaves Rates Steady
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Leaves Rates SteadyUnited States Interest RateThe Federal Reserve left the target range for its federal funds rate unchanged at 1 percent to 1.25 percent during its September 2017 meeting as widely expected. Policymakers kept forecasts for another rate hike this year and expect a higher GDP growth while core PCE inflation is seen lower. In addition, the central bank announced it will begin reducing its $4.5 trillion balance sheet in October. 2017-09-20T18:01:25
