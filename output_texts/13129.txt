Title: US Housing Starts Rise to 5-Month High in July
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Rise to 5-Month High in JulyUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the Unites States rose 2.1 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1211 thousand in July 2016 compared to a downwardly revised 1186 thousand in the previous month. The figure came in above market expectations of 1180 thousand to hit the highest level since February, as building activity increased across the board. Housing starts rose the most in the Northeast.2016-08-16T13:36:53
