Title: US Wholesale Inventories Rise at Faster Pace in January
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Rise at Faster Pace in JanuaryUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US increased 1.3 percent from the previous month in January 2021, following following an upwardly revised 0.5 percent growth in December, a preliminary estimate showed. Nondurable goods stocks rose 1.4 percent (vs 0.8 percent in December) and durable goods inventories were up 1.3 percent (vs 0.3 percent in December). On a yearly basis, wholesale inventories advanced 0.5 percent in January.2021-02-26T13:34:00
