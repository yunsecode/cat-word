Title: Wall Street Opens Slightly Higher
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Slightly HigherUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US traded mainly in the green at the opening on Friday, mostly driven by the tech sector after data showed retail sales recorded its biggest jump in 1 year. The Dow Jones traded near the flat line at 17727 points, the S&P 500 added 0.1% or 3 points and the Nasdaq rose 0.3% or 15 points around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2016-05-13T15:05:01.207
