Title: Dollar Lacks Direction after Jobs Report
Tag: Currency

Dollar Lacks Direction after Jobs ReportUnited States CurrencyThe DXY swung between small gains and a flat reading on Friday, as investors digest the January jobs report. Nonfarm payrolls increased by 225K, better than forecasts and figures for both November and December were revised up, showing a solid hiring. On the other hand, the unemployment rate rose to 3.6% and monthly earnings went up by a smaller-than-expected 0.2%.2020-02-07T14:17:00
