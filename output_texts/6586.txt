Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Little Changed
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Little ChangedUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury note was little changed at 1.45% on Tuesday, remaining much lower than an over 1-year high of 1.61% touched in the previous week as investors appear to acknowledge that central banks around the world will not tight monetary policy anytime soon. Treasuries have been rallying since the end of January when the Democrats won control of the US Senate, amid inflationary concerns arising from strong economic activity and further fiscal support. Fed Chair Powell recently reinforced the US economy needs support and the Fed is committed to the current policy stance as the economic recovery remains uneven while inflation is seen rising above 2% for some time.2021-03-02T08:08:00
