Title: US Initial Jobless Claims Set to Fall to 730K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Initial Jobless Claims Set to Fall to 730KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsInitial jobless claims are seen dropping to 730 thousand in the week ended March 20th from last week's one-month high of 770 thousand, but remaining well above pre-pandemic levels. Still, claims are expected to fall in the coming weeks following the approval of President Biden's relief package in early March and as several states ease coronavirus-induced restrictions amid the rapid pace of vaccinations. Continuing claims are seen declining to 4.04 million in the March 13th week from 4.12 million the week before, remaining well above February 2020 levels.2021-03-25T10:18:00
