Title: US Futures Lower on Martin Luther King Holiday
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Lower on Martin Luther King HolidayUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were trading lower on Monday amid rising coronavirus cases and restrictions and doubts over whether Congress will pass Joe Biden’s $1.9 trillion coronavirus relief plan. Meanwhile, US President-elect Joe Biden is due to be inaugurated on Wednesday amid risks of violence and security concerns. Coronavirus deaths in the US are approaching 400K and the number is expected to reach 500K next month, according to incoming CDC director. US financial markets are closed Monday for the Martin Luther King holiday.2021-01-18T09:59:00
