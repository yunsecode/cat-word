Title: US Services Sector Growth Highest Since 2005: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Sector Growth Highest Since 2005: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States jumped to 59.9 in January of 2018 from an upwardly revised 56 in December, beating market forecasts of 56.5. The reading pointed to the strongest expansion in the services sector since August of 2005 amid rising production, new orders and employment which was the strongest on record.2018-02-05T15:06:36
