Title: US Jobless Claims Down to 7-Year Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Down to 7-Year LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending May 10, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 297,000, a decrease of 24,000 from the previous week's revised level. This is the lowest level for initial claims since May 12, 2007 when they were 297,000. There were no special factors impacting this week's initial claims.
