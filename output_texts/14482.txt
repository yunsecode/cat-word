Title: US Economy Contracts 1% in Q1
Tag: Gdp Growth Rate

US Economy Contracts 1% in Q1United States Gdp Growth RateUnited States GDP shrank at an annual rate of 1.0 percent in the first quarter of 2014, according to the second estimate released by the Commerce Department. It is the worst performance in three years and comes lower than a preliminary estimated 0.1 percent expansion, due to a sharper fall in private inventory investment and a bigger trade deficit. 2014-05-30T15:25:26.517
