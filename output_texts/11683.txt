Title: Optimism Among US Small Businesses at 6-Month High
Tag: NFIB Business Optimism Index

Optimism Among US Small Businesses at 6-Month HighUnited States NFIB Business Optimism IndexThe NFIB's Index of Small Business Optimism in the US edged up to 105.3 in August of 2017 from 105.2 in July, above market expectations of 105. It is the highest reading since February as 4 of 10 index components improved: plans to make capital outlays (4 pp to net 32%), the highest since 2006; higher sales expectations (5 to 27); expected credit conditions (1 to -3) and good time to expand (4 to 27). On the other hand, expectations the economy will improve were flat (at 37) and deterioration was observed for: job creation (+1 to 18, still remain historically high), plans to raise employment (-1 to 18); plans to increase inventories (-3 to 2); current inventory (-3 to 5) and current job openings (-4 to 31). 2017-09-12T10:19:40
