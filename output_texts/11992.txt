Title: Dow Jones Hits Record High At Open
Tag: stock market

Dow Jones Hits Record High At OpenUnited States stock marketWall Street opened mainly flat on Wednesday after data for inflation and retail sales disappointed while investors await the Fed monetary policy decision later in the day. The Dow Jones increased 6 points or less than 0.1% to a new high of 21334.9 around 9 minutes after the opening bell. Meanwhile, the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq traded around the flatline at 2439.9 and 6214.9 respectively.2017-06-14T13:29:08
