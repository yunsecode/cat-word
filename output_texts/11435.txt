Title: US Stocks Surge to New Records on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge to New Records on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Tuesday November 21st of 2017, as investors showed optimism about recent company earnings, progress in NAFTA negotiations, and global economic growth. All three major indices closed at record highs. The Dow Jones gained 161 points or 0.7% to 23591. The S&P 500 climbed 17 points or 0.6% to 2599.The Nasdaq surged 72 points or 1.1% to 6862.2017-11-21T21:08:57
