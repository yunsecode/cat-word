Title: Dollar Rises To Fresh 14-Year High After Strong PMI Data
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rises To Fresh 14-Year High After Strong PMI DataUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar extended gains on Tuesday and hit its strongest level since December 2002, as better-than-expected manufacturing data for December lifted sentiment. The greenback rose by 0.5% against the yen to ¥118.02, by 0.7% against the euro to €1.038 and by 0.3% against the pound to £1.224 around 11:00 AM New York time.2017-01-03T16:02:58
