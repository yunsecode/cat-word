Title: US Retail Sales Rise 0.4% in December
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Rise 0.4% in DecemberUnited States Retail Sales MoMUS retail trade increased 0.4 percent month-over-month in December 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.9 percent rise in November and matching market expectations. The December gain was mainly supported by higher sales at gardening and building material stores and motor vehicle & parts dealers. Year-on, year, retail trade grew 5.4 percent, easing from a 6 percent increase in the previous month. Considering 2017 as a whole, sales advanced 4.2 percent compared to 3.2 percent in 2016.2018-01-12T13:33:07
