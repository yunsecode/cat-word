Title: US Inflation Rate Slows to 7-Month Low of 0.8%
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Slows to 7-Month Low of 0.8%United States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States rose 0.8 percent year-on-year in July of 2016, following a 1 percent increase in the previous two months and below market expectations of 0.9 percent. It is the lowest inflation figure since December last year as shelter cost rose at a slower pace, food inflation eased to a fresh 6-1/2-year low and energy cost fell further. 2016-08-16T13:33:59
