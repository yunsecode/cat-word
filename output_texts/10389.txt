Title: US Services Growth Lowest in a Year: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Growth Lowest in a Year: ISM United States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States fell to 55.7 in July of 2018 from 59.1 in June, well below market expectations of 58.6. It is the lowest reading since August of 2017. Although most firms remain positive about business conditions and the economy, tariffs and deliveries are an ongoing concern.2018-08-03T14:06:00
