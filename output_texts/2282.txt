Title: Wall Street Set to Extend Losses
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Set to Extend LossesUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures tracking the broader market were down over 0.5% on Friday, putting Wall Street on track to extend its decline as investors fretted over sky-high inflation and a gloomy global growth outlook. At the same time, prospects of an even more aggressive tightening policy further dented sentiment. FedEx plunged more than 20% in premarket trading after the logistics giant withdrew its full-year guidance while missing earnings estimates as the outlook for the global economy significantly worsened. In regular trading on Thursday, the Dow lost 0.6%, the S&P 500 dropped 1.1%, and the Nasdaq Composite tumbled 1.4%, with all three benchmarks on track to notch their fourth losing week in five.2022-09-16T11:35:00
