Title: US Treasury Yields Little Changed after Fed
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Little Changed after FedUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note was slightly lower at 1.3%, after Fed Chair Powell said during the press conference that tapering of bond purchases could begin as soon as November and that the process could be complete by mid-2022. Also, the central bank downgraded its GDP growth estimate for 2021 to 5.9% from 7% previously. 2021-09-22T18:20:00
