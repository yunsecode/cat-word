Title: Dow Cuts Gains, S&P and Nasdaq Fall
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Cuts Gains, S&P and Nasdaq FallUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones failed to hold opening gains to trade more than 100 points lower on Monday while both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq fell deeper into negative territory, down by 0.5% and 1% respectively. Investors were cautious ahead of the FOMC minutes release on Wednesday which could provide further clues on the pace of the Fed's rate hikes. Some Fed officials are also due to speak this week. Meanwhile, concerns of further curbs in China after the country reported covid deaths for the first time in six months during the weekend, also weighed on investors' risk appetite. On a positive note, Disney shares were up nearly 7% after the company announced that Bob Iger will return as CEO. The US bond market will be closed on Thursday for the Thanksgiving holiday and will close early on Friday.2022-11-21T15:12:00
