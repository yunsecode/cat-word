US Inflation Rises 1.5% in DecemberUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.5 percent in December on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on January 14.
