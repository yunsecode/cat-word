Title: US Stocks Set to End 3-Day Winning Streak
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Set to End 3-Day Winning StreakUnited States Stock MarketUS futures traded lower on Thursday, with contracts on the Dow Jones falling more than 100 points and both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq losing almost 0.5%. Stocks are set to end a 3-day winning streak, as the omicron rally paused and traders try to assess what will be the impact of the new omicron coronavirus variant on the economic recovery. Travel stocks were among the worst performers in pre-market trading. Traders also turn their attention to US inflation data due tomorrow which will likely show inflationary pressures persist, strengthening the case for the Fed to double the pace of taper next week.2021-12-09T11:58:00
