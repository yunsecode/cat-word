Title: US Services Activity Growth Eases to 11-Month Low
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Activity Growth Eases to 11-Month LowUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services PMI fell to 53.4 in December 2018 from 54.7 in the previous month and well below market expectations of 54.7, a preliminary estimate showed. The latest reading pointed to the weakest pace of expansion in the service sector since January as new work rose the least since April 2017 and unfinished business fell for the first time in four months. On the price front, average cost burdens increased at the slowest rate for one year.2018-12-14T14:49:18.747
