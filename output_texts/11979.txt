Title: US Stocks Fall As Tech Shares Tumble
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall As Tech Shares TumbleUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded in the red on Thursday amid a renewed tech selloff, with Alphabet, Facebook and Netflix losing more than 2% and Apple falling 1.4%. The Dow Jones was down 81 points or 0.4% to 21293; the S&P 500 declined 16 points or 0.7% to 2422 and the Nasdaq slumped 72 points or 1.2% to 6122 around 10:15 AM NY time.
