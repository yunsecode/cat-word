Title: US Wholesale Inventories Revised Higher
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Revised HigherUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US increased 1.3% mom in May of 2021, higher than initial estimates of a 1.1% rise and compared with an upwardly revised 1.1% gain in April. Inventories increased for durables (1.2% vs 0.7% in April), namely lumber (7.6% vs 1.5%), metals (2.4% vs 2.3%) and furniture (2.4% vs 2.5%). Inventories were also higher for nondurables (1.5% vs 1.7%), namely drugs (2.3% vs -0.2%). 2021-07-09T14:09:09.11
