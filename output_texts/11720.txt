Title: US Personal Spending Up 0.3% in July
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Up 0.3% in JulyUnited States Personal SpendingPersonal spending in US rose 0.3 percent month-on-month in July, below expectations of 0.4 percent gain but above upwardly revised 0.2 percent rise in June. The $29.3 billion increase in real PCE reflected an increase of $18.7 billion in spending for goods and an $11.8 billion increase in spending for services. Within goods, furnishings and durable household equipment was the leading contributor to the increase. Within services, the largest contributor to the increase was spending for food services and accommodations.2017-08-31T12:43:40
