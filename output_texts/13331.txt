Title: Wall Street Opens Lower As Tech Weighs
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Lower As Tech WeighsUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened in the red on Friday as gains in energy sector boosted by a recovery in oil prices were not enough to offset a selloff in technology sector after the news that Apple could be ordered to stop selling iPhone 6 in Beijing. The Dow Jones fell 0.24% or 42 points, the S&P 500 lost 0.27% or 5 points and the Nasdaq decreased 0.6% or 28 points around 25 minutes after the opening bell.2016-06-17T15:14:54
