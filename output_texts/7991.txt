Dollar at Near 1-Month LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was down to 95.9 on Wednesday, the lowest on a closing basis since June 10th as investors' risk appetite improved after Moderna said that its coronavirus vaccine produced antibodies in all patients in an early trial.
