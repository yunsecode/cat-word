Title: US Services Sector Growth at 6-Month Low: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Sector Growth at 6-Month Low: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe Markit Flash US Services PMI came in at 50.9 in August of 2016, down from 51.4 in the previous month and below market expectations of 52. It is the lowest reading since February with business activity, new orders and employment all slowing due to subdued demand conditions and uncertainty ahead of the presidential election. 2016-08-25T14:54:13.34
