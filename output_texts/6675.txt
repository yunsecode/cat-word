Title: US Mortgage Applications Drop for 2nd Week in a Row
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Drop for 2nd Week in a RowUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 5.1 percent in the week ended February 12th, a second consecutive week of decline, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to refinance a home loan dropped 4.7 percent, but were up 51 percent year-on-year; while those to purchase a home were down 6.1 percent, but were 15 percent higher than the previous year. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate increased 2 basis point to 2.98 percent last week, but was down 79 basis points compared with a year ago.2021-02-17T12:09:00
