Title: US Stocks Fall After Cohn Resignation
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall After Cohn ResignationUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones dropped 162 points, or 0.6%, to 24,724 right after the market opened on Wednesday, after Trump's economic adviser Gary Cohn resigned from the White House following the President's decision to impose steel and aluminum tariffs. Also, the S&P 500 retreated 13 points, or 0.5%, to 2,715; and the Nasdaq fell 23 points, or 0.3%, to 7,348.2018-03-07T14:45:06.817
