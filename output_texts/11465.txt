Title: US Consumer Sentiment Misses Forecasts
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Misses ForecastsUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US fell to 97.8 in November from 100.7 in October which was the strongest since January 2004. The reading came below expectations of 100.7, as current and expected economic conditions deteriorated and consumers expect the inflation to rise in the next year. 2017-11-10T15:08:23
