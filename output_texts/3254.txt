Title: US Crude Inventories Fall More than Expected: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Fall More than Expected: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 1.019 million barrels in the week ended May 20th, following a 3.394-million-barrel decline in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 0.737 million decrease. Also, crude stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, delivery hub dropped by 1.061 million barrels, after a 2.403-million-barrel fall. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks shrank by 0.482 million barrels, less than forecasts of a 0.634-million-barrel drop; while inventories of distillate fuels, which include diesel and heating oil, went up 1.657 million barrels.2022-05-25T14:56:39.513
