Title: US Export Prices Rise Faster than Expected
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise Faster than ExpectedUnited States Import Prices MoMExport prices in the US rose 0.6 percent month-over-month in February of 2019, following a downwardly revised 0.5 percent drop in January and beating market expectations of a 0.1 percent gain. Higher prices for both agricultural and nonagricultural commodities contributed to the February increase. Export prices for agricultural commodities rose 0.3 percent, after falling 2.1 percent in January. Nonagricultural export prices increased 0.7 percent, the largest monthly rise since April 2018. Higher prices for nonagricultural industrial supplies and materials led the increase, with rising cost for each of the finished goods categories contributing to the advance. Year-on-year, cost of exports went up 0.3 percent, after a 0.2 percent fall in January.2019-03-14T12:34:00
