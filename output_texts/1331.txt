Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Higher
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised HigherUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan consumer sentiment for the US was revised higher to 64.9 in January of 2023, the highest since April, from a preliminary of 64.6. The gauge for expectations was revised higher to 62.7 from 62 while the current conditions subindex was revised lower to 68.4 from 68.6. Meanwhile, inflation expectations for the year were revised lower to 3.9% from 4% in the preliminary estimate and the 5-year outlook was revised lower to 2.9% from 3%.2023-01-27T15:04:09.397
