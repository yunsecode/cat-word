Title: US Crude Stocks Fall More than Expected: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Fall More than Expected: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 7.056 million barrels in the week ended August 12th, the most since early April, while markets were expecting a smaller 0.275-million-barrel decrease. On top of that, gasoline stocks fell by 4.642 million barrels, compared to expectations of a 1.096 million drop. Meanwhile, crude inventories at Cushing, Oklahoma, increased by 0.192 million barrels, following a 0.723-million-barrel injection in the previous period. Distillate stockpiles, which include diesel and heating oil, climbed by 0.766million barrels against forecasts for a 0.440 million increase. 2022-08-17T14:37:00
