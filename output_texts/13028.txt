Title: US Stocks Inch Higher on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Inch Higher on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened slightly higher on Wednesday, partially recovering from the previous day’s slump amid a continued fall in oil prices while investors await EIA data on US crude inventories. The Dow Jones was up 30 points or 0.2%, the S&P 500 gained 5 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq traded 21 points or 0.4% higher around 5 minutes after the open. 2016-09-14T13:44:04.177
