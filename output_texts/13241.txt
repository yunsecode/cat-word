Title: US Stocks Open Flat

US Stocks Open FlatUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened flat on Monday amid lower oil prices, as investors weighed the turmoil in Turkey while better-than-expected earnings' results for Bank of America boosted sentiment. The Dow Jones retreated 0.08% or 15.6 points while the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq traded near the flat line around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2016-07-18T14:46:16
