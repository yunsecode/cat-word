Title: Wall Street Ends Higher
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Ends HigherUnited States Stock MarketAll three main US stocks ended Thursday’s session higher, erasing steep declines earlier in the session, as investors were assessing the Russian invasion of Ukraine and took advantage of lower valuations. US President Joe Biden announced additional sanctions on Russian banks and said the US would release additional barrels of oil as conditions warrant but felt short of barring Russia from the Swift network. Meanwhile, better than expected weekly jobless claims also tempered the gloomy mood across markets, while economic growth to end 2021 was slightly better than initially reported. The Dow Jones gained 90 points, after falling to levels not seen since March last year; the S&P 500 climbed 1.5%; and the Nasdaq rose more than 3% as investors moved back to the safety trade of big tech after a rough open.2022-02-24T21:03:00
