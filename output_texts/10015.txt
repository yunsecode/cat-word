Title: US Stocks Trade Mixed Ahead of Midterm Elections
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Mixed Ahead of Midterm ElectionsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded mixed on Monday, ahead of the US midterm elections. Financials were on the rise, boosted by better-than-expected earnings for Berkshire Hathaway while tech shares fell, mainly dragged down by Apple. The Dow Jones was up 0.6% and the S&P 500 added 0.3% while the Nasdaq traded 0.9% lower around 12:05 PM NY time.2018-11-05T17:11:00
