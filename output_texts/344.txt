Mortgage Applications Down for 2nd Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the US declined 4.6% in the week ended May 19th 2023, following a 5.7% drop in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association data showed. Applications to refinance a home loan sank 5.4% and those to buy a home fell 4.3%. Meanwhile, the average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($726,200 or less) jumped by 12bps to 6.69%, the highest level since early March. “Since rates have been so volatile and for-sale inventory still scarce, we have yet to see sustained growth in purchase applications,” said Joel Kan, MBA’s vice president.2023-05-24T11:05:00
