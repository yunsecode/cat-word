Title: US Jobless Claims Down to 331K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Down to 331K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending August 24, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 331,000, a decrease of 6,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 337,000. The 4-week moving average was 331,250, an increase of 750 from the previous week's unrevised average of 330,500.2013-08-29T13:40:04.433
