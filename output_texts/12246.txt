US Manufacturing PMI Revised Down In March: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe final Markit US Manufacturing PMI fell to 53.3 in March of 2017 from a preliminary reading of 53.4 and a final of 54.2 in February. It is the lowest reading in six months as output, new orders and payrolls rose at a slower pace. 2017-04-03T13:46:32.847
