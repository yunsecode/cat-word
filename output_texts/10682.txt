Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher on Wednesday United States Stock MarketWall Street closed mostly higher on Wednesday 23 May 2018 amid trade and geopolitical concerns after President Trump said he's not satisfied with China trade talks and his summit with North Korea could be cancelled or delayed if certain conditions are not met. Also, Fed minutes showed that the Fed will let inflation run above the 2 percent goal for a ‘temporary period’. The Dow Jones edged up 52 points or 0.2% to 24887. The S&P 500 climbed 9 points or 0.3% to 2733. The Nasdaq jumped 47 points or 0.6% to 7426. 2018-05-23T20:01:01
