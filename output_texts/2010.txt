Title: Mortgage Applications Fall as Rates Hit 2001-Highs
Tag: Mortgage Applications

Mortgage Applications Fall as Rates Hit 2001-HighsUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the US fell 1.7% in the week ending October 21st, it's fifth consecutive decline, as borrowing costs continue to rise, with the 30-year mortgage rate jumping 22bps to 7.16%, the highest reading since 2001, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association (MBA) showed. Mortgage application activity is now at its slowest pace since 1997. The purchase index went down 2.3% and applications to refinance a home loan were unchanged.2022-10-26T11:07:00
