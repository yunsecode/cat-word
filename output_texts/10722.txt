Title: US Consumer Sentiment Unchanged in May
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Unchanged in MayUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US was steady at 98.8 in May of 2018, the same as in April and slightly above market expectations of 98.5. Preliminary figures pointed to a small uptick in near term inflation, a fall in income expectations, and stabilization of the unemployment rate at decade lows.
