Title: US Stocks Remain Volatile
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Remain VolatileUnited States Stock MarketWall Street lack direction on Friday, erasing most of the gains at the open in another volatile trading session as investors remain very concerned about the impact of the coronavirus in the US economy. The dollar was down from over 3-year high levels seen on Thursday amid liquidity and funding needs. Also, the stay at home order issued by California is seen as an important move to contain the spread of the disease although it raises concerns of a looming recession. The Dow was up 0.5%, the S&P 500 0.2% and the Nasdaq gained 1.6% around 10:20 AM NY time.2020-03-20T14:19:29.577
