Title: US Jobless Claims Decline From 5-Month High To 258K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Decline From 5-Month High To 258KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 10 thousand to 258 thousand in the week ended December 3rd from the previous week's unrevised level of 268 thousand and in line with market expectations. It was the 92nd straight week that claims were below 300 thousand, the longest stretch since 1970.2016-12-08T13:36:35.68
