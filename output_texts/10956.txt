Title: Stocks of Crude Oil in the US Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

Stocks of Crude Oil in the US Unexpectedly FallUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States declined by 2.622 million barrels in the week ended March 16th 2018, after a 5.022 million increase in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 2.6 million rise. It was the largest decline in crude oil stocks in nine weeks. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories fell 1.693 million after slumping by 6.271 million in the previous week which was the biggest decline since September. It compared to forecasts of a 2.008 million drop.2018-03-21T14:34:58.603
