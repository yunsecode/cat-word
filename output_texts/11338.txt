Title: US Stocks Soar on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Soar on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened deeply in the green on Monday with the three main stock indexes reaching intraday records amid rising expectations the Congress will pass the tax bill that will cut the corporate tax rate to 21% from 35%. The Dow Jones jumped 197 points or 0.8%; the S&P 500 went up 18 points or 0.7% and the Nasdaq gained 50 points or 0.7% around 12 minutes after the opening bell.2017-12-18T14:47:31.703
