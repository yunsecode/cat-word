Tag: stock market

US Stocks Hit All-Time High Ahead of Fed MeetingUnited States stock marketThe Dow Jones hit an all-time high of 22,318 a few minutes after the open on Monday as investors are preparing themselves for the start of the Fed's meeting this week.
