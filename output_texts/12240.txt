Title: ISM New York Index Rises In March
Tag: Ism New York Index

ISM New York Index Rises In MarchUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM NY Current Business Conditions Index increased to 56.5 in March of 2017 from 51.3 in February, mainly due to a rise in purchases (57.4 from 53.3), higher current (65.4 from 62.1) and expected (80 from 66.7) revenues and a drop in prices paid (48.1 from 51.8). In addition, employment rose to 47.7 from 43.2. The six-month outlook also went up to 75.6 from 64.4.
