Title: US Productivity Contracts 0.6% QoQ in Q1
Tag: Productivity

US Productivity Contracts 0.6% QoQ in Q1United States ProductivityNonfarm business sector labor productivity in the United States decreased at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 0.6 percent during the first quarter of 2017, following a upwardly revised 1.8 percent rise in the previous period and below market expectations of an 0.2 percent gain. Output per worker increased 1 percent and hours worked rose 1.6 percent.
