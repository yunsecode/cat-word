Title: US Overseas Capital Purchases Lose Steam
Tag: Capital Flows

US Overseas Capital Purchases Lose Steam United States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought USD 31 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments, in November 2018 after buying USD 46.8 billion in October. Meanwhile, foreigners bought USD 37.6 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying USD 31.9 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors sold USD 9.3 billion of US treasuries, after buying USD 1.7 billion in October.2019-01-31T21:21:00
