Title: DXY Holds Above 105
Tag: Currency

DXY Holds Above 105United States CurrencyThe dollar index held above the 105 mark on Friday, remaining close to its highest in a month, after the latest jobs report showed the labour market remains strong, keeping pressure on the Fed. The US economy added 223K jobs in December, beating market expectations of 200K. Still, weaker-than-expected wage growth eased some concerns about the pace of the Federal Reserve's interest rate rises. Meanwhile, minutes of the FOMC's December meeting showed that policymakers committed to pushing rates higher and holding them at a restrictive level until there were clear signs that inflation was easing. The dollar rallied against most major currencies while it continued to depreciate against emerging market currencies.2023-01-06T13:45:00
