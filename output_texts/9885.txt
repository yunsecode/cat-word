Title: US Stocks Tumble on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Tumble on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on Friday 7 December 2018 after the jobs report showed the US economy added a lower-than-expected 155 thousand jobs in November. Also, worries about China and US trade war continued to weigh on investors' mood and Brent crude prices rose 5 percent. The Dow Jones plunged 559 points or 2.2% to 24388. The S&P plummeted 63 points or 2.3% to 2633. The Nasdaq sank 219 points or 3.1% to 6969.2018-12-07T21:04:00
