Title: US Stocks Close Mixed Ahead Of Fed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed Ahead Of Fed United States Stock MarketUS stocks were little changed on Monday as investors remained cautious ahead of a widely expected interest rate hike from the Fed on Wednesday. The Dow Jones fell 22 points, or 0.1%, to 20,881; the S&P 500 gained 1 point, or less than 0.1%, to 2,373; and the Nasdaq added 14 points, or 0.2%, to finish at 5,876.2017-03-13T20:10:07.38
