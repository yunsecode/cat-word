Title: US Budget Deficit Widens to 3.2% of the GDP in FY 2016
Tag: Government Budget Value

US Budget Deficit Widens to 3.2% of the GDP in FY 2016United States Government Budget ValueThe US government posted a USD 33 billion budget surplus in September of 2016, a 63.7 percent fall from a USD 91 billion surplus in the same month a year earlier but above market expectations of a USD 25 billion surplus. For the 2016  fiscal year, the country's budget deficit widened to USD 587 billion or 3.2 percent of the GDP, the highest in three years.2016-10-14T20:13:53
