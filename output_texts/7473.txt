Title: Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Rises at Faster Pace
Tag: Kansas Fed Manufacturing Index

Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Rises at Faster PaceUnited States Kansas Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Kansas City Fed's Manufacturing Production Index rose by 5 points to +23 in October 2020, matching August's near two-year high and suggesting an increase in manufacturing activity, mainly boosted by output at fabricated metal and machinery plants. Still, regional factory activity remained below year-ago levels for over half of firms. At the same time, shipments, new orders, employment, and employee workweek indexes rose faster than in September, but order backlog and supplier delivery time increased at a slower pace. Looking ahead, expectations for future output continued to expand.2020-10-22T15:18:08.527
