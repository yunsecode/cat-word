Title: S&P, Nasdaq Hit Fresh Record
Tag: Stock Market

S&P, Nasdaq Hit Fresh RecordUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Monday to extend the previous two session’s gains and book fresh S&P and Nasdaq highs, as positive news on COVID-19 treatments and vaccines nudged sentiment higher. On Sunday, the US FDA issued an emergency use authorization of convalescent plasma for hospitalized coronavirus patients, which is estimated to cut mortality rates by 35%. The Trump administration is also considering fast-tracking an experimental vaccine developed in the UK for use in the United States. The Dow Jones gained 379 points or 1.4% to 28,309. The S&P 500 climbed 34 points or 1% to a new record 3431. The Nasdaq added 68 points or 0.6% to a fresh high of 11,380.
