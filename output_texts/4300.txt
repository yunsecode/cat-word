Title: Bond Sell-off Shows No Sign of Abating
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Bond Sell-off Shows No Sign of AbatingUnited States Government Bond 10YThe US government debt market started 2022 on the back foot, as investors began pricing in the possibility of runaway inflation and, in turn, the prospects of faster tightening. The US yield on the 10-year note, which sets the tone for corporate and household borrowing costs worldwide, jumped above 1.96%, the highest in two years, in the aftermath of January's robust jobs, with markets now betting on more than five quarter-point Federal Reserve interest-rate hikes in 2022. Still, the focus will be on the US inflation report on Thursday, which could provide clues further about the timing of future rate hikes. 	2022-02-09T07:52:00
