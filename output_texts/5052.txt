Title: US Crude Stocks Rise More than Forecast
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Rise More than ForecastUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 4.267 million barrels in the week ending October 22nd, following a 0.431 million decline in the previous period and compared with market forecasts of a 1.914 million increase, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed.
