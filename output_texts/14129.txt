Housing Starts Edge Up, Permits Fall SharplyUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the US went up 0.2 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,206,000 in July, the highest since October of 2007 and following an upwardly revised 1,204,000 in June. In contrast, building permits shrank 16.3 percent to an annual rate of 1,119,000.2015-08-18T13:51:42.063
