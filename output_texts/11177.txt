Title: Fed Leaves Rates Steady
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Leaves Rates Steady United States Interest RateThe Federal Reserve kept its target range for the federal funds rate unchanged at 1.25-1.5 percent on January 31st 2018 but signaled a rate hike in March is on the table. The decision came in line with market forecasts. Policymakers said inflation is seen rising this year and economic conditions are expected to evolve in a manner that will warrant further gradual increases in the federal funds rate.2018-01-31T19:00:56
