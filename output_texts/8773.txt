Title: US Stocks Recover From Coronavirus-Driven Selloff
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Recover From Coronavirus-Driven SelloffUnited States Stock MarketAll three major stock indexes in the US traded higher on Wednesday, attempting to recover from the lowest level seen so far this year, with investors assessing the impact of the coronavirus outbreak in the global economy. Investors took advantage of a sharp drop in the previous session to open new positions, but fears that the global economy will slow dramatically in the first quarter because of China’s new virus capped gains. The Dow Jones rose 1.6%; the Nasdaq went up 1.8%, and the S&P 500 added 1.5% around 11:30 AM NY time.2020-02-26T14:34:00
