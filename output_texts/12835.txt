Title: Fed Leaves Rates Steady
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Leaves Rates Steady United States Interest RateThe Federal Reserve left the target range for its federal funds rate unchanged at 0.25 percent to 0.5 percent for the seventh time during its November 2016 meeting, saying the labor market has continued to strengthen and growth of economic activity has picked up. Policymakers also added that the case for an increase in the federal funds rate has continued to strengthen. 2016-11-02T18:00:44
