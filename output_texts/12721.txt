Title: US Personal Income Growth Beats Expectations In October
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Growth Beats Expectations In October United States Personal IncomePersonal income in the United States increased by 0.6 percent month-over-month in October 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.4 percent growth in September and above market expectations of 0.4 percent gain. Compensation of employees went up 0.5 percent (from +0.4 percent in September), driven by a 0.5 percent rise in wages and salaries; personal income receipts on assets jumped 1.2 percent (from +0.2 percent) and personal current transfer receipts increased by 0.5 percent (from +0.1 percent).2016-11-30T13:35:58.44
