Tag: Currency

Dollar Muted as Traders Mull Ukraine, Inflation RisksUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was little changed around 96 on Wednesday, as investors continued monitoring developments around the Ukraine crisis closely, while keeping an eye on accelerating inflation and ensuing rate hikes from the Federal Reserve. Fed governor Michelle Bowman suggested on Monday that a 50 basis point rate hike at the March meeting could happen if PCE inflation to be released later in the week comes in above expectations. Policymakers are coalescing around a plan and conditioning markets for a steady increase in interest rates starting in March. Meanwhile, Biden announced US sanctions against Russia on Tuesday targeting the country’s sovereign debt, financial firms and elites, after Moscow ordered troops into two separatist regions of Ukraine.2022-02-23T07:11:43.8
