Title: US Consumer Sentiment Remains At 9-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Remains At 9-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States was revised up to 93.4 from a preliminary of 93.1 in July of 2017 and compared to 95.1 in June. Still, it is the lowest reading since October of 2016 as the future expectations declined, although less than previously estimated, while barometer of current conditions rose further. 2017-07-28T14:13:24
