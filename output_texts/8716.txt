Title: US January Wholesale Inventories Revised Down
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US January Wholesale Inventories Revised DownUnited States Wholesale InventoriesUS wholesale inventories dropped 0.4 percent from a month earlier in January 2020 instead of falling 0.2 percent as previously reported, compared to a 0.3 percent decline in December. Apparel stocks were down 0.9 percent (vs -1.2 percent in December) while wholesale auto inventories rose slightly by 0.1 percent (vs -0.8 percent in December). The component of wholesale inventories that goes into the calculation of GDP also declined 0.4 percent in January. Year-on-year, stocks increased 0.4 percent.2020-03-06T15:06:00
