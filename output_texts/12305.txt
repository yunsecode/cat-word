Title: US Stocks Close Sharply Higher After Fed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Sharply Higher After FedUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks surged on Wednesday, after the Fed raised the target range for its federal funds rate by 25bps to 0.75% to 1% as widely expected. The Dow Jones closed 113 points up, or 0.5 percent, to 20,950.10. The S&P 500 rose by 20 points, or 0.8 percent, to 2,385.26. The Nasdaq finished 43 points up, or 0.7 percent, to 5,900.05. 2017-03-15T20:05:16
