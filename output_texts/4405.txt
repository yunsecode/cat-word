Title: US Stocks Rally to End January but Book Big Monthly Loss
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally to End January but Book Big Monthly LossUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks closed sharply higher on the last trading day of January, led by gains in consumer discretionary, communication services, and tech shares. The Dow Jones ended 406 points higher at 35131, the S&P 500 rallied 1.9% to 4515 and the Nasdaq jumped 3.4% to 14240. On the corporate front, Tesla stocks rallied almost 11% after Credit Suisse upgraded the company to “outperform". In the month, however, the S&P 500 lost almost 6% and the Nasdaq nearly 4%, their worst month since March 2020 while the Dow Jones fell 3.3% as the Fed moved to a more hawkish monetary policy with investors increasingly betting on five rate hikes in 2022.2022-01-31T20:30:00
