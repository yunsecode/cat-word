Title: US Mortgage Applications Jump 14.2%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Jump 14.2%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States surged 14.2 percent in the week ended July 1st of 2016 from the prior period, following a 2.6 percent rise in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went up 20.8 percent and applications to purchase a home rose 4.3 percent. Fixed 30-year mortgage rates declined 9bps to 3.66 percent, down for the fifth straight week and reaching a new low since May of 2013.2016-07-06T12:07:34
