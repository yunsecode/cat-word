Title: Dollar Regains Traction
Tag: Currency

Dollar Regains Traction United States CurrencyThe dollar index regained some ground toward the 93 level on Thursday, close to levels not seen since early April after data showed weekly initial claims fell for the third week, pointing to a healing labor market despite the latest surge in coronavirus cases due to the Delta variant. Figures came on the wheels of a stronger than expected July jobs report. Meantime, producer inflation rose by 1% in July, above market expectations. Consumer price data on Wednesday showed inflation remained high in July but increased at a moderated pace. Kansas City Fed President Esther George said on Wednesday the standard for reducing the bond-buying program may have already been met by the current spike in inflation, recent labor market improvements, and the expectation for continued strong demand.2021-08-12T12:51:00
