Title: US Jobless Rate Unchanged at 3.7% in November
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Unchanged at 3.7% in NovemberUnited States Unemployment RateThe unemployment rate in the US was unchanged at 3.7 percent in November 2022, matching market expectations and remaining close to September's 29-month low of 3.5 percent. The jobless rate has been in a narrow range of 3.5 percent to 3.7 percent since March, suggesting that the tight labor market will likely continue to contribute to inflationary pressure in the world's largest economy for some time to come. The number of unemployed persons rose by 48 thousand to 6.01 million in November, while the number of employed decreased by 138 thousand to 158.5 million.2022-12-02T13:34:00
