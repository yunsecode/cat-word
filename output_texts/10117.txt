Title: US Stocks Rebound on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street rebounded sharply on Friday 12 October 2018, recovering some of the losses accrued throughout the week triggered by rising yields, GDP revisions to global growth forecasts, and concerns over US trade policy. The Dow Jones gained 287 points or 1.2% to 25340, still losing 4% over the week. The S&P 500 climbed 39 points or 1.4% to 2767. The Nasdaq soared 168 points or 2.3% to 7497. 2018-10-12T20:08:26.86
