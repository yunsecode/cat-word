Title: US 2-Year Yield in Biggest 2-Day Fall since 2008
Tag: 2 Year Note Yield

US 2-Year Yield in Biggest 2-Day Fall since 2008United States 2 Year Note YieldYields on two-year notes slumped nine bps to 1.842% on Monday morning, following a 14 bps drop in the previous session and marking their biggest two-day fall since early-October 2008. The Federal Reserve is expected to ease policy this year to support growth, amid heightened global trade tensions. Also, ten-year yields slumped six bps to 2.08%, the lowest since September 2017.2019-06-03T08:24:00
