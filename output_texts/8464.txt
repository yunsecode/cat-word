Tag: Composite Pmi

US Composite PMI Slumps to Record LowUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI slumped to 27.4 in April 2020 from 40.9 in the previous month, a preliminary estimate showed, signaling the steepest reduction in private sector output since the series began in late-2009. Both service and manufacturing sector companies registered marked contractions of output amid the coronavirus pandemic.2020-04-23T13:54:34.01
