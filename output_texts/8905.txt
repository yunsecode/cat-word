US Mortgage Applications Rise: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States went up 13.5 percent in the week ended January 3rd 2019, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications increased 24.6 percent while applications to purchase a home rose 3.0 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went down 4bps to 3.91 percent.2020-01-08T12:26:57.083
