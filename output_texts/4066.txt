Title: Dollar Regains Momentum
Tag: Currency

Dollar Regains MomentumUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index managed to regain ground above the 98.00 mark, recovering from its worst day in two years that sent the US currency as low as 97.85 in the prior session. Investors continued to monitor developments surrounding the war in Ukraine while awaiting consumer prices data for clues on the Federal Reserve's plans to hike interest rates. Market moves came on the heels of a better than expected US labour report last week and surging inflation, with headline CPI expected to accelerate 7.9% year-over-year, rising further from January's 7.5% rise. Still, Fed Chair Powell pointed to a 25 basis point rate hike but opened the door for a more hawkish movement if inflation does not abate as anticipated. 2022-03-10T10:42:00
