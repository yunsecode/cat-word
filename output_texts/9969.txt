Title: US Stocks Rebound Sharply on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound Sharply on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Thursday 15 November 2018, as bank shares led by JP Morgan and tech stocks led by Apple responded positively to reported progress in US-China trade talks. The Dow Jones gained 209 points or 0.8% to 25289. The S&P 500 added 29 points or 1.1% to 2730. The Nasdaq surged 123 points or 1.7% to 7259.2018-11-15T21:10:29.167
