Title: US Stocks Close Up as Earnings Boost Sentiment
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Up as Earnings Boost SentimentUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mostly in the green on Wednesday October 18th of 2017, after better-than-expected quarterly results from IBM, Abbott Laboratories and Northern Trust Corp boosted sentiment. The Dow Jones gained 160 points or 0.7% to a fresh high of 23158. The S&P 500 edged up 2 points or 0.1% to a new record of 2561. The Nasdaq inched up 1 points or less than 0.1% to tie a high at 6624. 2017-10-18T20:09:09
