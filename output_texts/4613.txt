Title: US Crude Stocks Fall for 6th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Fall for 6th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 2.144 million barrels in the week ending December 31st, a sixth consecutive period of declines and compared with market forecasts of a 3.283 million drop, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories surged by 10.128 million barrels, the most since April 2020 and much more than market expectations of a 1.775 million increase.2022-01-05T15:52:00
