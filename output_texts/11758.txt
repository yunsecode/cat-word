Title: US Stocks Tumble On Thursday Over Policy Concerns

US Stocks Tumble On Thursday Over Policy ConcernsUnited States Stock MarketUS Stocks closed deep in the red on Thursday, as market volatility spiked higher on speculation that President Trump’s policy agenda will be affected going forward after controversial remarks over a rally in Virginia and a terrorists attack in Barcelona. The Dow Jones plunged 274 points or 1.2% to 21751. The S&P 500 retreated 36 points or 1.5% to 2432. The Nasdaq plummeted 123 points or 1.9% to 6222. 2017-08-17T20:06:13
