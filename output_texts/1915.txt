Title: Construction Spending in the US Unexpectedly Rebounds
Tag: Construction Spending

Construction Spending in the US Unexpectedly ReboundsUnited States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the US unexpectedly increased 0.2% month-over-month in September of 2022, rebounding from a downwardly revised 0.6% fall in August and beating market forecasts of a 0.5% drop. Spending on private construction went up 0.4%, led by a 1% surge in investment in nonresidential structures, namely for manufacturing (7.6%) and office (0.1%). Investment in residential construction was unchanged, with spending on single-family projects dropping 2.6% while outlays on multi-family housing projects increased 0.3%. Public investment on the other hand, fell 0.4% as educational construction was virtually unchanged while highway construction surged 1.7%.2022-11-01T15:17:49.82
