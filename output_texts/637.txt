Title: DXY Approaches 102
Tag: Currency

DXY Approaches 102United States CurrencyThe dollar strengthened sharply against a basket of major currencies, pushing the DXY towards the 102 mark on the back of a flight to safety as concerns about the banking system resurfaced following disappointing earnings from First Republic Bank and UBS. On top of that, the Federal Reserve will likely deliver another 25bps rate hike next week as signs of persistent inflation suggest the tightening cycle is not over yet. Still, money markets are now pricing in the peak for US interest rates in June and then a decline to end the year below 4.5%. Investors now look ahead to first-quarter GDP data and April consumer sentiment data for more clues on the state of the economy and more earnings reports from major US firms. This dollar’s strength was seen across the board, with some of the most pronounced buying activity against risk-sensitive currencies such as the Australian dollar.  2023-04-25T16:21:00
