Title: US Construction Spending Rises More than Expected
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Rises More than ExpectedUnited States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the US went up 0.3 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of USD 1.569 trillion in July of 2021 after showing no growth in June and above market expectations of a 0.2 percent gain. Spending on private construction increased 0.3 percent and public construction outlays rose 0.7 percent, led by increases in Power  (2.3 percent), highway and street (1.9 percent) and commercial (1.7 percent). 2021-09-01T14:05:00
