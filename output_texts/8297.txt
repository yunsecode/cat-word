Title: Stock Futures Lack Direction
Tag: Stock Market

Stock Futures Lack Direction United States Stock MarketUS equity futures struggle for direction Thursday, as investors digested the latest views from the Fed Chair. Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell pledged more stimulus measures, warning that economic recovery from the coronavirus is likely to be slow. Also weighing on sentiment were renewed tensions between Washington and Beijing over the coronavirus crises. President Donald Trump tweeted that any benefits from the US-China trade deal faint in comparison to the damage that has been caused by COVID-19. On Wednesday, the Dow Jones fell 517 points or 2.2% to 23,248 and linked three straight drops. The S&P 500 shed 50 points or 1.8% to 2820. The Nasdaq lost 139 points or 1.6% to 8863.2020-05-14T01:09:25.757
