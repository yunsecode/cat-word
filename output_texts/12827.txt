Title: US Factory Orders Rise More Than Expected in September
Tag: Factory Orders

US Factory Orders Rise More Than Expected in SeptemberUnited States Factory OrdersNew orders for manufactured goods in the United States rose by 0.3 percent month-over-month in September 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.4 percent growth in August and above market expectations of a 0.2 percent gain. It was a third consecutive month of increase, as new orders for manufactured nondurable goods went up 0.9 percent, while those for manufactured durable goods and transport equipment dropped 0.3 percent and 1.1 percent, respectively.2016-11-03T14:03:51
