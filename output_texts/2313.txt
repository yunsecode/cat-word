Title: US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than Expected
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than ExpectedUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States increased by USD 23.81 billion in July of 2022, down from a downwardly revised USD 39.1 billion gain in the previous month and well below market expectations of a USD 33 billion rise. Non-revolving credit, which includes auto and student loans went up by USD 12.91 billion while revolving credit, which includes credit card debt, rose by USD 10.9 billion. On an annual basis, the consumer credit advanced by 6.2 percent, following a downwardly revised 10.2 percent increase in June.2022-09-08T19:04:00
