Title: Dollar Rally Pauses ahead of FOMC
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rally Pauses ahead of FOMCUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index edged down to around 96.1 on Wednesday, pausing after 2 sessions of gains as investors await the FOMC minutes release later in the day for more clues on the timing of the Federal Reserve’s first interest-rate hike, specially if there will be any time between the end of asset purchases and the first rate increase. The Fed is expected to begin raising interest rates as early as March amid a robust US economic rebound and surging inflation and despite a spike in omicron coronavirus infections.2022-01-05T10:19:26.387
