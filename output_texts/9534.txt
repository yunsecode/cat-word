Title: US Stocks Trade Mixed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade MixedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded mixed on Tuesday, with Boeing shares extending the decline from the previous session after China, Indonesia, Ethiopia and the UK suspended flights of Being 737 MAX-8 following the crash a of a Boeing 737 MAX plane on Sunday in Nairobi. The Dow Jones lost 0.4% while the S&P 500 added 0.4% and the Nasdaq traded 0.6% higher around 12:15 PM NY time.
