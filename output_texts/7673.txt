Title: US Futures Fail to Hold Gains
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Fail to Hold GainsUnited States Stock MarketUS futures traded slightly lower on Thursday, with contracts on the Dow Jones falling near 50 points, the S&P 500 near 10 points and the Nasdaq near 50 points as investors take a breather after a big rally in the previous session. Meanwhile, the initial claims are set to remain below 1 million for the second straight week, a sign the labour market is slowly recovering from the coronavirus pandemic. On Wednesday, the Dow Jones added 440 points or 1.6% to 27,941. The S&P 500 climbed 67 points or 2% to 3399. The Nasdaq jumped 294 points or 2.7% to 11,142.2020-09-10T07:51:00
