Title: US Stocks End Mostly Up on Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mostly Up on EarningsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mostly in the green on Tuesday October 17th of 2017, as UnitedHealth Group and Johnson & Johnson beat estimates and US homebuilders’ confidence rebounded to a five-month high. The Dow Jones gained 40 points or 0.2% to a new record of 22997. The S&P 500 edged up 1 points or 0.1% to fresh high of 2559. In contrast, the Nasdaq stayed flat at 6624. 2017-10-17T20:08:22
