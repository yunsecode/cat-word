Title: US Services Growth Eases More than Expected: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Growth Eases More than Expected: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States fell to 58.8 in March of 2018 from 59.5 in February and compared to market forecasts of 59. A slowdown was mainly seen in new orders and production and price pressures intensified. On the other hand, employment went up faster and the majority of respondents remained positive about business conditions.2018-04-04T14:06:36.197
