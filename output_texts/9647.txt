Title: US Stocks Rally on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street rallied on Tuesday 12 February 2019, following news that a second US government shutdown can be avoided through a funding agreement and as investors continue optimistic over a US-China trade deal. Also, investors responded positively to new that lawmakers reached a tentative funding agreement to avoid a second partial government shutdown which includes $1.4 billion to build 55 miles of new border fencing with Mexico, less than $5.7 billion demanded by President Trump. The Dow Jones soared 373 points or 1.5% to 25426.The S&P 500 jumped 35 points or 1.3% to 2745. The Nasdaq surged 107 points or 1.5% to 7415.2019-02-12T21:11:00
