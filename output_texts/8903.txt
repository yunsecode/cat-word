Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Unexpectedly Rise
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Unexpectedly RiseUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 1.164 million barrels in the week ended January 3rd 2020, after a 11.463 million drop in the previous week and against market expectations of a 3.572 million decline, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories went up by 9.137 million barrels after rising by 3.212 million in the previous week and compared with market consensus of a 2.654 million increase.2020-01-08T15:33:05.463
