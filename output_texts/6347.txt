Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Fall More than Forecast
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Fall More than ForecastUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 5.889 million barrels in the April 9th week, following a 3.522 million decline in the previous period and compared with market forecasts of a 2.889 million drop, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report.
