Title: Mortgage Applications Edge Up, Interest Rates Fall: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

Mortgage Applications Edge Up, Interest Rates Fall: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the US edged 0.5% higher in the week ended June 16th, 2023, following a 7.2% jump in the previous week which was the biggest in nearly three months, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association data showed. Applications to buy a home were up 1.5% while those to refinance a home loan declined 2.1%. Meanwhile, the average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($726,200 or less) fell again by 4bps to 6.73%, the third straight week of decreases.26 days ago
