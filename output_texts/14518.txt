Title: US Consumer Sentiment Up to 9-Month High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Up to 9-Month HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe Thomson Reuters/University of Michigan's consumer sentiment index increased to a preliminary reading of 82.6 in April of 2014, up from March’s final 80.0. It is the highest level since July of 2013, as current conditions and expectations surged.2014-04-11T15:05:26.307
