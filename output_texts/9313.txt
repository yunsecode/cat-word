Title: US Exports Rise 1% in March
Tag: Exports

US Exports Rise 1% in MarchUnited States ExportsExports from the US rose USD 2.1 billion from the previous month to USD 212.0 billion in March 2019. Goods exports increased USD 2.0 billion to USD 141.7 billion, boosted by sales of industrial supplies and materials (up USD 1.7 billion), such as natural gas liquids, fuel oil, metallurgical grade coal, and other petroleum products. In addition, exports of foods, feeds, and beverages were up USD 0.8 billion, led by soybeans. By contrast, sales of civilian aircraft dropped USD 0.7 billion. Exports of services, including maintenance and repair services, and financial services, increased less than USD 0.1 billion to USD 70.3 billion in March. On a non seasonally adjusted basis, goods exports rose to all major trade partners: China (23.6 percent); Japan (18.3 percent); Canada (13.3 percent); the EU (7.2 percent); Mexico (8.0 percent), Brazil (22.9 percent) and OPEC (25.3 percent).2019-05-09T12:36:00
