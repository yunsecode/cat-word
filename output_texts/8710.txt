Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Below 0.4% for 1st Time
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Below 0.4% for 1st TimeUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on 10-year US Treasury fell to a fresh record low of 0.318% on Monday, as an all-out price war and worries regarding the fast-spreading coronavirus outbreak sent investors rushing to safe-haven assets. The entire US Treasury yield curve fell below 1% for the first time ever, as the yield on the 30-year bond also hit an all-time low of 0.70%, and that on the two-year note, which typically moves in step with interest rate expectations, dropped to 0.285%, its lowest level since 2014.2020-03-09T09:46:00
