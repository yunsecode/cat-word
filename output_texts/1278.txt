Title: DXY Hits Lowest Since April 2022
Tag: Currency

DXY Hits Lowest Since April 2022United States CurrencyThe dollar index extended losses to below 101.2 on Wednesday, the lowest since April last year, after Fed Chair Powell said during the press conference that the disinflationary process has begun. The US central bank raised rates by only 25bps as expected, still bringing borrowing costs to their highest since 2007. Looking ahead, the Committee said more rate increases will be appropriate.2023-02-01T19:03:00
