Title: US Imports Fall 0.8% MoM in July
Tag: Imports

US Imports Fall 0.8% MoM in JulyUnited States ImportsImports to the United States declined 0.8 percent to $225.8 billion in July of 2016 compared to the previous month, following a 1.9 percent growth in June. Imports of goods decreased $1.9 billion to $184.4 billion, led by pharmaceutical preparations (-$1.0 billion); cell phones and other household goods (-$0.6 billion); and civilian aircraft (-$0.9 billion). In contrast, inbound shipments of services increased $0.1 billion to $41.4 billion. 2016-09-02T13:58:10.89
