Title: US Corporate Profits Rise 7.6% QoQ in Q3
Tag: Corporate Profits

US Corporate Profits Rise 7.6% QoQ in Q3United States Corporate ProfitsCorporate profits in the United States increased by $112.7 billion, or 7.6 percent, to $1589.8 billion in the third quarter of 2016, after falling by 1.9 percent in the previous period, preliminary estimate showed. Net dividends rose by $18.8 billion, or 2 percent, to $972.8 billion and undistributed profits advanced by $93.9 billion, or 18 percent, to $617 billion. Also, net cash flow with inventory valuation adjustment, the internal funds available to corporations for investment, went up by $109.6 billion, or 5.3 percent, to $2185.7 billion.2016-11-29T13:41:44.22
