Title: US Producer Prices Rise The Most In Nearly 4 Years
Tag: Producer Price Inflation MoM

US Producer Prices Rise The Most In Nearly 4 YearsUnited States Producer Price Inflation MoMUS producer prices advanced 0.6 percent month-over-month in January 2017, following a 0.3 percent rise in the previous month and beating market consensus of a 0.3 percent gain. It was the sharpest increase since September 2012 as prices for final demand goods increased 1 percent, the largest rise since May 2015, driven by rising cost of energy products (4.7 percent from 1.8 percent in December). Also, prices for final demand services went up 0.3 percent, as the volatile trade services component, which measures changes in margins received by wholesalers and retailers, jumped 0.9 percent after showing no growth in the prior month. Year-on-year, producer prices rose 1.6 percent, the same as in December but above expectations of a 1.5 percent increase.2017-02-14T13:40:45.163
