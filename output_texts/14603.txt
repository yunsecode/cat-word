Title: US Consumer Sentiment Jumps in December
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Jumps in DecemberUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe Thomson Reuters/University of Michigan's consumer sentiment index rose in December to a preliminary reading of 82.5, from 75.1 in November. It is the highest value since July, due to an improved outlook on the economy and employment.2013-12-06T15:05:14.98
