Title: US 5th District Service Sector Activity Unchanged in September
Tag: Richmond Fed Services Index

US 5th District Service Sector Activity Unchanged in SeptemberUnited States Richmond Fed Services IndexThe Service Sector Activity Index in the US fifth district stagnated in September of 2022 from -12 in the previous month. The demand index rebounded to 5 from -11 in August, while capital expenditures rose to 14 from 13 and equipment and software spending rose to 14 from 12. At the same time, the gauge measuring number of employees rose to 11 from 10 in the prior month, but availability of skills needed tanked further to -15 from -5.
