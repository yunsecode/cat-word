Title: US Jobless Rate Seen at 8.2% in September
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Seen at 8.2% in SeptemberUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate is seen falling to 8.2 percent in September 2020, little-changed from August's 8.4 percent and compared to an all-time high of 14.7 percent hit in April. Still, the jobless rate will be more than twice 3.5 percent reported in February before the pandemic started as the recovery from COVID-19 shock showed signs of slowing amid diminishing government stimulus and a spike in new cases. The US employment report will be the last before the November presidential election.2020-10-02T08:09:00
