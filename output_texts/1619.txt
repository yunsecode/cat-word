Title: US Natural Gas Storage Declines Less than Forecasts: EIA
Tag: Natural Gas Stocks Change

US Natural Gas Storage Declines Less than Forecasts: EIAUnited States Natural Gas Stocks ChangeWorking gas held in storage facilities in the United States decreased by 21 billion cubic feet in the week ending December 2nd of 2022, following an 81 billion cubic feet draw in the previous week and less than market forecasts of a 31 billion cubic feet fall. Total working gas stocks in storage stand at 3.462 trillion cubic feet, down 51 billion cubic feet from a year ago and 58 billion cubic feet below the five-year average. 2022-12-08T15:39:39.747
