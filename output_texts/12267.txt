Title: US Stocks Fall on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened sharply in the red on Monday, dragged down mainly by financials and materials after President Trump failed to repeal Obamacare last week. The Dow Jones fell 167 points or 0.8%, the S&P 500 lost 20 points or 0.9% and the Nasdaq moved down 55 points or 1%  around 7 minutes after the opening bell. 2017-03-27T13:40:15
