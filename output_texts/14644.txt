Title: US Jobless Claims Down to 292K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Down to 292K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending September 7, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 292,000, a decrease of 31,000 from the previous week's unrevised figure of 323,000. The 4-week moving average was 321,250, a decrease of 7,500 from the previous week's revised average of 328,750.2013-09-12T13:34:07.413
