Title: US Stocks Finish Higher; Nasdaq Closes at Record High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Finish Higher; Nasdaq Closes at Record HighUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks closed higher on Tuesday with consumer staples and health care stocks among the best performers, despite a fall in oil prices and weak productivity data for the second quarter of the year. The Dow Jones rose 4 points, or less than 0.1%, to close at 18,533; the S&P 500 index finished up 1 point, or less than 0.1%, at 2,182; and the Nasdaq Composite climbed 12 points, or 0.2%, to end at a record high of 5,225.2016-08-09T21:19:14
