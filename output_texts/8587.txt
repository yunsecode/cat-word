Dollar Falls on ThursdayUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index continued to depreciate for the 3rd straight session on Thursday to trade around 100.2 after the jobless report showed initial claims jumped to an all-time high of 3.28 million in latest week, much worse than market forecasts of 1 million.
