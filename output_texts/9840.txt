Title: US Stocks Plummet After Fed Hike
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Plummet After Fed HikeUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on Wednesday 19 December 2018, as the Fed raised the benchmark interest rate by 25 bps, as expected, and cut its hike estimates for 2019 to two from three on a weaker economic outlook since the previous meeting. The Dow Jones plunged 352 points or 1.5% to 23324. The S&P 500 plummeted 39 points or 1.5% to 2507. The Nasdaq tumbled 147 points or 2.2% to 6637. 2018-12-19T21:13:00
