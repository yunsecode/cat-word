Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Flat after House Passes Stimulus Bill
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Flat after House Passes Stimulus BillUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on US 10-Year Treasury was little-changed at 0.74% on Friday, after the House of Representatives approved a $2.2 trillion aid package to support households and businesses hurt by the coronavirus pandemic. The rescue package is the largest fiscal relief measure ever, and is now expected to be signed into law by President Donald Trump. On Thursday, the US surpassed China as the country with the most Covid-19 confirmed cases.2020-03-27T18:17:00
