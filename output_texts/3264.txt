Title: US Services Sector Slows More than Expected: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Sector Slows More than Expected: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe S&P Global US Services PMI fell to 53.5 in May of 2022 from 55.6 in the previous month, the lowest in four months and below market consensus of 55.2, a preliminary estimate showed. Although the rise was supported by increased client demand, the pace of expansion was reportedly weighed down by hikes in selling prices and concerns over higher interest rates. Similarly, new business rose at a solid but softer pace in May. The rate of growth in new sales was the slowest since August 2020 and below the series average. New export business at service providers rose strongly, but at the slowest rate since the start of the year. On the price front, service providers registered the fastest rise in input prices on record in May while the rate of charge inflation eased from April’s record high and was the slowest for three months.
