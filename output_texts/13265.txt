Title: US Stocks Open At New Record
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open At New RecordUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Tuesday led by gains in energy and financials. Rising oil prices, easing concerns about political situation in the UK and better than expected earnings report from Alcoa lifted investors mood. The Dow Jones added 0.64% or 116 points, the S&P 500 rose 0.65% or 14 points and the Nasdaq increased 0.7% or 35 points, around 20 minutes after the opening bell.2016-07-12T14:53:59
