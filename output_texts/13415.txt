Title: US New Home Sales Up to 8-Year High
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Up to 8-Year HighUnited States New Home SalesNew US single-family home sales jumped 16.6 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 619,000 in April of 2016, much higher than an upwardly revised 531,000 in the previous month and compared to market expectations of 523,000. It is the highest reading since January of 2008 and the biggest gain since 1992.2016-05-24T15:07:23
