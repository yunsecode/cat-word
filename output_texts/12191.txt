US Crude Oil Inventories Fall For 2nd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 1.034 million barrels in the week ended April 14th, 2017, following a 2.166 million decrease in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 1.47 million decline.
