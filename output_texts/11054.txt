Title: US House Prices Rise Less than Expected: FHFA
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US House Prices Rise Less than Expected: FHFAUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States increased 0.3 percent month-over-month in December 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.5 percent gain in November and missing market expectations of 0.4 percent. Year-on-year, house prices went up 6.5 percent, the same pace as in the previous month.2018-02-27T14:03:18.18
