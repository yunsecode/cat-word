Title: US Services Sector Growth Slows to 13-Month Low
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Sector Growth Slows to 13-Month LowUnited States Services PMIThe Markit flash US Services PMI came in at 53.7 in January of 2016, lower than market expectations and the weakest since December of 2014. Cutbacks across the energy sector and a strong USD had a negative impact on business activity while new business growth picked up and job creation grew to a four-month high.2016-01-26T14:59:42
