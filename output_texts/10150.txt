Title: US Wage Growth Matches Forecasts
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wage Growth Matches ForecastsUnited States Average Hourly EarningsUS average hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls rose by 8 cents or 0.3 percent month-over-month to $27.24 in September of 2018. It follows a downwardly revised 0.3 percent rise in the previous month, matching market expectations. Over the year, average hourly earnings have increased by 73 cents, or 2.8 percent, easing from a 2.9 percent jump in August which was the highest annual gain since June of 2009. Average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees increased by 6 cents to $22.81.
