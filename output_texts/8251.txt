Title: US Private Sector Activity Falls at Softer Pace
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Activity Falls at Softer PaceUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI increased to 36.4 in May 2020 from the previous month's record low of 27.0, still indicating the second-sharpest decline in business activity since the series began in late-2009 as the economy began to reopen. New business and employment fell at the second-fastest rates in the series history, with foreign client demand remaining especially muted. On the price front, both input costs and output charges declined further in May. Looking ahead, business sentiment picked up from April’s all-time low, but remained weak overall.2020-05-21T13:57:28.477
