Title: US Dollar Trades around 93
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Trades around 93United States CurrencyThe US dollar index held around 93 against a basket of currencies on Friday, as investors await Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell speech later in the day at the Jackson Hole symposium for more details about Fed's plans for tapering of the stimulus. Investors were getting more confident the Federal Reserve will delay taper plans amid weak economic data and worries over the spread of the coronavirus delta variant but several officials implied yesterday that the time has come to start cutting on bond purchases.2021-08-27T05:05:00
