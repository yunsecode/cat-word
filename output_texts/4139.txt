Title: US Treasury Yields Decline
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields DeclineUnited States Government Bond 10YThe 10-year US treasury yield dropped as much as 8 basis points to below 1.9% on Monday as demand for safe-haven assets surged after the invasion of Ukraine by Russian forces continued and Western nations ramped up new sanctions on Russia in response. Meanwhile, 2-year yields fell eight basis points to 1.48%, while 30-year yields slipped six basis points to 2.21%.2022-02-28T00:37:00
