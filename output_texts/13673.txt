Title: US Durable Goods Orders Rebound in January
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Rebound in JanuaryUnited States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods jumped 4.9 percent in January of 2016, rising for the first time in three months, following a downwardly revised 4.6 percent fall in December. It is the biggest gain in ten months, higher than market expectations of a 2.5 percent increase. Excluding transportation, new orders went up 1.8 percent and without defense orders increased 4.5 percent. 2016-02-25T13:45:34
