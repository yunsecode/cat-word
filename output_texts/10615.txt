Title: US Budget Deficit Larger than Expected in May
Tag: Government Budget Value

US Budget Deficit Larger than Expected in MayUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US budget deficit widened to USD 147.0 billion in May 2018 from USD 88.0 billion in the same month of the previous year, and compared to market expectations of USD 144 billion. Outlays jumped 11 percent to USD 364 billion, while receipts dropped 10 percent to USD 217 billion.2018-06-12T18:04:00
