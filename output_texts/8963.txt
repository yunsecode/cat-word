Title: US Stocks Drop as Focus Turn to Fed, Trade
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Drop as Focus Turn to Fed, TradeUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks fell slightly on Monday, following four consecutive sessions of gains, as investors await a Fed policy announcement on Wednesday and any further developments on trade negotiations as another round of US tariffs on Chinese goods is set to kick in later this week. The Dow Jones lost 69 points or 0.3%; the S&P 500 declined 4 points or 0.1%; and the Nasdaq retreated 10 points or 0.1% around 12:30 PM NY time.2019-12-09T14:42:00
