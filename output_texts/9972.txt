Title: US Stocks Trade Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade LowerUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded in the red early on Thursday amid Brexit-related concerns after the UK Brexit Secretary Dominic Raab resigned as a draft deal with the EU was reached. Also, news that Warren Buffet exit Walmart also weighed on investors' mood. The Dow Jones fell 0.6%, the S&P 500 dropped 0.6% and the Nasdaq traded 0.4% lower around 35 minutes after the opening bell.2018-11-15T15:13:00.15
