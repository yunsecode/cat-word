U.S. Economy Adds 157K Jobs in JanuaryUnited States Non Farm PayrollsTotal nonfarm payroll employment increased by 157,000 in January. In 2012, employment growth averaged 181,000 per month. In January, job gains occurred in retail trade, construction, health care, and wholesale trade, while employment edged down in transportation and warehousing.2013-02-01T13:42:58.25
