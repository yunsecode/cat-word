Title: Wall Street Opens Higher As Oil Rebounds
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Higher As Oil ReboundsUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened in the green on Tuesday, led by gains in financials and energy shares as oil prices recovered. The Dow Jones added 0.85% or 151 points, the S&P 500 gained 0.71% or 15 points and the Nasdaq went up 0.43% or 21 points around 27 minutes after the opening bell.2016-05-10T15:06:12
