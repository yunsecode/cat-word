Title: US Stocks Rise On Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise On ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Thursday with the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq reaching record highs mainly boosted by the tech sector. Gains come despite lower oil prices as investors continue to digest FOMC minutes released yesterday where the Fed left the June rate hike on the table. The Dow Jones added 66 points or 0.3%, the S&P 500 went up 7 points or 0.3% and the Nasdaq gained 21 points or 0.4% around 20 minutes after the opening bell.2017-05-25T13:53:37
