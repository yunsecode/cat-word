Title: Dollar Index Hits 20-year High
Tag: currency

Dollar Index Hits 20-year HighUnited States currencyDXY increased to a 20-year high of 114.53 after a White House official ruled out a currency agreement to weaken the currency. Meantime, Chicago Fed President Charles Evans and St. Louis Fed President James Bullard made a case for more rate hikes, reaffirming the central bank's commitment to tame inflation even at the risk of a recession. Further, haven demand continued to support the greenback as investors rushed for its safety amid a highly uncertain global economic outlook and escalating recession risks. 2022-09-28T01:30:00
