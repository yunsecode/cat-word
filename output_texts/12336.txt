Title: US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than Expected
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than ExpectedUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States increased by $8.79 billion in January of 2017, following an upwardly revised $14.76 billion rise in December and well below market expectations of $17.1 billion. Revolving credit fell by $3.79 billion, after increasing by $3.58 billion in the previous month and non-revolving credit went up by $12.59 billion, compared with a $11.18 billion rise in December.2017-03-07T20:03:22.99
