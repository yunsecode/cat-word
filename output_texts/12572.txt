US Economy Adds Only 156K Jobs In DecemberUnited States Non Farm PayrollsNon Farm Payrolls in the US increased by 156 thousand in December of 2016, lower than upwardly revised 204 thousand in November and below market expectation of 178 thousand. Job growth occurred in health care and social assistance. Job growth totaled 2.2 million in 2016, less than the increase of 2.7 million in 2015.2017-01-06T13:31:18
