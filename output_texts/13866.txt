Title: US Current Account Deficit Widens in Q3
Tag: Current Account

US Current Account Deficit Widens in Q3United States Current AccountThe current account gap in the US rose to USD 124.1 billion or 2.7% of GDP in the third quarter of 2015 from an upwardly revised USD 111.1 billion or 2.5% of GDP in the second quarter. The increase in the deficit was largely accounted for by a decrease in the surplus on primary income (USD 46.1 billion from USD 52.8 billion) and an increase in the deficit on secondary income (USD -36.6 billion from USD -30.8 billion).2015-12-17T13:42:47
