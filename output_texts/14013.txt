Title: US Homebuilder Sentiment at 10-Year High
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Homebuilder Sentiment at 10-Year HighUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe National Association of Homebuilders' index of homebuilder confidence increased to 64 in October of 2015 from a downwardly revised 61 in September, reaching the highest reading since October of 2005. The gauge for current single-family home sales went up to 70 from 67 in the previous month and the index for home sales over the next six months rose to 75 from 65 while the index of prospective buyers remained steady at 47.
