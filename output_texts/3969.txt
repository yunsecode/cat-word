Title: US Construction Spending Misses Forecasts
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Misses ForecastsUnited States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the US increased 0.5 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of USD 1.704 trillion in February of 2022, easing from an upwardly revised 1.6 percent advance in January and below market expectations of a 1 percent gain. Spending on private construction rose 0.8 percent, mostly new single family residential construction (2.5 percent), power (1.4 percent), manufacturing (0.6 percent), lodging (0.8 percent) educational (0.2 percent) and transportation (2.3 percent). Meanwhile, public construction outlays went down 0.4 percent, dragged down by spending on nonresidential (-0.5 percent). 2022-04-01T14:05:32.943
