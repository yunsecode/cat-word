US Third Quarter GDP Revised UpUnited States GDP Growth RateReal gross domestic product in the United States increased at an annual rate of 2.6 percent in the third quarter of 2010, (that is, from the second quarter to the third quarter), according to the third estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.  In the second quarter, real GDP increased 1.7 percent.2010-12-22T15:34:21.027
