Title: US Consumer Sentiment Jumps to Near 14-Year High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Jumps to Near 14-Year HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States rose to 101.1 in October 2017 from 95.1 in September, way above market expectations of 95. It was the strongest number since January 2004 as the current conditions index rose to 116.4 from 111.7 in September and the gauge of consumer expectations increased to 91.3 from 84.4.2017-10-13T14:02:57
