Title: US Mortgage Applications Up 2.9%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Up 2.9%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 2.9 percent in the week ended June 17th 2016 from the prior period, following a 2.4 percent fall in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications increased 6.5 percent while applications to purchase a home shrank 2.4 percent. Fixed 30-year mortgage rates declined 3bps to 3.76 percent, down for the third straight week and reaching the lowest since May of 2013.2016-06-22T12:12:37
