Title: US Stocks Rise after China Plans to Increase Imports
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise after China Plans to Increase ImportsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street extended gains on Friday after news that China would increase its annual imports of US goods by over $1 trillion in the next six years. On Thursday, news that US Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin proposed lifting some or all tariffs imposed on Chinese imports ahead of the latest round of trade talks in Washington on January 30th-31st also raised hopes of a trade deal between the two countries. The Dow Jones rose 1.3%, the S&P 500 increased 1.3% and the Nasdaq traded 1.2% higher around 1:15 PM NY time.
