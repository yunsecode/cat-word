Title: US Consumer Credit Beats Forecasts
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Beats ForecastsUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States increased by USD 38.07 billion in April of 2022, after a downwardly revised USD 47.34 billion gain in the previous month and above market expectation of a USD 35 billion rise. Revolving credit, which includes credit card debt, rose by USD 17.77 billion while non-revolving credit, which includes auto and student loans went up by USD 20.3 billion.
