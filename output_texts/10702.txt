Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 2nd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 2nd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 1.4040 million barrels in the week ended May 11th 2018, after a 2.197 million drop in the previous period and higher than market expectations of a 0.763 million decrease. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories fell by 3.790 million barrels after decreasing by 2.174 million in the previous week, while markets were expecting 1.421 million drop.2018-05-16T14:55:36.7
