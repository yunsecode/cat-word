Title: US Consumer Sentiment Highest in 14 Years
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Highest in 14 YearsUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States jumped to 102 in March of 2018 from 99.7 in February, beating market expectations of 99.3. It is the highest reading since January of 2004, due to a new all-time record favorable assessment of current economic conditions, preliminary estimates showed.
