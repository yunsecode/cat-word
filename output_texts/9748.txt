Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Drop for 2nd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Drop for 2nd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil stocks fell by 2.683 million barrels in the week ended January 11th, 2019, following a 1.68 million decrease in the previous week. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories rose by 7.503 million barrels, after a 8.066 million rise in the previous week which was the highest gain in two years.2019-01-16T15:36:47.097
