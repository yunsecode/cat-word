Title: US House Prices Growth Matches Expectations: FHFA
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US House Prices Growth Matches Expectations: FHFAUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States rose 0.4 percent month-over-month in October 2016, following a 0.6 percent increase in September and in line with market expectations. Year-on-year, the FHFA house price index went up 6.2 percent compared to a 6.3 percent increase the previous month. 2016-12-22T14:09:49.64
