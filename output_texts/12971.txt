Title: US Stocks Trade Lower on Oil
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Lower on Oil United States Stock MarketWall Street opened slightly lower on Thursday amid volatile oil prices as investors became more skeptic regarding OPEC's deal to cut production and despite an upward revision to second quarter GDP growth. Dow Jones was down 15 points or 0.08%, the S&P 500 fell 2 points or 0.1% and the Nasdaq lost 8 points or 0.2% around 14 minutes after the opening bell.2016-09-29T13:54:30
