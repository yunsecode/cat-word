Title: US Construction Spending Falls Most in 1-1/2 Years
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Falls Most in 1-1/2 YearsUnited States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the US fell by 2.9 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of USD 1.35 trillion in April 2020, after being unchanged in March and compared to market expectations of a 6.5 percent tumble. That was the largest decrease in construction spending since October 2018 as private outlays declined by 3.0 percent led by falls in residential (-4.5 percent), transportation (-5.9 percent) and commercial spending (-2.3 percent). Meanwhile, public outlays dropped by 2.5 percent due to lower investment in highways, streets (-5.2 percent) and educational (-2.3 percent).2020-06-01T14:06:00
