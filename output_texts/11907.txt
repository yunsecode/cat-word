US Services Sector Growth Beats Forecasts: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States rose to 57.4 in June of 2017 from 56.9 in May, beating market expectations of 56.5. Business activity and new orders went up while employment slowed. The majority of respondents’ comments continue to be positive about business conditions and the overall economy.2017-07-06T14:08:57
