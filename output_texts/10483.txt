Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 6-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 6-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US fell to 97.1 in July of 2018 from 98.2 in June, missing market expectations of 98.2. It is the lowest reading in six months, due to a drop in the current conditions gauge amid rising concerns about the potential negative impact of tariffs on the domestic economy, preliminary estimates showed. 2018-07-13T14:05:58.237
