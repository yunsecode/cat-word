Title: Dollar Eases as Fed Outlook Mulled
Tag: Currency

Dollar Eases as Fed Outlook MulledUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index eased slightly to around 102.6 on Tuesday, falling for the second straight session as traders continued to assess the economic and monetary policy outlook. Last week, Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell said that further rate increases are likely ahead as inflation remains too high. Other Fed officials mirrored his statements, with San Francisco Fed Bank President Mary Daly suggesting that two more rate hikes this year is a “very reasonable” projection. Meanwhile, data released on Friday showed that US private sector activity slowed to a three-month low in June amid a deepening contraction in the manufacturing sector. The dollar also faced pressure from aggressive monetary tightening from other major central banks, with the European Central Bank and the Bank of England raising interest rates further in June.20 days ago
