Title: Wall Street Opens Lower Ahead of Fed, Brexit Vote
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Lower Ahead of Fed, Brexit VoteUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened in the red on Friday, amid further declines in oil prices, as investors remained cautious ahead of Fed's next meeting and U.K.’s Brexit vote due later this month. The Dow Jones lost 0.6% or 105 points, the S&P 500 fell 0.7% or 14 points and the Nasdaq slid 0.9% or 43 points around 5 minutes after the opening bell.2016-06-10T14:38:34
