U.S. Annual Inflation Rate at 3.9% in SeptemberUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.3 percent in September on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today.
