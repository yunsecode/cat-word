Title: US Stocks Hit Fresh Records on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Hit Fresh Records on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed modestly in the green on Monday October 16th of 2017, as crude oil prices advanced 1% during the session on Iraq turmoil and copper hit a 3-year high on stronger Chinese growth prospects. All three major indices notched new highs. The Dow Jones climbed 85 points or 0.4% to 22957. The S&P 500 edged up 4 points or 0.2% to 2558. The Nasdaq gained 18 points or 0.3% to 6624.2017-10-16T20:09:09
