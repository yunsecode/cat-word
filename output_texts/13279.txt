Title: Wall Street Extends Losses on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Extends Losses on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US traded lower for the second straight day dragged down by telecom, financials and energy sectors amid a retreat in oil prices while investors remain cautious ahead of FOMC minutes release. The Dow Jones lost 0.57% or 101 points, the S&P 500 fell 0.52% or 11 points and the Nasdaq decreased 0.32% or 16 points around 10:40 New York time.2016-07-06T15:41:49
