Title: US Unit Labor Costs Rise 0.3% in Q3
Tag: Labour Costs

US Unit Labor Costs Rise 0.3% in Q3United States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the nonfarm business sector in the United States increased an annualized 0.3 percent on quarter in the third quarter of 2016, compared to a downwardly revised 3.9 percent drop in the previous period and well below market expectations of 1.3 percent, preliminary estimates showed. It reflects a 3.4 percent increase in hourly compensation and a 3.1 percent growth in productivity. Year-on-year, unit labor costs increased 2.3 percent.
