Title: US Stocks Finish Mostly Lower After Yellen's Comments
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Finish Mostly Lower After Yellen's CommentsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mostly lower on Friday, hurt by rising dollar after Janet Yellen signaled the possibility of an interest-rate hike later this year. The Dow Jones lost 53 points or 0.29%, the S&P 500 declined 3.43 points or 0.16% while the Nasdaq increased 6.71 points or 0.13% higher. 2016-08-26T21:32:30
