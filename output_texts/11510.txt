Title: US Stocks Edge Up as Nasdaq Books New High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Up as Nasdaq Books New HighUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed modestly in the green on Monday October 31st of 2017, as President Donald Trump hinted he’ll name a new Fed chair Thursday, while investors remained expectant ahead of the Fed’s monetary policy announcement on Wednesday. A strong earnings season thus far nudged tech stocks to new highs. The Dow Jones advanced 29 points or 0.1% to 23377. The S&P 500 gained 2 points or 0.1% to 2575. The Nasdaq climbed 29 points or 0.4% to a new record of 6728. 2017-10-31T21:14:21
