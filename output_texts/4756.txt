Title: US Wages Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Rise Less than ExpectedUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings for all employees on US private nonfarm payrolls increased by 8 cents, or 0.3% over the month to $31.03 in November of 2021, slightly below market expectations of 0.4% and following a 0.4% rise in October. It was the smallest increase in average hourly earnings since March. In November, average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees rose by 12 cents to $26.40. Over the past 12 months, average hourly earnings have rose by 4.8 percent, the same pace as in the prior month but missing market estimates of 5%.2021-12-03T13:32:00
