US Home Prices Rise The Most In 31 Months: Case-ShillerUnited States Case Shiller Home Price Index YoYThe S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller composite index of 20 metropolitan areas in the US rose 5.7 percent year-on-year in January of 2017, following a downwardly revised 5.5 percent increase in the previous month. It is the biggest gain since July of 2017, beating market expectations of a 5.6 percent gain. Prices in Seattle rose the most (11.3 percent), followed by Portland (9.7 percent) and Denver (9.2 percent). On a monthly basis, prices increased 0.2 percent, easing from a 0.3 percent rise in December.
