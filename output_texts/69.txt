Title: US Stocks Fall, FOMC Minutes Eyed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall, FOMC Minutes EyedUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks were lower on Wednesday, with the Dow Jones falling about 100 points while both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq were down nearly 0.3% each, as investors return from the Independence Day holiday to face concerns about the health of the global economy. The services PMI data pointed to a sharp slowdown in China and a bigger than-expected loss of momentum in the Euro Area. Also, traders will keep a close eye on the FOMC minutes release later in the day for further clues on the Fed's plans, although Chair Powell has been reinforcing that interest rates will need to rise further this year. Factory orders are also due today while the payrolls report will be released Friday. Materials, financials and industrial were the worst performing sectors. 12 days ago
