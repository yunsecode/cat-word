Title: US Stocks Fall on Trade Concerns
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on Trade ConcernsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Thursday, tracking losses in Europe and Asia as trade war concerns renewed after the Trump Administration said it was considering raising tariffs on Chinese imports to 25% from 10% and China said will have to retaliate. The Dow Jones was down 0.8%, the S&P 500 fell 0.5% and the Nasdaq traded 0.4% lower around 8 minutes after the opening bell.2018-08-02T13:45:58.107
