Title: DXY Eases Gains
Tag: Currency

DXY Eases GainsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index pared some gains to 101.7 on Thursday, after fresh data reinforced the bets that Fed will soon pause the rate hike campaign. Producer inflation fell more than expected in April, while weekly claims rose to the highest level since October 2021, a sign the labour market is cooling. Meanwhile, markets continue to follow the banking sector crisis and the debt ceiling impasse. Treasury Secretary Janet Yellen said the government may be unable to meet all payment obligations as soon as June 1st. President Biden and House Speaker Kevin McCarthy are set to meet again on Friday to further discuss raising the debt ceiling.2023-05-11T12:37:00
