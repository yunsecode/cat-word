Title: US MBA Mortgage Applications Rise the Most in 10 Weeks
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US MBA Mortgage Applications Rise the Most in 10 WeeksUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States increased 8.9 percent in the week ended March 22nd 2019, following a 1.6 percent gain in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the biggest rise since the week ended January 11th. Refinance application advanced 12.4 percent and applications to purchase a home went up 6.4 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 10bps to 4.45 percent.2019-03-27T11:01:00
