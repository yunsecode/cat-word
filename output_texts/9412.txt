Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Friday

US Stocks Close Higher on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Friday 12 April 2019 following stronger-than-expected US corporate earnings from J.P. Morgan, Wells Fargo, First Republic Bank and PNC. The Dow Jones gained 269 points or 1.0% to 26412. The S&P 500 rose 19 points or 0.7% to 2907.
