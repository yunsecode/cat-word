Title: US Stocks Jump to New Records
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Jump to New RecordsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks hit new records on Wednesday as concerns about slowing global growth eased after Beijing reported the fewest number of new coronavirus cases since January, with its top medical adviser predicting the outbreak to end by April. On the corporate front, CVS Health gained on the back of better-than-expected quarterly profit. Micron Technology and Wynn Resorts were also among the best performers after both firms' shares were upgraded to "buy" by UBS and Bank of America Global Research respectively. Exxon Mobil and Chevron traded higher as oil prices rallied more than 3%. Cisco Systems, Applied Material and TripAdvisor will report results after the closing bell. The Dow Jones jumped more than 200 points or 0.8%; the S&P 500 rose 19 points or 0.6%; and the Nasdaq climbed 82 points or 0.9% around 02:40 PM NY time.2020-02-12T14:30:00
