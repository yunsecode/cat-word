US Shares RallyUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks markets traded in the green on Monday, following Friday's strong rally and buoyed by an increase in oil prices after crude rose above $70 a barrel for the first time since 2014. The Dow Jones climbed 136 points, or 0.6%, to 24,399; the S&P 500 advanced 16 points, or 0.6%, to 2,679; and the Nasdaq gained 58 points, or 0.8%, to 7,267 around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2018-05-07T13:45:01.97
