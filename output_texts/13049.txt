Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 0.9 percent in the week ended September 2nd 2016 from the prior period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It follows a 2.8 percent rise in the week before. Refinance applications increased 0.7 percent and applications to purchase a home went up 1.2 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates edged up 1bps 3.68 percent.2016-09-07T12:06:16.507
