Title: US Futures Cautious Ahead of Payrolls
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Cautious Ahead of PayrollsUnited States Stock MarketStock futures in the US were around the flatline on Friday as traders held back from making big bets ahead of the payrolls report due early in the morning. Both ADP and jobless claims figures released this week topped forecasts and a strong job growth would raise the odds the Federal Reserve will soon start tapering. On Thursday, the Dow Jones added 338 points or 1% to 34755; the S&P 500 surged 36 points or 0.8% to 4400 and the technology-focused Nasdaq Composite jumped 152 points or 1.1% to 14654. Meanwhile, the 3 key stock indexes are on track to book gains for the first week of October. 2021-10-08T09:30:00
