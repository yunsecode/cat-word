Title: U.S. Annual Inflation Down to 3.4% in November

U.S. Annual Inflation Down to 3.4% in NovemberUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) was unchanged in November on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on December 16th. Over the last 12 months, the all items index increased 3.4 percent before seasonal adjustment.2011-12-16T13:37:34
