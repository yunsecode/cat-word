Title: US Manufacturing Growth Eases to 2-1/2-Year Low: ISM
Tag: Business Confidence

US Manufacturing Growth Eases to 2-1/2-Year Low: ISMUnited States Business ConfidenceThe ISM Manufacturing PMI in the US fell to 52.1 in May 2019 from 52.8 in the previous month, missing market expectations of 53. The latest reading pointed to weakest pace of expansion in the manufacturing sector since October 2016 as production growth eased to the weakest since August 2016 and backlog of orders contracted for the first time since January 2017.2019-06-03T14:04:54.43
