Title: US Mortgage Rates at Fresh 4-Month Low: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Rate

US Mortgage Rates at Fresh 4-Month Low: MBAUnited States Mortgage RateThe average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($647,200 or less) in the US edged 1bps lower to 3.19% in the week ended January 27th, 2023, hitting a fresh low since September. The decline in mortgage rates tracks a fall in Treasury yields amid rising expectations the Federal Reserve will slow the pace of rate increases.2023-02-01T12:10:00
