Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Hovers Near Five-Month Low
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Hovers Near Five-Month LowUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note, seen as a proxy for global borrowing costs, bottomed around 3.4%, close to levels not seen since September 2022, as investors reassessed the Federal Reserve's plans for rate hikes. The world's most influential central bank raised interest rates by 25 basis points to a range between 4.5% and 4.75%, a less aggressive move than before, indicating growing confidence that inflation is on a downward trajectory. However, despite Fed Chair Powell's overall dovish tone, he still sees ongoing increases in the target range appropriate to attain a stance of monetary policy that is sufficiently restrictive to return inflation to 2%. Wall Street and the Fed are again in a standoff on the future path of interest rates, with the former betting on a rate cut later this year while the latter reaffirmed its view that interest rates will stay higher for longer. 2023-02-02T09:42:00
