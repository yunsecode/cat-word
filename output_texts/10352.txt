Title: US Home Builder Sentiment Falls to 11-Month Low
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Home Builder Sentiment Falls to 11-Month LowUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index fell to 67 in August 2018 from 68 in the previous month, as widely expected by markets. It was the lowest reading since September last year, due to declines in all components: current single-family home sales (73 vs 74 in July); prospective buyers (49 vs 51); and home sales over the next six months (72 vs 73).2018-08-15T14:20:00
