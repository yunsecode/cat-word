Title: US Stocks Rise After Inflation Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise After Inflation DataUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded in the green on Thursday after monthly inflation for April came in below expectations at 0.2 percent, easing concerns of rising inflationary pressures and faster Fed tightening. The Dow Jones added 147 points or 0.6%, the S&P 500 went up 14 points or 0.5% and the Nasdaq traded 43 points or 0.6% higher around 11:00 AM NY time.2018-05-10T15:06:44.813
