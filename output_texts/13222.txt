Title: US Stocks Open Flat
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Flat United States Stock MarketWall Street opened flat to lower on Friday as investors await data on manufacturing activity and several earnings reports. So far, General Electric, Honeywell, Whirlpool and Stanley Black & Decker reported better-than-expected revenues while Starbucks disappointed. The Dow Jones, the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq traded near the flatline 3 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-07-22T14:37:06
