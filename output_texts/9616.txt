Title: US Factory Growth Lowest since 2017: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Factory Growth Lowest since 2017: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI fell to 53.7 in february of 2019 from 54.9 in January and below market expectations of 54.7, preliminary estimates showed. The reading pointed to the slowest growth in factory activity since September of 2017, amid soft client demand, partly linked to uncertainty across manufacturing supply chains and concerns about the global trade outlook. There were also some reports that adverse weather conditions had disrupted production schedules in February.2019-02-21T14:50:48.473
