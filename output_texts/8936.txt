Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States decreased by 1.085 million barrels in the week ended December 13th 2019, after a 0.822 million rise in the previous week and against market expectations of a 1.288 million decline, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories went up by 2.529 million barrels after rising by 5.405 million in the previous week and compared with market consensus of a 2.178 million increase.2019-12-18T15:32:29.637
