Title: US Jobless Rate Unexpectedly Falls to 3.5%
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Unexpectedly Falls to 3.5%United States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate decreased to 3.5 percent in February of 2020 from 3.6 percent in the previous month while markets had expected it to be unchanged at 3.6 percent. The number of unemployed people decreased by 105 thousand to 5.79 million while employment rose by 45 thousand to 158.76 million. The unemployment rate has been either 3.5 percent or 3.6 percent for the past 6 months. Meantime, the labor force participation rate was unchanged at 63.4 percent. 2020-03-06T13:32:00
