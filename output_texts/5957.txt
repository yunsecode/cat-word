Title: US Goods Gap Narrows in April
Tag: Goods Trade Balance

US Goods Gap Narrows in AprilUnited States Goods Trade BalanceThe goods deficit in the US shrank to $85.23 billion in April of 2021 from an upwardly revised record $91.98 billion in March. Exports rose 1.2 percent to $144.7 billion, as strong sales of capital goods (4.8 percent) and industrial supplies (1.7 percent) partly offset an 8 percent plunge in those of autos. Meanwhile, imports fell 2.2 percent to $230 billion, amid lower purchases of consumer goods (-4.2 percent) and autos (-3.5 percent).2021-05-28T12:39:00
