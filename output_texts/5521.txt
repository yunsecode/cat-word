Title: US Dollar Remains Weak
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Remains WeakUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was around 92.00 in the first trading week of August, not far from July’s lows near 91.80, as fears about slowing US economic growth and a persistent dovish Fed spooked investors away from the greenback. Meantime, President Joe Biden said Tuesday that the US is suffering from the pandemic of unvaccinated people, urging state governors to not stand in the way of the vaccination. Traders now looked ahead to partial US labor data due later on Wednesday and non-farm payroll figures due on Friday.2021-08-04T04:39:28.967
