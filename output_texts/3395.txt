Title: US Wages Grow Less Than Expected
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Grow Less Than ExpectedUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls in the US increased by 10 cents, or 0.3%, to $31.85 in April of 2022, after an upwardly revised 0.5% rise in March and below market expectations for a 0.4% increase. In April, the average hourly earnings of private sector production and nonsupervisory employees rose by 10 cents, or 0.4%, to $27.12. Over the past 12 months, average hourly earnings have increased by 5.5%, following a 5.6% increase in March. 2022-05-06T12:34:00
