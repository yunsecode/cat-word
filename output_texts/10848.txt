Title: US Retail Trade Rebound Stronger than Expected
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Trade Rebound Stronger than ExpectedUnited States Retail Sales MoMUS retail trade rose by 0.6 percent month-over-month in March 2018, recovering from a 0.1 percent drop in February and beating market expectations of a 0.4 percent gain. It was the first month of increase since November, mainly boosted by purchases of motor vehicles. Considering the first quarter of the year, retail sales went up 0.2 percent.2018-04-16T12:33:59
