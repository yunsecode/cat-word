Title: US Trade Deficit Widens in April
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit Widens in AprilUnited States Balance of TradeThe US trade gap widened to USD 37.4 billion in April of 2016 from a downwardly revised USD 35.5 billion deficit in the previous month while below market expectations of USD 41.3 billion. Exports increased 1.5 percent to the largest since December 2015, mainly driven by industrial supplies and materials sales. Imports rose at a faster 2.1 percent, due to higher purchases of capital goods.2016-06-03T13:45:44
