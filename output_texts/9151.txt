Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 7th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 7th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States decreased by 8.496 million barrels in the week ended July 26th of 2019, after a 10.835 million plunge in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.588 million drop. Meantime, gasoline inventories fell 1.791 million, higher than a 0.226 million decrease in the prior week and missing market consensus of a 2.167 million decline.2019-07-31T14:32:00
