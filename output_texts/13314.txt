Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down to 93.5 in June
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down to 93.5 in JuneUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment index for the United States came in at 93.5 in June 2016, down from a preliminary reading of 94.3 and a final of 94.7 in the previous month. Consumers were less optimistic about current conditions and future economic prospects.2016-06-24T15:11:49
