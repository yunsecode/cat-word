Title: Wall Street Opens Mostly Lower Ahead Of Fed
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Mostly Lower Ahead Of FedUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened mostly in the red on Tuesday led by losses in consumer staples sector as risk aversion increased amid concerns about a potential UK exit from the European Union and ahead of the Fed decision on interest rates on Wednesday. The Dow Jones lost 0.16% or 30 points, the S&P 500 fell 0.15% or 3 points and the Nasdaq traded near the flatline at 4850 points around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2016-06-14T14:49:43
