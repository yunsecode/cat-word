Title: Chicago PMI Falls for 2nd Month
Tag: Chicago PMI

Chicago PMI Falls for 2nd MonthUnited States Chicago PMIThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer in the US fell for the second month to 58.2 in November of 2020 from 61.1 in October and below forecasts of 59. Still, figures pointed to a strong expansion in Chicago business activity. Among the main five indicators, new orders and production declined, while supplier deliveries saw the largest gain.2020-11-30T15:02:00
