Title: US Consumer Sentiment Beats Expectations
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Beats ExpectationsUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States came in at 94.3 in June 2016, down from 94.7 in the previous month, but beating market expectations of 94. Consumers were less optimistic due to increased concerns about future economic prospects, while assessments of current economic conditions improved to its highest level since July 2005, preliminary estimates showed. 2016-06-10T15:04:05
