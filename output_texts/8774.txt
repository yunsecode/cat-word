Title: US Mortgage Applications Rebound: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rebound: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States increased 1.5 percent in the week ended February 21st 2020, after a 6.4 percent drop in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home rose 5.7 percent while refinance applications went down 0.8 percent. Meantime, the average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 4bps to 3.73 percent.2020-02-26T12:03:15.227
