Title: US Stocks End Higher Nudged By Yellen’s Testimony
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Higher Nudged By Yellen’s TestimonyUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Wednesday, as oil advanced above $45 a barrel and Chairman Janet Yellen signaled the Fed will not rush to hike interest rates as inflation remains below target. Yellen’s testimony to Congress showed that the Fed is likely to start reducing its balance sheet this year as the US economy continues to expand at a moderate pace. The Dow Jones climbed 123 points or 0.6 percent to 21532, booking a new record. The S&P 500 jumped 18 points or 0.7 percent to 2443. The Nasdaq advanced 68 points or 1.1 percent to 6261. 2017-07-12T20:04:09
