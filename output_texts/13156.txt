Title: US Stocks Open Higher Amid Rising Oil Prices
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Higher Amid Rising Oil PricesUnited States Stock MarketUS equities traded higher on Monday, despite weak productivity data for the second quarter of the year, as a rise in oil prices boosted sentiment. The Dow Jones Industrial Average added 34 points, or 0.2%, the S&P 500 index gained 3 points, or 0.1%; and the Nasdaq Composite Index advanced 10 point, or 0.2% around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2016-08-09T14:48:35
