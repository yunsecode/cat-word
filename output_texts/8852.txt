Title: Dollar Hovers Near 2-Month High
Tag: Currency

Dollar Hovers Near 2-Month High United States CurrencyThe dollar index held near a two-month high on Wednesday, after the US Federal Reserve held interest rates steady, as widely expected, although it adjusted the interest on excess reserves rate to 1.60% from 1.55%. Officials expect that moving the IOER higher it will also boost the funds rate. The dollar index has been rising on worries about the potential economic impact of the coronavirus. The DXY was up 0.1% to 98.111 around 02:10 PM NY time.2020-01-29T19:12:00
