US Crude Stocks Fall for 4th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 7.169 million barrels in the week ending August 27th, a fourth consecutive period of declines and compared with market forecasts of a 3.088 million drop, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed.
