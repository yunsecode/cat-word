Title: DXY Eases from 20-Year Highs after Jobs Report
Tag: Currency

DXY Eases from 20-Year Highs after Jobs ReportUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index fell to 109.2 on Friday, below 20-year highs of near 110 hit earlier this week after the August jobs report showed the labor market may be cooling, easing the pressure on the Fed to raise rates aggressively. US economy added 315K in August, well below the downwardly revised 526K gain in July but more than forecasts of 300K. At the same time, the jobless rate rose unexpectedly to a 6-month high of 3.7% and the labour force participation rate increased to 62.4%, the highest since March; while wages rose less than expected. Fed officials recently signaled that the fight against inflation would take priority over growth concerns, with markets currently priced for another supersized 75 basis point rate increase in September.2022-09-02T12:38:00
