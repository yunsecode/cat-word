Title: ISM New York Index Rises Sharply in April
Tag: Ism New York Index

ISM New York Index Rises Sharply in AprilUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM NY Current Business Conditions Index increased sharply to 57 in April 2016 from 50.4 in the previous month. It was the highest reading since December as gauge of employment (49.9 from 40.9) and prices paid (48.1 from 44.7) rose. In contrast, quantity of purchases (48.2 from 57.1), and current (50 from 55.3) and expected (56 from 57.5) revenues fell. The 6-month outlook worsened to a 7-year low of 53.1 from 65 in March.2016-05-03T14:53:41
