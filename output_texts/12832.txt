Title: US Initial Jobless Claims at 3-Month High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Initial Jobless Claims at 3-Month HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits rose by 7 thousand to 265 thousand in the week ended October 29th, above market expectations of 255 thousand and reaching the highest figure since August 6th. However, it is the 87th straight week of initial claims below 300 thousand, the longest streak since 1970. 2016-11-03T12:35:59
