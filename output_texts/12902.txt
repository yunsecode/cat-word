Title: US Inflation Rate at Nearly 2-Year High in September
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate at Nearly 2-Year High in SeptemberUnited States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the US went up 1.5% yoy in September, higher than 1.1% in August and in line with market expectations. It is the highest inflation rate since October of 2014 boosted by higher shelter cost and a smaller drop in energy prices while food cost fell for the first time in more than six years.
