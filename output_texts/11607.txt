Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States declined 0.4 percent in the week ending September 29th 2017, after falling 0.5 percent in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications dropped 1.8 percent while applications to purchase a home rose 1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged up by 1bps to 4.12 percent. 2017-10-04T11:03:28
