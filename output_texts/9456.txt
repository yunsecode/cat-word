US Factory Growth Beats Forecasts: ISMUnited States Business ConfidenceThe ISM Manufacturing PMI in the US rose to 55.3 in March of 2019 from 54.2 in February which was the lowest reading since November of 2016. It compares with market expectations of 54.5. Faster increases were seen for new orders, production and employment.2019-04-01T14:06:00
