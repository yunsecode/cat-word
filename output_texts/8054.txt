Title: US Jobless Rate Expected to Fall to 12.3%
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Expected to Fall to 12.3%United States Unemployment RateThe unemployment rate in the US is expected to fall to 12.3 percent in June of 2020 from 13.3 percent in May and a record high of 14.7 percent in April as many businesses reponed after weeks of COVID-19 lockdowns. Still, official figures may be far off the reality as many people are being classified as employed even though they are absent from work. It is estimated that considering absent from work as unemployed, the jobless rate would reach 16.3 percent in May and 19.7 percent in April. 2020-07-02T07:09:00
