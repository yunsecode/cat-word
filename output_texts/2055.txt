Title: DXY Weakens Toward 112
Tag: Currency

DXY Weakens Toward 112United States CurrencyThe dollar index pulled back toward 112 on Friday and was on course for a 0.8% weekly drop, as investors scaled back bets on aggressive monetary tightening after a WSJ report saying that some Fed officials are concerned about overtightening with large rate hikes. The Federal Reserve is still expected to lift the fed funds rate by 75 bps in November, the fourth straight rate hike of that magnitude, but the December increase could be smaller. Philadelphia Fed President Patrick Harker said the central bank is likely to raise rates to “well above” 4% this year and hold them at restrictive levels for a while.2022-10-21T17:35:58.793
