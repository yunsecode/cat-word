Title: US Crude Inventories Jump for 1st Time in 6 Weeks
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Jump for 1st Time in 6 WeeksUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories jumped by 4.351 million barrels in the week ended January 15th, 2021, the first increase in six weeks and compared to market forecasts of a 1.167 million fall, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories decreased by 0.26 million barrels, while markets had forecast 2.771 million increase.2021-01-22T16:04:00
