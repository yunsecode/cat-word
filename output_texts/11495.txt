Title: US Labour Costs Rise 0.5% in Q3
Tag: Labour Costs

US Labour Costs Rise 0.5% in Q3United States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the US nonfarm business sector rose an annualized 0.5 percent on quarter in the third quarter of 2017, higher than an upwardly revised 0.3 percent increase in the second quarter and in line with market expectations, preliminary estimates showed. It reflects a 3.5 percent increase in hourly compensation and a 3.0 percent increase in productivity. Unit labor costs decreased 0.1 percent over the last four quarters.2017-11-02T12:38:47
