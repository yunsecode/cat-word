Title: Treasury Yields Edge Slightly Lower after FOMC
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Treasury Yields Edge Slightly Lower after FOMCUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note edged slightly lower to 3.9% on Wednesday after FOMC minutes showed no surprises by reiterating Fed's commitment to bring inflation down and to raise interest rates further, offering no changes on the Fed's tightening plans. Attention now turns to the US CPI report due Thursday. Fresh data showed producer prices increased more than expected in September, reinforcing the narrative that the US central bank will keep interest rates higher for longer to cool an overheated economy. 2022-10-12T18:24:00
