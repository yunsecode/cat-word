Title: US New Home Sales Rise 10.7%
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Rise 10.7%United States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in October 2015 were at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 495,000, up 10.7 percent from last month but below market expectations. The inventory of properties for sale reached the highest since early 2010 while both median and average prices decreased. 2015-11-25T15:07:03
