Title: US Wages Rise 0.3% MoM in July as Expected
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Rise 0.3% MoM in July as ExpectedUnited States Average Hourly EarningsUS average hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls rose by 7 cents, or 0.3 percent, to USD 27.05 in July 2018 and compared to a downwardly revised 0.1 percent gain in June. Figures came in line with market expectations. Over the year, average hourly earnings have increased by 71 cents, or 2.7 percent. Average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees increased by 3 cents to USD 22.65 in July.2018-08-03T12:42:00
