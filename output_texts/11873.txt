Title: Overseas Investors Bought $57.3B Of US Assets In May
Tag: Capital Flows

Overseas Investors Bought $57.3B Of US Assets In MayUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought USD 57.3 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments in May of 2017 after buying an upwardly revised USD 74.4 billion in April. Meanwhile, foreigners bought USD 91.9 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying USD 9.7 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors bought USD 46.4 billion of US treasuries, after selling USD 22.5 billion in April.2017-07-18T20:05:02.02
