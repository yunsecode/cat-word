Title: US Manufacturing Growth Slows To 9-Month Low: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing Growth Slows To 9-Month Low: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe seasonally adjusted IHS Markit final US Manufacturing PMI came in at 52 in June 2017, slightly below the earlier flash estimate of 52.1 and compared with May's final reading of 52.7. The figure pointed to the weakest pace of expansion since September last year, as output, new business and employment growth slowed.2017-07-03T13:48:51
