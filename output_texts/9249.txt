Title: US MBA Mortgage Applications Fall
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US MBA Mortgage Applications Fall United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 3.3 percent in the week ended May 24th 2019, following a 2.4 percent gain in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications declined 6.0 percent and applications to purchase a home dropped 1.4 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was unchanged at 4.33 percent.2019-05-29T11:01:00
