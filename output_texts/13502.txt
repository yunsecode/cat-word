Title: US Durable Goods Orders Below Expectations
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Below ExpectationsUnited States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods increased 0.8 percent in March from February of 2016, rebounding from an upwardly revised 3.1 percent fall in the previous period but below market forecasts of a 1.8 percent rise. Excluding transportation, new orders dropped 0.2 percent and without defense orders fell 1 percent.2016-04-26T13:34:18
