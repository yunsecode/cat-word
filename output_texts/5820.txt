Title: Dollar Struggles on Dovish Fed
Tag: Currency

Dollar Struggles on Dovish FedUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index lost further ground on Thursday, bottomed around the 91.70 level as signs of diminished enthusiasm on the US reflation trade along with a dovish stance from the Federal Reserve capped any upside momentum for the greenback. Investors seem to have acknowledged that the Fed just wanted to signal it was ready to deal with higher inflation without taking any steps to pull back its crisis-era stimulus. A slew of economic data, including somewhat worse-than-expected weekly jobless claims numbers and disappointing durable goods orders, also kept sentiment in check. 2021-06-24T12:59:00
