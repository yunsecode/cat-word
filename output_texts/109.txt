Title: US Wholesale Inventories Down for 3rd Month
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Down for 3rd MonthUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US decreased 0.1% month-over-month in May 2023, following a 0.3% drop in April, preliminary estimates showed. Inventories fell for nondurables by 1%, after a 1.7% decline in the previous period, but went up by 0.5% for durables, following 0.6% increase. Compared to a year earlier, wholesale inventories rose 3.6%.19 days ago
