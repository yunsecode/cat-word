Title: US Wholesale Inventories Rise Faster in August
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Rise Faster in AugustUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US increased 1.2 percent month-over-month to $731 billion in August of 2021, following a 0.6 percent rise in July, a preliminary estimate showed. It was the 13th consecutive month of gains, amid increases in both durable goods (1.1 percent vs 1.2 percent in July) and non-durable goods inventories (1.3 percent vs -0.3 percent). On a yearly basis, wholesale inventories advanced 12.3 percent in August.2021-09-28T12:34:00
