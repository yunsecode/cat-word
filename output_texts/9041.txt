Title: US Jobless Claims Rise in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits increased by 5 thousand to 218 thousand in the week ended October 26th from the previous week’s revised level of 213 thousand and compared with market expectations of 215 thousand. According to unadjusted data, the biggest rises were seen in California (+2,317); Virginia (+1,867); Georgia (+1,621) and New York (+1,179) while the largest declines were reported in Tennessee (-882) and Michigan (-752). 2019-10-31T12:32:00
