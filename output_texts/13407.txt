Title: US Jobless Claims Drop to 268K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Drop to 268KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 10,000 to 268,000 in the week ended May 21st compared to unrevised 278,000 in the previous period. Claims fell for the second time in five weeks, below market expectations of 275,000. It is the 64th consecutive week initial claims are below 300,000, the level associated with a healthy jobs market. 2016-05-26T13:40:12
