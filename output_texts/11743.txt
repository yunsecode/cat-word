Title: US Jobless Claims Rise Slightly to 234K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise Slightly to 234KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 2 thousand to 234 thousand in the week ended August 19th from the previous week's unrevised level of 232 thousand. It compares with market expectations of 238 thousand. The 4-week moving average which removes week-to-week volatility went down by 2.750 thousand to 237.750 thousand, the lowest since May.2017-08-24T12:32:50
