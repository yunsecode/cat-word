Title: US Retail Sales Drops 0.3% in August
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Drops 0.3% in AugustUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States declined 0.3 percent in August from July of 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.1 percent gain in the previous period and worse than market expectations of a 0.1 percent fall. It is first decline in 5 months as sales dropped for almost all stores except grocers and clothing. Biggest decreases were observed at auto dealers (-0.9 percent), gas stations (-0.8 percent), home-improvement centers (-1.4 percent), department stores (-0.6 percent).  Excluding autos, retail sales edged down 0.1 percent.2016-09-15T12:37:39
