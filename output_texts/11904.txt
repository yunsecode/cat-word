Title: US Jobless Rate Unexpectedly Rises To 4.4% In June
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Unexpectedly Rises To 4.4% In JuneUnited States Unemployment RateUS unemployment rate rose to 4.4 percent in June 2017 from the previous month's 16-year low of 4.3 percent and above market expectations of 4.3 percent. The number of unemployed persons was little changed at 7.0 million while the labor force participation rate edged up to 62.8 percent.2017-07-07T12:30:53
