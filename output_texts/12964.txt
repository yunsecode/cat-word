Title: US Chicago PMI Beats Expectations in September
Tag: Chicago Pmi

US Chicago PMI Beats Expectations in SeptemberUnited States Chicago PmiThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer increased by 2.7 points to 54.2 in September 2016 from 51.5 in August, beating market expectations of 52. It was the first rise in three months, driven by a sharp gain in production, which rose 7.3 points to 59.8, the highest since January 2016. Meanwhile, new orders and order backlogs were little changed and employment fell. 2016-09-30T13:53:51
