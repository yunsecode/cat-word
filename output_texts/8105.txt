Dollar Appreciates on Safe-Haven DemandUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index appreciated to 97.6 against a basket of currencies on Friday, close to levels not seen since the beginning of June as investors digest fears of a second wave of coronavirus infections and mixed economic data released during the week, including disappointing jobless claims figures. For the week, the dollar rose 0.5%.2020-06-19T20:11:00
