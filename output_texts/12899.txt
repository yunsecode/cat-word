Title: US Homebuilder Sentiment Falls in October
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Homebuilder Sentiment Falls in OctoberUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States fell to 63 in October of 2016 from 65 in September and in line with market expectations. Current single-family home sales fell 2 points to 69 and buyer traffic dropped 1 points to 46 while sales expectations over the next six months edged up 1 point to 72.
