Title: US PCE Price Index Stable in August
Tag: PCE Price Index Monthly Change

US PCE Price Index Stable in AugustUnited States PCE Price Index Monthly ChangeThe Personal Consumption Expenditures Price Index in the United States remained unchanged at 109.77 Index Points in August of 2015 from the previous month as purchases of goods decreased 0.4 percent and services increased 0.2 percent. Excluding food and energy, PCE prices edged up 0.1 percent. 2015-09-28T13:45:31
