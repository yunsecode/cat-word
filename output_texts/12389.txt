Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States decreased 2 percent in the week ended February 17th 2017, following a 3.7 percent drop in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications declined 1 percent and applications to purchase a home went down 2.8 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates increased by 4bps to 4.36 percent.2017-02-22T12:04:15.54
