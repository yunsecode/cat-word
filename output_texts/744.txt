Title: US Business Inventories Rise Less than Forecast
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Rise Less than ForecastUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers and trade inventories in the US rose by 0.2% from a month earlier in February 2023, following an upwardly revised 0.2% fall in the prior month and slightly below market forecasts of a 0.3% increase. Higher stocks at retailers (0.7% vs flat reading in January) and merchant wholesalers (0.1% vs -0.6%) outweighed reduced inventories at manufacturers (-0.1% vs -0.1%).
