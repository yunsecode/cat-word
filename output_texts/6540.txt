Title: US Stock Futures Edge Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Edge LowerUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures traded slightly in the red on Wednesday as traders await the final passage of the $1.9 trillion COVID-19 relief bill later in the day. At the same time, worries over an overheated economy and higher inflation grew, due to the massive stimulus and an initiative on larger infrastructure spending. Meanwhile, a survey published by the Commerce Department’s Census Bureau on Tuesday showed that nearly a third of US households receiving unemployment benefits are struggling to cover their routine expenses. In business news, Chevron reportedly outlined a plan to expand oil and gas output through 2025, but without spending significantly higher and pledged to limit its carbon emission.
