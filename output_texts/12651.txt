Title: US Inflation Rate Rises To Fresh 2-Year High
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Rises To Fresh 2-Year HighUnited States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States increased by 1.7 percent year-on-year in November 2016, following a 1.6 percent rise in October and in line with market expectations. It was the highest inflation rate since October 2014, mainly boosted by higher energy cost while food prices continued to fall. On a monthly basis, consumer prices rose by 0.2 percent after growing by 0.4 percent in October.2016-12-15T13:33:21
