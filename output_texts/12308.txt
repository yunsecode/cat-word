US Crude Oil Stocks Unexpectedly FallUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States declined by 0.237 million barrels in the week ended March 10th, 2017, following an 8.2 million increase in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 3.7 million rise. It is the first decline in ten weeks. Gasoline stocks fell by 3.1 million, compared to expectations of a 1.9 million drop.2017-03-15T14:34:22.743
