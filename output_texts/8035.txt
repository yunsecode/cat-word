Title: US Stock Futures Rise
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures RiseUnited States Stock MarketUS stock index futures rose late Monday, building on gains in the previous session driven by a strong performance in the technology sector. Investors took comfort in signs that the American economy was recovering despite risks tied to the spread of the new virus domestically. US services industry activity rebounded sharply in June, almost returning to its pre-coronavirus pandemic level, but a record surge of COVID-19 cases this month could hamper reopening plans. On Monday, the Dow Jones gained 460 points or 1.8% to 26,287. The S&P 500 jumped 50 points or 1.6% to 3180. The Nasdaq climbed 226 points or 2.2% to a new record of 10,434.2020-07-07T01:12:18.37
