Title: US Stocks Rise on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Monday as US-China trade tensions eased after President Trump tweeted "President Xi of China, and I, are working together to give massive Chinese phone company, ZTE, a way to get back into business, fast. Too many jobs in China lost. Commerce Department has been instructed to get it done!". The Dow Jones was up 121 points or 0.5%; the S&P 500 rose 9 points or 0.3%; and the Nasdaq traded 33 points or 0.4% higher around 10 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-05-14T13:45:44
