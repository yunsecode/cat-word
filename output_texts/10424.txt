Title: US Pending Home Sales Drop 2.5% YoY
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Drop 2.5% YoYUnited States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned homes in the United States shrank 2.5 percent year-on-year in June of 2018, following a 2.2 percent drop in May. It is the sixth consecutive month of annual declines in pending home sales. Sales fell in the the West (-5.6 percent), the Northeast (-4.1 percent), the Midwest (-2.1 percent) and the South (-0.3 percent). On a month-over-month basis, sales went up 0.9 percent, rebounding from a 0.5 percent drop in May and compared to market expectations of a 0.1 percent gain. 2018-07-30T14:08:44.367
