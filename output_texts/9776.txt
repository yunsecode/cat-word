Title: US Consumer Credit Growth Beats Expectations
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Growth Beats ExpectationsUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States went up by USD 22.2 billion in November 2018, down from a downwardly revised USD 24.9 billion gain in the previous month but above market expectations of a USD 17.5 billion rise. Revolving credit including credit card borrowing climbed USD 4.8 billion, compared to a USD 9.4 billion advance in October. Meantime, non-revolving credit including loans for education and automobiles jumped by USD 17.3 billion, after rising USD 15.7 billion in the prior month.2019-01-08T20:06:10.54
