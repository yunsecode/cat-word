Title: US Trade Deficit Largest since 2008
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit Largest since 2008United States Balance of TradeThe US trade deficit widened to USD 59.8 billion in December of 2018 from an upwardly revised USD 50.3 billion in the previous month and compared with market expectations of a USD 57.9 billion gap. It is the largest deficit since October of 2008 as exports declined for the third month and imports recovered. In 2018, the country's trade gap widened to a 10-year high, with the goods gap with China jumping to a record high despite tariffs on USD 250 billion worth of Chinese imports. 2019-03-06T13:33:00
