Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Sharply Higher
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Sharply Higher United States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States was revised sharply higher to 95.7 in January of 2018 from a preliminary of 94.4, beating market expectations of 95. It compares with 95.9 in December. Future expectations increased more than anticipated amid higher confidence in future job security and growth in wages as well as financial assets.2018-02-02T15:10:56.043
