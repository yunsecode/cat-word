Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 4th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 4th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 1.379 million barrels in the week ended November 15th 2019, following a 2.219 million gain in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 1.543 million rise, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories went up by 1.756 million barrels after increasing by 1.861 million in the previous week and compared with market consensus of a 0.87 million gain.2019-11-20T15:31:17.87
