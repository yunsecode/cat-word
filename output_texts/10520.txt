Title: US Services Growth at 4-Month High: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Growth at 4-Month High: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States rose to 59.1 in June of 2018 from 58.6 in May, beating market forecasts of 58.3. It is the highest reading since February as business activity and new orders rose faster and price pressures eased. Companies continue to be optimistic about business conditions and the overall economy although there is a continuing concern relating to tariffs, capacity constraints and delivery.2018-07-05T14:06:58.94
