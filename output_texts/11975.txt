Title: US Housing Starts At 8-Month Low
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts At 8-Month LowUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States slumped 5.5 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1092 thousand in May of 2017, following a downwardly revised 1156 thousand in the previous month and compared to market expectations of a 4.1 percent rise. It is the weakest construction activity in eight months as starts plunged in the Midwest and the South. 2017-06-16T12:32:44.233
