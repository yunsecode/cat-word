Title: US Stocks Open Higher as Oil Rises
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Higher as Oil RisesUnited States Stock MarketMarkets in the United States opened in the green on Tuesday after oil prices rebounded and traded above $30 a barrel. The Dow Jones was up 151 points or 0.95%, the S&P 500 added 0.45% and the Nasdaq gained 0.14% around 15 minutes after the open.  2016-01-26T14:47:06
