Title: US Stocks Rise on GDP, Canada Talks
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on GDP, Canada TalksUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded higher on Wednesday morning with the S&P and the Nasdaq hitting new record highs after the GDP growth for Q2 was revised higher to 4.2% from 4.1% and the US and Canada started NAFTA negotiations. The Dow Jones went up 0.3%, the S&P 500 gained 0.5% and the Nasdaq traded 0.8% higher around 11:35 AM NY time. 2018-08-29T15:14:00
