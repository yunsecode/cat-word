Title: US Corporate Profits Hit Record High in Q2
Tag: Corporate Profits

US Corporate Profits Hit Record High in Q2United States Corporate ProfitsCorporate profits in the United States rose 9.1 percent to a fresh record high of USD 2.62 trillion in the second quarter of 2022, following a 4.9 percent drop in the previous period. Net cash flow with inventory valuation adjustment, the internal funds available to corporations for investment, rose 5.8 percent to $3.34 trillion, while net dividends fell 0.2 percent to $1.47 trillion. Meanwhile, undistributed profits skyrocketed 23.8 percent to $1.15 trillion.2022-08-25T12:42:00
