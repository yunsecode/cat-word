Title: US Chicago Fed National Activity Index Picks Up
Tag: Chicago Fed National Activity Index

US Chicago Fed National Activity Index Picks UpUnited States Chicago Fed National Activity IndexThe Chicago Fed National Activity Index increased to 0.53 in July of 2021 from a downwardly revised -0.01 in June, pointing to a pickup in overall business activity. Production-related indicators contributed +0.38, up from -0.09 in June. The contribution of the sales, orders, and inventories category increased to +0.02 from -0.06; the contribution of the employment, unemployment, and hours category rose to +0.30 from +0.14; while the personal consumption and housing category contributed -0.15,down from +0.01 in June. The index’s three-month moving average, CFNAI-MA3, moved up to +0.23 in July from +0.01 in June.2021-08-23T12:37:00
