US Core PCE Inflation Tops ForecastUnited States Core PCE Price Index Annual ChangeThe US core PCE price index, the Federal Reserve’s preferred gauge to measure inflation, rose by 4.7% annually in April of 2023, accelerating from 4.6% in the previous month.
