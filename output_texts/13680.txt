US Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index Below Estimates United States Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index The Richmond Fed Manufacturing Activity Index went down to -4 in February of 2016 from 2 in the previous month, missing market expectations of 2. Manufacturing output in the US fifth district, including the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia contracted for the first time in three months: volume of new orders (-6 from 4 in January) and shipments (-11 from -6) fell while employment was steady at 9.2016-02-23T15:10:18
