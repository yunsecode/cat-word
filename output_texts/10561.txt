Title: US Home Price Growth Eases in April: Case-Shiller
Tag: Case Shiller Home Price Index YoY

US Home Price Growth Eases in April: Case-ShillerUnited States Case Shiller Home Price Index YoYThe S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller 20-City Composite Home Price Index in the US rose 6.6 percent year-on-year in April 2018, easing from a downwardly revised 6.7 percent increase in March and missing market expectations of a 6.8 percent advance. Seattle recorded the biggest increase (13.1 percent), followed by Las Vegas (12.7 percent) and San Francisco (10.9 percent). Meanwhile, the national index, covering all nine US census divisions rose 6.4 percent, down from 6.5 percent in the previous month.2018-06-26T13:04:00
