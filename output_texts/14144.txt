US Economy Expands 2.3% in Q2United States Gdp Growth RateUS economy grew 2.3 percent in the second quarter of 2015 compared to 0.6 percent expansion reported in the previous three month period, according to the advanced estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.
