Title: US Wages Rise at Faster Pace in December
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Rise at Faster Pace in DecemberUnited States Average Hourly EarningsUS average hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls rose by 9 cents, or 0.3 percent, to USD 26.63 in December 2017 after gaining 0.1 percent in the prior month. Over the year, average hourly earnings have risen by 65 cents, or 2.5 percent, following a 2.4 percent gain in November. Average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees increased by 7 cents to USD 22.30 in December.2018-01-05T14:02:24.2
