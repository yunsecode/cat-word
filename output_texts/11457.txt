Title: US Retail Sales Rise 0.2% in October
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Rise 0.2% in OctoberUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States rose 0.2 percent month-over-month in October of 2017, above market expectations of no growth and following a upwardly revised 1.9 percent growth in September. 9 of 13 major retail categories showed month-over-month increases.
