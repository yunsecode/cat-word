Title: US Stocks Rebound
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks ReboundUnited States Stock MarketMarkets in the US closed in the green on Thursday, recovering from a selloff in the previous day, led by gains in energy shares amid higher oil prices while comments from Fed policymakers suggested a more slower pace of tightening. The Dow Jones added 227 points or 1.41%, the S&P 500 gained 32 points or 1.67% and the Nasdaq closed 89 points or 1.97% higher. 2016-01-14T21:31:01.187
