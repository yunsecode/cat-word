Title: US Jobless Claims Unexpectedly Fall to Near 49-Year Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Unexpectedly Fall to Near 49-Year LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits went down by 10 thousand to 203 thousand in the week ending September 1 from the previous week's unrevised level of 213 thousand while markets had expected a slight increase to 214 thousand. This is the lowest level since December 6,1969 when it was 202,000. However, the Labor Day holiday was observed on Monday which could have influenced the estimates. According to unadjusted data, the largest declines were reported in Michigan (-1,606), Florida (-890) and New York (-837) while the biggest rises were seen in Indiana (+1,085), Washington (+665), Illinois (+597) and Ohio (+466).
