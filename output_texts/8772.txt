Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 5th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 5th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 0.452 million barrels in the week ended February 21st of 2020, following a 0.414 million gain in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.005 million gain, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went down by 2.691 million barrels after decreasing by 1.971 million in the previous week and compared with market consensus of a 2.245 million decline.2020-02-26T15:33:00
