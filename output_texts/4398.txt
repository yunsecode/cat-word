Title: US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised Higher
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised HigherUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI was revised higher to 55.5 in January of 2022 from a preliminary of 55, but continued to point to the weakest rise in factory activity since October of 2020, as output growth was muted. Demand conditions also softened further, with new orders rising at the slowest pace since September 2020. Muted client demand was reflected in only a fractional increase in employment. The softer rise in new orders allowed firms to partially work through backlogs of work, which expanded at the slowest pace for 11 months. Nonetheless, firms were at their most upbeat regarding the outlook for output since November 2020. Meanwhile, inflationary pressures remained marked. The rate of cost inflation eased to the softest for eight months, however, as firms also moderated the pace at which selling prices increased.2022-02-01T14:49:36.893
