Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 4-Month High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 4-Month HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan consumer sentiment for the US increased to 63.9 in June of 2023, the highest in four months, from 59.2 in May,  preliminary figures showed. Figures beat forecasts of 60, reflecting greater optimism as inflation eased and policymakers resolved the debt ceiling crisis. "Consumer sentiment is now 28% above the historic low from a year ago and may be resuming its upward trajectory since then", Surveys of Consumers Director Joanne Hsu said. In June, improvements were seen in both current economic conditions (68 vs 64.9) and consumer expectations (61.3 vs 55.4). Also, year-ahead inflation expectations receded for the second consecutive month to 3.3%, the lowest since March 2021, from 4.2% in May. In contrast, long-run inflation expectations were little changed from May at 3%.2023-06-16T14:04:00
