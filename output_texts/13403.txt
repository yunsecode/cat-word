Title: US GDP Growth Revised Up to 0.8% in Q1
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Revised Up to 0.8% in Q1United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy expanded an annualized 0.8 percent on quarter in the first three months of 2016, better than a 0.5 percent increase initially estimated, according to the second estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. Consumption continued to boost growth, spending on home building increased more than expected and the drag from net trade and inventories was smaller. In contrast, business investment fell faster than anticipated.2016-05-27T13:36:57
