Title: US House Prices Rise 5.7% YoY in June: Case-Shiller
Tag: Case Shiller Home Price Index YoY

US House Prices Rise 5.7% YoY in June: Case-ShillerUnited States Case Shiller Home Price Index YoYThe S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller composite home price index of 20 metropolitan areas in the US rose 5.7 percent year-on-year in June of 2017, the same as in May and in line with market expectations. Prices increased the most in Seattle (13.4 percent), Portland (8.2 percent) and Dallas (7.7 percent). Meanwhile, the national index, covering all nine US census divisions went up 5.8 percent, following a 5.7 percent gain in the prior month. 2017-08-29T13:05:21
