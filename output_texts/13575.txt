Title: US Mortgage Applications Down 1%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Down 1%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 1 percent in the week ended March 25th 2016 from the previous period, following a 3.3 percent drop in the last two weeks while fixed 30-year mortgage rates edged up 1 bps to 3.94 percent, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home rose 2.1 percent and refinance applications shrank 3.3 percent.2016-03-30T12:04:14.873
