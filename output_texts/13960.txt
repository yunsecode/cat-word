Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsApplications for U.S. house mortgages fell 1.3 percent in the week ended November 6th, 2015, following a 0.8 percent drop in the previous period as fixed 30-year mortgage rates went up by 11bps to 4.12 percent, the highest since the beginning of August. Refinancing applications fell 2.2 percent while purchase applications edged up 0.1 percent.2015-11-11T12:05:41.877
