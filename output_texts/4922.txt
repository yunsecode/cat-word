Title: US Wages Rise at Slower 0.4% MoM in October
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Rise at Slower 0.4% MoM in OctoberUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings for all employees on US private nonfarm payrolls rose by 11 cents, or 0.4% over the month to $30.96 in October of 2021, matching market expectations and following a 0.6% rise in September and August. In October, average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees rose by 10 cents to $26.26. Over the past 12 months, average hourly earnings have increased by 4.9%, up from a 4.6% rise in the prior month and in line with market forecasts.2021-11-05T12:32:00
