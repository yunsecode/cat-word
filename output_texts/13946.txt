Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsApplications for U.S. house mortgages went up 6.2 percent in the week ended November 13th, 2015, after falling in the previous three weeks and despite the fact that fixed 30-year mortgage rates went up by 6bps to 4.18 percent, the highest since mid-July. Refinancing applications increased 2 percent while purchase applications surged 12 percent.2015-11-18T12:14:31
