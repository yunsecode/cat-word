Title: Dollar Languishes on Dovish Fed Bets
Tag: Currency

Dollar Languishes on Dovish Fed BetsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index held its recent decline to below 100 on Friday, sinking to its lowest levels in 15 months as signs that US inflation is on a downward trend raised hopes that the Federal Reserve is nearing the end of its current monetary policy tightening cycle. Latest data showed that US consumer and producer inflation rose less than anticipated in June even as the US labor market remained robust. The US central bank is still seen raising rates by 25 basis points in July, while traders scaled back bets of further rate increases after this year. Market pricing also suggests that the Fed could start cutting rates next year. The dollar plunged to over one-year lows against the euro and sterling, while it declined to multi-month lows against the yen and antipodean currencies.3 days ago
