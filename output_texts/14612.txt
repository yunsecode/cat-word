Title: US Jobless Claims Down to 7-Week Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Down to 7-Week LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending November 16, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 323,000, a decrease of 21,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 344,000. The 4-week moving average was 338,500, a decrease of 6,750 from the previous week's revised average of 345,250.2013-11-21T13:35:09.5
