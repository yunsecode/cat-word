Title: US Retail Sales Rise for 3rd Straight Month
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Rise for 3rd Straight MonthUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States advanced 0.6 percent in June from May 2016, following a downwardly revised 0.2 percent gain in the previous period and beating market expectations of 0.1 percent rise. It was the third straight month of gains as sales of motor vehicles, furniture and building material rebounded. Retail sales, excluding automobiles, gasoline, building materials and food services, which correspond most closely with the consumer spending component of GDP, went up 0.5 percent last month after a 0.5 percent increase in May.2016-07-15T13:40:41
