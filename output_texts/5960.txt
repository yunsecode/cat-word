Title: US Treasury Yields Rise
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Rise United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note edged up to 1.62% on Friday, moving away from recent lows, amid prospects of a strong economic growth and concerns of high public debt. The latest claims report showed the labor market is recovering, with new claims falling to a fresh pre-pandemic low of 406K last week. Also, news showed President Biden will seek $6 trillion in US federal spending for the 2022 fiscal year, rising to $8.2 trillion by 2031. The new plan would push public debt to 117% of the GDP in 2031.
