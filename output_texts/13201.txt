Title: US Stocks End Mixed on Earnings, Oil Drop
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed on Earnings, Oil DropUnited States Stock MarketThe S&P 500 and the Nasdaq finished in the green on Thursday, while the Dow Jones closed lower, amid declines in oil prices while better-than-expected earnings' report from Facebook and an anticipation of results from tech Alphabet and Amazon.com boosted sentiment. The Dow Jones slipped 15.82 points, or 0.1%, dragged by shares of Boeing, which closed 2.2% lower. In contrast, the S&P 500 advanced 3.48 points, or 0.2%; and the Nasdaq rose 15.17 points, or 0.3%, to finish at 5,155, for its highest close since December last year.2016-07-28T21:36:01
