Title: US Services Growth Beat Forecasts: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Growth Beat Forecasts: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States jumped to 59.7 in February of 2019 from 56.7 in January, beating market expectations of 57.3. The reading pointed to the strongest expansion in the services sector in three months, as business activity, new orders and employment rose faster. Service providers remained mostly optimistic about overall business conditions and the economy but showed concerns about the uncertainty of tariffs, capacity constraints and employment resources.2019-03-05T15:05:00
