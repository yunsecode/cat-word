Title: US Business Inventories Above Expectations
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Above ExpectationsUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the US rose 1.4 percent from a month earlier in May of 2022, picking up from an upwardly revised 1.3 percent gain in the prior month and slightly above market expectations of a 1.3 percent increase. Stocks rose at a faster pace for manufacturers (1.3 percent vs 0.8 percent in April) and retailers (1.1 percent vs 0.8 percent), while inventory growth eased for merchant wholesalers (1.8 percent vs 2.3 percent). Year-on-year, business inventories jumped by 17.7 percent in May.
