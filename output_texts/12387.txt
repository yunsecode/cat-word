Title: Dow Hits Fresh All-Time High Ahead Of Fed Minutes
Tag: stock market

Dow Hits Fresh All-Time High Ahead Of Fed MinutesUnited States stock marketThe Dow Jones rose 24 points, or 0.1%, to a new all-time high of 20,766 around 12:30 PM NY time on Wednesday, ahead of minutes from the Federal Reserve, which could offer guidance on the timing and pace of rate increases. Meanwhile, the S&P 500 retreated 3 points, or 0.1%, to 2,362; and the Nasdaq declined 7 points, or 0.1%, to 5,859.2017-02-22T15:38:53
