Title: US Natural Gas Stocks Fall More than Expected: EIA
Tag: Natural Gas Stocks Change

US Natural Gas Stocks Fall More than Expected: EIAUnited States Natural Gas Stocks ChangeUS utilities pulled 71 bcf (billion cubic feet) of gas from storage during the week ended February 17, 2023, slightly more than market expectations of a 67 bcf drop. Still, it is much less than a decrease of 138 bcf in the same week last year and a five-year (2018-2022) average decline of 177 bcf s milder weather lead to lower heating demand for the fuel. Last week's decrease cut stockpiles to 2.195 trillion cubic feet (tcf), 395 bcf higher than last year at this time and 289 bcf above the five-year average of 1.906 tcf.2023-02-23T16:34:04.487
