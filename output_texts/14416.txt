Title: US Jobless Claims Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending August 16, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 298,000, a decrease of 14,000 from the previous week's revised level.
