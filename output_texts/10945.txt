Title: US Stocks Attempt to Recover
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Attempt to RecoverUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Friday, attempting to recover from a big sell-off in the previous session after the Chinese authorities responded to President Trump's $50 billion tariffs with plans to target 128 US products accounting for roughly $3 billion in imports. The Dow Jones added 93 points or 0.4%; the S&P 500 went up 7 points or 0.3%; the Nasdaq traded 8 points or 0.1% higher around 7 minutes after the opening bell.2018-03-23T13:45:47
