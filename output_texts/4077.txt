Title: US Imports Surge 1.2% to New Record
Tag: Imports

US Imports Surge 1.2% to New RecordUnited States ImportsImports to the United States increased 1.2 percent, or USD 3.8 billion, to a new record high of USD 314.1 billion in January of 2022. Purchases of goods were up USD 4.8 billion to USD 264.8 billion, due to imports of automotive vehicles, parts, and engines (USD 1.6 billion), industrial supplies and materials (USD 1.5 billion), particularly crude oil (USD 0.9 billion), natural gas (USD 0.6 billion) and copper (USD 0.6 billion). Also, imports grew for foods, feeds, and beverages (USD 1.4 billion) and capital goods (USD 1.1 billion). On the other hand, imports of services decreased USD 1.0 billion to USD 49.3 billion, led by transport (USD 0.8 billion) and travel (USD 0.5 billion).2022-03-08T13:42:00
