Title: US Jobless Claims Seen Dropping to 680K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Seen Dropping to 680KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsInitial jobless claims are seen falling to 680 thousand in the beginning of April, following an unexpected increase last week to 719 thousand. Claims hit 658 thousand in the March 20th week, their lowest level in a year, as more businesses reopened due to the vaccine rollout programme and the government's massive $1.9 trillion pandemic relief package. Meanwhile, continuing claims are seen declining to 3.65 million in the March 27th week from 3.79 million the week before, but remaining well above February 2020 levels.2021-04-08T10:32:00
