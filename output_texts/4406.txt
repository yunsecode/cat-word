Title: Chicago Business Barometer at 3-Month High
Tag: Chicago PMI

Chicago Business Barometer at 3-Month HighUnited States Chicago PMIThe Chicago PMI in the United States increased to 65.20 points in January from 64.30 points in December of 2021. It is the highest reading in 3 months, beating forecasts of 61.7. Order backlogs, employment and supplier deliveries all increased and prices slowed while production and orders fell.2022-01-31T18:55:22.927
