Title: US Goods Trade Gap Widens in June
Tag: Goods Trade Balance

US Goods Trade Gap Widens in JuneUnited States Goods Trade BalanceThe US goods trade deficit widened to USD 91.21 billion in June 2021, from USD 88.16 billion in the previous month and not far from an all-time high of USD 91.92 hit in March, the advance estimate showed. Exports edged up 0.3 percent, as increases in sales of industrial supplies (2.2 percent), automotive vehicles (1.7 percent) and consumer goods (0.7 percent) were partially offset by declines in foods, feeds, & beverages (-8.7 percent) and capital goods (-0.1 percent). Imports rose at a faster 1.5 percent, boosted by purchases of industrial supplies (8.5 percent), foods, feeds, & beverages (4.0 percent) and capital goods (1.1 percent). Meanwhile, purchases of vehicles and consumer goods were down 2.5 and 2.7 percent, respectively.2021-07-28T12:39:00
