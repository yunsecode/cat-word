Title: Dollar Trades Lower
Tag: Currency

Dollar Trades LowerUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded lower and below 95 on Thursday, close to levels not seen since early March as investors came to terms that US recovery will take longer as the coronavirus pandemic in the US is far from controlled and some states have rolled back reopening plans. Meanwhile, other countries including EU members seem to have passed the pick of infections and initiated new stimulus schemes to support further the economic recovery. Earlier in the session, the dollar briefly gained after the claims report showed initial claims rose for the first time since March.2020-07-23T14:13:50.4
