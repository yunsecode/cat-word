Title: US Jobless Claims Rise Less Than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise Less Than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 1,000 to 260,000 in the week ended September 10th compared with the previous week's unrevised level of 259,000 and below market expectations of 265,000. The four-week average, seen as a better measure of labor market trends as it removes week-to-week volatility, dropped to a five-week low of 260,750.2016-09-15T12:33:41.607
