Title: US Job Openings Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Job OffersJob openings in the United States increased by 149 thousand to 5.76 million in March of 2016 compared to an upwardly revised 5.61 million in the previous month and beating market expectations of 5.41 million. The number of positions waiting to be filled increased in professional and business services (+124,000), transportation, warehousing, and utilities (+35,000), and nondurable goods manufacturing(+29,000), while it decreased in retail trade (-80,000), educational services (-36,000), and wholesale trade (-35,000). 2016-05-10T15:09:51.253
