Title: Dollar Remains Subdued
Tag: Currency

Dollar Remains Subdued United States CurrencyThe dollar index consolidated slightly below the 105 mark, hovering at the lowest since late June, and erasing more than half of this year’s gains as prospects of a less aggressive tightening from the Federal Reserve spooked investors away from the greenback. The pledge by Fed Chair Powell to scale down the pace of rate increases has somewhat overshadowed last week's stronger-than-anticipated US jobs report. Money markets are pricing an 80% chance that the US central bank will hike rates by 50 basis points in December after delivering four successive 75 basis point rate increases. Still, investors see a loftier chance that the peak for the Fed funds will be higher than expected. The dollar was mostly steady against the euro and the British pound but it came under pressure against the Chinese yuan as bets of a swifter reopening of China's economy lent optimism to yuan bulls. 2022-12-05T11:15:00
