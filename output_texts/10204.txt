US House Price Growth Below Forecasts: Case-ShillerUnited States Case Shiller Home Price Index YoYThe S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller 20-City Composite Home Price Index in the US rose 5.9 percent from a year earlier in July 2018, following an upwardly revised 6.4 percent increase in June and missing market expectations of a 6.2 percent advance. Las Vegas recorded the biggest gain (13.7 percent), followed by Seattle (12.1 percent) and San Francisco (10.8 percent). Meanwhile, the national index, covering all nine US census divisions rose 6 percent in July, down from 6.2 percent in the previous month.2018-09-25T13:06:10.733
