Title: US Stocks Finish Mostly Lower As Oil Prices Drop
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Finish Mostly Lower As Oil Prices DropUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mostly lower on Monday amid a sharp decline in oil prices while investors await Fed’s Yellen speech on Friday for further clues on the timing of the next rate-hike. The Dow Jones lost 23.15 points or 0.12%, the S&P 500 finished near the flatline. Meantime, the Nasdaq rose 6.23 points or 0.12%.2016-08-22T21:19:19.21
