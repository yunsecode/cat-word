Title: US IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism At 8-Month Low
Tag: Economic Optimism Index

US IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism At 8-Month LowUnited States Economic Optimism IndexThe IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index declined 6.7 percent to 52.6 in December 2018, the lowest level since April and well below market consensus of 57.3. The Six-Month Economic Outlook, a measure of how consumers feel about the economy’s prospects in the next six months, dropped 12.8 percent to 46.4, the first negative reading since April and its lowest point in the Trump presidency. In addition, the Personal Financial Outlook, a measure of how Americans feel about their own finances in the next six months, fell 2.4 percent to 60.8; and Confidence in Federal Economic Policies, a proprietary IBD/TIPP measure of views on how government economic policies are working, declined 5.8 percent to 50.6.2018-12-04T15:12:00
