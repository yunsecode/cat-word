Title: Investors Pile into Government Debt
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Investors Pile into Government Debt United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note bottomed around 3.8%, a level not seen since early October, as investors continued to assess the outlook for monetary policy. Federal Reserve Vice Chair Lael Brainard signaled that the central bank could soon slow the pace of its interest rate increases, saying that policymakers had already done a significant job in taming inflation. Fed Governor Christopher Waller echoed a similar view but emphasized that the central bank is not close to a pause. Despite a higher degree of uncertainty regarding the Fed's rate path, this narrative of a possible policy pivot gained form on the heels of a softer-than-expected US inflation report, with money markets now pricing an 80% chance of a 50 bps hike in December.
