Title: US PCE Price Index Flat in July
Tag: PCE Price Index Monthly Change

US PCE Price Index Flat in July United States PCE Price Index Monthly ChangeThe price index for consumer spending in the United States remained unchanged in July of 2016, following a 0.1 percent rise in June. Increase in cost of services (+0.2 percent, the same as in June) was offset by 0.4 percent drop in prices of goods (no growth in June). Compared to the same month a year earlier, the PCE price index went up 0.8 percent, following 0.9 percent increase in June. Excluding food and energy, prices gained 0.1 percent month-over-month, unchanged from June. Year-on-year the core PCE price index increased 1.6 percent, also the same as in the previous month.
