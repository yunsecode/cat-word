Title: US Import Prices Post Biggest Drop in Over 2 Years
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Post Biggest Drop in Over 2 YearsUnited States Import Prices MoMUS import prices fell 0.4 percent month-over-month in June 2018, following an upwardly revised 0.9 percent jump in the previous month and missing market consensus of a 0.1 percent gain. The June decline was the largest monthly drop since February 2016 as import fuel prices decreased 0.7 percent, after a 6.1 percent advance in May, due to falling prices for both petroleum and natural gas. In addition, prices for nonfuel imports decreased 0.3 percent following a 0.2 percent increase in May, led by lower prices for foods, feeds, and beverages; consumer goods; capital goods; and automotive vehicles which more than offset higher prices for nonfuel industrial supplies and materials. Year-on-year, import prices went up 4.3 percent in June, after an upwardly revised 4.5 percent advance in May.2018-07-13T12:33:00
