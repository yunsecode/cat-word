Title: US Business Inventories Fall in January
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Fall in JanuaryUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the US fell 0.1 percent from a month earlier in January 2020, following a revised flat reading in December and matching market expectations. Stocks at manufacturers declined 0.1 percent (vs 0.4 percent in December) and those at merchant wholesalers dropped 0.4 percent (vs -0.3 percent in December). In addition, inventories at retailers were unchanged, after decreasing by 0.1 percent. Year-on-year, business inventories were up 1.1 percent in January. 2020-03-17T14:10:00
