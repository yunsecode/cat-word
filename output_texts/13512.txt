Title: US Jobless Claims Drop to Lowest Since 1973
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Drop to Lowest Since 1973United States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits was 247,000 in the week ended April 16th, a decrease of 6,000 from the previous week's unrevised level of 253,000, staying below market expectations of 263,000. It was the lowest reading since November 24, 1973 when it was recorded at 233,000.2016-04-21T13:32:08
