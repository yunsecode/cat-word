Title: US Factory Growth Highest in 3 Years
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Factory Growth Highest in 3 YearsUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit Manufacturing PMI for the US edged up to 55.7 in March of 2018 from 55.3 in February, beating market expectations of 55.5. The reading pointed to the fastest expansion in the manufacturing sector in 3 years, preliminary estimates showed. Stronger contributions came from employment, inventories and suppliers’ delivery times.2018-03-22T13:53:13.017
