Title: Dow Climbs 400 Points, Nasdaq Hits New High
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Climbs 400 Points, Nasdaq Hits New HighUnited States Stock MarketWall Street's main indexes rose on Monday with Nasdaq hitting a new high as investors anticipate that earnings reports will show resistance of key technology stocks to COVID-19 environment., as well as signs of progress in COVID-19 vaccine development. Also, signs of progress in COVID-19 vaccine development boosted the sentiment after experimental coronavirus vaccines from BioNTech and Pfizer received the US FDA's 'fast track' designation. At the same time, investors shrugged off concerns over the rising number of coronavirus cases. New cases in the US surged over the weekend, with Florida reporting a record increase of more than 15,000 infections in 24 hours. The Dow Jones advanced 430 points or 1.7% to above 26,500; the S&P 500 gained 40 points or 1.3% to near 3,230; and the Nasdaq added 180 points or 1.7% to 10,800 around 12:15 PM NY time.2020-07-13T13:32:00
