Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yields Continue to Fall
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yields Continue to FallUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note fell for the second session to 0.667% on Thursday, after the US recorded more than 45.5 thousand coronavirus infections on Wednesday, the highest on record according to NBC. The IMF expects the US economy to shrink 8% in 2020, compared to a 5.9% contraction estimated in April due to the effects of the coronavirus pandemic. Investors also await the jobless claims release later in the day, alongside final estimate of first-quarter GDP and durable goods orders. Initial claims are seen falling to a seasonally adjusted 1.3 million from 1.508 million a week earlier, but still making twice the peak during the 2007-09 Great Recession. 2020-06-25T09:37:00
