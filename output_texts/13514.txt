Title: US Existing Home Sales Surge 5.1%
Tag: Existing Home Sales

US Existing Home Sales Surge 5.1%United States Existing Home SalesSales of previously owned houses in the United States jumped 5.1 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 5330 thousand in March of 2016, recovering from an upwardly revised 7.3 percent drop in February and higher than market expectations of a 3.5 percent increase. Sales of single family went up 5.5 percent and those of condos increased 1.8 percent.
