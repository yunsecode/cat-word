Title: US New Home Sales Fall More than Expected
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Fall More than ExpectedUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States shrank 9.3 percent month-over-month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 625 thousand in December of 2017 from a downwardly revised 689 thousand in November which was the highest reading since October of 2007. Figures came below market forecasts of 676 thousand. Sales fell in all four regions.
