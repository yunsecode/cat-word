Title: US Durable Goods Orders Rebound Weaker than Expected
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Rebound Weaker than ExpectedUnited States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods surged 0.8 percent from a month earlier in November 2018, following a downwardly revised 4.3 percent plunge in October and missing market expectations of a 1.6 percent advance. Transportation equipment drove the increase. Orders for non-defense capital goods excluding aircraft, a closely watched proxy for business spending plans, dropped 0.6 percent in November, after a 0.5 percent gain in the previous month.2018-12-21T13:32:00
