Title: Wall Street Shrugs Off Coronavirus Fears
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Shrugs Off Coronavirus FearsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened higher on Friday, as worries about the impact that the spread of coronavirus from China might have on economic growth were offset by better-than-expected earnings from American Express and Intel. Also, money flowed into riskier investments after encouraging business activity data out of Europe lifted the mood. The Dow Jones rose 0.4%, the S&P 500 added 0.1% and the Nasdaq went up 0.4% around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2020-01-24T14:44:00
