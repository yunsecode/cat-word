Title: US Stocks Reach Fresh Records
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Reach Fresh RecordsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened sharply in the green on Tuesday with the 3 key stock indexes reaching record highs. Gains came amid optimism as the earnings season continues with results for Citigroup and UnitedHealth beating forecasts. The Dow Jones added 221 points or 0.9% to 26024; the S&P 500 went up 15 points or 0.6% to 2801 and the Nasdaq rose 54 points or 0.7% to 7314 around 6 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-01-16T14:30:04
