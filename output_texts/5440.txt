US Crude Stocks Fall More than ForecastUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 3.234 million barrels in the August 13th week, following a 0.447 million decrease in the previous period and compared with market consensus of a 1.055 million drop, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed.
