Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise in Latest Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise in Latest WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States jumped by 5.836 million barrels in the week ended July 13th 2018, following a 12.633 million drop in the previous week, which was the highest decline in crude inventories since the week ended September 2nd 2016. It compares with market expectations of a 3.622 million decrease. Meanwhile, stocks of gasoline went down by 3.165 million barrels while markets expected a 0.044 million drop. 2018-07-18T14:34:46.227
