Title: US New Home Sales Slide From 8-Year High
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Slide From 8-Year HighUnited States New Home SalesNew US single-family home sales fell 6 percent to 551,000 in May of 2016 compared to market expectations of 560,000. The April's sales was downwardly revised to 586,000 units, but still remained the highest since February 2008. The inventory of new homes on the market reached the highest since September of 2009.
