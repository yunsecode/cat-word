Title: US Inflation Rate Falls To 8-Month Low Of 1.6%
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Falls To 8-Month Low Of 1.6%United States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States increased 1.6 percent year-on-year in June of 2017, below 1.9 percent in May and compared to market expectations of 1.7 percent. It is the lowest inflation rate since October of 2016 due to a 0.4 percent fall in gasoline prices. On a monthly basis, consumer prices were flat, following a 0.1 percent drop in May and compared to forecasts of 0.1 percent.
