Title: US Stocks Retreat from Records
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Retreat from RecordsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street fell from record highs to close in the red on Thursday amid lower-than-expected results for companies including Kohl's and Macy's. Financials extended losses on Thursday, with shares of technology and industrial companies also retreating moderately. The Dow Jones declined 101 points or 0.4% to 23462. The S&P 500 fell 10 points or 0.4% to 2585. The Nasdaq lost 39 points or 0.6% to 6750.2017-11-09T21:39:15
