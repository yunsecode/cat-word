Title: US Stocks Recover on Jobs, Oil
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Recover on Jobs, OilUnited States Stock MarketUS equities gained on Friday afternoon, with three major indexes reaching the highest since January amid a better than expected nonfarm payrolls report and higher oil prices. The Dow Jones was up 64 points or 0.38% to 17008, the S&P 500 added 7 points or 0.49% to 2001 and the Nasdaq gained 17 points 0.37% to 4724 around 2:20 PM NY time.2016-03-04T17:29:14
