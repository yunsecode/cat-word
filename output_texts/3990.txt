Title: Dollar Eases from Three-Week Highs
Tag: Currency

Dollar Eases from Three-Week HighsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index eased from three-week highs to consolidate around 98.20 as investors took a breather after a recent rally driven by expectations of higher interest rates. Markets were recalibrating the higher possibility of a 50-bps hike in May following hawkish comments from several policymakers last week. Investors now look forward to the monthly jobs report, PCE inflation, and consumer confidence data in the US to guide the outlook for monetary policy. The most pronounced selling activity was against the euro, a move triggered by a potential ceasefire or peace deal between Russia and Ukraine. 2022-03-29T10:48:00
