Title: Dollar Extends Gains for 3rd Session
Tag: Currency

Dollar Extends Gains for 3rd SessionUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded flat to higher around 93.6 on Wednesday, the highest on a closing basis in a week and extending a 0.5% gain from the previous session. Investors continue to monitor the earnings season which has just started and digest a stalemate in US stimulus negotiations after Nancy Pelosi said the White House $1.8 trillion package proposal 'falls significantly short' of expectations. Still, Joe Biden is seen beating President Trump in the November 3rd presidential election, raising hopes of a large stimulus plan. Mounting coronavirus infections and further restrictions in Europe, Brexit worries, and an attempt from the PBoC to depreciate the yuan are also pressuring the dollar up. 2020-10-14T07:48:00
