Title: US Business Inventories Growth at 9-Month High
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Growth at 9-Month HighUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the United States increased 0.4 percent month-on-month in March of 2016, following 0.1 percent drop in the previous month and above market expectations of 0.2 percent rise. It was the biggest growth since June of 2015 as auto inventories increased by 2.3 percent, the fastest  pace since 2013. Retail inventories excluding autos, which go into the calculation of GDP, rose by 0.4 percent, higher than 0.2 percent in February. Year-on-year, inventories were up 1.5 percent.2016-05-13T15:19:20
