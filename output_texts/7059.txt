DXY Remains Near 90.50United States CurrencyThe dollar index gradually remained near 90.50 slightly up from an over 2-1/2-year low of 89.80 touched in the previous week as investors turned their attention to safe-haven assets because of persistent concerns about the new coronavirus strain.
