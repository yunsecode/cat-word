Title: US Job Cuts Lowest Since August
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Job Cuts Lowest Since AugustUnited States Challenger Job CutsJob cuts announced by US-based employers fell by 34 percent to 40,023 in April 2019, the lowest level since last August, from 60,587 in the previous month. The biggest cuts were reported for Industrial Goods manufacturers (5,159), followed by Consumer Products manufacturers (4,705) and Automotive manufacturers (3,915). Still, April cuts were up 11 percent compared to 36,081 cuts announced in the same month of 2018. So far this year, employers have announced 230,433 job cuts, 31 percent higher than the 176,280 announced in the first four months of last year. 2019-05-02T11:40:00
