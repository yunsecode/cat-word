Title: Dollar Index Hits 4-week Low
Tag: currency

Dollar Index Hits 4-week LowUnited States currencyDXY decreased to a 4-week low of 105.24, as the US economy unexpectedly shrank for a second straight quarter in the three months to June of 2022, stoking recession fears and building expectations that the Fed may need to slow down the pace of interest rate hikes. The GDP data was released last week, just a day after the US Federal Reserve raised its policy rate by 75bps in a widely expected move, with chair Powell sounding less hawkish on future rate hikes.
