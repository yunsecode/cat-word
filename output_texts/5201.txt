Title: US Jobless Rate Seen Falling to 5.1%
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Seen Falling to 5.1%United States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate likely dropped further last month to 5.1 percent, the lowest since March 2020, amid a steady recovery in the job market and as the negative effects of both Hurricane Ida and the Delta variant's summer spike start to fade. The rate would still remain well above the pre-crisis level of about 3.5 percent due to ongoing labor shortages, but is seen declining further in the coming months as companies fill widespread vacancies and as more workers go back into the labor force.2021-10-08T11:49:09.463
