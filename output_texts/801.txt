Title: US 10-Year Bond Yield Surges after NFP
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Bond Yield Surges after NFPUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note jumped almost 10bps to 3.37% on Friday, after the NFP report showed the US economy added 236K jobs in March, slightly less than forecasts of 239K, but still pointing to a strong job growth. At the same time, the unemployment rate unexpectedly edged lower and annual pay growth slowed more than anticipated. The payrolls report renewed bets of a 25bps rate hike from the Fed next month. Prior to the NFP release, a majority of investors were seeing the central bank pausing the tightening cycle, after data during the week including jobless claims, ISM PMIs, ADP and the JOLTS report showed tighter financial conditions are already hurting the US economy. The Securities Industry and Financial Markets Association Trading recommended trading in the bond market to close at 12:00 PM today.2023-04-07T12:43:00
