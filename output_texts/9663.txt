Title: US MBA Mortgage Applications Fall 2.5% in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US MBA Mortgage Applications Fall 2.5% in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States decreased 2.5 percent in the week ended February 1st 2019, following a 3.0 percent fall in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications rose 0.3 percent and applications to purchase a home declined 4.9 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went down by 7bps to 4.69 percent.2019-02-06T12:02:13.017
