Title: US Retail Sales Post Biggest Gain in 7 Months
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Post Biggest Gain in 7 MonthsUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States grew by 0.6 percent month-over-month in July 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.3 percent gain in June and beating market expectations of 0.4 percent. It was the sharpest increase in retail trade since December last year, mainly driven by higher sales at miscellaneous store retailers and motor vehicle and parts dealers.2017-08-15T12:30:59
