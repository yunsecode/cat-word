Title: US Producer Prices Flat in August
Tag: Producer Price Inflation MoM

US Producer Prices Flat in AugustUnited States Producer Price Inflation MoMProducer prices for final demand in the United States were unchanged on the month in August 2016, following a 0.4 percent drop in the previous month and below market expectations of 0.1 percent increase. The index for final demand goods prices declined 0.4 percent, led by lower cost of food (-1.6 percent) and energy (-0.8 percent). Meanwhile, prices for final demand services rose 0.1 percent, as cost less trade, transportation, and warehousing increased 0.5 percent. Compared to the same month of the previous year, factory gate prices were flat.2016-09-15T12:40:36.763
