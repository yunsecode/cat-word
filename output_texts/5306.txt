Title: Dollar Hovers at 3-Week High
Tag: Currency

Dollar Hovers at 3-Week HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was around 92.6 on Wednesday, close to levels not seen since the end of August, as the initial enthusiasm over slowing inflation faded. Inflation fell more than expected last month but prices remain elevated, raising further concerns over persistent price pressure and uncertainties over the timing of Fed tapering. Tapering tends to benefit the dollar as it will mean the number of dollars in circulation will be lower. 2021-09-15T07:26:33.257
