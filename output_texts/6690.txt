Title: Dollar Weakness Persists
Tag: Currency

Dollar Weakness Persists United States CurrencyThe US dollar struggled to find much upside momentum so far this week, keeping depressed in the 90.50 neighbourhood as money continued to flow into riskier currencies amid optimism about vaccines and the renewed focus on US fiscal stimulus. Progress on a massive pandemic-relief package with markets expecting the price tag to be somewhere between $1.3T and Biden’s $1.9T proposal favoured bearish traders, primarily because it has eroded dollar’s safe-haven appeal. On top of that, the risks posed by record-high debt levels put further pressure on the greenback. Still, these same prospects of a large additional injection of economic stimulus from the Biden administration pushed longer-term Treasury yields to levels not seen since February, which, seems to be directly linked to the dollar’s strength seen earlier this year.2021-02-15T22:49:00
