Title: US PCE Inflation Lowest since May 2021
Tag: PCE Price Index Annual Change

US PCE Inflation Lowest since May 2021United States PCE Price Index Annual ChangeThe personal consumption expenditure price index in the United States rose by 4.2% year-on-year in March 2023, the lowest since May 2021 and easing from an upwardly revised 5.1% rise in the previous month. Prices for goods were up 1.6% (vs 4.7% in February) and prices for services increased 5.7% (vs 5.6%). Food cost rose 9.7% (vs 11.1%) and energy prices increased 5.5% (vs 5.8%). Compared to the previous month, the PCE Price Index went up by 0.1%.2023-04-28T12:32:00
