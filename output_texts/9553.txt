Title: US Consumer Credit Growth Beats Expectations
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Growth Beats ExpectationsUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States went up by USD 17.05 billion in January 2019, up from a downwardly revised USD 15.36 billion gain in the previous month and above market expectations of a USD 16.0 billion rise. Revolving credit including credit card borrowing climbed USD 2.5 billion, compared to a downwardly revised USD 1.0 billion advance in December. Meantime, non-revolving credit including loans for education and automobiles rose by USD 14.5 billion, little changed from a downwardly revised USD 14.4 billion in the prior month. Year-on-year, consumer credit went up 5.1 percent, as revolving and non-revolving credit increased by 2.9 percent and 5.9 percent, respectively.2019-03-07T20:07:00
