Title: Bitcoin Bounces Back Above $40,000
Tag: Currency

Bitcoin Bounces Back Above $40,000United States CurrencyBitcoin traded above $40,000 on Wednesday, consolidating a rebound from an over 2-month low below $35,000 touched last weekend after China and the US tightened regulations related to cryptocurrencies. Bitcoin’s rollercoaster ride started after Tesla has suspended vehicle purchases using the cryptocurrency on May 12th, mentioning the rapidly increasing use of fossil fuels for Bitcoin mining and transactions.
