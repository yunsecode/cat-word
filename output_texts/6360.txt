Title: US Stock Futures Trade at Fresh Record Valuations
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Trade at Fresh Record ValuationsUnited States Stock MarketUS futures traded slightly off fresh record highs in early Asian trade as market participants shrugged off the Food and Drug Administration’s request for states to pause administering Johnson & Johnson’s Covid-19 vaccine after six people in the U.S. developed a rare disorder involving blood clots. The news sparked a rotation into the tech sector, as Tesla and Apple reasserted their dominance, soaring 8% and 2.5%, respectively. Meantime, JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs and Wells Fargo are due to report earnings before the bell Wednesday. US 10-year bond yields eased to near 3-week lows of 1.616% after the US Labor Department reported Tuesday that its consumer-price index jumped 2.6% in the year ended March, the biggest 12-month increase since August 2018. On Tuesday, the S&P 500 climbed 0.4% to close at a record high; the Nasdaq Composite rallied 1.21%, while the Dow Jones fell 0.2%.
