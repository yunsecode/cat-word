Title: US Industrial Output Growth Beats Estimates
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Output Growth Beats EstimatesUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial production in the United States rose 0.5 percent from a month earlier in April 2023, beating market expectations of flat reading. Manufacturing advanced 1 percent, well above forecasts of a 0.1 percent increase bolstered by a strong gain in the output of motor vehicles and parts; factory output excluding motor vehicles and parts moved up 0.4 percent. The index for mining rose 0.6 percent, while the index for utilities dropped 3.1 percent, as milder temperatures in April lowered demand for heating. Capacity utilization edged up to 79.7 percent in April, a rate that is equal to its long-run (1972–2022) average.
