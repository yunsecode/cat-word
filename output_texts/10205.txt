Title: US Stocks Close Mostly in the Red
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mostly in the Red United States Stock MarketWall Street closed mostly in the red on Monday 24 September 2018 with only the Nasdaq inching up, on fears of Deputy Attorney General Rosenstein’s departure and as fresh trade worries hit sentiment after news that China cancelled trade talks with the US and a 10% US tariff came into effect. Also, Brent crude rose to above $81 a barrel, a high since November 2014, ahead of the start of sanctions by the US on Iran and after OPEC leaders decided against an immediate extra increase in output despite President Trump's demands for action to cool the market. The Dow Jones lost 181 points or 0.7% to 26562. The S&P 500 retreated 10 points or 0.4% to 2919. In contrast, the Nasdaq edged up 6 points or 0.1% to 7993.2018-09-24T20:00:00
