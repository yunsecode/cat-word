Title: US Stocks Rise on Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on EarningsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Friday, attempting to recover from losses in the previous session after several companies reported better-than-expected earnings including Procter & Gamble, Honeywell, American Express and PayPal. The Dow Jones went up 0.7%, the S&P 500 rose 0.7% and the Nasdaq traded 1% higher around 20 minutes after the opening bell
