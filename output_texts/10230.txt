Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Tuesday 18 September 2018, as US tariffs imposed on China for about US $ 200 billion were lower than expected. The Dow Jones gained 185 points or 0.7% to 26247. The S&P 500 added 16 points or 0.5% to 2904. The Nasdaq climbed 60 points or 0.8% to 7956. 2018-09-18T20:14:00
