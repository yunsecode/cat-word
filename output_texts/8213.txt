Title: US Wholesale Inventories Rebound in April
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Rebound in AprilUnited States Wholesale InventoriesUS wholesale inventories went up 0.4 percent from a month earlier in April of 2020, recovering from an upwardly revised 1 percent drop in March, a preliminary estimate showed. Stocks of nondurable goods rebounded 1.3 percent, after a 3.3 percent slump in the prior month, while durable goods inventories edged down 0.2 percent (vs 0.5 percent). Year-on-year, wholesale inventories were down 2.6 percent in April.2020-05-29T12:35:00
