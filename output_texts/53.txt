Title: US Job Quits Rise to 5-Month High
Tag: Job Quits

US Job Quits Rise to 5-Month HighUnited States Job QuitsThe number of job quits in the United States rose by 250K from the previous month to 4.02 million in May of 2023, the highest level since last December. That was below record peaks of roughly 4.5 million quits reached in November 2021 but was still historically high. The number of quits increased mostly in health care and social assistance (+69,000) and in construction (+57,000). The quits rate, which measures the proportion of voluntary job leavers among total employment, was up to 2.6% in May from 2.4% in the prior month.11 days ago
