Title: US Stocks Close Lower on Oil, Earnings Concerns
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Lower on Oil, Earnings ConcernsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks closed in the red on Tuesday amid a drop in oil prices, as a downbeat start to earnings season and strengthening expectations of a rate rise this year by the Federal Reserve weighed on sentiment. The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 200 points, or 1.1%, to close at 18,129; the S&P 500 Index closed down 27 points, or 1.2%, at 2,137; and the Nasdaq Composite index dropped 82 points, or 1.5%, to close at 5,247.2016-10-11T20:18:49.19
