Title: US 10Y Bond Yield at 4-Month Lows
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield at 4-Month LowsUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note was around 4-month lows of 1.43% in early July after the jobs reports showed a robust job gain, but not strong enough to raise inflation and tightening concerns.
