Title: Dow And Nasdaq Close At Record High
Tag: Stock Market

Dow And Nasdaq Close At Record HighUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mostly higher on Tuesday with the Dow and the Nasdaq hitting fresh highs, amid higher-than-expected economic optimism data, and the earnings season continues: GM earnings and profit beat expectations while Michael Kors earnings beat forecasts but revenues were lower than expected. The Dow Jones went up 38 points or 0.2% to a new high of 20090; the Nasdaq gained 11 points or 0.2% to a new high of 5674 and the S&P 500 finished near the flatline.2017-02-07T21:16:41.007
