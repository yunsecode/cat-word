Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Below Expectations
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Below ExpectationsUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 0.25 million barrels in the week ending November 13th, well below a 4.224 million rise in the previous period and much lower than market expectations of a 1.9 million gain. Although it is the smallest increase since mid-September, it brings the total number to 487.3 million barrels, the highest for this time of year since 1930. In contrast, gasoline stocks went up by 1.009 million barrels, after falling for five straight weeks and beating market forecasts for a 400 thousand drop. 2015-11-18T15:35:11
