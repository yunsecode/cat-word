Title: US Stocks Rally, Nasdaq Breaks Above 6,000
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally, Nasdaq Breaks Above 6,000United States Stock MarketUS equities traded in the green on Tuesday boosted by stronger-than-expected earnings reports from Caterpillar, McDonald's, 3M and DuPont. The Dow Jones jumped 182 points, or 0.9%, to 20,946; the S&P 500 added 10 points, or 0.4%, to 2,384; and the Nasdaq advanced 22 points, or 0.4%, to 6,006 around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2017-04-25T13:38:49
