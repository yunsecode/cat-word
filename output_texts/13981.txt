Title: US Auto Sales Grow 13.6% in October
Tag: Total Vehicle Sales

US Auto Sales Grow 13.6% in OctoberUnited States Total Vehicle SalesTotal vehicle sales in the United States increased by 13.6 percent year-on-year in October of 2015 with a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 18.24 million vehicles and beating market expectations of 17.7 million. It was the highest annualized rate since July of 2005 and the highest October level since 2001. The booming October sales materialized despite concerns about a slowdown in consumer spending and stagnant wages.2015-11-03T20:10:57
