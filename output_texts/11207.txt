Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 10th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 10th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 1.071 million barrels in the week ended January 192th 2018, following a 6.861million drop in the previous period and compared with market expectations of a 1 million decline. It marks the tenth consecutive week of falls, the longest losing streak since at least 1982. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went up by 3.098 million, following a 3.62 million rise in the previous week and above market expectations of a 2.486 million increase.
