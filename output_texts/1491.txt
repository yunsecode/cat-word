Title: Treasury Yields Steady Following Fed Minutes
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Treasury Yields Steady Following Fed MinutesUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note was stable at the 3.7% level as investors digested minutes from the FOMC’s December meeting. Policymakers confirmed their previous hawkish signals that interest rates must continue to rise and remain at a restrictive level for a prolonged period to bring inflation down to target. Officials also noted that it may take time for price growth to converge to the desired level, raising concerns that high inflation amid elevated borrowing costs will pressure the US economy into a recession. Increasing worries of a slowing economy drove investors to pile onto government debt during the last week.
