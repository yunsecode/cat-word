Title: US Factory Growth Lowest since 2016: ISM
Tag: Business Confidence

US Factory Growth Lowest since 2016: ISMUnited States Business ConfidenceThe ISM Manufacturing PMI in the US fell to 54.2 in February of 2019 from 56.6 in January, below market expectations of 55.5. The reading pointed to the slowest growth in factory activity since November of 2016 as new orders, production and employment increased less. 2019-03-01T15:05:00
