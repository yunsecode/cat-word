Title: Dow Jones and S&P 500 Surge to New Highs
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Jones and S&P 500 Surge to New HighsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Thursday 20 September 2018 with the Dow and S&P 500 soaring to new records, as fears of a US-China trade war ease and emerging market-linked stocks continued to recover. The Dow Jones surged 251 points or 1.0% to 26657, notching its first record close since January. The S&P 500 jumped 23 points or 0.8% to 2931. The Nasdaq climbed 78 points or 1.0% to 8028.2018-09-20T20:08:11.59
