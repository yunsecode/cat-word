Title: Dow Jones Hits Record High, S&P 500 & Nasdaq Slide
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Jones Hits Record High, S&P 500 & Nasdaq SlideUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones gained 61 points or 0.3% to a fresh record high of 21891 on July 31st as earnings results continued to improve investors' mood and WTI crude oil surpassed the $50 per barrel benchmark to book a 9-week high. In contrast, the S&P 500 edged down 2 points or 0.1% to 2470 and the Nasdaq lost 27 points or 0.4% to 6348.2017-07-31T20:07:32
