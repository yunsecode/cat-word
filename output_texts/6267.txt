Title: US 10-Year Yield Retreats
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Yield RetreatsUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note edged down to 1.54% on Thursday, close to five-week lows and after dropping below its 50-day moving average for the first time since November. A fiercely anticipated auction of $24 billion of 20-year bonds was met with sufficient demand on Wednesday. Investors also await economic data releases early in the morning including the claims report and existing home sales for an update on the economic recovery. Meanwhile, traders continue to digest strong corporate results, prospects of robust economic recovery and rising coronavirus infections in several countries including India, Japan and Canada.2021-04-22T08:20:05.873
