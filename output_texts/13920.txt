US Crude Oil Inventories in Line with ExpectationsUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 0.96 million barrels in the week ending November 20 of 2015, compared to market expectations of a 1 million rise. Gasoline inventories rose by 2478 thousand, the highest gain in eight weeks.
