Title: US House Prices Rise 0.5%: FHFA
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US House Prices Rise 0.5%: FHFAUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States increased 0.50 percent in November of 2015 over the previous month, the same as in October and beating market expectations of a 0.4 percent gain. Year-on-year, the FHFA house price index went up 5.9 percent, slower than a 6.1 percent rise in the previous month. 2016-01-26T14:08:36
