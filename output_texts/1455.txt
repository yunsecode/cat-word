Title: US Core CPI Matches Forecasts
Tag: Core Inflation Rate MoM

US Core CPI Matches ForecastsUnited States Core Inflation Rate MoMUS core consumer prices, which exclude volatile items such as food and energy, went up by 0.3% from a month earlier in December of 2022, compared to a 0.2% rise in the prior month, in line with market expectations. The indexes which increased in December include shelter, household furnishings and operations, motor vehicle insurance, recreation, and apparel, while the decreases were reported for used cars and trucks, and airline fares. Year-on-year, core consumer prices advanced 5.7% in December, slowing from a 6% increase in November, and matching market forecasts.
