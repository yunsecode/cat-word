Title: US Stocks Fall on Tariffs Announcement
Tag: stock market

US Stocks Fall on Tariffs AnnouncementUnited States stock marketWall Street traded lower on Thursday after the Trump administration said it will begin imposing tariffs on steel and aluminum imports from the EU, Canada and Mexico starting Friday. The Dow Jones lost 230 points or 0.9%, the S&P fell 11 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq traded nearly flat around 11:25 AM NY time.2018-05-31T14:49:00
