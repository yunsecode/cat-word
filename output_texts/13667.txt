Title: US Personal Spending, Income Up 0.5% in January
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending, Income Up 0.5% in JanuaryUnited States Personal SpendingPersonal consumption expenditures increased sharply by 0.5 percent from preceding month in January of 2016 following an upwardly revised 0.1 percent growth in December while beating market expectations of 0.3 percent, boosted by higher purchases of durable goods (+1.2 percent) and services (+0.6 percent). Income went up 0.5 percent, compared to 0.3 percent in December and above forecasts, led by an increase of 0.6 percent in wages and salaries.2016-02-26T15:15:11
