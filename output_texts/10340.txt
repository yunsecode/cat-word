Title: US Consumer Sentiment Lowest in Near a Year
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Lowest in Near a YearUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US fell to 95.3 in August of 2018 from 97.9 in July, below market expectations of 98. It is the lowest reading since September last year amid weaker assessments of buying conditions, mainly due to less favorable perceptions of market prices, preliminary estimates showed. 2018-08-17T14:06:08.267
