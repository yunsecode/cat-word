Title: US Stocks Close Lower as Tech Shares Fall
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Lower as Tech Shares FallUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks erased early gains and closed lower on Tuesday in abbreviated trading ahead of the Independence Day holiday, as losses in technology firms and banks offset gains in health care and energy companies. The Dow Jones fell 132 points, or 0.5%, to 24,175; the S&P 500 dropped 13 points, or 0.5%, to 2,713; and the Nasdaq shed 65 points, or 0.9%, to 7,503.2018-07-03T17:18:00
