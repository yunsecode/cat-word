Title: US Import Prices Drop 1.2% in December
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Drop 1.2% in DecemberUnited States Import Prices MoMImport prices in the United States decreased by 1.2 percent month-on-month in December of 2015 following a 0.5 percent drop in November but beating market forecasts of 1.4 percent fall. It was the largest monthly decrease since August, driven by a 9.5 percent drop in fuel prices. Also, non-fuel prices declined by 0.3 percent. In 2015, import prices fell 8.2 percent, reaching the largest calendar-year decline since 2008 when the index fell 10.1 percent. 2016-01-14T13:33:34
