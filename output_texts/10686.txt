Title: US Composite PMI Higher than Expected in May
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Composite PMI Higher than Expected in MayUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI rose to 55.7 in May of 2018 from 54.9 in April, beating market forecasts of 55, preliminary estimates showed. The reading pointed to the strongest expansion in private sector activity in three months. Both services (55.7 from 54.6, 3-month high) and manufacturing (56.6 from 56.5, 44-month high) rose at faster paces.2018-05-23T14:04:05.917
