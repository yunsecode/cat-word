Title: US Retail Sales Up 0.5%
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Up 0.5%United States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States went up 0.5 percent in May from April of 2016, following a 1.3 percent rise in the previous period and above market expectations of a 0.3 percent increase. It is the second straight month of gains, boosted by sales of automobiles and other goods. 2016-06-14T13:36:24
