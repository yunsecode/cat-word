Title: Wall Street Retreats Slightly from Records
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Retreats Slightly from RecordsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks traded slightly lower on Monday, following record closes last week, as investors await the earnings season to kick-off and key economic data. All 3 main stock indexes were down around 0.2%. Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan Chase and Wells Fargo are due to report quarterly results on Wednesday. Inflation rate, export and import prices, retail sales and industrial production data will also be in the spotlight. Meanwhile, Jerome Powell said during an interview to CBS news' "60 Minutes" the Fed wants to see inflation move up to 2% and the US economy is at an inflection point and is about to start growing much more quickly although there is still the risk the disease would spread again. Last week, the Dow added more than 1%, the S&P 500 gained nearly 2%, while the Nasdaq Composite rallied 2.4%.2021-04-12T13:33:00
