Title: US GDP Growth Highest in Near 4 Years in Q2
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Highest in Near 4 Years in Q2United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy advanced an annualized 4.1 percent on quarter in the second quarter of 2018, well above an upwardly revised 2.2 percent expansion in the previous period and in line with market expectations. It is the strongest growth rate since the third quarter of 2014 amid higher consumer spending and soybean exports while business spending slowed, the advance estimate showed. 2018-07-27T12:41:26.41
