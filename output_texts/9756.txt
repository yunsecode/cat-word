Title: US Stocks Rebound
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks ReboundUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded mainly higher on Tuesday, led by tech shares after Netflix announced it will increase subscription prices. Gains come despite JPMorgan reported lower-than-expected earnings . Also, investors' mood improved after news of new stimulus measures in China, including investment projects and local government bond issuance. The Dow Jones gained 0.2%, the S&P 500 rose 0.6% and the Nasdaq traded 1.1% higher around 25 minutes after the opening bell.
