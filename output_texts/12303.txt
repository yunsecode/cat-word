Title: US Jobless Claims Drop to 241K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Drop to 241K United States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits went down by 2 thousand to 241 thousand in the week ended March 11th 2017, slightly below market expectations of 243 thousand. The 4-week moving average was 237.25 Thousand, an increase of 750 from the previous week's unrevised average of 236.5. 2017-03-16T12:31:49.797
