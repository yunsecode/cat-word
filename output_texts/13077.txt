Title: US Stocks Continue to Fall on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Continue to Fall on Wednesday United States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Wednesday, extending Tuesday losses as the dollar continues to strengthen and oil prices to fall. The decline comes despite ADP data showed the US private sector added slightly more jobs than expected in August. The Dow Jones was down 38 points or 0.2%, the S&P 500 declined 4 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq lost 8 points or 0.2% around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2016-08-31T14:52:01.63
