Title: US Manufacturing PMI Revised Lower: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing PMI Revised Lower: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI was revised slightly down to 55.3 from a preliminary of 55.4 in November of 2018 and 55.7 in October. However, the reading indicated a solid improvement in the health of the sector that was above the series trend as new orders rose the most since May and job creation was also stronger. On the other hand, business confidence was the lowest since September of 2017 amid concerns over the sustainability of the current sequence of new order growth.2018-12-03T14:52:00
