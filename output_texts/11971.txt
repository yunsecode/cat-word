Title: Dow And S&P Hit All-Time High
Tag: stock market

Dow And S&P Hit All-Time HighUnited States stock marketWall Street opened in the green on Monday amid a recovery in tech shares, with Alphabet, Facebook, Amazon, Apple and Netflix rising more than 1%. The Dow Jones went up 80 points or 0.4% to a new high of 21463; the S&P 500 added 12 points or 0.5% to a record of 2444.9 and the Nasdaq jumped 59 points or 1% to 6211 around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2017-06-19T13:28:42
