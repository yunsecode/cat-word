Title: US Personal Income Rises More than Expected in October
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Rises More than Expected in OctoberUnited States Personal IncomePersonal income went up 0.5 percent month-over-month in October of 2018 after rising 0.2 percent in September and beating market expectations of 0.4 percent. It is the largest increase in personal income since January and primarily reflected hikes in wages and salaries, proprietors’ income, and government social benefits to persons. Wages and salaries, the largest component of personal income, increased 0.3 percent in October, the same as in September.2018-11-29T13:31:00
