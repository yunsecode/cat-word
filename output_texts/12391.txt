Title: US Private Sector Grows At Slower Pace: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Grows At Slower Pace: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe Markit Flash US Composite Output Index dropped to 54.3 in February 2017 from 55.8 in the previous month. It was the weakest reading in two months, as growth slowed across both service sector (PMI at 53.9 from 55.6 in January) and manufacturing (PMI at 54.3 from 55). Also, business optimism moderated among US private sector firms, driven by weaker confidence across the service economy.2017-02-21T15:05:33
