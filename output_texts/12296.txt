Title: US Manufacturing Output Rises The Most In Near 2 Years
Tag: Manufacturing Production

US Manufacturing Output Rises The Most In Near 2 YearsUnited States Manufacturing ProductionManufacturing production in the United States increased 1.2 percent year-on-year in February of 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.6 percent rise in January. It is the biggest gain since April of 2015. On a monthly basis, factory output increased 0.5 percent, the same as in the previous period and marking the sixth consecutive increase, led by advances of more than 1 percent for nonmetallic mineral products, fabricated metal products, and machinery. In addition, production increased for durables (0.6 percent) and nondurables (0.4 percent).2017-03-17T13:52:35.707
