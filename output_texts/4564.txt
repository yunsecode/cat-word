Title: US Import Prices Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Unexpectedly FallUnited States Import Prices MoMThe price index for US imports dropped 0.2 percent from a month earlier in December 2021, following a 0.7 percent increase in November and missing market expectations of a 0.3 percent rise. It was the first monthly decrease since August as import fuel prices decreased 6.5 percent, the most since April 2020, due to a 6.0 percent fall in petroleum cost and a 13.7 percent decline in natural gas prices. Meanwhile, nonfuel import prices rose 0.5 percent for the third consecutive month in December, boosted by prices for nonfuel industrial supplies and materials; consumer goods; foods, feeds, and beverages; automotive vehicles; and capital goods. On a yearly basis, US import prices increased 10.4 percent in December.2022-01-14T13:32:00
