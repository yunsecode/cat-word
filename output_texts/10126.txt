Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Rise More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 5.987 million barrels in the week ended October 5th 2018, following a 7.975 million gain in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.62 million increase. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories unexpectedly increased by 0.951 million barrels while markets expected a 0.042 million decrease.2018-10-11T15:01:25.313
