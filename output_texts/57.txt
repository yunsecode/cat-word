Title: US Exports Down to 14-Month Low
Tag: Exports

US Exports Down to 14-Month LowUnited States ExportsExports from the United States decreased by USD 2.1 billion to USD 247.1 billion in May 2023, the lowest level since March 2022, reflecting weakened global demand. Exports of goods were down USD 2.5 billion mainly due to lower sales of soybeans, as well as industrial supplies and materials including crude oil, other petroleum products and natural gas liquids. Exports of services rose USD 0.4 billion, boosted by travel, transport and other business services.11 days ago
