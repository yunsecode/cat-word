Title: US Services Sector Growth at 7-Month High: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Sector Growth at 7-Month High: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index went up 3.6 percentage points to 56.5 percent in June of 2016, up from 52.9 in May and well above market expectations of 53.3. It is the highest reading since November last year, boosted by new orders and new business and a recovery in employment. 2016-07-06T15:05:43
