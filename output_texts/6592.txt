Title: Dollar at 1-Month High
Tag: Currency

Dollar at 1-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rebounded from early losses to trade slightly higher around 91 on Monday, the strongest level on a closing basis since February 4th, as investors digest a fall in Treasury yields and prospects of a swift economic recovery supported by fiscal stimulus. The House of Representatives approved President Joe Biden's $1.9 trillion pandemic aid package that includes extra $1,400 checks for most Americans. The bill will now move to the Senate.2021-03-01T09:28:00
