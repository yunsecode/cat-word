Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 13-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 13-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe preliminary reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States fell to 87.9 in October of 2016, well below market expectations of 91.9 and compared to 91.2 in September. It is the lowest figure since September last year as subindex of future expectations declined.2016-10-14T14:07:50
