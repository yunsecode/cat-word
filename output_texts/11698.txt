Title: US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI Rises in August
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI Rises in AugustUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States went up to 55.3 in August of 2017 from 53.9 in July, compared to market expectations of 55.4. Improvements were seen in production, new orders and employment while inventories slowed and price pressures increased. The majority of services providers are optimistic about business conditions going forward.2017-09-06T14:02:53.337
