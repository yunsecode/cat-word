Title: US Futures Rise Sharply
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Rise SharplyUnited States Stock MarketStock futures in the US traded sharply higher on Friday afternoon in Asia on hopes for targeted US stimulus and upbeat Chinese economic data. President Donald Trump Thursday said talks with Congress had restarted after calling off talks earlier this week. Meantime, services growth in China accelerated in September, reflecting ongoing economic recovery and the effective control of the COVID-19 pandemic. Market participants also increasingly expecting the Democrats to take back the White House, and possibly the Senate as well, in the November elections, Reuters quoting analysts reported. On Thursday, the Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 122 points, or 0.43%, to 28,426, the S&P 500 gained 27 points, or 0.8%, to 3,447 and the Nasdaq Composite added 56 points, or 0.5%, to 11,421.2020-10-09T05:48:00
