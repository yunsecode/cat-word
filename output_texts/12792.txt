Title: US Consumer Sentiment Up to 5-Month High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Up to 5-Month HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe preliminary reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States increased to 91.6 in November of 2016 from a final reading of 87.2 in October. It is the highest reading since June as consumers were more confident about future and present conditions and inflation expectations increased.
