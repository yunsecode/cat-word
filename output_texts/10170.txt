Title: US Stocks Rise on ADP Jobs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on ADP JobsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Wednesday, with the Dow touching a new record high after ADP data showed the US private sector added a stronger-than-expected 230K jobs in September. The Dow Jones, the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq all traded near 0.5% higher around 7 minutes after the opening bell.2018-10-03T13:30:00
