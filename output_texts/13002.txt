Title: US Stocks Open Higher Ahead of Fed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Higher Ahead of FedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Wednesday as investors await Fed interest rate decision later in the day while the Bank of Japan left its key rate unchanged but said it would introduce yield curve control to hasten its fight against deflation. The Dow Jones added 88 points or 0.5%, the S&P 500 went up 9 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq gained 22 points or 0.4% around 7 minutes after the opening bell.2016-09-21T13:43:04.367
