Title: US Stocks Rise Amid Strong Jobs Report
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise Amid Strong Jobs ReportUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded modestly in the green on Friday, after the jobs report for January showed the economy added a better-than-expected 304K jobs. Also, earnings from Amazon and Exxon beat forecasts, boosting investors’ mood. The Dow Jones rose 0.7%, the S&P 500 gained 0.4% and the Nasdaq went up 0.2% around 10:35 AM NY time.2019-02-01T15:43:14.91
