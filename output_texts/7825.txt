Title: US Stock Futures Set to Open Higher on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Set to Open Higher on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks futures pointed to a positive start on Wednesday, as investors await updates on a new coronavirus relief package. At the same time, President Donald Trump announced that the US government will purchase 100 million doses of Moderna’s experimental COVID-19 vaccine, which is currently in late-stage human trials. Meanwhile, presidential candidate Joe Biden chose Senator Kamala Harris as his running mate. On Tuesday, the Dow Jones lost 105 points or 0.4% to 27,687; the S&P 500 retreated 27 points or 0.8% to 3334; and the Nasdaq shed 186 points or 1.7% to 10,783.2020-08-12T08:41:00
