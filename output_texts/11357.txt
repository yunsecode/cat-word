Title: Dow Jones Hits Record High Ahead of Fed Decision
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Jones Hits Record High Ahead of Fed DecisionUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones hit an all-time high of 24,565 right after the market opened on Wednesday as investors await an interest rate decision from the Federal Reserve later in the day. Also, the S&P 500 added 6 points, or 0.2%, to 2,670; and the Nasdaq rose 27 points, or 0.4%, to 6,889.2017-12-13T14:36:33
