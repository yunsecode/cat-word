Title: US Industrial Output Rises The Most In 3 Years
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Output Rises The Most In 3 YearsUnited States Industrial Production MomUS industrial production increased by 1 percent month-over-month in April 2017, following a downwardly revised 0.4 percent rise in March and beating market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. It was the largest increase in industrial production since February 2014, as output rebounded for both manufacturing and mining while utilities grew at a slower pace.2017-05-16T13:18:14
