Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall Further: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall Further: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States dropped 1.8 percent in the week ended September 7th 2018, following a 0.1 percent decline in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications fell 5.9 percent while applications to purchase a home went up 0.9 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate increased by 4bps to 4.84 percent.2018-09-12T11:04:17.407
