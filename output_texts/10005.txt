Title: US Stocks Rise after Elections
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise after ElectionsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Wednesday after results from the midterm elections came in line with expectations, with Democrats gaining control of the House and Republicans increasing Senate majority. The Dow Jones was up 0.9%, the S&P 500 rose 1.1% and the Nasdaq traded 1.2% higher around 12 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-11-07T14:45:00
