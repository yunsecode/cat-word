Title: US Jobless Rate Falls to Over Five-Decade Low
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Falls to Over Five-Decade LowUnited States Unemployment RateThe unemployment rate in the US inched lower to 3.4 percent in January 2023, the lowest level since May 1969 and below market expectations of 3.6 percent, as the number of unemployed people declined by 28 thousand to 5.69 million and the number of employed increased by 894 thousand to 160.1 million. The latest jobs report came on the heels of a sharp decline in weekly jobless claims to nine-month lows and a larger-than-expected increase in the level of job openings in December to a five-month high, pointing to a still-tight labor market.2023-02-03T13:33:00
