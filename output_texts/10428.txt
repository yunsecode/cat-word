Title: US July Consumer Sentiment Revised Higher
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US July Consumer Sentiment Revised HigherUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US came in at a six-month low of 97.9 in July 2018, above the preliminary reading of 97.1 and little-changed from June's 98.2. Both consumer expectations and current economic conditions came in stronger than initially thought.  2018-07-27T14:03:00
