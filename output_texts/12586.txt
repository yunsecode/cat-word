Title: US Stocks Rise Ahead Of Fed Minutes
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise Ahead Of Fed MinutesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Wednesday as investors await FOMC minutes release later in the day for further clues on the timing of Fed's next rate hike. The Dow Jones gained 58 points or 0.3%, the S&P 500 went up 9 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq traded 25 points or 0.5% higher around 6 minutes after the opening bell.2017-01-04T14:40:52
