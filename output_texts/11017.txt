Title: US IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Falls from 13-Month High
Tag: Economic Optimism Index

US IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Falls from 13-Month HighUnited States Economic Optimism IndexThe IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index in the United States fell 1.9 percent to 55.6 in March 2018 from a 13-month high of 56.7 in February, and missing market expectations of 58.2. The six-month economic outlook sub-index dropped 8 percent to 52.9 from last month’s 57.5, which had been the highest reading since October 2012. Meanwhile, the personal financial outlook sub-index remained unchanged at 63.8 in March, while the gauge measuring confidence in federal economic policies continued its upward trajectory, rising 2.7 percent, to 50.0 from 48.7 in February.This marks a return to positive territory for the first time in 13 months.2018-03-06T15:07:01
