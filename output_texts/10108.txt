Title: US Job Openings Hit Record High in August
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Hit Record High in AugustUnited States Job OffersThe number of job openings increased to 7.136 million in August 2018 from an upwardly revised 7.077 million in July, reaching an all-time high and above market expectations of 6.945 million. The number of job openings was almost unchanged for total non-farm payrolls, private and government. Job openings advanced in federal government (+15,000). The number of job openings was little changed in all four regions. Meanwhile, hiring rose to 5.784 million in August from 5.713 million in the previous month, also hitting an all-time high.2018-10-16T14:02:00
