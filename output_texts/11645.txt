Title: United States Services PMI Falls More than Anticipated
Tag: Services PMI

United States Services PMI Falls More than AnticipatedUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services PMI fell to 55.1 in September of 2017 from 56 in August which was the highest reading in 21 months. Figures came below market expectations of 55.9, flash estimates showed. A robust rise in new work led to solid employment growth and another increase in backlogs of work. Also, input price inflation slowed and average prices charged by service sector firms increased at one of the fastest rates seen over the past three years. In addition, service providers were the least optimistic about the business outlook since September 2016, mainly due to renewed concerns about sales growth over the year ahead.2017-09-22T14:04:27.887
