Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Up in August
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Up in AugustUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US was revised higher to 96.2 in August of 2018 from 95.3 in the preliminary estimate. It compares with 97.9 in July and market expectations of 95.5. Yet, it is the lowest reading since January as current conditions were seen the weakest since November of 2016.2018-08-31T14:07:51.907
