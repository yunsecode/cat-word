Title: US Posts $53 Billion Budget Deficit in May
Tag: Government Budget Value

US Posts $53 Billion Budget Deficit in MayUnited States Government Budget ValueThe United States recorded a budget gap of USD 53 billion in May 2016, down 38 percent from a USD 84 billion deficit in the same month a year earlier, as outlays fell 7 percent to USD 277 billion while receipts rose 6 percent to USD 225 billion. The current fiscal year-to-date deficit was USD 407 billion, up 11 percent from a USD 367 billion deficit at this time last year.2016-06-10T19:11:02
