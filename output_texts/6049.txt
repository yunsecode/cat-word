Title: US Export Prices Rise More than Forecast
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise More than ForecastUnited States Export Prices MoMPrices for US exports increased 0.8 percent from a month earlier in April of 2021, following an upwardly revised 2.4 percent advance in March and above market expectations of a 0.6 percent gain. The price index for US exports has not recorded a monthly decline since a 3.5 percent drop in April 2020. A 0.9 percent advance in nonagricultural prices and a 0.6 percent rise in agricultural prices both contributed to the April increase in US export prices. Year-on-year, export prices rose 14.4 percent, the largest 12-month increase since the index was first published in September 1983.2021-05-14T12:33:28.453
