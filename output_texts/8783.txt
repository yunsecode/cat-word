Title: Wall Street Tumbles as Coronavirus Cases Surge
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Tumbles as Coronavirus Cases SurgeUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks sank at the open on Monday, following its Asian and European peers, amid renewed concerns about the global impact of the coronavirus after the  number of cases outside China increased, namely in Italy, South Korea and Iran. The Dow Jones plummet 3%, the S&P 500 went down 2.8% and the Nasdaq tumbled 3.3% around 5 minutes after the opening bell.
