Title: Wall Street Opens Nearly Flat
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Nearly FlatUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the United States opened flat to lower on Tuesday, failing to keep Monday gains despite a rebound in oil prices and as investors were waiting for new economic data for further clues on the timing of Fed’s next rate hike. The Dow Jones was down 19 points or 0.1%, while the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq traded near the flatline around 15 minutes after the opening bell.
