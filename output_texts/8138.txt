Title: US Futures Rebound, Dow Up more than 600 Points
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Rebound, Dow Up more than 600 PointsUnited States Stock MarketStock futures in the US traded sharply higher on Friday, attempting to rebound from more than a 5% decline in the previous session. Dow was gaining more than 600 points, the S&P 500 more than 60 points and the Nasdaq more than 150 points as investors digest the probability of a second wave of coronavirus infections as some states including Texas, California and Florida reported a resurgence in new cases. Houston-area officials are “getting close” to reimpose stay-at-home orders. On Thursday, the Dow Jones plummeted 1862 points or 6.9% to 25,128. The S&P 500 tanked 188 points or 5.9% to 3002. The Nasdaq plunged 528 points or 5.3% to 9493.2020-06-12T09:40:00
