Title: Wall Street Opens Mostly Higher on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Mostly Higher on FridayUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened mostly in the green on Friday, with the Dow and the S&P hitting fresh all time highs. Better than expected earnings results from Citigroup and retail sales data beating market consensus boosted investors' mood. The Dow Jones added 0.14% or 25 points, the S&P 500 rose 0.1% or 2 points and the Nasdaq traded near the flatline around 10:00 AM New York time.2016-07-15T15:18:46
