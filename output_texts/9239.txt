Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States decreased by 0.282 million barrels in the week ended May 24th 2019, following a 4.74 million rise in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 0.857 million drop. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories increased 2.204 million, after a 3.716 million gain in the prior week and against market consensus of a 0.528 million decrease.2019-05-30T15:02:07.8
