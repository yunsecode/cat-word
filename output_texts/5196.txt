Title: US Wholesale Inventories Unrevised in August
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Unrevised in AugustUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US rose 1.2 percent month-over-month to $731.1 billion in August of 2021, in line with the preliminary estimate and accelerating from a 0.6 percent increase in July. It was the 13th consecutive month of gains, amid increases in both durable goods (1.2 percent vs 1.2 percent in July) and non-durable goods inventories (1.1 percent vs -0.3 percent). On a yearly basis, wholesale inventories advanced 12.3 percent in August. 2021-10-08T14:04:02.057
