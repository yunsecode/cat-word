Title: US 30-Year Mortgage Rate Hits 6.8%
Tag: Mortgage Rate

US 30-Year Mortgage Rate Hits 6.8%United States Mortgage RateThe average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($647,200 or less) in the US reached 6.81% in the first week of October, a new high since July of 2006 and compared to 6.75% in the previous week. Rates have been tracking a rise in Treasury yields and were much lower at 3.1% a year earlier. “The news that job growth and wage growth continued in September is positive for the housing market, as higher incomes support housing demand. However, it also pushed off the possibility of any near-term pivot from the Federal Reserve on its plans for additional rate hikes,” Michael Fratantoni, MBA’s chief economist said.2022-10-12T11:13:00
