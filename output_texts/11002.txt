Title: US Stocks Rise After Tariff Exclusions
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise After Tariff ExclusionsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Thursday as President Trump excluded Mexico and Canada from steel and aluminum tariffs and left the door opened for other countries. The Dow Jones added 94 points or 0.4% to 24895; the S&P 500 went up 12 points or 0.5% to 2739; the Nasdaq closed 31 points or 0.4% higher at 7428. 2018-03-08T21:51:50
