Title: US Export Prices Fall for 3rd Month
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Fall for 3rd MonthUnited States Export Prices MoMUS export prices declined 0.8 percent from a month earlier in September of 2022, slightly below market expectations of a 1 percent drop and easing from an upwardly revised 1.7 percent decrease in the previous year. It was the third consecutive monthly decreases in export prices with contributions from both agricultural (-1 percent) and non-agricultural (-0.9 percent) outbound sales. On a yearly basis, prices for US exports rose by 9.5 percent, easing from the downwardly revised 10.7 percent jump in the prior month.2022-10-14T12:37:00
