Title: Overseas Investors Buy $138.7B US Assets in April
Tag: Capital Flows

Overseas Investors Buy $138.7B US Assets in AprilUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought USD 138.7 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments in April of 2018, after selling USD 38.5 billion in the previous month. Meanwhile, foreigners bought USD 93.9 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying USD 61.8 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors sold USD 4.8 billion of Treasures in April after selling USD 4.9 billion in the previous month.2018-06-16T11:32:54.787
