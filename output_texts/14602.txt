Title: US Jobless Claims Rise in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending December 7, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 368,000, an increase of 68,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 300,000. The 4-week moving average was 328,750, an increase of 6,000 from the previous week's revised average of 322,750.2013-12-12T13:34:30.503
