Title: Wall Street Cuts Losses
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Cuts LossesUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks erased most losses after falling as much as 2% at the open on Thursday, as investors were assessing the Russian invasion of Ukraine and took advantage of lower valuations. US President Joe Biden announced additional sanctions on Russian banks and said the US would release additional barrels of oil as conditions warrant but felt short of barring Russia from the Swift network. Meanwhile, better than expected weekly jobless claims also tempered the gloomy mood across markets, while economic growth to end 2021 was slightly better than initially reported. Heading into the last hour of trading, the Dow Jones was down only about 300 points after falling to levels not seen since March last year; the S&P 500 turned positive; and the Nasdaq rose almost 2% as investors moved back to the safety trade of big tech after a rough open. 2022-02-24T19:48:00
