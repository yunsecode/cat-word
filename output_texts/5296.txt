Title: US Business Inventories Continue to Rise
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Continue to RiseUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the US increased 0.5% mom in July of 2021, following an upwardly revised 0.9% rise in June and in line with market expectations. It was the 13th consecutive month of gains in business inventories. Stocks were up at wholesalers (0.6%), manufacturers (0.5%) and retailers (0.4%). Year-on-year, business inventories jumped 7.2%.2021-09-16T14:06:45.123
