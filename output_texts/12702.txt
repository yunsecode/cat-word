Title: NY Business Activity Rebounds in November
Tag: Ism New York Index

NY Business Activity Rebounds in NovemberUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM NY Current Business Conditions Index rose to 52.5 in November of 2016, the highest in four months from 49.2 in October. The reading pointed to an expansion of a business sector as volume of purchases rebounded (56 from 44.3 in October) and faster pace of growth was reported for: employment (52.3 from 50.6), current revenues (52.1 from 51.4), expected revenues (66.7 from 60.3) and six-month outlook (60.8 from 56.9). Prices paid also went up (69.2 from 55.6). 2016-12-02T15:00:26
