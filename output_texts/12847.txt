Title: US Stocks Gain on Strong China Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Gain on Strong China DataUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks rose slightly on Tuesday, boosted by stronger than expected Chinese economic activty data for October while investors remained cautious ahead of the Fed's monetary policy decision on Wednesday. The Dow Jones increased 29 points, or 0.2%; the S&P 500 added 5 points, or 0.2%; and the Nasdaq advanced 12 points, or 0.2%, around 5 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-11-01T13:33:32
