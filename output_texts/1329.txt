Title: US Futures Fall, Fed and Earnings Eyed
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Fall, Fed and Earnings EyedUnited States Stock MarketUS futures kicked off the week in the red, with contracts on the Dow Jones futures falling over 200 points while both the S&P and Nasdaq lost around 1% each, as investors take a cautious approach ahead of a key Fed policy decision. The central bank is expected to raise the fed funds rate by a smaller 25bps on Wednesday, following a 50bps increase in December. Traders also brace for a big week of earnings, including results from McDonald’s, General Motors, Apple, Meta, Amazon and Alphabet. Last week, the Dow gained 1.8%, the S&P 500 2.5% and the Nasdaq 4.3%, and are on track to book its best start to the year since 1999.2023-01-30T09:56:51.24
