Title: US Core PCE Inflation Surpasses Expectations
Tag: Core PCE Price Index Annual Change

US Core PCE Inflation Surpasses ExpectationsUnited States Core PCE Price Index Annual ChangeThe US core PCE price index, the Federal Reserve’s preferred gauge to measure inflation, rose by 4.6% annually in March of 2023, easing from the 4.7% in the previous month and matching the 13-month low from December 2022. The result overshot market expectations of 4.5%, raising bets that the Federal Reserve will commit to its hawkish stance and leave rates elevated for a prolonged period.2023-04-28T12:52:55.263
