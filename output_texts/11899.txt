Title: US Consumer Credit Rises More Than Expected In May
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Rises More Than Expected In MayUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States rose by USD 18.4 billion in May 2017, following an upwardly revised USD 12.9 billion gain in April and beating market expectations of USD 12.6 billion. Revolving credit increased by USD 7.4 billion after rising by USD 1.2 billion in the previous month. Nonrevolving credit went up by USD 11.0 billion, compared with a USD 11.8 billion gain in April.2017-07-10T19:04:25.6
