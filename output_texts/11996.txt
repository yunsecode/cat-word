Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose by 2.8 percent in the week ended June 9th 2017, following a 7.1 percent increase in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went up 9.2 percent while applications to purchase a home shrank 2.8 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged down by 1bps to 4.13 percent, the lowest since November of 2016.2017-06-14T11:05:53.257
