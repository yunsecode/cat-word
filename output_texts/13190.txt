Title: Wall Street Ends Mixed Amid Oil Slump
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Ends Mixed Amid Oil SlumpUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US closed mixed on Monday as falling oil prices and lower-than-expected manufacturing activity data weighed on investors' sentiment. The Dow Jones lost 0.15% or 27.73 points, the S&P 500 decreased 0.13% or 2.76 points. In contrast, the Nasdaq added 0.43% or 22.07 points led by gains in tech sector. 2016-08-01T21:24:01
