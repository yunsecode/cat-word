Title: US Wages Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings in the United States rose by 0.4 percent or by 10 cents to $26 in December from November of 2016. It follows 2 cents (-0.1 percent) decline in the previous period, beating market expectations of a 0.3 percent rise. Over the year, average hourly earnings have risen by 2.9 percent, the biggest gain since 2009.2017-01-06T14:57:04
