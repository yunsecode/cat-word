Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yields Below 3.8%
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yields Below 3.8%United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note fell 11bps to 3.7% to kick off a holiday-shortened week, the lowest in two weeks, after touching 3.85% on June 14th after the Federal Reserve kept the fed funds rate steady as expected, but signalled further hikes would be necessary. Traders remain focused on the monetary policy outlook and eagerly await comments from several Fed officials this week, specially Chair Powell semiannual report to Congress for further clues on the central bank's next steps. Around 72% of market participants expect the Fed to raise rates by 25bps next month.27 days ago
