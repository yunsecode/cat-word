Title: US Stocks Fall on Trade Worries
Tag: stock market

US Stocks Fall on Trade WorriesUnited States stock marketWall Street opened in the red on Monday amid rising trade concerns after news that President Trump will impose restrictions on Chinese investment in US companies and start-ups. The Dow dropped 0.8%, the S&P 500 fell 0.7% and the Nasdaq lost 1.2% around 12 minutes after the opening bell.2018-06-25T13:47:00
