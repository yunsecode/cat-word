Title: US Treasury Yields Rise on China Stimulus Measures
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Rise on China Stimulus MeasuresUnited States Government Bond 10YThe US benchmark 10-year yield rose by 7.7bps in morning trading to 1.5357% on Tuesday as concerns about the impact of the coronavirus on the global economy eased after China's central bank injected a total of 1.7 trillion yuan through reverse repos in the banking system, saying its determined to stabilize financial market expectations.2020-02-04T14:33:00
