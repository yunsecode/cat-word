Title: US Q3 Nonfarm Labour Productivity Rises 3.1% QoQ
Tag: Productivity

US Q3 Nonfarm Labour Productivity Rises 3.1% QoQUnited States ProductivityNonfarm business sector labor productivity in the United States increased at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 3.1 percent during the third quarter of 2016, following a 0.2 percent decline in the previous period and in line with preliminary figures. It was the first increase after three consecutive declines, as output grew by 3.6 percent and hours worked rose by 0.5 percent. Year-on-year, productivity was unchanged.2016-12-06T13:37:04
