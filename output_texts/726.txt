US Crude Inventories Drop More than Expected: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the US fell by 2.675 million barrels in the week that ended April 14th, 2023, above the market expectation of a 2.464 million decline and following a 0.377 million barrel increase in the previous week, data from the API's Weekly Statistical Bulletin showed.
