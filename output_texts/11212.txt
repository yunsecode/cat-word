Title: US Factory Growth at Near 3-Year High: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Factory Growth at Near 3-Year High: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit Manufacturing PMI for the US rose to 55.5 in January of 2018 from 55.1 in December and slightly above market expectations of 55, preliminary estimates showed. The reading pointed to the strongest expansion in factory activity since March of 2015 amid faster rises in production volumes and incoming new work. Also, export sales expanded the most since August of 2016 and more favourable demand conditions encouraged another robust rise in employment, although the rate of job creation eased slightly from December’s 39-month peak.2018-01-24T14:49:18
