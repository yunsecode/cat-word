Title: US Labor Costs Rise The Most Since 2007
Tag: Employment Cost Index

US Labor Costs Rise The Most Since 2007United States Employment Cost IndexCompensation costs for civilian workers increased by 0.8 percent quarter-on-quarter in the first three month of 2017, following a 0.5 percent rise in the previous period and above market expectations of a 0.6 percent growth. It was the largest increase since Q4 of 2007. Wages and salaries, which make up about 70 percent of compensation costs, increased by 0.8 percent (0.5 percent in Q4); and benefits, which make up the remaining 30 percent, climbed by 0.7 percent (0.5 percent in Q4).2017-04-28T12:39:39
