Title: US Inflation Rate at Over 6-Year High
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate at Over 6-Year HighUnited States Inflation RateThe inflation rate in the US increased to 2.8 percent in May of 2018 from 2.5 percent in April, beating market forecasts of 2.7 percent. It is the highest inflation rate since February of 2012. On a monthly basis, consumer prices edged up 0.2 percent, the same as in April and in line with expectations. Gasoline and shelter made the largest upward contributions.2018-06-12T12:33:29.887
