Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise Slightly in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise Slightly in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States edged up 0.1 percent in the week ending August 11th, 2017, following a 3 percent rise in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went up 1.6 percent while applications to purchase a home fell 1.5 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate declined by 2bps to 4.12 percent, reaching the lowest since November.
