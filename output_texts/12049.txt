Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down In May
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down In MayUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States was at 97.1 in May of 2017, only slightly up from 97 in April and below initial estimates of 97.7. Both current and future expectations were revised down, final figures showed. Yet, it is still the highest reading in four months.2017-05-26T14:14:01
