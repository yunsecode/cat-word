Title: US Nonfarm Labour Costs Rise Less Than Expected In Q4
Tag: Labour Costs

US Nonfarm Labour Costs Rise Less Than Expected In Q4United States Labour CostsNonfarm business sector unit labor costs in the United States increased by an annualized 1.7 percent on quarter in the fourth quarter of 2016, following a downwardly revised 0.2 percent growth in the previous period but missing market expectations of 1.9 percent gain. Hourly compensation went up by 3 percent and productivity advanced by 1.3 percent, the preliminary estimate showed. Considering full year of 2016, unit labor costs rose by 2.6 percent, the largest increase since 2007.2017-02-02T13:33:17
