Title: US Stocks Rebound from Last Week's Rout
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound from Last Week's RoutUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones and the S&P 500 bounced back on Monday as stronger-than-expected business activity data and promising developments toward a coronavirus vaccine raised hopes of a faster-than-expected global economic recovery. IHS Markit said their US manufacturing and services PMI’s hit multiyear highs, sparking a broader rotation into cyclical and value shares. Energy, industrials and financials, tied to a reviving economy, led all five of eight major Dow Jones sectors in the green, with Boeing and Chevron among the biggest gainers, up 6% and 4%, respectively. Meanwhile, the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite fell into negative territory as investors dropped technology shares.  2020-11-23T19:25:00
