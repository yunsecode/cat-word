Title: Dow And Nasdaq Hit Record High
Tag: Stock Market

Dow And Nasdaq Hit Record High United States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Tuesday with the Dow and the Nasdaq hitting fresh highs as the earnings season continues: GM earnings and profit beat expectations while Michael Kors earnings beat forecasts but revenues were lower than expected. The Dow Jones went up 86 points or 0.4% to a new high of 20139.25; the Nasdaq gained 15 points or 0.3% to a new high of 5678.53 and the S&P 500 added 6 points or 0.3% around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2017-02-07T14:58:47
