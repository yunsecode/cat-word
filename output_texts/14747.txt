U.S. Annual Inflation Down to 1.4% in JulyUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) was unchanged in July on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on August 15th.
