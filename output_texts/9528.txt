Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Fall United States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil stocks decreased by 3.862 million barrels in the week ended March 8th of 2019, following a 7.069 million rise in the previous week and missing market expectations of a 2.824 million gain. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories fell by 4.624 million barrels, after a 4.227 million decline in the previous week.
