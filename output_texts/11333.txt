Title: US Housing Starts Unexpectedly Rise in November
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Unexpectedly Rise in NovemberUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States went up 3.3 percent month-over-month to an annualized rate of 1,297 thousand in November of 2017, the highest since October of 2016 and beating market expectations of a 3.2 percent fall. It follows a downwardly revised 1,256 thousand in October. Single family starts jumped 5.3 percent to 930 thousand, the highest since September of 2007; while multi-family segment rose 0.8 percent to 359 thousand.2017-12-19T13:38:22
