Title: US Imports Fall 0.7% In March
Tag: Imports

US Imports Fall 0.7% In MarchUnited States ImportsTotal imports to the United States dropped 0.7 percent month-over-month to USD 234.7 billion in March 2017, as imports of capital goods fell 1.8 percent from a two-year high and those of civilian aircraft shrank 27.1 percent. Meanwhile, purchases of automobiles and parts rose 3.9 percent. On a non-seasonally adjusted basis, imports of goods rose 4.4 percent from China to USD 34.2 billion, boosted by cellphones. Also, purchases increased from Mexico (17.3 percent), the EU (13.9 percent) and Canada (13 percent).2017-05-04T12:38:49
