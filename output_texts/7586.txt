Title: US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly FallUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil stocks fell by 1.980 million barrels in the week ended September 25th, 2020, the third consecutive week of decline and compared to market expectations of a 1.569 million rise, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories were up by 0.683 million barrels, while markets had forecast a 1.083 million drop.2020-09-30T14:33:00
