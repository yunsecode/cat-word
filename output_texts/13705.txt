Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 4-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 4-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment fell to 90.7 in February of 2016 from 92 in January, much lower than market expectations of 92. It is the weakest figure in four months, due to lower current and future expectations and lower inflation outlook.
