Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall For 4th Straight Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall For 4th Straight Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States decreased sharply by 9.2 percent in the week ended November 11th 2016 from the prior period, following a 1.2 percent fall in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It was the fourth consecutive month of decline. Refinance applications went down 10.9 percent and applications to purchase a home dropped 6.2 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates went up 18bps to 3.95 percent, the highest since January.2016-11-16T12:03:11.98
