Title: US Stocks Rebound After Spike In Consumer Confidence
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound After Spike In Consumer ConfidenceUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Tuesday and the Dow Jones halted an eight-day slide, as banks stocks led gains after a surge in consumer confidence renewed optimism in US economic growth. The Dow Jones increased by 151 points, or 0.7 percent, to 20702. The S&P 500 rose by 17 points, or 0.7 percent, to 2359. The Nasdaq gained 35 points, or 0.6 percent, to 5875. 2017-03-28T20:02:58
