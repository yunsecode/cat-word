Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States decreased 3.9 percent in the week ended May 29th, down from a 2.7 percent rise in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications declined 8.6 percent while applications to purchase a home climbed 5.3 percent. Meantime, the average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went down 5bps to a record low of 3.37 percent.2020-06-03T11:02:00
