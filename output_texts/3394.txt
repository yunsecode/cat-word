Title: US Jobless Rate Above Forecasts
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Above ForecastsUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate was unchanged at 3.6 percent in April of 2022, remaining the lowest since February 2020 and compared with market expectations of 3.5 percent. The number of unemployed people declined by 11 thousand to 5.941 million, while employment levels fell by 353 thousand to 158.105 million. Meanwhile, the labor force participation rate fell to a 3-month low of 62.2 percent in April, from 62.4 percent in March.2022-05-06T12:35:00
