US Composite PMI Revised Down in August: PMIUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit Composite PMI for the US came in at 55.3 in August of 2017, down from a preliminary reading of 56 but above 54.6 in July. It is the highest reading since January as the services sector expanded at the fastest since November of 2015 (56 from 54.7) while manufacturing growth slowed (52.8 from 53.3). 2017-09-06T14:29:05.96
