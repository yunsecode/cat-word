Title: US Homebuilder Sentiment Stronger than Forecast
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Homebuilder Sentiment Stronger than ForecastUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB housing market index in the US climbed 21 points from the previous month to 58 in June 2020, easily beating market expectations of 45, as several states lifted the lockdowns restrictions imposed to combat COVID-19. The current single-family sub-index rose to 63 from 42 in May and the one for prospective buyers went up to 43 from 21. Additionally, the gauge for home sales over the next six months increased to 68 from 46.2020-06-16T14:09:00
