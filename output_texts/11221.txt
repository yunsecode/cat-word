Title: US Stocks Hit Record High After Senate Deal Reached
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Hit Record High After Senate Deal ReachedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street rebounded to trade in the green on Monday, with the 3 main stock indexes reaching record highs after news that the Senate reached a deal to end the US government shutdown. The Dow Jones increased 86 points or 0.3% to 26158; the S&P 500 went up 15 points or 0.5% to 2825 and the Nasdaq traded 52 points or 0.7% higher at 7388 around 12:40 PM NY time.
