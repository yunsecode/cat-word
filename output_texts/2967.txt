Title: Wall Street Set to Rally at the Open, GS Results Beat
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Set to Rally at the Open, GS Results BeatUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks are set to extend Friday's rally and open sharply higher on Monday, as investors scaled back expectations the Fed will deliver a big rate hike next week. Traders now mostly bet on a 75bps increase in the fed funds rate, instead of 100bps last week. Meanwhile, focus turns to the earnings season which enters a busy phase. Goldman Sachs profit, revenue and earnings topped estimates, sending the bank's shares more than 2% higher in pre-market trading. Stocks of Boeing jumped more than 4% in pre-market hours after the company announced that Delta Air Lines ordered 100 Boeing 737 Max jets. On the other hand, earnings from Bank of America came slightly below forecasts. Contracts on the three key index futures were up more than 1%.2022-07-18T12:05:00
