Title: US Consumer Credit Growth Misses Forecasts
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Growth Misses ForecastsUnited States Consumer CreditTotal consumer credit in the US went up $11.65 billion in December of 2022, after a $27.96 billion rise in the previous month and missing market expectations of a $25 billion rise. On an annual basis, consumer credit was up by 2.9 percent in December after a 7.1 percent gain in the prior month. Revolving credit, like credit cards, was up 7.3 percent, compared to a 16.9 percent rise in the prior month. Nonrevolving credit, typically auto and student loans, increased by 1.5 percent, following a 3.9 percent gain in the prior month.
