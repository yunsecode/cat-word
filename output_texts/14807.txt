Title: U.S. Economic Growth Revised up to 1.9% in Q1
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

U.S. Economic Growth Revised up to 1.9% in Q1United States GDP Growth RateReal gross domestic product in the United States increased at an annual rate of 1.9 percent in the first quarter of 2011, (that is, from the fourth quarter to the first quarter), according to the "third" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. In the fourth quarter, real GDP increased 3.1 percent.2011-06-27T14:05:18.177
