Title: US Stocks Erase Gains
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Erase GainsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks reversed early gains and turned negative around midday Wednesday despite stronger-than-expected earnings reports from IBM, United Technologies and Procter & Gamble, as persistent concerns about global economic outlook and falling oil prices weighed on sentiment. The Dow Jones edged 0.2% lower to 24,360; the S&P 500 lost 0.4% to 2,622; and the Nasdaq retreated 0.6% to 6,976 around 12:30 PM New York time.2019-01-23T17:34:00
