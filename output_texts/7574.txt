Title: US Government Bond Yields Fall as Trump Tests Positive for Coronavirus
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Government Bond Yields Fall as Trump Tests Positive for CoronavirusUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US benchmark 10-year Treasury note dropped to as low as 0.6528% on Friday as investors turned to safer assets after US President Trump and first lady Melania tested positive for COVID-19, with just weeks to go before the November presidential election. Investor focus will now shift to nonfarm payrolls and unemployment figures due later in the day, as well as consumer sentiment and factory orders.2020-10-02T09:30:00
