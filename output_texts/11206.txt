Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Wednesday 24 January 2018, with the Dow Jones indexes reaching record highs as earnings season continues, with Abbott Laboratories, United Technologies, Baker Hughes and Comcast beating market expectations. Meantime, the USD traded lower on comments from US Treasury Secretary Mnuchin, who said a weak dollar is good for the US economy. The Dow Jones gained 41 points or 0.2% to 26252. The S&P 500 edged down 2 points or 0.1% to 2838. The Nasdaq lost 45 points or 0.6% to 7415. 2018-01-24T21:09:04
