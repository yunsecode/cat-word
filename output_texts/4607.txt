Title: US Bond Selloff Continues
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Bond Selloff ContinuesUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury yield rallied for a 5th consecutive session to 1.75% on Thursday, the highest on a closing basis in 2 years, after the FOMC minutes showed the Fed will likely raise rates sooner than expected and braced for the possibility of cutting its bond holdings. Interest rate futures now roughly see an 80% chance of a rate hike in the fed funds rate at the March meeting. Meanwhile, the payrolls report due tomorrow will be in the spotlight and a strong reading would add to expectations for a rate hike this quarter. 2022-01-06T09:03:00
