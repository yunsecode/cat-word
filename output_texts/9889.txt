Title: US Wholesale Inventories Revised Higher in October
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Revised Higher in OctoberUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the United States increased 0.8 percent month-over-month to USD 652.1 billion in October 2018, slightly above a preliminary estimate of 0.7 percent and following an upwardly revised 0.7 percent rise in September. Automotive stocks jumped 3.4 percent (vs 2.5 percent in September) and machinery inventories climbed 2.1 percent (vs 1.4 percent). Stocks also rose at computer equipment (3.6 percent vs 2.5 percent), professional equipment (1.8 percent vs 1.1 percent) and electrical (0.9 percent vs 1.1 percent). Meanwhile, nondurable inventories fell 0.6 percent (vs -0.2 percent in September).  Year-on-year, inventories rose 6.9 percent.2018-12-07T15:04:00
