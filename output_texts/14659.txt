Title: U.S. Jobless Claims Up to 333K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

U.S. Jobless Claims Up to 333K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending August 3, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 333,000, an increase of 5,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 328,000. The 4-week moving average was 335,500, a decrease of 6,250 from the previous week's revised average of 341,750.2013-08-08T13:40:39.017
