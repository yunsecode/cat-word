Wall Street Closes MixedUnited States Stock MarketMarkets in the United States closed mixed on Wednesday after Fed Chair Yellen said tighter financial conditions and foreign developments could weigh down on the U.S. growth outlook in her testimony before Congress, decreasing expectations for a near-term rate hike. The Dow Jones lost 99 points or 0.62%, the S&P 500 ended near the flatline while the Nasdaq  gained 0.35%.2016-02-10T21:47:13
