U.S. Dollar: Whose problem is it now?United States CurrencyThe United States has been for a long time indifferent to a falling dollar. For example, in 1971, John Connally, U.S. Secretary of the Treasury, told a group of visiting Europeans that the dollar was “our currency, but your problem”.
