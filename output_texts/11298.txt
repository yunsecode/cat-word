Title: US Mortgage Applications Up 0.7% in Last Week of 2017
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Up 0.7% in Last Week of 2017United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 0.7 percent in the last week of 2017, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications increased 1.4 percent while applications to purchase a home edged down 0.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was 4.25 percent, the highest since April.2018-01-03T12:39:36
