Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Rises after Jobs Report
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Rises after Jobs ReportUnited States Government Bond 10YUS 10-year Treasury yields jumped to 0.657% on Friday, before giving up gains to trade at 0.631%, after latest data showed the US economy cut 20.5 million jobs in April, compared to market expectations of a 22 million loss, bringing the unemployment rate to 14.7%, also below forecasts of 16%. Meanwhile, two-year Treasury yields touched a record low of 0.105%.2020-05-08T13:23:00
