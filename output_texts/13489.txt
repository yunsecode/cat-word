Title: Wall Street Opens Lower
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens LowerUnited States Stock MarketShares in the US opened lower on Thursday following earlier losses in Asia and Europe after the BoJ disappointed markets by keeping monetary policy unchanged and GDP data showed the US economy slowed more than expected. The Dow Jones lost 0.57% or 103 points, the S&P 500 slid 0.4% or 8 points and the Nasdaq fell 0.2% or 10 points around 5 minutes after the opening bell.2016-04-28T14:51:01
