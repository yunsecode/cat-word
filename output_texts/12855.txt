Title: US Stocks Fall After FBI Restarts Clinton Email Probe
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall After FBI Restarts Clinton Email ProbeUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Friday, despite better-than-expected GDP growth figures for the third quarter, after the news that the FBI reopened an investigation into a Democratic presidential candidate Hillary Clinton's emails, raising uncertainties over elections results. The Dow Jones closed near the flatline, the S&P 500 went down 6 points or 0.3% and the Nasdaq lost 26 points or 0.5%. On a week, the Dow Jones edged up 0.1%, while the S&P 500 declined 1.2% and the Nasdaq fell 1.3%.2016-10-28T20:11:08
