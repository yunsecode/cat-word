Title: US Manufacturing Sector Slows for Second Straight Month
Tag: Business Confidence

US Manufacturing Sector Slows for Second Straight MonthUnited States Business ConfidenceIn January of 2014, the US ISM Manufacturing PMI registered 51.3 percent, a decrease of 5.2 percentage points from December's seasonally adjusted reading of 56.5 percent, due to a sharp fall in new orders.
