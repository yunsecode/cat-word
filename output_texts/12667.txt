Title: US Export Prices Fall Less Than Expected In November
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Fall Less Than Expected In NovemberUnited States Export Prices MoMUS export prices decreased 0.1 percent on month in November 2016, after increasing by 0.2 percent in October while better than market expectations of 0.2 percent drop. Prices of non-agricultural exports fell 0.1 percent, following a 0.2 percent growth, led by lower prices for industrial supplies and consumer goods. In contrast, agricultural exports' prices went up 0.6 percent, compared to a 0.5 percent growth in the previous month, driven by vegetables(+11.5 percent). Year-on-year, export prices declined 0.3 percent.2016-12-13T13:39:35.137
