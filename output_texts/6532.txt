House Approves $1.9T Relief PackageUnited States Government Budget ValueHouse Democrats have approved a massive $1.9 trillion coronavirus relief package designed to soften the economic blow from the pandemic, putting the world’s largest economy on a rosier path to recovery. The plan includes a new round of direct payments of up to $1,400 for most Americans, extends federal unemployment benefits of $300 a week and provides hundreds of billions in aid to state and local governments. 2021-03-10T23:16:00
