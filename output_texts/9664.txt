Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately in the green on Tuesday 5 February 2019, as investors await President Trump's State of the Union speech later in the day. The Dow Jones advanced 171 points or 0.7% to 25410. The S&P 500 added 13 points or 0.5% to 2738. The Nasdaq climbed 55 points or 0.7% to 7402.2019-02-05T21:05:04.813
