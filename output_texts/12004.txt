Title: US Tech Selloff Continues On Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Tech Selloff Continues On MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Monday, dragged down by tech shares, namely Apple, Microsoft and Alphabet. The tech selloff began on Friday amid rising concerns the sector is overvalued after a report from Goldman Sachs said gains in several big sector stocks this year was “cause for a pause”. The Dow Jones was down 30 points or 0.1%, the S&P 500 fell 7 points or 0.3% and the Nasdaq slumped 77 points or 1.3% around 12 minutes after the opening bell. 2017-06-12T13:56:54
