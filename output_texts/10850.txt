Title: US Consumer Sentiment Falls More than Expected
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Falls More than ExpectedUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US fell to 97.8 in April of 2018 from 101.4 in March and below market expectations of 100.5. Both current and future conditions subindexes declined, mainly due to concerns about the potential impact of President Trump's trade policies on the domestic economy, preliminary estimates showed. 2018-04-13T14:07:00
