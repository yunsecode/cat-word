Title: Foreigners Buy $125 Billion of US Assets in August
Tag: Capital Flows

Foreigners Buy $125 Billion of US Assets in AugustUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought USD 125 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments in August 2017 after selling USD 7.3 billion in July. Meanwhile, foreigners bought USD 67.2 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying USD 1.2 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors bought USD 11.5 billion of US treasuries, after selling USD 0.5 billion in July.2017-10-17T20:07:34.627
