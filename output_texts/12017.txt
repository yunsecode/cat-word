Title: US Services Sector Growth Slows Slightly In May: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Sector Growth Slows Slightly In May: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States fell to 56.9 in May of 2017 from 57.5 in April, slightly below market expectations of 57. Production and new orders rose at a slower pace while employment picked up. The majority of respondents’ comments continue to indicate optimism about business conditions and the overall economy.2017-06-05T14:08:29
