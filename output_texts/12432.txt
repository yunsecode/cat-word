Title: Stocks in United States Hit All-time High
Tag: stock market

Stocks in United States Hit All-time HighUnited States stock marketThe three major stock indexes in the US hit record highs on Thursday morning after President Trump said he plans to make an announcement on a much-anticipated tax reform in the next few weeks. The Dow Jones added 124 points or 0.6% to 201,177.93; the S&P 500 gained 12 points or 0.5% to 2306.66 and the Nasdaq traded 30 points or 0.5% higher at 5712.76 around 12:50 PM NY time.
