Title: US Jobless Claims Fall to 267K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall to 267KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits was 267,000 in the week ended April 2nd, a decrease of 9,000 from the previous week's level of 276,000. Figures came below market expectations of 270,000, marking the 57th straight week claims are below 300,000, the level associated with a healthy jobs market.2016-04-07T13:35:50.5
