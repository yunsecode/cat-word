Title: US Stock Futures Drop
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Drop United States Stock MarketUS stock futures were lower on Wednesday night as doubts over an additional round of US fiscal stimulus and worries about a new wave of lockdowns around the world curbed investors’ appetite for riskier assets. Sentiment also took a hit on news that Johnson & Johnson and Eli Lilly were halting its trials of an experimental Covid-19 vaccine and treatment, respectively, due to safety concerns. On Wednesday, the Dow Jones dropped 166 points or 0.6% to 28,514. The S&P 500 declined 23 points or 0.7% to 3489. The Nasdaq fell 95 points or 0.8% to 11,769.2020-10-15T02:03:50.883
