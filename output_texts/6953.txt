Title: US Futures Extend Declines
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Extend DeclinesUnited States Stock MarketUS Futures continued to retreat in early trade on Monday as market participants digested Biden’s US$1.9 trillion coronavirus rescue package, called the American Rescue Plan, which includes extra $1,400 checks for most Americans, a temporary boost in unemployment benefits, and a rise in the federal minimum wage to $15 per hour. Meantime, Biden is planning a 10-day blitz of executive action on what his administration is calling the “four crises” facing the country — Covid-19, the economic downturn, racial injustice and climate change. In other news, earnings season ramps up when investors return on Tuesday, while stock and bond markets closed on Monday for the Martin Luther King Jr. Day. In recent data, US retail trade fell 0.7% from a month earlier in December, the third consecutive month of declines. Last week, the Dow Jones retreated 177 points to 30,814. The S&P 500 shed 27 points to 3768. The Nasdaq lost 114 points to 12,999. 2021-01-18T00:19:00
