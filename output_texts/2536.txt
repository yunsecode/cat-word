Title: US Exports Hit Fresh Record High
Tag: Exports

US Exports Hit Fresh Record HighUnited States ExportsExports from the United States increased by 1.7 percent or USD 4.3 billion from the previous month to USD 260.8 billion in June of 2022, the highest since the series began in 1950. Exports of goods increased by USD 3.5 billion, driven by industrial supplies and materials (USD 4.7 billion) such as nonmonetary gold (USD 1.8 billion) and natural gas (USD 1.6 billion). Meanwhile, sales of foods, feeds, and beverages increased by USD 0.9 billion while those of civilian aircraft decreased by USD 0.8 billion. Exports of services grew by USD 0.7 billion to USD 77.8 billion, particularly travel (USD 0.3 billion) and transport (USD 0.3 billion).2022-08-04T12:42:00
