Title: US Natural Gas Stocks Rise More than Expected: EIA
Tag: Natural Gas Stocks Change

US Natural Gas Stocks Rise More than Expected: EIAUnited States Natural Gas Stocks ChangeUS utilities added 125 billion cubic feet (bcf) of gas to storage during the week ended October 7th, above market expectations of a 123 bcf build due to mild weather and an increase in wind power. It compares with an increase of 86 in the same week last year and a five-year (2017-2021) average increase of 82 bcf.2022-10-13T14:37:08.5
