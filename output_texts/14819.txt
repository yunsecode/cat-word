Title: U.S. Jobless Drops to 8.8%, Payrolls Increase by 216K in March
Tag: Unemployment Rate

U.S. Jobless Drops to 8.8%, Payrolls Increase by 216K in MarchUnited States Unemployment RateU.S. Nonfarm payroll employment increased by 216,000 in March, and the unemployment rate was little changed at 8.8 percent, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on April 1. Job gains occurred in professional and business services, health care, leisure and hospitality, and mining. Employment in manufacturing continued to trend up.2011-04-01T12:39:18.357
