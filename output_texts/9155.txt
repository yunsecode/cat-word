Title: US Government Debt Yields Rally after GDP Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Government Debt Yields Rally after GDP DataUnited States Government Bond 10YUS Treasury yields rose sharply on Friday, after the advance estimate of US second-quarter GDP growth came in stronger than expected. The 10-year Treasury note climbed to 2.091%, the 30-year note yield advanced to 2.607%, while the yield on the 2-year note went up to 2.884%. 2019-07-26T13:01:00
