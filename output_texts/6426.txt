Tag: Currency

Dollar Retreats as Claims RiseUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index fell to as low as 93.08 on Thursday, before paring some losses to above 93.1 after latest data showed initial claims rose unexpectedly last week but investors continue to bet on a strong economic recovery amid further fiscal stimulus and coronavirus vaccination. US President Biden announced a $2.25 trillion package, the second major stimulus plan after the $1.9 trillion bill. However, the new spending push is likely to get strong GOP opposition, specially over a corporate tax hike increase to 28%.2021-04-01T12:49:00
