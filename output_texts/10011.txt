Title: US Economic Optimism Index Edges Down in November
Tag: Economic Optimism Index

US Economic Optimism Index Edges Down in NovemberUnited States Economic Optimism IndexThe IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index decreased to 56.4 in November 2018 from 57.8 in October, missing market expectations of 59.2. The Personal Financial Outlook, a measure of how Americans feel about their own finances in the next six months, fell 4.4 points to 62.3 and the Six-Month Economic Outlook, a measure of how consumers feel about the economy’s prospects in the next six months, edged down by 0.1 points to 53.2. Conversely, the Confidence in Federal Economic Policies, a proprietary IBD/TIPP measure of views on how government economic policies are working, went up 0.3 points to 53.7. 2018-11-06T15:05:00
