Title: US Services PMI Revised Up In January: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services PMI Revised Up In January: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe final Markit US Services PMI increased to 55.6 in January of 2017 from a preliminary of 55.1 and 53.9 in December. It is the highest reading since November of 2015, driven by an increase in incoming new work, higher levels of activity and backlogs of work while payrolls eased slightly. In addition, greater willingness to spend among clients and signs of an upturn in domestic economic conditions contributed to a stronger business optimism, the highest since May of 2015.2017-02-03T14:56:24.11
