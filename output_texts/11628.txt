Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Drop
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly DropUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 1.846 million barrels in the week ended September 22nd, 2017, following a 4.591 million rise in the previous period. It is the first decline in crude oil inventories in four weeks, compared to market expectations of a 3.422 million increase. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks increased by 1.107 million following a 2.125 million slump in the previous week. It marks the first decline in four weeks after suppliers drew from tank storage to replace gallons lost from oil refineries flooded and destroyed during Hurricane Harvey. 2017-09-27T14:36:44.03
