Title: US Consumer Credit Rises Less than Expected
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Rises Less than ExpectedUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States rose by USD 13.06 billion in August of 2017, following a downwardly revised USD 17.72 billion gain in July and below market expectations of USD 16 billion. Revolving credit increased by USD 5.76 billion after rising by USD 2.06 billion in the previous month. Non-revolving credit went up by USD 7.31 billion, compared with a USD 15.65 billion gain in July.2017-10-06T19:03:59.897
