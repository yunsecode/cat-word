Title: US Stock Futures Retrace Record Highs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Retrace Record HighsUnited States Stock MarketUS Futures eased from record highs in early Asian trade Monday, following the 3rd consecutive week of gains amid the US Federal Reserve’s expectations of a swifter economic recovery as investors paused ahead of US earnings, while bond markets remained elevated ahead of potentially strong readings for inflation and retail sales this week. Traders were also monitoring fallout from Alibaba Group Holdings after China hit the e-commerce giant with a record US$2.75 billion fine. US 10-year bond yields were at 1.673%, as Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell noted that the US economy is at a turning point thanks to government support and a speedy campaign to inoculate Americans against Covid-19. On the data front, US producer prices rose more than expected in March, recording the biggest annual gain in 9-1/2 years. Last week, the Dow added more than 1%, the S&P 500 gained nearly 2%, while the Nasdaq Composite rallied 2.4%.2021-04-12T00:26:00
