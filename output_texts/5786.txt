Title: US Chicago PMI Falls from 48-Year Highs
Tag: Chicago PMI

US Chicago PMI Falls from 48-Year HighsUnited States Chicago PMIThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer in the US fell to 66.1 in June of 2021 from 75.2 in May which was the highest since November of 1973 and below market forecasts of 70. largest decline, among the main five indicators, order backlogs saw the largest decline, with supplier deliveries posting the only gain. Through Q2 2021, the index surged 7.9 points to 71.1, its highest quarterly reading since Q4 1973. 2021-06-30T14:26:00
