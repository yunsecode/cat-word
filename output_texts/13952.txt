Title: US Business Inventories Rise 0.3% in September
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Rise 0.3% in SeptemberUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories increased unexpectedly by 0.3 percent month-on-month to $1,817.5 billion in September of 2015, following an upwardly revised 0.1 percent growth in the previous month and beating market expectations. Retail inventories excluding autos, which go into the calculation of GDP, increased by 0.5 percent in September after an upwardly revised 0.5 percent gain (against +0.4 percent previously estimated) in August. Year-on-year, business inventories were up 2.5 percent from $1,773.2 a year earlier. Economists expect that third quarter GDP can be revised up later this month.2015-11-13T15:08:41
