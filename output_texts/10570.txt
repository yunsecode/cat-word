Title: US Private Sector Activity Growth Higher than Expected
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Activity Growth Higher than Expected United States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI fell to 56 in June of 2018 from 56.6 in May, still above market expectations of 55.1, the flash estimate showed. The reading pointed to a weaker pace of expansion in the private sector activity, as both services (PMI at 56.5 vs 56.8 in May) and manufacturing sectors (PMI at 54.6 vs 56.4) eased. New business slowed to a six-month low and employment growth increased at a softer pace. Finally, expectations for the next twelve months were the least upbeat since January. On the price front, cost pressures persisted driven by higher manufacturing input prices, average prices charged by private sector firms increased at its fastest pace since September of 2014.2018-06-22T14:02:00
