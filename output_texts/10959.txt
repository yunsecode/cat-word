Title: US Mortgage Applications Decline for 1st Time in 4 Weeks
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Decline for 1st Time in 4 WeeksUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 1.1 percent in the week ended March 16th 2018, following a 0.9 percent increase in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications shrank 4.5 percent while applications to purchase a home went up 1.4 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged down by 1bps 4.68 percent2018-03-21T11:06:33
