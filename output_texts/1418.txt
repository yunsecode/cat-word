Title: US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly Rise
Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly RiseUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 7.615 million barrels in the week ended January 13th, 2023, after a record high of 14.865 million barrels gain in the previous week, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed. It came above analyst expectations of a 1.750 million barrels decline.
