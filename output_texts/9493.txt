Title: US Posts Largest Budget Gap Ever for February
Tag: Government Budget Value

US Posts Largest Budget Gap Ever for FebruaryUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US government budget deficit increased to USD 234 billion in February of 2019 from a USD 215 billion gap a year earlier and above market expectations of a USD 227 billion shortfall. It is the highest budget gap ever for a February month as federal spending rose 8 percent on the year while receipts increased only 7 percent. 2019-03-22T18:05:00
