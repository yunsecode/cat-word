Title: US Factory Growth Slows More than Expected: ISM
Tag: Business Confidence

US Factory Growth Slows More than Expected: ISMUnited States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management’s Manufacturing PMI in the US fell to 59.8 in September of 2018 from 61.3 in August which was the highest reading since May of 2004. Figures came slightly below market expectations of 60.1 as new orders and inventories rose less. Manufacturers remain concerned about tariff-related activity, including how reciprocal tariffs will impact company revenue and current manufacturing locations.2018-10-01T14:09:27.66
