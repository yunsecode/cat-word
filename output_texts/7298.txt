Title: Dow Jones Fails to Hold Gains
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Jones Fails to Hold GainsUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones swung between gains and losses on Wednesday, pausing a 2-day rally which was triggered by prospects of a working coronavirus vaccine. Meanwhile, the Nasdaq and the S&P 500 rebounded from the previous session losses, boosted by tech shares, following a sell-off earlier in the week. On the coronavirus front, US reported a single-day record of more than 130,000 new cases on Tuesday and the number of hospitalizations surpassed its April peak. 2020-11-11T15:20:00
