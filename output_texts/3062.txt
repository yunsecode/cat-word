Title: US Jobless Claims Fall Less than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing new claims for unemployment benefits decreased by 2K to 231K in the week that ended June 25th, compared with market forecasts of 228K, pointing to tight labour conditions. On a non-seasonally adjusted basis, initial claims rose by 1,060 from the previous week to 207,421, with notable increases seen in New Jersey (+5,471), Massachusetts (+3,152), Ohio (+2,472) and Kentucky (+1.501). The 4-week moving average was 231,750, an increase of 7,250 from the previous week's upwardly revised average.2022-06-30T12:36:00
