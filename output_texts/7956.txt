Title: US Stock Futures Rally
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures RallyUnited States Stock MarketUS stock index futures rose overnight as major tech companies are expected to report quarterly results on Wednesday, with Microsoft and Tesla among the most highly awaited. Investors will also focus on further US coronavirus-related fiscal stimulus after $3 trillion was injected earlier this year to support the world’s largest economy. On Tuesday, the Dow Jones climbed 159 points or 0.6% to 26,840. The S&P 500 gained 5 points or 0.2% to 3257. The Nasdaq lost 87 points or 0.8% to 10,680.2020-07-22T01:00:00
