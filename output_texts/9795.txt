Title: US Stocks Plunge on Apple Warning, Weak Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Plunge on Apple Warning, Weak DataUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks extended losses Thursday afternoon led by a sell-off in technology shares after Apple cut its revenue forecast for the first time in nearly 12 years amid renewed concerns about China's economic slowdown. In addition, December's data showed the ISM Manufacturing PMI dropped the most since October 2008 to its lowest level since November 2016. The Dow Jones tumbled 2.3% to 22,806; the S&P 500 slumped 1.9% to 2,463; and the Nasdaq fell 2.3% to 6,513.2019-01-03T18:32:00
