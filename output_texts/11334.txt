Title: US Building Permits Fall Less than Expected
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States Building PermitsBuilding permits in the United States fell 1.4 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,298 thousand in November 2017, following a 7.4 percent jump in October while markets were expecting a bigger 3.1 percent drop. Multi-family homes permits declined 6.4 percent to 436 thousand while single-family authorizations rose 1.4 percent to 862 thousand. Building permits decreased in the Northeast (-5.7 percent), the Midwest (-4.7 percent) and the West (-3 percent), but rose in the South (1.4 percent).2017-12-19T13:33:38
