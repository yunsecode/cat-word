Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Hovers at 1-Year High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Hovers at 1-Year HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury note remained around 1.3% on Friday, close to near 1-year high of 1.33% touched on Wednesday. Treasury yields rallied this week amid inflationary concerns arising from strong economic activity and further fiscal stimulus, which means more debt and more Treasury issuance to pay for it. Still, Fed officials recently reiterated inflation is expected to move up and that they were still prepared to keep their easy monetary policy on track.2021-02-19T09:23:00
