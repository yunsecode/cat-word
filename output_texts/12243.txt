Title: US Trade Deficit Lower Than Expected
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit Lower Than ExpectedUnited States Balance of TradeThe goods and services deficit in the United States narrowed to USD 43.6 billion in February of 2017 from a downwardly revised USD 48.2 billion gap a month earlier and lower than market expectations of a USD 44.8 billion shortfall. Exports increased 0.2 percent and hit a new high since December of 2014. Shipments of automobiles and parts reached the highest since July 2014 and sales of industrial supplies and materials were the highest since December of 2015. In contrast, imports went down 1.8 percent.2017-04-04T12:29:04
