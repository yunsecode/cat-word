Title: Dollar Books 2nd Straight Weekly Loss
Tag: Currency

Dollar Books 2nd Straight Weekly LossUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded around 97.1 during a light trading session on Friday, as equity and bond markets in the US were closed for the Independence Day holiday. For the week, the dollar lost near 0.2%. Investors remain very concerned about the consequences of the second wave of the coronavirus in the US despite upbeat economic data released during the week, namely the ISM manufacturing PMI and the jobs report.2020-07-03T18:22:00
