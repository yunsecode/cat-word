Title: US Retail Sales Edge Up 0.2%
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Edge Up 0.2%United States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States increased 0.2 percent in November from October, slightly below market expectations of a 0.3 percent rise but the biggest gain in four months. Excluding autos, sales went up 0.4 percent, better than forecasts while core retail sales jumped 0.6 percent. 2015-12-11T13:36:51
