Title: US Corporate Profits Revised Lower in Q2
Tag: Corporate Profits

US Corporate Profits Revised Lower in Q2United States Corporate ProfitsCorporate profits in the United States rose 6.2 percent to a fresh record high of USD 2.53 trillion in the second quarter of 2022, less than previous estimates of a 9.1 percent surge and following a downwardly revised 2.5 percent drop in the previous period. Net cash flow with inventory valuation adjustment, the internal funds available to corporations for investment, rose 4.1 percent to USD 2.95 trillion, while net dividends fell 0.2 percent to USD 1.74 trillion. Meanwhile, undistributed profits skyrocketed 24 percent to USD 0.78 trillion. 2022-09-29T12:34:00
