Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most in 7 Weeks
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most in 7 WeeksUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 2.237 million barrels in the week ended November 3rd, 2017, following a 2.435 million drop in the previous period and compared with market expectations of a 2.876 million decline. It is the biggest increase in crude oil inventories in seven weeks. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went down by 3.312 million, following a 4 million drop in the previous week and much worse than market expectations of a 1.938 million decrease.
