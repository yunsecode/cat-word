Title: Wall Street Opens Higher on Economic Data, Oil
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Higher on Economic Data, OilUnited States Stock MarketUS equities traded higher on Thursday as investors sentiment was boosted by a rebound in oil prices, better than expected earnings' reports from Macy's and Kohl's and economic data. 
Jobless claims marked 75 consecutive week below 300,000, the longest streak since 1970 and exports and imports prices grew more than expected in July. The Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 70 points, or 0.4%; the S&P 500 index added 7 points, or 0.3%; and the Nasdaq Composite Index rose 25 points, or 0.5% around 15 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-08-11T14:46:34
