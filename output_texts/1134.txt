Title: US Capital Inflows at 6-Month Low
Tag: Capital Flows

US Capital Inflows at 6-Month LowUnited States Capital FlowsThe United States recorded a capital and financial account surplus of $28.6 billion in December of 2022, narrowing from an upwardly revised $213.4 billion surplus in the prior month. Of this, net foreign private inflows were $40 billion, while net foreign official outflows were $11.4 billion. Foreign residents increased their holdings of long-term US securities in the period, with net purchases at $103.3 billion, including $102.3 billion in net purchases by foreign investors and $1 billion from foreign official institutions.
