Title: US Output Growth Accelerates to 7-Month High: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Output Growth Accelerates to 7-Month High: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI was revised lower to 55.5 in February 2018 from 55.8 previously estimated and compared to January's 54.4. It was the highest reading since July, as a quicker upturn in business activity in the service sector (PMI at 56 vs 54.2 in January) offset the slowdown in manufacturing output (PMI at 53 vs 54.9 in January).2019-03-05T14:55:00
