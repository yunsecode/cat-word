Title: Wall Street Maintains Upswing
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Maintains UpswingUnited States Stock MarketUS stock indices recovered from morning losses on Thursday, with the Dow Jones up by 250 points while the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq added 1.4% and 2.2%, respectively, as news that key lenders will support First Republic Bank offered some respite for the troubled banking sector. It was reported that major US banks agreed to deposit $30 billion in the regional lender, sending its shares over 10% higher after opening 36% in the red. Earlier, Treasury Secretary Yellen told the Senate finance committee that the American financial system is in good shape despite the collapse of two midsize banks. On the data front, first-time filings for unemployment insurance declined while the housing starts and building permits showed the housing market is recovering. 2023-03-16T18:06:00
