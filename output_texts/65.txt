US Crude Oil Inventories Fall the Most in Over 1 Month: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the US dropped by 4.382 million barrels in the week that ended June 30th, 2023, marking the third consecutive draw and the sharpest since mid-May, data from the API's Weekly Statistical Bulletin showed. It extended the decrease of 2.408 million barrels the previous week.11 days ago
