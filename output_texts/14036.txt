Title: US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than Expected
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than ExpectedUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer borrowing in the US increased by $16.02 Billion in August, below market expectations but bringing the total to a fresh record of $3.47 trillion. Car and student loans rose by $12 billion while credit cards borrowing grew by $4 billion.
