Title: US Jobless Claims Lowest Since 1969
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Lowest Since 1969United States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits fell by 10 thousand to 210 thousand in the week ended February 24th 2018, from a downwardly revised 220 thousand in the previous week. The figure surprised markets who expected a rise in claims to 226 thousand. It was the lowest level since December of 1969 when it was 202 thousand. According to unadjusted data, the biggest declines in initial claims were seen in California (-8,742); Pennsylvania (-1,969); New Jersey (-1,554) and Texas (-1,351). Claims in Puerto Rico declined by 1,003 and in Virgin Islands by 22. Claims taking procedures in Puerto Rico and in the Virgin Islands have still not returned to normal. 2018-03-01T13:33:39
