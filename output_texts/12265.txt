Title: US Stocks Closed Mixed On Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Closed Mixed On MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Monday’s session, partially recovering from a sharp drop at open as weakness overseas following the decision by Republican leaders to withdraw their bill to repeal and replace Obamacare eased. The Dow Jones closed 46 points down, or 0.2 percent, to 20551. It is the 8th consecutive fall and the longest losing streak since 2011. The S&P 500 fell 2 points, or 0.1 percent, to 2342. In contrast, the Nasdaq increased by 12 points, or 0.2 percent, to 5840. 2017-03-27T20:02:58
