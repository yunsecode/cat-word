Title: US Consumer Sentiment Remains Near 13 Year High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Remains Near 13 Year HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe preliminary reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States rose to 97.6 in March of 2017 from 96.3 in the previous month and beating market forecasts of 97. Current conditions reached the highest since November of 2000, largely due to improved personal finances.
