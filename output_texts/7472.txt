Title: US Stocks Remain Under Pressure
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Remain Under PressureUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones and the S&P 500 moved slightly higher, while the Nasdaq fell into negative territory on Thursday afternoon, after House Speaker Pelosi said Democrat lawmakers and White House officials were “just about there” on a coronavirus aid package but warned that it could take “a while” for Congress to actually approve relief legislation. Investors will also keep an eye on the final debate between Donald Trump and Joe Biden, amid news that Iran and Russia are attempting to interfere with the presidential election. On the corporate front, earnings from Coca-Cola beat forecasts while those from AT&T came in line with expectations. Tesla quarterly results released late on Wednesday also topped estimates. In contrast, those for Kimberly Clark came slightly below forecasts. Intel is due to report today after markets close.2020-10-22T15:45:00
