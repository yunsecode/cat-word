Title: US Dollar Resumes The Rally
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Resumes The RallyUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index has extended its upward momentum, getting closer to a four-month high of 93.19 touched on Wednesday as demand for safe-heaven assets remains strong on worries about the further spread of the Delta COVID-19 variant. Also, while concerns are increasing about the US economic recovery, the pace of growth is still much stronger compared to other countries. Looking deeper, the dollar has been strengthening from mid-June after the Fed sharply raised its forecasts for inflation this year and brought forward the time frame on when it will hike interest rates.  2021-07-23T15:02:00
