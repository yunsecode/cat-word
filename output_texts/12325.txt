Title: US Import Prices Rise Despite A Drop In Fuel Prices
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Rise Despite A Drop In Fuel PricesUnited States Import Prices MoMPrices for US imports increased by 0.2 percent month-over-month in February 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.6 percent gain in January and above market consensus of a 0.1 percent rise. Nonfuel import prices rose 0.3 percent, the first monthly advance since July last year, led by rising prices for industrial supplies and materials; consumer goods; and foods, feeds, and beverages. Meanwhile, prices for import fuel fell 0.7 percent, the first downturn since November 2016. Year-on-year, import prices jumped 4.6 percent, the largest gain since February 2012.2017-03-09T13:33:42
