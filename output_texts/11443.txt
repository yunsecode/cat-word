Title: US Home Builder Confidence at 8-Month High
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Home Builder Confidence at 8-Month HighUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States rose 2 points to 70 in November of 2017, beating market expectations of 68. It is the highest reading since March. The component gauging current sales rose 2 points to 77; the index charting sales expectations in the next six months increased 1 point to 77; and the component measuring buyer traffic went up 2 points to 50.2017-11-16T15:09:13.017
