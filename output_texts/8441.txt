Title: US Goods Gap Rises amid Falling Exports
Tag: Goods Trade Balance

US Goods Gap Rises amid Falling Exports United States Goods Trade BalanceThe goods deficit in the US widened to USD 64.22 billion in March of 2020 from USD 59.9 billion in the previous month, which was the smallest goods trade gap since September of 2016. Exports plunged 6.7 percent to USD 127.6 billion, mainly due to a 17.8 percent drop in sales of automotive vehicles and a 7.5 percent decline in industrial supplies, an advance estimate showed. Imports fell at a slower 2.4 percent to USD 191.87 billion, mainly due to purchases of consumer goods (-8.3 percent). 2020-04-28T12:37:00
