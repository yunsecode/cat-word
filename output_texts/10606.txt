Title: US Retail Sales Post Biggest Gain in 6 Months
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Post Biggest Gain in 6 MonthsUnited States Retail Sales MoMUS retail trade rose by 0.8 percent month-over-month in May 2018, following an upwardly revised 0.4 percent advance in April and easily beating market expectations of a 0.4 percent gain. It was the steepest increase in retail trade since November, mainly boosted by higher sales at gasoline stations and motor vehicle & parts dealers.2018-06-14T12:32:00
