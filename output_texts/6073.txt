Title: Nasdaq Ends Sharply Lower in Tech Sell-Off
Tag: Stock Market

Nasdaq Ends Sharply Lower in Tech Sell-OffUnited States Stock MarketThe tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite plunged more than 2%, and the S&P 500 finished sharply lower on Monday, as investors ditched growth and technology names on growing worries about inflation and higher taxes. Growth-oriented stocks were likely to continue underperforming in this current market environment, with rising commodity prices and supply chain issues sparking concerns about higher inflation, which could erode the value of future earnings. Meanwhile, the blue-chip Dow's losses were limited by a rotation into value stocks as investors continued to move to groups that do well in an economic recovery. President Biden delivered remarks on the economy Monday and will meet with Republican and Democratic leaders on Wednesday to discuss several topics, including his $4 trillion legislative proposals. The Nasdaq lost 350 points or 2.6% to 13,402. The S&P 500 declined 44 points or 1% to 4,188. The Dow retreated 35 points or 0.1% to 34,743.2021-05-10T20:14:00
