Title: U.S. GDP Expands 2 Percent in Q3
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

U.S. GDP Expands 2 Percent in Q3United States GDP Growth RateU.S. Real gross domestic product increased at an annual rate of 2.0 percent in the third quarter of 2012 (that is, from the second quarter to the third quarter), according to the "advance" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.  In the second quarter, real GDP increased 1.3 percent.2012-10-26T13:35:03
