Title: US Stocks Hit New Records
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Hit New RecordsUnited States Stock MarketAll three major US stock market indexes reached intraday and closing records on Friday in spite of lower than expected growth in non farm payrolls and wages. The Dow Jones gained 61 points or 0.3 percent to 21206. The S&P 500 climbed 9 points or 0.4 percent to 2439. The Nasdaq jumped 59 points or 0.9 percent to 6306. All three major indices advanced during the week, with the Dow Jones rising 0.6 percent; the S&P 500 1.0 percent; and the Nasdaq 1.5 percent. 2017-06-02T20:06:33
