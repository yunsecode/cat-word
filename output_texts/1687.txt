Title: US Corporate Profits Edge Down in Q3
Tag: Corporate Profits

US Corporate Profits Edge Down in Q3United States Corporate ProfitsCorporate profits in the United States went down 0.2 percent to USD 2.52 trillion in the third quarter of 2022, after a 6.2 percent rise in the previous period. Net cash flow with inventory valuation adjustment, the internal funds available to corporations for investment, rose 5.2 percent to USD 3.11 trillion, while net dividends fell 3.1 percent to USD 1.69 trillion. Meanwhile, undistributed profits climbed 6.2 percent to USD 0.83 trillion.2022-11-30T13:35:18.127
