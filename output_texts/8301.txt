Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States increased 0.3 percent in the week ended May 8th 2020, following a 0.1 percent rise in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home increased for the fourth straight week by 10.6 percent while refinance applications dropped 3.3 percent. Meantime, the average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged up 3bps from a record low to 3.43 percent.2020-05-13T11:04:00
