Title: US Futures Reverse Gains
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Reverse GainsUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were nearly flat on Tuesday, pausing after a 4-day rally, as investors brace for the start of the earnings season and expect a rebound in corporate results. BlackRock, Citigroup, Johnson & Johnson, JPMorgan Chase and Delta Airlines are due to report today. Apple will also launch the first ever 5G iPhone and Amazon’s Prime Day also starts on Tuesday. Meanwhile, traders digest a pause in Johnson & Johnson’s Covid-19 vaccine study due to an unexplained illness in a study participant, and await further developments on the new stimulus bill. On Monday, the Dow Jones added 251 points or 0.9% to 28,838. The S&P 500 climbed 57 points or 1.6% to 3534. The Nasdaq jumped 296 points or 2.6% to 11,876.2020-10-13T07:19:00
