Title: US Building Permits Fall to Over 1-Year Low
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Fall to Over 1-Year LowUnited States Building PermitsBuilding permits in the United States dropped 5.7 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,229 thousand in August 2018, while markets were expecting a 0.1 percent decline to 1,310 thousand. Permits were at the lowest level since May 2017 as single-family authorizations fell 6.1 percent to 820 thousand and multi-family permits decreased 4.9 percent to 409 thousand. Across regions, permits declined in the Northeast (-19.2 percent to 101 thousand), West (-8.4 percent to 304 thousand), South (-2.9 percent to 646 thousand) and Midwest (-1.7 percent to 178 thousand).2018-09-19T12:36:00
