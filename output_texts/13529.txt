Title: US Inflation Rate Slows for 2nd Month
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Slows for 2nd MonthUnited States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States rose 0.9 percent year-on-year in March of 2016, lower than 1 percent in the previous month and below market expectations of a 1.1 percent increase. Cost of shelter, medical care and food slowed while energy prices declined slightly more. On a monthly basis, consumer prices rose 0.1 percent. 2016-04-14T13:36:34
