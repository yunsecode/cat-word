Title: US Core Inflation Rate Drops to Over 9-Year Low
Tag: Core Inflation Rate

US Core Inflation Rate Drops to Over 9-Year LowUnited States Core Inflation RateUS core consumer price inflation rate, which excludes volatile items such as food and energy, decreased to 1.2 percent year-on-year in May 2020 from 1.4 percent in the previous month and below market expectations of 1.3 percent. It was the lowest rate since March 2011 amid efforts to contain the rapid spread of the coronavirus pandemic.2020-06-10T12:32:52.287
