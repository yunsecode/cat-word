Title: Wall Street Fails to Sustain Gains on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Fails to Sustain Gains on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the United States opened mainly flat on Tuesday among mixed earnings reports. Goldman Sachs joined Citigroup, JP Morgan and Bank of America with better-than-anticipated results while Netflix and Yahoo disappointed. The Dow Jones and the Nasdaq opened near the flatline while the S&P 500 lost 3 points or 0.1% around 5 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-07-19T14:43:13.567
