Title: US Stocks Fall on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Thursday, following gains in the last four sessions, mainly dragged down by shares of retailers and airlines. Macy's reported weak sales results and lowered its forecast for earnings and revenues and American Airlines cut its revenue forecasts. The Dow Jones lost 0.5%, the S&P 500 fell 0.7% and the Nasdaq traded 0.9% lower around 6 minutes after the opening bell. 2019-01-10T14:45:22.183
