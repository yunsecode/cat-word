Title: US Retail Sales Post Biggest Drop in Over 9 Years
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Post Biggest Drop in Over 9 YearsUnited States Retail Sales MoMUS retail trade fell by 1.2 percent from a month earlier in December 2018, following a revised 0.1 percent growth in November and missing market expectations of 0.2 percent gain. It was the steepest decline in trade since September 2009, as sales fell in almost all categories. Excluding automobiles, gasoline, building materials and food services, retail sales dropped 1.7 percent in December after an increase of 1 percent in November. 2019-02-14T13:33:00
