Title: US Jobless Claims Rise More than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits increased by 10 thousand to 231 thousand in the week ending December 30 from the previous week’s revised level of 221 thousand and above market expectations of 220 thousand. According to unadjusted data, the biggest increases were seen in New Jersey (+7,784); Pennsylvania (+7,410); Michigan (+7,001); Connecticut (+5,465) and Ohio (+5,013) while the largest declines were reported in California (-10,907); Texas (-4,800); Florida (-1,959) and North Carolina (-1,759). 2019-01-03T13:32:00
