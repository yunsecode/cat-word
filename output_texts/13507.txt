US Factory Activity Growth Weakest Since 2009: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe flash Markit Manufacturing PMI for the United States came in at 50.8 in April of 2016, down from 51.5 in the previous month and below market expectations of 52. It is the lowest reading since September of 2009 as production almost stagnated, new business growth slowed and job creation was the weakest since June of 2013.2016-04-22T14:52:38
