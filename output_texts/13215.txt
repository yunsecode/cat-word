Title: US Stocks Open Flat on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Flat on Tuesday United States Stock MarketWall Street opened around the flatline on Tuesday as investors remained cautious ahead of Fed and BoJ monetary policy decisions during the week, falling oil prices and mixed earnings reports. Results for McDonald’s missed expectations while those for DuPont, United Technologies, Verizon, 3M and Caterpillar came better than expected although shares from the latest two declined after a cut in forecasts. Apple and Twitter are scheduled to report results later in the day. The Dow Jones fell 10 points or 0.07%; the S&P 500 added 1 point or 0.04% and the Nasdaq was up 4 points or 0.09% around 5 minutes after the opening bell.2016-07-26T14:47:51
