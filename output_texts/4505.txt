Title: US Stocks Sell-off Sharply
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Sell-off SharplyUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks sank further on Monday as traders remain cautious ahead of big tech earnings reports this week including IBM, Microsoft, Tesla, and Apple, and started finally pricing in that the Federal Reserve is set to raise rates three to four times this year and start quantitative tightening. So far 74% of S&P 500 companies that have reported results beat Wall Street estimates, but some key firms including Goldman Sachs and Netflix disappointed last week. The Dow dipped more than 900 points to the lowest since June, S&P 500 sank more than 3%, and Nasdaq pullback more than 4% around midday.2022-01-24T16:57:00.08
