Title: US Imports Jump 2.3% To 3-Year High On Oil, Autos
Tag: Imports

US Imports Jump 2.3% To 3-Year High On Oil, AutosUnited States ImportsTotal imports to the United States climbed 2.3 percent month-over-month to USD 240.6 billion in January 2017. It was the highest value since December 2014, as petroleum imports were the largest in two years and purchases of automobiles hit a record high. Also, imports rose for cellphones, industrial supplies and materials, and capital goods. Imports of goods from China increased 5.1 percent while those from Japan and Germany tumbled 13.9 percent and 9.2 percent, respectively.
