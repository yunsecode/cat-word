Title: US Private Sector Activity Stagnates
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Activity StagnatesUnited States Composite PmiThe final Markit US Composite PMI came in at 50 in February of 2016, compared to a flash reading of 50.1 and 53.2 in the previous month. Private sector activity stagnated for the first time since October of 2013 as services contracted (PMI at 49.7) and manufacturing slowed (PMI at 51.3).2016-03-03T14:58:08.327
