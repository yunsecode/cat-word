Title: US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI Highest Since 2005
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI Highest Since 2005United States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States jumped to 59.8 in September of 2017 from 55.3 in August, beating market expectations of 55.5. It is the highest reading since August of 2005, mainly boosted by a rise in production and new orders, despite the impact on the supply chain from the hurricanes.2017-10-04T14:08:43
