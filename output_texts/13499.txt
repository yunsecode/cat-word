Title: US Private Sector Activity at 3-Month High
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Activity at 3-Month HighUnited States Composite PmiThe seasonally adjusted Markit Flash US Composite PMI Output Index was recorded at 51.7, higher than 51.3 in the previous month and the highest reading since January. A faster upturn in service sector activity (flash index at 52.1 in April) offset a weak manufacturing production growth (flash output index at 50.3). Meanwhile, job creation stood at its lowest level since January of 2015.2016-04-26T15:10:36.3
