US Personal Spending Rises 0.2%, Lowest In 8 MonthsUnited States Personal SpendingPersonal sending in the United States rose 0.2 percent in November from October of 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.4 percent gain in the previous period. It is the lowest rise since a flat reading in March and below market expectations of a 0.3 percent increase.
