Title: US Stocks Pare Some Losses
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Pare Some LossesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street pared some earlier losses on Monday after Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer said the Senate is very close to reaching a deal on the coronavirus stimulus plan that failed to pass on Sunday. However, investors remain very concerned about the impact of the coronavirus as the number of cases in the US passed 35K and more states are issuing stay at home orders. Traders welcomed Fed's extensive new measures to support the economy, including an asset purchase program in the amounts needed to support smooth market functioning and the provision of up to $300 billion in new financing for employers, consumers, and businesses, although the improvement proved temporary. The Dow Jones was down 3.4%, the S&P 500 fell 3.3% and the Nasdaq went down 1.2% around 3 PM NY time.2020-03-23T17:35:00
