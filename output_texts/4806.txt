Title: US Core Capital Goods Orders Rise More than Expected
Tag: Non Defense Capital Goods Orders Ex Aircraft

US Core Capital Goods Orders Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Non Defense Capital Goods Orders Ex AircraftOrders for non-defense capital goods excluding aircraft in the United States, a closely watched proxy for business spending plans, rose by 0.6 percent from a month earlier in October 2021, following an upwardly revised 1.3 percent increase in September and above market expectations of 0.5 percent. 2021-11-24T13:56:00
