Title: US Consumer Sentiment At Nearly 13-Year High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment At Nearly 13-Year HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe final reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States rose to 98.2 in December 2016 compared to a preliminary figure of 98 and a final 93.8 in November. It was the highest reading since January 2004, as consumers expected a favorable impact of Trump's policies on the economy.2016-12-23T15:09:34.08
