Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Hits 6-Week Low
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Hits 6-Week LowUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury extended losses to a 6-week low of 1.424% on Tuesday as traders remain concerned over high inflation and digest a report saying President Biden interviewed Fed Governor Lael Brainard, seen as more dovish, for the chair job. Last week, the US 10-year bond yield slid almost 10% after the Fed announced an expected reduction in stimulus but signalled no hurry to hike interest rates while reiterating inflationary pressures are likely to be transitory. Meanwhile, the yield on 30-year Treasury TIPS (inflation-protected securities) fell to a record low of -0.508%.2021-11-09T13:51:00
