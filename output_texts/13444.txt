Title: Wall Street Opens Higher as Oil Rallies
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Higher as Oil RalliesUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened in the green on Thursday mainly boosted by the energy sector amid strong gains in oil prices although data showed initial jobless claims hit a 15-month high, import prices rose less than expected and export prices increased for the first time in eleven months. The Dow Jones added 0.32% or 58 points, the S&P 500 rose 0.3% or 6 points and the Nasdaq went up 0.1% or 5 points around 20 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-05-12T15:03:33.38
