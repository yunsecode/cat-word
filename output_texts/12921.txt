Title: US Stocks Close Mixed After FOMC Minutes
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed After FOMC MinutesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Wednesday after minutes from last Fed meeting showed policymakers agreed that the case for a rate hike had strengthened in recent months, raising expectations of a rate increase soon. The Dow Jones added 15 points or 0.1%, the S&P 500 gained 2 points or 0.1% and the Nasdaq lost 8 points or 0.2%.2016-10-12T20:23:16.17
