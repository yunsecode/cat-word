Title: US Industrial Output Rises More than Expected in December
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Output Rises More than Expected in DecemberUnited States Industrial Production MomUS industrial production increased by 0.9 percent month-over-month in December 2017, easily beating market expectations of 0.4 percent and following a downwardly revised 0.1 percent drop in November. Utilities output rebounded sharply and mining rose further while manufacturing growth slowed. Considering 2017 as a whole, industrial production increased by 1.8 percent, the first and largest gain since 2014.2018-01-17T14:18:59
