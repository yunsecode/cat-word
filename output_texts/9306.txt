Title: US Stocks Extend Losses
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend LossesUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks fell on Friday as investors await further news on trade negotiations after President Trump tweeted that there is "no rush" to reach an agreement with Beijing and that the process to impose 25% tariffs on the remaining USD 325 billion of Chinese goods has already begun. The Dow Jones dropped 277 points, or 1.1%, to 25,551; the S&P 500 lost 38 points, or 1.3%, to 2,833; and the Nasdaq plunged 125 points, or 1.6%, to 7,790.2019-05-10T14:46:19.5
