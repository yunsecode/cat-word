Title: US Stocks Extend Declines
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend DeclinesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded lower on Monday as investors remain very concerned about the impact of the coronavirus as the number of cases in the US passed 35K and more states are issuing stay at home orders. Investors welcomed Fed's extensive new measures to support the economy, including an asset purchase program in the amounts needed to support smooth market functioning and the provision of up to $300 billion in new financing for employers, consumers, and businesses, although the improvement proved temporary. The Dow Jones was down 3%, the S&P 500 fell 3.1% and the Nasdaq went down 1.9% around 9 minutes after the opening bell.2020-03-23T13:40:00
