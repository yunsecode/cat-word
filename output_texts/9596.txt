Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise for Second Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise for Second Week: MBA United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 5.3 percent in the week ended February 22nd 2019, following a 3.6 percent increase in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications advanced 4.6 percent and applications to purchase a home jumped 6.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went down by 1bp to 4.65 percent.2019-02-27T12:02:00
