Title: US Housing Starts Fall Much More than Expected
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Fall Much More than ExpectedUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States slumped 8.2 percent month-over-month to an annualized rate of 1,192 thousand in December of 2017, compared to market expectations of a 1.7 percent decline. It is the biggest drop since November of 2016 amid an 11.8 percent decline in a construction of single-family units. Considering full 2017, housing starts went up 2.4 percent to 1,202,100 and building permits rose 4.7 percent to 1,263,400. Both reached the highest level since 2007.2018-01-18T13:33:50
