Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Friday 18 May 2018, as tensions between China and the US weighed on investor sentiment while both countries began the second round of trade talks and President Trump told reporters he doubted negotiations would be successful. The Dow Jones edged up 7 points or less than 0.1% to 24721. In contrast, the S&P 500 dipped 7 points or 0.3% to 2713 and the Nasdaq lost 28 points or 0.4% to 7354.2018-05-18T20:02:09.91
