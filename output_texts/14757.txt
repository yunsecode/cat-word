Title: U.S. GDP Revised Down to 1.9% in Q1
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

U.S. GDP Revised Down to 1.9% in Q1United States GDP Growth RateReal gross domestic product located in the United States increased at an annual rate of 1.9 percent in the first quarter of 2012 (that is, from the fourth quarter to the first quarter), according to the "second" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. In the fourth quarter of 2011, real GDP increased 3.0 percent.2012-06-03T22:28:19.357
