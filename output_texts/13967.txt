Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds 271K Jobs in OctoberUnited States Non Farm PayrollsTotal nonfarm payroll employment increased by 271,000 in October, the most this year,  followed by a revised 137,000 gain in September. Job gains occurred in professional and  business services, health care, retail trade, food services and drinking  places, and construction.
