Title: US Stocks Have Worst Week Since 2016
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Have Worst Week Since 2016United States Stock MarketThe sell-off in Wall Street continued on Friday, erasing small gains at the open amid mounting fears of a trade war between the US and China. On Thursday, President Trump announced it will impose restrictions on Chinese investment and tariffs on nearly $50 billion worth of Chinese imports. China responded with plans to target 128 US products accounting for roughly $3 billion in imports. The Dow Jones slumped 425 points or 1.8% to 23533; the S&P 500 sank 53 points or 2.1% to 2589; the Nasdaq went down 174 points or 2.4% to 6993. On the week, the Dow lost 5.7%; the S&P fell 5.9%; and the Nasdaq declined 6.5%. It is the worst week since January of 2016. 2018-03-23T20:27:26.777
