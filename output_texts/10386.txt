Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Monday 6 August 2018, as tech stocks extended gains led by Apple and Facebook, offsetting the escalating trade war between China and the US. The Dow Jones climbed 40 points or 0.2% to 25502. The S&P 500 added 10 points or 0.4% to 2850. The Nasdaq advanced 48 points or 0.6% to 7860. 2018-08-06T20:13:33.493
