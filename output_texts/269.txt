Title: US Treasury Yields Little-Changed ahead of NFP
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Little-Changed ahead of NFPUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note was little-changed at 3.6% on Friday, as traders await the nonfarm payrolls report and welcome the passage of the Fiscal Responsibility Act in the Senate. The US economy is expected to have added 190K jobs in May, slowing from 253K in April, further supporting bets the Fed will pause the tightening cycle this month. Investors now assign a higher 75% chance the Fed will keep the target for the federal funds rate steady at 5-5.25%. Meanwhile, the debt deal now goes to President Biden who is set to sign it shortly before the default deadline. 2023-06-02T09:07:57.827
