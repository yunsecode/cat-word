Title: US Mortgage Applications Increase 2.9%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Increase 2.9%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 2.9 percent in the week ended September 30th 2016 from the prior period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the first rise in three weeks. Refinance applications went up 4.7 percent and applications to purchase a home edged up 0.1 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates fell 4bps to 3.62 percent.2016-10-05T11:04:26.437
