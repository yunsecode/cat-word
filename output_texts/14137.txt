Title: US Jobless Claims Rise Slightly in the Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise Slightly in the Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits was 270,000 in the week ended August 1st, an increase of 3,000 from the previous week's unrevised level of 267,000. The 4-week moving average was 268,250, a decrease of 6,500 from the previous week's average of 274,750.2015-08-06T13:40:53.83
