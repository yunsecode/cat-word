Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil stocks rose by 1.263 million barrels in the week ending February 1st, 2019, following a 0.919 million increase in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.179 million gain. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went up by 0.513 million barrels, after a 2.235 million drop in the previous week. 2019-02-06T15:31:10.097
