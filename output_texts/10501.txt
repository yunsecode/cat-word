Title: US Job Openings Down from Record High
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Down from Record HighUnited States Job OffersThe number of job openings in the US fell to 6.638 million in May of 2018 from an all-time high of 6.84 million in April, beating market expectations of 6.583 million. The job openings rate was 4.3 percent. Job openings decreased for total private (-228,000) and information (-60,000) and arts, entertainment, and recreation (-27,000) and was little changed for government. In contrast, job openings increased in federal government (+12,000) and mining and logging (+10,000). Meanwhile, the number of hires was little changed at 5.8 million.2018-07-10T14:09:00
