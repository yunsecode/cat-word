Title: US PCE Inflation Slows in August, Core Steady at 2%
Tag: PCE Price Index Monthly Change

US PCE Inflation Slows in August, Core Steady at 2%United States PCE Price Index Monthly ChangeThe personal consumption expenditure (PCE) price index in the United States increased 0.1 percent month-over-month in August of 2018, the same as in each of the previous two months and slightly below market expectations of a 0.2 percent rise. Prices of goods went down 0.1 percent after being flat in July: cost of durable goods declined (-0.3 percent compared to +0.4 percent) while prices of nondurables edged up 0.1 percent after a 0.2 percent fall. Cost of services rose 0.2 percent, the same as in both July and June. Excluding food and energy, PCE prices were unchanged, following a 0.2 percent rise in July and below expectations of a 0.1 percent gain. Year-on-year, the PCE price index advanced 2.2 percent, after a 2.3 percent gain in the prior month, and the core index rose 2.0 percent, the same as in July. Core PCE inflation is the Fed's preferred measure of inflation and the central bank targets it at 2 percent.2018-09-28T12:43:00
