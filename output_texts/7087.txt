Title: Factory Activity in Philadelphia Below Forecasts
Tag: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index

Factory Activity in Philadelphia Below ForecastsUnited States Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index in the US fell to 11.1 in December of 2020 from 26.3 in November and below market expectations of 20. It was the lowest reading since May. Indicators for general activity, new orders, shipments, and employment remained positive for the seventh consecutive month but fell from their readings in November. Changes in future indexes were mixed but suggest that overall growth is expected to continue over the next six months.
