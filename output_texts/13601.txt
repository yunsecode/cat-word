Title: US Consumer Sentiment Drops to 5-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Drops to 5-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States came in at 90 in March of 2016 from 91.7 in the previous month and hitting its lowest reading since October 2015, as both future expectations and current conditions deteriorated sharply. Markets were expecting the index to rise to 92.2.2016-03-18T14:06:37
