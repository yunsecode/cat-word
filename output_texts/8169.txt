Title: Dollar Rises After Upbeat US Jobs Report, Posts 3rd Weekly Loss
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rises After Upbeat US Jobs Report, Posts 3rd Weekly LossUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rose to 97.0 against a basket of currencies on Friday after the US jobs report showed non-farm payrolls rose unexpectedly in May after a record drop in April, while the unemployment rate declined to 13.3% from the previous month's all-time high. For the week, the dollar index lost 1.4 percent, the third consecutive weekly decline. 2020-06-05T20:36:00
