Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Drop for 5th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Drop for 5th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 2.057 million barrels in the week ended September 14th 2018, following a 5.296 million drop in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.5 million decrease. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories declined by 1.719 million barrels while markets expected a 0.104 million fall. 2018-09-19T14:34:53.51
