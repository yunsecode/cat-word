Title: US Retail Sales Rise The Most Since 2015
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Rise The Most Since 2015United States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the US rose 1.6% month-over-month in September of 2017, slightly below market expectations of a 1.7% but following a revised 0.1% fall in August. It is the biggest gain since March 2015 as motor vehicles sales recovered after hurricanes and higher prices boosted receipts at gasoline stations
