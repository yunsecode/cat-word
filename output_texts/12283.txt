Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 4.954 million barrels in the week ended March 17th, 2017, following a 0.237 million drop in the previous week and way above market expectations of a 2.8 million rise. Gasoline stocks fell by 2.81 million, compared to expectations of a 2 million drop.
