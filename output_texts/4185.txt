US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than Expected: API United States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 5.983 million barrels in the week ended February 18th of 2022, after a 1.076-million-barrel draw in the previous week and well above market expectations of a 0.767-million-barrel increase.
