Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than Expected United States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 3.548 million barrels in the week ended January 24th 2020, after a 0.405 million drop in the previous week and against market expectations of a 0.482 million increase, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories went up by 1.203 million barrels after increasing by 1.745 million in the previous week and compared with market consensus of a 1.32 million gain.2020-01-29T15:33:51.47
