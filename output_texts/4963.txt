Title: US Crude Stocks Rise for 2nd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Rise for 2nd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 3.291 million barrels in the week ending October 29th, following a 4.267 million advance in the previous period and compared with market forecasts of a 2.225 million increase, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories were down by 1.488 million barrels, more than an expected 1.333 million drop.2021-11-03T14:35:16.333
