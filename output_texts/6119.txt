US Crude Stocks Fall by Most Since OctoberUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 7.7 million barrels in the week ended April 30th of 2021, following a 4.3 million gain in the previous week, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed.
