Title: US NY Empire State Manufacturing Index Stays Negative
Tag: NY Empire State Manufacturing Index

US NY Empire State Manufacturing Index Stays NegativeUnited States NY Empire State Manufacturing IndexThe NY Empire State Manufacturing Index remained into negative territory but inched up to -1.99 in September 2016, compared to -4.21 in the previous month. Inventory levels declined at a faster pace and delivery times have shortened. Meanwhile, new orders and shipments declined markedly and labor market conditions weakened, with both employment levels and the average workweek reported as lower. Price indexes remained close to last month’s levels, and indicated ongoing moderate input price increases coupled with a continued slight growth in selling prices. Indexes for the six-month outlook suggested that firms generally expect conditions to improve in the months ahead.2016-09-15T12:56:30
