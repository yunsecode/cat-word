Title: US Stocks Trade Mainly Flat After Mixed Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Mainly Flat After Mixed DataUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened mainly flat on Thursday amid mixed economic data: retail sales and industrial production fell more-than-expected while jobless claims beat forecasts. The Dow Jones and the S&P 500 traded near the flatline while the Nasdaq was up 12 points or 0.2% around 10 minutes after the open.2016-09-15T13:51:05.08
