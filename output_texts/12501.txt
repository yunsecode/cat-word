Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise For 3rd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise For 3rd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 2.84 million barrels in the week ended January 20th, 2017, following a 2.347 million increase in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.815 million rise. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks jumped by 6.796 million, compared to expectations of a 0.498 million increase.
