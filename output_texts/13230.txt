Title: US Stocks Gain; Dow Ends at Record High for 7th Day
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Gain; Dow Ends at Record High for 7th DayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Wednesday amid rising oil prices, as better-than-expected earnings' reports mainly from Microsoft and Morgan Stanley boosted optimism among investors. The Dow Jones rose 0.19% or 36 points to end at a record high for the seventh straight day of 18595 points, with nine consecutive days of gains for the first time since 2013; the S&P 500 gained 0.43% or 9.2 points and the Nasdaq advanced 1.06% or 53.6 points.2016-07-20T21:20:38
