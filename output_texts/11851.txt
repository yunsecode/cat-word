Title: US Stocks Edge Higher On Tuesday Amid Strong Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Higher On Tuesday Amid Strong EarningsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Tuesday amid better than expected earnings report for several companies including Caterpillar, 3M, DuPont, General Motors and McDonald's. Equities were also nudged by a climb in oil and copper prices. The Dow Jones gained 100 points or 0.5% to 21613. The S&P 500 added 7 points or 0.3% to a new high of 2477. The Nasdaq advanced 1 point or less than 0.1% to a fresh record of 6412.2017-07-25T20:03:16
