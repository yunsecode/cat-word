Title: Dollar Eases, Still Poised for Quarterly Gains
Tag: Currency

Dollar Eases, Still Poised for Quarterly GainsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar weakened to 112, the lowest in a week after Fed Brainard warned about the impact of rapidly rising rates on global markets. Still, the dollar is on track for its fourth monthly gain and up almost 10% this quarter, supported by expectations that the Federal Reserve would remain aggressive in battling inflation even at the risk of a recession. The Fed raised interest rates by 75 basis points for a third consecutive meeting in September while forecasting rates to peak at 4.6% next year with no cuts until 2024, pushing back any dovish pivot that the markets were hoping for in the near term. This dollar’s strength was seen across the board, with some of the most pronounced buying activity against the British pound and the euro amid growing economic and energy worries.2022-09-30T15:28:00
