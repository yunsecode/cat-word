Title: US Wages Grow Faster Than Expected
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Grow Faster Than ExpectedUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage Hourly Earnings in the United States increased 0.40 percent in October of 2015 over the previous month and 2.5 percent over the same month a year earlier. The numbers beat estimates of a 0.2 percent month-over-month rise, and a 2.3 per cent year-over-year rise.2015-11-06T14:23:39.363
