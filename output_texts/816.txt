Title: US Composite PMI Revised Lower, Still the Highest in 9 Months
Tag: Composite PMI

US Composite PMI Revised Lower, Still the Highest in 9 MonthsUnited States Composite PMIThe S&P Global US Composite PMI came in at 52.3 in March 2023, revised lower from the preliminary estimate of 53.3 but above February final reading of 50.1. The latest PMI figure signaled a modest rise in business activity that was the fastest since last June, mainly led by a steeper increase in service sector output. Meanwhile, manufacturers recorded only a fractional advance in output. Overall new business grew for the first time in six months driven by solid domestic demand. Employment levels rose at a faster rate, while backlogs of work increased for the first time since last September. On the price front, a further marked rise in input costs was reflected in a faster increase in output charges.2023-04-05T13:54:27.127
