Title: US Treasury Yields Extend the Rise after NFP, ISM Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Extend the Rise after NFP, ISM DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note, seen as a proxy for global borrowing costs, rose 15bps to 3.55% on Friday, after the payrolls report totally surprised on the upside, showing the US economy added 517K jobs and the unemployment rate fell to 3.4%. Also, the ISM Services PMI pointed to a way bigger-than-expected improvement in the services sector last month. The strong data added to evidence the US labour market remains strong and the economy continues to be resilient. Traders now see the Fed raising the fed funds rate to 5%-5.25% before pausing, above 5% expected before the payrolls release. Meanwhile, the two-year Treasury yields have climbed 20bps to 4.29% and the thirty-year one 10bps to 3.66%.
