Title: US FHFA House Prices Rise 0.4% in February
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US FHFA House Prices Rise 0.4% in FebruaryUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States increased by 0.4 percent on the month in February of 2016, the same pace as a downwardly revised 0.4 percent rise in January. Year-on-year, the FHFA house price index went up at a slower 5.6 percent compared with a 6 percent growth in the previous month.2016-04-21T14:04:14.48
