Title: US Futures Rise after Russia Pulls Troops
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Rise after Russia Pulls TroopsUnited States Stock MarketUS futures surged on Tuesday, with contracts on the Dow Jones rising around 300 points while the S&P 500 gained 1% and Nasdaq 100 futures added more than 1.5% as some Russian troops near the Ukrainian border are returning to their bases, Russia's defence ministry allegedly said, easing some concerns of an imminent war in Europe. At the same time, Fed's tightening plans remain in the spotlight as traders await the FOMC minutes tomorrow for further clarification if the central bank will deliver a 50bps rate hike next month.2022-02-15T11:22:24.333
