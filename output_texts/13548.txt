Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Fall United States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil stocks in the United States declined by 4.937 million barrels in the week ended April 1st, 2016, below market expectations of a 3.25 million increase and marking the first fall after seven straight weeks of rises. In the previous week, stocks went up by 2.299 million barrels. Gasoline stocks increased for the first time in seven weeks, up by 1.438 million barrels, and compared to market expectations of a 0.96 million drop.
