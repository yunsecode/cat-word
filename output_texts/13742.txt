Title: US Markit Services PMI at 2-Year Low
Tag: Services PMI

US Markit Services PMI at 2-Year LowUnited States Services PMIThe final Markit Services PMI for the United States came in at 53.2 in January of 2016, down from a preliminary estimate of 53.7 and a final of 54.3 in the previous month. It is the lowest figure since October of 2013, due to a slowdown in output and new business while employment was sustained. However, the twelve-month outlook remains subdued.2016-02-03T14:56:14
