Title: US Stocks Finish Lower On Friday, Gain On Week
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Finish Lower On Friday, Gain On WeekUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Friday dragged down by healthcare sector and stronger dollar. The Dow Jones went down 36 points or 0.2%, the S&P 500 lost 5 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq declined 12 points or 0.2%, after hitting a record intraday high. Meanwhile, on a week, Dow Jones added 0.1%, the S&P 500 increased 0.8% and the Nasdaq rose 1.6%.2016-11-18T21:24:54
