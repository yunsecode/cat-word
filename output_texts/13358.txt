Title: Wall Street Opens Lower After 3-Day Rally
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Lower After 3-Day RallyUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened in the red on Thursday, following three days of gains, led by losses in financials sector and amid a retreat in oil prices. The Dow Jones lost 0.2% or 38 points, the S&P 500 fell 0.3% or 7 points and the Nasdaq slid 0.25 percent or 12 points around 30 minutes after the opening bell.2016-06-09T15:18:36.02
