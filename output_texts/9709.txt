Title: US Stocks End Mixed on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Tuesday 29 January 2019, as tech stocks tumbled amid sanctions and slower growth forecasts in China tech companies. The Dow Jones gained 52 points or 0.2% to 24580. In contrast, the S&P 500 edged down 4 points or 0.2% to 2640. The Nasdaq shed 57 points or 0.8% to 7028.2019-01-29T21:17:00
