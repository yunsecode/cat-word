Title: US PCE Prices Rise at a Slower 0.1%
Tag: PCE Price Index Monthly Change

US PCE Prices Rise at a Slower 0.1%United States PCE Price Index Monthly ChangeThe personal consumption expenditure price index in the United States rose 0.1% month-over-month in May of 2023, below 0.4% in April. Prices for services increased 0.3% while goods cost went down 0.1%. Food prices increased 0.1% and energy sank 3.9%. Excluding food and energy, the PCE price index increased 0.3%. Year-on-year, PCE prices increased 3.8%, the lowest reading since April of 2021, compared to a downwardly revised 4.3% advance in April and the core rate which is the Federal Reserve’s preferred gauge to measure inflation, increased 4.6%, compared to market expectations of 4.7%.17 days ago
