Title: US Wages Grow Less Than Expected
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Grow Less Than ExpectedUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings for all employees on US private nonfarm payrolls rose by 8 cents, or 0.2%, to $33.09 in February 2023, while markets had expected them to remain at 0.3%. This was the smallest growth in average hourly earnings in a year. Average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees rose by 13 cents, or 0.5%, to $28.42. Over the past 12 months, average hourly earnings have increased by 4.6%, up from 4.4% in the prior month but slightly below market forecasts of a 4.7% rise.2023-03-10T13:32:00
