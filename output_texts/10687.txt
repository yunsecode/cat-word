Title: US Manufacturing Growth Highest since 2014: PMI
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing Growth Highest since 2014: PMIUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI edged up to 56.6 in May of 2018 from 56.5 in April, slightly above market expectations of 56.5, preliminary estimates showed. The reading pointed to the strongest growth rate in factory activity since September of 2014 amid faster rises in production and incoming new business due to improving economic conditions and a continued recovery in domestic sales. 2018-05-23T13:52:01.647
