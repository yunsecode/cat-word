Title: US Wholesale Inventories Rise the Least in 5 Months
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Rise the Least in 5 MonthsUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the United States increased 0.3 percent month-over-month to USD 654.0 billion in November of 2018, below market expectations of 0.5 percent and following an upwardly revised 0.9 percent rise in October. It was the lowest gain in wholesale inventories since June, as durable goods stocks slowed (0.7 percent from 1.7 percent in October), namely automotive (0.7 percent from 2.9 percent); computer equipment (0.5 percent from 3.7 percent); professional equipment (1.4 percent from 2.1 percent) and electrical (0.6 percent from 1.1 percent). Meanwhile, nondurable inventories fell 0.5 percent (from -0.4 percent in October). Year-on-year, inventories advanced 6.5 percent.2019-02-01T15:02:00
