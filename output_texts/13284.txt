Title: US Trade Deficit at 3-Month High
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit at 3-Month High United States Balance of TradeThe trade gap in the United States widened to USD 41.14 billion in May of 2016 from a USD 37.4 billion deficit in the previous month. Figures came worse than market expectations of a USD 40 billion shortfall as exports shrank 0.2 percent hurt by a stronger dollar and imports rose 1.6 percent due to higher oil prices.2016-07-06T13:36:20
