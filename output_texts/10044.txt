Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 3.217 million barrels in the week ended October 26th 2018, following a 6.346 million increase in the previous week and below market expectations of a 4.11 million gain. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories decreased by 3.161 million barrels while markets expected a 2.137 million decline.2018-10-31T14:37:00
