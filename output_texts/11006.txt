Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States went up 2.408 million barrels in the week ended March 2nd, after a 3.019 million increase in the previous period and below market expectations of a 2.723 million rise. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories fell 0.788 million, the first decline in over a month, following a 2.483 million increase in the previous week and compared to forecasts of a 1.201 million drop.2018-03-07T15:34:41
