Title: US Business Inventories Fall 0.1%
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Fall 0.1%United States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the United States declined 0.1 percent month-on-month in February of 2016, following a revised 0.1 percent fall in the previous month and in line with market estimates. Retail inventories excluding autos, which go into the calculation of GDP, increased by 0.3 percent, higher than 0.2 percent in January. Year-on-year, inventories were up 1.2 percent.2016-04-13T15:08:42.717
