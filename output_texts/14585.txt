Title: United States Unemployment Rate Down to 5-Year Low in December
Tag: Unemployment Rate

United States Unemployment Rate Down to 5-Year Low in DecemberUnited States Unemployment RateIn December, US unemployment rate eased from 7.0 percent to 6.7 percent, the lowest level since October of 2008. The number of unemployed persons declined by 490,000 to 10.4 million. Over the year, the number of unemployed persons and the unemployment rate were down by 1.9 million and 1.2 percentage points, respectively.2014-01-10T13:39:14.63
