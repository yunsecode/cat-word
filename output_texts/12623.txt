Title: US GDP Growth Rate Revised Up To 3.5% In Q3
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Rate Revised Up To 3.5% In Q3United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy advanced an annualized 3.5 percent on quarter in the three months to September of 2016, up from 1.4 percent growth in the previous period and better than a 3.2 percent expansion in the second estimate. It is the highest growth rate in two years as personal consumption, investment in structures and intellectual property products and government expenditure rose faster than anticipated and exports were boosted by boom in soybean shipments.2016-12-22T13:37:25
