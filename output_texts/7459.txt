Title: US Futures Point to Gains at the Open
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Point to Gains at the OpenUnited States Stock MarketStock futures in the US attempted to rebound on Tuesday, following a big sell-off in the previous session, with contracts on the Dow Jones trading nearly flat, while both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq gained near 0.3%. Investors continue to digest mounting coronavirus infections while deaths surpassed 225K in the US. Meanwhile, hopes for further stimulus before the presidential election next week faded further after White House economic adviser Larry Kudlow told differences between the two sides “have narrowed,” but “the more it narrows, the more conditions come up on the other side”. On the corporate front, 3M, Advanced Micro Devices, Caterpillar, Eli Lilly, Merck & Co, Microsoft and Pfizer are expected to report today. On Monday, the Dow Jones plunged 650 points or 2.3% to 27,686. The S&P 500 plummeted 64 points or 1.9% to 3401. The Nasdaq sank 189 points or 1.6% to 11,359.2020-10-27T09:00:28.757
