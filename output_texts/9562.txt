Title: US Gasoline Stocks Post Biggest Fall in Over 4 Months
Tag: Gasoline Stocks Change

US Gasoline Stocks Post Biggest Fall in Over 4 MonthsUnited States Gasoline Stocks ChangeStocks of gasoline in the United States declined for the third straight week, down by 4.227 million barrels in the week ended March 1st of 2019, following a 1.906 million decrease in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 2.084 million drop. It is the biggest decline since the week ended October 19th of 2018. 2019-03-06T15:58:41.65
