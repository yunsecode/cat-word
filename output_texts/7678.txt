Title: US Stocks Rebound after Big Tech Sell-off
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound after Big Tech Sell-offUnited States Stock MarketMain US stock market indexes rebounded on Wednesday, as a sell-off in tech stocks halted, and as investors shrugged off news that AstraZeneca had paused trials of its experimental COVID-19 vaccine. The Dow Jones was up more than 300 points, the S&P 500 more than 40 points and the Nasdaq gained more than 200 points. All six big tech stocks Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Facebook, Tesla and Apple were trading in positive territory.
