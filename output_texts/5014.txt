Title: US Q3 Employment Costs Rise Faster than Expected
Tag: Employment Cost Index

US Q3 Employment Costs Rise Faster than ExpectedUnited States Employment Cost IndexCompensation costs for civilian workers in the US increased 1.3 percent quarter-over-quarter in the three months ending September of 2021, quickening from a 0.7 percent rise in the previous period and faster than market expectations of a 0.9 percent rise. Wages and salaries increased 1.5 percent (vs 0.9 percent in Q2) and benefit cost went up 0.9 percent (vs 0.4 percent).
