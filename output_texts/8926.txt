Title: Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Contracts Further
Tag: Kansas Fed Manufacturing Index

Tenth District Manufacturing Activity Contracts FurtherUnited States Kansas Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Kansas City Fed's Manufacturing Production Index fell to -7 in December 2019 from -5 in the previous month, well below market forecasts of +2. The composite index, an average of the production, new orders, employment, supplier delivery time, and raw materials inventory indexes, dropped to -8 from -3 in November, driven by weak activity at durable goods plants, especially from declines in: wood products, nonmetallic mineral products, primary metal, fabricated metal products, machinery, and computer and electronic products manufacturing. The shipments index turned negative and the production, new orders, order backlog, and employment indexes declined further. Aside from the index for raw materials prices, only the supplier delivery time index remained slightly positive.2019-12-20T16:11:42.81
