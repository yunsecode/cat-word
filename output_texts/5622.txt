Title: US Dollar Stays Near 4-Month High
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Stays Near 4-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was around 92.6 on Tuesday, not far from four-month highs of 93.19 touched last week, as traders await the  FOMC meeting on Wednesday for clues on the timing of stimulus tapering. Last week, the greenback ended a volatile week by gaining 0.2%, benefiting from a safe-haven bid on fears that a surge in infections of the Delta variant could derail the global recovery. The dollar strengthened from mid-June after the Fed sharply raised its forecasts for inflation this year and brought forward the time frame on when it will hike interest rates. 2021-07-27T04:46:00
