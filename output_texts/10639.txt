Title: ISM New York Index Falls in May
Tag: Ism New York Index

ISM New York Index Falls in May United States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions in the United States declined to 56.4 in May of 2018 from 64.3 in April. A slowdown was seen for employment (50.2 from 58.3) and expected revenues (71.7 from 76.3). Also, quantity of purchases was flat (at 50), current revenues shrank (43.8 from 55.6) and prices paid accelerated (72.9 from 65.8). On the other hand, the Six-Month Outlook rose to 66.9 from 58.4.2018-06-04T14:49:43.243
