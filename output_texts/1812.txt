Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Retreats After CPI
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Retreats After CPIUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note fell sharply to 3.8%, the lowest in one month, after fresh CPI data came cooler than expected. Consumer prices rose by 7.7% in October, slowing from the 8.2% in the prior month and below expectations of 8%. Core inflation also surprised to the downside at 6.3%, easing pressure on the Federal Reserve to hike rates for an extended period as signaled in its November meeting. Fed fund futures show that markets expect a 50bps rate hike by the Fed in December after four consecutive 75bps increases, while expectations for the terminal rate eased to 5%. In the meantime, investors continued to monitor results from mid-term elections, pointing to a split Senate and an advantage for the Republican party in the House of Representatives. Bond markets in the US will be closed Friday in observance of the national holiday.2022-11-10T13:35:00
