Title: Dow Jones Hits 25,000 for 1st Time
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Jones Hits 25,000 for 1st TimeUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green and continued to break records on Thursday, with all 3 main stock indexes reaching record highs and the Dow breaking above 25,000 for the first time. Investors' mood improved after the ADP report showed US companies added the most jobs in 9 months in December and the Challenger report showed the number of layoffs in 2017 was the lowest since 1990. The nonfarm payroll report for December is due tomorrow. The Dow Jones went up 96 points or 0.4% to 25018; the S&P 500 rose 10 points or 0.4% to 2722 and the Nasdaq traded 21 points or 0.3% higher at 7086 around 10 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-01-04T14:30:02
