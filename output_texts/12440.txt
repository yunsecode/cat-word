Title: US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than Expected In December
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than Expected In December United States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States increased by $14.16 billion in December of 2016, following an upwardly revised $25.2 billion rise in November and well below market expectations of $20 billion. Revolving credit rose by $2.37 billion, after increasing by $11.77 billion in the previous month and non-revolving credit went up by $11.79 billion, compared with a $13.44 billion rise in November.2017-02-07T20:04:51.55
