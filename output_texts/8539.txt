Title: US Wage Growth Above Forecasts
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wage Growth Above ForecastsUnited States Average Hourly EarningsUS average hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls increased by 11 cents, or 0.4 percent over the month to USD 28.62 in March of 2020, after increasing 0.3 percent in February and surprising markets who expected a 0.2 percent gain. This was the strongest increase in wages since last November. Year-on-year, average hourly earnings have increased by 3.1 percent, slightly above 3 percent in the prior month and market consensus of 3 percent. In March, average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees increased by 10 cents to USD 24.07.
