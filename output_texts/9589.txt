Title: Chicago PMI Rebounds in February
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago PMI Rebounds in FebruaryUnited States Chicago PmiThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer for the US rose to 64.7 in February of 2019 from 56.7 in January, beating market expectations of 57. It is the highest reading since December of 2017 as four of the five Barometer components increased. The pick-up in demand contributed the most to the Barometer’s rise. New orders rose by 15.2 points, the most since January 2016; production was up 8.5 points to a fresh six-month high; and order backlogs were up by 5.6 points. On the other hand, supplier delivery times hit the lowest level since June of 2017, declining for the fourth consecutive month.
