Title: US Stocks Open Mixed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open MixedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened mixed on Thursday following a big rally in the previous session as the Fed left rates steady as expected but signaled a pause in future rate hikes. Mixed earnings reports weighed on investors' mood as Microsoft revenues missed expectations and Tesla earnings came below forecasts while EPS from General Electric and Facebook beat expectations. The Dow Jones dropped 0.7%, the S&P 500 traded nearly flat and the Nasdaq added 0.4% around 6 minutes after the opening bell.2019-01-31T14:41:00
