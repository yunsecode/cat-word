Title: Us Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 3rd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

Us Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 3rd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil stocks in the United States declined by 3.226 million barrels in the week ended June 3rd, 2016, following a 1.366 million fall in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 2.7 million drop. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks rose by 1.01 million barrels after falling by 1.492 million barrels in the previous week.2016-06-08T15:38:24.387
