Title: US Q4 Labour Costs Rise Much Less than Expected
Tag: Labour Costs

US Q4 Labour Costs Rise Much Less than ExpectedUnited States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the US nonfarm business sector edged up 0.3 percent in the fourth quarter of 2021, below market forecasts of a 1.5 percent rise and following a downwardly revised 9.3 percent gain in the previous period. It reflects a 6.9 percent jump in hourly compensation and a 6.6 percent rise in productivity. Unit labor costs increased 3.1 percent over the last four quarters, as hourly compensation increased 5.1 percent and productivity increased a 2.0 percent.2022-02-03T13:36:00
