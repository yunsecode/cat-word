Title: Dollar Struggles for Traction
Tag: Currency

Dollar Struggles for Traction United States CurrencyThe dollar index remained under pressure around 90 in the last week of December as long-awaited financial aid in the US, Brexit trade deal and prospects of a swift economic recovery in 2021 spooked investors away from safe-haven assets. Adding to diminished interest for safety were concerns about rising levels of debt in the US. Unprecedented spending led to large budget and current account deficits and has made the greenback an increasingly unattractive investment. 2020-12-28T00:57:00
