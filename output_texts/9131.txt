Title: US Durable Goods Orders Rise More than Expected
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods rose 2.1 percent from a month earlier in July 2019, the most since August last year, following a downwardly revised 1.8 percent gain and beating market expectations of a 1.2 percent increase. Transportation equipment, up for two consecutive months, drove the increase. Meanwhile, demand for machinery declined.2019-08-26T12:32:00
