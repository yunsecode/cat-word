Title: Wall Street Shrugs Off Disappointing US Jobs Data
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Shrugs Off Disappointing US Jobs DataUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened higher on Friday, despite weaker-than-expected US employment data from December. Money continued to flow into riskier investments after the US and Iran indicated that they were not seeking further conflict, easing worries about the risk of war in the Middle East. On the trade front, President Trump said on Thursday that the preliminary agreement between the US and China could be signed shortly after January 15th. The Nasdaq added 21 points or 0.2% to reach a record high; The S&P rose 5 points or 0.2% while the Dow Jones was nearly flat 5 minutes after the opening bell.2020-01-10T14:36:00
