Title: US Inflation Rate Slows to 1%
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Slows to 1%United States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States went up 1 percent year-on-year in February of 2016, slowing from a 1.4 percent increase in the previous month due to lower energy cost. Core inflation accelerated for the fourth straight month to 2.3 percent, reaching the highest since May of 2012 as cost of rents and medical care increased at a faster pace. 2016-03-16T12:36:28
