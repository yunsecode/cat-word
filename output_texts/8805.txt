Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most Since November
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most Since NovemberUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States jumped by 7.459 million barrels in the week ended February 7th 2020, following a 3.355 million gain in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.987 million rise, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. It was the biggest increase in oil inventories since the week ended November 1st.  Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went down by 0.095 million barrels after decreasing by 0.091 million in the previous week and compared with market consensus of a 0.546 million gain.2020-02-12T15:34:31.7
