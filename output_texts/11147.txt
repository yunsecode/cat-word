Title: US Stocks Plunge on Monday to Extend Selloff
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Plunge on Monday to Extend SelloffUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on Monday 5 February 2018, after a strong jobs and rising wages report on Friday prompted a rise in Treasury yields and raised concerns over higher inflation and interest rates. All major indices plummeted on Monday to erase the yearly advance, with plunging sector across the board. The Dow Jones retreated 1175 points or 4.6% to 24346. The S&P 500 fell 113 points or 4.1% to 2649. The Nasdaq lost 273 points or 3.8% to 6968. 2018-02-05T21:07:55
