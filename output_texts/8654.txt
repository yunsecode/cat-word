Title: US Homebuilder Sentiment Falls More than Expected
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Homebuilder Sentiment Falls More than ExpectedUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB housing market index in the US fell to 72 in March of 2020 from 74 in the previous month and below market forecasts of 73. The current single-family sub-index declined to 79 from 81 in February; the sub-index for home sales for the next six months dropped to 75 from 79 and prospective buyers also went down to 56 from 57. It is important to note that half of the builder responses were collected prior to March 4, so the recent stock market declines and the rising economic impact of the coronavirus will be reflected more in next month's report, said NAHB chief economist Robert Dietz.2020-03-17T14:16:00
