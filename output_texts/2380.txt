Title: US Trade Goods Gap Narrows in July
Tag: Goods Trade Balance

US Trade Goods Gap Narrows in JulyUnited States Goods Trade BalanceThe US goods trade deficit narrowed to USD 89.1 billion in July of 2022 from a revised USD 98.6 billion in the previous month, the advance estimate showed. Exports dropped 0.2 percent over the last month to USD 147.3 billion, dragged by lower sales of industrial supplies (-2.3 percent) and consumer goods (-3.4 percent). Meanwhile, imports fell 3.5 percent to USD 234 billion as lower purchases of industrial supplies (-2.9 percent) were offset by higher imports of capital goods (0.5 percent).
