Title: US Weekly Jobless Claims Seen Falling to Lowest in Over a Year
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Weekly Jobless Claims Seen Falling to Lowest in Over a YearUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsInitial jobless claims are likely to decrease to an over year low of 540 thousand in the May 1st week, as the gradual re-opening of the economy prompted employers to hire more workers to respond to growing demand. The labor market recovery has been supported by the government's $1.9 trillion relief package and the acceleration in the pace of COVID-19 vaccinations. Meanwhile, continuing claims are seen declining to 3.62 million in the April 24th week from 3.66 million the week before, but remaining well above February 2020 levels.2021-05-06T09:53:20.903
