Title: Jobless Claims Fall to Nearly 15-Year Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

Jobless Claims Fall to Nearly 15-Year LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing new claims for unemployment benefits decreased by 43,000 to a seasonally adjusted 265,000 in the week ending January 24th. This is the lowest level for initial claims since April 15, 2000 when it was 259,000.2015-01-29T13:39:49.563
