Title: US Housing Starts Rise Above Forecast
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Rise Above ForecastUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the US rose 5.7 percent from a month earlier to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,235 thousand units in April 2019, more than an expected 1,205 thousand and following a revised 1.7 percent advance in March. Construction of both single- and multi-family housing units increased.2019-05-16T12:31:00
