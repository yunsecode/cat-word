Title: US Stocks Set to Open Modestly Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Set to Open Modestly HigherUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures traded modestly higher on Friday after halting a three-day losing streak in the prior session, as risk appetite returned following better-than-expected jobless claims figures. The number of Americans filing new claims for unemployment benefits dropped by 34 thousand to 444 thousand in the week ending May 15th, a fresh 14-month low and below market expectations of 450 thousand. Meantime, the US Treasury Department on Thursday offered to accept a global minimum corporate tax of at least 15% during international negotiations. On Thursday, the Dow rose 188.11 points, or 0.55%, to 34084.15, the S&P 500 gained 43.44 points, or 1.06%, to 4159.12 and the Nasdaq Composite added 236 points, or 1.77%, to 13535.74.2021-05-21T06:18:00
