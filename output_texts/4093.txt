Title: US Jobless Rate Falls More than Expected
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Falls More than ExpectedUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate edged down to 3.8 percent in February of 2022 from 4 percent in the previous month, a new pandemic low and below market expectations of 3.9 percent. The number of unemployed persons edged down by 243 thousand to 6.270 million. In February 2020, prior to the coronavirus pandemic, the unemployment rate was 3.5 percent, and the number of unemployed persons was 5.7 million. Meanwhile, the labor force participation rate edged up to 62.3 percent in February, the highest level since March 2020.2022-03-04T13:33:00
