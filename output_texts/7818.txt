Title: US Futures Lower Ahead of Jobless Claims
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Lower Ahead of Jobless ClaimsUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures traded lower on Thursday, ahead of the release of weekly unemployment claims data and as investors await for news about a new coronavirus relief package after President Trump accused congressional Democrats of not wanting to negotiate. The US weekly jobless report is expected to show a slight decline in initial claims to 1.12 million during the week ending on August 8th, bringing the total reported since March 21st to nearly 56.4 million. On Wednesday, the Dow Jones climbed 290 points or 1.1% to 29,977; the S&P 500 gained 47 points or 1.4% to 3380; and the Nasdaq jumped 229 points or 2.1% to 11,012..2020-08-13T09:57:30.123
