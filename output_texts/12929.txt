Title: Dollar Hits 11-Week High
Tag: Currency

Dollar Hits 11-Week HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rose on Tuesday to the highest since July after a speech from Fed official Charles Evans raised expectations of a Fed rate hike this year. The greenback gained around 0.7% against the Euro, 0.8% against the British pound, 0.7% against the Aussie, 0.9% against the Kiwi dollar and 0.2% against the yen around 08:00 AM NY time.
