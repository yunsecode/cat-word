Title: US Labor Productivity Growth Confirmed at 3-Year High
Tag: Productivity

US Labor Productivity Growth Confirmed at 3-Year HighUnited States ProductivityLabor productivity in the US non-farm business sector rose an annualized 2.9 percent during the second quarter of 2018, unrevised from the preliminary reading and following a 0.3 percent gain in the previous three-month period. It was the largest quarterly gain since the first quarter of 2015, as output grew 5 percent and hours worked rose at a slower 2 percent. Year-on-year, productivity advanced 1.3 percent, reflecting a 3.5 percent growth in output and a 2.2-percent increase in hours worked.2018-09-06T12:34:00
