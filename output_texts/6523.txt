Title: Dollar Struggles to Find Momentum
Tag: Currency

Dollar Struggles to Find MomentumUnited States CurrencyUS Dollar Index bottomed near weekly lows around 91.40 on Friday and is poised to end the week on a subdued note as a further retreat in US Treasury yields spooked investors away from the greenback. Recent economic figures from the Labor Department showed the US core inflation came in weaker than expected and hit its lowest level in eight months, sending longer-term yields lower as investors hoped for a more upbeat outlook on consumer prices. Still, additional economic stimulus in the form of a $1.9 trillion coronavirus relief package and better-than-expected weekly jobless claims numbers reinforced the view of a robust US economic bounce, paving the way for further dollar strength in the medium-term.2021-03-12T01:07:18.297
