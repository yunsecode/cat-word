Title: United States Inflation Rate at 1.7% in December of 2012
Tag: Inflation Rate

United States Inflation Rate at 1.7% in December of 2012United States Inflation RateThe all items index increased 1.7 percent over the last 12 months, compared to a 1.8 percent figure in November. The index for all items less food and energy rose 1.9 percent over the last 12 months, the same figure as last month. The food index has risen 1.8 percent over the last 12 months, and the energy index has risen 0.5 percent.2013-01-16T13:35:42.58
