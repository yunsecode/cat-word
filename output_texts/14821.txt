U.S. Inflation Rises to 2.1% in FebruaryUnited States Inflation RateU.S. Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 2.1 percent before seasonal adjustment over the last 12 months, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on March 17.
