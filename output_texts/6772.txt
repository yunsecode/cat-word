Title: Dollar Rally Gathers Pace
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rally Gathers Pace United States CurrencyThe dollar index held above the 90.50 region on Friday and was poised for its biggest weekly gain since October as expectations that the US economy would outperform its peers encouraged investors to buy the greenback. Progress on a massive pandemic-relief package with markets expecting the price tag to be somewhere between $1.3T and Biden’s $1.9T proposal, pushed the US treasury yields close to levels not seen since March, which, in turn, drove buying into the greenback. Also highlighting the US economic rebound was the recent evidence that the labour market’s recovery is gathering pace. These factors contrasted with a decline in eurozone GDP after renewed coronavirus-induced restrictions. Still, the medium to long-term outlook remains bearish owing to the risks posed by record-high debt levels. 2021-02-04T18:48:00
