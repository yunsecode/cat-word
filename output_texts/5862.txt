Title: Mortgage Applications Rise the Most in 2 Months: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

Mortgage Applications Rise the Most in 2 Months: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the US increased 4.2% in the week ending June 11th, the first rise in 4 weeks, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to refinance a home loan surged 5.5% and purchases were up 1.1%. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged down by 4 bps to 3.11 percent, the lowest in 5 weeks. “An almost 5 percent increase in government purchase applications drove most of last week’s gain while also tempering the recent growth in loan sizes,” Joel Kan, MBA’s associate vice president of economic and industry forecasting, said.2021-06-16T11:04:00
