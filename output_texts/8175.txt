Title: US Average Hourly Earnings Fall in May
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Average Hourly Earnings Fall in MayUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings for all employees on US private nonfarm payrolls fell by 29 cents to $29.75, following a gain of $1.35 in April. Average hourly earnings of  private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees decreased by 14 cents to $25.00 in May. The decreases in average hourly earnings largely reflect job gains among lower-paid workers; this change put downward pressure on the average hourly earnings estimates.2020-06-05T12:34:27.49
