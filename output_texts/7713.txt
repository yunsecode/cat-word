Title: US Q2 Nonfarm Labor Costs Revised Lower
Tag: Labour Costs

US Q2 Nonfarm Labor Costs Revised LowerUnited States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the US nonfarm business sector climbed at an annual rate of 9.0 percent in the second quarter of 2020, compared to a preliminary estimate of a 12.2 percent gain and following a 9.6 percent rise in the prior period. A 20 percent increase in hourly compensation outpaced the 10.1 percent increase in productivity.2020-09-03T12:35:00
