Title: US Personal Spending Rises the Least in 5 Months
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Rises the Least in 5 MonthsUnited States Personal SpendingPersonal consumption expenditures in the US increased 0.2 percent month-over-month in January of 2018, following a 0.4 percent rise in December, matching market expectations. It is the lowest gain in household consumption in five months as spending on durables contracted and consumption of services eased.2018-03-01T13:34:38
