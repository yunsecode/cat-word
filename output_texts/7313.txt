Dollar Index Tests Session Peak Near 93 United States CurrencyThe US dollar extended the buying momentum overnight on expectations that central banks would not have to flood markets with more liquidity if a coronavirus vaccine is available soon. At the same time, prospects of a divided Congress and more modest economic stimulus also supported greenback's upward trend. Still, the long-term outlook remains clouded by rising levels of debt coupled with expectations for an extended period of low-interest rates. 2020-11-09T18:39:00
