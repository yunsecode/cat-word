Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most in 3 Months
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most in 3 MonthsUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States jumped by 6.218 million barrels in the week ended April 27th 2018, after a 2.17 million rise in the previous period and compared with market expectations of a 0.739 million increase. It is the biggest rise in crude oil stocks in 13 weeks. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories increased by 1.171 million barrels after rising by 0.84 million in the previous week, while markets were expecting 0.587 million drop.
