Title: US Private Sector Growth Eases Less than Estimated
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Growth Eases Less than EstimatedUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI stood at 54.4 in December 2018, above the preliminary estimate of 53.6 and compared to November's 54.7. It was the lowest reading since September as new order growth softened for the third successive month in December to the joint-weakest since April 2017 and new export orders also increased at a slower pace. In addition, the rate of job creation eased to the second-softest in 18 months. On the price front, input cost inflation softened in December to the slowest since January and charge inflation also eased to the weakest in 2018.2019-01-04T14:57:00
