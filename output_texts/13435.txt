Title: Foreigners Sell $98.3 Billion of US Assets in March
Tag: Capital Flows

Foreigners Sell $98.3 Billion of US Assets in MarchUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors sold $98.3 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments, in March of 2016, after buying a downwardly revised $31.7 billion in February. Foreigners bought $78.1 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying an upwardly revised $72.6 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors also bought $23.6 billion of US Treasuries, after buying $9.9 billion in February.2016-05-16T21:10:48.373
