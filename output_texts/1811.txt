Title: US Food Inflation Slows for 2nd Month
Tag: Food Inflation

US Food Inflation Slows for 2nd MonthUnited States Food InflationCost of food in the United States increased at a slower 10.9% over a year ago in October of 2022, easing further from an 11.2% rise in the previous month. Prices slowed down for food at home (12.4% vs 13% in September), including cereals and bakery products (15.9% vs 16.2%); dairy and related products (15.5% vs 15.9%) and meats, poultry, fish, and eggs (8% vs 9.8%) and fruits & vegetables (9.3% vs 10.4%). Meanwhile, food away from home was more expensive (8.6% vs 8.5%).2022-11-10T13:38:00
