Title: US Treasury Yields Higher as Interest Rates Set to Rise Further
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Higher as Interest Rates Set to Rise FurtherUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note edged higher to 3.76% after remarks from Fed Chair to the Congress reinforced last week message that the Fed will raise rates further this year to fight high inflation. The Federal Reserve kept the fed funds rate steady as expected in June, but signalled further hikes would be necessary. Around 79% of market participants expect the Fed to raise rates by 25bps next month. Two-year Treasury yields considered most sensitive to interest rates rose to 4.7%.26 days ago
