Title: US Private Sector Adds 205K Jobs
Tag: ADP Employment Change

US Private Sector Adds 205K JobsUnited States ADP Employment ChangePrivate businesses in the United States hired 205 thousand workers in January of 2016 compared to an upwardly revised 267 thousand in December and better than market expectations of 195K. The service-providing sector added 192 thousand jobs and goods-producing added 13 thousand: professional/business activities added 44 thousand jobs, trade/transportation/utilities 35 thousand, construction 21 thousand, financial activities 19 thousand while manufacturing created no jobs. 2016-02-03T13:16:30
