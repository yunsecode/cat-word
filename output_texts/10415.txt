Title: Chicago PMI at 6-Month High in July
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago PMI at 6-Month High in JulyUnited States Chicago PmiThe Chicago Business Barometer for the US rose 1.4 points to 65.5 in July of 2018, beating market expectations of 62 and reaching a new high since January. New orders and production also recorded six-month highs in July, traditionally a busy month for firms coinciding with the summer holiday season. Also, backlogs of orders capped a third straight rise by hitting a nine-month high; employment rose for the third month and price pressures were the highest since September of 2008 with multiple firms attributing it to recently implemented tariffs on imported goods.2018-07-31T13:58:10.303
