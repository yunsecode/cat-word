Title: US Dollar Softens ahead of Fed Rate Decision
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Softens ahead of Fed Rate DecisionUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was around 92.6 on Wednesday, off from four-month highs of 93.19 hit last week, as traders await the FOMC meeting for clues on the timing of tapering as policymakers face soaring US inflation. Last week, the greenback ended a volatile week by gaining 0.2%, benefiting from a safe-haven bid on fears that a surge in infections of the Delta variant could derail the global recovery. The dollar strengthened from mid-June after the Fed sharply raised its forecasts for inflation this year and brought forward the time frame on when it will hike interest rates.2021-07-28T04:02:00
