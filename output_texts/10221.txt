Title: US Jobless Claims Drop For Third Straight Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Drop For Third Straight WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits decreased by 3 thousand to 201 thousand in the week ending September 15 from the previous week's unrevised level of 204 thousand, missing market expectations of 210 thousand. Claims fell for the third straight week to hit the lowest level since November 15, 1969. According to unadjusted data, the largest decreases were seen in Illinois (-752), Iowa (-632) and Kansas (-575) while the biggest rises were reported in California (+2,470), New Jersey (+1,398) and Georgia (+1,381).
