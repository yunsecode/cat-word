Title: Dollar Steadies as Traders Weigh Fed Outlook
Tag: Currency

Dollar Steadies as Traders Weigh Fed OutlookUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index steadied near 105 on Friday as investors continued to assess the outlook for Federal Reserve monetary policy in light of recent commentary from central bank officials. Atlanta Fed President Raphael Bostic supported 25 basis point rate increases and said the central bank could be in a position to pause rate hikes sometime this summer. However, Fed Governor Christopher Waller maintained a hawkish view, saying rates could peak higher if labor and inflation numbers don’t cool as expected. On Thursday, the greenback gained about 0.6% after fresh data pointed to a still-tight labor market, reinforcing expectations of prolonged tightening. Investors now look ahead to US services activity data and further central bank commentary on Friday.2023-03-03T03:41:00
