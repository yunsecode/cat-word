Title: US Treasury Yields Steady Ahead of FOMC
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Steady Ahead of FOMCUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury note was little changed at 1.26% on Wednesday as investors await the FOMC minutes release later in the day for a further update on the Fed's next steps. Recent economic data including the Michigan consumer confidence and retail sales disappointed, easing some bets the Fed would soon start tapering. However, fears the economic activity is slowing persisted as well as the prospect that the rapid spread of the coronavirus delta strain would drag the economy even lower.2021-08-18T07:47:20.483
