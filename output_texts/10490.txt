Title: US Inflation Rate Highest since 2012
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Highest since 2012United States Inflation RateThe inflation rate in the US edged up to 2.9 percent in June of 2018 from 2.8 percent in May, matching market expectations. It is the highest rate since February of 2012 when inflation was also at 2.9 percent. Inflation was higher only in December of 2011 when it reached 3 percent. On a monthly basis, prices increased 0.1 percent, lower than 0.2 percent in May and forecasts of 0.2 percent. Prices of shelter, gasoline, and food made the main upward pressure.2018-07-12T12:35:00
