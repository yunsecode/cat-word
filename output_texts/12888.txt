Title: US Stocks Fall on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened mainly lower on Thursday amid falling oil prices, a rising dollar, above estimates data for jobless claims and mixed results for companies: Travelers beat forecasts for both earnings and revenues while Verizon beat on earnings but missed on revenues. The Dow Jones traded near the flatline while the S&P 500 fell 3 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq lost 13 points or 0.3% around 8 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-10-20T13:51:48
