Title: US Trade Deficit Narrows in July
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit Narrows in JulyUnited States Balance of TradeThe trade gap in the United States declined to USD 39.47 billion in July of 2016 from an upwardly revised USD 44.66 billion deficit in the previous month and below market expectations of a USD 42.7 billion shortfall. Imports declined 0.8 percent while exports rose 1.9 percent to the highest level since September 2015.2016-09-02T13:37:19.403
