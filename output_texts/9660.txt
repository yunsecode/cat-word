Title: US Trade Gap Lowest in 5 Months
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Gap Lowest in 5 MonthsUnited States Balance of TradeThe U.S. trade deficit narrowed to USD 49.3 billion in November of 2018 from an upwardly revised USD 55.7 billion in the previous month and compared with market expectations of a USD 54 billion gap. It is the lowest deficit in five months as imports plunged the most since March of 2016 from a record high value reached in the previous month. The goods deficit decreased USD 6.7 billion to USD 71.6 billion. The services surplus shrank USD 0.3 billion to USD 22.3 billion.2019-02-06T13:36:00
