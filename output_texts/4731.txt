Title: Dollar Edges Lower as Concerns Over Omicron Ease
Tag: Currency

Dollar Edges Lower as Concerns Over Omicron EaseUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index edged down to 96 on Wednesday as encouraging news regarding the omicron coronavirus variant boosted investors' risk appetite. Pfizer and BioNTech announced a 3rd dose of its coronavirus vaccine is effective in neutralizing the omicron variant and other studies suggested that although the new strain is more contagious it appears to be less severe than initially expected. Traders now await more economic data for the US to be released during the week including the inflation rate which could strengthen the case for the Fed to continue to tighten. 2021-12-08T13:10:00
