US Stocks Plunge on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on Monday 19 November 2019, dragged by steep declines in shares of Apple, Netflix, Alphabet and Facebook. Negative data flow for tech shares ranged from a production cuts on lower demand to mounting regulatory and antitrust risks. The Dow Jones plunged 395 points or 1.6% to 25018. The S&P plummeted 46 points or 1.7% to 2691. The Nasdaq tanked 219 points or 3.0% to 7029. 2018-11-19T21:02:32.973
