Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall the Most Since 2016
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall the Most Since 2016United States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States slumped by 12.788 million barrels in the week ended June 21st of 2019, following a 3.106 million decrease in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.54 million decline. It was the biggest fall in crude inventories since the week ended September 2nd of 2016. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went down 0.996 million, less than a 1.692 million drop in the prior week and missing market consensus of a 0.288 million gain.  2019-06-26T14:33:00
