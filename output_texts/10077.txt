Title: US Private Sector Growth at 3-Month High: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Growth at 3-Month High: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI rose to 54.8 in October 2018 from 53.9 in September, preliminary figures showed. The reading pointed to the fastest expansion in private sector activity since July, as manufacturing activity growth picked up to 5-month high (PMI at 55.9 from 55.6 in September) and the services sector rose faster (PMI at 54.7 from 53.5).2018-10-24T13:56:00
