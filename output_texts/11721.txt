Title: US Jobless Claims Rise to 236K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise to 236K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 1 thousand to 236 thousand in the week ended August 26th from the previous week's revised level of 235 thousand and in line with market expectations. Meanwhile, the 4-week moving average, which removes week-to-week volatility, fell by 1.25 thousand to 236.75 thousand.2017-08-31T12:31:53.403
