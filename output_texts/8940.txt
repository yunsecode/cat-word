Title: US Housing Starts Beat Forecasts
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Beat ForecastsUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the US went up 3.2 percent month-over-month to an annualized 1.365 million units in November of 2019. It compares with an upwardly revised 1.323 million in the previous month and market expectations of 1.345 million. This is 13.6 percent above the November 2018 rate of 1,202,000. Single-family housing starts in November were at a rate of 938,000; this is 2.4 percent above the revised October figure of 916,000. The November rate for units in buildings with five units or more was 404,000. The housing market has been boosted by a fall in mortgage rates after the Fed cut key rates 3 times this year.2019-12-17T13:33:00
