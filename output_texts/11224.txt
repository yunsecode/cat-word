Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Friday to Book Weekly Gain
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher on Friday to Book Weekly GainUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Friday 19 January 2018, as the government shutdown looming in Washington and a dip in the University of Michigan's consumer confidence to the lowest reading in six months was not enough to offset bearish sentiment as earnings season continues. The Dow Jones gained 54 points or 0.2% to 26072. The S&P 500 climbed 12 points or 0.4% to a fresh record of 2810. The Nasdaq advanced 40 points or 0.6% to a new high of 7336. During the week, the Dow Jones jumped 1.0%; the S&P 500 0.9%; and the Nasdaq 1.0%. 2018-01-19T21:13:01.83
