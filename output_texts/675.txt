Title: Dollar Steadies as Fed Outlook Mulled
Tag: Currency

Dollar Steadies as Fed Outlook MulledUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index steadied around 101.8 on Monday as investors continued to assess the outlook for the US economy and Federal Reserve monetary policy. Data released on Friday showed US business activity expanded the most in nearly a year in April, bolstering the case for further monetary tightening. Investors now look ahead to first quarter US GDP numbers and April consumer sentiment data this week for clues into the state of the country's economy, among others. The Fed is widely expected to deliver another 25 basis point interest rate hike in May, though traders will be monitoring guidance on the future rate path. The European Central Bank is also anticipated to raise rates further, but the market remains split on whether it would be a quarter- or half-percentage point increase next month.2023-04-24T03:09:00
