Title: US Crude Stocks Rise for 1st Time in 2 Months
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Rise for 1st Time in 2 MonthsUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 4.578 million barrels in the week ending September 24th, ending a seven-week period of decline and compared with market forecasts of a 1.652 million drop, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories were up by 0.193 million barrels, less than expectations of a 1.4 million rise.2021-09-29T14:41:43.24
