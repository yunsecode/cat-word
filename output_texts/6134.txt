Title: US Stock Futures Hover Near Record Highs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Hover Near Record HighsUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures were little changed on Tuesday, hovering near their record levels after modest gains in the previous session. Federal Reserve chair Jerome Powell on Monday said the US economy was doing better but was "not out of the woods yet". On the bond market, US Treasury yields fell on Monday after data showed manufacturing activity growth slowing in April amid supply chain challenges and rising demand fueled by the COVID-19 vaccine rollout and fiscal aid. Traders now anticipate services data due on Wednesday and non-farm payroll numbers on Friday. On Monday, the Dow rose 0.7% to close at 34,113, while the S&P 500 went up 0.27% to 4,193. In contrast, the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.48% to 13,895. 2021-05-04T06:09:00
