Title: US Stocks Plunge on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Plunge on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on Thursday 03 January 2019 after Apple cut its revenue forecast for the first time in nearly 12 years amid renewed concerns about China's economic slowdown, triggering a sell-off in tech shares excluding Netflix (+1.3%), benefitted by strong Bird Box and Black Mirror numbers. The Dow Jones plummeted 662 points or 2.8% to 22685. The S&P 500 plunged 62 points or 2.5% to 2448. The Nasdaq retreated 202 points or 3.0% to 6464.2019-01-03T21:06:00
