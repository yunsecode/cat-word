Title: Fed Raises Rate To 1.25%
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Raises Rate To 1.25%United States Interest RateThe Federal Reserve raised the target range for its federal funds rate by 25bps to 1 percent to 1.25 percent during its June 2017 meeting, in line with market expectations. Policymakers kept forecasts for three rate hikes this year while increasing growth projections and lowering inflation expectations. In addition, details on how the central bank will start reducing its USD 4.5 trillion portfolio were also provided.2017-06-14T17:59:51
