Title: Dallas Fed Services Index Lowest since July 2020
Tag: Dallas Fed Services Index

Dallas Fed Services Index Lowest since July 2020United States Dallas Fed Services IndexThe Dallas Fed General Business Activity index for services in Texas fell to -13.6 in October of 2022, the lowest reading since July of 2020, from -5.4 in September, pointing to worsening perceptions of broader business conditions as uncertainty continued to increase. The company outlook index also fell to 9.1 from -1.4 and the outlook uncertainty index increased 13 points to 27.6.2022-11-01T15:04:21.353
