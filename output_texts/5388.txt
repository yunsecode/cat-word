Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Remains High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Remains HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note was around 1.34% on Friday, below one-month highs of 1.37% the day before but above levels from last week. Investors await a speech from Fed Chair at the Jackson Hole symposium in the morning for more details about Fed's plans for tapering of the stimulus. Investors were getting more confident the Federal Reserve will delay taper plans amid weak economic data and worries over the spread of the coronavirus delta variant but several officials implied yesterday that the time has come to start cutting on bond purchases. 2021-08-27T08:06:00
