Title: U.S. Employment Stagnated in August
Tag: Unemployment Rate

U.S. Employment Stagnated in AugustUnited States Unemployment RateU.S. nonfarm payroll employment was unchanged (0) in August, and the unemployment rate held at 9.1 percent, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on September 2. Employment in most major industries changed little over the month. Health care continued to add jobs, and a decline in information employment reflected a strike. Government employment continued to trend down.2011-09-02T13:38:13.22
