Title: US Existing Home Sales Fall More Than Expected In April
Tag: Existing Home Sales

US Existing Home Sales Fall More Than Expected In AprilUnited States Existing Home SalesSales of previously owned houses in the United States fell 2.3 percent month-over-month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 5.57 million in April 2017 from a ten-year high of 5.70 million in March and below market expectations of 5.65 million. Sales of single family houses dropped 2.4 percent after rising by 4.1 percent in the previous month and those of condos declined 1.6 percent, following a 5 percent gain in March. The average price went up 3.2 percent after rising by 3.4 percent in the previous month and the months' worth of supply went up to 4.2 percent from 3.8 percent. In addition, the number of houses available in the market increased to 1.93 million from 1.80 million in March.2017-05-24T14:03:06
