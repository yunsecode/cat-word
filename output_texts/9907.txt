Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States went up 2 percent in the week ended November 30th 2018, after a 5.5 percent jump in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications increased 6.2 percent and applications to purchase a home went up 0.8 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 4bps to 5.08 percent. 2018-12-05T12:03:52.307
