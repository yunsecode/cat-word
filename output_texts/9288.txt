Title: US Stocks Recover from Early Losses
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Recover from Early LossesUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks reversed early losses and traded higher on Wednesday after CNBC reported that the Trump administration is planing to delay tariffs on car imports by up to six months amid ongoing trade negotiations with China. Meanwhile, poor economic data showed retail sales and industrial output fell unexpectedly last month, weighing on sentiment. The Dow Jones gained 21 points, or 0.1%, to 25,553; the S&P 500 advanced 9 points, or 0.3%, 2,843; and the Nasdaq climbed 58 points, or 0.8%, to 7,794 around 11 AM NY time.2019-05-15T14:52:25.79
