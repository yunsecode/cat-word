Title: US Retail Sales Unexpectedly Fall In June
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Unexpectedly Fall In JuneUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States decreased by 0.2 percent month-over-month in June 2017, following a downwardly revised 0.1 percent drop in May and missing market expectations of a 0.1 percent gain. It was the second straight month of decline in retail trade, mainly driven by lower sales at miscellaneous store retailers and gasoline stations. On a year-on-year basis, retail sales rose 2.8 percent.2017-07-14T12:31:22
