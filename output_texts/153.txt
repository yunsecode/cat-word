US Crude Oil Inventories Drop More than Expected: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the US fell by 1.246 million barrels in the week ended June 16th, 2023, after a 1.024 million barrels increase in the previous week and well below market expectations of a 0.433 million barrel decline, data from the API's Weekly Statistical Bulletin showed.
