Title: US Stocks Fall On Friday To End Strong Quarter
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall On Friday To End Strong QuarterUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed modestly in the red on Friday, as continued uncertainty about the outlook for President Donald Trump's policy agenda led to choppy trading. On Friday, the Dow Jones lost 65 points, or 0.3 percent, to 20663. The S&P 500 fell 5 points, or 0.2 percent, to 2363. The Nasdaq lost 3 points and stayed practically unchanged. During the week, the Dow Jones gained 0.3 percent; the S&P 500 0.8 percent; and the Nasdaq 1.4 percent. For the whole first quarter of 2017, all major indices booked strong gains, with the S&P increasing by 4.6 percent; the S&P 500 by 5.5 percent; and the Nasdaq by 9.8 percent.  2017-03-31T20:10:52
