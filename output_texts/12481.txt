Title: US Pending Home Sales Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned US homes went up 0.3 percent year-on-year in December of 2016, following a 0.4 percent drop in November. Compared to the previous month, sales increased 1.6 percent after slumping by 2.5 percent in November and beating market expectations of 1.1 percent. Across main four regions, contracts rose in the West (5 percent) and South (2.4 percent) but fell in the Northeast (-1.6 percent) and the Midwest (-0.8 percent).2017-01-30T15:10:28.367
