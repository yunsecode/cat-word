Title: US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than Expected
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than ExpectedUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States increased by $13.42 billion in April 2016 following a downwardly revised $28.38 billion rise in March, way below market expectations of a $18 billion gain. Non-revolving credit rose by $11.77 billion, after increasing by $17.94 billion in the previous month and revolving credit went up by only $1.65 billion, compared with a $10.44 billion rise in March.2016-06-07T20:05:21
