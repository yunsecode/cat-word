Title: US Jobless Claims Rise to 5-Week High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise to 5-Week HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 3,000 to 269,000 in the week ended July 30th compared with the previous week's unrevised level of 266,000. The figure came in above market expectations of 265,000. Still, this marks 74 consecutive weeks of initial claims below 300,000, the longest streak since 1973.2016-08-04T13:35:05
