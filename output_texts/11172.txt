Title: US Unit Labor Costs Jump 2% in Q4
Tag: Labour Costs

US Unit Labor Costs Jump 2% in Q4United States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the US nonfarm business sector rose an annualized 2 percent in the fourth quarter of 2017, following a 0.1 percent drop in the previous three-month period and beating market expectations of 0.9 percent. The increase was led by  a 1.8-percent advance in hourly compensation. Considering full 2017, labor costs edged up 0.2 percent, following a 1.1 percent rise in 2016.2018-02-01T13:35:02
