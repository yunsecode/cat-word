Title: US Crude Stocks Fall for 3rd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Fall for 3rd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories dropped by 5.241 million barrels in the June 4th week, a third consecutive period of decline and compared with market forecasts of a 2.036 million fall, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. Meantime, gasoline inventories rose by 7.046 million barrels, the most since April 2020 and compared with consensus of a smaller 0.698 million advance. 2021-06-09T14:34:49.067
