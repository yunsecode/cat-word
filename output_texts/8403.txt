Title: US Exports Tumble 9.6% to Over 3-Year Low
Tag: Exports

US Exports Tumble 9.6% to Over 3-Year LowUnited States ExportsExports from the United States dropped by USD 20.0 billion from the previous month to USD 187.7 billion in March 2020, the lowest level since November 2016, as the COVID-19 crisis hit global demand. There was a USD 9.2 billion decline in goods sales and a USD 10.8 billion fall in service exports mostly due to travel and transport. Within goods, exports were down for industrial supplies and materials (down USD 2.9 billion), such as crude oil, fuel oil and other petroleum products; automotive vehicles, parts, and engines (down USD 2.5 billion); and capital goods (down USD 2.0 billion) in particular other industrial machinery, civilian aircraft, parts and engines.2020-05-05T12:33:00
