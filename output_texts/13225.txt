Title: Wall Street Opens Nearly Flat on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Nearly Flat on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the United States opened flat to lower on Thursday as investors await earnings reports for several companies. Meanwhile, General Motors’ results came better than anticipated. The Dow Jones lost 15 points or 0.1%, the S&P 500 was down 2 points or 0.1% while the Nasdaq traded near the flatline around 3 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-07-21T14:42:14.54
