Title: US Factory Orders Fall More Than Expected In May
Tag: Factory Orders

US Factory Orders Fall More Than Expected In MayUnited States Factory OrdersNew orders for manufactured goods in the United States fell by 0.8 percent month-over-month in May 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.3 percent drop in April and worse than market expectations of a 0.5 percent decline. It was the biggest drop in new orders for US-made goods since November last year, as orders fell for transport equipment (-3 percent), computers and electronic products (-0.2 percent) and non-durable goods (-0.8 percent). Compared with the same month a year ago, factory orders were up 4.8 percent.2017-07-05T14:03:04
