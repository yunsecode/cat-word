Title: Wall Street Regains Some Momentum
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Regains Some Momentum United States Stock MarketWall Street's main indexes began a holiday-shortened week in the green as investors took advantage of a selloff in the previous week to open new positions, but fears that the global economy will slow dramatically amid tightening financial conditions kept sentiment in check. Airline stocks were among the biggest gainers ahead of an anticipated leisure travel boom, with Spirit Airlines surging almost 10% after JetBlue raised its takeover offer. Recently beaten-down technology stocks also enjoy some respite, with Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Facebook, Tesla, and Apple trading in positive territory. Still, investors brace for further volatility in equities, with Fed Chair Jerome Powell set to testify before Congress Wednesday and Thursday.2022-06-21T13:33:00
