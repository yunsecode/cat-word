Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall 3.7%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall 3.7%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States decreased 3.7 percent in the week ended February 10th 2017, following a 2.3 percent drop in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications declined 2.9 percent and applications to purchase a home went down 4.5 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates fell by 3bps to 4.32 percent.2017-02-15T12:06:15
