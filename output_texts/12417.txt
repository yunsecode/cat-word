Title: US Stocks Recover After Yellen Speech
Tag: stock market

US Stocks Recover After Yellen SpeechUnited States stock marketWall Street traded in positive territory on Tuesday afternoon, recovering from earlier declines after remarks from Chair Yellen before Congress suggested the Fed could raise rates soon. All three major stock indexes reached record highs: the Dow Jones gained 58 points or 0.3% to 20,470.36; the S&P 500 went up 6 points or 0.3% to 2,334.25 and the Nasdaq rose 14 points or 0.2% to 5,777.46 around 1:30 PM NY time.2017-02-14T16:57:47
