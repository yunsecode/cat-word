Title: US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI Disappoints
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI DisappointsUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States fell to 56.8 in April of 2018 from 58.8 in March, well below market expectations of 58.1. The reading pointed to the lowest expansion in the non-manufacturing sector in four months amid a slowdown in production and employment and uncertainty about tariffs and the effect on the cost of goods. However, firms remain positive about business conditions and the economy.2018-05-03T14:06:56
