Title: Dollar Remains at 1-Month Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar Remains at 1-Month LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index remained near 4-week lows of 92.5 at the beginning of September, as investors look forward to Friday’s payroll report for a clearer direction on the labour market recovery. Initial claims fell more than expected to a new pandemic low and the job cuts were the lowest in 24 years but the ADP report yesterday showed the private employment grew much less than expected. The Fed is focusing on its recovery to set the timeline to monetary policy tightening in the US.2021-09-02T12:48:00
