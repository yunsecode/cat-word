Title: US Building Permits Fall Less Than Expected
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Fall Less Than Expected United States Building PermitsBuilding permits for privately-owned housing units in the United States decreased by 3.9 percent month-on-month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,232,000 in December of 2015 while analysts were expecting a 6.4 percent drop. Single-family authorizations rose 1.8 percent to 740,000 and those of units in buildings with five units or more went down 13.5 percent to 455,000. Year-on-year, building permits increased by 14.4 percent. Considering full 2015, building permits were 12 percent higher at 1,178,400 compared to 2014.
