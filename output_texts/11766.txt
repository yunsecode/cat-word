Title: US Housing Starts Unexpectedly Fall in July
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Unexpectedly Fall in JulyUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States slumped 4.8 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1155 thousand in July of 2017, following a downwardly revised 1213 thousand in the previous month and compared to market expectations of a 0.5 percent rise. Starts declined in the Northeast, Midwest and the West.
