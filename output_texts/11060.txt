Title: US New Home Sales Fall for 2nd Month
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Fall for 2nd MonthUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States shrank 7.8 percent month-over-month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 593 thousand in January of 2018 from an upwardly revised 643 thousand in December. It is the lowest reading since August and compares with market forecasts of a 3.8 percent rise to 650 thousand. Sales fell in the Northeast and the South.2018-02-26T15:03:04
