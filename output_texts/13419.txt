Title: US Existing Home Sales Rise for 2nd Month
Tag: Existing Home Sales

US Existing Home Sales Rise for 2nd MonthUnited States Existing Home SalesSales of previously owned houses in the United States increased 1.7 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 5450 thousand in April of 2016, following an upwardly revised 5.7 percent gain in March and higher than market expectations of a 1.3 percent rise. Sales of single family went up 0.6 percent and those of condos climbed 10.3 percent. The average price rose 4 percent and the months’ worth of supply went up to 4.7 from 4.4. Sales were up 6 percent from a year ago.2016-05-20T15:04:40
