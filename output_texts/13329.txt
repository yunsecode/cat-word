Title: Wall Street Opens Higher Ahead Of Yellen, Brexit
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Higher Ahead Of Yellen, BrexitUnited States Stock MarketEquities in the US opened in the green on Tuesday led by gains in the tech sector while energy shares fell amid a retreat in oil prices. Meanwhile, investors await Fed's Yellen two-day testimony before Congress and the UK referendum on EU membership. The Dow Jones added 0.1% or 15 points, the S&P 500 rose 0.2% or 4 points and the Nasdaq gained 0.26% or 13 points around 20 minutes after the opening bell.2016-06-21T14:54:40.867
