Title: US Services Sector Growth At 16-Month High: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Sector Growth At 16-Month High: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index rose to 57.6 in February of 2017 from 56.5 in January and beating market expectations of 56.5. It is the highest reading since October of 2015, boosted by a surge in activity and rising new orders and employment. The majority of service providers indicated a positive outlook on business conditions and the overall economy although some uncertainty persists. 2017-03-03T15:03:09
