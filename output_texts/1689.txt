Title: Mortgage Rates Fall for 3rd Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Rate

Mortgage Rates Fall for 3rd Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage RateThe average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($647,200 or less) in the US fell by 18bps to 6.49% in the week ended November 25th, following a 23bps drop in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association (MBA) showed. It is the third consecutive week that borrowing costs fall and now stand at their lowest level in since mid-September, tracking a retreat in bond yields. 2022-11-30T12:11:44.857
