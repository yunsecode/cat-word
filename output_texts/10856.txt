Title: US Stocks Rebound
Tag: stock market

US Stocks ReboundUnited States stock marketWall Street opened sharply higher on Thursday as worries over a US military action on Syria eased after President Trump tweeted "Never said when an attack on Syria would take place. Could be very soon or not so soon at all!". The Dow Jones was up 286 points or 1.2%, the S&P 500 rose 23 points or 0.9% and the Nasdaq traded 63 points or 0.9% higher around 20 minutes after the opening bell.2018-04-12T13:42:05
