Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States declined 2.6 percent in the week ended July 27th 2018, after edging down 0.2 percent in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home dropped 3.1 percent and refinance applications went down 1.7 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate jumped 7bps at 4.84 percent.2018-08-01T11:03:11.37
