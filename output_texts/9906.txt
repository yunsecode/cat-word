Title: US Job Cuts Fall to 53.1K in November: Challenger
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Job Cuts Fall to 53.1K in November: Challenger United States Challenger Job CutsJob cuts announced by US-based employers fell by 29.8 percent month-over-month to 53,073 in November of 2018 but rose 51.5 percent year-on-year. General Motors announced it would cut 15 percent of its workforce, or up to 14,000 employees, after offering 18,000 buyouts to workers in an effort to save over $6 billion. Challenger counted 14,000 cuts due to cost-cutting. GM’s announcement is the 7th largest single job cut announcement by an Automotive company since 2001. So far this year, employers have announced 494,775 cuts, 28 percent higher than the 386,347 announced through this point last year. This is the highest 11-month total since 2015, when 574,888 cuts were tracked through November. Retail continues to lead all sectors in job cut announcements with 96,504, followed by telecommunications (59,518), financials (41,351), health care (39,443), consumer products (30,733) and autos (28,529 cuts).2018-12-06T12:39:08.677
