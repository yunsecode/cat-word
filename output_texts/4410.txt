Title: US Stocks Likely to Open Mixed and End January Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Likely to Open Mixed and End January LowerUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were mixed on the last trading day of January, with contracts on the Dow Jones falling more than 150 points while the S&P 500 edged lower and the Nasdaq attempted to remain in the green. So far on the month, the Dow and S&P 500 are down 5% and 7%, respectively, and head for their worst month in over a year. The Nasdaq is off by 12% and is on track for its worst month since October 2008. Markets have been volatile this month due to concerns the Fed will adopt a more hawkish monetary policy with investors increasingly betting on 5 rate hikes in the fed funds rate until December. Also, the earnings season has seen some surprises on the downside. Advanced Micro, Exxon Mobil, GM, Alphabet, Meta, Amazon, Honeywell, Ford, PayPal, and Wynn Resorts are due to report this week. The payrolls report, JOLTS and ADP figures will also be in the spotlight this week.
