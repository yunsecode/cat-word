Title: US Stocks Fall After Fed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall After FedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Wednesday after the Fed left rates unchanged as expected but said both inflation and core inflation have moved close to 2%, suggesting higher inflation ahead. The Dow Jones lost 174 points or 0.7% to 23925; the S&P 500 fell 19 points or 0.7% to 2636; and the Nasdaq closed 30 points or 0.4% lower at 7101. 2018-05-02T20:54:00.107
