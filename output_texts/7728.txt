Title: US Crude Stocks Fall for 6th Straight Week
Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

US Crude Stocks Fall for 6th Straight WeekUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States slumped by 6.4 million barrels in the week ended August 28th of 2020, following a 4.5 million gain in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 1.9 million decline, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed. 2020-09-01T20:56:00
