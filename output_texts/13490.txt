Title: US Jobless Claims Rise to 257K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise to 257KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits was 257,000 in the week ended April 23rd, an increase of 9,000 from the previous week's revised level of 248,000, better than market expectations of 260,000. This marks 60 consecutive weeks of initial claims below 300,000, the longest streak since 1973.2016-04-28T13:34:20
