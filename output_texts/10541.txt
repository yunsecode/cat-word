Title: Chicago Business Barometer Hits 5-Month High
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago Business Barometer Hits 5-Month HighUnited States Chicago PmiThe Chicago Business Barometer in the US rose 1.4 points to 64.1 in June 2018, beating market expectations of 60. It was the highest level since January, as new order growth picked up for a second straight month to a five-month high and firms' unfulfilled orders continued to rise. On the other hand, production lost ground for the fourth time since peaking in December.2018-06-29T14:23:00
