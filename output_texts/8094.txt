Title: Dollar rebounds from 2-Week Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar rebounds from 2-Week LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index gained slightly to 96.8 on Wednesday, recovering from a 2-week low reached in the previous session, amid a general decline in investors' risk appetite. Fears of a second wave of coronavirus mount as new infections are rising in the US, China, Latin America, India and Germany. Also, Bloomberg reported that the Trump administration is considering new tariffs on $3.1 billion of exports from the UK, France, Spain and Germany, raising further trade tensions. However, the US dollar is near levels not seen since early March, as investors still hope for a fast global economic recovery.2020-06-24T09:41:00
