Title: US Futures Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures HigherUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were higher on the Thanksgiving, extending gains from the previous session, as investors continue to bet the American economic recovery will remain resilient despite persistent price pressures and an eventual faster monetary policy tightening by the Fed. Recent FOMC minutes showed the Fed is ready to cut stimulus and raise interest rates sooner than anticipated if inflation continued to run high. On Wednesday, the Dow Jones ended little changed at 35804, the S&P 500 gained 0.2% to 4701 and the Nasdaq closed 0.4% higher at 15845. The stock market will be closed on Thursday and will close early on Friday for the Thanksgiving holiday.2021-11-25T08:21:02.21
