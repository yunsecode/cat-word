Title: Dollar Hits 11-Month High
Tag: Currency

Dollar Hits 11-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index gained for the sixth straight day on Monday, hitting 99.9, the highest since December last year amid rising expectations of a fiscal boost and tax cuts under Trump's presidency and more Fed rate hikes. The greenback was up 1.3% against the Euro at 1.07; 0.5% against the Pound; 1% against the yen; 1% against the Swiss franc and 0.3% against the Loonie around 9:30 AM London time.2016-11-14T09:36:16.493
