Title: US Wages Rise Less than Expected in June
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Rise Less than Expected in JuneUnited States Average Hourly EarningsUS average hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls rose by 5 cents to USD 26.98, or 0.2 percent, in June 2018, following an unrevised 0.3 percent gain in May and slightly below market expectations of a 0.3 percent rise. Over the year, average hourly earnings have increased by 72 cents, or 2.7 percent. Average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees increased by 4 cents to USD 22.62 in June.
