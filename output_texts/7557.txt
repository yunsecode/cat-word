Title: Dow and S&P 500 Rise for 2nd Day
Tag: Stock Market

Dow and S&P 500 Rise for 2nd DayUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones rose more than 100 points and the S&P 500 gained 15 points on Tuesday, extending the previous session's rally. Fed Chair Powell called for additional fiscal and monetary stimulus to help the US economy amid the pandemic crisis, saying that too little support would lead to a weak recovery, while the risks of overdoing it seems to be smaller. At the same time, the White House doctor said that President Trump had a restful first night at home and that today he reports no symptoms. On the stimulus front, there is still no deal between Democrats and Republicans and the House Speaker Pelosi and the Treasury Secretary Mnuchin plan to speak again today. Boeing shares fell after the company said it expects the Covid-19 crisis to hurt sales for more than a decade. The Nasdaq traded slightly lower as news emerged that a House panel is about to propose reforms in the tech sector to prevent companies like Amazon or Apple from owning marketplaces and selling their own products on them.2020-10-06T17:34:00
