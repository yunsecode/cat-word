US Stocks Fall on Thursday United States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Thursday, following losses in most European and Asian shares after trade data for China showed exports fell much more than anticipated and imports declined. The Dow Jones was down 142 points or 0.8%, the S&P 500 fell 19 points or 0.9% and the Nasdaq lost 49 points or 0.9% around 13 minutes after the opening bell.2016-10-13T13:47:32
