Title: US New Home Sales at 10-Month Low
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales at 10-Month LowUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States shrank 11.5 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 468,000 in September of 2015, the lowest since November last year. The stock of new houses for sale increased to its highest since March of 2010.2015-10-26T14:05:06
