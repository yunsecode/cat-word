Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yields Rise for 2nd Day
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yields Rise for 2nd DayUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note increased for the second straight session to 0.689% on Monday, as investors move from safe-haven assets amid easing global economic worries after economies began to gradually reopen. The rate of coronavirus related hospitalizations in the state of New York, the most affected in the country, slowed to levels not seen since March. On Sunday, White House officials said that informal talks with Republicans and Democrats about another aid package have started after the US reported a further 20.5 million jobs from nonfarm payrolls were lost due to the coronavirus pandemic and more are expected in the coming months.2020-05-11T11:15:00
