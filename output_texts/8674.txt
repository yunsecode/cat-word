Title: Dollar Loses Near 1% after Fed Move
Tag: Currency

Dollar Loses Near 1% after Fed MoveUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index dropped near 1% to 97.75 on Sunday night after the Federal Reserve cut the target range for its federal funds rate by 100 bps to 0-0.25% during an emergency move. The measure aims to protect the economy from the impact of the coronavirus and follows another emergency 50bps slash on March 3rd. 2020-03-15T22:12:00
