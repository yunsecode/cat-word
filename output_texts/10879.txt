Title: Fed Gradual Rate Hikes Likely to Continue
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Gradual Rate Hikes Likely to ContinueUnited States Interest RateFOMC's patient approach has paid dividends and contributed to the strong economy we have today, Fed Chair Powell said in his first speech as chairman on the economic outlook at The Economic Club of Chicago, suggesting the Fed remains cautious on the economic outlook and the path of rate hikes. Fed Chair added that as long as the economy continues broadly on its current path, further gradual increases in the federal funds rate will best promote the goals of 2 percent inflation, a sustained economic expansion and a strong labor market.2018-04-06T18:16:26.167
