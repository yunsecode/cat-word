Title: Chicago Fed National Activity Index at 12-Month High
Tag: Chicago Fed National Activity Index

Chicago Fed National Activity Index at 12-Month HighUnited States Chicago Fed National Activity IndexThe Chicago Fed National Activity Index in the US rose to 0.27 in July of 2016 from a downwardly revised 0.05 in June. It is the highest figure since July of 2015, beating market expectations of 0.2, led by improvements in production-related indicators. The contribution from production-related indicators rose to 0.23 from 0.07 in June; employment-related indicators contributed 0.09, up from 0.05 while the contribution of the personal consumption and housing category was steady at –0.06. The index’s three-month moving average increased to –0.10 from –0.19.
