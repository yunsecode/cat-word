Title: US Exports at 10-Month High
Tag: Exports

US Exports at 10-Month HighUnited States ExportsExports from the United States increased 3.4 percent from the previous month to USD 190 billion in December of 2020, the highest level since February and the seventh straight month of rises, as global demand continued to recover from the coronavirus pandemic shock. Goods exports were up by USD 6.0 billion to USD 133.5 billion, boosted by sales of industrial supplies and materials (USD 1.8 billion), such as crude oil (USD 1.3 billion), and foods, feeds, and beverages (USD 1.4 billion). Services exports increased by USD 0.3 billion to USD 56.5 billion, led by travel (USD 0.2 billion). For 2020, exports were USD 2,131.9 billion, down USD 396.4 billion from 2019.2021-02-05T13:35:00
