Title: Wall Street Rally Gains Steam
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Rally Gains SteamUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow added almost 700 points on Tuesday, and the S&P 500 and Nasdaq rallied 2.6% and 2.7%, respectively, on hopes that weaker-than-expected US economic data would temper the extreme hawkish tone from the Federal Reserve. The number of job openings in the US fell more than expected in August, signaling that tighter financial conditions could be cooling the job market. This data came a day after a gauge of US manufacturing from ISM showed activity declined to the lowest since May 2020. Investors also welcomed a fading dollar and government bond yields, which brought some respite to beaten-down technology and other high-growth stocks. Meanwhile, Fed officials continue to advocate for further rate hikes. New York Fed Bank President John Williams, Federal Reserve Governor Philip Jefferson, and San Francisco Federal Reserve Bank President Mary Daly echoed that reducing inflation is the top priority and that the job is far from done. 2022-10-04T13:32:00
