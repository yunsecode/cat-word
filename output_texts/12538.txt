Title: US Consumer Sentiment Falls Slightly In January
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Falls Slightly In JanuaryUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe preliminary reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States edged down to 98.1 in January of 2017 from a 13-year high of 98.2 in the previous month and lower than market forecasts of 98.5. Future expectations declined and inflationary pressures jumped.
