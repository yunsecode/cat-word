Title: Wall Street Pares Gains
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Pares Gains United States Stock MarketUS stocks pared some of their early gains that drove the Dow Jones index above 30,330 points for the first time on Monday, as investors remained concerned over the threats posed by renewed restrictions on the business activity, despite the roll-out of the first coronavirus vaccines. Investors also welcomed the news that US lawmakers plan to split a $908 COVID-19 aid-bill into two parts to make it easier for approval. Meantime, the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq Composite were lifted by a 30% jump in Alexion Pharmaceuticals stocks after AstraZeneca said it would buy the US biotech firm.2020-12-14T19:28:02.023
