Title: US Housing Starts Fall to 7-Month Low
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Fall to 7-Month LowUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States shrank 11 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1060 thousand in October of 2015, compared to a downwardly revised 1191 thousand in the previous month. It is the lowest figure since May, well below market expectations but remaining above 1 million for 7th straight month.2015-11-18T13:36:27
