Title: US Natural Gas Stocks Rise Less than Expected: EIA
Tag: Natural Gas Stocks Change

US Natural Gas Stocks Rise Less than Expected: EIAUnited States Natural Gas Stocks ChangeUS utilities added 84 billion cubic feet of gas into storage during the week ended June 9, 2023, less than market expectations of a 95 bcf increase. That compared with an increase of 94 bcf in the same week last year and a five-year (2018-2022) average increase of 84 bcf. Last week's increase brought stockpiles to 2.634 trillion cubic feet (tcf), 552 bcf higher than last year and 353 bcf above the five-year average of 2.281 bcf. At 2.634 tcf, total working gas is within the five-year historical range.2023-06-15T14:32:35.987
