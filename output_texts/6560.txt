Title: US Stocks Extend Gains
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend GainsUnited States Stock MarketUS futures rose in the early trading in Asia with Dow Jones jumping as much as 200 points as investors become more optimistic about global economic recovery. Long-awaited President Biden's $1.9 trillion coronavirus aid bill was approved by the Senate on Saturday and sent back to the House for voting. Meanwhile,  on Friday data showed non-farm payroll employment grew more than expected in February. On Friday, the Dow Jones surged 574 points or 1.9% to 31,498. The S&P 500 added 73 points or 2% to 3842. The Nasdaq jumped 197 points or 1.6% to 12,920. 2021-03-08T00:04:00
