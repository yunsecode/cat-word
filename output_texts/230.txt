Title: US Natural Gas Stocks Rise More than Expected: EIA
Tag: Natural Gas Stocks Change

US Natural Gas Stocks Rise More than Expected: EIAUnited States Natural Gas Stocks ChangeUS utilities added 118 billion cubic feet of gas into storage during the week ended June 2, 2023, slightly more than market expectations of a 113 bcf increase as mild weather limited demand for the fuel for both heating and cooling. Last week's increase brought stockpiles to 2.550 trillion cubic feet (tcf), 562 bcf higher than last year and 353 bcf above the five-year average of 2.197 bcf. At 2.550 tcf, total working gas is within the five-year historical range.2023-06-08T14:36:21.893
