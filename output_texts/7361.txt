Title: US Stock Futures Cut Gains to Point to Mixed Start
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Cut Gains to Point to Mixed StartUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures pointed to a mixed start on Wednesday, with Dow Jones futures falling around 300 points after a 200-point rally earlier in the evening, as investors hope that a clear winner would emerge from the US presidential election and fiscal stimulus talks would soon be reactivated. Polls have closed in more than half of all states, including Florida, Ohio, North Carolina and Georgia, with Biden sweeping up 131 electoral votes so far and Trump picking up 98 votes. In Florida, which is a key battleground state, the result was too close to call, while Pennsylvania, North Carolina and Ohio were too early to call. Markets are also watching some key Senate races, which could lead to Democrats taking control of Congress. On Tuesday, the Dow Jones jumped 555 points or 2.1% to 27,480. The S&P 500 climbed 59 points or 1.8% to 3369. The Nasdaq surged 203 points or 1.9% to 11,161.2020-11-04T01:18:00
