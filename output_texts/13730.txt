Title: US Exports Down 0.3% in December, -4.8% in 2015
Tag: Exports

US Exports Down 0.3% in December, -4.8% in 2015United States ExportsExports in the United States declined 0.3 percent to USD 181.5 billion in December from USD 182.01 billion in the previous month.
