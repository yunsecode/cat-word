Title: US 10-Year Bond Yield Falls after Fed
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Bond Yield Falls after FedUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note declined to 0.6586% on Monday as investors rush for safe assets amid mounting fears over the economic impact of the coronavirus and after another emergency interest rate cut from the Fed failed to add confidence. The yield on the 30-year Treasury bond also declined to 1.2893%.2020-03-16T10:51:00
