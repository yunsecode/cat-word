Title: US Treasury Yields Edge Up
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Edge UpUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note rose to 1.47%, above a two-and-a-half-month low of 1.36% hit on December 3rd as rising rates of Covid and the new Omicron variant pose a new risk to the US economy. The Omicron variant of coronavirus is now the dominant variant in the US and makes up 73.2% of recent cases. The rate of daily infections is now double from late October, when it was at a 3-month low. In addition, Goldman Sachs lowered US 2022 economic growth as US Senator Joe Manchin said he would not support President Biden's $1.9 trillion Build Back Better Act. The Federal Reserve’s more hawkish turn on monetary policy also remained in focus. The US Fed said at its December meeting it would speed up its tapering of bond purchases, putting it on track to conclude it in March 2022 and paving the way for three interest rate hikes by the end of next year. 2021-12-21T08:21:00
