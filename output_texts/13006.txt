Title: US Housing Starts Fall More than Expected
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States shrank 5.8 percent to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1142 thousand in August from July of 2016, after rising a downwardly revised 1.4 percent in the previous period. Figures came much worse than market expectations of a 1.7 percent decline, reaching the lowest in three months. Permits for future construction fell 0.4 percent to 1139 thousand. 2016-09-20T12:38:29.373
