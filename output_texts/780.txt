Title: US Core Consumer Prices Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Core Inflation Rate MoM

US Core Consumer Prices Rise Less than ExpectedUnited States Core Inflation Rate MoMUS core consumer prices, which exclude volatile items such as food and energy, rose by 0.4 percent from a month earlier in March of 2023, in line with market expectations, and slowing slightly from the 0.5 percent increase in the previous month. Consumer costs continued to fall for used cars and trucks and medical care services, offsetting the increase for transportation services and shelter. Year-on-year, core consumer prices advanced by 5.6 percent.2023-04-12T12:33:00
