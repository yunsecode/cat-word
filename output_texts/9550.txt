Title: US Wages Rise More than Expected in February
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Rise More than Expected in FebruaryUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings for all employees on US private nonfarm payrolls increased by 11 cents, or 0.4 percent from the previous month to USD 27.66 in February 2019, following a 0.1 percent gain in January and beating market estimates of a 0.3 percent rise. That left the annual increase in wages at 3.4 percent compared to a downwardly revised 3.1 percent gain in January and above market expectations of a 3.3 percent rise. Meanwhile, average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees went up by 8 cents, or 0.4 percent, to USD 23.18 in February.
