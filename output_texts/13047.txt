Title: US Job Openings at Record High in July
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings at Record High in JulyUnited States Job OffersJob openings in the United States increased by 228,000 to 5.87 million in July 2016 compared to an upwardly revised 5.64 million in the previous month, beating market expectations of 5.58 million. The number of positions waiting to be filled increased mostly in professional and business services (+166,000) and durable goods manufacturing (+27,000) but decreased in health  care and social assistance (-63,000). Among  regions, job openings increased by the most in the West (+76,000), followed by the Midwest (+74,000) and the Northeast (+74,000).2016-09-07T15:09:28
