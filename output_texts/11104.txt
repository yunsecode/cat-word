Title: US Business Inventories Growth Above Forecasts
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Growth Above ForecastsUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the United States rose 0.4 percent month-over-month in December 2017, the same as in November and above market expectations of 0.3 percent. Stocks rose 0.5 percent at manufacturers and 0.2 percent at retailers, the same as in November. On the other hand, inventories rose slightly less at wholesalers (0.4 percent compared to 0.6 percent) and declined more at autos/parts dealers (-0.4 percent compared to -0.2 percent). Inventories excluding autos, which go into the calculation of GDP, increased 0.5 percent, higher than 0.4 percent in November.2018-02-14T15:08:53.627
