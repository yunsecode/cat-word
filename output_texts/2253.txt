Title: US Mortgage Rates Climb to Highest since October 2008
Tag: 30 Year Mortgage Rate

US Mortgage Rates Climb to Highest since October 2008United States 30 Year Mortgage RateThe average rate on a 30-year fixed mortgage climbed to 6.29% this week, the highest since October 2008, up from 6.02% last week and 2.88% a year ago, according to a survey of lenders by mortgage giant Freddie Mac. It comes after the 10-year Treasury yield hit its highest level since 2011. The 15-year fixed-rate mortgage averaged 5.44%, up from 5.21% last week and 5.21% a year ago. The average rate on a five-year Treasury-indexed hybrid adjustable-rate mortgage, or ARM, was 4.97%, up from 4.93% last week and 2.43% last year.2022-09-22T15:23:00
