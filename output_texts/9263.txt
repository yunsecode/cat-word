Title: US Stocks Plunge
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks PlungeUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks fell sharply on Thursday amid ongoing trade tensions with China as companies such as Arm Holdings, Panasonic, Vodafone and BT Group suspended some business with Huawei, following the Trump administration's decision to add Chinese telecom giant to a blacklist. The Dow Jones plunged more than 300 points, or 1.3% to 25,443; the S&P 500 lost 1.2% to 2,821; and the Nasdaq fell 1.4% to 7,640 around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2019-05-23T13:42:43.857
