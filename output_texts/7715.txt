Title: US Futures Take a Breather
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Take a BreatherUnited States Stock MarketDow futures were nearly flat while the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq traded in the red on Thursday as investors took a breather after a rally the day before. Cyclical stocks outperformed on Wednesday, in a sign of a broader economic recovery from the coronavirus pandemic. Meanwhile, investors await the claims reports, the ISM non-manufacturing PMI, productivity and trade figures to be released later in the day. On Wednesday, the Dow Jones soared 455 points or 1.6% to 29,099. The S&P 500 jumped 54 points or 1.5% to a new high 3581. The Nasdaq climbed 117 points or 1% to a fresh record 12,056..2020-09-03T07:39:00
