Title: US Jobless Claims Rise to 251K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise to 251K United States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 18 thousand to 251 thousand in the week ended November 19th from the previous week's revised level of 233 thousand and slightly above market expectations of 250 thousand. This marks 90 consecutive weeks of initial claims below 300 thousand, the longest streak since 1970.2016-11-23T13:35:31
