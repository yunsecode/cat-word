Title: US Stocks Close Mostly Higher Ahead of Jobs Report
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mostly Higher Ahead of Jobs ReportUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks finished mostly in the green on Thursday, recovering from earlier losses, as investors awaited August jobs report due tomorrow while a sharp drop in oil prices weighed on energy shares. The Dow Jones Industrial Average added 18.4 points, or 0.1%, to 18,419.3; and the Nasdaq Composite Index gained 13.99 points, or 0.3%, to 5,227.2. Meanwhile, the S&P 500 fell 0.1 point, or less than 0.1%, to 2170.9.2016-09-01T21:23:16
