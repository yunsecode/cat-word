Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsApplications for house mortgages in US fell 1.1 percent in the week ended December 11th, 2015, following a 1.2 percent rise in the previous week. Fixed 30-year mortgage rates were unchanged at 4.14 percent. Refinancing applications went up 1.4 percent while purchase applications fell 2.8 percent.2015-12-16T12:03:50
