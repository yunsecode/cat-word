Title: US Stocks Open in Record Territory
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open in Record TerritoryUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Thursday, with the three major stock indexes touching record highs after booking gains in the previous session. The technology sector was among the best performers after Facebook earnings beat expectations. The Dow Jones gained 67 points or 0.3% to 21778, the S&P 500 added 2 points or less than 0.1% to 2480 and the Nasdaq traded 22 points or 0.3% higher at 6445 around 7 minutes after the opening bell.
