Title: DXY Recovers from 9-Month Low
Tag: Currency

DXY Recovers from 9-Month LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rose to above 102 on Friday, recovering from a nine-month low of 100.82 in the previous session, after the latest jobs surprised on the upside complicating the Federal Reserve's quest to slow down the pace of tightening. The US economy added 517K jobs in January, the most since July and much above market expectations of 185K. Still, the greenback remains down more than 10% from its September peak as the Federal Reserve delivered a smaller 25 basis point rate hike in a widely expected move, while Fed Chair Jerome Powell said that the “disinflationary process has started.”.2023-02-03T13:37:00
