Title: US Futures Attempt to Rebound
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Attempt to ReboundUnited States Stock MarketStock futures in the US were nearly flat on Thursday, attempting to recover from earlier losses amid uncertainty about a new stimulus bill. House Speaker Nancy Pelosi and Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin failed to announce a deal on Wednesday and will speak again on Thursday, raising odds an agreement before the presidential election in less than 2 weeks is unlikely. Investors will also keep an eye on the final debate between Donald Trump and Joe Biden, amid news that Iran and Russia are attempting to interfere with the presidential election. On the corporate front, earnings from Coca-Cola beat forecasts while those from AT&T came in line with expectations. Tesla quarterly results released late on Wednesday also topped estimates. In contrast, those for Kimberly Clark came slightly below forecasts. Intel is due to report today as well.2020-10-22T11:07:00
