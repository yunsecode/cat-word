Title: US Mortgage Applications Rebound: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rebound: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 4.9 percent in the week ended April 13th 2018, following a 1.9 percent drop in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went up 3.5 percent and applications to purchase a home jumped 6.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was unchanged at 4.66 percent.2018-04-18T11:06:31.877
