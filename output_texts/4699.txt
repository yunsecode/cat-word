Tag: Currency

Dollar Steadies as Fed Announcement MulledUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index stabilized around 96.4 on Thursday, after a highly volatile session a day before as the Federal Reserve issued its policy statement. The Federal Reserve said it would end pandemic-era asset purchases in March and begin raising interest rates amid low unemployment and high inflation. Traders initially bought the dollar on prospects of higher interest rates, but the markets reversed after investors turned bullish on the economy and embraced risk assets. The greenback also remained vulnerable to the fast-spreading omicron variant, as a potential hit to the global economy could cloud the outlook and delay stimulus and rate lift off.2021-12-16T05:35:42.727
