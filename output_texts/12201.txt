Title: US Homebuilder Sentiment Falls From 11-Year High
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Homebuilder Sentiment Falls From 11-Year HighUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States fell to 68 in April 2017 from an eleven-year high of 71 in the previous month and below market consensus of 70. The index of current single-family home sales decreased 3 points to 74; sales expectations over the next six months went down 3 points to 75; and buyer traffic edged down 1 point to 52.2017-04-17T14:01:49.967
