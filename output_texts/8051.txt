Title: US Wages Fall More than Expected
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings for all employees on US private nonfarm payrolls fell by 35 cents, or 1.2 percent over the month to USD $29.37 in June of 2020, after a 1 percent decrease in May and compared with market expectations of a 0.7 percent drop. Year-on-year, average hourly earnings have increased by 5 percent, below a downwardly revised 6.6 percent in the prior month and market consensus of 5.3 percent. Average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees decreased by 23 cents to $24.74 in June. The decreases in average hourly earnings largely reflected job gains among lower-paid workers; these changes put downward pressure on the average hourly earnings estimates.2020-07-02T12:31:00
