US Home Builder Sentiment Below ForecastsUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States was unchanged at 62 in March 2019, the and below market consensus of 63. The index component that tracks views of current sales conditions rose to 68 from 66 in February and the gauge of home sales over the next six months advanced to 71 from 68.
