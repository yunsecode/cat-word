Title: US Services Sector Growth at 11-Month Low: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Sector Growth at 11-Month Low: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe final Markit US Services PMI for December of 2015 came in at 54.3, slightly up from a flash reading of 53.7 but lower than 56.1 in November. Both output and new business growth eased to the lowest since January, optimism moderated to the weakest level since July and input cost inflation eased. 2016-01-06T14:51:07
