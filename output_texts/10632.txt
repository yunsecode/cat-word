Title: US Exports Hit Record High
Tag: Exports

US Exports Hit Record HighUnited States ExportsExports of goods and services from the US rose 0.3 percent from the previous month to an all-time high of USD 211.2 billion in April 2018. Goods exports increased by USD 0.3 billion to USD 141.2 billion, as sales rose for: industrial supplies and materials (up USD 1.3 billion), mainly fuel oil and other petroleum products; and food, feeds, and beverages (up USD 0.7 billion), such as soybeans and corn. By contrast, exports of capital goods dropped USD 1.4 billion, due to lower sales of civilian aircraft. Exports of services went up USD 0.3 billion to USD 70.0 billion in April. On a non-seasonally adjusted basis, exports fell to China (-17.1 percent), Japan (-10.5 percent), the EU (-10.2 percent) and Canada (-5.3 percent), but rose to Mexico (2.4 percent).2018-06-06T12:32:00
