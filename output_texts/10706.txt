Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 4th Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 4th WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States dropped 2.7 percent in the week ended May 11th 2018, after declining 0.4 percent in the previous period. It marks the fourth consecutive week of falls in mortgage applications, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications slumped 3.8 percent, with the index reaching the lowest since August 2008 and applications to purchase a home fell 2.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged down by 1bps to 4.77 percent.2018-05-16T11:07:20.18
