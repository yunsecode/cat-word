Title: US Nonfarm Labour Productivity Revised Up in Q3
Tag: Productivity

US Nonfarm Labour Productivity Revised Up in Q3United States ProductivityNonfarm labor productivity increased at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 2.2 percent qoq in third quarter of 2015, higher than a preliminary estimate of 1.6 percent gain but slower than an upwardly revised 3.5 percent rise in the previous period, final estimates showed. Output increased more than expected by 1.8 percent (1.2 percent in the preliminary estimate) and hours worked decreased at a slower pace of 0.3 percent (-0.5 percent in the preliminary estimate), the first drop since the third quarter of 2009. Year-on-year, productivity went up 0.6 percent, compared to an initial estimate of 0.4 percent, reflecting increases in output and hours worked of 2.5 percent and 1.9 percent, respectively.2015-12-02T13:40:47.353
