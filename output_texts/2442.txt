Title: US Manufacturing Production Rebounds More than Expected
Tag: Manufacturing Production MoM

US Manufacturing Production Rebounds More than ExpectedUnited States Manufacturing Production MoMManufacturing production in the United States rose by 0.7 percent in July of 2022, rebounding from the downwardly revised 0.4 percent drop in the previous month and beating expectations of a 0.2 percent increase. Durable manufacturing rose by 1.3 percent, supported by a 6.6 percent gain for motor vehicles, while fabricated metals, aerospace, and miscellaneous transportation equipment all added more than 1 percent. Non-durable manufacturing edged 0.1 percent higher as gains for apparel and leather, chemicals, and plastics and rubber offset declines in other industries. Capacity utilization for manufacturing rose by 0.5 percentage points to 79.8 percent, 1.6 percentage points above the long-run average.2022-08-16T13:40:45.097
