Title: Wall Street Struggles to Maintain Momentum
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Struggles to Maintain MomentumUnited States Stock MarketAll three major US stock indexes opened Monday's session in the red, struggling to build on the momentum from last week's solid performance as investors refrained from placing big bets ahead of a busy week of economic data and earnings reports. With sentiment rattled by worries that an aggressive tightening from the Federal Reserve will drag the economy into a recession, investors will await essential inflation and consumer confidence data later this week for clues on the central bank's rate-hike path. Aside from the recession narrative, earnings from several US companies, particularly those from JPMorgan Chase, Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo, and Citigroup, due at the end of the week, will be a crucial driver for prices. Meanwhile, Twitter shares slumped about 10% after Elon Musk said he canceled his takeover deal because the social media company violated several merger agreement provisions.2022-07-11T13:39:00
