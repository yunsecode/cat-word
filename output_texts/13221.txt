Title: US Manufacturing Activity At 9-Month High
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing Activity At 9-Month HighUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe Markit Flash US Manufacturing PMI came in at 52.9 in July of 2016, up from 51.3 in June and much better than market expectations of 51.6. It is the highest reading since October as output and new business growth accelerated and payrolls rose the most in 12 months. 2016-07-22T14:54:07
