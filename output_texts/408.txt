US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Rise: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the US increased by 3.69 million barrels in the week that ended May 12th, 2023, following a 3.618 million barrel rise in the previous week and compared to market expectation of a 1.300 million barrel decline, data from the API's Weekly Statistical Bulletin showed.
