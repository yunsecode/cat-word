Title: S&P And Nasdaq Touch All-Time Highs
Tag: Stock Market

S&P And Nasdaq Touch All-Time HighsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened mostly in the green on Wednesday, with the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq touching record highs amid better-than-expected data for housing starts and building permits and mixed earnings reports. The S&P 500 went up 6 points or 0.3% to 2467 and the Nasdaq added 31 points or 0.5% to 6375 around 15 minutes after the opening bell. Meanwhile, the Dow Jones traded near the flatline.2017-07-19T13:47:23
