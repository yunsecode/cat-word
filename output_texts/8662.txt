Title: US Stock Futures Rebound
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures ReboundUnited States Stock MarketStock futures in the US were up on Tuesday and pointed to a rebound at the open after the biggest sell-off since 1987 the day before. S&P 500 futures gained more than 5% and reached their upper limit of trading. CNN reported that Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin asked GOP senators to support the House coronavirus relief bill and the next stimulus package the administration will launch. On Monday, stocks sold off again in another trade-halting session, dipping further into negative territory after President Trump warned the economic disruption from the virus could last until August and that the economy could fall into a recession while Fed moves failed to calm investors' concerns. The Dow Jones dropped 2997 points or 12.9% to 20189. The S&P 500 sank 325 points or 12% to 2386. The Nasdaq plummeted 970 points or 12.3% to 6905.2020-03-17T08:34:38.817
