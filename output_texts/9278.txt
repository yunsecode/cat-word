Title: US Stocks Close Higher Boosted by Earnings

US Stocks Close Higher Boosted by EarningsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Thursday 16 May 2019 despite ongoing trade concerns, buoyed by strong earnings results from Walmart and Cisco Systems.
