Title: US Stocks Close Lower on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Lower on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately lower on Tuesday 23 October 2018 partially recovering from a sharp decline early in the session after disappointing outlooks from Caterpillar and 3M added to concerns ranging from global growth to Saudi Arabia's diplomatic row. The Dow Jones lost 126 points or 0.5% to 25191. The S&P 500 retreated 15 points or 0.6% to 2741. The Nasdaq declined 31 points or 0.4% to 7438. 2018-10-23T20:08:38.277
