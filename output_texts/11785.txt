Title: US Jobless Claims Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 3 thousand to 244 thousand in the week ended August 5th from the previous week's revised level of 241 thousand and above market expectations of 240 thousand. Meanwhile, the 4-week moving average, which removes week-to-week volatility, fell by 1 thousand to 241 thousand. 2017-08-10T12:30:23.597
