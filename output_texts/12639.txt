Title: US Stocks Gain On Oil, Dollar
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Gain On Oil, DollarUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks rose on Friday amid a rally in oil prices and stabilization in the US dollar after hitting its highest level since 2002 the previous day. The Dow Jones gained 38 points, or 0.2%; the S&P 500 advanced 3 points, or 0.2%; and the Nasdaq added 4 points, or 0.1%, around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2016-12-16T14:46:23.827
