Title: US Initial Jobless Claims Set to Fall to New 1-Year Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Initial Jobless Claims Set to Fall to New 1-Year LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsInitial jobless claims are seen falling to 680 thousand in the March 27th week, lowest since the pandemic hit the labor market in March 2020. Claims are expected to fall in the coming weeks following the approval of President Biden's relief package in early March and as several states ease coronavirus-induced restrictions amid the rapid pace of vaccination. Meanwhile, continuing claims are seen declining to 3.775 million in the March 20th week from 3.870 million the week before, but remaining well above February 2020 levels.2021-04-01T09:11:14.647
