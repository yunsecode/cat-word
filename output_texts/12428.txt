Title: US Export Prices Rise 0.1% MoM In January
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise 0.1% MoM In JanuaryUnited States Export Prices MoMUS export prices edged up 0.1 percent on month in January 2017, after increasing by an upwardly revised 0.4 percent in December and in line with market expectations. Rising prices for nonagricultural exports (0.1 percent) offset falling agricultural prices (-0.1 percent). With the exception of a 0.8 percent drop in August 2016, export prices have trended up since April 2016. Compared with the same month of 2016, export prices rose by 2.3 percent, the largest advance since January 2012. 2017-02-10T13:37:15.57
