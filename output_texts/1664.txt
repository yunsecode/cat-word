Title: US Initial Jobless Claims Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Initial Jobless Claims Unexpectedly FallUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing new claims for unemployment benefits decreased by 16,000 to 225,000 on the week ending November 26th, compared to market expectations of 235,000. The 4-week moving average was 228,750, an increase of 1,750 from the previous week's revised average. On a seasonally unadjusted basis, initial claims fell by 50,512 to 198,557, with notable decreases in Montana (-318), North Carolina (-133), and Arkansas (-131). 2022-12-01T13:33:00
