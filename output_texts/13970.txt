Title: US Jobless Claims Rise the Most Since February
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise the Most Since FebruaryUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits went up by 16,000 to 276,000 in the week ended October 31st, from the previous week's unrevised level of 260,000. It was the largest increase in eight months bringing total number of claims to the highest level in five weeks. In spite of the rise, last week claims marked the 35th straight week below the 300,000 threshold and for most October, they remained near 42-year low.2015-11-05T13:32:05
