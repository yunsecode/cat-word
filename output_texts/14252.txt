Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 4-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 4-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's preliminary reading on the overall index of consumer sentiment came in at 91.2 in March, down from 95.4 in February and well below market expectations. The consumer sentiment fell for the second month as current and future expectations both declined.2015-03-13T14:19:39.273
