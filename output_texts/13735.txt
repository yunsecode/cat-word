Title: US Stocks Open Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open LowerUnited States Stock MarketMarkets in Wall Street were trading slightly lower on Thursday amid higher oil prices, after data showed planned layoffs and jobless claims increased while productivity shrank more than expected. The Dow Jones was down 17 points or 0.1%, the S&P 500 edged down 0.37% and the Nasdaq lost 0.56% few minutes after the opening. 2016-02-04T14:44:28
