Title: Foreigners Buy $80.4 Billion of US Assets in April
Tag: Capital Flows

Foreigners Buy $80.4 Billion of US Assets in AprilUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought $80.4 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments in April of 2016, after selling a downwardly revised $98.1 billion in March. Foreigners sold $79.6 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying $78.1 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors also sold $74.6 billion of US Treasuries, after buying $23.6 billion in March.2016-06-15T21:08:09
