Title: US Personal Income Beats Forecasts in October
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Beats Forecasts in OctoberUnited States Personal IncomePersonal income in the United States increased 0.4 percent or USD 65.1 billion month-over-month in October of 2017, the same as in September. The reading came better than market expectations of a 0.3 percent as wages and salaries and personal interest income grew faster. Disposable personal income (DPI) went up 0.5 percent or USD 66.1 billion. Real DPI increased 0.3 percent.2017-11-30T13:37:35
