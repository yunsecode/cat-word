Title: US Services Sector Growth At 3-Month Low
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Sector Growth At 3-Month LowUnited States Services PMIThe flash Markit Services PMI in the United States fell to 53.4 in December of 2016 from 54.6 in the previous month and below market expectations of 55.2. It is the lowest value in three months as new business growth eased and input cost inflation and prices charged picked up. In contrast, payrolls rose the most since March and optimism regarding the year-ahead business outlook increased. 2016-12-19T15:02:06.98
