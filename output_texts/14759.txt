U.S. Annual Inflation Down to 2.3% in AprilUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) was unchanged in April on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of  Labor Statistics reported today.
