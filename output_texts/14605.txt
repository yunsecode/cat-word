Title: US Nonfarm Payrolls Slightly Up to 203K in November
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Nonfarm Payrolls Slightly Up to 203K in NovemberUnited States Non Farm PayrollsTotal nonfarm payroll employment increased by 203,000 in November, slightly up from a revised 200,000 in October. Job growth averaged 195,000 per month over the prior 12 months. In November, job gains occurred in transportation and warehousing, health care, and manufacturing.2013-12-06T13:38:19.013
