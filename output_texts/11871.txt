Title: US Housing Starts Rebound in June
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Rebound in JuneUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States jumped 8.3 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1215 thousand in June 2017. This followed an upwardly revised 1122 thousand in the previous month and beat market expectations of a 5.8 percent rise. It is the strongest construction activity in four months as starts increased in the Northeast, the Midwest and the West.2017-07-19T12:31:54
