Title: Chicago PMI Remains Strong
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago PMI Remains StrongUnited States Chicago PmiThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer in the US fell to 61.1 in October of 2020 from 62.4 in September which was the highest since February of 2009. Still, figures pointed to a strong expansion in Chicago business activity, above the long-term average of 54.6 and market forecasts of 58. Production recorded the largest decline and new orders was the only category to show a monthly uptick, reaching the highest since November of 2018.2020-10-30T13:56:42.01
