Title: US Crude Inventories Above Estimates: API

US Crude Inventories Above Estimates: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the US rose by 3.618 million barrels in the week that ended May 5th, 2023, following a 3.939 million barrel decline in the previous week and well above market expectation of a 1.600 million barrel decrease, data from the API's Weekly Statistical Bulletin showed.
