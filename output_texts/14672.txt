Title: US Jobless Claims Rise to 360K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise to 360K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending July 6, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 360,000, an increase of 16,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 344,000. The 4-week moving average was 351,750, an increase of 6,000 from the previous week's revised average of 345,750.2013-07-11T13:36:35.703
