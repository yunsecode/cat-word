Title: Dollar Inches Up in Cautious Trading
Tag: Currency

Dollar Inches Up in Cautious TradingUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index inched up above 96.2 on Wednesday as a recent rally in riskier assets took a breather, while moves remained subdued amid a holiday-thinned trading. The greenback started to gain ground against major currencies on Tuesday including the euro, pound, kiwi and aussie, while it continued to strengthen versus the safe haven yen. Emerging market currencies also faced pressure from the dollar’s firmness, with sharp losses from the Philippine peso and Indonesian rupiah. Moreover, robust US economic data and persistent inflation provided additional support to the dollar, with the Fed-preferred PCE index also surging 5.7% in November, its highest level since 1982.
