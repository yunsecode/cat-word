Title: US Home Builder Confidence at 5-Month High
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Home Builder Confidence at 5-Month HighUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States came in at 60 in June of 2016, up from 58 in the previous four months. Figures came above market expectations of 59. The sales expectations subindex rose 5 points to 70; the index gauging buyer traffic went up 3 points to 47 and the component charting current sales conditions edged up 1 point to 64.2016-06-16T15:16:00.037
