Title: US 10-Year Bond Yield Jumps on Strong Labor Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Bond Yield Jumps on Strong Labor DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the 10-year US treasury note jumped to 2.8% after fresh jobs data surpassed market expectations in multiple gauges, strengthening bets that the Fed will continue its aggressive hiking momentum. 528 thousand non-farm payrolls were added to the American economy in July, more than double of expectations, while the unemployment rate dropped to 3.5% and wages grew by 0.5% on the month. Stronger labor market figures add to recent hawkish comments by Fed policymakers, which remain fully committed to taming surging inflation. 2022-08-05T12:46:00
