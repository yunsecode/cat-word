Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Rises after PPI, Claims Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Rises after PPI, Claims DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note, seen as a proxy for global borrowing costs, was above 3.8%, a level not seen in more than a month, as investors adjust their portfolios for a higher terminal rate. The closely watched US CPI reading for January landed at 6.4%, the lowest since October 2021, still above economists' forecast of 6.2%. Producer prices also came above forecasts while initial jobless claims surprised on the downside, which supports Fed's view that the labour market remains tight and inflation elevated, opening the door to further rate hikes. Money markets have now priced at least two more 25 basis point rate hikes this year and see interest rates peaking at 5.2% by July.2023-02-16T13:48:52.79
