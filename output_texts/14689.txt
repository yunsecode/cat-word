Title: US GDP Growth Revised Down to 2.4% in Q1
Tag: Gdp Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Revised Down to 2.4% in Q1United States Gdp Growth RateReal gross domestic product in the United States increased at an annual rate of 2.4 percent in the first quarter of 2013 (that is, from the fourth quarter to the first quarter), according to the "second" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. Drop in government spending and slower inventory building overshadowed the big increase in consumer spending. 2013-05-30T13:48:08.487
