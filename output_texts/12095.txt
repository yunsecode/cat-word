Title: Foreign Investors Sell $0.7 Billion Of US Assets In March
Tag: Capital Flows

Foreign Investors Sell $0.7 Billion Of US Assets In MarchUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors sold $0.7 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments, in March 2017 after buying a downwardly revised $13.2 billion in February. Meanwhile, foreigners bought $59.8 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying $53.1 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors bought $24.4 billion of US treasuries, after selling $13.5 billion in February.2017-05-15T20:03:05.913
