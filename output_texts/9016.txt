Title: US Stocks Mostly Muted
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Mostly MutedUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened close to the flat line on Thursday retreating from fresh highs reached in the previous session, amid mixed economic data and persistent concerns regarding trade negotiations. Producer prices increased the most in six months while weekly jobless claims rose more than expected and Walmart quarterly earnings beat forecasts. The Dow Jones traded near the flat line; while the S&P 500 fell 2 points or 0.1%, and the Nasdaq lost 18 points or 0.2% around 20 minutes after the opening bell.2019-11-14T14:51:00
