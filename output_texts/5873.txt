Title: New York State Manufacturing Growth Misses Forecasts
Tag: NY Empire State Manufacturing Index

New York State Manufacturing Growth Misses ForecastsUnited States NY Empire State Manufacturing IndexThe New York Empire State Manufacturing Index fell seven points to 17.4 in June of 2021, below market expectations of 23. New orders (16.3 vs 28.9) and shipments (14.2 vs 29.7) increased moderately, and there was a rise in unfilled orders (7.9 vs 21.4). Delivery times lengthened at a record-setting pace (29.8 vs 23.6), and inventories edged lower (-2.6 vs 7.1). Employment levels (12.3 vs 13.6) and the average workweek (15.1 vs 18.7) continued to grow modestly, and both input prices (79.8 vs 83.5) and selling prices (33.3 vs 37.1) continued to rise sharply. Looking ahead, firms remained optimistic that conditions would improve over the next six months, with the index for future employment reaching a record high.2021-06-15T12:36:00
