Title: US Labor Productivity Rises More than Expected in Q4
Tag: Productivity

US Labor Productivity Rises More than Expected in Q4United States ProductivityLabor productivity in the US non-farm business sector rose by an annualized 1.9 percent during the fourth quarter of 2018, beating market expectations of 1.6 percent and following a 1.8 percent advance in the previous three-month period. Output grew by 3.1 percent and hours worked by 1.2 percent. Year-on-year, productivity increased by 1.8 percent, reflecting a 3.7 percent gain in output and a 1.9 percent rise in hours worked. Considering 2018 full year, labor productivity rose by 1.3 percent, the most since 2010.2019-03-07T13:34:00
