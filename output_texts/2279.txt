Title: US Capital Inflow Widens in July
Tag: Capital Flows

US Capital Inflow Widens in JulyUnited States Capital FlowsThe United States recorded a capital and financial account surplus of USD 153.5 billion in July of 2022, widening from the upwardly revised USD 22.3 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors acquired a net USD 23.1 billion in Treasuries in the period, easing from the USD 58.9 billion inflow in the previous month. Meanwhile, foreigners bought USD 21.4 billion of long-term US securities.
