Title: U.S. Trade Deficit Narrows in May
Tag: Balance of Trade

U.S. Trade Deficit Narrows in MayUnited States Balance of TradeU.S. May exports of $183.1 billion and imports of $231.8 billion resulted in a goods and services deficit of $48.7 billion, down from $50.6 billion in April, revised. May exports were $0.4 billion more than April exports of $182.7 billion. May imports were $1.6 billion less than April imports of $233.3 billion.2012-07-11T17:10:19.87
