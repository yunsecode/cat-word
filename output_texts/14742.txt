Title: U.S. Annual Inflation Up to 1.7% in August
Tag: Inflation Rate

U.S. Annual Inflation Up to 1.7% in AugustUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased  0.6 percent in August on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau  of Labor Statistics reported on September 14th. Over the last 12 months, the all  items index increased 1.7 percent before seasonal adjustment.  2012-09-14T13:33:30.543
