Title: Dollar Becomes Less Attractive
Tag: Currency

Dollar Becomes Less AttractiveUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar continued to fall on Monday after the Federal Reserve cut the target range for its federal funds rate by 100 bps to 0-0.25% during an emergency move the day before. The measure aims to protect the economy from the impact of the coronavirus and follows another emergency 50bps slash on March 3rd. However, the cuts were not able to calm investors concerns about the outbreak, prompting a rush for safe haven and making the dollar less attractive as rates are now very low. The DXY was down near 0.7% to 98.06 around 10:00 AM NY time.2020-03-16T14:07:00
