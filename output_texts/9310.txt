Title: US Stocks Resume Sell-Off
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Resume Sell-OffUnited States Stock MarketUS stock markets traded deep in the red on Thursday after President Donald Trump said Beijing "broke the deal" on trade and confirmed plans to hike tariffs on Chinese imports on Friday. The Dow Jones plunged 330 points, or 1.3%, to 25,637; the S&P 500 lost 37 points, or 1.2%, to 2,844; and the Nasdaq slumped 129 points, or 1.6%, to 7,815 around 10:30 AM NY time.2019-05-09T14:23:25.627
