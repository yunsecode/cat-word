Title: US Consumer Credit Grows Above Expectations
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Grows Above ExpectationsUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States went up by USD 25.84 billion in March of 2021, following a revised USD 26.13 billion gain in the previous month and comfortably beating market expectations of a USD 20 billion rise. Revolving credit increased by USD 6.4 billion while non-revolving credit went up by USD 19.5 billion.
