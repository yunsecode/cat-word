Title: US Factory Activity at 22-Month Low
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Factory Activity at 22-Month LowUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe final Markit Manufacturing PMI in the United States came in at 53 in August, slightly better than a preliminary reading of 52.9 but down from 53.80 in July of 2015. It is the lowest figure since October of 2013 as output, new orders and job creation all slowed.2015-09-01T14:54:15.657
