Title: US Services PMI Revised Higher In June: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services PMI Revised Higher In June: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services PMI increased to 54.2 in June of 2017 from a preliminary of 53 and 53.6 in May. It is the highest reading in five months amid increased new orders and strong client demand. Employment remained strong while input prices rose the most in two years. The degree of optimism among firms in the US services sector increased, with confidence hitting the second-strongest level since May 2015. 2017-07-06T13:56:32
