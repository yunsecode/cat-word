Title: US Crude Inventories Rise Less than Expected: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Rise Less than Expected: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories went up by 0.692 million barrels to 414.4 million barrels in the week ended April 22nd, recovering from an 8.02 million drop in the previous period and less than market expectations of a 2 million rise. Crude stocks at the Cushing, Oklahoma, increased by 1.298 million barrels. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks fell by 1.573 million barrels to 230.8 million barrels, versus expectations for a 0.808 million increase; and distillate stockpiles which include diesel and heating oil declined by 1.449 million barrels to 107.3 million barrels, more than expectations for a 0.292 million barrel fall.2022-04-27T14:36:00
