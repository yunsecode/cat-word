Title: US Stocks Fall on Tariffs
Tag: stock market

US Stocks Fall on TariffsUnited States stock marketWall Street opened sharply lower on Monday, following losses in Europe and in Asia as US-China trade war concerns persist. On Friday, President Trump announced tariffs on $50 billion imports from China. China retaliated with tariffs on $50 billion of US imports. The Dow Jones was down 1%, the S&P 500 declined 0.8% and the Nasdaq traded 0.9% lower around five minutes after the opening bell.
