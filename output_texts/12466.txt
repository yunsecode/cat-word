Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Much More Than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Much More Than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 6.466 million barrels in the week ended January 27th, 2017, following a 2.84 million increase in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 3.289 million rise. It is the biggest jump in 13 weeks. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks increased by 3.866 million, compared to expectations of a 0.982 million rise.
