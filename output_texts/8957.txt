Title: US Stocks Fall on Weak Economic Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on Weak Economic DataUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Thursday after Labor Department data showed that initial weekly jobs claim rose to the highest level since 2017. The data comes after the Federal Reserve held interest rates steady on Wednesday while investors await to see whether a new round of tariffs on Chinese goods takes effect next week. The Dow Jones lost 36 points, or 0.2%; the S&P 500 retreated 3 points, or 0.1%; and the NASDAQ fell 18 points, or 0.2%, around 5 minutes after the opening bell.2019-12-12T14:35:00
