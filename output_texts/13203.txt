Title: US Jobless Claims at 4-Week High of 266K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims at 4-Week High of 266KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 14,000 to 266,000 in the week ended July 23rd compared with the previous week's downwardly revised level of 252,000. The figure came above market expectations of 260,000, the highest in four weeks. However, the four-week moving average fell to 256,500, the lowest since April. 2016-07-28T13:36:50
