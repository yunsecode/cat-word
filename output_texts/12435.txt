Title: US Jobless Claims Drop To Nearly 43-Year Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Drop To Nearly 43-Year LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 12 thousand to 234 thousand in the week ended February 4th 2017, below market expectations of 250 thousand. It is the smallest figure since the week ended November 12th 2016 when claims came in at 233,000, the lowest in 43 years. Claims have been below 300 thousand, the level associated with a healthy labor market for 101 straight weeks. That’s the longest streak since 1970. Yet, the 4-week moving average considered a better measure as removes week-to-week volatility reached the lowest since 1973. 2017-02-09T13:32:35
