Title: Dollar Eases Ahead of Fed Minutes
Tag: Currency

Dollar Eases Ahead of Fed MinutesUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index slipped to around 104 on Wednesday, easing from a two-week high of 104.86 reached in the previous session as investors cautiously awaited the Federal Reserve’s December meeting minutes, which could provide clues on its policy tightening path. Investors are also looking ahead to a raft of US economic data this week, headlined by the December jobs report on Friday. Recession fears in the US gripped markets at the start of the year, with former New York Fed President William Dudley saying that a US recession is on the cards but will not likely be severe. On top of that, former Fed Chair Alan Greenspan said a recession is the “most likely outcome” for the world’s largest economy. The US central bank hiked its federal-funds rate by a cumulative 4.25% in 2022, bringing borrowing costs to the highest since 2007. It also signaled further tightening in an ongoing effort to bring down inflation.2023-01-04T04:18:00
