Title: US Stocks Rise As Fed Meeting Begins
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise As Fed Meeting BeginsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks gained on Tuesday, as the Fed began a two-day monetary policy meeting, where the first interest rate hike of 2016 is expected to be announced. The Dow Jones rose 77 points, or 0.4%; the S&P 500 gained 10 points, or 0.4%; and the Nasdaq Composite added 29 points, or 0.5%, around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2016-12-13T14:41:28.033
