Title: US Energy Inflation at Over 1-1/2-Year Low
Tag: Energy Inflation

US Energy Inflation at Over 1-1/2-Year LowUnited States Energy InflationCost of energy in the US increased at a slower 17.6% year-on-year in October of 2022, easing from a 19.8% rise in the previous month. It was the lowest reading since March of 2021, amid a further slowdown in prices of gasoline (17.5% from 18.2% in September), electricity (14.1% vs 15.5%) and natural gas (20% vs 33.1%). Meanwhile, costs continued to soar for fuel oil (68.5% vs 58.1%).2022-11-10T13:47:00
