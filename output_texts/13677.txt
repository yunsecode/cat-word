Title: US Service Sector Shrinks in February
Tag: Services PMI

US Service Sector Shrinks in FebruaryUnited States Services PMIThe Markit flash US Services PMI came in at 49.8 in February of 2016, down from 53.2 in January and lower than market expectations of 53.5. It is the first decline in services sector business activity since October of 2013 due to a snow disruption in East coast and lower new work while job growth was sustained. Yet, business outlook fell to its lowest since August of 2010. 2016-02-24T14:53:15.85
