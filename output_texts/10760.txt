Title: US Unit Labor Costs Rise Less than Expected in Q1
Tag: Labour Costs

US Unit Labor Costs Rise Less than Expected in Q1United States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the US non-farm business sector rose an annualized 2.7 percent in the first quarter of 2018, following a downwardly revised 2.1 percent increase in the previous three-month period and missing market expectations of 2.9 percent. Hourly compensation advanced 3.4 percent and productivity rose at a softer 0.7 percent. Year-on-year, unit labor costs increased 1.1 percent.2018-05-03T12:33:19
