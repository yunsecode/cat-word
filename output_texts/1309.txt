Title: United States Chicago PMI Drops in January
Tag: Chicago PMI

United States Chicago PMI Drops in JanuaryUnited States Chicago PMIThe Chicago PMI in the United States fell back to 44.3 points in January of 2023 from 44.9 in December and compared to market forecasts of 45. The reading pointed to a fifth consecutive month of contraction in business activity in the Chicago region. The Employment subindex ticked down by -0.6 points to a four-month low of
 42.0 in January as one-quarter of firms reported lower employment levels. Labor shortages continue to hamper business activity and were reported by some firms as making up the greatest bottleneck to production.2023-01-31T14:50:00
