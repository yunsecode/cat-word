Title: US Manufacturing Growth Slows to Decade Low: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing Growth Slows to Decade Low: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI dropped to 50.6 in May 2019 from the previous month's 52.6 and below market expectations of 52.5, a preliminary estimate showed. The latest reading pointed to the weakest pace of expansion in the manufacturing sector since September 2009, as new orders fell for the first time since August 2009 while output, employment and pre-production inventories all rose at softer rates. 2019-05-23T13:47:00
