Title: Initial Claims Match Forecasts at 230K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

Initial Claims Match Forecasts at 230KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing new claims for unemployment benefits edged higher by 4K to 230K in the week ending December 3rd, matching market expectations. The previous week's level was revised up by 1K from 225K to 226K. The 4-week moving average which removes week-to-week volatility was 230K, the highest level since the last week of August. Non-seasonally adjusted data showed claims rose the most in California (16.057K), amid a big tech layoff, followed by Texas (7.9K) and Georgia (7.57K). Initial claims averaged 224K so far in the fourth quarter, a level consistent with tight labour market. Meanwhile, continuing claims rose for an eighth straight week to 1.671 million in the last week of November, the highest since the week ending February 5th.2022-12-08T13:32:00
