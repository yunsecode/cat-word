Title: US Corporate Layoffs Rise in September
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Corporate Layoffs Rise in SeptemberUnited States Challenger Job CutsThe number of announced corporate layoffs in the United States increased 38 percent month over month to 44,324 in September of 2016. The rise was led by the education sector, where job cuts increased by 363 percent to 8,671. The bulk came from the collapse of for-profit college ITT Technical Institute, which led to 8,000 job losses. The computer industry continued to see heavy job cutting, announcing another 4,152 job cuts and the retail sector announced 7,296 job cuts. Considering the third quarter of the year, employers announced 121,858 job cuts, down 8 percent from 132,834 in the second quarter and 41 percent lower than the 205,759 job cuts announced a year earlier.2016-10-06T11:39:03.9
