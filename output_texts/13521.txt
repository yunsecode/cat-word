Title: Wall Street Opens Lower
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens LowerUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened lower on Monday pushed down by a decline in crude oil prices after major oil producers failed to agree on production freeze during the meeting held on Sunday. The Dow Jones was down 0.2% or 35.54 points, the S&P 500 fell by 0.06% or 1.22 points and the Nasdaq Composite slid 0.27% or 13.31 points, around twenty minutes after the opening bell.2016-04-18T15:15:20
