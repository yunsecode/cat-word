Title: US Budget Gap Narrows 30% in July
Tag: Government Budget Value

US Budget Gap Narrows 30% in JulyUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US government budget deficit narrowed by 30% to USD 211 billion in July of 2022 from USD 302 billion in the corresponding month of the previous year, compared to market expectations of a USD 194 billion gap. Receipts grew by 3 percent to USD 269 billion, while outlays fell by 15% to USD 480 billion mainly due to COVID-19 relief spending. The US federal deficit was at USD 726 billion in the ten months of the 2022 fiscal year, 71% lower than the USD 2,54 billion from the corresponding period of 2021.2022-08-10T18:04:00
