Title: U.S. Trade Deficit Narrows in February
Tag: Balance Of Trade

U.S. Trade Deficit Narrows in FebruaryUnited States Balance Of TradeThe U.S. Census Bureau and the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis, through the Department of Commerce, announced that total February exports of $186.0 billion and imports of $228.9 billion resulted in a goods and services deficit of $43.0 billion, down from $44.5 billion in January, revised. February exports were $1.6 billion more than January exports of $184.4 billion. February imports were $0.1 billion more than January imports of $228.9 billion.2013-04-05T13:47:11.097
