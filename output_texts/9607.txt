Title: US Stocks Rise after March Deadline Extended
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise after March Deadline ExtendedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened sharply higher on Monday after President Trump tweeted on Sunday that the US has made substantial progress in trade talks with China, following extended trade talks during the weekend, and the March 1st deadline to increase tariffs on Chinese imports will be delayed. The Dow Jones jumped 0.6%, the S&P 500 went up 0.6% and the Nasdaq traded 0.9% higher around 8 minutes after the opening bell.
