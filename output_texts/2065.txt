Title: US Natural Gas Stocks Rise More than Expected: EIA
Tag: Natural Gas Stocks Change

US Natural Gas Stocks Rise More than Expected: EIAUnited States Natural Gas Stocks ChangeUS utilities added 111 billion cubic feet (bcf) of gas to storage during the week ended October 14th, above market expectations of a 105 bcf build due to mild weather and an increase in wind power. It compares with an increase of 91 in the same week last year and a five-year (2017-2021) average increase of 73 bcf.2022-10-20T14:32:20.94
