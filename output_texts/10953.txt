Title: US House Prices Rise 0.8% in January: FHFA
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US House Prices Rise 0.8% in January: FHFAUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States increased 0.8 percent month-over-month in January of 2018, following an upwardly revised 0.4 percent gain in December and above market expectations of 0.5 percent. Year-on-year, house prices went up 7.3 percent.2018-03-22T13:03:09.317
