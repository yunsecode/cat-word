Title: US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI Falls to 20-Month Low
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI Falls to 20-Month LowUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index registered 55.3 percent in December of 2015, down from 55.9 percent in November and below market forecasts. It is the lowest reading since April of 2014 mainly due to a drop in prices, supplier deliveries and inventories.2016-01-06T15:09:15
