Title: US Jobless Rate Unexpectedly Rises in January
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Unexpectedly Rises in JanuaryUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate rose to 3.6 percent in January 2020 from the previous month's 50-year low and above market expectations of 3.5 percent. The number of unemployed people increased by 139 thousand to 5.89 million while employment fell by 89 thousand to 158.71 million. The labor force participation rate rose 0.2 p.p. to 63.4 percent.2020-02-07T13:31:00
