Title: US Trade Gap Below Forecasts
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Gap Below ForecastsUnited States Balance of TradeThe US trade deficit narrowed to $70 billion in July of 2021, slightly below market forecasts of a $71 billion gap, mainly due to a drop in the goods deficit to $87.7 billion and a decrease in the services surplus to $17.7 billion. Exports were up 1.3% mom to $212.9 billion, the highest since May of 2019, led by sales of capital goods, gem diamonds, autos and parts, trucks, buses, and special purpose vehicles, other business services and charges for intellectual property products. Imports edged down 0.2% mom to $282.9 billion, after touching a record high of $283.3 billion in June, led by falls in purchases of toys, games, and sporting goods, cell phones, nonmonetary gold and lumber. The deficit with China decreased $2.0 billion to $25 billion in July and the gap with Canada fell $1.9 billion to $3.6 billion while the shortfall with Mexico widened by $1.3 billion to $8.5 billion.2021-09-02T12:34:00
