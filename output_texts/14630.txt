U.S. Unemployment Rate Down to 7.2% in SeptemberUnited States Unemployment RateU.S. unemployment rate was little changed at 7.2 percent in September and total nonfarm payroll employment rose by 148,000, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on October 22nd.
