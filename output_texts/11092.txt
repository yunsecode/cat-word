Title: US Home Builder Sentiment Matches Forecasts
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Home Builder Sentiment Matches ForecastsUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States was unchanged at 72 in February of 2018, the same as in January and in line with market expectations. The component gauging current home sales edged down to 78 from 79 and the component measuring buyer traffic was flat at 54. On the other hand, the index charting sales expectations in the next six months went up 2 points to 80.2018-02-15T15:05:21.147
