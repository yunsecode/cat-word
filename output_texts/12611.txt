Title: US Stocks Rise On Friday, Gain On The Week
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise On Friday, Gain On The Week United States Stock MarketWall Street closed slightly higher on Friday led by health-care, consumer and financial shares. The Dow Jones added 15 points or less than 0.1%, failing to top the 20,000 mark; the S&P 500 went up 3 points or 0.1% and the Nasdaq closed 15 points or 0.3% higher. On the week, the Dow Jones added 0.5%, the seventh straight week of gains; the S&P 500 increased 0.3% and the Nasdaq gained 0.5%. 2016-12-23T21:31:55.973
