Title: Factory Activity in New York State Fell Sharply
Tag: NY Empire State Manufacturing Index

Factory Activity in New York State Fell SharplyUnited States NY Empire State Manufacturing IndexThe New York Fed's Empire State manufacturing index registered minus 14.7 this month after a minus 14.9 in August pointing to the contraction in factory activity for the second consecutive month. New orders came in at -12.9 and shipments registered -8.0. In addition, a measure of employment fell to -6.2 in September, first decline in more than two years.
