Title: New York Business Activity Rebounds
Tag: Ism New York Index

New York Business Activity ReboundsUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions Index increased to 65.8 in October from 44.5 in September of 2015, reaching the highest in three months while the Six-Month Outlook increased to a four-month high of 74.0. Relief was recorded in questions related to the New York metro area, but not in questions related to company-specific activities. Job growth and purchase volume both contracted back to back for the first time in three years.2015-11-03T14:54:57.767
