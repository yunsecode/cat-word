Title: US Stocks Sink
Tag: stock market

US Stocks Sink United States stock marketWall Street plunged at the open on Wednesday as trade war concerns escalated after China said would target 106 US products with a 25% tariff in response to President Trumps' list of Chinese imports to be targeted. The Dow Jones slumped 445 points or 1.9%, the S&P 500 fell 36 points or 1.4% and the Nasdaq declined 100 points or 1.4% around 3 minutes after the opening bell.2018-04-04T13:30:27
