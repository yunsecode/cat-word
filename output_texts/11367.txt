Title: US Stocks Edge Higher on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Higher on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed modestly in the green on Monday 11 December 2017, as the Dow Jones and S&P 500 booked new records ahead of a series of central bank policy meetings this week. Technology hardware and media shares led gains on Monday, with the Dow Jones gaining 57 points or 0.2% to 24386; the S&P 500 increasing 6 points or 0.2% to 2658; and the Nasdaq advancing 35 points or 0.5% to 6875.2017-12-11T21:04:29
