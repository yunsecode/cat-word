Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Rise for 2nd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Rise for 2nd Week United States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 4.74 million barrels in the week ended May 17th 2019, following a 5.431 million rise in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 0.599 million drop. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories rose by 3.716 million, after a 1.123 million decline in the prior week and against market consensus of a 0.816 million decrease. 2019-05-22T14:32:00
