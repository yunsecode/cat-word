Title: Dollar Falls after Midterm Elections
Tag: Currency

Dollar Falls after Midterm ElectionsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index declined on Wednesday as outcome of the US midterm elections, with Democrats gaining control of the House and Republicans increasing Senate majority, lowered expectations of further economic stimulus such as tax cuts. The DXY was down 0.5% to 95.83 around 9:15 AM London time, the lowest in near 3 weeks.2018-11-07T09:16:00
