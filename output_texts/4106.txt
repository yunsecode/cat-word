Title: US Labour Productivity Unrevised in Q4
Tag: Productivity

US Labour Productivity Unrevised in Q4United States ProductivityNonfarm labor productivity in the US rose an annualized 6.6 percent in the fourth quarter of 2021, matching an earlier estimate and following a downwardly revised 3.9 percent decline in the previous period. Figures came slightly below market expectations of a 6.7 percent gain. It was the largest increase in productivity since the second quarter of 2020, as output increased 9.1 percent (vs 2 percent in Q3) and hours worked increased 2.4 percent (vs 6.2 percent). Year-on-year, nonfarm business sector labor productivity advanced 1 percent, compared with a preliminary reading of 2 percent.
