Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Falls on Debt Ceiling Uncertainty
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Falls on Debt Ceiling Uncertainty United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note fell by around 5 basis points to 3.64% on Monday, reflecting increasing uncertainty surrounding the debt ceiling impasse in the US. President Biden is scheduled to meet with House Speaker Kevin McCarthy today in an attempt to reach a deal, following a failed meeting between top debt ceiling negotiators on Friday. At the same time, Treasury Secretary Janet Yellen said on Sunday that the likelihood of the Treasury being able to pay all US bills by June 15th is quite low. Additionally, traders are closely watching for any indications of the Federal Reserve's future actions. On Friday, Fed Chair Powell said that because of stress in the banking sector, it might be unnecessary to further raise rates to curb inflation. As such, the probability of a pause in the rate hike cycle has increased, with traders currently assigning an 86% chance that the Fed will maintain steady rates in June.2023-05-22T08:07:00
