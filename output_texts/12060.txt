Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall For 7th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall For 7th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 4.432 million barrels in the week ended May 19th, 2017, following a 1.753 million decrease in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 2.42 million decline. It is the seventh consecutive week of falls. Gasoline stocks went down by 787 thousand, compared to expectations of 1194 thousand decrease.
