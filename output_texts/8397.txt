Title: US Mortgage Applications Rebound in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rebound in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 0.1 percent in the week ended May 1st 2020, rebounding from a 3.3 percent fall in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home increased 5.8 percent while refinance applications dropped 1.7 percent. Meantime, the average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 3bps to 3.40 percent, its lowest level on record.2020-05-06T11:02:00
