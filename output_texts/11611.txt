Title: US Construction Spending Rises More than Expected
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Rises More than Expected United States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the US rose 0.5 percent month-on-month to USD 1.22 trillion in August 2017, following an upwardly revised 1.2 percent drop in July and beating market expectations of a 0.4 percent gain. Investment in public projects rebounded 0.7 percent after slumping 3.3 percent in July, as outlays on educational projects grew 3.5 percent while highways construction spending fell 1.3 percent. Also, private construction spending rose 0.4 percent, recovering from a 0.5 percent drop in July, as spending on private nonresidential structures went up 0.5 percent and outlays on residential buildings increased 0.4 percent.2017-10-02T14:09:25
