Title: US Services Sector Growth at 3-Month High
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Sector Growth at 3-Month HighUnited States Services PMIThe final Markit US Services PMI came in at 56.1 in November of 2015, lower than a preliminary estimate of 56.5 but up from 54.8 in October, the highest in three months. Business activity and new orders increased at a faster pace, job growth rebounded from October’s eight-month low and cost pressures remained muted but prices charged went up.2015-12-03T14:51:46
