Title: US Mortgage Applications Rebound In Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rebound In Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 2.7 percent in the week ended April 21st 2017, following a 1.8 percent fall in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications jumped 7.2 percent while applications to purchase a home went down 1 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates fell by 2bps to 4.2 percent. It is the lowest rate since the week ended November 18th 2016.2017-04-26T11:14:27.907
