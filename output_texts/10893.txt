Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Fall the Most since January
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Fall the Most since JanuaryUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 4.617 million barrels in the week ended March 30th 2018, after a 1.643 million rise in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 1.4 million increase. It is the biggest decline in 11 weeks. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories fell by 1.116 million barrels after decreasing by 3.472 million in the previous week, while markets were expecting a higher 1.5 million drop.2018-04-04T15:17:14
