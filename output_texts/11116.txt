US Stocks Extend Recover United States Stock MarketWall Street closed deep in the green on Monday 12 February 2018, bouncing back from the worst weekly selloff in two years as investors ignored another rise in Treasury yields and turned their attention to higher oil prices and President Trump's infrastructure plan. The Dow Jones gained 410 points or 1.7% to 24601. The S&P 500 advanced 36 points or 1.4% to 2656. The Nasdaq climbed 107 points or 1.6% to 6982. 2018-02-12T21:08:49
