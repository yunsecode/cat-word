Title: US Stocks Fall on Lower Oil Prices
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on Lower Oil PricesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Monday amid a fall in oil prices while investors await Fed’s Yellen speech on Friday for further clues on the timing of the next rate-hike. The Dow Jones declined 74 points or 0.4%, the S&P 500 lost 7 points or 0.3% and the Nasdaq traded 7 points or 0.1% lower around 11 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-08-22T14:49:47.663
