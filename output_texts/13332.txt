Title: US Building Permits Rise 0.7% in May
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Rise 0.7% in MayUnited States Building PermitsBuilding permits in the United States increased 0.7 percent to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1138 thousand in May of 2016, following 1130 thousand in the previous month. The reading missed market consensus of 1150 thousand, as single-family authorizations fell 2 percent to 726 thousand while multi-family building permits increased 6.7 percent to 381 thousand. 2016-06-17T13:41:30.667
