Title: US Manufacturing PMI Revised Lower: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing PMI Revised Lower: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI was revised lower to 51.3 in October 2019 from a preliminary estimate of 51.5 and compared to the previous month's 51.1. The latest reading remained consistent with a modest improvement in the health of the manufacturing sector, as output and new order growth rates accelerated to six-month highs and employment rose at the quickest pace since May.2019-11-01T13:51:00
