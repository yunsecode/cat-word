Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 4.9 million barrels in the week ended October 7th, 2016, compared to market expectations of a 1.75 million gain. It was the first increase in over a month, following a 3 million drop in the previous week. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks fell by 1.9 million barrels while markets were expecting a 1.4 million decline.2016-10-13T15:07:06.757
