Title: US Mortgage Applications Drop for First Time in 1 Month
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Drop for First Time in 1 MonthUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 3.1 percent in the week ending November 24th 2017, after rising slightly by 0.1 percent in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications dropped 7.7 percent while applications to purchase a home rose 1.8 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was unchanged at 4.20 percent.2017-11-29T12:12:15.467
