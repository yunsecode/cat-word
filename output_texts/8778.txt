Title: Wall Street Bounces Back after Monday’s Sharp Drop
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Bounces Back after Monday’s Sharp DropUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks bounced back on Tuesday, after recording their worst losses in two years in the previous session, with investors assessing the impact of the coronavirus outbreak in the global economy. Risk appetite showed signs of returning to markets on expectations that the US economy remained resilient despite the impact the new epidemic is having on other advanced economies. The Dow Jones rose 0.2%; the Nasdaq went up 0.2%, and the S&P 500 added 0.1% around 20 minutes after the opening bell.2020-02-25T14:51:00
