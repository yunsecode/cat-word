Title: US Inflation Rate Unchanged at Over 6-Year High
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Unchanged at Over 6-Year HighUnited States Inflation RateUS annual inflation rate stood at 2.9 percent in July 2018, unchanged from the previous month and slightly below market expectations of 3 percent. Still, inflation remained at its highest level since February 2012. Core inflation, which excludes food and energy, rose to 2.4 percent in July from 2.3 percent in June, hitting the highest rate since September 2008.2018-08-10T12:32:00
