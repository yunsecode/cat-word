Title: US Construction Spending Drops 0.5% in February
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Drops 0.5% in February United States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the United States decreased 0.50 percent month-on-month in February of 2016,  compared to an upwardly revised 2.1 percent gain in the previous month and below market estimates of 0.1 percent rise. It was the weakest reading since November, as spending on public construction fell 1.7 percent, dragged down by educational and highway construction. Private construction spending declined at a slower 0.1 percent, due to the fall in nonresidential construction (-1.3 percent). In contrast, residential construction went up 0.9 percent, the biggest gain since October 2007. 2016-04-01T15:13:17
