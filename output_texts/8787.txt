Title: US Output Contracts for 1st Time Since 2013
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Output Contracts for 1st Time Since 2013United States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI fell to 49.6 in February 2020 from 53.3 in the previous month, pointing to the first month of contraction in the private sector since October 2013, a preliminary estimate showed. Services activity dropped for the first time in four years, while manufacturing growth slowed to a six-month low. Overall new orders decreased for the first time since the series began in 2009 and employment growth slowed to a four-month low. Still, business confidence strengthened to an eight-month high, but remained historically subdued as firms highlighted ongoing global uncertainty and the outbreak of coronavirus.2020-02-21T14:55:00
