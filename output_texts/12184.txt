Title: US Service Activity Growth Below Expectations: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Service Activity Growth Below Expectations: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe seasonally adjusted Markit Flash U.S. Services PMI Business Activity Index fell to 52.5 in April 2017 from 52.8 in March and below market expectations of 53. The reading pointed to the weakest expansion across the service sector since September last year, as new business growth remained moderate and backlogs of work declined for the third consecutive month. Also, employment growth slowed to the weakest level since July 2010. Still, service providers remained upbeat about their prospects for growth over the next 12 months.2017-04-21T13:49:54
