Title: US Export Prices Rise More Than Expected In February
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise More Than Expected In FebruaryUnited States Export Prices MoMUS export prices went up 0.3 percent on the month in February 2017, after increasing by an upwardly revised 0.2 percent in January and above market expectations of 0.2 percent. Agricultural exports prices jumped 1.4 percent (0.1 percent in January), driven by a 24.3 percent rise in vegetable prices, as well as higher cost of soybeans and corn. Non-agricultural exports cost increased 0.3 percent (0.2 percent in January), led by rising prices for capital goods and industrial supplies and materials, which more than offset lower consumer goods prices. Compared with the same month of 2016, export prices increased by 3.1 percent, the largest rise since December 2011.2017-03-09T13:35:33
