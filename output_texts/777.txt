Title: US Food Inflation Hits Over 1-Year Low
Tag: Food Inflation

US Food Inflation Hits Over 1-Year LowUnited States Food InflationFood prices in the United States increased at a slower 8.5% from a year earlier in March 2023, decelerating from a 9.5% rise in February and a peak of 11.4% in last August. It was the lowest reading since February of 2022, as prices continued to slow down for food at home (8.4% vs 10.2% in February), while accelerating further for food away from home (8.8% vs 8.4%).2023-04-12T12:42:00
