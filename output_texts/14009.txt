Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsApplications for U.S. house mortgages surged 11.8 percent in the week ended October 16th, recovering from a 27.6 percent drop in the previous period, as fixed 30-year mortgage rates averaged 3.95 percent, the lowest in 5-1/2 months. Refinancing applications went up 8.8 percent and purchase applications rose 16.4 percent.2015-10-21T12:08:54
