Title: US Budget Surplus Widens in September
Tag: Government Budget Value

US Budget Surplus Widens in SeptemberUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US government budget surplus increased to USD 119 billion in September 2018 from USD 8 billion in the same month of the previous year, above market expectations of USD 107.5 billion. Outlays dropped 34.1 percent to USD 224 billion while receipts decreased at a softer 1.5 percent to USD 344 billion.2018-10-15T18:07:00
