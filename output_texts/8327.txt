Title: US Labor Productivity Falls Less than Forecasts
Tag: Productivity

US Labor Productivity Falls Less than ForecastsUnited States ProductivityLabor productivity in the US non-farm business sector decreased by an annualized 2.5 percent during the first quarter of 2020, after a 1.2 percent increase in the previous three-month period and compared to market expectations of a 5.5 percent decline, a preliminary estimate showed. It was the biggest fall in labor productivity since the last quarter of 2015. Output fell 6.2 percent, down from a 2.4 percent rise in Q4; and hours worked dropped 3.8 percent, reversing a 1.2 percent gain. Manufacturing sector labor productivity slumped 3.3 percent, as output contracted 7.1 percent and hours worked were down 3.9 percent.2020-05-07T12:32:00
