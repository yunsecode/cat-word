Title: US Consumer Sentiment Beats Forecasts
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Beats ForecastsUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US increased to 97.8 in March of 2019 from 93.8 in February, beating market expectations of 95.3, preliminary estimates showed. It is the highest reading in three months, amid rising income and lower inflation expectations and more positive growth prospects. 2019-03-15T14:09:37.38
