Title: US Jobless Claims Fall to Lowest Level in 5 Weeks
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall to Lowest Level in 5 WeeksUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 1,000 to 261,000 in the week ended August 20th compared with the previous week's unrevised level of 262,000. The figure came in below market expectations of 265,000 to hit its lowest level in five weeks.2016-08-25T13:32:21
