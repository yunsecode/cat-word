Title: US Business Inventories Unchanged in March
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Unchanged in MarchUnited States Business InventoriesBusiness inventories in the United States were unchanged in March 2019, following a 0.3 percent increase in February and matching market expectations. Inventories at manufacturers rose 0.4 percent (vs 0.3 percent in February), while stocks at retailers dropped 0.3 percent (vs 0.2 percent in February) and those at merchant wholesalers were down 0.1 percent (vs 0.4 percent in February). Retail inventories excluding autos, which go into the calculation of GDP, were flat in March.2019-05-15T14:04:48.647
