Title: US Core Index Rises the Most since 1991
Tag: Core Inflation Rate MoM

US Core Index Rises the Most since 1991United States Core Inflation Rate MoMUS core consumer prices, which exclude volatile items such as food and energy, went up by 0.6 percent from a month earlier in July 2020, the largest increase since January 1991, and above market consensus of 0.2 percent, following the gradual easing of lockdown restrictions in some states. Motor vehicle insurance cost continued to rise sharply in July, after recent declines. In addition, shelter, communication, used cars and trucks, and medical care prices also increased in July, while the index for recreation declined.
