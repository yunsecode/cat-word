Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall 1.6%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall 1.6%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 1.6 percent in the week ended May 13th 2016 from the prior period, after rising 0.4 percent in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Fixed 30-year mortgage rates remained at 3.82 percent. Refinance applications went up 1.4 percent while applications to purchase a home fell 5.8 percent.2016-05-18T12:05:02
