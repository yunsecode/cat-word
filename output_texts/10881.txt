Title: US Economy Adds Less Jobs than Expected
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds Less Jobs than ExpectedUnited States Non Farm PayrollsNon farm payrolls in the United States increased by 103 thousand in March of 2018, following an upwardly revised 326 thousand in February. It is the lowest reading since September and well below market expectations of 193 thousand. Employment grew in manufacturing, health care, and mining but fell in construction and retail sectors. 2018-04-06T12:32:48
