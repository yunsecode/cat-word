Title: US Companies Cut Only 15K Jobs in February
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Companies Cut Only 15K Jobs in FebruaryUnited States Challenger Job CutsJob cuts announced by US-based companies fell to 15,245 in February of 2022 from 19,064 in January with 5,558 positions eliminated due to market conditions. Another 4,671 cuts were due to store, unit, or plant closing, while 1,430 cuts were due to workers refusing to get vaccinated against employer policy. In the first two months of the year, employers announced 34,309 cuts, down 70% year-on-year and the lowest January-February total since records began in 1993. Meanwhile, employers announced plans to hire 215,127 workers, the highest February total on record. “The latest numbers give more evidence that job creation is strong, and employers continue to hold fast to their workforces. The churn in the labor market is coming from resignations,” said Andrew Challenger, Senior Vice President of Challenger, Gray & Christmas, Inc.2022-03-03T12:35:00
