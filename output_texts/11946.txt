Title: US Pending Home Sales Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Unexpectedly FallUnited States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned homes in the United States shrank 1.7 percent year-on-year in May of 2017, following an upwardly revised 3.7 percent drop in April. Compared to the previous month, pending home sales went down 0.8 percent, following an upwardly revised 1.7 percent fall in April and compared to market expectations of a 0.8 percent rise. Across main four regions, contracts fell in the Northeast (-0.8 percent), the South (-1.2 percent) and the West (-1.3 percent) and were flat in the Midwest. 2017-06-28T14:07:58
