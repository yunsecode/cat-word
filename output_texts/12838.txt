Title: US Stocks Fall Ahead of Fed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall Ahead of FedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Wednesday ahead of Fed's monetary policy decision later in the day and amid falling oil prices and tightening polls for the US presidential election. The Dow Jones lost 3 points or 0.2%, the S&P 500 went down 4 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq traded 5 points or 0.1% lower around 11 minutes after the opening bell.2016-11-02T13:47:24
