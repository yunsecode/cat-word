Title: US Jobless Claims Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 6 thousand to 244 thousand in the week ended February 18th 2017, from the previous week's downwardly revised level of 238 thousand and above market expectations of 241 thousand. Claims have been below 300 thousand for 103 straight weeks, the longest streak since 1970.2017-02-23T13:33:05.897
