FED Cuts Bond Buying Program and Changes Forward GuidanceUnited States Interest RateAt its March 19th meeting, the U.S. Federal Reserve decided to drop the 6.5 percent unemployment threshold and pledged it would rely on a wide range of measures in deciding when to raise interest rates.
