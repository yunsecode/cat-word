Dollar Hits Fresh 2-Year LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar fell below 92 to a more than two-year low on Tuesday the Fed unveiled a new strategy for monetary policy that will allow temporary increases in inflation, cementing expectations that the central bank will keep interest rates at ultra-low levels.
