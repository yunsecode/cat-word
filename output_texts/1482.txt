US Natural Gas Storage Declines Sharply: EIAUnited States Natural Gas Stocks ChangeUS utilities pulled 221 billion cubic feet (bcf) of gas from storage during the week ended  December 30th, way more than a decrease of 46 bcf in the same week last year and a five-year (2017-2021) average decline of 98 bcf. Colder-than-normal weather prompted consumers to burn lots of gas to heat their homes and businesses. Figures compared with market expectations for a 228 bcf drop.2023-01-05T15:37:22.47
