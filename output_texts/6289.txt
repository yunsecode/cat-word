Title: Wall Street Extends Losses
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Extends LossesUnited States Stock MarketAll three major US stock indexes finished in negative territory for a second straight day on Tuesday, as much of the enthusiasm over strong earnings momentum faded, with investors getting worried about a pandemic that is far from controlled. Falls were most pronounced in those economically sensitive sectors of the market that had recently outperformed, including cyclical travel and leisure stocks. On the earnings front, Johnson & Johnson beat expectations for quarterly revenue and profit, while Procter & Gamble also surprised markets on the upside. 2021-04-20T20:03:00
