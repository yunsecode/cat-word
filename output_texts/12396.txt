Title: US Stocks Slip to End Rally
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Slip to End RallyUnited States Stock MarketMost US stock indices closed modestly lower on Thursday to end the rally, with the Dow eking out gain. Investors took some profit as they await for details on President’s Trump pro-growth policy promises and as they assess the timing of the Fed’s next rate increase. Gold futures closed at more than 3-month high. Except for the Dow, which edged up 8 points up or less than 0.1 percent to book a new all-time high at 20,619.77, the other two major indices closed on the red. The S&P declined 2 points or 0.1 percent to 2,347.22; and the Nasdaq edged down 5 points or 0.1 percent to 5,814.90. 2017-02-16T21:05:57
