Title: Private Payrolls Beat on the Upside
Tag: Nonfarm Payrolls Private

Private Payrolls Beat on the UpsideUnited States Nonfarm Payrolls PrivatePrivate payrolls in the United States increased by 221K in November of 2022, the least since April of 2021, but beating market forecasts of 190K. Most job gains were seen in leisure and hospitality (88K), including a gain of 62K in food services and drinking places; and health care (45K). In contrast, employment fell in retail trade (-30K) and transportation and warehousing (-15K).2022-12-02T13:45:43.593
