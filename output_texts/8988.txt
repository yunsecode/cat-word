Title: Chicago PMI Recovers from 4 Year Low
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago PMI Recovers from 4 Year LowUnited States Chicago PmiThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer in the US increased to 46.3 in November of 2019 from 43.2 in October which was the lowest since December 2015. Still, the index came below market expectations of 47 and remained in contraction for the third straight month. The New Orders and Order Backlogs indices saw the largest monthly improvement in November, while Supplier Deliveries fell the most.
