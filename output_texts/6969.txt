Title: US Crude Inventories Drop for 5th Straight Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Drop for 5th Straight WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories dropped by 3.247 million barrels in the week ended January 8th, 2021, a fifth consecutive period of decline and compared with market expectations of a 2.266 million fall, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories were up by 4.395 million barrels, while markets had forecast a smaller 2.695 million increase.2021-01-13T15:39:24.353
