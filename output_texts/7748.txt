Title: Treasury Yields Hit 2-Month High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Treasury Yields Hit 2-Month HighUnited States Government Bond 10YYields on 10-year Treasury notes have advanced in five consecutive sessions to a two-month peak of 0.77% on Friday after Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell announced a flexible form of average inflation targeting in a widely expected move. On this new strategy for monetary policy, the Fed is likely to keep short-term interest rates at ultra-low levels for years. 2020-08-27T17:35:00
