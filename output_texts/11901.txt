Title: US Stocks Rise After Strong Payrolls
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise After Strong PayrollsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded higher on Friday after the jobs report showed the US economy added 222 thousand payrolls in June, beating expectations of 179 thousand. The Dow Jones added 95 points or 0.4%, the S&P 500 went up 16 points or 0.7% and the Nasdaq traded 64 points or 1% higher around 12:20 PM NY time.2017-07-07T16:24:48
