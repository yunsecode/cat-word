Title: US Dollar Index Trades Flat
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Index Trades Flat United States CurrencyThe US Dollar Index was little changed at 90 on Monday as investors weighed weaker than expected economic data against the prospects of rising interest rates. The US economy added 559K jobs in May, above an upwardly revised 278K in April but below market forecasts of 650K. Over the weekend, G7 finance ministers backed a historic US proposal that calls for corporations around the world to pay at least a 15% tax on earnings. Meantime, Treasury Secretary Janet Yellen noted that President Joe Biden should push forward with his $4 trillion spending plans even if they trigger inflation that persists into next year and higher interest rates. Yellen also said higher interest rates would be a ‘Plus’ for the US. 2021-06-07T00:13:00
