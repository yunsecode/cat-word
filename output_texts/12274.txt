Title: US Services Sector Rises The Least In 6 Months: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Sector Rises The Least In 6 Months: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe Markit flash US services PMI decreased to 52.9 in March of 2017 from 53.8 in February and well below expectations of 54.2. It is the lowest value in six months, as new work was the lowest in 12 months and employment slowed. Yet, job creation in March was one of the weakest reported over the past three years. Input cost inflation was relatively subdued.
