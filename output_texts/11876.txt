Title: US Import Prices Fall For 2nd Month
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Fall For 2nd MonthUnited States Import Prices MoMUS import prices went down 0.2 percent on the month in June of 2017, following a downwardly revised 0.1 percent drop in May, in line with market expectations. Lower fuel prices drove the decline (-2.2 percent from -1.2 percent in May), which more than offset higher nonfuel prices (0.1 percent from 0 percent), namely food, feeds and beverages (0.9 percent from 1 percent) and capital goods (0.2 percent from 0 percent). On the other hand, prices went down for industrial supplies (-0.9 percent from -0.8 percent); motor vehicles and parts (-0.2 percent from 0.1 percent) and consumer goods (-0.1 percent from 0 percent). Year-on-year, import prices increased 1.5 percent. 2017-07-18T12:33:30
