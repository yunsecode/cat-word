Title: U.S. Jobless Claims Up to 308K In Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

U.S. Jobless Claims Up to 308K In Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending September 28, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 308,000, an increase of 1,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 307,000. The 4-week moving average was 305,000, a decrease of 3,750 from the previous week's revised average of 308,750.2013-10-03T13:34:59.177
