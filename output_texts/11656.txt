Title: US Mortgage Applications Decline the Most Since July 2016
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Decline the Most Since July 2016United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 9.7 percent in the week ending September 15th, 2017, after rising 9.9 percent in the previous period. It is the sharpest decline in mortgage applications since July of 2016, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home slumped 10.8 percent and refinance applications dropped 8.5 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged up by 1bps to 4.04 percent. 2017-09-20T11:06:14.513
