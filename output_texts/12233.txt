US Services Sector Growth At 5-Month Low: ISM United States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States fell to 55.2 in March of 2017 after reaching a 16-month high of 57.6 in February and below market expectations of 57. It is the lowest reading since October of 2016 as production, new orders, employment and inventories slowed and inventories contracted. 2017-04-05T14:00:46
