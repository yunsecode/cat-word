U.S. Inflation Raises 2.7% in MarchUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.5 percent in March on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on April 15.
