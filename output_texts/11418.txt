Title: US House Prices Rise Less than Expected in September: FHFA
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US House Prices Rise Less than Expected in September: FHFAUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States increased 0.3 percent month-over-month in September 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.8 percent gain in August and missing market expectations of 0.6 percent. Year-on-year, prices went up 6.3 percent after rising 6.6 percent in August.2017-11-28T14:04:06
