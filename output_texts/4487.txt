Title: Treasury Yields Edge Up
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Treasury Yields Edge UpUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note inched higher to 1.79%, moving away from 2-week lows early in the week, as market volatility continues and investors await the FOMC decision. The Fed is set to raise the fed funds rate in March and reduce the balance-sheet later this year although the size of the rate hike is still uncertain and investors worry the Fed will tighten monetary policy more aggressively than anticipated. 2022-01-25T19:05:00
