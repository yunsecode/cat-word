Title: Wall Street Rallies Ahead of Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Rallies Ahead of Earnings United States Stock MarketAll three major stock indexes finished in the green on Monday, as pessimism among investors has faded in past days betting on the Fed's slower tightening, ahead of busy earnings week. The Dow added 250 points after advancing more than 400 points during the session, while the S&P 500 roughly gained 1.2% and climbed above the 4000 mark. The Nasdaq 100 added 2%, as investors are eyeing earnings after tech firms shift their focus to cost cuts in recent weeks. Apple was up 2.3% after UBS stuck with its earnings forecast of $1.93 for Apple. Meanwhile, Tesla jumped 7.7% and Spotify was roughly up 2.1%.
