Title: US Jobless Claims Seen Falling Below 400K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Seen Falling Below 400KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsInitial jobless claims likely fell back below the 400 thousand level for the first time in three weeks, moving closer to a pandemic low of 374 thousand reached at the start of June. The easing of coronavirus restrictions and the rapid pace of vaccination, coupled with ongoing government support, has been boosting economic activity and demand for labor. However, employers across the country have been complaining about the struggle to fill open positions, citing ongoing labor shortages due to enhanced benefits, concerns about contracting COVID-19 and finding childcare. Looking ahead, the total number of claimants should continue to decline, following the early phase-out of federal enhanced unemployment benefits across many states ahead of the official September expiration date, and as school reopen and demand over the summer picks up.2021-07-01T11:31:11.687
