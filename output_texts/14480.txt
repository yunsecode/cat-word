Title: US Manufacturing Accelerates in May
Tag: Business Confidence

US Manufacturing Accelerates in MayUnited States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management national factory activity index has increased to 55.4 in May from 54.9 in April. The ISM had earlier mistakenly reported the index fell to 53.2 in May. While new orders, production and customer inventories reported strong gains, factory job growth slowed.
