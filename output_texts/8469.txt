Title: US Stocks Tumble Despite House Deal
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Tumble Despite House DealUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on the largest decline in almost three weeks, as plunging oil prices outweighed a new fiscal deal in Congress. On Tuesday, Brent prices plummeted 22% to extend Monday’s losses and a historic negative turn in WTI crude, as global demand slumps on extended social distancing measures. Meanwhile, the US Senate reached a $484 billion aid agreement that includes $60 billion in loans to small businesses and $75 billion in funding for hospitals and $25 billion for testing, with the rest going to the Paycheck Protection Program. The bill is expected to pass Tuesday afternoon and sent to the House. The Dow Jones lost 632 points or 2.7% to 23019 and the S&P 500 shed 87 points or 3.1% to 2737, both indices booking a near-two week low, while the Nasdaq retreated 298 points or 3.5% to a one-week low of 8263.2020-04-21T20:04:00
