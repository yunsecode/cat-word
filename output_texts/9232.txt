Title: US May Consumer Sentiment Revised Lower
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US May Consumer Sentiment Revised LowerUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US was revised lower to 100 in May 2019 from a preliminary 102.4, missing market expectations of 101.5. It was the highest reading since September last year, as confidence remained at very favorable levels, still significantly eroded in the last two weeks of May due to unfavorable references to tariffs.2019-05-31T14:05:00
