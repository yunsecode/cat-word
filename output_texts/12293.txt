Title: US Chicago Fed National Activity Index Beats Expectations
Tag: Chicago Fed National Activity Index

US Chicago Fed National Activity Index Beats ExpectationsUnited States Chicago Fed National Activity IndexThe Chicago Fed National Activity Index in the United States rose to 0.34 in February 2017 from an upwardly revised -0.02 in the previous month and beating market expectations of 0.03. The contribution from employment-related indicators increased to 0.21 from 0.06 in January; production-related indicators edged up to 0.09 from 0.04 and sales, orders and inventories rebounded to 0.08 to from -0.01. The contribution from personal consumption and housing was still negative, but less than in the previous period (-0.03 from -0.11).2017-03-20T12:38:19
