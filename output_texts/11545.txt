Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on EarningsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Tuesday, with the Dow reaching a record high amid better-than-expected earnings for 3M, Caterpillar and General Motors. The Dow Jones was up 167 points or 0.7%; the S&P 500 went up 5 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq added 12 points or 0.2% around 6 minutes after the opening bell.2017-10-24T13:30:06
