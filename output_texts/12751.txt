Title: US Existing Home Sales at 9-1/2-Year High
Tag: Existing Home Sales

US Existing Home Sales at 9-1/2-Year HighUnited States Existing Home SalesSales of previously owned houses in the United States rose 2 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 5600 thousand in October of 2016. It is the highest figure since February of 2007, beating market expectations of a 0.5 percent fall or 5430 thousand. Sales of single family homes went up 2.3 percent to 4990 thousand while those of condos were flat at 610 thousand. The average price fell 1 percent and the months’ worth of supply went down to 4.3 from 4.4.
