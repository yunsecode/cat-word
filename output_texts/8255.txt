Title: US Dollar Strengthens amid US-China Tensions
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Strengthens amid US-China TensionsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rose for the first time in 5 sessions to trade above 99.3 on Thursday as investors’ risk appetite declined amid increasing tensions between the US and China. On Wednesday, the US Senate passed a bill that can ban Chineses companies from listing in American exchanges. The bill is set to apply to all foreign companies although it is targeted to China. Also, President Trump accused China of being is on a massive disinformation campaign and that all comes from the top.2020-05-21T08:32:00
