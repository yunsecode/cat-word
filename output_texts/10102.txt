Title: US Housing Starts Fall More than Expected
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the US dropped 5.3 percent from a month earlier to an annualized rate of 1,201 thousand in September of 2018, following a 7.1 percent gain in August and compared with market expectations of a 4.5 percent fall. Starts in the South declined the most since October 2015 mainly due to Hurricane Florence, which hit North and South Carolina in September.2018-10-17T12:37:00
