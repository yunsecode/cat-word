Title: US Planned Job Cuts at 3-Month Low
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Planned Job Cuts at 3-Month LowUnited States Challenger Job CutsThe number of announced corporate layoffs in the United States fell 29 percent month over month to 32,188 in August of 2016, reaching the lowest in three months. Biggest job cuts occurred in the computer sector (6,103), with the bulk of the cuts coming from Cisco Systems, which announced plans to reduce its workforce by 5,500; entertainment and leisure (3,307); industrial goods (3,073); energy (2,430) and transportation (2,223). Year-on-year, layoffs fell 22 percent. To date, employers have announced 391,288 job cuts in 2016, down 10 percent from 434,554 job cuts in the same period of 2015.2016-09-01T12:41:04.093
