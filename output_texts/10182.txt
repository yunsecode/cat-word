Title: US Manufacturing PMI Confirmed at 4-Month High: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing PMI Confirmed at 4-Month High: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI rose to 55.6 in September of 2018 from 54.7 in August, matching the preliminary estimate. The reading pointed to the strongest growth in factory activity in four months, driven by sharper rises in output and new orders, though new business from abroad continued to expand at only a marginal pace. Also, the average for the third quarter was strong overall but signaled the softest expansion since the fourth quarter of 2017.2018-10-01T13:48:58.657
