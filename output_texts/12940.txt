Title: US Jobless Claims Down to 249K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Down to 249KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits fell by 5,000 to 249,000 in the week ended October 1st, below market expectations of 257,000 and reaching the lowest figure since mid-April. The four-week average, which smooths out week-to-week volatility in the claims data, dropped to 253,500, the lowest since 1973. 2016-10-06T12:37:09.047
