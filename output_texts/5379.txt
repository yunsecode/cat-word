Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Eases After Fed Powell Remarks
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Eases After Fed Powell RemarksUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note was down to 1.32% on Friday, moving further away from a one-and-half-month high of 1.37% hit on Thursday, after Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell indicated that the central bank is likely to begin tapering its $120 billion in monthly bond purchases before the end of the year, though he still sees interest rate hikes off in the distance.2021-08-27T14:22:00
