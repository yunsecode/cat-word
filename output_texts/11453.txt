Title: US Business Inventories Stall in September
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Stall in SeptemberUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the United States were flat month-over-month in September of 2017, following a downwardly revised 0.6 percent rise in August and in line with market expectations. Inventories rose at manufacturers (0.7 percent, compared to 0.6 percent in August) but fell at retailers (-0.9 percent from 0.6 percent) and slowed at wholesalers (0.3 percent from 0.8 percent). Business sales jumped 1.4 percent in September, following a 0.8 percent increase in August. 2017-11-15T15:15:59.103
