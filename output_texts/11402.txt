Title: US Chicago PMI at 3-Month Low
Tag: Chicago Pmi

US Chicago PMI at 3-Month LowUnited States Chicago PmiThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer fell to 63.9 in November of 2017 from 66.2 in October which was the highest since March of 2011. Figures came better than market expectations of 63. Output expanded at a robust rate in November, as businesses continued to benefit from healthy domestic conditions and an abating of the disruption brought about by the recent adverse weather. New orders, although retreating from October’s four-month high, remained strong. After slipping into contraction territory in October, the employment indicator returned to expansion in November. On the other hand, order backlogs lost ground and inflationary pressures at the factory gate remained elevated, with the prices paid indicator at the third-highest level this year.2017-11-30T15:10:49.453
