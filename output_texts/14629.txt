Title: U.S. Jobless Claims Fall Less Than Expected In Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

U.S. Jobless Claims Fall Less Than Expected In Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending October 19, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 350,000, a decrease of 12,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 362,000.
