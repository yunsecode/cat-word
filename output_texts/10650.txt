Title: US Unemployment Rate Falls to 18-Year Low
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Unemployment Rate Falls to 18-Year LowUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate fell to 3.8 percent in May 2018 from 3.9 percent in the previous month, and below market expectations of 3.9 percent. It was the lowest rate since April 2000, as the number of unemployed decreased by 281 thousand to 6.07 million and employment rose by 293 thousand to 155.47 million.2018-06-01T12:31:00
