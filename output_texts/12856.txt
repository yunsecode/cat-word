Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down in October
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down in OctoberUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe final reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States decreased to 87.2 in October of 2016 from a preliminary reading of 87.9 and a final of 91.2 in September. It is the lowest reading since September of 2015 due to less favorable prospects for the national economy, with half of all consumers anticipating an economic downturn sometime in the next five years.2016-10-28T14:08:51.967
