Title: DXY Falls after FOMC Minutes
Tag: Currency

DXY Falls after FOMC MinutesUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index erased gains but held above 113 on Wednesday, after several Fed officials said it would be important to calibrate the pace of further policy tightening to mitigate the risk of significant adverse effects on the economic outlook, the minutes of the Sept. 20-21 meeting showed. Still, many participants emphasized the cost of taking too little action to bring down inflation likely outweighed the cost of taking too much action. Investors now focus on Thursday’s CPI print, which could add to market bets for a fourth 75 bps rate hike by the central bank in November. The latest data showed that producer price growth rose by a more-than-expected 0.4% in September, rebounding from the decline in August and adding to the urgency for the Fed to tame inflation. Moreover, the dollar continued to benefit from strong demand for haven currencies due to a highly uncertain outlook amid geopolitical tension, with the IMF slashing its 2023 growth forecast for the global economy.2022-10-12T18:08:00
