Title: Dow Falls Most since July, S&P Posts Worst Sessions since May
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Falls Most since July, S&P Posts Worst Sessions since MayUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones shed more than 600 points on Monday, the most since July 19th and the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq suffered the worst session since May after falling sharply the previous week as investors' sentiment was hurt by a potential default from Chinese property giant Evergrande and rising Covid-19 cases ahead of the FOMC meeting later in the week. The Fed has made it already clear that tapering could occur this year but investors are waiting for more details, particularly after mixed economic data was released last week. The delta variant of the coronavirus pushed up infections to levels last seen in January, wiping out months of progress despite vaccination efforts as North America heads to colder weather.
