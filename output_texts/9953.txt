Title: US Durable Goods Orders Post Biggest Fall in Over a Year
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Post Biggest Fall in Over a YearUnited States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods slumped 4.4 percent from a month earlier in October 2018, following a downwardly revised 0.1 percent decline in September and worse than market expectations of a 2.5 percent drop. It was the largest fall in durable goods orders since July 2017 as demand for transportation equipment dropped.2018-11-21T13:32:58.92
