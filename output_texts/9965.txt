Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Friday 16 November 2018, as tech stocks fell modestly mainly dragged by a 19% decline in Nvidia shares following weaker than expected revenues for Q3 and a downwardly revised outlook. The Dow Jones gained 124 points or 0.5% to 25413. The S&P 500 added 6 points or 0.2% to 2736. In contrast, the Nasdaq retreated 11 points or 0.2% to 7248. 2018-11-16T21:11:05.813
