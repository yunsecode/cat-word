Title: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index Falls from Record High
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index Falls from Record HighUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Manufacturing Activity Index in the US fifth district including the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia fell to 15 in October of 2018 from an all-time high of 29 in September and well below market expectations of 25. Slower increases were seen in shipments (7 from 33), new orders (20 from 34), backlog of orders (13 from 20), capital expenditures (21 from 29) and inventories (1 from 6). Also, price pressures intensified for both prices paid (5.68 from 3.47) and received (2.84 from 1.93). On the other hand, employment rose faster (19 from 16). However, survey respondents were optimistic, expecting to see positive growth across most measures in the coming months.
