Title: Dollar Eases Back From 1-Week Highs
Tag: Currency

Dollar Eases Back From 1-Week HighsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was off from a one-week high of 93.20 but seems to be capped by the 93 level on expectations that central banks would not have to flood markets with more liquidity if a coronavirus vaccine is available soon. At the same time, higher yields on US bonds compared with those on European ones spooked investors away from the euro. 2020-11-12T02:07:32.03
