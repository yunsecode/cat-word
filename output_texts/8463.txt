Title: US New Home Sales Lowest in Near 1 Year
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Lowest in Near 1 YearUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family homes in the United States plunged 15.4 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 627 thousand in March of 2020, below a downwardly revised 741 thousand in February. It is the lowest reading since May of 2019 and the biggest drop since July of 2013. However, further declines are expected in the coming months due to the coronavirus outbreak. Sales fell in the Northeast (-41.5 percent to 24 thousand), the West (-38.5 percent to 139 thousand), the Midwest (-8.1 percent to 79 thousand) and the South (-0.8 percent to 385 thousand). There were 333,000 new homes on the market, up 2.8 percent from February. At March's sales pace it would take 5.4 months to clear the supply of houses on the market. The median new house price rose to USD 321,400 from USD 310,600 a year ago. Year-on-year new home sales declined 9.5 percent.2020-04-23T14:12:00
