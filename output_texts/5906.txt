Dollar Rises Slightly United States CurrencyThe dollar index edged up to 90.1 on Tuesday, as investors remain cautious ahead of US inflation release later in the week. The consumer price index report is set to provide more clarity on the Fed's next moves. Jobs growth in May was not as strong as expected quelling expectations of an early tapering in the Federal Reserve’s stimulus.
