Title: USD Appreciates Sharply
Tag: Currency

USD Appreciates SharplyUnited States CurrencyThe dollar strengthened toward the 103 mark as investors bolstered bets that the Federal Reserve would hike rates in May following Friday's payrolls data. The Labor Department's closely watched report showed the U.S. nonfarm payrolls increased by 236,000 jobs in March, coming close to expectations of 239,000. The unemployment rate also fell to 3.5% compared with the forecasts of 3.6%. Markets are now pricing in an over 70% chance that the Fed would raise rates by 25 basis points next month. Investors also await the US CPI report later this week for further clues on the tightening path. The dollar's strength was seen across the board, with some of the most pronounced buying activity against the Japanese yen after Japan's new central bank governor, Kazuo Ueda, hinted at an accommodative monetary stance.2023-04-10T14:34:00
