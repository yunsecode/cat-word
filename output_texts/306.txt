Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Higher but Remains Lowest in 6 Months
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Higher but Remains Lowest in 6 MonthsUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan consumer sentiment for the US was revised higher to 59.2 in May of 2023 from an initial estimate of 57.7, but remaining the lowest level in six months, amid worries about the path of the economy. This decline mirrors the 2011 debt ceiling crisis, during which sentiment also plunged, according to Surveys of Consumers Director Joanne Hsu. The expectations gauge was revised to 55.4 from 53.4 and the current economic conditions subindex to 64.9 from 64.5. Inflation expectations were also revised lower for both the year ahead (4.2% vs 4.5% in the preliminary estimate) and the five-year outlook (3.1% vs 3.2%).2023-05-26T14:06:00
