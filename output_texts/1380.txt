Title: US Mortgage Rates Continue to Fall: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Rate

US Mortgage Rates Continue to Fall: MBAUnited States Mortgage RateThe average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($647,200 or less) in the US dropped 3bps to 6.2% in the week ended January 20th, 2023. The rate remains the lowest since September. The decline in mortgage rates tracks a fall in Treasury yields amid rising expectations the Federal Reserve will slow the pace of rate increases.2023-01-25T12:13:00
