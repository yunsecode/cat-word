Title: US Stocks Open Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open HigherUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Tuesday as investors shrug off latest exchange in the trade war between the US and China. Yesterday, Trump administration announced a 10% tariff on $200 billion worth of Chinese products while today China responded with tariffs between 5% to 10% on $60 billion worth of US imports. The Dow Jones gained 0.4%, the S&P 500 went up 0.3% and the Nasdaq traded 0.6% higher around 20 minutes after the opening bell.2018-09-18T13:51:00
