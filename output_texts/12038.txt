Title: US Challenger Job Cuts At 13-Month High In May
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Challenger Job Cuts At 13-Month High In MayUnited States Challenger Job CutsUS based employers announced plans to shed 51,692 workers from payrolls in May of 2017, 41.2 percent higher than 36,602 in April and the highest since April of 2016. 20,000 of those job cuts were announced by Ford Motor Company. As a result, the automotive industry led all industries in May with 20,271 job cuts. The retail industry continued to shed jobs (5,777), mostly due to a changing consumer behavior in grocery shopping. Employers have announced a total of 214,495 job cuts so far this year, down 28 percent from the same period a year earlier. Retailers continued to announce the most job cuts this year with 55,910. 2017-06-01T11:47:37
