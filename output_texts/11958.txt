Title: US New Home Sales Beat Forecasts
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Beat ForecastsUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States increased 2.9 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 610 thousand in May of 2017 from an upwardly revised 593 thousand in April. The figure came above market expectations of 597 thousand, as sales rose in the South and the West.2017-06-23T14:03:48
