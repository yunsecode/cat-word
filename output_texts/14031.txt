Title: US Wholesale Inventories Edge Up, Sales Fall
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Edge Up, Sales FallUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US increased 0.1 percent in August of 2015, rebounding from a downwardly revised 0.3 percent drop in July and above market expectations, boosted by stocks of durable goods, computers and software. In contrast, sales shrank 1 percent.
