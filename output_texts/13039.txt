Title: US Wholesale Inventories Unchanged in July
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Unchanged in JulyUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale stocks in the United States were nearly flat at $591.3 billion in July from June 2016, following a 0.3 percent gain in the previous period and in line with market expectations. Inventories of durable goods rose 0.3 percent, boosted by professional and commercial equipment and supplies (+1.4 percent). Meanwhile stocks of nondurable goods fell 0.3 percent, as inventories of farm product raw materials were down 2 percent from last month, while inventories of chemicals and allied products increased 1.4 percent. Wholesale stocks excluding autos, the component that goes into GDP calculation, was also unchanged.2016-09-09T15:06:52
