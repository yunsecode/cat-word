Title: US Building Permits Fall More than Expected in December
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Fall More than Expected in DecemberUnited States Building PermitsBuilding permits in the United States fell 3.9 percent from a month earlier to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,416 thousand in December 2019, missing market expectations of 1,468 thousand. Authorisations for the volatile multi-family housing segment declined 9.6 percent to 500 thousand and single-family permits dropped 0.5 percent to 916 thousand. Across regions, permits went down in the Northeast (-15.7 percent to 134 thousand) and the South (-6.5 percent to 688 thousand), but rose in the Midwest (0.5 percent to 207 thousand); and the West (3.8 percent to 387 thousand). Permits for November were revised to 1,474 thousand from 1,482 thousand. Considering full 2019, building permits increased a non-seasonally adjusted 3.9 percent to 1368.8 thousand.2020-01-17T13:32:00
