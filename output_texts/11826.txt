Title: US Personal Spending Edges Up 0.1% in June
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Edges Up 0.1% in JuneUnited States Personal SpendingPersonal spending in the United States edged up 0.1 percent month-over-month in June of 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.2 percent rise in May and in line with market expectations. A rise in services spending offset declines in purchases of both durable and nondurable goods. Meanwhile, personal income was flat in June, the worst performance in seven months and well below expectations of a 0.4 percent gain. 2017-08-01T12:36:08
