Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Monday 12 March 2018, as trade-war fears arising by the recently announced tariffs to US imports of steel and aluminum triggered criticisms from global leaders and investors. The Dow Jones closed down 157 points or 0.6% to 25179. The S&P 500 edged down 4 points or 0.1% to 2783. In contrast, the Nasdaq gained 28 points or 0.4% to a new high of 7588. 2018-03-12T20:14:25
