Title: US Q1 Labor Productivity Revised Lower
Tag: Productivity

US Q1 Labor Productivity Revised LowerUnited States ProductivityLabor productivity in the US non-farm business sector increased by an annualized 3.4 percent during the first quarter of 2019, below a preliminary estimate of 3.6 percent and following a 1.3 percent rise in the previous three-month period. Figures came below market expectations of 3.5 percent. Output increased 3.9 percent and hours worked increased 0.5 percent. Year-on-year, productivity rose 2.4 percent, the most since the third quarter of 2010, reflecting a 3.9 percent increase in output and a 1.5 percent increase in hours worked.
