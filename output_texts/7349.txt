Title: US Crude Inventories Fall the Most in 2 Months
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Fall the Most in 2 MonthsUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 7.998 million barrels in the week ended October 30th 2020, following a 4.32 million rise in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 0.89 million increase, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. That was the steepest decline in crude stocks since the week ended August 28th. Meantime, gasoline inventories were up by 1.541 million barrels, while markets had forecast a drop of 0.871 million.2020-11-04T15:33:00
