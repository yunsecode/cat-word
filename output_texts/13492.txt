Title: Fed Leaves Rates on Hold
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Leaves Rates on HoldUnited States Interest RateThe Federal Reserve left the target range for its federal funds rate unchanged at 0.25 percent to 0.5 percent for the third time during its April 2016 meeting. Policymakers said labor market conditions improved but economic activity appears to have slowed although concerns regarding risks from global economic and financial developments to the US economy eased.2016-04-27T19:00:43
