Title: US Stocks Fall on Oil
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on OilUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded lower on Tuesday as oil prices continued to slide. The Dow Jones was 225 points or 1.37% down, the S&P 500 declined 1.19% and the Nasdaq lost 0.88% around 40 minutes after the open. Meanwhile, WTIcrude was down 4.9% to $30.07 a barrel and Brent crude fell 4.9% to $32.59 per barrel. 2016-02-02T15:13:38
