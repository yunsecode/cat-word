Title: Dow, S&P Close at Fresh Record High
Tag: Stock Market

Dow, S&P Close at Fresh Record High United States Stock MarketUS stocks finished higher on Monday, as stronger-than-expected earnings' report from Bank of America as well as news of merger between Japan's Softbank and British chipmaker ARM Holdings boosted sentiment. The Dow Jones was up for the seventh consecutive day by 0.09% or 16.5 points to hit a fresh record high and the S&P 500 increased 0.24% or 5.15 points for a new all-time high. The Nasdaq added 0.52% or 26.19 points and closed at the highest level in 2016.2016-07-18T21:50:48.28
