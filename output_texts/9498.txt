Title: US Private Sector Output Growth Slows in March: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Output Growth Slows in March: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI dropped to 54.3 in March 2019 from a seven-month high of 55.5 in the previous month and below market consensus of 55.2, a preliminary estimate showed. The latest reading pointed to the weakest upturn in private sector business activity since September 2018, as new work rose the least since April 2017 and employment growth eased to the weakest since June 2017. On the price front, input price inflation fell to a two-year low and prices charged by private sector firms increased at the softest pace since October 2017. Looking ahead, business optimism dipped to the lowest since June 2016.2019-03-22T13:55:52.22
