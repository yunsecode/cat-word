Title: Overseas Investors Sell US Assets in December
Tag: Capital Flows

Overseas Investors Sell US Assets in DecemberUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors sold USD 33.1 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments, in December 2018 after buying an upwardly revised USD 32.1 billion in November. Meanwhile, foreigners sold USD 48.3 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying a downwardly revised USD 32.0 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors sold USD 77.4 billion of US treasuries, after selling an upwardly revised USD 13.2 billion in November.2019-02-15T21:21:00
