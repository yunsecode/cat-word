Title: US GDP Growth Strongest Since Q3 2014
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Strongest Since Q3 2014United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy advanced an annualized 4.2 percent on quarter in the second quarter of 2018, unrevised from the previous estimate and in line with market expectations. A downward revision to private inventory investment was offset by small upward revisions to most other GDP components, the third estimate showed. Imports were revised slightly down.2018-09-27T12:35:00
