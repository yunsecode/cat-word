Title: Wall Street Opens Higher, S&P Hits Fresh High
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Higher, S&P Hits Fresh HighUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened in the green on Monday, extending Friday’s gains after the jobs report for June came well above expectations. The Dow Jones added 0.43% or 79 points, the S&P 500 gained 0.3% or 6 points, hitting a new all-time intraday high and the Nasdaq rose 0.64% or 32 points around 30 minutes after the opening bell.2016-07-11T15:09:29
