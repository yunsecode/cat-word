Title: US Personal Income Rises the Least in Over a Year
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Rises the Least in Over a YearUnited States Personal IncomeUS personal income increased by 0.2 percent from a month earlier in September 2018, easing from an upwardly revised 0.4 percent growth in August and missing market expectations of 0.3 percent. It was the smallest gain in personal income since June 2017, as increases in wages and salaries, government social benefits to persons, and rental income of persons were partially offset by a decline in proprietors’ income.2018-10-29T12:32:00
