Title: US July Wholesale Inventories Rise Less
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US July Wholesale Inventories Rise LessUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US went up by 0.8 percent month-over-month to $902.9 billion in July of 2022, following a 1.9 percent rise in the previous month, a preliminary estimate showed. Stocks of durable goods rose at a slower pace of 1.3 percent (vs 2.3 percent in June) while inventory levels of nondurable goods were flat, after increasing 1.2 percent in the prior month.
