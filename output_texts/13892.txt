Title: US Consumer Credit Change Below Forecasts
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Change Below ForecastsUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States increased by 15.98 USD billion in October of 2015, lower than a downwardly revised 28.57 USD billion in September and below market expectations of a 20 USD billion gain. Revolving credit went up by 0.179 USD billion, slowing sharply from a 6.65 USD billion rise in the previous month and nonrevolving credit increased by 15.8 USD billion, compared to a 21.9 billion USD rise in September.2015-12-07T20:08:38.28
