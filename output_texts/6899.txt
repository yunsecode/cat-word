Title: US Stocks Close Mixed, Apple Nudges Nasdaq to High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed, Apple Nudges Nasdaq to HighUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed once again in a volatile session with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq booking fresh highs, as investors turn their attention to earnings season and Big Tech reports. On Monday, shares of Apple nudged the Nasdaq higher ahead of its quarterly numbers. On the fiscal side, negotiations continue as President Joe Biden tries to push through a $1.9 trillion stimulus program that many congressional Republicans oppose. On the pandemic front, hospitalizations continue to trend down at a painfully slow level and vaccinations roll out as expected. The Dow Jones lost 0.1%. In contrast, the S&P 500 jumped 0.4% and the Nasdaq 0.7% to book new records.2021-01-25T21:05:00
