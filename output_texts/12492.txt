Title: US New Home Sales Down To 10-Month Low
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Down To 10-Month LowUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States shrank 10.4 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 536,000 in December of 2016, much worse than market expectations of a 1 percent drop. It is the lowest figure since February. Considering full 2016, new home sales went up 12.2 percent to 563,000 the highest since 2007.
