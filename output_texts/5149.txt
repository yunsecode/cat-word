Dollar Edges Up to Start the WeekUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was higher around 94 on Monday, in line with a rise in Treasury yields and not far from 13-month high levels, amid a general risk-off mood and as investors bet the American economy is in better shape to fully recover from the coronavirus pandemic despite inflationary pressures and an early Fed tapering. Fresh data showed the Chinese economy slowed more than expected in Q3 while oil prices continued to soar to multi-year highs.
