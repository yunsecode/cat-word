Title: Dollar Rally Takes a Pause
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rally Takes a PauseUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index bottomed around 90.70 on Monday, moving away from the key 91 regions as money flew to riskier assets amid prospects of more policy support to sustain the US economic recovery. The House of Representatives has voted to approve President Joe Biden's $1.9 trillion pandemic aid package that includes extra $1,400 checks for most Americans. The most pronounced selling activity was against the Australian and New Zealand dollars.
