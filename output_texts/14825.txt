Title: U.S. GDP Growth Revised Down to 2.8% in Q4
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

U.S. GDP Growth Revised Down to 2.8% in Q4United States GDP Growth RateU.S. real gross domestic product increased at an annual rate of 2.8 percent in the fourth quarter of 2010, (that is, from the third quarter to the fourth quarter), according to the "second" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.  In the third quarter, real GDP increased 2.6 percent.2011-02-25T13:37:23
