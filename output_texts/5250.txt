Title: US Stocks Sell-Off as Yields Surge
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Sell-Off as Yields Surge United States Stock MarketWall Street closed sharply lower on Tuesday as a spike in Treasury yields dragged tech shares down. The yield on the 10-year Treasury topped 3-months highs of 1.5% and the 5-year rate rose above 1% for the first time since February 2020 as investor's bets grew after the FOMC meeting last week that the Fed will soon start reducing stimulus. The Nasdaq declined 2.8%, the most since March, the S&P 500 fell 2% and the Dow Jones declined around 570 points. Meanwhile, Fed Chair Powell said in his testimony before the US Senate the economy had all but met the test for tapering and that there's still a long way from meeting the test for maximum employment.2021-09-28T20:04:00
