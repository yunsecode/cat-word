Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States dropped 3.3 percent in the week ended April 24th of 2020, following a 0.3 percent fall in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went down 7.3 percent while applications to purchase a home increased 11.6 percent. Meantime, the average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 2bps to 3.43 percent, its lowest level on record.2020-04-29T11:02:16.597
