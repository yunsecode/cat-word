Title: New York Business Activity Growth Slows
Tag: Ism New York Index

New York Business Activity Growth SlowsUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM NY Current Business Conditions Index decreased to 54.6 in January of 2016 from 62 in the previous month and the 6-month outlook worsened to 63.3 from 70.7. At the company level, employment grew for the first time in five months (56.9 from 42.9) and prices paid increased (60.7 from 55) while quantity of purchases contracted (46.4 from 50) and current revenues shrank (45.8 from 60). Expected revenues were steady (50).2016-02-02T14:58:02.77
