Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Hits 1-1/2-Month High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Hits 1-1/2-Month HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note rose above 1.37% on Thursday, its highest level since mid-July, after Federal Reserve official James Bullard reiterated he wants the bond buying to end by early next year, and that a quick end to taper allows optionality for rate hikes. Investors now await a speech from Fed Chair at the Jackson Hole symposium tomorrow for more details about Fed's plans for stimulus. 2021-08-26T12:56:00
