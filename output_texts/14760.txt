Title: U.S. Trade Deficit Widens in March
Tag: Balance of Trade

U.S. Trade Deficit Widens in MarchUnited States Balance of TradeTotal March exports of $186.8 billion and imports of $238.6 billion resulted in a goods and services deficit of $51.8 billion, up from $45.4 billion in February, revised. March exports were $5.3 billion more than February exports of $181.5 billion. March imports were $11.7 billion more than February imports of $226.9 billion.
