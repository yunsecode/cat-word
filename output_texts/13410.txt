Title: Wall Street Opens Higher on Oil Rally
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Higher on Oil RallyUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened higher on Wednesday, extending Tuesday’s gains, led by the energy sector as oil prices climbed. The Dow Jones added 0.64% or 114 points, the S&P 500 rose 0.6% or 13 points and the Nasdaq increased 0.45% or 22 points around 20 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-05-25T15:00:11
