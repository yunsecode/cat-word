Title: US Futures Hover at Record Levels
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Hover at Record LevelsUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were little changed around record levels on Tuesday amid strong corporate results. Earnings from Pfizer, Estée Lauder, DuPont, KKR & Co and Under Armour topped forecasts. So far, 55.8% of S&P 500 companies have reported quarterly financial results, with 82% beating earnings estimates, according to FactSet. On the other hand, Tesla shares led premarket losses and fell more than 5% after Elon Musk said the company has not signed contract with Hertz yet and after news that the carmaker will recall 11,700 vehicles. Meanwhile, the Fed's 2-day monetary policy meeting kicks off today and the central bank is expected to announce a reduction in its bond purchases. The payrolls report due Friday will also be in the spotlight. 2021-11-02T11:36:00
