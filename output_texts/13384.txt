Title: US Jobless Claims At 5-Week Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims At 5-Week LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits was recorded at 267,000, a decrease of 1,000 from the previous week's unrevised level of 268,000, below market expectations of 270,000. This marks 65 consecutive weeks of initial claims below 300,000, the longest streak since 1973.2016-06-02T13:33:20
