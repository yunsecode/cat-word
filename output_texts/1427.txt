Title: US Mortgage Rates Lowest in 4-Months: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Rate

US Mortgage Rates Lowest in 4-Months: MBAUnited States Mortgage RateThe average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($647,200 or less) in the US dropped 19bps to 6.23% in the week ended January 13th, 2023. It is the lowest rate since September and about a percentage point below the peak of 7.16% reported in October. The decline in mortgage rates tracks a fall in Treasury yields amid further signs of slowing inflationary pressures. 2023-01-18T12:17:00
