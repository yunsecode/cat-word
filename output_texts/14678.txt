Title: U.S. Jobless Claims Down to 346K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

U.S. Jobless Claims Down to 346K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending June 22, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 346,000, a decrease of 9,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 355,000. The 4-week moving average was 345,750, a decrease of 2,750 from the previous week's revised average of 348,500.2013-06-27T14:10:54.947
