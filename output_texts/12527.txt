Title: Fed Expects Rates To Rise A Few Times A Year Until 2019
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Expects Rates To Rise A Few Times A Year Until 2019United States Interest RateAs the economy is near maximum employment and inflation is moving toward Fed's goal of 2 percent, it makes sense to gradually reduce the level of monetary policy support, Fed Chair Yellen said at the Commonwealth Club, San Francisco, California. Yellen also said that the Federal Open Market Committee expects to increase the central bank's federal funds rate target a few times a year until 2019, and that waiting too long to begin raising interest rates could risk a "nasty surprise".2017-01-18T20:16:06
