Title: US Pending Home Sales Up 4.2% YoY in December
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Up 4.2% YoY in DecemberUnited States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned US homes increased 4.2 percent in December of 2015 over the same month in the previous year, the biggest gain in four months. Month-on-month, pending home sales index edged up 0.1 percent after falling an upwardly revised 1.1 percent in November and much lower than market expectations of a 0.8 percent rise. Contracts rose 6.1 percent in the Northeast but were down 1 percent in the Midwest, fell 0.5 percent in the South and declined 2.1 percent in the West. 2016-01-28T15:10:38.23
