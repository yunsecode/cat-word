Title: Dollar Holds Steady
Tag: Currency

Dollar Holds SteadyUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index held steady above 112 on Monday as investors looked ahead to US manufacturing data and speeches from Federal Reserve officials for insight on the state of the world’s largest economy and to guide the rates outlook. Markit PMI and ISM manufacturing data for the US are due for release later on Monday, while Fed officials Raphael Bostic and Thomas Barkin are set to give separate speeches. The dollar has climbed steadily throughout the year as the US central bank aggressively raised interest rates to quell surging inflation. The Fed also forecasted rates to peak at 4.6% next year with no cuts until 2024, shooting down any dovish pivot that the markets were hoping for in the near term. Global recession fears and the US economy’s relative strength also boosted safe-haven demand for the dollar at the expense of other assets.2022-10-03T05:30:21.48
