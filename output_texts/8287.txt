Title: US Business Inventories Fall 0.2%
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Fall 0.2%United States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the US fell 0.2 percent from a month earlier in March of 2020, following an upwardly revised 0.5 percent drop in February and matching market expectations. Inventories declined faster for both wholesalers (-0.8 percent vs -0.7 percent) and manufacturers (-0.8 percent vs -0.4 percent). On the other hand, stocks at retailers increased 1 percent, rebounding from a 0.3 percent decrease. Year-on-year, business inventories edged down 0.3 percent.2020-05-15T14:03:00
