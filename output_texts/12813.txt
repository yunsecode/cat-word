Title: Wall Street Opens Slightly Lower on Election Day
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Slightly Lower on Election DayUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened in the red on Tuesday, following a rally in the previous day as investors await the results of the US presidential election. The Dow Jones declined 35 points or 0.2%, the S&P 500 lost 6 points or 0.3% and the Nasdaq traded 12 points or 0.2% lower around 3 minutes after the opening bell.2016-11-08T14:36:56
