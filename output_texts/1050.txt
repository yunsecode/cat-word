Title: US Private Sector Output Stabilizes in February
Tag: Composite PMI

US Private Sector Output Stabilizes in FebruaryUnited States Composite PMIThe S&P Global US Composite PMI stood at 50.1 in February 2023, little-changed from a preliminary estimate of 50.2 and well above January's 46.8. The latest data signaled an end to a seven-month sequence of contraction and indicated broadly stable levels of business activity at private sector firms. New orders declined the least in four months, while the rate of job creation accelerated to a five-month high and backlogs of work fell for a fifth month running. On the price front, input costs rose at the second-slowest pace since October 2020. Nonetheless, output charges increased at a steeper pace, as firms sought to pass through higher cost burdens to their customers.2023-03-03T14:58:20.373
