Title: Dallas Fed Services Index Lowest Since July 2020
Tag: Dallas Fed Services Index

Dallas Fed Services Index Lowest Since July 2020United States Dallas Fed Services IndexThe Dallas Fed General Business Activity index for services in Texas fell to -19.8 in December of 2022, the lowest since July 2020, sinking well below the series average of 3.5, and compared to the -11 in the prior month. Perceptions of broader business conditions dropped further with the company outlook gauge falling sharply to -9.9 from -4.1 in the prior month. On the other hand, the uncertainty outlook eased to 22 from 23.4 in the previous period. 2022-12-28T15:51:00
