Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed higher on Tuesday, despite fall in oil prices, as better-than-expected US consumer confidence data and the first presidential debate between Hillary Clinton and Donald Trump on Monday night lifted investors' sentiment. The Dow Jones went up 133.5 points or 0.74%, the S&P 500 added 13.8 points or 0.64% and the Nasdaq increased 48.2 points or 0.92%.2016-09-27T20:15:27.247
