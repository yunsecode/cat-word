Title: US ISM NY Index at 4-Month Low in September
Tag: Ism New York Index

US ISM NY Index at 4-Month Low in SeptemberUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions in the United States fell to 49.7 in September of 2017 from 56.6 in August. It is the lowest reading in four months as declines were seen in employment (48.3 from 56.4); quantity of purchases (48.1 from 51.6) and expected revenues (52 from 68.8) while price pressures increased (52.3 from 50). On the other hand, current revenues went up (50 from 48.4). The six-month outlook also went down to 58.4 from 60.5.2017-10-03T15:39:34.287
