Title: Dollar Rises on Hawkish Fed Talk
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rises on Hawkish Fed TalkUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index bounced back to around 108.7 on Wednesday, rising again toward its highest levels in 20 years, as the latest hawkish Federal Reserve commentary stressing the need for more aggressive rate hikes to control inflation outweighed weak US economic data. Minneapolis Fed President Neel Kashkari said his biggest fear was underestimating the extent of price pressures, and that the central bank would have to be more aggressive for longer if inflation persists. Investors are also gearing up for Fed Chair Jerome Powell’s speech at the annual Jackson Hole symposium later this week for guidance on the central bank’s tightening plans. Meanwhile, markets are currently undecided on whether the Fed would deliver a third straight 75 basis point or a milder half-percentage point rate increase in September.2022-08-24T04:42:14.46
