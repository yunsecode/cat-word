Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States climbed 15.3 percent in the week ended March 27th 2020, after declining 29.4 percent in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications rose 25.5 percent while applications to purchase a home dropped 10.8 percent. Meantime, the average fixed 30-year mortgage rate decreased 35bps to 3.47 percent, the lowest on record.2020-04-01T11:02:00
