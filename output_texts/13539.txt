Title: US Import Prices Rise for 1st Time in 9 Months
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Rise for 1st Time in 9 MonthsUnited States Import Prices MoMImport prices in the United States increased 0.2 percent in March from February of 2016, rebounding from an upwardly revised 0.4 percent fall in the previous period but lower than market expectations of a 1 percent gain. It is the first increase in nine months, driven by a 4.9 percent surge in imported fuel prices which offset a 0.1 percent fall in nonfuel prices, namely consumer goods, foods, feeds, and beverages and capital goods. Year-on-year, import prices declined 6.2 percent.2016-04-12T13:40:01.217
