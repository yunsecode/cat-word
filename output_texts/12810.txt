Title: US Stocks Close Higher Ahead of Election Results
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher Ahead of Election ResultsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks finished higher on Tuesday, as investors awaited the results of the US presidential election. The Dow Jones Industrial Average gained 73 points, or 0.4%, to 18,332; the S&P 500 rose 8 points, or 0.4%, to 2,140; and the Nasdaq Composite ended up 27 points, or 0.5%, at 5,193.2016-11-08T21:15:18.237
