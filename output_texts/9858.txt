Title: US NY Empire State Manufacturing Index Below Expectations
Tag: NY Empire State Manufacturing Index

US NY Empire State Manufacturing Index Below ExpectationsUnited States NY Empire State Manufacturing IndexThe New York Empire State Manufacturing Index in the United States fell 12.4 points from the previous month to 10.9 in December of 2018 and missing market expectations of 20. The reading pointed to the lowest gain in manufacturing in the NY state since May last year, as shipments (21 from 28), inventories (7.1 from 10.9), new orders (14.5 from 20.4), average workweek (8 from 9.2), prices paid (39.7 from 44.5) and prices received (12.8 from 13.1) eased. On the other hand, employment (26.1 from 14.1) increased. Looking ahead, firms remained fairly optimistic about the six-month outlook, though optimism was slightly more tempered than in November.2018-12-17T13:33:00
