Title: US Stocks Surge to New Records
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge to New RecordsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street’s green start the year continued on Wednesday 3 January 2018, as FOMC minutes showed policymakers expecting tax legislation to boost growth, with most participants reiterating their support for continuing a gradual tightening. All three major indices closed at fresh records, with the Dow Jones gaining 99 points or 0.4% to 24923; the S&P 500 climbing 17 points or 0.6% to 2713; and the Nasdaq jumping 59 points or 0.8% to 7066.2018-01-03T21:13:42
