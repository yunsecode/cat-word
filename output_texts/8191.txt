Title: US 30-year Treasury Yield at 2-1/2-Month High, 5Y/30Y Gap Widest Since 2017
Tag: 30 Year Bond Yield

US 30-year Treasury Yield at 2-1/2-Month High, 5Y/30Y Gap Widest Since 2017 United States 30 Year Bond YieldThe yield on the 30-year US government bond rose to 1.5% on Tuesday, its highest level since mid-March, amid hopes of an economic recovery and less interventions from the Federal Reserve in the market for long-dated government debt. At the same time, the yield on the 5-year bond was little changed at 0.3%, resulting in a five-year/30-year 118bps gap, the widest on a closing basis since 2017. 2020-06-03T12:05:00
