U.S. Inflation Rate Rises to 3.6% in MayUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased  0.2 percent in May on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on June 15. Over the last 12 months, the all  items index increased 3.6 percent before seasonal adjustment.2011-06-15T13:39:41.28
