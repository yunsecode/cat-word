Title: US Stocks Pare Gains to Close Mixed on China Tariffs News
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Pare Gains to Close Mixed on China Tariffs NewsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed practically flat on Friday 14 September 2018 after a green start, as President Trump reportedly told aides to proceed with tariffs on US $200 billion more in Chinese products, despite recent attempts to restart trade talks. The Dow Jones edged up 9 points or less than 0.1% to 26155. The S&P 500 inched up 1 points or less than 0.1% to 2905. In contrast, the Nasdaq lost 4 points or 0.1% to 8010. 2018-09-14T20:16:20.833
