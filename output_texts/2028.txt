Title: Global Bond Yields Retreat
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Global Bond Yields RetreatUnited States Government Bond 10YBond yields in the US and Europe were falling on Tuesday, as investors were hoping that the impact of high-interest rates on economic growth will force central banks to slow down monetary tightening. The yield on the 10-year Treasury note was down by around 15 bps to 4.08%. Meanwhile, Britain's 10-year gilt fell for a 2nd session to 3.6% as markets hoped new PM Sunak could offer more predictability and stability.2022-10-25T14:07:44.81
