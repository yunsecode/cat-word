Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 3rd Straight Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 3rd Straight WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States went up 1.055 million barrels in the week ended August 5th, 2016, following a 1.413 million increase in the previous week. It was the third consecutive rise while markets were expecting a 1.5 million drop. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks fell by a higher-than-expected 2.807 million barrels, after declining by 3.262 million in the previous week.2016-08-10T15:33:05.01
