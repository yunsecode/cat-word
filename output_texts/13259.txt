Title: US Import Prices Rise Less Than Expected
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Rise Less Than ExpectedUnited States Import Prices MoMImport prices in the United States increased by 0.2 percent on the month in June 2016, after rising 1.4 percent in May, missing market expectations of 0.5 percent gain. The advance was driven by higher fuel prices (+6.2 percent), as prices of petroleum (+6.4 percent) and natural gas (+5.2 percent) went up sharply. Meanwhile, a decline in nonfuel prices (-0.3 percent), dragged lower by cost of capital goods prices (-0.3 percent) and consumer goods, excluding automobiles (-0.2 percent), weighed on growth. Despite the recent advances, import prices remain down on a yearly basis, falling 4.8 percent for the year ended in June.2016-07-13T13:39:30
