Title: US Shares Set to Open in the Green
Tag: Stock Market

US Shares Set to Open in the GreenUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures were modestly higher on Wednesday after all three major US stock indexes ended in the red Tuesday amid worries the equity markets are overvalued and fears about the impact of the spread of the Delta strain. In recent data, the annual inflation rate in the US eased to 5.3% in August from a 13-year high of 5.4% reported in June and July, matching market expectations. Meantime, small business optimism in the country rebounded slightly in August. On the political front, House Democrats released their proposed tax increases on Monday, including a rise in the corporate tax rate to 26.5% from 21%, a 3-percentage-point surtax on people making over $5 million, and increase capital-gains taxes. Such tax increases aim to cover the $3.5 trillion new spending plan. The proposal is set to be voted this week by the Democrats.  The Dow Jones fell 292 points or 0.8%; the S&P 500 lost 0.6% and the Nasdaq Composite retreated 0.5%.
