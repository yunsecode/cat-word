Title: US Unemployment Rate Falls To Near 10-Year Low
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Unemployment Rate Falls To Near 10-Year LowUnited States Unemployment RateUS unemployment rate fell to 4.4 percent in April 2017 from 4.5 percent in the previous month, better than market expectations of 4.6 percent. It was the lowest jobless rate since May 2007, as the number of unemployed persons declined by 146 thousand to 7.1 million and the labor force participation rate edged down to 62.9 percent from an 11-month high of 63 percent in March.2017-05-05T12:30:53
