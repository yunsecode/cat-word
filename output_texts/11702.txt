Title: US Trade Gap Lower than Expected
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Gap Lower than ExpectedUnited States Balance of TradeThe trade deficit in the United States widened slightly to USD 43.7 billion in July of 2017, following a downwardly revised USD 43.5 billion gap in June. It compares with market expectations of a bigger USD 44.6 billion shortfall. Exports declined 0.3 percent and imports fell at a slower 0.2 percent. The trade gap with China reached an 11-month high and the one with the EU was the highest in 8 months.
