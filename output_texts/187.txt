Title: US Futures Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures LowerUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were lower on Thursday, with contracts on the Dow Jones falling almost 100 points, the S&P 500 0.4% and the Nasdaq 100 0.7%, as investors anticipate interest rates will rise further. The Federal Reserve kept the fed funds rates steady yesterday, in line with expectations, but warned further tightening would be necessary. Traders are now bracing for two more 25bps increases this year, with the first possibly as soon as July. Investors will also keep a close eye on a batch of economic data due today, including the claims report, retail sales and industrial production.
