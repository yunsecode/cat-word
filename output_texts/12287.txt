Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 2.7 percent in the week ended March 17th 2017, following a 3.1 percent rise in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went down 3.3 percent and applications to purchase a home decreased 2.1 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates were unchanged at 4.46 percent, the highest since April of 2014.2017-03-22T11:02:08.483
