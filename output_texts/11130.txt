Title: US Jobless Claims Beat Forecasts
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Beat ForecastsUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits dropped by 9 thousand to 221 thousand in the week ended February 3rd 2018, well below market expectations of 232 thousand. It is the lowest value in three weeks. The 4-week moving average which removes week-to-week volatility fell to the lowest since March 1973.2018-02-08T13:33:36
