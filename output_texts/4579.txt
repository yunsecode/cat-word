US Bond Yield Rally Pauses United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note edged lower for a 3rd straight session to 1.74% on Wednesday, after touching a 2-year high of 1.8% early in the week, as investors await the US inflation report due early in the morning and after Fed Chair Powell remarks before the Senate dashed expectations for a more hawkish approach.
