US Crude Stocks Fall for 5th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 3.576 million barrels in the week ending December 24th, a fifth consecutive period of declines and compared with market forecasts of a 3.143 million drop, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed.
