US Economy Adds 222K Jobs In JuneUnited States Non Farm PayrollsNon farm payrolls in the United States increased by 222 thousand in June of 2017, above an upwardly revised 152 thousand in May and compared to market expectations of 179 thousand. It is the highest figure in four months as employment increased in health care, social assistance, financial activities, and mining.2017-07-07T12:31:35
