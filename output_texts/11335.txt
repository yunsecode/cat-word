Title: US Current Account Gap Lowest in 3 Years
Tag: Current Account

US Current Account Gap Lowest in 3 YearsUnited States Current AccountThe US current account deficit decreased to USD 100.6 billion or 2.1 percent of GDP in the third quarter of 2017 from an upwardly revised USD 124.4 billion in the second quarter or 2.6 percent of GDP. Figures came better than market expectations of a USD 116.8 billion shortfall. It was the smallest current account gap since the third quarter of 2014, as secondary income gap decreased by USD 10 billion to USD 23.2 billion and the goods deficit also fell by USD 6.1 billion to USD 195.3 billion. Meantime, the primary income surplus increased by USD 6.5 billion to USD 57.0 billion and the services surplus widened by USD 1.2 billion to USD 60.9 billion.
