Title: US Trade Deficit Widens Slightly In January
Tag: Balance Of Trade

US Trade Deficit Widens Slightly In JanuaryUnited States Balance Of TradeTotal January exports of $192.5 billion and imports of $231.6 billion resulted in a goods and services deficit of $39.1 billion, up from $39.0 billion in December, revised. January exports were $1.2 billion more than December exports of $191.3 billion. January imports were $1.3 billion more than December imports of $230.3 billion.2014-03-07T13:48:46.687
