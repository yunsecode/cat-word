Title: US Stocks Pull Back
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Pull BackUnited States Stock MarketWall Street reversed early gains and traded lower in another volatile trading session as investors remain very concerned about the impact of the coronavirus on the US economy. Also, the stay at home order issued by California is seen as an important move to contain the spread of the disease. On Friday, the Federal Reserve expanded its asset purchase program to include municipal bonds. The Dow Jones was down 686 points, or 3.4%, the S&P 500 dropped 83 points, or 3.4% and the Nasdaq fell 182 points, or 2.5% around 3 PM NY time.2020-03-20T16:31:00
