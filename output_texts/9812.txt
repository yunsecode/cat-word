Title: US Pending Home Sales Disappoint
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales DisappointUnited States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned US homes slumped 7.7 percent from a year earlier in November 2018, following a 6.7 percent drop in the previous month. It marks the eleventh consecutive month of declines in pending home sales and the steepest since April of 2014. The biggest fall was recorded in the West (-12.2 percent), followed by the Midwest (-7 percent), South (-7.4 percent) and Northeast (-3.5 percent). On a month-over-month basis, sales fell 0.7 percent, following a 2.6 percent drop in October and missing market expectations of a 0.7 percent advance. 2018-12-28T15:07:00
