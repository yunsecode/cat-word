Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 2nd week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 2nd weekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 1.854 million barrels in the week ended November 10th, 2017, following a 2.237 million rise in the previous period and compared with market expectations of a 2.2 million decline. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went up by 0.894 million, following a 3.312 million drop in the previous week and beating market expectations of a 0.919 million decrease.2017-11-15T15:32:36
