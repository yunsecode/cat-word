Title: Crude Oil Stocks Increase by 4.15 Million Bbl
Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

Crude Oil Stocks Increase by 4.15 Million BblUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeAPI Crude Oil Stock in the United States increased by 4.15 Million Bbl last week on September 23, up from a change of 1.04 Million Bbl the previous week.
