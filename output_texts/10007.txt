Title: US 10-Year Yield Falls after Midterm Elections
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Yield Falls after Midterm ElectionsUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note fell to 3.178% on Wednesday from a high of 3.25% overnight after the US midterm elections resulted in a split Congress with Democrats winning the House and Republicans keeping the Senate, making it more difficult to pass further economic measures such as tax cuts and infrastructure spending plans.2018-11-07T09:38:02.89
