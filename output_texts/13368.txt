Title: US Unit Labor Costs Up 4.5% in Q1
Tag: Labour Costs

US Unit Labor Costs Up 4.5% in Q1United States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the nonfarm business sector in the United States increased 4.5 percent in the first quarter of 2016, higher than a 4.1 percent gain earlier estimated but lower than an upwardly revised 5.4 percent growth in the previous period. Hourly compensation increased 3.9 percent while productivity fell 0.6 percent, final figures showed. Unit labor costs increased 3.0 percent over the last four quarters.2016-06-07T13:38:06
