Title: US Factory Growth Lowest since 2017
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Factory Growth Lowest since 2017United States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI fell to 52.5 in March of 2019 from 53 in February and below market expectations of 53.6, preliminary estimates showed. The reading pointed to the lowest expansion in factory activity since June of 2017 amid softer rises in output, new orders and employment. Also, the latest expansion of production volumes was only modest and the least marked since June 2016.  2019-03-22T13:50:05.073
