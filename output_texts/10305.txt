Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall Again: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall Again: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States declined 1.7 percent in the week ended August 24th 2018, after rising 4.2 percent in the previous week which was the first increase in six weeks, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home went down 0.9 percent and refinance applications decreased 3 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 3bps to 4.78 percent.2018-08-29T11:04:11.153
