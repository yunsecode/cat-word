Title: US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories unexpectedly fell by 3.394 million barrels in the week ended May 13th, after an 8.487-million-barrel addition in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 1.383 million rise. Also, crude stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, delivery hub fell by 2.403 million barrels, after a 1.379-million-barrel increase. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks shrank by 4.779 million barrels, the most since mid-October 2021 and much more than forecasts of a 1.333 million barrel drop; and inventories of distillate fuels, which include diesel and heating oil, went up 1.235 million barrels.2022-05-18T14:35:00
