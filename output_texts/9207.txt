Title: US Producer Prices Rise 0.1% in May
Tag: Producer Price Inflation MoM

US Producer Prices Rise 0.1% in MayUnited States Producer Price Inflation MoMProducer prices for final demand in the US edged up 0.1 percent from a month earlier in May 2019 after increasing 0.2 percent in April, as an increase in services costs was partially offset by declines in energy and food prices. Excluding food and energy, producer prices were 0.2 percent higher in May, following a 0.1 percent gain in April. Year-on-year, the PPI climbed 1.8 percent, slowing from April's 2.2 percent advance; while the core index rose 2.3 percent, slightly below the previous month's 2.4 percent.2019-06-11T12:35:00
