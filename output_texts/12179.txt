Title: US Chicago Fed National Activity Falls Sharply In March
Tag: Chicago Fed National Activity Index

US Chicago Fed National Activity Falls Sharply In MarchUnited States Chicago Fed National Activity IndexThe Chicago Fed National Activity Index in the United States fell to 0.08 in March of 2017 from a downwardly revised 0.27 in the previous month and well below market expectations of 0.3. The contribution from employment-related indicators declined to 0.02 from 0.2; sales, orders, and inventories edged down to 0.07 from 0.09. In addition, the contribution from production-related indicators was unchanged at 0.04 and the one from personal consumption and housing remained negative (-0.05 from -0.06).2017-04-24T12:38:09.137
