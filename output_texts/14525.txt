Title: US ISM Manufacturing PMI Accelerates for a Second Consecutive Month
Tag: Business Confidence

US ISM Manufacturing PMI Accelerates for a Second Consecutive Month United States Business ConfidenceIn March of 2014, the ISM PMI registered 53.7 percent, an increase of 0.5 percentage point from February's reading of 53.2 percent, indicating expansion in manufacturing for the 10th consecutive month, due to a rebound in the production subindex and a rise in new orders.2014-04-01T14:10:01.33
