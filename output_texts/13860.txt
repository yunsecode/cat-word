Title: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index At 5-Month High
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index At 5-Month HighUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index The Richmond Fed Manufacturing Activity Index increased to 6 in December following three consecutive declines and pointing to an expansion in manufacturing output in the US fifth district, including the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia. The volume of new orders picked up in December, while shipments flattened. Manufacturing employment strengthened this month and average wages rose moderately.2015-12-22T15:05:25
