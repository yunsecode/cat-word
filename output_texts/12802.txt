Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States went up by 2.4 million barrels in the week ended November 4th, 2016, following a 14.42 million jump in the previous period and above market expectations of a 1.33 million increase. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks decreased by 2.8 million barrels while markets were expecting a 1 million fall.2016-11-09T15:33:55
