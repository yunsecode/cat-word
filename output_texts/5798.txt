Title: Nasdaq, S&P 500 Notch Records
Tag: Stock Market

Nasdaq, S&P 500 Notch RecordsUnited States Stock MarketThe tech-heavy Nasdaq and the S&P 500 finished in record territory on Monday as investors moved to the safety trade of big tech amid concerns over the latest increase in coronavirus infections. On top of that, investors appeared quite reluctant to open new positions ahead of Friday’s closely watched US payrolls report for June after Fed policymakers at their last meeting stressed a commitment to pursuing full employment. Meanwhile, the blue-chip Dow lost more than 150 points, with Boeing Co tumbling nearly 4% amid fears that the spread of the delta variant could prompt renewed travel restrictions.
