Title: US New Home Sales Rise For 2nd Month
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Rise For 2nd MonthUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States increased 0.8 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 610 thousand in June of 2017 from a downwardly revised 605 thousand in May. The figure came below market expectations of 615 thousand. Sales rose in the Midwest and the West but fell in the South and were flat in the Northeast.
