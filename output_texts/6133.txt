Title: US 10-Year Treasury Note Yield Falls Below 1.6%
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Note Yield Falls Below 1.6%United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note dropped to 1.56% on Tuesday, easing further from an over two-week high of 1.69% hit on Thursday, as inflation concerns eased following a disappointing report on US manufacturing activity and after US Treasury Secretary Janet Yellen said on Sunday that Biden's plans for infrastructure would not boost prices. Investors now brace for a busy week for US economic data including the ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI, foreign trade balance and April payrolls. Last week, investors welcomed US President Biden's $1.8 trillion stimulus package proposal and the dovish stance of the Federal Reserve, as well as upbeat data showing the US GDP increased at a 6.4% annualized rate last quarter and weekly jobless claims dropped to an over year low.2021-05-04T08:18:00
