Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall The Most In 5-1/2-Months
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall The Most In 5-1/2-MonthsUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States shrank 4.1 percent in the week ended May 12th 2017, after rising 2.4 percent in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the biggest fall since the week ended November 25th 2016. Refinance applications declined 5.7 percent and applications to purchase a home went down 2.7 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates were recorded at 4.23 percent for the third week.2017-05-17T11:05:54.24
