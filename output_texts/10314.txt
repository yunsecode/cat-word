Title: US Stocks Rise on NAFTA News
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on NAFTA NewsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Monday, with the S&P and the Nasdaq reaching new record highs after news that the US and Mexico reached a preliminary deal on NAFTA and an announcement is expected later in the day. The Dow Jones jumped 0.6%, the S&P 500 gained 0.5% and the Nasdaq traded 0.6% higher around 5 minutes after the opening bell.
