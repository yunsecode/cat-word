Title: US Job Openings at 5-Month Low of 5.5M
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings at 5-Month Low of 5.5MUnited States Job OffersJob openings in the United States decreased by 345,000 to 5.5 million in May of 2016 compared to an upwardly revised 5.845 million in the previous month and below market expectations of 5.7 million. The number of positions waiting to be filled fell in wholesale trade (-104,000), other services (-98,000), and real estate and rental and leasing (-53,000). The number of job openings decreased for total private (-353,000) and was little changed for government (+8,000). Among regions, openings fell in the South (-152,000) and the Midwest (-120,000).2016-07-12T15:13:19
