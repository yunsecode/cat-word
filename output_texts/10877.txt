Title: US Stocks Plunge on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Plunge on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on 6 April 2018 amid renewed concerns of a trade war between the US and China after President Trump proposed an additional $100 billion tariff on Chinese products. In addition, the March jobs report showed the economy added 103 thousand jobs, well below forecasts of 193 thousand. The Dow Jones lost 572 points or 2.3% to 23933. The S&P 500 plunged 58 points or 2.2% to 2604. The Nasdaq plummeted 161 points or 2.3% to 6915.2018-04-06T20:03:05
