Title: US Durable Goods Orders Slump 5.1%
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Slump 5.1%United States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods shrank 5.1 percent in December of 2015, the biggest decline since August of 2014 and following a revised 0.5 percent drop in November. The plunge came much higher than expected, due to a 12.4 percent decline in transportation orders. Excluding transportation, new orders decreased 1.2 percent and without defense, orders went down 2.9 percent. 2016-01-28T13:37:21
