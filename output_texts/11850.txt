Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise 0.4% in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise 0.4% in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States edged up 0.4 percent in the week ending July 21st, 2017, following a 6.3 percent jump in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications rose 3.4 percent while applications to purchase a home fell 2.2 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate declined 5bps to 4.17 percent.2017-07-26T11:01:54.73
