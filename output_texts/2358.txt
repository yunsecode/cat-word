Title: US Stocks Edge Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge HigherUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks were slightly higher on Tuesday, as investors take a breath after a two-day slide prompted by Fed Chair remarks at the Jackson Hole symposium that the central bank will continue to raise interest rates to fight inflation despite a slowing economy. The Dow Jones added almost 100 points, the S&P 500 0.3% and the Nasdaq 0.6%. Consumer discretionary, financials and tech sectors were among the top performers. On the other hand, shares of Twitter were down almost 1% after Elon Musk cited a whistle-blower claims as a new reason to terminate the $44 billion takeover. Traders now await the CB consumer confidence and the JOLTS report due today.2022-08-30T13:39:00
