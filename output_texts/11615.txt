Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down in September
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down in September United States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States fell to 95.1 in September of 2017 from 96.8 in August. It was also lower than a preliminary reading of 95.3. Future expectations and current financial conditions of households declined mainly due to the impact of hurricanes. 2017-09-29T14:22:20.277
