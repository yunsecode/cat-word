Title: US Stocks Surge
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge United States Stock MarketThe Dow added over 300 points on Tuesday, and the S&P 500 and Nasdaq jumped roughly 1.5% and 2.5%, respectively, as a softer-than-expected reading for inflation reinforced bets that the Federal Reserve will soon slow its aggressive tightening campaign. The producer price index, a gauge of prices for final demand products, rose 0.2% over the previous month in October, while analysts expected a 0.4% gain. The report also showed the PPI coming in softer than expected on a year-on-year basis and in its core reading. Speculation about a possible policy pivot was also encouraged by Fed Vice Chair Lael Brainard, who signaled that the central bank could soon slow the pace of its interest rate increases. The market movement came in tandem with a weaker dollar and softening Treasury yields, with prompted a rally in high-growth and tech stocks.
