Title: US Stocks Revert Deep Losses to Close Higher on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Revert Deep Losses to Close Higher on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Friday 10 May 2019, as investors reacted positively from President Trump comments that conversations with China over trade policy will continue, paring deep losses earlier in the session. The Dow Jones gained 114 or 0.4%. The S&P 500 climbed 11 points or 0.4%. The Nasdaq edged up 6 points or 0.1%.2019-05-10T20:19:00
