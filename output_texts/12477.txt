US Home Prices Rise Faster Than Expected: Case-ShillerUnited States Case Shiller Home Price Index YoYThe S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller composite index of 20 metropolitan areas in the US rose 5.3 percent year-on-year in November 2016, following a 5.1 percent increase in the previous month and beating market consensus of a 5.1 percent gain. Prices in Seattle rose the most (+10.4 percent), followed by Portland (+10.1 percent) and Denver (+8.7 percent). Meanwhile, the national index, covering all nine US census divisions, rose by 5.6 percent from the previous year, up from 5.5 percent last month. On a monthly basis, prices increased by 0.2 percent after edging up by 0.1 percent in October and in line with expectations.2017-01-31T14:09:50.177
