Title: US Stock Futures Drop
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures DropUnited States Stock MarketWall Street's main indexes looked set to start the week in the red, amid concerns over reopening the US economy too soon after data from the World Health Organization showed the US recorded the highest one-day death toll between Thursday and Friday. Weighing further on sentiment were renewed tensions between the US and China after President Trump threatened to impose new tariffs on China over the coronavirus crisis. On Friday, the Dow Jones retreated 622 points or 2.6% to 23,724. The S&P 500 shed 82 points or 2.8% to 2831. The Nasdaq lost 285 points or 3.2% to 8605. 2020-05-03T23:31:00
