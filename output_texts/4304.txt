US 10 Year Bond Yield Approches 2%United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note increased to above 1.96% on Wednesday, the highest since August 2019, as investors await US inflation data on Thursday. A hot inflation number could strengthen the case for a more aggressive Fed stance.
