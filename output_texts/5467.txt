Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Bounces Back to 1-Month High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Bounces Back to 1-Month HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note rose above 1.37% on Thursday, its highest since mid-July, as another strong initial claims report pointed to a continued labor market recovery. Yields have been also bolstered by comments from two Federal Reserve officials on Tuesday that suggested the central bank could soon reduce or taper its asset purchases.2021-08-12T12:45:00
