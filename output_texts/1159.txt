Title: US CPI Rises 0.5% in January, Most in Three Months
Tag: Inflation Rate MoM

US CPI Rises 0.5% in January, Most in Three MonthsUnited States Inflation Rate MoMConsumer prices in the US increased 0.5% month-over-month in January of 2023, the most in three months, and in line with the market forecast. The index for shelter was by far the largest contributor (0.7%), accounting for nearly half of the monthly all-items increase followed by food (0.5%), gasoline (2.4%), and natural gas (6.7%). Increases were also seen for prices of motor vehicle insurance (1.4%), recreation (0.5%), and apparel (0.8%).2023-02-14T13:44:00
