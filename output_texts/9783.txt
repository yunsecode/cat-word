Title: US Services Growth Below Forecasts: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Growth Below Forecasts: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States fell to 57.6 in December of 2018 from 60.7 in November and below market expectations of 59. The reading pointed to the weakest expansion in the services sector in five months, amid a slowdown in business activity and employment. Respondents also indicated that there is still concern about tariffs despite the hold on increases by the US and China and that capacity constraints have lessened; however, employment-resource challenges remain.2019-01-07T15:05:00
