Title: US Economy Adds Less Jobs Than Expected in September
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds Less Jobs Than Expected in SeptemberUnited States Non Farm PayrollsTotal nonfarm payroll employment in the United States increased by 156,000 in September of 2016, less than a upwardly revised 167,000 in August and below market expectations of 175,000. The change for July was revised down from 275,000 to 252,000.  Employment gains occurred in professional and business services and in health care. 2016-10-07T12:32:56
