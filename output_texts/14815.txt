Title: U.S. Economy Grew 1.8% in Q1
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

U.S. Economy Grew 1.8% in Q1United States GDP Growth RateUnited States GDP increased at an annual rate of 1.8 percent in the first quarter of 2011 (that is, from the fourth quarter to the first quarter) according to the advance estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.  In the fourth quarter, real GDP increased 3.1 percent.2011-04-28T13:39:12.667
