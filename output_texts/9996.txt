Title: US Consumer Sentiment Falls Less than Expected
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Falls Less than ExpectedUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US fell to 98.3 in November of 2018 from 98.6 in October but slightly higher than market expectations of 98. It is the lowest reading in three months, mainly due to a fall in consumer expectations, preliminary estimates showed. Data was collected until Wednesday night so there was only a one-day overlap after the mid-term election results were known by consumers.2018-11-09T15:05:00
