Title: US Retail Sales Rise Less Than Expected
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Rise Less Than ExpectedUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States increased 0.6 percent month-over-month in December of 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.2 percent rise in November and below market expectations of a 0.7 percent gain. Sales of autos and gasoline rebounded. Considering the three months to December, retail sales went up 1.6 percent and for full 2016 sales rose 3.3 percent, higher than 2.3 percent in 2015.2017-01-13T13:31:58
