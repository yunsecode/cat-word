Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 12-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 12-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's preliminary reading on the overall index of consumer sentiment came in at 85.7 in September, down from 91.9 in August and below market expectations. Consumer sentiment fell for the third straight month to the lowest figure in a year, as current and future expectations worsened while inflation expectations rose slightly. 2015-09-11T15:09:26.83
