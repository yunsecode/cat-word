Title: US Initial Jobless Claims Rise To 7-Week High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Initial Jobless Claims Rise To 7-Week HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 15 thousand to 258 thousand in the week ended March 18th 2017, above market expectations of 240 thousand. It is the highest reading in seven weeks. The 4-week moving average that removes week-to-week volatility increased by 1,000 to 240,000. 2017-03-23T12:32:47
