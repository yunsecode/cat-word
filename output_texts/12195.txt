Title: US Stocks Fall On Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall On TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Tuesday, mainly dragged down by financials and health shares after earnings reports for Goldman Sachs disappointed and Johnson & Johnson revenues were lower than anticipated. The Dow Jones lost 85 points or 0.4%, the S&P 500 declined 6 points or 0.3% and the Nasdaq traded 13 points or 0.2% lower around 8 minutes after the opening bell. 2017-04-18T13:42:57.293
