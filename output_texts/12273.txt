Title: US Private Sector Activity Slows In March: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Activity Slows In March: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe Markit flash US composite PMI decreased to 53.2 in March of 2017 from 54.1 in February, the lowest in six months. Both services (52.9 from 53.8) and manufacturing (53.4 from 54.2) slowed due to softer business activity and and lower payrolls. 2017-03-24T14:02:59
