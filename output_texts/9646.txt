Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 4th Straight Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 4th Straight Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States decreased 3.7 percent in the week ending February 8th 2019, following a 2.5 percent fall in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It was the fourth consecutive week of declines, as refinance applications went down 0.1 percent and applications to purchase home dropped 6.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate decreased by 4bps to 4.65 percent.2019-02-13T12:01:54.537
