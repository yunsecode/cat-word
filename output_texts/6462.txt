US Crude Oil Stocks Unexpectedly Jump United States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil inventories in the US rose by 1.912 million barrels in the week ended March 19th, 2021, after increasing by 2.396 million in the previous week and compared with market forecasts of a 0.272 million fall, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories went up by 0.203 million barrels, well below market expectations of a 1.186 million rise.2021-03-24T14:33:00
