Title: US Home Builder Sentiment Lowest in Over 2 Years
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Home Builder Sentiment Lowest in Over 2 YearsUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index for the US slumped to 60 in November of 2018 from 68 in October and well below market expectations of 67. It is the lowest reading since August of 2016. The subindex for current single-family home sales fell to 67 from 74; the gauge for home sales over the next six months went down to 65 from 75 and prospective buyers slipped to 45 from 53.2018-11-19T15:07:19.163
