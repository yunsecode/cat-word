Title: US Stocks Close At Record High For 3rd Session
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close At Record High For 3rd SessionUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed at a record high for the third session on Monday, led by gains in financial shares. Stocks extended last week gains after President Trump said he will make an announcement on “phenomenal” tax reform in the next few weeks. In addition, investors await Fed Yellen testimony before Congress on Tuesday for further clues on the timing of Fed's next rate hike. The Dow Jones gained 143 points or 0.7% to 20,412.16; the S&P 500 went up 12 points or 0.5% to 2,328.25 and the Nasdaq closed 30 points or 0.5% higher at 5,763.96. 2017-02-13T21:18:45
