Title: US Crude Inventories Fall More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories dropped by 7.355 million barrels in the June 11th week, a fourth consecutive period of decline and the largest since the last week of April, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. The number also compared with forecasts of a 3.29 million fall. Meantime, gasoline inventories rose by 1.954 million barrels, defying market consensus of a 0.614 million drop. 2021-06-16T14:43:00
