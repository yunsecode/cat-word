Title: US Jobless Rate Ticks Up to 4%

US Jobless Rate Ticks Up to 4%United States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate edged up to 4.0 percent in January of 2022, little changed from December's new pandemic low but slightly above market expectations of 3.9 percent.
