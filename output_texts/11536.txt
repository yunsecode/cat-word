Title: US New Home Sales at Near 10-Year High
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales at Near 10-Year HighUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States jumped 18.9 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 667 thousand in September of 2017, beating market expectations of a 0.9 percent decline. It is the highest value since October of 2007 and the largest percentage gain since January of 1992. Sales rose in all four regions with those in the South surging 25.8 percent after a 1 percent fall in August which was partly the result of Hurricanes Harvey and Irma.2017-10-25T14:07:35
