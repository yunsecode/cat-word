Title: US Stocks Fall on Lower Oil Prices
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on Lower Oil PricesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Wednesday, extending losses for the second day as oil prices continued to retreat. The Dow Jones went down 140 points or 0.6%, the S&P 500 lost 14 points or 0.5% and the Nasdaq traded 63 points or 1% lower around 20 minutes after the opening bell.
