Title: Wall Street Opens Slightly Higher Ahead of Yellen
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Slightly Higher Ahead of YellenUnited States Stock MarketEquities in the US opened slightly in the green on Friday led by gains in financials and consumer discretionary sectors while energy stocks led losses amid a retreat in oil prices. Investors remain cautious ahead of Fed Chair Janet Yellen speech later in the day where further clues regarding a June rate hike are expected and after the second estimate of Q1 GDP was revised upwards to 0.8%. The Dow Jones added  0.17% or 29 points, the S&P gained 0.21% or 5 points and the Nasdaq rose 0.38% or 19 points around 60 minutes after the opening bell.2016-05-27T15:39:39.5
