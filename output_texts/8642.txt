Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Rise Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 1.954 million barrels in the week ended March 13th of 2020, following a 7.664 million gain in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 3.256 million gain, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went down by 6.180 million barrels after decreasing by 5.048 million in the previous week and compared with market consensus of a 2.89 million decline.2020-03-18T14:33:01.193
