US Consumer Sentiment Confirmed at 14-Month HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe Thomson Reuters/University of Michigan's final reading on the overall index of consumer sentiment came in at 84.6 in September, unchanged from the preliminary estimate and up from 82.5 in August.
