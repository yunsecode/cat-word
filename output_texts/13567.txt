Title: US Economy Adds 215K Jobs in March
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds 215K Jobs in MarchUnited States Non Farm PayrollsTotal nonfarm payroll employment increased by 215,000 in March of 2016, lower than an upwardly revised 245,000 in the previous month but beating market expectations of 205,000. Employment gains occurred in retail trade, construction, and health care, while job losses occurred in manufacturing and mining.2016-04-01T13:31:46
