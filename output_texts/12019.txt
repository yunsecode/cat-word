Title: US Business Growth At 3-Month High: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Business Growth At 3-Month High: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI was recorded at 53.6 in May of 2017, down from a preliminary reading of 53.9 but above 53.2 in April. It is the highest reading in three months as services rose faster than in the previous month (53.6 from 53.1) while manufacturing eased slightly (52.7 from 52.8). The trend for the first five months of 2017 have been stronger than for the same period in 2016. 2017-06-05T13:57:52
