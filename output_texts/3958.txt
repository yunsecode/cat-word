Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Rise: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 1.08 million barrels in the week ended April 1st of 2022, following a 3.01 million barrels fall in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.056 million decline. It was the first increase in US crude inventories in three weeks, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed.
