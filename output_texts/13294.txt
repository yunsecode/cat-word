Title: US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised Slightly Down
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised Slightly DownUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe final Markit Manufacturing PMI for the United States came in at 51.3 in June of 2016, slightly below a preliminary reading of 51.4 but above 50.7 in May. Yet, it is still the highest reading in three months. Production increased and new business grew the most since March while payroll numbers expanded moderately and stocks of purchases fell further. 2016-07-01T14:51:54.773
