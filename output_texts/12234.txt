Title: US Composite PMI Revised Down In March: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Composite PMI Revised Down In March: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe final Markit US Composite PMI fell to 53 in March of 2017 from a preliminary reading of 53.2 and 54.1 in February. It is the lowest reading in six months as both manufacturing (53.3 from 54.2) and services (52.8 from 53.8) rose at a slower pace. New business also eased to the lowest in six months. 2017-04-05T13:53:49.233
