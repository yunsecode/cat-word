Title: US Jobless Claims Lowest in 15 Years
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Lowest in 15 YearsUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing new claims for unemployment benefits was 262 thousand in the week ended April 25th, a decrease of 34,000 from the previous week's level. This is the lowest level for initial claims since April 15, 2000 when it was 259 thousand.2015-04-30T13:41:26.903
