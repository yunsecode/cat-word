Title: Unemployment Rate Declines to 5.3% in June
Tag: Unemployment Rate

Unemployment Rate Declines to 5.3% in JuneUnited States Unemployment RateTotal nonfarm payroll employment increased by 223,000 in June, and the unemployment rate declined to 5.3 percent, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today.  Job gains occurred in professional and business services, health care, retail trade, financial activities, and in transportation and warehousing. 2015-07-02T13:49:59.103
