Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Rise Unexpectedly
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Rise UnexpectedlyUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 0.007 million barrels in the week ended December 28th 2018, following a 0.046 million decrease in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 3.086 million decline. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories increased by 6.890 million barrels, while markets expected a 1.967 million gain.2019-01-04T16:01:48.637
