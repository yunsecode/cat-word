Title: US Retail Sales Rise Much Less than Anticipated
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Rise Much Less than AnticipatedUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States edged up 0.1 percent month-over-month in September of 2018, the same as in the previous month and well below market expectations of a 0.6 percent rise. Spending at restaurants and bars went down 1.8 percent, the most since December of 2016.2018-10-15T12:34:47.933
