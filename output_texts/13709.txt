Title: US Import Prices Fall 1.1% in January
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Fall 1.1% in JanuaryUnited States Import Prices MoMImport prices in the United States decreased by 1.1 percent month-on-month in January of 2016, the same as in the previous month but beating market forecasts of 1.4 percent fall, mainly driven by lower fuel prices (-12.4 percent). Also, non-fuel prices declined by 0.2 percent. Compared to the same month in the previous year, import prices dropped 6.2 percent.2016-02-12T13:37:46
