Title: Dow and S&P 500 Cut Gains After Powell Speech
Tag: Stock Market

Dow and S&P 500 Cut Gains After Powell SpeechUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones rose 40 points and the S&P 500 was up just 4 points in the afternoon trade on Tuesday, erasing most of their gains from earlier in the session. Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell called for additional fiscal and monetary stimulus to help the US economy amid the pandemic crisis, saying that too little support would lead to a weak recovery, while the risks of overdoing it seem to be smaller. Meanwhile, traders await clarity on Trump's political agenda after the President left the hospital on Monday, where he was treated for coronavirus. On the stimulus front, there's still no deal between Democrats and Republicans and the House Speaker Nancy Pelosi and the Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin plan to speak again today. Meanwhile, the Nasdaq traded more than 0.5% lower as news emerged that a House panel is about to propose reforms in the tech sector to prevent companies like Amazon or Apple from owning marketplaces and selling their own products on them.2020-10-06T16:27:00
