Title: US Stocks Rise
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks RiseUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Friday, amid news that the Fed is considering reducing its balance sheet sooner than anticipated and after Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin said both the US and China were “making a lot of progress” in trade talks. The Dow Jones went up 0.7%, the S&P 500 rose 0.6% and the Nasdaq traded 0.6% higher around 8 minutes after the opening bell. 2019-01-25T14:50:00
