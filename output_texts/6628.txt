Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Falls for 2nd Day
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Falls for 2nd DayUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury retreated for the 2nd consecutive session to 1.36% on Wednesday, after touching 1.39% on Monday, but remained close to levels not seen in a year. Treasuries have been rallying since the end of January amid inflationary concerns arising from strong economic activity and further fiscal stimulus. The gap between 5 and 30-year yields touched the highest level in more than six years on Monday, also reflecting investor expectations of a strong economic recovery. Still, Fed officials recently reiterated inflation is expected to move up and that they were still prepared to keep their easy monetary policy on track while full growth would take some time.2021-02-24T09:15:00
