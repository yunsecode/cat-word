U.S. Annual Inflation Down to 1.6% in JanuaryUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) was unchanged in January on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today.
