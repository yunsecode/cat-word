Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 6-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 6-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States fell to 94.4 in January of 2018 from 95.9 in December and below expectations of 97, according to preliminary figures. It was the lowest reading in six months, as consumers evaluated current economic conditions less favorably amid uncertainties about the delayed impact of the tax reforms.2018-01-19T15:04:48
