Title: US Jobless Claims Rise to 265K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise to 265KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits was 265,000 in the week ended March 12th, an increase of 7,000 from the previous week's revised level of 258,000 while staying below market expectations of 268,000. This marks 54 consecutive weeks of initial claims below 300,000, the longest streak since 1973.2016-03-17T12:32:26
