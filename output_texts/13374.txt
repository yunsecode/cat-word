Title: US Markit Services PMI Revised Slightly Up
Tag: Services PMI

US Markit Services PMI Revised Slightly UpUnited States Services PMIThe final Markit US Services PMI came in at 51.3 in May of 2016, slightly up from a preliminary reading of 51.2 but lower than 52.8 in the previous month. Activity and new business growth slowed, backlogs of work were lower and payrolls recorded the weakest rise since January last year. In addition, business optimism eased to a post-crisis low.2016-06-03T14:59:21.957
