Title: US Stocks Rise after Canada Joins Trade Deal
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise after Canada Joins Trade DealUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Monday after Canada agreed to join a trade deal with the US and Mexico, replacing the NAFTA agreement and ending months of uncertainty. The Dow Jones was up 0.6%, the S&P 500 gained 0.5% and the Nasdaq traded 0.7% higher around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2018-10-01T13:43:00
