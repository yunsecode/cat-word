Title: U.S. GDP Revised Down to 1% in Q2
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

U.S. GDP Revised Down to 1% in Q2United States GDP Growth RateU.S. real gross domestic product increased at an annual rate of 1.0 percent in the second quarter of 2011, (that is, from the first quarter to the second quarter), according to the "second" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.  In the first quarter, real GDP increased 0.4 percent.2011-08-26T13:36:52.693
