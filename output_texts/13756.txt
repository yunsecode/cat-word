Title: US Stocks Open Higher on BoJ
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Higher on BoJUnited States Stock MarketMarkets in Wall Street started higher on Friday, following earlier gains in Asia and Europe after the Bank of Japan adopted a negative interest rate policy. The Dow Jones was up 94 points or 0.59%, the S&P 500 gained 0.5% and the Nasdaq traded 0.35% higher around 5 minutes after the open. 2016-01-29T14:40:03
