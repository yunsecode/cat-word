Title: US Stock Futures Rise
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Rise United States Stock MarketUS stock index futures gained late Sunday as investors bet on more fiscal stimulus to drive a post-pandemic economic revival, even as coronavirus infections continued to rise in many US states. House Speaker Nancy Pelosi set a Tuesday deadline for the White House to agree on further fiscal stimulus and said she was optimistic about negotiations after speaking with Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin during the weekend. Investors await another batch of earnings reports, with IBM, Netflix and Tesla set to release results this week. Last week, the Dow gained 0.1% and the S&P 0.2%, notching their third straight weekly gain. The Nasdaq added 0.8%, posting a four-week winning streak.2020-10-18T22:32:00
