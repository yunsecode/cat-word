Title: Wall Street Close Mostly Flat on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Close Mostly Flat on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketUS equities finished mostly flat on Thursday after the Bank of England cut rates for the first time since 2009 to a record low of 0.25 percent and despite rising oil prices. Also, investors await July jobs report due Friday. The Dow Jones Industrial Average and the S&P 500 index finished near the flatline while the Nasdaq Composite Index advanced 6.51 points, or 0.13%.2016-08-04T21:19:36.957
