Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 3.577 million barrels in the week ended November 23rd 2018, following a 4.851 million increase in the previous week. It compares with market expectations of a 0.769 million rise. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories declined by 0.764 million barrels after a 1.295 million drop in the previous week.2018-11-28T15:34:00
