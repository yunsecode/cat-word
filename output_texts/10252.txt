Title: US Inflation Rate Slows to 2.7%
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Slows to 2.7% United States Inflation RateAnnual inflation rate in the US fell to 2.7 percent in August of 2018 from 2.9 percent in July and below market expectations of 2.8 percent. It is the lowest reading in four months amid a slowdown in cost of fuel, gasoline and shelter. On a monthly basis, consumer prices increased 0.2 percent, the same as in July and lower than forecasts of 0.3 percent.2018-09-13T12:32:00
