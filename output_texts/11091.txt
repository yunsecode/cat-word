Title: US Treasuries Oversold Abroad in December
Tag: Capital Flows

US Treasuries Oversold Abroad in DecemberUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors sold USD 119.3 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments in December of 2017, after buying a downwardly revised USD 33.5 billion in the previous month. The figure contrasted sharply with market expectations of USD 37.0 billion of sold assets. Meanwhile, foreigners bought USD 27.3 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying USD 57.5 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors sold USD 16.0 billion of Treasures in December after selling USD 18.8 billion in the previous month.2018-02-15T21:09:02
