Title: US Mortgage Applications Sink as Rates Move Higher
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Sink as Rates Move HigherUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the US sank 7.7% in the week ended February 10th, 2023, erasing a 7.4% jump in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to refinance a home loan plunged 12.5% and those to purchase a home loan went down 5.5%. Meanwhile, the average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($726,200 or less) jumped 21bps to 6.39%, the highest in five weeks. “Mortgage rates increased across the board last week, pushed higher by market expectations that inflation will persist, thus requiring the Federal Reserve to keep monetary policy restrictive for a longer time,” said Joel Kan, MBA’s vice president and deputy chief economist.2023-02-15T12:07:00
