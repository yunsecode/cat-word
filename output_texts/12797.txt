Title: US Stocks Continue to Rally
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Continue to Rally United States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Thursday with the Dow Jones touching an all time high, as investors shrug off the initial impact of Trump's victory and expectations for a fiscal boost increased. The Dow Jones was up 135 points or 0.7%, the S&P 500 added 12 points or 0.6% and the Nasdaq traded 33 points or 0.6% around 6 minutes after the opening bell.2016-11-10T14:47:04
