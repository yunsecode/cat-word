Title: Dollar Weakens Slightly
Tag: Currency

Dollar Weakens Slightly United States CurrencyThe dollar index traded slightly lower around 93.5 on Thursday as investors' risk appetite improved amid renewed hopes of further stimulus in the US. Earlier in the week, President Trump urged the Congress to pass a new coronavirus aid bill to support airlines and companies and fund the Paycheck Protection Program. On the political front, recent polls showed Joe Biden is widening his lead against President Trump.2020-10-08T07:26:00
