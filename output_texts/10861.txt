Title: US Stocks Close Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close LowerUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately down on Wednesday 11 April 2018, after minutes from the last FOMC meeting showed that the outlook for the economy strengthened and inflation is expected to move up in coming months, and after consumer prices increased 2.4 percent year-on-year in March 2018, above 2.2 percent in February. The Dow Jones lost 219 points or 0.9% to 24189. The S&P 500 slipped 15 points or 0.6% to 2642. The Nasdaq declined 25 points or 0.4% to 7069. 2018-04-11T20:06:12
