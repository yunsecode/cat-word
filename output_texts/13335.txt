Title: Wall Street Extends Losses On Oil
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Extends Losses On OilUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US opened lower on Thursday, extending a 5-day slide and dragged down by energy shares amid a retreat in oil prices. The Dow Jones lost 0.7% or 126 points, the S&P 500 slid 0.75% or 16 points and the Nasdaq fell 0.8% or 38 points around 20 minutes after the opening bell.2016-06-16T15:00:40.48
