Title: US Economy Adds Only 142K Jobs In September
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds Only 142K Jobs In SeptemberUnited States Non Farm PayrollsAmerican payrolls increased by 142,000 in September, well below market expectations while the unemployment rate was unchanged at 5.1 percent, matching forecasts. Job gains occurred in health care and information, while mining employment fell. Thus far in 2015, job growth has averaged 198,000 per month, compared with an average monthly gain of 260,000 in 2014.
