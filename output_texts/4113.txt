Title: US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly Fall: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly Fall: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 2.597 million barrels in the week ended February 25th, following a 4.515 million rise in the previous period and compared with market forecasts of a 2.748-million-barrel gain, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. Crude stocks at the Cushing, Oklahoma, delivery hub declined by 0.972 million, while gasoline inventories decreased by 0.468 million barrels against market expectations of a 1.375-million-barrel drop. 2022-03-02T15:35:00
