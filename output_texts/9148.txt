Title: US Stocks Tumble as Trump Announces New Tariffs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Tumble as Trump Announces New TariffsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks reversed early gains and plunged on Thursday afternoon after President Donald Trump announced the US will be imposing 10% tariffs on $300 billion of Chinese goods starting September 1st, despite ongoing trade talks between the countries. The Dow Jones fell 0.7% to 26,676; the S&P 500 decreased 0.9% to 2,955; and the Nasdaq went down 0.9% to 8,110 around 2PM EDT.2019-08-01T18:06:00
