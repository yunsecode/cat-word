Title: US Jobless Claims Down to 14-Year Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Down to 14-Year LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending October 11, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 264,000, a decrease of 23,000 from the previous week's unrevised level of 287,000. This is the lowest level for initial claims since April 15, 2000 when it was 259,000. 2014-10-16T13:38:04.987
