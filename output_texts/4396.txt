Title: 10-Year Treasury Rebounds From Near 1-Week Low
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

10-Year Treasury Rebounds From Near 1-Week LowUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury note was around 1.8%, still below 2020-highs of 1.9% hit in mid-January, as investors continue to wonder if the Fed will need to tight monetary policy faster than anticipated to tackle rising inflation. The US central bank indicated last week that it would likely hike interest rates in March, with markets pricing in five quarter-point rate hikes this year. Meanwhile, jobs openings topped forecasts and remained near record highs, as employers were still having a difficult time filling positions. The payrolls and ADP releases will also be in the spotlight this week.2022-02-01T16:26:00
