US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly RiseUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States went up by 3.803 million barrels in the week ended July 27th 2018, following a 6.147 million drop in the previous week. It compares with market expectations of a 2.794 million decrease. Meanwhile, stocks of gasoline went down by 2.536 million barrels while markets expected a 1.288 million drop. 2018-08-01T14:32:28.553
