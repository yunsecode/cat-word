Title: US Trade Gap at 9-Year High
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Gap at 9-Year HighUnited States Balance of TradeThe US recorded a USD 53.1 billion trade gap in December of 2017, following a downwardly revised 50.4 billion gap in November and compared to market expectations of a USD 52 billion shortfall. It is the biggest trade gap since October of 2008 as imports rose 2.5 percent to a  new record high of 256.5 billion,and exports grew 1.8 percent to a new record high of 203.4 billion. Considering full 2017, the US trade gap widened 12.1 percent to USD 566.0 billion, the highest since 2008.2018-02-06T13:36:09
