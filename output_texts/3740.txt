Title: Mortgage Applications Fall for 7th Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

Mortgage Applications Fall for 7th WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the US declined 8.3% in the week ending April 22nd, a 7th straight week of falls, as mortgage rates continue to march higher, data from the MBA showed. The refinancing index sank 9% and the purchase index 7.6%. The average contract rate on a 30-year fixed-rate mortgage increased to 5.37%, the highest since 2009, from 5.20%. “The recent decrease in purchase applications is an indication of potential weakness in home sales in the coming months,” said Joel Kan, an MBA economist. “The ARM share of applications last week was over 9% by loan count and 17% based on dollar volume. At 9%, the ARM share was double what it was three months ago, which also coincides with the 1.5 percentage point increase in the 30-year fixed rate,” noted Kan.2022-04-27T11:05:00
