Title: ISM New York Index at 15-Month High
Tag: Ism New York Index

ISM New York Index at 15-Month HighUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions index rose to 53.5 in July of 2020 from 39.5 in the previous month. It was the highest reading since April of 2019, as the city's economy recovers from the coronavirus hit. Employment, a seasonally adjusted index, rose 16.8 points to reach a 6 month high of 50.3. Current revenues rose 13.2 points to a 5 month high of 43.8 and expected revenues rose 7.2 points to 39.6, marking the second consecutive month of increases and a 5 month high. Meantime, quantity of purchases fell 2.7 points to 42.0 from the 7 month high of 44.7 reported in June.2020-08-04T14:02:00
