Title: Fed Chair Powell Reiterates the Need for Further Rate Hikes
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Chair Powell Reiterates the Need for Further Rate Hikes United States Interest RateFed Chair Powell said at the ECB Forum on Central Banking that more restriction is coming and that interest rates will need to rise again this year, seeing two more rate hikes, at least. At the same time, Powell added that he wouldn’t take consecutive-meeting rate hikes off the table. Also, Fed Chair noted the US economy is quite resilient and a recession, although possible, is not the most likely case. The Fed left the target for the funds rate unchanged at 5%-5.25% in June but signaled rates may go to 5.6% by year-end if the economy and inflation do not slow down more. 19 days ago
