Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 14-Month High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 14-Month HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe Thomson Reuters/University of Michigan's consumer sentiment increased to a preliminary reading of 84.6 in September from 82.5 in August. It is the highest figure since July of 2013, as consumers are more confident about future economic conditions.2014-09-12T15:07:55.62
