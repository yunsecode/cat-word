Title: Dollar Eases from Two-Decade Peak
Tag: Currency

Dollar Eases from Two-Decade PeakUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index weakened below the 114 mark as easing Treasury yields combined with a general risk appetite prompted some profit-taking after a rally that saw the US currency hit its highest level since May 2002. Still, hawkish remarks from Federal Reserve officials and the rejection of a possible currency agreement among major economies limited further downside momentum. A chorus of Fed policymakers indicated the central bank’s determination to do what is necessary to bring down inflation, even at the risk of a recession and further market volatility. Meanwhile, White House National Economic Council Director Brian Deese rejected the idea of another 1985-type currency accord to weaken the dollar. 2022-09-28T15:16:41.877
