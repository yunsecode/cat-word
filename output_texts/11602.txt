Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall Much More than Anticipated
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall Much More than AnticipatedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 6.023 million barrels in the week ended September 29th, 2017, following a 1.846 million decline in the previous period. It is the biggest drop in eight weeks and much worse than market expectations of a 0.756 million fall. Gasoline inventories went up by 1.644 million, the biggest gain since the first week of August.2017-10-04T14:35:15.473
