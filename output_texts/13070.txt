Title: US Labour Productivity Falls 0.6% in Q2
Tag: Productivity

US Labour Productivity Falls 0.6% in Q2United States ProductivityLabor productivity in the nonfarm business sector in the United States decreased at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 0.6 percent on quarter in the three months to June of 2016, more than preliminary estimates of a 0.5 percent drop and following a 0.6 percent decline in the previous period, final figures showed. Output increased 1.1 percent, lower than earlier estimates of 1.2 percent and hours worked went up 1.7 percent, also below 1.8 percent in the preliminary figures. Year-on-year, productivity fell 0.4 percent, the biggest decline in three years. 2016-09-01T13:46:36.097
