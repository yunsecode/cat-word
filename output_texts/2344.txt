Title: US Labor Costs Revised Lower in Q2
Tag: Labour Costs

US Labor Costs Revised Lower in Q2United States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the US non-farm business sector jumped by 10.2 percent in the second quarter of 2022, below initial estimates of a 10.8 percent rise and following a 12.6 percent surge in the first quarter. It reflects a 4.4 percent decrease in hourly compensation and a 4.1 percent decline in labor productivity. Unit labor costs increased by 9.3 percent over the last four quarters, the largest increase since 1982.
