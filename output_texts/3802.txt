Title: Wall Street Trades Mostly Lower
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Trades Mostly LowerUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks pared some losses on Monday but mostly remained in the negative territory with Dow Jones down more than 200 points in the afternoon trade, S&P declining almost 1%, and Nasdaq attempting to hold small gains. Investors remained concerned about Covid-19 lockdowns in China and inflation currently running at a 4-decades high, which has already prompted the Federal Reserve to signal big increases in the Fed funds rate this year. Among single stocks, Twitter shares climbed more than 6% on reports the company is in advance talks to sell itself to Elon Musk and a deal could be reached as soon as Monday. Meanwhile, financials and energy shares led the sell-off with crude oil prices retreating more than 6%.2022-04-25T17:44:00
