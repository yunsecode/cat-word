Title: US Philadelphia Manufacturing Index Above Forecasts
Tag: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index

US Philadelphia Manufacturing Index Above ForecastsUnited States Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index in the United States fell 0.7 points to 22.2 in October 2018, above market expectations of 20. A slowdown was seen in new orders (down 2 points to 19.3) while shipments (up 5 points to 24.5) and employment (up 2 points to 19.5) increased. Meantime, the current inventories index, unfilled orders and delivery times were almost unchanged with all index near zero. Expectations for the next six months remained optimistic.2018-10-18T12:33:00
