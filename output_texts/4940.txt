Title: US Q3 Labor Productivity Falls the Most Since 1981
Tag: Productivity

US Q3 Labor Productivity Falls the Most Since 1981United States ProductivityNonfarm labor productivity in the US fell an annualized 5.0 percent in the third quarter of 2021, more than market expectations of a 3.0 percent drop and after an upwardly revised 2.4 percent gain in the prior period. Output increased 1.7 percent (vs 8.5 percent in Q2) and hours worked jumped 7.0 percent (vs 5.9 percent). Year-on-year, nonfarm business sector labor productivity decreased 0.5 percent, following an upwardly revised 1.9 percent advance in the second quarter.
