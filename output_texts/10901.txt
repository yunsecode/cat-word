Title: US Stocks Rebound on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Tuesday 3 April 2018, recovering from a sell-off in the previous session after tech shares led by Amazon slumped amid growing concerns of tight regulation. The Dow Jones closed up 389 points or 1.7% to 24033. The S&P 500 jumped 33 points or 1.3% to 2614. The Nasdaq climbed 71 points or 1.0% to 6941.2018-04-03T20:10:44.247
