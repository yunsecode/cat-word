Title: US New Home Sales Fall Much More than Expected
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Fall Much More than ExpectedUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States slumped 6.9 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 607 thousand in January of 2019, following an upwardly revised 652 thousand in December and worse than market expectations of 620 thousand. Sales declined in the South, the Midwest and the Northeast but rose in the West.2019-03-14T14:04:00
