Title: US Durable Goods Orders Rise 3.4% In April
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Rise 3.4% In AprilUnited States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods surged 3.4 percent in April from March of 2016, following an upwardly revised 1.9 percent rise in the previous period and much better than market expectations of a 0.5 percent increase. It is the biggest gain since January, boosted by demand for transportation equipment. Excluding transportation, new orders rose 0.4 percent and without defense orders increased 3.7 percent. In contrast, capital goods orders fell 0.8 percent, down for the third straight month.2016-05-26T13:40:05
