Title: US Jobless Claims Rise to 6-Month High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise to 6-Month HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits was 293,000 in the week ended January 16th, an increase of 10,000 from the previous week's revised level of 283,000 while staying way above market expectations of 278,000. It was the highest value since July of 2015.2016-01-21T13:33:46
