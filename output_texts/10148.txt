Title: US Stocks Extend Declines
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend DeclinesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded in the red on Friday as Treasury yields continued to rise after the jobs report showed the US economy added a less-than-expected 134 thousand jobs but unemployment fell to the lowest in near 49 years. Also, nonfarm payrolls for August were revised sharply higher. The Dow Jones fell 0.8%, the S&P went down 0.7% and the Nasdaq traded 1.4% lower around 11:50 AM NY time.2018-10-05T15:55:00
