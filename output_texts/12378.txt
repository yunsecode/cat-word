Title: US New Home Sales Rise Less Than Expected
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Rise Less Than ExpectedUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States went up 3.7 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 555,000 in January of 2017. It follows a downwardly revised 535,000 in the previous month, but lower than market expectations of 570,000.2017-02-24T15:07:47
