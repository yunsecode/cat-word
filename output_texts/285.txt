Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Rise: API
Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Rise: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the US rose by 5.202 million barrels in the week ended May 26th, 2023, after a 6.799 million barrels decline in the previous week. It pointed to the biggest weekly gain since the week ended on February 24th, 2023, beating market expectations of a 1.22 million barrel decrease, data from the API's Weekly Statistical Bulletin showed.
