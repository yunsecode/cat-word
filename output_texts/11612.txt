Title: US ISM Manufacturing PMI Highest Since 2004
Tag: Business Confidence

US ISM Manufacturing PMI Highest Since 2004United States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management’s Manufacturing PMI in the US rose to 60.8 in September of 2017 from 58.8 in August, beating market expectations of 58. It is the highest reading since May of 2004, boosted by a rise in new orders, production and employment.
