Title: US Factory Activity Rises Slightly
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Factory Activity Rises SlightlyUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe flash Markit manufacturing PMI for the United States came in at 51.4 in March of 2016, slightly up from 51.3 in the previous month but lower than market expectations of 51.8. Output and new business increased at a slightly faster pace than in February while factory gate prices decreased for the second straight month.2016-03-22T13:52:41
