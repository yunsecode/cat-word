Title: US Exports Hits 7-Month High in June
Tag: Exports

US Exports Hits 7-Month High in JuneUnited States ExportsTotal exports from the United States edged up 0.3 percent to $183.2 billion in June from May of 2016, following a 0.1 percent drop in the previous period. Exports of goods increased $0.5 billion to $120.4 billion, led by foods, feeds, and beverages; capital goods, civilian aircraft; consumer goods, artwork,antiques, stamps, and other collectibles.2016-08-05T14:17:18
