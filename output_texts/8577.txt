US Stocks Rally PausesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded lower on Friday, ending a 3-day rally as investors remain very concerned about the economic impact of the coronavirus and wonder if announced stimulus measures will be enough to offset it. The number of coronavirus infections continues to increase and the US now has more cases than China.
