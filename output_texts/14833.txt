Title: US Unemployment Rate Falls to 9.4% in December
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Unemployment Rate Falls to 9.4% in DecemberUnited States Unemployment RateThe unemployment rate fell by 0.4 percentage point to 9.4 percent in December, and nonfarm payroll employment increased by 103,000, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. Employment rose in leisure and hospitality and in health care but was little changed in other major industries.
