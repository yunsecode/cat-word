Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Edges Lower, Inflation Eyed
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Edges Lower, Inflation EyedUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note decreased slightly to 3.5%, following its peak of 3.52% early in the week, as investors assess the monetary policy outlook and await the US inflation report on Wednesday. The payrolls report for April showed a larger-than-expected job gain and rising wage inflation, which has tempered fears of a recession and made it more difficult for the Federal Reserve to justify cutting interest rates. At the same time, traders are continuously evaluating the health of the banking sector, with the Fed's Loan Officer Survey illustrating a persistent tightening of lending conditions, primarily due to the impact of the Fed's monetary policy as opposed to the recent banking turmoil. In the meantime, short-term Treasury yields have increased as the debt-ceiling deadline draws near.2023-05-09T08:46:00
