Title: US Stocks Close Lower Ahead Of Trump Inauguration
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Lower Ahead Of Trump InaugurationUnited States Stock MarketUS equities closed in the red on Thursday as investors remained cautious ahead of President-elect Donald Trump's inauguration on Friday. The Dow Jones retreated 72 points, or 0.4%, to 19,732, the fifth straight session of decline; the S&P 500 closed down 8 points, or 0.4%, at 2,264; and the Nasdaq fell 16 points, or 0.3%, at 5,540.2017-01-19T21:21:09
