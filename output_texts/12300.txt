Title: Philadelphia Fed Manuf Index Falls Less Than Expected
Tag: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index

Philadelphia Fed Manuf Index Falls Less Than ExpectedUnited States Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index fell to 32.8 in March of 2017 from 43.3 in February but beating market expectations of 30. Prices paid (40.7 from 29.9) and received (20.6 from 10.6) rose significantly. Meantime, new orders (38.6 from 38 in February), shipments (32.9 from 28.6) and employment (17.5 from 11.1) increased and inventories recovered (11.8 from -4.7).2017-03-16T12:43:44
