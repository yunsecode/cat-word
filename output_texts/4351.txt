Title: US Job Cuts Marginally Higher in January
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Job Cuts Marginally Higher in JanuaryUnited States Challenger Job CutsJob cuts announced by US-based companies rose marginally by 0.1 percent from a month earlier but plunged 76 percent from a year earlier to 19,064 in January 2022, as companies struggled with staff reluctance to get vaccinated. Around 5,757 of layoffs were due to companies firing workers who refused to take the COVID-19 vaccines, bringing the total of job cuts due to vaccine refusal to 13,931 since June 2021. Companies in the health care sector announced the most job cuts (5,053), followed by the warehousing (3,051), services (1,786), entertainment & leisure (1,691), and consumer product manufacturing businesses (1,432). 2022-02-03T12:33:00
