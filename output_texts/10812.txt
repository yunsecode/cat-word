Title: US Richmond Fed Index Unexpectedly Falls in April
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

US Richmond Fed Index Unexpectedly Falls in AprilUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Manufacturing Activity Index in the US fifth district including the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia slumped to -3 in April of 2018 from 15 in March. It is the lowest reading since September of 2016 and well below market expectations of 16. A contraction was seen in shipments (-8 from 15); new orders (-9 from 17) and backlogs of orders (-4 from 10). Also, prices paid rose faster (2.43 from 2.39) while prices received slowed (1.46 from 1.54). The local business conditions index fell to -1 from 7, suggesting worsening conditions for District manufacturers. On the other hand, faster rises were recorded for finished goods inventories (15 from 5); number of employees (12 from 11); and wages (27 from 22).2018-04-24T14:11:08.687
