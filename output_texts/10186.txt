Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Lower in September
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Lower in SeptemberUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US was revised down to 100.1 in September of 2018 from a preliminary reading of 100.8. Yet, the reading was higher than 96.2 in August and topped 100.0 for only the third time since January of 2004. The single issue that was cited as having a potential negative impact on the economy was tariffs. Concerns about the negative impact of tariffs were cited by nearly one-third of all consumers in September.
