Title: US Mortgage Applications Down 3.3%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Down 3.3%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 3.3 percent in the week ended March 11th 2016 from the previous period, following a 0.2 percent gain last week while fixed 30-year mortgage rates increased 5 bps to 3.94 percent, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home rose 0.3 percent while refinance applications shrank 5.6 percent. 2016-03-16T11:05:11.13
