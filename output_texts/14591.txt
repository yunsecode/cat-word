Title: US New Home Sales Fall From 5-Year High
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Fall From 5-Year High United States New Home SalesIn November of 2013, sales of new single-family houses in the US were at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 464,000. This is 2.1 percent below the revised October rate of 474,000, the highest in 5 years, but is 16.6 percent above the November 2012 estimate of 398,000.2013-12-24T15:09:33.263
