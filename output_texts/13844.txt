Title: US Markit PMI Slightly Below Expectations
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Markit PMI Slightly Below ExpectationsUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe final Markit US Manufacturing PMI came in at 51.2 in December of 2015, lower than a preliminary reading of 51.3 and a final of 52.8 in the previous month. It is the lowest figure since October of 2012 as output and new business growth slowed and input costs declined again.2016-01-04T14:51:32.307
