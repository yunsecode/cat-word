Title: US Housing Starts Fall 8.8% in March
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Fall 8.8% in MarchUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the Unites States fell 8.8 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1089 thousand in March of  2016, compared to an upwardly revised 1194 thousand in the previous month. It is the lowest reading since October last year and well below market expectations of 1170 thousand. Building permits slumped 7.7 percent, hitting a one-year low.2016-04-19T13:33:11
