US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than Expected: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States surged by 4.784 million barrels in the week ended April 22nd of 2022, following a 4.496 million barrels slump in the previous week and well above market expectations of a 2.000 million rise.  It was the largest injection of crude oil US inventories since the week ended February 18th, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed.2022-04-26T20:48:00
