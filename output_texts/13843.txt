Title: US Factory Activity at 6-1/2-Year Low
Tag: Business Confidence

US Factory Activity at 6-1/2-Year LowUnited States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management’s Manufacturing PMI decreased to 48.2 in December of 2015 from 48.6 in November. It is the second consecutive month of contraction in the manufacturing activity and the lowest reading since June of 2009 due to falls in new orders, production, employment and raw materials inventories.2016-01-04T15:06:26
