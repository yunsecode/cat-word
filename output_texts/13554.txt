Title: US Stocks Open Lower for 2nd Day
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Lower for 2nd Day United States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Tuesday, following earlier losses in Europe and Asia as volatile oil prices and weak economic data released increased worries over global growth outlook. The Dow Jones fell 82 points or 0.46%, the S&P 500 was down 0.68% and the Nasdaq lost 0.69% around 7 minutes after the open.2016-04-05T14:43:03.99
