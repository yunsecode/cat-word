Title: US Net Capital Outflow $152.9B in September
Tag: Capital Flows

US Net Capital Outflow $152.9B in September United States Capital FlowsOverseas investors sold $152.9 billion of US assets in September of 2016, including short-dated instruments after buying a downwardly revised $40 billion in August. Foreigners also sold $63.3 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying a downwardly revised $28.5 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors sold a record $76.6 billion of US Treasuries, after selling $24.8 billion in August. 2016-11-16T21:09:35
