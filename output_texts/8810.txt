Title: US Stocks Close Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher United States Stock MarketWall Street finished in the green on Monday, with both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq reaching new record highs, led by the tech sector. Amazon shares jumped 2.6% to finish above $2100 for the first time, extending gains since January 30th when the company reported better-than-expected earnings. Meanwhile, Lyft and Uber rallied after the latter’s strong Q4 earnings while Apple shares declined amid concerns the coronavirus outbreak will hurt production of the iPhone. Yet, mounting concerns over the coronavirus’s impact on the Chinese economy were more than offset by a brighter US economic outlook. The Dow Jones added 174 points or 0.6% to 29277. The S&P 500 rose 24 points or 0.7% to 3352. The Nasdaq climbed 108 points or 1.1% to 9628.
