Title: US Stocks Extend Gains on Trade Optimism
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend Gains on Trade OptimismUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks rose on Friday on hopes that a US-China trade deal could be reached after Beijing said that the country's negotiators are maintaining "effective communication" on trade. The Dow Jones gained 122 points, or 0.5%; the S&P 500 advanced 13 points, or 0.4%; and the Nasdaq added 27 points, or 0.3% around 30 minutes after the opening bell.2019-08-30T13:30:00
