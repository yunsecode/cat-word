Title: US Stocks Fall on Deutsche Bank Concerns

US Stocks Fall on Deutsche Bank ConcernsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed lower on Thursday weighed down by sharp fall in Deutsche Bank shares after a report that some hedge funds clients have started shifting business to other banks.
