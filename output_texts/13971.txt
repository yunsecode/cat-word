Title: US Challenger Job Cuts Down 14% MoM in October
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Challenger Job Cuts Down 14% MoM in OctoberUnited States Challenger Job CutsThe number of announced corporate layoffs in the United States fell by 14 percent to 50504 in October from 58877 in September of 2015. Around 13,671 layoffs were attributed to lower oil prices, the highest number in six months and 27 percent of all cuts announced during the month. Year-on-year, layoffs fell 1.3 percent. So far in 2015,  employers have announced 543,935 job cuts,  up 31 percent from the year-to-date period in 2014, with oil prices responsible for about a fifth of that.2015-11-05T12:37:46
