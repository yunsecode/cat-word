Title: US Jobless Rate Drops to Near 17-1/2-Year Low
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Drops to Near 17-1/2-Year LowUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate fell to 3.9 percent in April 2018 from 4.1 percent in the previous month, and below market expectations of 4 percent. It was the lowest rate since December 2000, as 236 thousand people exited the labor force. The number of unemployed decreased by 239 thousand to 6.35 million and employment was almost unchanged at 155.18 million.2018-05-04T12:31:14
