Title: US Stocks Fall amid Lower Yields
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall amid Lower YieldsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Wednesday, following losses in Europe and Asia as the 10-year Treasury yield fell to its lower level since September 2017 amid persistent global growth concerns and escalating trade tensions between the US and China. On Tuesday, China’s powerful planning body threatened to use rare earths exports, a main component to US technology industry. The Dow Jones dropped 0.7%; the S&P 500 fell 0.7%; and the Nasdaq declined 0.8% around 09:45 NY time.2019-05-29T13:30:00
