Title: US Jobless Claims Little Changed in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Little Changed in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending August 30, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 302,000, an increase of 4,000 from the previous week's unrevised level of 298,000. The 4-week moving average was 302,750, an increase of 3,000 from the previous week's unrevised average of 299,750.
