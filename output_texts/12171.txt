Title: US New Home Sales Rise To 8-Month High In March
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Rise To 8-Month High In MarchUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States unexpectedly jumped by 5.8 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 621 thousand in March 2017 from a downwardly revised 587 thousand in February. The figure came in well above market expectations of 583 thousand to hit the highest level since July 2016, as sales rose mostly in the Northeast region (25.8 percent) and in the West (16.7 percent).2017-04-25T14:05:01
