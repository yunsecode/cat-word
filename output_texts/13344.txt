Title: US Industrial Production Falls More than Expected
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Production Falls More than ExpectedUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial output in the United States declined 0.4 percent in May from April of 2016, following a downwardly revised 0.6 percent rise in the previous period and worse than market expectations of a 0.2 percent drop. Automobiles and electricity accounted the most for the decline, offsetting a rebound in coal mining.2016-06-15T14:20:55
