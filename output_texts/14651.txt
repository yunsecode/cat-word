Title: US GDP Growth Revised Up to 2.5% in Q2
Tag: Gdp Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Revised Up to 2.5% in Q2United States Gdp Growth RateIn Q2 of 2013, United States' real gross domestic product increased at an annual rate of 2.5 percent, up from a preliminary estimate of 1.7 percent, according to the second estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. In the first quarter, real GDP increased 1.1 percent.2013-08-29T13:43:11.113
