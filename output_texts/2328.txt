Title: 10-Year Treasury Yield Consolidates Around 3.3%
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

10-Year Treasury Yield Consolidates Around 3.3%United States Government Bond 10YThe 10-year US Treasury note yield consolidated around 3.3%, not far from an over 10-year peak of 3.5% touched in June, as investors reassessed the outlook for monetary policy as the Federal Reserve seeks to rein in soaring inflation by hiking interest rates further even as growth slows. While the US economy is slowing and some industries such as housing show signs of weakness, the labor market has remained robust and quieted fears of a recession. The latest data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics showed that the US economy added 315,000 jobs in August, exceeding analysts' forecasts. Money markets are now pricing on an almost 90% chance of another supersized 75 basis-point interest rate hike in September. 2022-09-06T09:31:00
