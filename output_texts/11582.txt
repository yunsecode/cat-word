Title: US Stocks Hit Fresh Highs as Minutes Show Divided Fed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Hit Fresh Highs as Minutes Show Divided FedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed modestly in the green on Wednesday October 11th of 2017 and major indices booked new highs, as minutes from the last FOMC meeting showed that several Fed policymakers thought that another increase in the target range later this year was likely to be warranted, despite a detailed debate about the risk of stubbornly low inflation not being transitory. The Dow Jones gained 42 points or 0.2% to 22873. The S&P 500 edged up 5 points or 0.2% to 2555. The Nasdaq advanced 16 points or 0.3% to 6604.2017-10-11T20:00:44
