Title: US Stocks Edge Up on Wednesday as Oil Climbs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Up on Wednesday as Oil ClimbsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed modestly in the green on Wednesday September 13th of 2017, as stocks of gasoline in the United States decreased by 8428 thousand barrels in the week ending September 8 of 2017, the biggest drop on record amid a severe hurricane season. Crude oil gained over 2 percent after the closing bell, nudging all major indices to fresh records. The Dow Jones gained 39 points or 0.2% to 22158.The S&P 500 inched up 1 point or less than 0.1% to 2497. The Nasdaq edged up 6 points or 0.1% to 6460. 2017-09-13T20:07:10
