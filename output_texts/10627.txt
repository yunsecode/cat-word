Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Unexpectedly
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise UnexpectedlyUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 2.072 million barrels in the week ended June 1st 2018, after a 3.62 million drop in the previous period and compared with market expectations of a 1.824 million decline. Meantime, gasoline inventories went up by 4.603 million barrels after increasing 0.534 million in the previous week, while markets were expecting a smaller 0.587 million rise.2018-06-06T14:35:15.64
