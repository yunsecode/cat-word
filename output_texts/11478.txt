Title: US Stocks Book Fresh Records amid Jobs Report
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Book Fresh Records amid Jobs ReportUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed higher on Friday November 3rd of 2017 with the three major indices booking fresh records, as non-farm payrolls in the United States increased by 261 thousand in October of 2017, compared with market expectations of a 310 thousand rise. The unemployment rate edged down by 0.1 percentage point to 4.1 percent in October, marking the lowest unemployment rate since December 2000. The Dow Jones gained 23 points or 0.1% to 23539. The S&P 500 advanced 8 points or 0.3% to 2588. The Nasdaq climbed 49 points or 0.7% to 6764. During the week, the Dow Jones jumped 0.4%; the S&P 500 0.3%; and the Nasdaq 0.9%.2017-11-03T20:12:50
