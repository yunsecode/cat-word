Title: US Factory Orders Rise 1.5% in October
Tag: Factory Orders

US Factory Orders Rise 1.5% in OctoberUnited States Factory OrdersNew orders for manufactured goods in the United States expanded by a higher-than-expected 1.5 percent month-on-month in October of 2015, following two consecutive monthly declines. It was the highest gain in four months, as demand for transportation equipment increased sharply by 7.9 percent, due to rising aircraft orders, while motor vehicle orders fell 2 percent. Despite the increase, factory activity remains weak, mainly due to a strong dollar and spending cuts in the energy sector. 2015-12-03T15:13:54
