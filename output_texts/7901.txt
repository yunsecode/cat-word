Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 1st Time in a Month
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 1st Time in a MonthUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 0.8 percent in the week ended July 24th, following three consecutive periods of increases, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Homebuyer moortgage applications were down 1.5 percent, while applications to refinance a home loan dropped 0.4 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate stood at 3.20 percent, remaining close to an all-time low of 3.19 percent hit two weeks ago.2020-07-29T11:05:00
