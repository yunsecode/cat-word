Title: US Building Permits Tumble to Over 2-Year Low
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Tumble to Over 2-Year LowUnited States Building PermitsBuilding permits in the United States tumbled 11.2 percent from a month earlier to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1.342 million in November 2022, the lowest level since June 2020 and well below market expectations of 1.485 million. Permits, a proxy for future construction, have been falling as soaring prices and rising mortgage rates hit demand and activity. Single-family authorizations declined 7.1 percent to a rate of 781 thousand, the lowest since May 2020, while the volatile multi-segment also dropped 16.4 percent to a 14-month low of 561 thousand. Permits were down in the South (-12.2 percent to 755 thousand), West (-16.4 percent to 276 thousand) and Midwest (-6.2 percent to 197 thousand), but were slightly up in the Northeast (1.8 percent to 114 thousand).2022-12-20T13:38:00
