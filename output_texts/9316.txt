Title: US Initial Jobless Claims Fall Less than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Initial Jobless Claims Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment dropped by 2 thousand to 228 thousand in the week ending May 4th 2019 from the previous week's unrevised level of 230 thousand. It compares with market expectations of 220 thousand. According to unadjusted data, the largest decreases were observed in New Jersey (-6,343), Connecticut (-3,204) and California (-1,277) while the biggest rises were recorded in New York (+9,514) and Illinois (+2,098).
