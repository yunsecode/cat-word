Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Remains Near 1-Year High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Remains Near 1-Year HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury remained near 1-year high of 1.6% on Monday after strong US jobs report on Friday and the passage of President Biden's $1.9 trillion coronavirus aid bill by the Senate on Saturday raised expectations of solid economic recovery and a boost in inflation in the coming months. 2021-03-08T00:45:10.3
