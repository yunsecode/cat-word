Title: US 10-Year Yield at Near 10-Month High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Yield at Near 10-Month HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the 10-year US Treasury rose for the 5th straight session on Wednesday to hit 2.59 percent, the highest since mid-March, after a report showed Chinese officials recommended slowing or halting US bond purchases. Also, the Bank of Japan trimmed on Tuesday the amount of its purchases of Japanese government bonds.2018-01-10T14:06:06
