Title: US Stocks End Mixed; Nasdaq Closes at 2016 High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed; Nasdaq Closes at 2016 HighUnited States Stock MarketThe S&P 500 and the Nasdaq finished slightly higher on Tuesday, while the Dow Jones closed lower, as investors remained cautious ahead of Fed’s policy decision on Wednesday while a fall in oil prices weighed on sentiment. The Dow Jones slipped 19.31 points, or 0.1%, dragged lower by shares of McDonalds, which closed 4.5% lower due to disappointing earnings' report. The S&P 500 advanced 0.70 points, or less than 0.1%; and the Nasdaq rose 12.42 points, or 0.2%, to finish at 5,111, for its highest close of 2016.2016-07-26T21:24:15
