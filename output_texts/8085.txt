Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yields at 2-Week Low
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yields at 2-Week LowUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note fell for the second session to 0.664% on Thursday, its lowest level on a closing basis since June 11th, after the US recorded more than 45.5 thousand coronavirus infections on Wednesday, the highest on record according to NBC. The IMF expects the US economy to shrink 8% in 2020, compared to a 5.9% contraction estimated in April due to the effects of the coronavirus pandemic. Recent data showed the US jobless claims rose more than expected for the second week, with more 1.48 million Americans filling for unemployment benefits; and the US economy was confirmed contracting by an annualized 5.0 percent in the first three months of the year, the most since the last quarter of 2008. 2020-06-25T13:05:00
