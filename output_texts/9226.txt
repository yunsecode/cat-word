Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Drops Below 2.1%
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Drops Below 2.1% United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note fell as much as 5.5bps to a near 21-month low of 2.0693% on Monday, amid rising expectations that the Federal Reserve will ease policy this year to support growth, amid heightened global trade tensions. Since the beginning of May, the 10-year bond yield has fallen by 38bps, the biggest monthly drop since January. Also, the 30-year bond yield decreased 4.8bps to 2.5204%, its lowest level since November of 2016 and that on the 2-year was down 8.2bps to an 18-month low of 1.8397%.  2019-06-03T13:33:00
