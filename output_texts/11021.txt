Title: US Services Growth Slows Less than Expected
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Growth Slows Less than ExpectedUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States edged down to 59.5 in February of 2018 from an over 12-year high of 59.9 in January, beating market forecasts of 59. A slowdown was seen in employment while production and new orders rose faster; price pressures eased; and the outlook for business conditions and the economy remained positive.2018-03-05T15:06:59.27
