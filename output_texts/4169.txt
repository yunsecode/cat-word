Title: US Crude Inventories Rise More than Expected: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Rise More than Expected: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 4.515 million barrels in the week ended February 18th, the most since October last year and following a 1.121 million increase in the previous period. It compares with market forecasts of a 0.442-million-barrel rise, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. Crude stocks at the Cushing, Oklahoma, delivery hub however declined by 2.049 million and gasoline inventories decreased by 0.582 million barrels, less than market expectations of a 1.331 million drop. Meanwhile, distillate stockpiles which include diesel and heating oil went down 0.585 million barrels, compared to forecasts of a 1.763-million-barrel decline.2022-02-24T16:41:43.647
