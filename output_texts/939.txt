Title: Dollar Edges Higher as Risk-Off Mood Returns
Tag: Currency

Dollar Edges Higher as Risk-Off Mood ReturnsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index edged slightly higher to around 104 on Monday, as cautiousness and risk aversion returned to the market after an initial enthusiasm over the takeover of Credit Suisse by UBS and global central banks' action to boost dollar liquidity faded and concerns over the health of the global banking system reemerged, ahead of this week's Fed monetary policy decision. The Swiss government engineered a forced takeover of Credit Suisse by UBS in response to client outflows and a dramatic selloff in the target company’s stock and bonds. Meanwhile, the Federal Reserve and five other central banks announced during the weekend coordinated action to improve liquidity in US dollar swap arrangements to maintain stability in the global financial system. At the same time, investors look ahead to the Fed’s policy decision on Wednesday, with the central bank expected to deliver a more moderate 25 bps rate increase in light of easing inflationary pressures and the recent banking turmoil.2023-03-20T08:18:00
