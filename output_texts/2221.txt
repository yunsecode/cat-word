Title: US Wholesale Inventories Growth Picks Up in August
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Growth Picks Up in AugustUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US rose by 1.3 percent month-over-month to $913.1 billion in August of 2022, picking up from a 0.6 percent rise in the prior month, preliminary estimates showed. It was the 25th consecutive month of growth in inventories, as stocks rebounded sharply for non-durable goods (1.3 percent vs -0.1 percent in July) and rose quicker for durable goods (1.3 percent vs 1.1 percent). On an annual basis, wholesale inventories grew by 25.1 percent in August, unchanged from the prior month.2022-09-28T12:38:00
