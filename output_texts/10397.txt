Title: US Jobless Rate Decreases to 3.9% in July
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Decreases to 3.9% in JulyUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate fell to 3.9 percent in July 2018 from 4.0 percent in June, matching market expectations. The jobless rate touched an 18-year low of 3.8% in May. The number of unemployed persons declined by 284,000 to 6.3 million and employment was almost unchanged at 156.0 million.2018-08-03T12:32:00
