Title: US Imports Fall from Record
Tag: Imports

US Imports Fall from RecordUnited States ImportsImports to the United States declined by USD 3.8 billion to USD 273.9 billion in April 2021, from last month's all-time high, due to lower purchases of consumer goods (down USD 2.6 billion) such as other textile apparel and household goods; toys, games, and sporting goods; and household appliances. Also, imports of automotive vehicles, parts, and engines were down USD 1.1 billion. Imports of services, however, increased USD 0.7 billion, boosted by travel and transport.2021-06-08T12:37:00
