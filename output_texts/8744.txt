Title: ISM New York Index Recovers in February
Tag: Ism New York Index

ISM New York Index Recovers in FebruaryUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions jumped to 51.9 in February of 2020 from 45.8 in the previous month, mainly due to a rise in current revenues (58.3 from 51.1). On the other hand, declines were seen for employment (49.3 from 56.1), purchases (41.7 from 43.1), prices paid (54.2 from 68.1) and expected revenues (50 from 64.3). The Six-Month Outlook fell to a 4-month low of 53.8 from 57.3.2020-03-03T15:48:03.897
