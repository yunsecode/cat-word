Title: Dollar Falls after Trump Criticizes Fed
Tag: Currency

Dollar Falls after Trump Criticizes FedUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index against a basket of major currencies continued to decline during early trading hours in Europe on Tuesday after President Trump said overnight he is not thrilled about the Fed’s decisions this year to raise interest rates and that he would criticize the Fed if it continues to raise rates.
