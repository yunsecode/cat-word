Title: US Jobless Rate Seen at 7.7% in October
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Seen at 7.7% in OctoberUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate is seen falling to 7.7 percent in October 2020, little-changed from September's 7.9 percent, a number that has been biased down by people misclassifying themselves as being “employed but absent from work.” October's jobless rate would compare with an all-time high of 14.7 percent hit in April but would still remain well above pre-pandemic levels of about 3.5 percent, as the labor market recovery from COVID-19 shock showed signs of slowing amid a lack of fiscal stimulus and spiraling new coronavirus infections.2020-11-06T09:19:00
