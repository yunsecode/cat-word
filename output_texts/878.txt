Title: US Stock Futures Rise
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures RiseUnited States Stock MarketStock futures contracts tied to the Dow Jones rose 0.8% on Wednesday, while those linked to the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 were up roughly 1% each, amid easing concerns about a banking crisis while a rally in Chinese tech stocks lifted sentiment. Michael Barr, the Federal Reserve’s vice chairman for supervision, said that SVB’s problems were due to poor risk management, suggesting it could be an isolated case. Rate-sensitive companies, including Microsoft, Alphabet, and Tesla, enjoyed some respite in premarket trading, up between 1% and 2% as yields declined. Meanwhile, Lululemon Athletica soared almost 15% after forecasting annual sales and profit above expectations. In regular trading on Tuesday, the Dow fell 0.1%, the S&P 500 lost 0.2%, and the Nasdaq dropped 0.5%. 2023-03-29T12:03:00
