Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised DownUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's final reading on the overall index of consumer sentiment came in at 90 in October of 2015, lower than a preliminary reading of 92.1 and below market expectations. Consumers were less confident over future and current conditions and inflation expectations for the next 5 to 10 years decreased. Still, the consumer confidence index increased from a final reading of 87.2 in September. 2015-10-30T14:05:57
