Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Unchanged After PCE Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Unchanged After PCE DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note rose to 1.53% on Friday, after economic data showed an unexpected stagnation in personal spending and a jump in PCE prices. The annual core PCE price inflation, the Federal Reserve's preferred gauge of inflation, rose to 3.4% in May, the highest level since the 1990s and in line with market expectations. The yield remained well above the four-month low of 1.36% touched early in the week as US Fed officials tried to reassure financial markets the recent spike in prices is temporary and there will be no immediate tightening of monetary policy.2021-06-25T13:02:00
