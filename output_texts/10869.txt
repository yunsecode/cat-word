Title: US Wholesale Inventories Rise the Most since 2013
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Rise the Most since 2013United States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the United States increased 1 percent month-over-month to USD 625.6 billion in February of 2018, following a downwardly revised 0.9 percent gain in January and slightly below a preliminary estimate of 1.1 percent. It is the biggest gain in wholesale stocks since October of 2013. Inventories rose for durable goods (1.1 percent vs 0.2 percent in January), namely machinery (1.5 percent vs 0.2 percent), professional equipment (1.7 percent vs -0.9 percent) and computer equipment (2.8 percent vs -2.1 percent) while autos went down 0.1 percent. Also, stocks rose less for non-durable goods (0.8 percent vs 2 percent) and fell for petroleum (-3.7 percent vs 3.3 percent). Wholesale stocks excluding autos, the component that goes into the GDP calculation went up 1.1 percent.2018-04-10T14:12:51.483
