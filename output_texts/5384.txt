Title: US PCE Prices Continue to Rise in July
Tag: PCE Price Index Monthly Change

US PCE Prices Continue to Rise in JulyUnited States PCE Price Index Monthly ChangeThe personal consumption expenditure price index in the United States rose 0.4 percent from a month earlier in July 2021, following a 0.5 percent gain in June, boosted by steady increases in cost for goods (0.5 percent vs 0.7 percent in June) and services (0.4 percent, the same as in June). Within goods, prices were up for nondurable (0.6 percent, the same as in June) and durable goods (0.3 percent vs 1.0 percent). Excluding food and energy, PCE prices advanced 0.3 percent in July, in line with market expectations. Year-on-year, the PCE price index advanced 4.2 percent and the core index increased 3.6 percent.2021-08-27T12:39:00
