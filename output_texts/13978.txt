Title: US Trade Deficit at 7-Month Low
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit at 7-Month LowUnited States Balance of TradeThe trade gap in the United States narrowed to USD 40.81 billion in September of 2015, compared to a downwardly revised USD 48 billion in August and the lowest since February. Exports went up 1.6 percent led by artwork, antique, stamps, jewelry and capital goods while lower prices and higher domestic oil production brought imports down. 2015-11-04T13:37:02
