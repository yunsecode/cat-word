Title: US Stocks Close With Weekly Losses
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close With Weekly LossesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed modestly higher on Friday ahead of next week’s Fed meeting. All major indices rose after upbeat jobs data cleared the way for the Fed to raise interest rates in March’s meeting. The Dow Jones closed 45 points up, or 0.2 percent, to 20,902.98. The S&P 500 increased by 8 points, or 0.3 percent, to 2,372.60. The Nasdaq gained 23 points, or 0.4 percent, to 5,861.72. All indices ended with weekly losses, with the Dow Jones falling 0.5 percent; the S&P 500 0.4 percent; and the Nasdaq 0.2 percent.2017-03-10T21:11:12
