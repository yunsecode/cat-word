US Home Prices Rise More than Expected: Case-ShillerUnited States Case Shiller Home Price Index YoYThe S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller 20-City Composite Home Price Index in the US rose 6.5 percent year-on-year in May 2018, following an upwardly revised 6.7 percent advance in April and beating market expectations of 6.4 percent. Seattle recorded the biggest increase (13.6 percent), followed by Las Vegas (12.6 percent) and San Francisco (10.9 percent). Meanwhile, the national index, covering all nine US census divisions rose 6.4 percent in May, the same pace as in the previous month.2018-07-31T13:08:33.87
