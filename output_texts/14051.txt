Title: US Jobless Claims at 4-Week High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims at 4-Week HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits was 277,000 in the week ended September 26th, an increase of 10,000 from the previous week's level of 267,000. However, claims stayed below 300,000 for the 30th straight week, the 4-week moving average decreased and continuing claims were at the lowest since November of 2000, pointing to labour market strength.2015-10-01T13:41:30
