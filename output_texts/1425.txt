Title: 10-Year Treasury Yield Falls to 1-Month Low
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

10-Year Treasury Yield Falls to 1-Month LowUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note fell towards the 4.4% mark in the third week of January, the lowest in over one month and approaching levels last seen in September, as a bigger-than-expected monthly decline in US producer prices and retail sales strengthened bets that the Federal Reserve will further slow the pace of interest rate hikes. The data furthered recent evidence that prices are slowing after consumer inflation fell for the sixth consecutive month in December. Consequently, money markets priced an over 95% chance of a softer 25bps interest rate increase in the central bank’s February decision before reaching a terminal rate of 5% by the end of the first quarter. Still, hawkish rhetoric by Fed policymakers warns that the fight against inflation is far from over.2023-01-18T13:54:34.463
