Title: US Stocks Rally amid Stimulus, Falling Claims
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally amid Stimulus, Falling ClaimsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street rallied on Thursday, with the Dow Jones and  S&P 500 soaring to fresh records as investors welcomed new stimulus, steady Treasuries and lower-than-expected claims. The House passed the $1.9 trillion stimulus bill and President Biden is expected to sign it later today. Initial jobless claims fell more than expected to a 4-month low of 712K in the first week of March. The tech-heavy Nasdaq outperformed its peers, with Facebook soaring more than 4%.2021-03-11T14:34:00
