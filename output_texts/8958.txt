Title: US Producer Prices Unchanged in November
Tag: Producer Prices Change

US Producer Prices Unchanged in NovemberUnited States Producer Prices ChangeProducer prices for final demand in the US were unchanged from the previous month in November 2019, after a 0.4 percent increase in the previous month and missing market expectations of 0.2 percent rise. Goods cost went up 0.3 percent (vs 0.7 percent in October) amid prices for meats, gasoline, chicken eggs, diesel fuel, fresh and dry vegetables, and tobacco. On the other hand, services prices moved down 0.3 percent, the most since February 2017, mainly due to food wholesaling and hospital outpatient care. Excluding food and energy, producer prices decreased 0.2 percent, against market consensus of a 0.2 percent gain. Year-on-year, the PPI rose 1.1 percent, matching October's rise, which was the smallest increase since October 2016; and the core index went up 1.3 percent. 2019-12-12T13:34:00
