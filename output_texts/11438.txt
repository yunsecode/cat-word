Title: US Chicago Fed Activity Index Highest Since 2012
Tag: Chicago Fed National Activity Index

US Chicago Fed Activity Index Highest Since 2012United States Chicago Fed National Activity IndexThe Chicago Fed National Activity Index in the United States jumped to 0.65 in October of 2017 from an upwardly revised 0.36 in September, beating market expectations of 0.2. It is the highest reading since January of 2012, mainly due to a surge in the contribution from production-related indicators (0.53 in October from 0.18 in September). The sales, orders, and inventories category (0.05 from 0.08) and employment-related indicators (0.11 from 0.13) also contributed positively though the contribution edged down. On the other hand, the contribution of the personal consumption and housing category fell to –0.04 from –0.02. The index’s three-month moving average increased to 0.28 in October from 0.01 in September.2017-11-21T13:50:05
