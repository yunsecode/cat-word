Title: Global Coronavirus Cases Surpass 2.5M
Tag: Coronavirus Cases

Global Coronavirus Cases Surpass 2.5MUnited States Coronavirus CasesReported COVID-19 cases around the world surpassed 2.5 million, of which more than 175 thousand died, and over 675 thousand recovered. In the US, the epicentre of the disease, the number of infections surpassed 820 thousand, around 32% of cases globally. Across the Atlantic, Spain, the worst-affected European country, registered 204,178 cases, followed by Italy (183,957) and France (156,522). The US is the country with more deaths caused by the virus, (43,545), followed by Italy (24,648) and Spain (21,282).2020-04-21T22:53:00
