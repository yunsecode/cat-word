Title: US Exports At 1-1/2-Year High In December
Tag: Exports

US Exports At 1-1/2-Year High In DecemberUnited States ExportsTotal exports from the United States jumped 2.7 percent month-over-month to USD 190.7 billion in December of 2016. It is the highest value since April of 2015 and follows a 0.2 percent fall in November and a 1.8 percent drop in October. Exports of goods increased 3.9 percent to USD 126.9 billion, mainly boosted by capital goods (USD 3.3 billion); civilian aircraft (USD 1.0 billion); engines for civilian aircraft (USD 1.0 billion); industrial supplies and materials (USD 0.7 billion); natural gas (USD 0.2 billion) and fuel oil (USD $0.2 billion). Exports of services increased 0.4 percent to USD 63.8 billion. Sales jumped to the European Union (10.1 percent), Japan (4.2 percent) and to OPEC (26.5 percent) but fell to China (-4.1 percent), Canada (-2.8 percent), Mexico (-2.5 percent) and Brazil (-14 percent). Considering full 2016, exports decreased 2.3 percent.2017-02-07T14:15:41
