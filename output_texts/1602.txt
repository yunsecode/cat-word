Title: US Consumer Prices Rise 0.1% MoM
Tag: Inflation Rate MoM

US Consumer Prices Rise 0.1% MoMUnited States Inflation Rate MoMThe Consumer Price Index in the United States increased by 0.1% from the prior month in November of 2022, slowing from the 0.4% increase in October and below expectations of a 0.3% rise. The energy price index fell by 1.6%, nearly erasing the 1.8% jump in the prior month amid lower prices for gasoline (-2% vs 4.4% in October), electricity (-0.2% vs 0.1%), and utility gas services (-3.5% vs -4.6%). Meanwhile, the cost of food rose by 0.5%, slightly easing from the 0.6% jump in the prior month as costs for both foods at home (0.5% vs 0.4%) and food away from home (0.5% vs 0.9%) rose steadily. In the meantime, shelter prices rose by 0.6% and were responsible for nearly half of the increase in the CPI besides food and energy, slowing slightly from the 0.8% rise in October, lifted by rent (0.8%) and owners’ equivalent rent (0.7%). Other increases were noted for communication (1% vs -0.1%) and recreation (0.5% vs 0.7%).
