Title: US Futures Lower Ahead of Powell, Yellen
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Lower Ahead of Powell, YellenUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were lower on Tuesday, reversing some of the previous session gains, as investors await Fed Chair Powell and Treasury Secretary Yellen's first joint hearing on the CARES Act before Congress. Meanwhile, Treasury yields continue to retreat from 14-month highs as the bond market stabilises ahead of key auctions during the week. Also, investors continue to doubt prospects of a strong global recovery amid a surge in cases and lockdowns in Europe. On the data front, new home sales will be in the spotlight. On Monday, the Dow Jones rose 103 points or 0.3% to 32,731. The S&P 500 gained 28 points or 0.7% to 3,941. The Nasdaq jumped 162 points or 1.2% to 13,378. 2021-03-23T09:57:00
