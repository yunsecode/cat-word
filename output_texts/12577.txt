Title: US Private Sector Activity Growth Revised Up: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Activity Growth Revised Up: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe final Markit US Composite PMI came in at 54.1 in December of 2016, higher than a preliminary reading of 53.7 but lower than 54.9 in November. Services growth eased (53.9 from 54.6 in November) while manufacturing accelerated to the highest since March of 2015 (54.3 from 54.1). The average reading for the final quarter of 2016 (54.6) was the highest since the last three months of 2015.2017-01-05T14:58:16.947
