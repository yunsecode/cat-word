Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Slightly Lower than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil stocks in the United States rose by 2.08 million barrels in the week ended April 15th, 2016, slightly below market expectations. In the previous week, stocks increased by 6.6 million barrels. Gasoline stocks declined for the second straight week, down by 0.11 million barrels, less than market expectations.2016-04-20T15:36:08.4
