Title: 10-Year Treasury Yield Extends Decline
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

10-Year Treasury Yield Extends Decline United States Government Bond 10YThe 10-year US Treasury note yield extended its decline towards the 3% mark, a level not seen in three weeks, as investors continued to pile into safe-haven assets amid concerns that tightening financial conditions could tip the US economy into a recession. Federal Reserve officials have reiterated their determination to rein in inflation, setting expectations for a back-to-back 75 basis point rate hike in July. Meanwhile, the core PCE Price Index inflation, the preferred gauge of inflation by the Fed, eased to a six-month low of 4.7 percent in May 2022, indicating that price increases could be slowing. Inflation data followed worse-than-expected GDP figures and dire consumer confidence data earlier this week.
