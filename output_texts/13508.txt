Title: US Stocks Open Lower on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Lower on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Thursday amid a retreat in oil prices, mixed earnings reports for some companies and mixed economic data: initial jobless claims were lower than expected while the Chicago Fed Index and the Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index came negative. The Dow Jones lost 0.15% or 27.4 points, the S&P 500 fell 0.22% or 4.7 points and the Nasdaq Composite went down 0.21% or 10.1 points around 18 minutes after the opening bell.2016-04-21T15:00:14
