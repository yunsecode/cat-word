US Stock Futures ReboundUnited States Stock MarketWall Street is set to start Wednesday’s session on a high note as investors grew optimistic that a vaccine will emerge to halt the pandemic's rising spread. President Donald Trump said the US government will purchase 100 million doses of Moderna’s experimental coronavirus vaccine. Still, doubts on whether Congress can agree on a US fiscal support bill rattled investors' sentiment. Meanwhile, presidential candidate Joe Biden announced Sen. Kamala Harris of California as his 2020 vice presidential running mate. The Dow Jones lost 105 points or 0.4% to 27,687. The S&P 500 retreated 27 points or 0.8% to 3334.
