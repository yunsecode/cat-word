Title: US Output Growth Eases in March: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Output Growth Eases in March: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI came in at 54.6 in March 2019, slightly above the preliminary estimate of 54.3 and compared to February's final 55.5. Services activity growth remained strong (PMI at 55.3 vs 56 in February), while manufacturing output expanded at the slowest rate since June 2017 (PMI at 52.4 vs 53 in February). New export orders remained in expansionary territory and employment and backlogs increased at a modest pace. On the price front, input price inflation dipped to a two-year low and output charges rose at softer rate. Looking ahead, business confidence fell to the lowest since September 2016.2019-04-03T13:59:00
