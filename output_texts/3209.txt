Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the US fell 2.3% in the week ended May 27th to the lowest level since December of 2018, a third consecutive decrease, pointing to further cooling in a housing market amid high prices and rising borrowing costs. Applications to refinance a home dropped 5.4% and those to purchase a home went down 0.6%. Meanwhile, the average contract rate on a 30-year fixed-rate mortgage fell by 13bps to 5.33%.2022-06-01T11:12:00
