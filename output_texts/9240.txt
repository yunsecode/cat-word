Title: US Pending Home Sales Fall 2% YoY in April
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Fall 2% YoY in AprilUnited States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned US homes declined 2.8 percent from a year earlier in April 2019, following a downwardly 1.1 percent fall in the previous month. It was the 16th consecutive month of annual decreases. Sales went down in the Midwest (-2.4 percent), Northeast (-2.1 percent), South (-1.8 percent) and in the West (-1.5 percent). On a month-over-month basis, sales declined 1.5 percent, after a downwardly revised 3.8 percent gain in the prior month and well below market expectations of a 0.9 percent increase.2019-05-30T14:05:00
