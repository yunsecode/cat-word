Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise The Most Since January
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise The Most Since JanuaryUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States jumped 10 percent in the week ended April 8th 2016 from the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the biggest increase since the second week of January as fixed 30-year mortgage rates fell 4 bps to 3.82 percent, the lowest since January of 2015. Refinance applications surged 11.3 percent and applications to purchase a home grew 8.4 percent.2016-04-13T12:06:07
