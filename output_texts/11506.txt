Tag: Business Confidence

US Factory Activity Growth Falls in October: ISM United States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management’s Manufacturing PMI in the US fell to 58.7 in October of 2017 from a 13-year high of 60.8 in September. Figures came below market expectations of 59.5 as growth slowed among most subindexes and inventories contracted again.
