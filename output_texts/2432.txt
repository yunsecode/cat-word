Title: Dollar Extends Rally Towards 107
Tag: Currency

Dollar Extends Rally Towards 107United States CurrencyThe dollar index strengthened towards the 107 mark, a level not seen in three weeks, as investors awaited minutes from the Federal Reserve's meeting to gauge the economy's health and the pace of interest rate hikes. On the data front, the Commerce Department reported that retail sales stalled in July, indicating that sky-high inflation continued to curb consumer spending. Meanwhile, the Federal Reserve has been reassuring markets that a dovish pivot is unlikely despite some evidence that inflation is cooling. However, it has constantly avoided putting exact numbers on the size of the next rate hike in September. 2022-08-17T12:42:18.027
