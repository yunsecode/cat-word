Title: US Exports Decrease for 1st Time this Year
Tag: Exports

US Exports Decrease for 1st Time this YearUnited States ExportsExports from the United States went down by $0.7 billion from the previous month to $258.9 billion in August of 2022, the first decrease in exports this year. Sales of goods decreased by $0.7 billion to $182.5 billion in August, especially nonmonetary gold (down by $2.0 billion) and crude oil (down by $1.0 billion). Meanwhile, exports of automotive vehicles, parts, and engines decreased by $1.1 billion while those of consumer goods increased by $1.3 billion. Exports of services decreased by less than $0.1 billion to $76.4 billion.2022-10-05T12:41:38.697
