Title: US Stocks Open At Record High On Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open At Record High On MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in record territory on Monday, extending last week gains after President Trump said he will make an announcement on “phenomenal”  tax reform in the next few weeks. In addition, investors await Fed Yellen testimony before Congress on Tuesday for further clues on the timing of Fed's next rate hike. The Dow Jones gained 77 points or 0.4% to 20,346.52; the S&P 500 went up 6 points or 0.3% to 2,322.54 and the Nasdaq traded 22 points or 0.4% higher at 5,755.86 around 5 minutes after the opening bell.2017-02-13T14:45:00
