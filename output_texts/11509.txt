Title: US Mortgage Applications Decline for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Decline for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 2.6 percent in the week ending October 27th 2017, following a 4.6 percent drop in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went down 4.5 percent and applications to purchase a home decreased 0.8 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate rose by 4bps to 4.22 percent, reaching the highest since mid-July. 2017-11-01T11:07:37.967
