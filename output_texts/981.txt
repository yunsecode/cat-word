Title: Wall Street Set to Open Sharply Lower
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Set to Open Sharply Lower  United States Stock MarketStock futures contracts tied to the blue-chip Dow and S&P 500 fell almost 2% on Wednesday, while those linked to the Nasdaq 100 were down 1.5%, as renewed fears about systemic risk in the banking sector hammered the appetite for riskier assets. The shift in sentiment was triggered by an over 25% slump in shares of Credit Suisse after the top shareholder Saudi National Bank ruled out more aid leading to the halt in trading of several European banks. Meanwhile, Citizens Financial Group and US Bancorp declined nearly 4% in premarket trading, dragging down shares in larger lenders, including Well Fargo and Bank of America. On the economic front, the Labor Department's PPI report showed that headline and core inflation slowed more than expected in February, opening the door for a possible pause in the Federal Reserve tightening cycle.2023-03-15T12:54:00
