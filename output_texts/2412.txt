Title: Wall Street Losses Intensify
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Losses Intensify United States Stock MarketThe major US stock indices fell sharply on Monday, extending losses from the previous week with pressure from all sectors, as investors braced for higher borrowing costs following hawkish remarks from several Fed policymakers last week. Money markets expect the Federal Reserve will raise its key interest rate by another 75bps in September, with attention turning to Fed Chair Jerome Powell's speech at the central bank's annual Jackson Hole symposium. The Dow was 650 points lower, on course for its worst session since June, while the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq trailed 2.1% and 2.5%, respectively. Tech giants were among the biggest laggards, with Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, and Apple trading sharply in the red.2022-08-22T18:21:00
