Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Drop to 4-Week LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 11 thousand to 254 thousand in the week ended November 5th from the previous week's unrevised level of 265 thousand.
