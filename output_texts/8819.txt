Title: US Wages Growth Below Estimates
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Growth Below EstimatesUnited States Average Hourly EarningsUS average hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls rose by 7 cents, or 0.2 percent month-over-month to USD 28.44 in January 2020, after increasing 0.1 percent in December but missing market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. Year-on-year, average hourly earnings have increased by 3.1 percent, following an upwardly revised 3 percent rise in the previous month and slightly above market estimates of 3 percent. In January, average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees were little-changed at USD 23.87.
