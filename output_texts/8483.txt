Title: US Crude Stocks Rise at Record Pace for 2nd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Rise at Record Pace for 2nd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude inventories in the US jumped by 19.248 million barrels in the week ended April 10th 2020, the biggest one-week rise in crude inventories since the series began in 1982 and compared with market expectations of a 11.676 million advance, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went up by 4.914 million in the week, below market consensus of a 6.386 million increase.2020-04-15T14:35:00
