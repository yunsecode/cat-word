Title: US Personal Income Unexpectedly Flat in June
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Unexpectedly Flat in JuneUnited States Personal IncomePersonal income in the United States was unchanged in June 2017, following a downwardly revised 0.3 percent gain in May and missing market expectations of 0.4 percent. Decreases in personal dividend income (-3 percent from 4.8 percent in May) and personal interest income (-1 percent from -0.9 percent) were partially offset by an increase in compensation of employees (0.4 percent from 0.2 percent). Meanwhile, contributions for government social insurance, a subtraction in the calculation of personal income, rose 0.4 percent after increasing by 0.2 percent in May.2017-08-01T12:32:38
