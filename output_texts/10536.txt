Title: US Stocks Fall as Trade Concerns Mount
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall as Trade Concerns MountUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Monday amid higher trade concerns after news that the EU threatened to tariff $300 billion of US imports in response to US threat to charge European auto imports. Also, Canada began imposing retaliatory tariffs on $12.6 billion in US goods on Sunday and US tariffs on Chinese imports are expected to take effect on Friday. The Dow Jones declined 0.6%, the S&P 500 fell 0.6% and the Nasdaq traded 0.6% lower around 6 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-07-02T14:01:32.853
