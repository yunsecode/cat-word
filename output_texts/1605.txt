Title: 10-Year Treasury Yield Extends Rise
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

10-Year Treasury Yield Extends RiseUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note approached 3.6%, extending its rebound from the three-month low of 3.4% touched on December 7th as investors await the key US CPI release on Tuesday and the Federal Reserve’s final policy decision of the year on the following day. Consumer prices are expected to have slowed for the fifth straight month in November, consistent with fresh data that showed consumer inflation expectations dropped to the lowest in 14 months. Evidence of slowing inflation drove money markets to price a 50bps rate hike by the Fed this week, easing after four straight 75bps increases since June. Still, concerns of unsustainable price growth remain as producer prices surprised on the upside. At the same time, strong ISM services data and the strong jobs report for November extended some leeway for the Federal Reserve to continue raising borrowing costs. 2022-12-12T16:34:16.903
