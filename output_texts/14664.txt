Title: US Jobless Claims Down To 326K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Down To 326K in Latest Week United States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending July 27, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 326,000, a decrease of 19,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 345,000. The 4-week moving average was 341,250, a decrease of 4,500 from the previous week's revised average of 345,750.
