Title: US Stocks Retreat
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks RetreatUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Thursday, after closing nearly 5% higher in the previous session, when the Dow Jones posted its biggest single-day gain ever. Renewed trade tensions between the US and China weighed on investors' mood after news that President Trump is considering banning US companies from using telecommunications equipment made by Huawei and ZTE. A retreat in oil prices and the partial shutdown of the US government also contributed to the decline. The Dow Jones lost 1.2%, the S&P 500 declined 1% and the Nasdaq traded 0.9% lower around 10 minutes after the opening bell.
