Title: US Jobless Claims Rise to 229K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise to 229K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits edged up to 229,000 in the week ended March 17th 2018, from an unrevised 226,000 in the previous week. Figures came slightly above market expectations of 225,000. According to unadjusted data, the biggest rises in initial claims were seen in NY (1,276), New Jersey (554) and Connecticut (528) while the sharpest falls were recorded in California (-1,871), Georgia (-1,049) and Michigan (-891). Claims taking procedures in Puerto Rico (-481) and in the Virgin Islands (1) have still not returned to normal.2018-03-22T12:35:00
