Title: Wall St Regains Some Traction
Tag: Stock Market

Wall St Regains Some Traction United States Stock MarketThe Dow added roughly 200 points in early deals on Friday, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq advanced 0.8% and 0.9%, respectively, as investors took advantage of lower valuations at the end of a roller-coaster week dominated by worries about soaring inflation, as well as what monetary-policy tightening means for economic growth. Sentiment got an extra boost from news that China trimmed its mortgage reference rate by a wide margin, the second cut this year, with the world's second-largest economy seeking to revive the housing market to support a slowing economy. However, disappointing forecasts from retailers, including Walmart and Target, have rattled markets this week. Ross Stores, which saw its shares plunge over 20%, was the latest retailer to report how inflation hit profit margins. On the week, the Nasdaq and the S&P 500 were on track to book their seventh straight weekly loss and the Dow its eighth week of losses.2022-05-20T13:35:00
