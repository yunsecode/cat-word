Title: US Stocks Fall As Cyberattacks Hit Businesses
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall As Cyberattacks Hit BusinessesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Tuesday, as several global business reported cyberattacks in the form of ransomware against their computer systems. Technology shares led declines, with the sector also shaken by a $2.71 billion fine imposed by the European Union on Google (-2.5% on the session) for favoring its shopping service in search results. The Dow Jones lost 99 points or 0.5 percent to 21311. The S&P retreated 20 points or 0.8 percent to 2419. The Nasdaq plunged 101 points or 1.6 percent to 6147. 2017-06-27T20:04:55
