Title: US Futures Edge Up After Monday's Rally
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Edge Up After Monday's RallyUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures edged higher on Tuesday after the major averages rebounded on the first trading day of the month, as weaker-than-expected manufacturing data allayed fears about the Federal Reserve’s aggressive rate hike path. Futures contracts tied to the three major indexes were all trading in positive territory. In regular trading on Monday, the Dow rallied 2.66%, the S&P 500 surged 2.59% and the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite jumped 2.27%, with all three benchmarks rebounding from their lowest levels in about two years. The September ISM PMI showed manufacturing activity grew at its slowest pace in 30 months as new orders headed into contractionary territory, in a sign that tightening monetary conditions may be taking a toll on demand. Treasury yields fell on the news, with investors hoping that the Fed would slow the pace of its rate increases. Markets now look ahead to the monthly US jobs report due on Friday, as well as earnings reports from a raft of companies this week.2022-10-03T23:29:00
