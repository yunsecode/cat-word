Title: US Stocks Close Lower as Oil Retreats
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Lower as Oil RetreatsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street finished lower on Thursday, as a fall in oil prices weighed on energy and industrial stocks and disappointing results mainly from Intel and American Express hurt sentiment ahead of next week's Fed meeting. The Dow Jones fell 0.42% or 78 points, ending its nine-session win streak; the S&P 500 lost 0.36% or 8 points; and the Nasdaq declined 0.31% or 16 points.2016-07-21T21:27:47
