Title: US Building Permits Top Expectations
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Top ExpectationsUnited States Building PermitsBuilding permits in the US, a proxy for future construction, went up 1.4% month-over-month to an annualized 1.564 million in September of 2022, beating market expectations of 1.53 million. Approvals of units in buildings with five units or more surged 8.2 percent to 644 thousand while single-family authorizations dropped 3.1 percent to 872 thousand units. Permits rose in the Midwest (4 percent to 210 thousand), the South (3.1 percent to 852 thousand) and the West (0.3 percent to 37 thousand) but fell in the Northeast (-9.4 percent to 125 thousand). 2022-10-19T12:34:00
