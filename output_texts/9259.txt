Title: US New Home Sales Fall More than Expected
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Fall More than ExpectedUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States dropped 6.9 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 673 thousand in April 2019, from a revised 11-1/2-year high of 723 in March, while markets had forecast a 2.8 percent fall to 675 thousand. April's decline followed three straight monthly increases, as prices surged 8.8 percent from a year ago to the highest level since December 2017.2019-05-23T14:06:37.247
