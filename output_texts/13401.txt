Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down in May
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down in MayUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe final University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States came in at 94.7 in May of 2016, down from 95.8 in the preliminary release but higher than 89 in the previous month. Future expectations decreased while the current conditions index rose to its highest since January 2007.2016-05-27T15:12:11.483
