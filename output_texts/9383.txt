Title: US New Home Sales Jump to Near 1-1/2-Year High
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Jump to Near 1-1/2-Year HighUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States rose 4.5 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 692 thousand in March 2019, the highest level since November 2017, while markets had forecast a 2.5 percent fall to 650 thousand. It was the third straight monthly increase in new home sales, amid lower mortgage rates and house prices.2019-04-23T14:03:00
