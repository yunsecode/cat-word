Title: US Stocks Start Week Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Start Week LowerUnited States Stock MarketWall Street's main indexes closed at session lows on Monday, with the Dow Jones falling around 250 points, the S&P retreating 0.7% and the Nasdaq shedding 0.6%  as a rally in some commodity prices rekindled fears of persistent inflation and concerns over a global slowdown. WTI crude futures surged to a 7-year high, Chinese coal futures hit a fresh record and aluminum jumped to a new 13-year high. Also, market participants assessed a news report that China is expanding its crackdown to banks. At the same time, traders await the earnings season to kick-off with major banks set to report third-quarter results this week, while the FOMC minutes due Wednesday will be in the spotlight for any clues on the Fed's tapering timeline. Markets are anticipating some announcement next month.2021-10-11T20:14:00
