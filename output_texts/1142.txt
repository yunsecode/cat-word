Title: US Business Inventories Rise 0.3% as Expected
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Rise 0.3% as ExpectedUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the US increased by 0.3% from a month earlier in December of 2022, following a downwardly revised 0.3% rise in the prior month, matching market expectations. Stocks rose for manufacturers (0.4 percent vs flat reading in November) and retailers (0.7 percent vs flat reading), while inventory growth eased for merchant wholesalers (0.1 percent vs 0.9 percent).
