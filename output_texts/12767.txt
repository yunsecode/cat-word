Title: US Inflation Rate Hits 2-Year High of 1.6%
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Hits 2-Year High of 1.6%United States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States went up 1.6 percent year-on-year in October of 2016, up from a 1.5 percent rise in September and in line with market expectations. It is the highest inflation rate since October of 2014, boosted by rising shelter and energy cost while food prices declined for the second month. On a monthly basis, consumer prices increased 0.4 percent, above market expectations of 0.3 percent. 2016-11-17T13:34:54
