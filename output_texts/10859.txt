Title: US Import Prices Unchanged in March
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Unchanged in MarchUnited States Import Prices MoMUS import prices were unchanged in March 2018, missing market expectations of a 0.2 percent gain and following a downwardly revised 0.3 percent advance in February. Prices for imported fuel fell 1.6 percent in March, the largest monthly decline since last June, due to lower prices for both petroleum (-1.3 percent) and natural gas (-9.5 percent). Meanwhile, the price index for non-fuel imports rose 0.2 percent in March, following a 0.5 percent increase in February, as higher costs of non-fuel industrial supplies and materials; capital goods; and foods, feeds, and beverages, offset declining prices for automotive vehicles and consumer goods. Year-on-year, import prices advanced 3.6 percent in March.2018-04-12T12:33:08
