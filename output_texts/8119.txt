Title: Dollar Gains on US Retail Trade Report
Tag: Currency

Dollar Gains on US Retail Trade ReportUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rose above 97.0 against a basket of currencies on Tuesday after economic data showed the US retail trade jumped 17.7% in May, recovering from April's record fall and easily beating market expectations. The currency had depreciated earlier in the session after the Federal Reserve launched its Main Street Lending Program and as the Trump administration is set to unveil a nearly $1 trillion infrastructure spending proposal.2020-06-16T16:57:00
