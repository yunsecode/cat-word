Title: US Energy Inflation Hits Near 2-Year Low
Tag: Energy Inflation

US Energy Inflation Hits Near 2-Year LowUnited States Energy InflationThe cost of energy in the US increased at a much slower 7.3% year-on-year in December of 2022, down significantly from a 13.1% rise in the previous month. It was the lowest reading since February of 2021, supported by a decrease in prices of gasoline (-1.5% vs 10.1% in November) and a slowdown for fuel oil (41.5% vs 65.7%). On the other hand, cost accelerated for electricity (14.3% vs 13.7%) and natural gas (19.3% vs 15.5%).
