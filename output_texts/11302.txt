Title: US Stocks Dip on Final Trading Day of 2017
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Dip on Final Trading Day of 2017United States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on the final trading session of 2017, as little news on Friday 29 December 2017 left solid annual gains in equities amid steady global monetary policy and improving growth numbers. On Friday, financial and healthcare stocks dipped as investors assessed the impact of new taxes for repatriated capital. The Dow Jones lost 118 points or 0.5% to 24719. The S&P 500 dropped 14 points or 0.5% to 2674. The Nasdaq retreated 47 points or 0.7% to 6903. During the week, the Dow Jones declined 0.1%; the S&P 500 0.3%; and the Nasdaq 0.8%. Nonetheless, for the whole 2017, the Dow Jones soared 25.1%; the S&P 500 19.4%; and the Nasdaq 28.2%. 2017-12-29T21:12:59
