Title: US Wholesale Inventories Post Biggest Gain in 15 Months
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Post Biggest Gain in 15 MonthsUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the United States increased 1.1 percent month-over-month to USD 626.7 billion in February of 2018, following an upwardly revised 1.0 percent gain in January and above market expectations of a 0.5 percent rise, the advance estimate showed. Stocks of durable goods advanced 1.2 percent (vs 0.3 percent in January) and those of non-durable goods advanced 0.9 percent (vs 2.0 percent in January).2018-03-28T12:38:30.567
