Title: Fed Policymakers Saw July Cut as Mid-Cycle Adjustment
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Policymakers Saw July Cut as Mid-Cycle AdjustmentUnited States Interest RateFed officials viewed their interest-rate cut as an adjustment that would help counter the effects on the outlook of weak global growth and trade policy uncertainty while promoting a faster return of inflation to the central bank's target, minutes of the July meeting showed. Policymakers also noted that further policy action would be guided by incoming information and its implications for the economic outlook and that any appearance of following a preset course should be avoided.2019-08-21T18:29:00
