Title: US Jobless Claims Unexpectedly Rise to 216K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Unexpectedly Rise to 216KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits increased by 2 thousand to 216 thousand in the week ending November 10 from the previous week's unrevised level of 214 thousand while markets had expected a drop to 212 thousand. However, the Veterans Day holiday was observed on Monday which could have influenced the data. Claims for North Carolina continued to be affected by Hurricane Florence while in Florida and Georgia they were impacted by Hurricane Michael. According to unadjusted data, the biggest rises in claims were seen in California (+3,735), New York (+3,682), New Jersey (+2,801) and Minnesota (+2,154) while the largest declines were registered in Michigan (-3,913), North Carolina (-1,670) and Texas (-1,246). New applications for US unemployment aid also went up in Georgia (+807) but they fell in Florida (-337).
