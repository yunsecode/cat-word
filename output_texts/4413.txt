Title: Treasury Yields Edge Higher, Payrolls Eyed
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Treasury Yields Edge Higher, Payrolls EyedUnited States Government Bond 10YUS 10-year Treasury yields edged higher to 1.81% on Monday, still below 2020-highs of 1.9% early in the month, but the yield curve flattened to its lowest level in three months, as investors continue to wonder if the Fed will need to tight monetary policy faster than anticipated to tackle rising inflation. Meanwhile, traders will get an update on the strength of the US labor market this week with the payrolls, JOLTS and ADP releases. 2022-01-31T11:25:00
