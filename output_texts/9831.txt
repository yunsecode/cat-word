Title: US GDP Growth Revised Down to 3.4% in Q3
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Revised Down to 3.4% in Q3United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy advanced an annualized 3.4 percent on quarter in the third quarter of 2018, slightly below earlier estimates of a 3.5 percent growth, final figures showed. It follows a 4.2 percent growth in the previous period which was the highest since the third quarter of 2014. Personal consumption expenditures (PCE) and exports were revised down, and private inventory investment was revised up. However, the general picture of economic growth remains the same.
