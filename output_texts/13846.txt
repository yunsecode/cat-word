Title: Chicago PMI Contracts The Most Since July 2009
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago PMI Contracts The Most Since July 2009United States Chicago PmiThe Chicago PMI in the United States decreased by 5.8 points to 42.90 points in December of 2015. It is the biggest contraction since July 2009 as order backlogs and new orders dropped to the lowest level since May 2009,  employment component declined back below the 50 neutral mark and production was down for the sixth time this year. On the other hand, Supplier Deliveries expanded 4.8 points to 56.5. 2015-12-31T15:27:27
