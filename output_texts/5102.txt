Title: US Dollar Trims Early Losses after Powell Speech
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Trims Early Losses after Powell SpeechUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar index trimmed early losses and hovered around 93.7 on Friday, as traders digested remarks from Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell indicating the central bank is ready to begin tapering. Powell warned that the central bank’s cautious approach wasn’t designed for the current framework, adding that upside risks on the supply chain, inflation, and wages are likely to last longer than previously anticipated and well into 2022. Still, the greenback is close to its lowest in nearly four weeks and is on track to finish the week 0.3% lower.2021-10-22T15:41:58.033
