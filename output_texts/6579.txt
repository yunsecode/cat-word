Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Little Changed at 1-Year High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Little Changed at 1-Year HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury steadied around 1.42% in the beginning of March, much lower than 1.61% touched later in February but remaining close to levels not seen in 1 year. The bond sell-off eased amid slowing concerns over inflationary pressures and as investors appear to acknowledge that central banks around the world will not tight monetary policy anytime soon. Treasuries have been rallying since the end of January when the Democrats won control of the US Senate, amid inflationary concerns arising from strong economic activity and further fiscal support. Fed Chair Powell recently reinforced the US economy needs support and the Fed is committed to the current policy stance as the economic recovery remains uneven while inflation is seen rising above 2% for some time.2021-03-03T08:39:00
