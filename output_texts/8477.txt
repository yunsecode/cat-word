Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yields Rise
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yields RiseUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note rose to 0.64% on Friday, after the Federal Reserve announced that it would buy securities at a pace of about $15 billion a day, slower than around $30 billion a day this week. For the week, the US 10-year Treasury note dropped around 8bps.2020-04-17T20:50:00
