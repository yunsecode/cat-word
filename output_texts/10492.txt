Title: US Stocks Close Lower on New Tariffs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Lower on New TariffsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately in the red on Wednesday 11 June 2018, ending a 4-day winning streak and tracking losses in Europe and in Asia after President Trump said he will impose new tariffs on $200 billion of Chinese imports. The Dow Jones declined 219 points or 0.9% to 24700. The S&P 500 lost 20 points or 0.7% to 2774. The Nasdaq dropped 43 points or 0.6% to 7717.
