Title: New York State Manufacturing Growth Above Expectations
Tag: NY Empire State Manufacturing Index

New York State Manufacturing Growth Above ExpectationsUnited States NY Empire State Manufacturing IndexThe New York Empire State Manufacturing Index in the United States increased 2.2 points from the previous month to 23.3 in November of 2018, beating market expectations of 20 and pointing to the highest gain in manufacturing in the NY state in three months. Shipments (28 from 26.3), inventories (10.9 from 0.8), employment (14.1 from 9) and average workweek (9.2 from 0.2) accelerated. On the other hand, new orders slowed (20.4 from 22.5), prices paid rose (44.5 from 42) and prices received eased (13.1 from 14.3). Looking ahead, firms remained fairly optimistic about the six-month outlook.
