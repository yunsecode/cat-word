Title: Most US Stocks Fall After Health-Care Vote Delayed
Tag: Stock Market

Most US Stocks Fall After Health-Care Vote DelayedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mainly lower on Thursday, reversing earlier gains after the Obamacare repeal vote was delayed. The Dow Jones closed nearly flat at 20,656.58; the S&P 500 lost 2 points or 0.1% to 2,345.96 and the Nasdaq declined 4 points or 0.1% to 5,817.96.2017-03-23T20:17:46
