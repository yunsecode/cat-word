Title: US Mortgage Rates on the Rise Again: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Rate

US Mortgage Rates on the Rise Again: MBAUnited States Mortgage RateThe average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($647,200 or less) in the US increased to 6.58% in the week ended December 30th 2022, compared to 6.42% a week earlier, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association (MBA) showed. It is the highest rate since mid-November and close to 2001-highs seen in the beginning of November, which were prompted by a surge in Treasury yields. 2023-01-04T12:19:00
