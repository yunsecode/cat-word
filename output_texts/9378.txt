Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 5.479 million barrels in the week ended April 19th of 2019, after a 1.396 million decrease in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 1.255 million gain. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories fell by 2.129 million, following a 1.174 million drop in the prior week and compared with forecasts of a 1.042 million decline.2019-04-24T14:31:00
