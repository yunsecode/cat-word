Title: Dollar Recovers from 4-Week Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar Recovers from 4-Week LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index appreciated to 96.7 on Thursday, moving away from a four-week low of 96.2 hit earlier in the session as investors rushed for safety as the number of coronavirus cases in the US hit 3 million and continues to rise at record rates. Traders try to assess the impact of surging infections on the American economy while several Fed officials have raised fresh doubts about the durability of the recovery. In addition, the US Supreme Court ruled that a New York prosecutor can obtain President Trump’s financial records, but blocked for now the House of Representatives from obtaining similar documents.2020-07-09T16:25:00
