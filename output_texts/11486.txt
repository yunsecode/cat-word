Title: US Trade Gap Slightly Higher than Expected
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Gap Slightly Higher than ExpectedUnited States Balance of TradeThe trade deficit in the United States widened to USD 43.5 billion in September 2017 from an upwardly revised USD 42.8 billion in August and compared to market expectations of a USD 43.2 billion gap. Exports rose 1.1 percent to the highest since December of 2014 while imports increased at a slightly faster 1.2 percent.2017-11-03T12:43:38
