Title: US Producer Prices Growth Above Expectations
Tag: Producer Price Inflation MoM

US Producer Prices Growth Above Expectations United States Producer Price Inflation MoMProducer prices for final demand in the United States advanced 0.1 percent month-on-month in January of 2016, following a 0.2 percent fall in the previous month and above market expectations of a 0.2 percent drop. Prices for final demand services edged up 0.5 percent, as trade services rose 0.9 percent and transport increased 0.4 percent. Meantime, cost of final demand goods fell 0.7 percent, mainly due to lower energy prices (-5 percent). Year-on-year, producer prices dropped 0.2 percent.2016-02-17T13:41:06
