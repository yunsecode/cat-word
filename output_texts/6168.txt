Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Rises to Over 2-Week High after GDP Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Rises to Over 2-Week High after GDP DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note rose to an over 2-week high of 1.677% on Thursday, after GDP data showed the US economy advanced 6.4% in the first quarter helped by strong consumer spending. Meantime, the Labor Department said that new jobless applications declined to another pandemic low. On Wednesday, the Federal Reserve left rates and asset purchases unchanged, despite acknowledging an improving economic outlook and a rise in inflation. In addition, US President Biden proposed a $1.8 trillion stimulus package, pleading to raise taxes on wealthy Americans to help pay for his "American Families Plan". Auctions held in recent weeks were met with sufficient demand, easing fears that investors would struggle to soak up the flood of supply hitting the market.2021-04-29T12:52:00
