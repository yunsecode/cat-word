Title: Dollar Erases Some Losses After Trump Tweet
Tag: Currency

Dollar Erases Some Losses After Trump TweetUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index erased some losses on Thursday after President Trump tweeted that China and the US were getting very close to a big deal. Earlier, the dollar touched 97.168, the lowest since July 21st as the Federal Reserve left the fed funds rate steady and signalled no plans to change it in 2020. Earlier in September, the so-called dot plot of interest rate projections was showing roughly half of the FOMC members were still expecting a rate hike.2019-12-12T15:43:00
