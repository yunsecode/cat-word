Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Unemployment Rate Rises To 4.8% In JanuaryUnited States Unemployment RateUS unemployment rate rose to 4.8 percent in January 2017 from 4.7 percent in the previous month and above market expectations of 4.7 percent. The number of unemployed persons was almost unchanged at 7.6 million while the labor force participation rate increased by 0.2 percentage point to 62.9 percent.2017-02-03T13:32:09.177
