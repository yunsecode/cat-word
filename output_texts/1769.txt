Title: Net Purchases of US Treasuries Declines From Previous Month
Tag: Foreign Bond Investment

Net Purchases of US Treasuries Declines From Previous MonthUnited States Foreign Bond InvestmentNet Purchases of US Treasury Bonds and Notes increased by $60.4 billion in September of 2022, down from an increase of $174.2 billion in the previous month. U.S. residents decreased their holdings of long-term foreign securities, with net sales of $24.2 billion. Foreign residents decreased their holdings of U.S. Treasury bills by $9.5 billion. Foreign resident holdings of all dollar-denominated short-term U.S. securities and other custody liabilities decreased by $8.9 billion.2022-11-16T21:26:00
