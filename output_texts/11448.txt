Title: US Import Prices Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Rise Less than ExpectedUnited States Import Prices MoMPrices for US imports edged up 0.2 percent month-over-month in October of 2017, easing from an upwardly revised 0.8 percent gain in September and below market expectations of 0.4 percent. Fuel prices went up 1.4 percent, lower than a 5.5 percent rise in the previous month. as a 1.7 percent gain in petroleum prices offset a 6.7 percent drop in natural gas prices. Cost of non-fuel imports rose 0.2 percent, slightly below 0.3 percent in September, mainly driven by rise in nonfuel industrial supplies and materials prices and capital goods. In contrast, decline was observed in prices of automotive vehicles; consumer goods; and foods, feeds, and beverages. Year-on-year, import prices rose 2.5 percent. 2017-11-16T13:39:16
