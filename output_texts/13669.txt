Title: US Stocks Trade Higher

US Stocks Trade HigherUnited States Stock MarketMarkets in the US opened higher on Friday as revised data showed the GDP expanded at a faster 1 percent, higher than a 0.7 percent advance estimate while oil prices rallied. The Dow Jones was up 81 points or 0.49%, the S&P 500 added 0.52% and the Nasdaq gained 0.71% around 10 minutes after the open.2016-02-26T14:46:17
