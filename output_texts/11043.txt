Title: US Stocks Trade Higher After Selloff
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Higher After Selloff United States Stock MarketUS stocks climbed on Wednesday, rebounding from sharp losses in the previous session after new Fed Chairman Jerome Powell said the US economic growth was strengthening and the central bank will remain on track to gradually raise short-term interest rates. The Dow Jones rose 145 points, or 0.6%, to 25,555; the S&P 500 added 15 points, or 0.5%, to 2,759; and the Nasdaq advanced 43 points, or 0.6%, to 7,373 around 10:30 AM New York time.2018-02-28T15:28:25
