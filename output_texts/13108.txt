Title: US New Home Sales Jump to Nearly 9-Year High
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Jump to Nearly 9-Year HighUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States surged 12.4 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 654,000 in July of 2016. It is the highest figure since October of 2007 and much better than market expectations of 580,000. Figures for June were revised down by 10,000 to 582,000.2016-08-23T15:05:03.497
