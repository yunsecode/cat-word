Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Holds Near 3.4%
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Holds Near 3.4%United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note, seen as a proxy for global borrowing costs, bottomed around 3.4%, close to levels not seen since September 2022, as mounting fears of a sharp economic downturn and prospects of a less aggressive Federal Reserve boosted appetite for government debt. Data released Wednesday showed that Americans curbed spending while business investment fell, heightening concerns that the economy may be moving closer to recession. At the same time, producer prices slid by the most since the pandemic's start, offering further evidence that inflation had already peaked while giving the Fed room to slow its monetary tightening. Money markets are now pricing an over 95% chance that the US central bank will hike rates by 25 basis points in February. Still, hawkish remarks from several Fed policymakers highlighted that the fight against inflation is far from over.2023-01-19T08:57:00
