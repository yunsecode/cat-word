Title: U.S. Annual Inflation Rate Down to 1.1% in April
Tag: Inflation Rate

U.S. Annual Inflation Rate Down to 1.1% in AprilUnited States Inflation RateOver the last 12 months, the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 1.1 percent before seasonal adjustment. The index decreased 0.4 percent in April on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on May 16th. 2013-05-16T13:42:26.99
