Title: US Stocks Rally on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green and extended gains with the Dow Jones booking its best day since November, as the GameStop trading mania continued to reverse course. On Tuesday, shares of AMC and GameStop continued to decline sharply and erased most of their recent gains. On the policy side, investors continued to monitor stimulus negotiations in Washington after congressional Republicans made a counteroffer to President Biden’s $1.9 trillion stimulus plan. On the corporate side, shares of Amazon and Alphabet rose more than 3%, as both tech giants are set to release quarterly earnings after the closing bell. The Dow Jones jumped 476 points or 1.6% to 30,688. The S&P 500 increased 52 points 1.4% to 3826. The Nasdaq added 209 points or 1.6% to 13,613.2021-02-02T21:06:00
