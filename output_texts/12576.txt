Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Sector Growth Remains Strong: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index came in at 57.2 in December, the same as in the previous month and the highest since October of 2015. Figures came better than market expectations of 56.6 as new orders increased while business activity and employment eased and price pressures went up. 2017-01-05T15:07:00
