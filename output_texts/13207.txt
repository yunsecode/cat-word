Title: US Pending Home Sales Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Rise Less than Expected United States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned US homes increased 1 percent year-on-year in June of 2016, recovering from a 0.2 percent fall in May. Compared to the previous month, sales edged up 0.2 percent, following a 3.7 percent drop in the previous period but lower than market expectations of a 1.4 percent rise. Sales in the Northeast went up 3.2 percent and those in the Midwest increased 0.8 percent while sales fell in the South by 0.6 percent and in the West by 1.3 percent.2016-07-27T15:17:54.63
