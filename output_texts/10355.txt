Title: US Stocks Trade Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade LowerUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened in the red on Wednesday with tech shares and banks among the worst performers and despite upbeat economic data, after Turkey's government announced it would impose extra tariffs on US imports, including rice, cars, alcohol and coal. The Dow Jones dropped 205 points, or 0.8%, to 25,095; the S&P 500 fell 23 points, or 0.8%, to 2,817; and the Nasdaq retreated 66 points, or 0.8%, to 7,806 in early New York trading.2018-08-15T13:53:23.61
