Title: US Factory Activity Growth at Near 10-Year Low: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Factory Activity Growth at Near 10-Year Low: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI was revised lower to 50.5 in May 2019 from a preliminary estimate of 50.6 and well below the previous month's final 52.6. The latest reading pointed to the weakest pace of expansion in the manufacturing sector since September 2009 as output growth eased and new orders fell for the first time since August 2009. In addition, employment rose at the slowest rate since March 2017 and backlogs of work were unchanged. Looking ahead, expectations for growth dipped to their joint-lowest since the series began in July 2012.2019-06-03T13:49:00
