Title: US Jobless Claims Below Estimates in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Below Estimates in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits rose by 1 thousand to 218 thousand in the week ending in July 28 from the previous week's unrevised level of 217,000. It compares with market expectations of 220 thousand. According to unadjusted data, the biggest rises in claims were posted in Iowa (+1,069), Nevada (+276), Maryland (+255) and Virginia (+197) while the largest declines were seen in Michigan (-6,125), California (-2,485) and Missouri (-2,349).
