Title: US Wholesale Inventories Revised Higher
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Revised HigherUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US went up at a record 2.3% mom to $759.4 billion in October of 2021, slightly higher than initial estimates of a 2.2% rise. Increases were seen in inventories of durables (2.1%), namely metals (6.6%), hardware (3.6%) and autos (2.3%). Stocks of nondurable goods jumped 2.6%, namely petroleum (12.1%), apparel (4.4%) and farm products (4.2%). 2021-12-09T15:05:32.723
