Title: US Stocks to Open Slightly Higher, Fed Awaited
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks to Open Slightly Higher, Fed AwaitedUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were slightly higher on Wednesday, with contracts on the Dow Jones adding almost 150 points while both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq gained 0.5% each, as investors await the FOMC decision later in the day. The Fed is expected to lift its policy rate by 75 bps for the third consecutive time and provide fresh forecasts for inflation, GDP, unemployment and interest rate. At the same time, Treasury yields edged lower after President Putin announcement of a partial mobilization of Russian population, escalating the war in Ukraine. In regular trading on Tuesday, the Dow fell 1%, the S&P 500 dropped 1.1% and the Nasdaq 1%, with all three benchmarks closing at their lowest levels in 2 months.2022-09-21T12:53:00
