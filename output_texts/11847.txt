Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 4th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 4th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 7.208 million barrels in the week ended July 21st, 2017, following a 4.727 million drop in the previous period and much higher than market expectations of a 2629 million decline. Gasoline stocks decreased by 1015 thousand, compared to expectations of a 614 thousand decline.2017-07-26T14:32:52.15
