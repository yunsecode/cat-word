Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Unexpectedly Rise
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Unexpectedly RiseUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 0.594 million barrels in the April 16th week, following a 5.889 million decline in the previous period and compared with market forecasts of a 2.975 million drop, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report.
