Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 3rd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 3rd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States decreased by 4.771 million barrels in the week ended August 30th of 2019, after a 10.027 million plunge in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.488 million drop. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories fell 2.396 million, following a 2.09 million fall in the prior week and against market consensus of a 1.523 million drop.2019-09-05T15:01:20.543
