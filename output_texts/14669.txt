Title: US Jobless Claims Up To 343K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Up To 343K in Latest Week  United States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending July 20, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 343,000, an increase of 7,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 336,000. The 4-week moving average was 345,250, a decrease of 1,250 from the previous week's revised average of 346,500.2013-07-25T13:37:55.96
