Title: US Crude Inventories Rise Unexpectedly
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Rise UnexpectedlyUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 1.285 million barrels in the week ended February 19th, 2021, ending a four-week period of declines and defying market forecasts of a 5.19 million drop, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories increased by 0.012 million barrels, while markets had forecast a 3.062 million fall.2021-02-24T15:33:00
