Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Up To 13-Year High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Up To 13-Year HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe final reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States rose to 98.5 in January of 2017 compared to a preliminary figure of 98.1 and 98.2 in December. It is the highest reading since January of 2004, due to a more optimistic outlook for the economy and job growth during the year ahead as well as more favorable economic prospects over the next five years.2017-01-27T15:07:13
