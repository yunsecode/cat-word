Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Rise Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil inventories in the United States rose by 1.317 million barrels in the week ended March 11th, 2016, lower than market expectations of a 3.4 million rise. In the previous week, stocks went up by 3.88 million barrels. Gasoline stocks declined for the fourth straight week, down by 747 million barrels in the week ended March 11th, 2016 and compared to market expectations of a 2.33 million drop. 2016-03-16T14:37:02.767
