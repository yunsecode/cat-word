Title: US Stocks Fall On Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall On MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Monday as investors were less optimistic amid geopolitical concerns after North Korea fired four missiles into the Sea of Japan and a slump in Deutsche Bank shares after the bank announced a plan to raise capital. The Dow Jones declined 75 points or 0.4%, the S&P 500 went down 12 points or 0.5% and the Nasdaq lost 29 points or 0.5% around 10 minutes after the opening bell. 2017-03-06T14:45:31.833
