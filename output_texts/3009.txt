Title: Wall Street Begins Week with Losses
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Begins Week with LossesUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones lost over 100 points on Monday, while the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq were down roughly 1% and 2%, respectively, as investors appeared quite reluctant to open new positions ahead of a busy week of economic data and earnings reports. With sentiment rattled by worries that an aggressive tightening from the Federal Reserve will drag the economy into a recession, investors will await essential inflation and consumer confidence data later this week for clues on the central bank's rate-hike path. At the same time, the start of the corporate earnings season will provide investors with further clues about the health of corporate America. Meanwhile, Twitter shares slumped about 10% after Elon Musk said he canceled his takeover deal because the social media company violated several merger agreement provisions.2022-07-11T15:30:50.827
