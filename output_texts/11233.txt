Title: US Stocks Surge to Fresh Highs on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge to Fresh Highs on Wednesday United States Stock MarketWall Street closed deep in the green on Wednesday 17 January 2017, as all three major indices hit new highs led by gains in technology and consumer staples shares. The earnings season continued with slightly better-than-expected results for Bank of America and US Bancorp. Earnings from Goldman Sachs (-1.9 %) also beat forecasts but the bank reported its first quarterly loss since 2011 amid a drop in revenues from bond trading and a hit from tax reform. The Dow Jones soared 323 points or 1.3% to 26116. The S&P 500 jumped 26 points or 0.9% to 2802. The Nasdaq climbed 75 points or 1.0% to 7298. 2018-01-17T21:16:19
