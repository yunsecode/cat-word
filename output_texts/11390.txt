Title: US Trade Deficit at 9-Month High
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit at 9-Month HighUnited States Balance of TradeThe US trade deficit widened to USD 48.7 billion in October of 2017 from an upwardly revised USD 44.9 billion gap in September and above market expectations of USD 47.5 billion. It is the highest trade shortfall in nine months as imports jumped 1.6 percent to a record high boosted by purchases of crude oil, food, cellphones and other goods while exports were flat.2017-12-05T13:34:05
