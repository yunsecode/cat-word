Title: US Imports Rise 1.1% in March
Tag: Imports

US Imports Rise 1.1% in MarchUnited States ImportsImports to the US increased USD 2.8 billion from a month earlier to USD 262.0 billion in March 2019. Goods imports advanced USD 2.6 billion to USD 214.1 billion, led by industrial supplies and materials (up USD 2.4 billion), in particular crude oil, organic chemicals and other petroleum products. Also, food imports rose USD 1.0 billion to the highest on record. Consumer goods imports, however, fell USD 0.7 billion amid declines in purchases of cellphones and other household goods. Imports of services, including travel and maintenance and repair services, increased USD 0.2 billion to USD 47.8 billion in March. On a non seasonally adjusted basis, goods imports rose from the EU (18.8 percent), Canada (19.2 percent), Japan (14.6 percent), Mexico (13.5 percent), Brazil (16.4 percent) and OPEC (8.2 percent). Imports from China slumped 6.1 percent.2019-05-09T12:37:00
