Title: US Jobless Rate Seen at 6.8% in November
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Seen at 6.8% in NovemberUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate is seen easing to 6.8 percent in November 2020, little-changed from October's 6.9 percent, a number that has been biased down by people misclassifying themselves as being “employed but absent from work.” November's jobless rate would compare with an all-time high of 14.7 percent hit in April but would still remain well above pre-pandemic levels of about 3.5 percent, as the labor market recovery from COVID-19 shock showed signs of slowing amid a lack of fiscal stimulus and spiraling new coronavirus infections.2020-12-04T09:03:34.22
