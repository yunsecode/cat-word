Title: US Imports Fall 1.6% in January
Tag: Imports

US Imports Fall 1.6% in JanuaryUnited States ImportsImports to the United States dropped USD 4.2 billion from the previous month to USD 253.9 billion in January 2020, led by declines in purchases of industrial supplies and materials, such as non-monetary gold and other petroleum products, as well as imports of other goods. On a non seasonally adjusted basis, imports were down from the EU (-6.3 percent), Japan (-9.5 percent), Canada (-7.3 percent) and China (-1.1 percent). Imports from Mexico, however, rose 2.6 percent.2020-03-06T13:39:00
