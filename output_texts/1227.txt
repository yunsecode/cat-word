Title: Wall Street Set to Open Sharply Lower
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Set to Open Sharply Lower United States Stock MarketStock futures contracts tied to the Dow Jones fell 0.8% on Monday, while those linked to the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 were down 1.1% and 1.2%, respectively, as investors reassessed the outlook for monetary policy. Last week's blockbuster US jobs report and ISM data pointing to a robust services sector added to concerns about persistent inflation and bolstered the case for more rate increases. Now, attention shifts to Federal Reserve Chair Powell's Tuesday speech at the Economic Club of Washington for clues about how much more the central bank may tighten financial conditions to cool the economy. Another slew of earnings releases, including those from Pepsi, Uber, and Walt Disney, will offer further insight into how businesses deal with growing macro headwinds. 2023-02-06T10:45:00
