Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Rise for 2nd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Rise for 2nd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States went up 1.413 million barrels in the week ended July 29th, 2016, following a 1.671 million increase in the previous week. It was the second straight rise while markets were expecting a 1.25 million drop. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks fell by a higher-than-expected 3.262 million barrels, after increasing by 0.452 million in the previous week.2016-08-03T15:33:52
