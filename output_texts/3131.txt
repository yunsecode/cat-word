Title: US Business Inventories Continue to Rise Strongly
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Continue to Rise StronglyUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the US rose 1.2 percent from a month earlier in April of 2022, easing from an upwardly revised 2.4 percent gain in the prior month and in line with market expectations. Stocks rose the most among merchant wholesalers (2.2 percent vs 2.7 percent in March), while gains were more subtle for retailers (0.7 percent vs 3.1 percent) and manufacturers (0.6 percent vs 1.4 percent).
