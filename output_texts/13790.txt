Title: US Housing Starts Unexpectedly Fall in December
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Unexpectedly Fall in DecemberUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the Unites States decreased by 2.5 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1149 thousand in December of 2015, compared to an upwardly revised 1179 thousand in the previous month while missing market expectations of 1.6 percent growth. For full year of 2015, housing starts increased by 10.8 percent compared to the same period of 2014.2016-01-20T13:33:12
