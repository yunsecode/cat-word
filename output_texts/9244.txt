Title: US Q1 GDP Growth Revised Down to 3.1%
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US Q1 GDP Growth Revised Down to 3.1% United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy advanced an annualized 3.1 percent in the first quarter of 2019, slightly below earlier figures of a 3.2 percent expansion and in line with market expectations, the second estimate showed. It follows a 2.2 percent growth in the previous three-month period. Nonresidential fixed investment and private inventory investment were revised lower and while upward revisions were seen exports and personal consumption expenditures.2019-05-30T12:32:00
