Title: US Personal Spending Matches Expectations
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Matches ExpectationsUnited States Personal SpendingConsumer spending in the United States edged up 0.1 percent in February from January of 2016, following a downwardly revised 0.1 percent gain in the previous period. Figures came in line with market forecasts as spending on durable goods went up 0.1 percent and services grew 0.4 percent while consumption of nondurables declined 1.1 percent. The personal consumption expenditure price index dropped 0.1 percent.2016-03-28T12:41:46
