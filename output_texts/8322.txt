US Stock Futures Rise Ahead of Jobs ReportUnited States Stock MarketUS stock index futures gained on Friday, with investors awaiting data on non-farm payrolls to get a clearer picture of the economic blow from the novel coronavirus. On Thursday, tech stocks drove Wall Street higher and sent Nasdaq back in positive territory for the year. The Dow Jones gained 211 points or 0.9% to 23,876.
