Title: Foreigners Buy US Assets for 2nd Month in August
Tag: Capital Flows

Foreigners Buy US Assets for 2nd Month in AugustUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought $73.8 billion of US assets in August of 2016, including short-dated instruments after buying a downwardly revised $118 billion in July. Foreigners also bought $48.3 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying a downwardly revised $102.8 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors sold $24.8 billion of US Treasuries, after selling $13.1 billion in July. 2016-10-18T20:13:32.6
