Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 2nd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 2nd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 2.206 million barrels in the week ended June 7th 2019, following a 6.771 million jump in the previous week and against market expectations of a 0.481 million decrease. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went up 0.764 million, below a 3.205 million rise in the prior week and beating market consensus of a 0.743 million gain.2019-06-12T14:31:00
