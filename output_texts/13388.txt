Title: US ISM Manufacturing PMI Beats Expectations
Tag: Business Confidence

US ISM Manufacturing PMI Beats ExpectationsUnited States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management’s Manufacturing PMI came in at 51.3 in May of 2016, up from 50.8 in April and much better than market expectations of 50.4. The gauge of supplier deliveries jumped to 54.1, the highest level since December 2014 while new orders and production growth eased slightly and employment and inventories fell.2016-06-01T15:15:27
