Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Lower
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised LowerUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US fell further to 97.5 in November of 2018 from a preliminary reading of 98.3 and 98.6 in October. It is the lowest value in three months. Both current conditions and expectations were revised lower although the drops were more related to income than political party.
