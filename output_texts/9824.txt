Title: US Chicago Fed National Activity Index at 3-Month High
Tag: Chicago Fed National Activity Index

US Chicago Fed National Activity Index at 3-Month HighUnited States Chicago Fed National Activity IndexThe Chicago Fed National Activity Index rose to +0.22 in November 2018 from a downwardly revised +0.00 in the previous month. It is the highest reading since August, as the contribution from production-related indicators to the CFNAI increased to +0.08 in November (vs –0.09 in October) and from industrial production rose 0.6 percent in November (vs -0.2 in the prior month). The contribution from the sales, orders, and inventories category to the CFNAI moved up to +0.09 in November (vs –0.02 in October). Meanwhile, employment-related indicators contributed +0.10 to the CFNAI in November, down from +0.15 in October, as total nonfarm payrolls increased by 155,000 in November after rising by 237,000 in the previous month. The contribution of the personal consumption and housing category to the CFNAI ticked down to –0.05 in November from –0.04 in October.
