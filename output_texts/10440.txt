Title: US Stocks Surge on Trade Concessions
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge on Trade ConcessionsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on 25 July 2018, as strong earnings and reports that President Trump secured concessions from the European Union to avoid a trade war boosted investors’ confidence. The Dow Jones jumped 172 points or 0.7% to 25414. The S&P 500 climbed 26 points or 0.9% to 2846. The Nasdaq soared 92 points or 1.2% to a new high of 7932, ahead of Facebook’s earnings report. 2018-07-25T20:09:04.417
