Title: US Composite PMI Revised Higher: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Composite PMI Revised Higher: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit Composite PMI was revised higher to 52.6 in July 2019 from a preliminary estimate of 51.6 in the previous month and compared to June's final reading of 51.5. Services activity output increased at a faster pace (PMI at 53.0 from 51.5) while manufacturing expansion was the weakest since September 2009 (PMI at 50.4 from 50.6).2019-08-05T13:56:00
