Title: US Housing Starts at 1-Year High
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts at 1-Year HighUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States jumped 13.7 percent month-over-month to an annualized rate of 1,290 thousand in October of 2017, the highest in a year and beating market expectations of a 5.6 percent rise to 1,180 thousand. It follows an upwardly revised 1,135 thousand in September, which was the lowest reading since September of 2016, mainly due to disruptions caused by Hurricanes Harvey and Irma in the South. 2017-11-17T13:37:03
