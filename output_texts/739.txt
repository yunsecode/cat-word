Title: Dollar Firms Up on Fed Rate Hike Bets
Tag: Currency

Dollar Firms Up on Fed Rate Hike BetsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index held above 102 on Tuesday after gaining more than 1% in the past two sessions, as robust US economic data bolstered market expectations that the Federal Reserve will raise interest rates again in May. Latest data showed that confidence among US single-family homebuilders improved for a fourth straight month in April, while manufacturing activity in New York expanded for the first time in five months. Solid corporate earnings results from major US banks also allayed some concerns about the banking crisis that plunged the sector last month. Markets are currently pricing a near certainty that the Fed would hike rate by 25 basis points next month, while still expecting rate cuts towards the end of the year.
