Title: US Jobless Claims Fall To 44-Year Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall To 44-Year LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 19 thousand to 223 thousand in the week ended February 25th 2017, from the previous week's revised level of 242 thousand. The figure came in below expectations of 243 thousand to hit the lowest level since March 1973. Also, the four-week moving average of claims, which removes week-to-week volatility, fell 6.25 thousand to 234.25 thousand, the lowest reading since April 1973. 2017-03-02T13:31:52
