Title: US Mortgage Applications Post 1st Rise in 3 Weeks
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Post 1st Rise in 3 WeeksUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 2.8 percent in the week ended August 26th 2016 from the prior period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It follows a 2.1 percent drop in the week before. Refinance applications increased 3.7 percent and applications to purchase a home went up 1.3 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates were steady at 3.67 percent.2016-08-31T12:03:22.51
