Title: US Export Prices Rise The Most In 7 Months
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise The Most In 7 MonthsUnited States Export Prices MoMPrices for US exports increased 0.4 percent month-over-month in July of 2017, recovering from a 0.2 percent drop in June and above market expectations of a 0.2 percent gain. It is the strongest rise in export prices since December as cost grew for both agricultural (2.1 percent from -1.4 percent in June) and non-agricultural goods (3 percent  from a flat reading). Year-on-year, export prices advanced 0.8 percent after rising 0.6 percent in June.2017-08-15T12:47:21
