Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly Shrink
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly ShrinkUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 1.616 million barrels in the week ended February 16th, after a 1.841 million increase in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 1.795 million rise. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went up by 0.261 million, following a 3.599 million increase in the previous week and beating market expectations of a 0.283 million fall.2018-02-22T16:03:16
