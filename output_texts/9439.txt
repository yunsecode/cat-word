Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Thursday 4 April 2019, as US jobless claims were the lowest since 1969; President Trump delayed closing the US-Mexico border for at least a year, and China-US trade negotiations continued to progress favourably. The Dow Jones climbed 167 points or 0.6% to 26385. The S&P 500 edged up 6 points or 0.2% to 2879. In contrast, the Nasdaq inched down 4 points or less than 0.1% to 7892.2019-04-04T20:04:00
