Title: US Wholesale Inventories Rise For 17th Month
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Rise For 17th MonthUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US advanced 2.2 percent month-over-month to $789.4 billion in December of 2021, up from a 1.7 percent increase in the prior month and above a preliminary estimate of 2.1 percent. It was the 17th consecutive month of gains, amid increases in inventories of both durable goods (2.6 percent vs 2.6 percent in November) and nondurable ones (1.6 percent vs 0.4 percent). On a yearly basis, wholesale inventories advanced 18.5 percent in December.2022-02-09T15:01:00
