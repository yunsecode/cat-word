Title: US Goods Trade Deficit Highest in Over 1-1/2 Year
Tag: Goods Trade Balance

US Goods Trade Deficit Highest in Over 1-1/2 YearUnited States Goods Trade BalanceThe US trade deficit on goods widened to USD 79.32 billion in July of 2020 from a revised $70.99 billion in the previous month, a preliminary estimate showed. That was the smallest trade gap since December 2018. Exports rose 11.8 percent, boosted by sales of automotive vehicles (44.6 percent); consumer goods (21.4 percent); capital goods (7.5 percent); industrial supplies (7.1 percent) and foods, feeds, & beverages (2.1 percent). Imports climbed 11.8 percent mainly due to purchases of automotive vehicles (41.3 percent) and industrial supplies (10.7 percent). 2020-08-28T12:39:00
