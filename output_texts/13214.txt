Title: US Services Sector Growth at 5-Month Low
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Sector Growth at 5-Month LowUnited States Services PMIThe Markit Flash US Services PMI came in at 50.9 in July of 2016, down from 51.4 in the previous month. Figures came well below market expectations of 52, due to a slower increase in new business and muted cost inflation. In contrast, new orders and employment picked up and business sentiment recovered to the highest so far this year, suggesting a stronger expansion in coming months. 2016-07-26T14:59:43.647
