Title: US CPI Rises 0.3% MoM in September
Tag: Inflation Rate Mom

US CPI Rises 0.3% MoM in SeptemberUnited States Inflation Rate MomThe Consumer Price Index in the United States increased 0.30 percent in September of 2016 over the previous month boosted by higher cost of shelter and gasoline. The gasoline index rose 5.8 percent and accounted for more than half of the all items increase. The shelter index increased 0.4 percent, its largest increase since May. The energy index increased 2.9 percent, its largest advance since April. The index for food, in contrast, was unchanged for the third consecutive month, as the food at home index continued to decline. 2016-10-18T13:22:56.183
