US Mortgage Applications Rebound: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 4.7 percent in the week ending December 1st 2017, after falling by 3.1 percent in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications jumped 9 percent and applications to purchase a home rose 2.4 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged down by 1bps 4.19 percent.2017-12-06T12:04:48.657
