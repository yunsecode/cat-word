Title: US Industrial Output Rises The Most In Over 2 Years
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Output Rises The Most In Over 2 YearsUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial production in the United States grew by 0.8 percent month-over-month in December 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.7 percent fall in November and better than market expectations of a 0.6 percent rise. It was the biggest gain since November 2014, as utility output jumped 6.6 percent, the most since December 1989. Industrial production, however, fell 0.6 percent in the fourth quarter compared with the same period of 2015.2017-01-18T14:18:22
