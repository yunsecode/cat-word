Title: US Stocks Decline Sharply as October Rout Extends
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Decline Sharply as October Rout ExtendsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed sharply lower on Friday 26 October 2018, as the the global October rout triggered by rising yields, trade policy uncertainty, and lackluster earnings continued. The Dow Jones plunged 296 points or 1.2% to 24688. The S&P 500 dropped 47 points or 1.7% to 2659. The Nasdaq retreated 151 points or 2.1% to 7167. 2018-10-26T20:14:34.9
