Title: US Mortgage Applications Slump 2.5%: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Slump 2.5%: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States decreased 2.5 percent in the week ended April 27th 2018, after edging down 0.2 percent in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications declined 3.5 percent and applications to purchase a home fell 1.6 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate rose by 7bps to 4.8 percent, a new high since September 2013.2018-05-02T11:03:22.103
