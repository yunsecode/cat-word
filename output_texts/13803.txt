Title: US Retail Sales Unexpectedly Fall 0.1%
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Unexpectedly Fall 0.1%United States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States shrank 0.1 percent in December from November of 2015, below market expectations and following an upwardly revised 0.4 percent rise in the previous period, mainly due to lower sales of winter apparel and gasoline. Excluding autos, sales also fell 0.1 percent.
