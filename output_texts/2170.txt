Title: Dollar Holds Gains on Fed Rate Hike Bets
Tag: Currency

Dollar Holds Gains on Fed Rate Hike BetsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded around 111 in choppy trade on Thursday, holding onto gains from the previous session as better-than-expected US data and hawkish remarks from US policymakers supported the case for continued Federal Reserve monetary tightening. Markets digested an ADP report pointing to another robust private sector hiring and ISM Services PMI data that beat estimates, signaling that the US economy remains resilient and countering weak data from earlier this week. On the monetary policy front, Atlanta Fed Bank President Raphael Bostic said he favored raising rates to 4.5% by year-end or 125 basis points of additional tightening, while San Francisco Fed Bank President Mary Daly pushed back against speculations for a Fed pivot next year. Meanwhile, an improving risk sentiment on Thursday weighed on the dollar, with US futures, commodities and risk-sensitive currencies rising in early trade.2022-10-06T02:50:06.573
