Title: US Stocks Rebound
Tag: stock market

US Stocks ReboundUnited States stock marketWall Street traded in the green on Monday, recovering from its worst week since the 2008 financial crisis as investors hope that major central banks including the Fed, the ECB and the BoJ will introduce additional monetary stimulus to reduce the impact of coronavirus, while recession worries resurfaced after latest Markit PMI data showed the coronavirus hurt new export orders in China and disrupted supply chains in the Euro Area, Germany and France. The Dow was up 2.7%, the S&P 500 gained 2.2% and the Nasdaq went up 2.4% around 2:05 pm NY time.2020-03-02T16:14:00
