Title: US Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index at 5-Month Low
Tag: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index

US Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index at 5-Month LowUnited States Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Philadelphia Fed diffusion index for current general manufacturing activity decreased to 22.2 in January of 2018 from an upwardly revised reading of 27.9 in December. The figure came below market consensus of 25.0, reaching the lowest since August last year. The index for current new orders decreased 18 points to 10.1. In addition, both the delivery times (6.1 from 11.0) and unfilled orders (-1.8 from 12.8) indexes went down. The unfilled orders index was negative for the first time in 16 months, and the index of delivery times fell to its lowest reading in 10 months. Meanwhile, the shipments index remained at a high reading of 30.3. The firms reported an increase in manufacturing employment this month. The current employment index, while still positive, fell 3 points to 16.8. On the price front, inflationary pressures were reflected on higher input costs and selling prices.2018-01-18T13:34:06
