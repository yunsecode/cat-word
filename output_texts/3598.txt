Title: US Employment Costs Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Employment Cost Index

US Employment Costs Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Employment Cost IndexCompensation costs for civilian workers in the US increased 1.4 percent quarter-over-quarter in the first three months of 2022, quickening from a 1.0 percent rise in the previous period and faster than market expectations of a 1.1 percent raise as a tight labour market boosted competition for workers. Wages and salaries increased 1.2 percent (vs 1.0 percent in Q4 2021) and benefits went up 1.8 percent (vs 0.9 percent). Compared with a year earlier, the labor costs surged 4.5%, the most since 2001.
