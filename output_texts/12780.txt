Title: US Business Inventories Rise Less Than Expected
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Rise Less Than ExpectedUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the United States edged up 0.1 percent month-over-month in September of 2016, following a 0.1 percent gain in August and lower than market expectations of a 0.2 percent increase. Retail inventories excluding autos, which go into the calculation of GDP were flat after rising 0.1 percent in August. Year-on-year, inventories were up 0.6 percent.2016-11-15T15:08:57.123
