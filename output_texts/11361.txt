Title: US Stocks End Mixed Ahead of Fed Meeting
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed Ahead of Fed MeetingUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Tuesday 12 December 2017, as the Dow Jones and S&P 500 booked fresh records ahead of the Fed’s Wednesday meeting where policymakers are expected to raise rates and offer further guidance for its 2018 roadmap. The Dow Jones gained 128 points or 0.5% to 24514. The S&P 500 advanced 4 points or 0.2% to 2664. In contrast, the Nasdaq lost 13 points or 0.2% to 6862.2017-12-12T21:09:06
