Title: US Export Prices Rebound More than Expected in May
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rebound More than Expected in MayUnited States Export Prices MoMExport prices in the US increased 0.5 percent from a month earlier in May of 2020, up from a 3.3 percent fall in the previous month and compared to market expectations of a 0.6 percent gain. Nonagricultural export prices rose 0.6 percent in May, after declining 5.7 percent from January to April, as rising prices for nonagricultural industrial supplies and materials more than offset declining prices for capital goods, consumer goods, automotive vehicles, and nonagricultural foods. Meantime, agricultural exports cost declined 0.5 percent, less than a 3.1 percent drop in April, as lower prices for corn, dairy products, and soybeans more than offset higher prices for meat, vegetables, and cotton. Year-on-year, export prices declined 6 percent, after plunging 6.8 percent in the prior month.2020-06-12T12:35:00
