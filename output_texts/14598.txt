Title: U.S. Housing Starts Near 6-Year High in November
Tag: Housing Starts

U.S. Housing Starts Near 6-Year High in NovemberUnited States Housing StartsPrivately-owned housing starts in November were at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,091,000. This is 22.7 percent above the revised October estimate of 889,000 and is 29.6 percent above the November 2012 rate of 842,000.2013-12-18T14:00:02.22
