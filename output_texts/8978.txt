Title: US November Composite PMI Revised Higher
Tag: Composite Pmi

US November Composite PMI Revised HigherUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI came in at four-month high of 52.0 in November 2019, little-changed from a preliminary 51.9 and compared to October's final reading of 50.9. Growth was supported by stronger increases in activity across both the manufacturing (PMI at 52.6 vs 51.3 in October) and service sectors (PMI at 51.6 vs 50.6 in October). Employment rebounded amid greater new business inflows and a rise in backlogs of work, while business confidence remained subdued.2019-12-04T14:58:00
