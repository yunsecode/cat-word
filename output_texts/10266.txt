Title: US Wages Rise 0.4% MoM in August, Above Forecasts
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Rise 0.4% MoM in August, Above ForecastsUnited States Average Hourly EarningsUS average hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls rose by 10 cents to USD 27.16, or 0.4 percent, in August 2018, following an unrevised 0.3 percent gain in July and above expectations of a 0.2 percent gain. Over the year, average hourly earnings have increased by 77 cents, or 2.9 percent, the largest increase since June 2009, from 2.7 percent in July. Average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees increased by 7 cents to USD 22.73 in August.
