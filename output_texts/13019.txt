US Industrial Production Falls More than ExpectedUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial production in the United States declined 0.4 percent in August from July of 2016, following a downwardly revised 0.6 percent gain in the previous month and worse than market expectations of a 0.3 percent fall.
