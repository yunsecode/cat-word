Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall the Most in 5 Weeks: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall the Most in 5 Weeks: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States decreased 4.6 percent in the week ending October 20th 2017, after rising 3.6 percent in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the biggest decline in five weeks as refinance applications went down 3 percent and applications to purchase a home slumped 6.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate rose by 4bps to 4.18 percent.2017-10-25T12:03:11.57
