Title: US Consumer Sentiment Down to 4-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Down to 4-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe Thomson Reuters/University of Michigan's consumer sentiment index came in at a final reading of 80.0 in March, marginally up from a preliminary 79.9, and down from 81.6 in February. It is the lowest rate since November of 2013, hurt by lower future expectations.2014-03-28T14:08:35.013
