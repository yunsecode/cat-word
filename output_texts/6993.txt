Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Seen at 6.8% in DecemberUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate is seen picking up to 6.8 percent in December 2020, from 6.7 percent in the previous month and well above pre-pandemic levels of about 3.5 percent. The labor market recovery slowed amid a lack of fiscal stimulus and record COVID-19 infections that prompted many US states to impose restrictive measures to respond to the outbreak.2021-01-08T08:28:00
