Title: US Mortgage Applications Increase in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Increase in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 3.3 percent in the week ending September 1st, 2017, after falling 2.3 percent in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home went up 1.4 percent and refinance applications increased 5.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 5bps to 4.06 percent, the lowest since November. 2017-09-06T11:01:43.2
