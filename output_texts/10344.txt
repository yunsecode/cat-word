Title: US Building Permits Rise More than Expected in July
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Rise More than Expected in JulyUnited States Building PermitsBuilding permits in the United States increased 1.5 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,311 thousand in July 2018, while markets were expecting a 1.4 percent gain to 1,310 thousand. Single-family authorizations rose 1.9 percent to 865 thousand and multi-family permits advanced 0.7 percent to 442 thousand. Across regions, permits went up in the Northeast (5.9 percent), Midwest (5.8 percent) and in the West (1.2 percent), but dropped in the South (-0.3 percent).2018-08-16T12:40:00
