Title: US Inflation Rate Down To 2.4% In March
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Down To 2.4% In MarchUnited States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States increased 2.4 percent year-on-year in March of 2017, lower than 2.7 percent in February and below market expectations of 2.6 percent. It is the lowest inflation rate in three months due to a slowdown in energy and services cost. On a monthly basis, consumer prices went down 0.3 percent, the first drop in 13 months.2017-04-14T12:30:46
