Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Rise the Most in 11 Months: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 7.757 million barrels in the week ended April 8th of 2022, following a 1.08 million barrels increase in the previous week and well above market expectations of a 1.367 million addition. It was the largest injection of crude oil US inventories since the first week of May 2021, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed.
