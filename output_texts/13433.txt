Title: US Housing Starts Rise More Than Expected in April
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Rise More Than Expected in AprilUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the Unites States rose 6.6 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1172 thousand in April 2016, compared to an upwardly revised 1099 thousand in the previous month. The figure came in above market expectations of 1127 thousand, boosted by single-family housing starts. Meanwhile, building permits went up 3.6 percent, following a 7.3 percent drop in March, but lower than an expected 4.3 percent gain. 2016-05-17T13:38:46.357
