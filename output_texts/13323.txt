Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Fall Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil inventories in the United States declined by 0.917 million barrels in the week ended June 17th, 2016, following a 0.933 million fall in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 1.67 million drop. It is the fifth straight decline in oil inventories. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks rose by 0.627 million barrels after falling by 2.625 million barrels in the previous week.2016-06-22T15:35:33
