Title: US Exports Climb to Fresh Record
Tag: Exports

US Exports Climb to Fresh RecordUnited States ExportsExports from the United States increased by 3.5 percent or USD 8.5 billion from the previous month to USD 252.6 billion in April of 2022, the highest since the series began in 1950. Exports of goods increased by USD 6.1 billion to USD 176.1 billion, driven by industrial supplies and materials (USD 2.3 billion), mainly natural gas (USD 0.9 billion) and other petroleum products (USD 0.7 billion). Also, sales of foods, feeds, and beverages increased by USD 2.2 billion. Meantime, services exports increased by USD 2.4 billion to USD 76.5 billion, led by travel (USD 1.5 billion) and transport (USD 0.3 billion).2022-06-07T12:38:21.363
