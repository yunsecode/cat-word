Title: US Trade Gap Biggest in 5 Months
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Gap Biggest in 5 MonthsUnited States Balance of TradeThe United States recorded a trade deficit of $48.3 billion in August of 2015, up $6.5 billion from $41.8 billion in July.  Imports increased 1.2 percent due to rising imports of consumer goods and exports fell 2 percent to their lowest level since October 2012 due to lower oil prices and strong dollar. 2015-10-06T13:37:01
