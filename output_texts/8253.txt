Title: Factory Activity in Philadelphia Falls Again
Tag: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index

Factory Activity in Philadelphia Falls AgainUnited States Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index in the US increased to -43.1 in May of 2020 from -56.6 in April which was the lowest reading since July 1980,  due to the coronavirus pandemic. Figures compare with market forecasts of -41.5 and point to the third straight month of falling factory activity in Philadelphia. Smaller declines were seen for new orders (-25.7 from -70.9), shipments (-30.3 from -74.1), employees (-15.3 from -46.7) and average workweek (-7.1 from -54.5). Also, inventories increased (11.7 from -10.2) and price pressures intensified for prices paid (3.2 from -9.3). The firms expect the current slump in manufacturing activity to last less than six months, as the broadest indicator of future activity strengthened further from last month’s reading; furthermore, the firms continue to expect overall growth in new orders, shipments, and employment over the next six months.
