Dollar Nearly FlatUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index cut earlier losses to trade slightly higher at 92.3 on Friday amid increasing uncertainty over further stimulus after Treasury Secretary Mnuchin said he will let the Fed’s emergency lending programs to expire by the end of the year.
