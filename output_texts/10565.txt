Title: US New Home Sales Beat Forecasts
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Beat ForecastsUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States jumped 6.7 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 689 thousand in May of 2018, following an upwardly revised 3.7 percent fall in April. It is the highest rate since November, beating market expectations of a 0.7 percent increase. Sales in the South hit their highest level in nearly 11 years.2018-06-25T14:04:27.29
