Title: US Capital Inflow Shrinks in April
Tag: Capital Flows

US Capital Inflow Shrinks in AprilUnited States Capital FlowsThe United States posted a capital and financial account surplus of $48.4 billion in April of 2023, marking the fifth increase in the last six months while narrowing from an upwardly revised $129 billion surplus in the prior month. The net foreign private inflows were recorded at $2.5 billion, and net foreign official inflows were $45.9 billion. Foreign residents increased their holdings of long-term U.S. securities in April; net purchases were $97.6 billion. Net purchases by private foreign investors were $59.4 billion, while net purchases by foreign official institutions were $38.3 billion.
