Title: US Futures Slump as Coronavirus Spreads
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Slump as Coronavirus SpreadsUnited States Stock MarketUS Stocks Futures were sharply down on Monday morning in Europe as investors have been frightened by a rapid spread of Chinese coronavirus. As of Monday, the death tall increased to 81 and more than 2700 people were reported to be infected. Dow Jones was down more than 470 points or 1.7%, S&P 500 decreased 55 points or 1.6% and NASDAQ100 dropped almost 200 points or 2.2% around 11AM London time.   2020-01-27T11:10:00
