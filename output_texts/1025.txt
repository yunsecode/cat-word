Title: US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 1.694 million barrels in the week ended March 3rd, compared with market expectations of a 0.395 million barrel increase, the latest US Energy Information Administration report showed. Also, crude stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, went down by 0.89 million barrels, following a 0.307 million build and gasoline stocks declined by 1.134 million, less than market expectations of a 1.863 million draw. On the other hand, distillate stockpiles, which include diesel and heating oil, increased by 0.138 million, versus forecasts for a 1.038 million drop.2023-03-08T15:51:00
