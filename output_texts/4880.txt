Title: US Treasury Yields Steady to Start the Week
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Steady to Start the WeekUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on benchmark 10-year Treasury note steadied around 1.57% on Monday, remaining below 3-month highs hit in October, as traders continue to worry that inflation will stay high for longer than expected which could force the Fed to tighten soon. Bond-market expectations for inflation over the coming decade are near the highest since 2006, according to Bloomberg. This week, the Treasury will auction $23 billion in 20-year bonds and $14 billion in 10-Year TIPS.2021-11-15T08:58:00
