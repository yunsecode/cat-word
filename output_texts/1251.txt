Title: US Natural Gas Storage Falls More than Expected: EIA

US Natural Gas Storage Falls More than Expected: EIAUnited States Natural Gas Stocks ChangeUS utilities pulled 151 bcf of gas from storage during the week ended January 27, slightly more than market expectations of a 142 bcf fall.
