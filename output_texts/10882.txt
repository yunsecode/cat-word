Title: US Unemployment Rate Unchanged at 17-Year Low
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Unemployment Rate Unchanged at 17-Year LowUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate stood at a 17-year low of 4.1 percent for the sixth consecutive month in March 2018, slightly above market expectations of 4 percent. The number of unemployed decreased by 121 thousand to 6.59 million and employment fell 37 thousand to 155.18 million. The labor force participation rate dropped to 62.9 percent from a five-month high of 63 percent in February.2018-04-06T12:31:27
