Title: Investors Rush to Safety of Government Bonds
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Investors Rush to Safety of Government BondsUnited States Government Bond 10YMajor core government debt rallied on Thursday as investors rushed into safe-haven assets amid lingering concerns that an aggressive tightening from central banks to rein on record inflation could hurt global economic growth. The yield on the 10-year US Treasury note, which sets the tone for corporate and household borrowing costs worldwide, eased to a two-week low of around 2.8%. Meantime, Germany's 10-year Bund yield, the benchmark for the region, was down ten basis points at 0.88%, a level not seen since late April. Italy's 10-year spread, an influential gauge of risk in eurozone bond markets owing to the vast scale of Italian debt, narrowed to more than 2.74 percentage points.2022-05-12T08:26:00
