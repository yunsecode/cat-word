Title: US Stocks Trade Mixed on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Mixed on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened mixed on Monday, following a sell-off on Friday that extended to Europe and Asia during Monday on rising concerns of a Fed rate hike next week. The Dow Jones declined 25 points or 0.1%, the S&P 500 traded near the flatline and the Nasdaq gained 5 points or 0.1% around 7 minutes after the opening bell.2016-09-12T13:44:37
