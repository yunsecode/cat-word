Title: US Trade Gap Narrows to 8-Month Low
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Gap Narrows to 8-Month LowUnited States Balance of TradeThe goods and services deficit in the United States narrowed to USD 43.6 billion in June 2017 from USD 46.4 billion a month earlier and compared to market expectations of a USD 45 billion gap. It was the smallest trade deficit since October 2016, as exports increased to their highest level in 2-1/2 years.2017-08-04T12:34:37.917
