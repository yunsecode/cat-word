Title: US Crude Stocks Drop the Most in 17 Years: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Drop the Most in 17 Years: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell sharply by 14.51 million barrels in the week ended September 2nd, 2016, following a 2.28 million rise in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 0.4 million gain. It was the biggest fall since 1999, as Tropical Storm Hermine, which threatened the Gulf Coast refining region last week, led to the loss of some US oil production. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks fell by 4.21 million barrels after declining by 0.69 million in the previous week and compared to market expectations of 0.5 million fall.2016-09-08T16:06:09
