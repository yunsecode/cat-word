Title: US Services Sector Growth at 3-Month Low
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Sector Growth at 3-Month LowUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index registered 56.9 percent in September of 2015, down from 59 in August and below market expectations. Growth in the services sector slowed for the second straight month to its lowest since June as business activity, new orders and prices decreased while job creation went up.2015-10-05T15:12:09.177
