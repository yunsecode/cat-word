Title: Dollar Rises amid US-China Tensions
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rises amid US-China TensionsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index extended gains for a 2nd straight session to trade above 99.7 on Friday amid increasing tensions between the US and China and concerns about the global recovery. China said will impose a new national law on Hong Kong and President Trump said the US will reply very strongly against an attempt to gain more control over Hong Kong. Also, Beijing will not set a GDP growth target for this year amid uncertainty regarding the coronavirus, raising questions about the strength of the recovery in the country.  2020-05-22T08:14:00
