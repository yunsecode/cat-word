Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Falls Slightly after Fed, GDP
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Falls Slightly after Fed, GDP United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note edged lower to near 2.7%, a level not seen in two months, after the Fed hiked the funds rate by 75bps as broadly expected and hinted that a slowdown in the rate hike path could come. At the same time, the advance GDP estimate showed the US economy shrank for a second consecutive quarter in Q2. Still, Fed Powell said he did not believe the US was in a recession and pointed to strength in the economy, namely the labour market. The NFP report next week will provide an update on how the jobs market is evolving2022-07-28T12:58:00
