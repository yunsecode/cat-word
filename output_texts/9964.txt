Title: US Stocks Fall on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Monday, mainly dragged down by losses in Apple shares after news that the company cut orders for the 3 models launched in September iPhone XR, XS and XS Max. The Dow Jones fell 0.1%, the S&P 500 declined 0.2% and the Nasdaq traded 0.6% lower around 14 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-11-19T14:49:29.64
