Title: US Stocks Open Little Changed After Jobs Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Little Changed After Jobs DataUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened flat to slightly higher on Friday as concerns over the presidential election outcome persist and after October payrolls came lower than expected while wage growth picked up. The Dow Jones traded near the flatline while the S&P 500 gained 3 points or 0.1% and the Nasdaq added 7 points or 0.1% around 7 minutes after the opening bell.2016-11-04T13:44:39
