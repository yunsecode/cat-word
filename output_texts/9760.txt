Title: US Stocks Finish Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Finish LowerUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Monday after data showed exports and imports from China unexpectedly fell in December due to trade tariffs and weaker demand, raising concerns about global economic growth. Also, the earnings season kicked off with Citigroup reporting weaker-than-expected revenues while earnings came above forecasts. The Dow Jones went down 0.4% or 86 points to 23910, the S&P 500 lost 0.5% or 14 points to 2583 and the Nasdaq closed 0.9% or 66 points lower at 6906.2019-01-14T21:14:00
