Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall Much Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall Much Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States dropped by 0.046 million barrels in the week ended December 21st 2018, following a 0.497 million decline in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 2.869 million fall. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories increased by 3.003 million barrels, above market consensus of a 0.028 million rise.2018-12-28T16:10:06.95
