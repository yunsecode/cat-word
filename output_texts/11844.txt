Title: US Stocks End Higher On Wednesday And Hit New Highs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Higher On Wednesday And Hit New HighsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Wednesday July 26th of 2017, as the Fed left the target range for its federal funds rate unchanged at 1% to 1.25% and said it will start reducing its $4.5 trillion portfolio relatively soon. Boeing (+9.6%) and AT&T (+5.1%) were among the top gainers on Wednesday. The Dow Jones advanced 98 points or 0.5% to a new record of 21711. The S&P 500 edged up 1 point or less than 0.1% to a fresh high of 2478. The Nasdaq gained 11 points or 0.2% to a new record of 6423.2017-07-26T20:02:07
