Title: US Stocks Fall on Trade Tensions
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on Trade TensionsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks fell on Friday due to ongoing trade tensions with China following the Trump administration's decision to add Huawei Technologies to a trade blacklist, aimed at preventing the Chinese telecom giant to conduct business with US companies. Last week, the US hiked tariffs on $200 billion of Chinese goods while Beijing retaliated Monday with higher levies on $60 billion of US products. The Dow Jones dropped 109 points, or 0.4%, to 25,754; the S&P 500 lost 13 points, or 0.5%, to 2,863; and the Nasdaq retreated 40 points, or 0.5%, to 7,856 around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2019-05-17T13:45:30.423
