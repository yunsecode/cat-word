Title: US Export Prices Rise the Most Since 2018
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise the Most Since 2018United States Export Prices MoMExport prices in the US advanced 2.5 percent from a month earlier in January of 2021, the most since October of 2018, following a 1.3 percent increase in December and above market expectations of 0.7 percent. It was the eighth straight monthly rise in export prices, with higher agricultural and nonagricultural prices being the main driver for the January increase. Year-on-year, export prices went up 2.3 percent, the largest increase since October of 2018, following a revised 0.4 percent gain in prior month. 2021-02-18T13:35:00
