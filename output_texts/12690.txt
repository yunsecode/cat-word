Title: US Stocks Edge Lower On Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Lower On TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks fell slightly on Tuesday amid a decline in oil prices, after data from the US showed labor productivity rose for the first time in one year and the trade deficit widened more than expected following a drop in exports. Meanwhile, investors remained cautious ahead of the Fed's meeting next week and ECB's policy decision on Thursday. The Dow Jones retreated 15 points, or 0.1%; the S&P 500 went down 2 points, or 0.1%; and the Nasdaq was flat around 15 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-12-06T14:51:29.257
