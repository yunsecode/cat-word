Title: US Jobless Claims Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending February 1, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 331,000, a decrease of 20,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 351,000. The 4-week moving average was 334,000, an increase of 250 from the previous week's revised average of 333,750.2014-02-06T13:36:44.853
