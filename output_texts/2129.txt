Title: US Crude Stocks Rise the Most Since March 2021: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Rise the Most Since March 2021: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories jumped by 9.88 million barrels in the week ended October 7th, the most since March 2021 and compared with market expectations of a 2.05-million-barrel decrease, according to EIA's latest report. Also, gasoline stocks rose by 2.023 million barrels, versus forecasts for a 1.825 million draw. Meanwhile, distillate stockpiles, which include diesel and heating oil, declined by 4.853 million barrels, above the consensus of a 2.05 million decrease. Crude stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, went down by 0.309 million barrels, following a 0.273 million increase.2022-10-13T15:10:56.667
