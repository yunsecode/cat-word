Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 7-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 7-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's preliminary reading on the overall index of consumer sentiment came in at 88.6 in May, down from 95.9 in April and well below market expectations. Both current and future expectations declined while the inflation outlook rose.2015-05-15T15:11:42.553
