U.S. Unemployment Rate Up to 7.3% in OctoberUnited States Unemployment RateU.S. unemployment rate was little changed at 7.3 percent in October and total nonfarm payroll employment rose by 204,000, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on November 8th.
