Title: US Personal Spending Rises 0.2% and Matches Forecats
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Rises 0.2% and Matches ForecatsUnited States Personal SpendingPersonal spending in the US rose 0.2 percent month-over-month in February of 2020, the same as in the previous month and in line with market expectations. Real personal spending increased 0.1 percent, also the same as in January, boosted by a 0.2 percent increase in spending for services (vs a flat reading in January) that was partly offset by a 0.2 percent decrease in spending for goods. Within services, the leading contributor to the increase was spending on electricity and gas. Within goods, the leading contributors to the decrease were spending on motor vehicles and parts as well as recreational goods and vehicles.2020-03-27T12:34:00
