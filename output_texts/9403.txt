Title: US Exports Rise 1.1% MoM
Tag: Exports

US Exports Rise 1.1% MoMUnited States ExportsExports of goods and services from the US rose 1.1 percent, or USD 2.3 billion, from the previous month to USD 209.7 billion in February of 2019. Goods exports increased USD 2.1 billion to USD 139.5 billion, boosted by sales of civilian aircrafts (up USD 2.2 billion), and automotive vehicles, parts and engines (up USD 0.6 billion) while industrial supplies and materials exports decreased USD 0.4 billion. In addition, exports of services went up USD 0.2 billion to a record high of USD 70.1 billion, in particular transport (up USD 0.2 billion) and other business services, which includes research and development services; professional and management services; and technical, trade-related, and other services (up USD 0.1 billion). On a non-seasonally adjusted basis, exports advanced to China (18.2 percent); the EU (2.5 percent) and Canada (2.1 percent), but fell to Japan (-13.9 percent); Brazil (-13.1 percent);  Mexico (-7.8 percent) and OPEC (-3.3 percent).2019-04-17T12:33:00
