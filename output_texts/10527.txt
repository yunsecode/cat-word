Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States edged down 0.5 percent in the week ended June 29th 2018, after a 4.9 percent slump in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications declined 2 percent while applications to purchase a home went up 0.5 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 5bps to 4.79 percent.2018-07-04T11:15:42.59
