US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States went up by 8.209 million barrels in the week ended March 3rd, 2017, following a 1.501 million increase in the previous week and well above market expectations of a 1.967 million rise.
