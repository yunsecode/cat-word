Title: US Trade Deficit Widens In December
Tag: Balance Of Trade

US Trade Deficit Widens In DecemberUnited States Balance Of TradeDecember exports of $191.3 billion and imports of $230.0 billion resulted in a goods and services deficit of $38.7 billion, up from $34.6 billion in November, revised. December exports were $3.5 billion less than November exports of $194.8 billion. December imports were $0.6 billion more than November imports of $229.4 billion.2014-02-06T13:39:04.12
