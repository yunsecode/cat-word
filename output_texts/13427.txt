US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly RiseUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil stocks in the United States unexpectedly increased by 1.310 million barrels in the week ended May 13th, 2016, compared to market expectations of a 2.833 fall.
