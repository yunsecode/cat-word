Wall Street Extends LossesUnited States Stock MarketMarkets in the US closed in the red on Monday, mainly dragged down by financial and bank shares as global growth and credit default concerns weighted on investors. The Dow Jones lost 177 points or 1.1%, the Nasdaq ended 1.82% lower and the S&P 500 was 1.42% down, closing at its lowest level since April of 2014. 2016-02-08T21:30:35.483
