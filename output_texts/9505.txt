Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Wednesday 20 March 2019 amid new concerns over a trade deal between the US and China and a new Fed policy stance. Federal Reserve policymakers now expect rates to remain at current levels this year, compared to December’s projection of two hikes. The FOMC also pledged to start slowing the shrinking of its balance sheet in May and stop the drawdown altogether at the end of September. The Dow Jones declined 142 points or 0.6% to 25746 and the S&P 500 lost 8 points or 0.3% to 2824. In contrast, the Nasdaq edged up 5 points or 0.1% to 7729.2019-03-20T20:11:00
