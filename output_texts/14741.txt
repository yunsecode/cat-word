U.S. GDP Revised Down to 1.3 Percent in Q2United States GDP Growth RateU.S. Real gross domestic product increased at an annual rate  of 1.3 percent in the second quarter of 2012 (that is, from the first quarter to the second quarter), according to the "third" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.
