Title: US Jobless Claims Down to 339K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Down to 339K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending December 28, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 339,000, a decrease of 2,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 341,000. The 4-week moving average was 357,250, an increase of 8,500 from the previous week's revised average of 348,750.2014-01-02T13:33:53.427
