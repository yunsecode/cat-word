Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Post 1st Fall in 4 Weeks
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Post 1st Fall in 4 WeeksUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States declined by 2.508 million barrels in the week ended August 12th, 2016, following a 1.055 million increase in the previous week. It was the first drop in four weeks, compared to market expectations of a 0.5 million rise. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks fell by a higher-than-expected 2.724 million barrels, after declining by 2.807 million in the previous week.2016-08-17T15:35:36.927
