Title: US Business Inventories Drop The Most Since 2011
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Drop The Most Since 2011United States Business InventoriesBusiness inventories in the United States decreased by 0.2 percent month-on-month in November 2015, after a downwardly revised 0.1 percent drop in the previous month and dropping the most since September 2011. Retail inventories excluding autos, which go into the calculation of GDP, rose 0.2 percent in November after a downwardly revised 0.3 percent gain in October.2016-01-15T15:07:03
