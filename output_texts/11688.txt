Title: US Consumer Credit Rises More than Expected
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Rises More than ExpectedUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States rose by USD 18.5 billion in July of 2017, following a downwardly revised USD 11.83 billion gain in June, beating market expectations of USD 15.1 billion. Revolving credit increased by USD 2.62 billion after rising by USD 4.76 billion in the previous month. Non-revolving credit went up by USD 15.88 billion, compared with a USD 7.07 billion gain in June.2017-09-08T19:00:06.633
