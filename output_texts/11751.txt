Title: US Stocks Surge On Tuesday Amid Tax Reform Optimism
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge On Tuesday Amid Tax Reform OptimismUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Tuesday August 22nd of 2017, as stronger than expected earnings reports from mining heavyweights Antofagasta and BHP Billiton nudged global stock markets, while risk aversion retreated a day after President Trump unveiled his military policy for Afghanistan. The Dow Jones gained 196 points or 0.9% to 21900. The S&P 500 jumped 24 points or 1.0% to 2453. The Nasdaq climbed 84 points or 1.4% to 6297. 2017-08-22T20:08:45
