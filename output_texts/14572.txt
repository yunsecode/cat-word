Title: US Jobless Claims Up to 6-Week High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Up to 6-Week HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending January 25, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 348,000, an increase of 19,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 329,000. The 4-week moving average was 333,000, an increase of 750 from the previous week's revised average of 332,250.2014-01-30T13:36:12.587
