Title: US Building Permits Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Building PermitsBuilding permits in the United States jumped 7.4 percent to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1,254 thousand in June 2017 from 1,168 thousand in May and above market expectations of 1,200 thousand. Permits for the construction of multi-family homes rose 13.9 percent to 443 thousand in June and single-family authorizations increased for the first time in four months by 4.1 percent to 811 thousand. The number of building permits issued went up in the Midwest (19.7 percent), in the West (9.9 percent), and in the South (6.9 percent), but declined in the Northeast (-13.9 percent).2017-07-19T12:32:39
