Title: US Export Prices Rise 0.8% MoM in January
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise 0.8% MoM in JanuaryUnited States Export Prices MoMPrices for US exports increased 0.8 percent month-over-month in January of 2018, following a revised 0.1 percent gain in December and above market expectations of a 0.3 percent rise. It was the highest gain in export prices since September. Prices of non-agricultural goods advanced 0.9 percent, the most since September, after gaining 0.1 percent in December, amid increases in costs of nonagricultural industrial supplies and materials; capital goods; automotive vehicles; and consumer goods. Meanwhile, prices of agricultural goods fell 0.1 percent compared to a 0.3 percent decrease in the prior month, driven by a 5.8 percent slump in soybean and other oilseeds prices. Year-on-year, export prices went up 3.4 percent.
