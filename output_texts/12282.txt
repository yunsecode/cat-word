Title: Dollar Continues To Fall
Tag: Currency

Dollar Continues To Fall United States CurrencyThe dollar index extended losses on Wednesday, following a a 0.6% drop on Tuesday and hitting the lowest since January 31st. The greenback started to weaken last week after the Fed signaled only two more rate hikes this year while some investors expected a more aggressive tightening. The slide intensified yesterday amid concerns of President Trump's ability to pass reforms and policies ahead of the health-care bill vote tomorrow.2017-03-22T16:07:18
