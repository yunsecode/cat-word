Title: US Small Business Confidence At Near 2-Yeah High
Tag: NFIB Business Optimism Index

US Small Business Confidence At Near 2-Yeah HighUnited States NFIB Business Optimism IndexThe NFIB Business Optimism Index in the United States increased to 98.4 in November of 2016 from 94.9 in the previous month, above market expectations of 96.7. It was the highest figure since December 2014,  as business owners expected much better business conditions under new leadership in Washington (a net 38 percent from -6 percent). Also, small businesses expected higher sales (net 20 percent from 4 percent) and  job creation plans increased (net 23 percent from 9 percent). 2016-12-13T11:26:56
