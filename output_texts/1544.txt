Title: Wall Street Rallies Amid Upbeat Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Rallies Amid Upbeat EarningsUnited States Stock MarketMajor US stock indices closed sharply higher on Wednesday, as strong corporate results and consumer confidence ramped up hopes that the US economy is more resilient to tighter monetary policy than previously expected. The Dow Jones added 520 points and the S&P 500 jumped 1.5%. The Nasdaq also rose by 1.5% and rebounded from the last session’s underperformance, as Treasury yields stabilized from yesterday’s selloff and supported gains for the tech sector. Nike shares soared 13% after the sporting-goods company posted its best quarterly revenue growth in more than a decade, while FedEx jumped more than 3% as earnings beat estimates. On the data front, the CB consumer confidence gauge topped estimates to reach its highest level since April. On the other hand, existing home sales sank more than anticipated in a sign high mortgage rates are weighing heavily on the housing market. 2022-12-21T21:04:00
