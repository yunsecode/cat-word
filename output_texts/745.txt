Title: US Consumer Sentiment Surprises on the Upside
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Surprises on the UpsideUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan consumer sentiment for the US unexpectedly increased to 63.5 in April of 2023 from 62 in March, beating forecasts of 62, preliminary estimates showed. A rise was seen in both current conditions (68.6 vs 66.3) and expectations (60.3 vs 59.2). Meanwhile inflation for the year-ahead is seen jumping to a five-month high of 4.6% from 3.6% while the five-year gauge was unchanged at 2.9%. While consumers have noted the easing of inflation among durable goods and cars, they still expect high inflation to persist, at least in the short run.2023-04-14T14:06:00
