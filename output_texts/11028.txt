Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Slightly Down
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Slightly DownUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States was revised down to 99.7 in February of 2018 from an initial estimate of 99.9. Still, it was higher than 95.7 in January and market expectations of 99.5. It is the second strongest reading since 2004, with consumers basing their optimism on favorable assessments of jobs, wages, and higher after-tax pay. 2018-03-02T15:06:34.02
