Title: US Crude Oil Socks Fall Much Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Socks Fall Much Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the US edged down by 0.24 million barrels in the latest week, the second consecutive decline, but well below market forecasts of a 1.705 million drop, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. However, stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, jumped by 2.37 million, the biggest rise since mid-February. Gasoline inventories were up by 3.882 million, above forecasts of 1.798 million.2021-12-08T15:37:55.9
