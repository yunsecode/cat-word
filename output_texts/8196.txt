Title: US Stocks Jump on Reopening Bets
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Jump on Reopening BetsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Tuesday despite ongoing civil unrest, as investors focused on the economy reopening and the S&P hit a 12-week high. On Monday night, President Trump warned he will deploy the military if states and cities failed to quell nationwide demonstrations. Meanwhile, New York City imposed a curfew until June 7 to curb protests, while other cities instituted similar measures. Meanwhile, coronavirus cases gradually slow down, yet daily cases remain above 15 thousand and deaths averaging nearly one thousand. The Dow Jones added 268 points or 1.1% to 25,743. The S&P 500 climbed 25 points or 0.8% to 2081. The Nasdaq gained 56 points or 0.6% to 9608.
