Title: US Stock Futures Extend Fresh Records
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Extend Fresh Records United States Stock MarketUS Futures moved higher in early Asian trade on Monday, after the Dow Jones and the S&P 500 finished at record levels Friday as a disappointing payroll report eased worries about the Federal Reserve reducing its massive support anytime soon. Nonfarm payrolls increased by 266K only, well below forecasts of nearly one million, while the unemployment rate rose to 6.1% amid an escalating shortage of available workers. Meantime, traders are monitoring reports of a ransomware attack on the US’ largest fuel pipeline. US Commerce Secretary Gina Raimondo noted that the Federal government is working to avoid supply disruptions after Colonial Pipeline suspended operations on Friday. Last week, the Dow surged 2.7% to snap a two-week losing streak. The S&P 500 added 1.2%. Meanwhile, the tech-heavy Nasdaq dropped 1.5%.2021-05-09T23:51:00
