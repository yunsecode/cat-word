Title: US Exports Hits Record High
Tag: Exports

US Exports Hits Record High United States ExportsExports from the United States increased by 1.8 percent or USD 4.1 billion from the previous month to USD 228.6 billion in February of 2022, the highest since the series began in 1950. Exports of goods increased by USD 2.8 billion to USD 158.8 billion, driven by sales of industrial supplies and materials (+USD 1.7 billion), while sales of consumer goods increased by USD 1.3 billion due to pharmaceutical preparations (+USD 1.5 billion). On the other hand, exports of capital goods fell by USD 0.7 billion due to civilian aircraft (-USD 1.0 billion). Meantime, services exports increased by USD 1.3 billion to USD 69.9 billion, led by travel (+USD 1.2 billion). 2022-04-05T12:39:00
