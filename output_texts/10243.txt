Title: US Retail Sales Rise the Least in 6 Months
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Rise the Least in 6 MonthsUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the US edged up 0.1 percent month-over-month in August of 2018, following an upwardly revised 0.7 percent rise in July and well below market expectations of a 0.4 percent increase. It is the smallest gain in retail sales in six months, mainly due to falls in purchases of motor vehicles, clothing and furniture.
