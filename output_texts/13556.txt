Title: US Exports Post 1st Rise in 5 Months
Tag: Exports

US Exports Post 1st Rise in 5 MonthsUnited States ExportsExports in the United States increased 1 percent to USD 178 billion in February from January of 2016. It is the first gain in five months. Sales of goods increased USD 1.8 billion to USD 118.6 billion, mainly driven by sales of consumer goods, diamonds, pharmaceutical preparations and other goods. Exports to the European Union went up 10.2 percent, those to Canada rose 6.0 percent and sales to Mexico grew 0.9 percent while exports to China fell 2 percent.2016-04-05T13:58:33.1
