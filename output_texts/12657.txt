Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall For 4th Straight Week: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 2.563 million barrels in the week ended December 9th, 2016, following a 2.389 million decline in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 1.584 million decrease. It is the fourth consecutive drop and the biggest in eleven weeks. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks rose by 0.497 million, much lower than expectations of a 2.540 million increase. 2016-12-14T15:35:19.333
