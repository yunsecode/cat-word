Title: US Stocks Rise on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Tuesday after inflation data came in line with expectations while President Trump announced Mike Pompeo, director of the CIA will replace Rex Tillerson as a Secretary of State. The Dow Jones added 118 points or 0.5%; the S&P 500 rose 14 points or 0.5%; and the Nasdaq gained 44 points or 0.6% around 10 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-03-13T13:47:26
