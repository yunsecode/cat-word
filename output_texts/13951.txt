Title: NY Factory Activity Shrinks for 4th Straight Month
Tag: NY Empire State Manufacturing Index

NY Factory Activity Shrinks for 4th Straight MonthUnited States NY Empire State Manufacturing IndexThe New York Empire State Business Conditions Index edged up to -10.74 in November of 2015, after registering -11.36 in the previous month, but remained below zero for the fourth consecutive month. Measures of shipments (-4.1 from -13.61), new orders (-11.82 from -18.91) and employment (-7.27 from -8.49) rose, even as they stayed in negative territory. Six-month outlook was little changed  (20.33 from 23.36), and suggested that optimism about future conditions remained tepid, even though employment is expected to increase. Price indexes pointed to the increase in input prices (4.55 from 0.94) while selling prices (-4.55 from -8.49) continue to decline though at a slower pace.2015-11-16T13:45:30
