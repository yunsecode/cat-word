Title: US Stocks Open Slightly Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Slightly HigherUnited States Stock MarketStock market in the United States opened higher on Monday despite an earlier sell-off in China and falling oil prices. The Dow Jones was up 59 points or 0.36%, the S&P 500 gained 2 points or 0.1% and the Nasdaq rose 4 points or 0.06% 25 minutes after opening. Gains was led by technology, consumer discretionary and consumer staples shares. 2016-01-11T14:55:06
