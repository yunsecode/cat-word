Title: US Stocks Fall After Immigration Ban
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall After Immigration BanUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed lower on Monday amid rising concerns over Trump's policies after the President signed an executive order temporarily banning immigration from Iraq, Syria, Iran, Sudan, Libya, Somalia and Yemen, leading to protests and confusions at the airports. The Dow Jones fell 123 points or 0.6%, the S&P 500 went down 14 points or 0.6% and the Nasdaq lost 47 points or 0.8%, the worst result of this year.2017-01-30T21:21:08.53
