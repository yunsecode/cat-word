Title: US Labor Force Participation Rate Drops to 62.7%
Tag: Labor Force Participation Rate

US Labor Force Participation Rate Drops to 62.7%United States Labor Force Participation RateThe labor force participation rate in the US decreased by 0.7 percentage point over the month to 62.7 percent, the lowest since August 2018. Total employment fell by 3.0 million to 155.8 million, and the employment-population ratio, at 60.0 percent, dropped by 1.1 percentage points over the month.2020-04-03T12:42:24.233
