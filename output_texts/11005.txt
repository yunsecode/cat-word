Title: US Consumer Credit Climbs Below Expectations
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Climbs Below ExpectationsUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States rose by USD 13.91 billion in January of 2018, following an upwardly revised USD 19.21 billion gain in the previous month and below market expectations of USD 17.90 billion. Revolving credit edged up by USD 0.70 billion after rising by USD 6.10 billion in the previous month.
