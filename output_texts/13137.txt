Title: US Business Inventories Rise 0.2% MoM in June
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Rise 0.2% MoM in JuneUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the United States went up 0.2 percent month-on-month in June 2016, the same pace as in the previous month and above market expectations of 0.1 percent gain. Retail inventories excluding autos, which go into the calculation of GDP, rose 0.2 percent in June after a 0.3 percent gain in May.2016-08-12T15:05:57.923
