Title: Fed Yellen Shows Confidence in US Economy
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Yellen Shows Confidence in US EconomyUnited States Interest RateEconomic and financial data received since last FOMC meeting has been in line with forecasts and economic growth is expected to be sufficient to achieve labor-market improvement and higher inflation, Fed Chair Yellen said in a speech at the Economic Club of Washington, suggesting the Fed is on track to raise rates in December. However, Fed Chair also said new economic data received until next meeting will be assessed. 2015-12-02T17:48:35.327
