Title: US Stocks End Mixed on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketUS Stocks closed mixed on Thursday 24 January 2019, as attention shifted from the IMF’s revised growth forecasts to earnings season and the US government shutdown, with the Senate blocking bills on Thursday to fund the federal government. The Dow Jones edged down 22 points or 0.1% to 24553. The S&P 500 inched up 4 points or 0.1% to 2642. The Nasdaq climbed 48 points or 0.7% to 7074.2019-01-24T21:11:00
