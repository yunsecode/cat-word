Title: US Inflation Rate Edges Up to 2.2%
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Edges Up to 2.2%United States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States increased 2.2 percent year-on-year in February of 2018, slightly above 2.1 percent in January and in line with market expectations. The monthly rate eased to 0.2 percent from 0.5 percent, also matching forecasts. Prices rose for shelter, apparel, and motor vehicle insurance while food index was unchanged and energy increased only slightly. 2018-03-13T12:33:12.837
