Title: Dollar at Near 2-Year High
Tag: currency

Dollar at Near 2-Year HighUnited States currencyThe dollar index hit a 22-month high of 97.712 on Tuesday after data showed new home sales in the US, which account for about 11.7 percent of housing market sales, increased for the third straight month in March to their highest level since November 2017, while markets had forecast a 2.5 percent fall. The data follows recent positive news on retail sales and exports.2019-04-23T14:15:00
