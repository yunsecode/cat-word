Title: US Jobless Claims Lower Than Expected At 247K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Lower Than Expected At 247KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 10 thousand to 247 thousand in the week ended January 7th 2017, below market expectations of 255 thousand. It is the 97th consecutive week of initial claims below 300 thousand, the longest streak since 1970. Yet, the 4-week moving average that removes week-to-week volatility fell by 1.75 thousand to 256.5 thousand.2017-01-12T13:33:19
