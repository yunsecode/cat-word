Title: US Stocks Trade Higher after Volatile Week
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Higher after Volatile WeekUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks rose on Friday despite trade uncertainty and global growth worries, with shares of Intel, Walgreens Boots, Caterpillar, IBM and Cisco among the best performers. The Dow Jones climbed 157 points, or 0.6%; the S&P 500 advanced 24 points, or 0.9%; and the Nasdaq jumped 85 points, or 1.1% around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2019-08-16T13:45:00
