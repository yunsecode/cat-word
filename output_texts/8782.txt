Title: Dow Drops 1,000 Points
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Drops 1,000 PointsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks traded sharply lower on Monday, following its Asian and European peers, amid renewed concerns about the global impact of the coronavirus after the number of cases outside China increased, namely in Italy, South Korea and Iran. The Dow Jones plummeted 1,040 points, the most since February 2018; the S&P went down 3.5%, the biggest percentage drop since October 2018; and the Nasdaq tumbled 4.1% around 1 pm NY time.2020-02-24T17:27:00
