Title: US Inflation Rate Rises Above Forecasts in March
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Rises Above Forecasts in MarchUnited States Inflation RateThe US annual inflation rate rose to 1.9 percent in March 2019 from a two-and-a-half-year low of 1.5 percent in the previous month, slightly above market consensus of 1.8 percent. Food prices rose at faster pace while energy deflation eased. The core inflation rate, which excludes volatile items such as food and energy, edged down to 2 percent from 2.1 percent in February, just below forecasts of 2.1 percent.2019-04-10T12:32:00
