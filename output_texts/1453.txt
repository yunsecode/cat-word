Title: US CPI Falls for 1st Time since May 2020
Tag: Inflation Rate MoM

US CPI Falls for 1st Time since May 2020United States Inflation Rate MoMThe Consumer Price Index in the United States decreased 0.1% month-over-month in December of 2022, the first decline since May of 2020 and beating market forecasts of a flat reading. Gasoline costs dropped 9.4%, more than offsetting a 0.8% increase in shelter prices. Food prices were up 0.3%, below 0.5% in the previous month. Other increases were also seen for household furnishings and operations (0.3%), motor vehicle insurance (0.6%), recreation (0.2%), and apparel (0.5%). In contrast, the indexes for used cars and trucks (-2.5%), and airline fares (-3.1%) were among those that decreased over the month.2023-01-12T13:41:00
