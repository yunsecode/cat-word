Title: US Import Prices Drop The Most In 9 Months
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Drop The Most In 9 MonthsUnited States Import Prices MoMPrices for US imports decreased by 0.3 percent month-over-month in November 2016, following a downwardly revised 0.4 percent rise in October but above market consensus of a 0.4 percent fall. It was the largest drop since February, as import fuel prices fell 3.9 percent, after a 6.9 percent gain in October; while non-fuel prices fell for the second straight month by 0.1 percent. Despite rising throughout much of 2016, import prices remained down on a yearly basis, declining 0.1 percent between November 2015 and November 2016.2016-12-13T13:36:19
