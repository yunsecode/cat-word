Title: US Stocks Book Fresh Records on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Book Fresh Records on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately in the green on Thursday 11 January 2018, as energy shares rallied following a recent upswing in oil prices and lower gas inventories, and as investors awaited Friday’s banks earnings releases. All three major indices booked fresh highs. The Dow Jones gained 206 points or 0.8% to 25575. The S&P 500 advanced 20 points or 0.7% to 2768. The Nasdaq climbed 58 points or 0.8% to 7212.2018-01-11T21:11:12.38
