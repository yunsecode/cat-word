Title: US Stocks Rebound Sharply as House Passes Tax Bill
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound Sharply as House Passes Tax BillUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Thursday, following losses in the previous 2 sessions. Sentiment was nudged after House Republicans passed a tax bill to overhaul the current fiscal code by slashing the corporate tax rate. The Senate is still debating its own separate plan. Investors' mood also improved after better-than-expected earnings for Cisco and Wal-Mart. The Dow Jones advanced 187 points or 0.8% to 23458. The S&P 500 gained 21 points or 0.8% to 2585. The Nasdaq surged by 87 points or 1.3% to a new record of 6793.2017-11-16T21:03:36
