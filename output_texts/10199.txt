Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Rise Unexpectedly
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Rise UnexpectedlyUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 1.852 million barrels in the week ended September 21st 2018, following a 2.057 million drop in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 1.279 million decrease. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories increased by 1.530 million barrels while markets expected a 0.788 million gain.2018-09-26T14:31:48.717
