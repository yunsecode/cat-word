US Stocks Plummet as Rout ExtendsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed sharply lower on Wednesday 10 October 2018 to record the biggest drop since February as the sell-off triggered by rising yields continued and the tech rout extended, with Netflix falling 8% and Amazon 5%. The Dow Jones lost 832 points or 3.2% to 25599. The S&P 500 retreated 95 points or 3.3% to 2786. The Nasdaq plunged 316 points or 4.1% to 7422.2018-10-10T20:09:49.273
