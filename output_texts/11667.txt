Title: US Consumer Sentiment Falls Less Than Expected
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Falls Less Than ExpectedUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States fell to 95.3 in September of 2017 from 96.8 in August but slightly above market expectations of 95.1. It is the lowest reading in three months, as the two hurricanes raised concerns over the outlook for the national economy, leading to a fall in expectations. 2017-09-15T14:11:17
