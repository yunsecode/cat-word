Title: US Stocks Gain on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Gain on Tuesday United States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Tuesday as investors await a flash PMI reading and housing data while better-than-expected earnings reports for Best Buy lifted the mood despite a further drop in oil prices. The Dow Jones was up 101 points or 0.6%, the S&P 500 gained 10 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq traded 25 points or 0.5% higher around 8 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-08-23T14:43:57.82
