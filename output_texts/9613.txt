Title: US Crude Inventories Rise More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil stocks increased by 3.672 million barrels in the week ending February 15th, 2019, following a 3.633 million advance in the previous week and above market expectations of a 3.08 million rise. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories fell by 1.454 million barrels.2019-02-21T16:04:00
