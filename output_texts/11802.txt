Tag: stock market

Dow Hits New Record After Strong Jobs ReportUnited States stock marketThe Dow Jones gained 52 points, or 0.2%, to a new all-time high of 22,078 on Friday, following stronger than expected jobs data from the US. Also, the S&P 500 advanced 6 points, or 0.2%, to 2,478; and the Nasdaq rose 10 points, or 0.2%, to 6,351 around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2017-08-04T13:29:03
