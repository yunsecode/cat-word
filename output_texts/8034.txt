Title: Dollar Appreciates on Tuesday
Tag: Currency

Dollar Appreciates on Tuesday United States CurrencyThe dollar index was trading higher at 96.9 on Tuesday as investors' risk appetite eased amid mounting concerns about a spike in coronavirus cases in the US, India, and Brazil. At the same time, better-than-expected economic data for the US continues to offer some hope about the economic recovery. In contrast, the European Commission lowered its GDP forecasts for 2020 and 2021 for Europe, saying that the lifting of COVID-19 lockdown measures in some countries was proceeding less swiftly than it had initially predicted.2020-07-07T09:41:00
