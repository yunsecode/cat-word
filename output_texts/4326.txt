Title: Dollar Finds Footing as Taper Looms
Tag: Currency

Dollar Finds Footing as Taper Looms United States CurrencyThe dollar index regained some ground above the 95 mark on Friday, recovering from a sell-off that took it to its lowest level in nearly a month as investors reassessed the outlook for tightening monetary policy. The market moves came after the US labour report showed the US economy added 467K jobs in January of 2022, much above markets expectations of 150K, pointing to the much weaker impact of the Omicron variant on US employment. Such a reading raised the odds of interest rate rises to cool a rapidly growing economy and tame inflation. 2022-02-04T13:57:00
