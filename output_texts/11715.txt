Title: US Jobless Rate Unexpectedly Rises to 4.4% in August
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Unexpectedly Rises to 4.4% in AugustUnited States Unemployment RateUS unemployment rate unexpectedly rose to 4.4 percent in August 2017 from 4.3 percent in the previous month and above market consensus of 4.3 percent. The number of unemployed persons increased by 151 thousand to 7.1 million while the labor force participation rate was flat at 62.9 percent. 2017-09-01T12:30:48
