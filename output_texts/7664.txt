Title: Dollar on Track for 2nd Week of Gains
Tag: Currency

Dollar on Track for 2nd Week of GainsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded around 93.2 on Friday and was on track to report an 0.5% for the week, the first back-to-back weekly gain since May. The dollar reached a 28-month low of 92.144 on August 31st but has recovered more than 1% since then amid rising optimism about the US economic recovery.2020-09-11T09:19:00
