Title: US ISM New York Index Falls Further in February
Tag: Ism New York Index

US ISM New York Index Falls Further in FebruaryUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions index in the United States decreased to 61.1 in February of 2019 from 63.4 in January, pointing to a new-low growth in the New York area since June. Expected revenues fell (65.4 from 71.4 in January) while current revenues were seen higher (76.9 from 60), prices paid slowed (60.7 from 72.5) and both quantity of purchases (65.4 from 44.7) and employment (60.7 from 53.1) increased. Meanwhile, the Six-Month Outlook improved to 71.5 from 58.3.2019-03-04T15:26:37.703
