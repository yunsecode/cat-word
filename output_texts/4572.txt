Title: Dollar Holds Near 2-Month Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar Holds Near 2-Month LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index declined for a 3rd straight session to below 95 on Thursday, as hot inflation data did not have much of an effect on an already hawkish rate outlook. The December consumer price index increased 7% from a year earlier, accelerating at its fastest pace since June 1982. However, the hot inflation report failed to support the dollar further as it came in line with expectations and has been largely priced in to the markets. Moreover, Federal Reserve chair Jerome Powell dashed expectations for a more hawkish approach in a Congressional testimony earlier this week. While acknowledging that the US economy was ready for higher interest rates and quantitative tightening to combat inflation, he said policymakers were still debating approaches to reducing the Fed’s balance sheet which could take up to four meetings to arrive at such a decision.2022-01-13T06:27:00
