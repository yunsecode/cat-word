Title: US Export Prices Rise 0.2% MoM In October
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise 0.2% MoM In October United States Export Prices MoMUS export prices rose 0.2 percent on month in October 2016, after increasing by 0.3 percent in September and in a line with market expectations. Prices of agricultural exports' went up 0.4 percent, compared to a 1.1 percent drop in the previous month, led by fruits (+4.4 percent), nuts (+5.1 percent), and wheat (+4 percent). Also, cost of non-agricultural exports rose 0.2 percent, following a 0.4 percent growth, as higher prices for industrial supplies and materials and automotive vehicles more than offset falling prices for consumer and capital goods. Year-on-year, export prices declined 1.1 percent, the lowest fall since October 2014. 2016-11-15T13:39:26.48
