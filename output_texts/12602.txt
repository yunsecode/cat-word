Title: US Stocks Close Lower On Wednesday

US Stocks Close Lower On WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks finished lower on Wednesday, after the pending home sales unexpectedly fell to ten-month low level. The Dow lost 111 points, or 0.6% to 19,884, while investors awaited it would nudge beyond 20,000, a level it has never breached. The S&P 500 declined 19 point, or 0.8%; and the Nasdaq decreased 49 points, or 0.9%.2016-12-28T21:23:53
