Title: US GDP Growth Confirmed at 2.5% in Q2
Tag: Gdp Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Confirmed at 2.5% in Q2United States Gdp Growth RateUS GDP expanded at an annual rate of 2.5 percent in the second quarter of 2013, according to BEA's third estimate and unchanged from second estimate released later August. The acceleration in GDP primarily reflected upturns in exports and in nonresidential fixed investment, a smaller decrease in federal government spending, and an upturn in state and local government spending that were partly offset by an acceleration in imports and deceleration in private inventory investment and in personal consumption expenditure.2013-09-26T13:45:43.49
