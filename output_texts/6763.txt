Title: US Jobless Rate Seen at 6.7% in January
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Seen at 6.7% in JanuaryUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate is seen unchanged at 6.7 percent in January 2021, adding to signs of a slowdown in the labor market recovery amid a lack of fiscal stimulus and record COVID-19 infections that prompted many US states to impose restrictive measures to respond to the outbreak. The jobless rate has slowed from an all-time high of 14.8 percent seen in April last year, but remains well above pre-pandemic levels of about 3.5 percent.2021-02-05T09:45:45.29
