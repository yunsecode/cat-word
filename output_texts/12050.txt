Title: US Corporate Profits Unexpectedly Fall In Q1
Tag: Corporate Profits

US Corporate Profits Unexpectedly Fall In Q1United States Corporate ProfitsCorporate profits in the United States fell by $40.3 billion, or 2.5 percent, to $1571.2 billion in the first quarter of 2017, following a 2.3 percent rise in the previous period, and well below market expectations of 2.3 percent increase, according to preliminary estimates. The undistributed profits shrank by $49.6 billion, or 7.9 percent, to $579.8 billion while the net dividends rose $9.3 billion, or 1 percent, to $991.3 billion. Also, net cash flow with inventory valuation adjustment, the internal funds available to corporations for investment, went down by $39.5 billion, or 1.8 percent, to $2152.6 billion.2017-05-26T12:38:14.627
