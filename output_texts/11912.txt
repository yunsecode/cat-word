Title: US Jobless Claims Rise To 5-Week High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise To 5-Week High United States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 4 thousand to 248 thousand in the week ended July 1st from the previous week's unrevised level of 244 thousand and above market expectations of 243 thousand. It was the highest level since the week ended May 27th. Meanwhile, the 4-week moving average, which removes week-to-week volatility, increased by 750 to 243 thousand.2017-07-06T12:34:34.517
