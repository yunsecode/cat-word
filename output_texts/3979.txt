Title: US Personal Income Rises 0.5% in February
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Rises 0.5% in FebruaryUnited States Personal IncomePersonal income in the United States increased 0.5 percent from a month earlier in February 2022, following a revised 0.1 percent growth in January and matching market expectations, as a rise in compensation offset a decrease in government social benefits. The increase in compensation reflected gains in private and government wages and salaries. In contrast, a reduction in “other” benefits within government social benefits was partly offset by increases in Medicare and Medicaid. 2022-03-31T12:32:00
