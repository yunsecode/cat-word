Title: Chicago PMI Remains Highest Since January 2015
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago PMI Remains Highest Since January 2015United States Chicago PmiThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer rose to 57.7 in March of 2017 from 57.4 in the previous month, beating market expectations of 56.9. It is the highest reading since January 2015, as production, new orders, backlogs of work and inventories expanded while employment slipped back into contraction. The Q1 average came in at 55.1, the highest level since Q4 2014.2017-03-31T13:55:35
