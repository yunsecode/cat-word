Title: US Stocks Gain On Banks
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Gain On BanksUnited States Stock MarketUS equities traded higher on Monday with bank and technology stocks among the best performers ahead of June's manufacturing activity figures and auto sales. The Dow Jones added 119 points, or 0.6%, to 21,469; the S&P 500 went up 12 points, or 0.5%, to 2,436; and the Nasdaq gained 20 points, or 0.3%, to 6,160 around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2017-07-03T13:44:42
