Title: US Stocks Edge Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge LowerUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones and S&P 500 fell on Monday from all-time highs reported last week as investors await more details on a fresh pandemic relief package, and remain concerned about rising coronavirus cases and a new round of lockdowns. US lawmakers are working on the approval of a new $908 billion bill before Congress breaks for the year, with House Speaker Pelosi, Senate Minority leader Schumer and a number of Republican senators endorsing the outline of the plan. A weaker-than-expected US jobs report was also seen as evidence that relief spending was becoming urgent. Meanwhile, the Nasdaq hit a new record.2020-12-07T14:32:00
