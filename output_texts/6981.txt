Title: US Stocks Fall from Record Highs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall from Record HighsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street sank on Monday, moving away from record close levels on Friday, as investors digest prospects of further stimulus, political turmoil and rising coronavirus infections. On Friday, Joe Biden said his economic package will be in the trillions of dollars and that more details will be provided Thursday during a formal announcement. Also, House Speaker Nancy Pelosi said the House will move to impeach President Trump as soon as this week, unless Vice President Mike Pence and the cabinet invoke the 25th Amendment, a move which will likely create some instability in the Congress. Meanwhile, the earnings season will kick off later in the week with reports from Citigroup, Wells Fargo and JPMorgan. S&P 500 earnings are seen dropping 12% yoy in the final three months of 2020, according to data from Bloomberg. 2021-01-11T14:31:00
