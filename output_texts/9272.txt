Title: US Stocks Retreat on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Retreat on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Monday 20 May 2019, with tech stocks booking sharp losses after Google, Intel, Qualcomm and Broadcom suspended some business with Huawei Technologies following the Trump administration's decision to add the Chinese telecommunications giant to a trade blacklist. The Dow Jones lost 84 points or 0.3%. The S&P 500 retreated 19 points or 0.7%. The Nasdaq plummeted 114 points or 1.5%.2019-05-20T20:09:45.29
