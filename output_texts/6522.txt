Title: Dollar on the Rise as Yields Soar
Tag: Currency

Dollar on the Rise as Yields SoarUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index jumped to 91.8 on Friday as Treasury yields soared again to levels not seen in over a year amid prospects of a fast economic recovery. President Biden signed the $1.9 trillion stimulus bill into law on Thursday and Treasury Secretary Yellen said Americans could start receiving payments during the weekend while the recent claims report showed initial claims were the lowest in 4 months. The greenback has gained more than 2% so far this year as upbeat economic data and massive stimulus bill prompted fears of rising inflation and led to a bond-selloff.2021-03-12T09:08:34.37
