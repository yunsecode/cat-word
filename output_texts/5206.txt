US Consumer Credit Lower than ExpectedUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States increased by USD 14.38 billion in August of 2021, following an upwardly revised USD 17.2 billion gain in the previous month, missing market expectations of a USD 17.5 billion rise.
