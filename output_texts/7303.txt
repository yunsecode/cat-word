Title: US Stock Futures Rise
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures RiseUnited States Stock MarketStock futures in the US rose markedly on Wednesday, amid rotation of technology and stay-at-home stocks into cyclical names following an announcement from Pfizer and BioNTech that their COVID-19 vaccine was more than 90% effective in large-scale trials. Meantime, the US Food and Drug Administration said that Eli Lilly’s antibody-drug could be used to treat mild-to-moderate cases of coronavirus in patients who are older than 12 years old. On Tuesday, the Dow Jones added 262 points or 0.9% to 29,420. In contrast, the S&P 500 lost 5 points or 0.1% to 3546, while the Nasdaq sank 160 points or 1.4% to 11,554.2020-11-11T08:13:00
