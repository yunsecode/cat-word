Title: Dollar Strengthens as Risk Appetite Diminishes
Tag: Currency

Dollar Strengthens as Risk Appetite DiminishesUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index bounced back from 3-week lows to trade around 93.5, amid a general pause in risk appetite as investors await the US earnings season to start and digest a pause in Johnson & Johnson’s Covid-19 vaccine study due to an unexplained illness in a study participant. Traders continue to follow any developments on the new stimulus bill. The Trump administration called on Congress to pass a simpler bill using leftover funds from an expired small-business loan program, after the near $1.9 trillion proposal hit resistance from both Democrats and Republicans over the weekend. 2020-10-13T07:56:00
