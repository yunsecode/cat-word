Title: US Crude Stocks Fall by Most Since October
Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

US Crude Stocks Fall by Most Since OctoberUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 5.8 million barrels in the week ended January 8th of 2021, following a 1.7 million drop in the previous week, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed. It was the third straight weekly decline and the biggest draw since the week ended October 30th. 2021-01-12T21:39:08.957
