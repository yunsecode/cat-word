Title: US Trade Deficit Narrows in November
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit Narrows in NovemberUnited States Balance of TradeUS goods and services deficit was $42.4 billion in November of 2015, down $2.2 billion from $44.6 billion in October. The lower deficit was mostly a result of falling shipments of electronic goods as well as lower prices for crude oil and other commodities such as steel.
