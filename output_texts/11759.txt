Title: US Stocks Fall on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded sharply in the red on Thursday, mainly weighed down by tech and financial shares amid disappointing results for Cisco and Wal-Mart. Investors were also concerned over President Trump's ability to deliver pro growth reforms as some advisory groups were dissolved in response to the President's actions after the Charlottesville episode. The Dow Jones was down 162 points or 0.7%, the S&P 500 declined 24 points or 1% and the Nasdaq lost 75 points or 1.2% around 1:36 PM NY time.2017-08-17T17:53:39
