Title: US Wholesale Stocks Fall in September
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Stocks Fall in SeptemberUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US fell 0.1 percent month-over-month in September 2020, following a downwardly revised 0.3 percent gain in August, a preliminary estimate showed. Durable goods stocks declined 0.1 percent, compared to a 0.5 percent advance in the previous period, while nondurable goods inventories were flat for the second consecutive month. Year-on-year, wholesale stocks dropped 4.5 percent.2020-10-28T12:36:00
