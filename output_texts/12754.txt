Title: US Stocks Rise, Reach Intraday High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise, Reach Intraday HighUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Monday amid a dollar retreat and a rise in oil prices. The Dow Jones added 58 points or 0.3%, the S&P 500 gained 10 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq rose 28 points or 0.5% around 8 minutes after the opening bell. All the three indexes reached record intraday high.2016-11-21T14:49:06
