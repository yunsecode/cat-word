Title: US Stocks Rise on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Monday, extending gains from Friday. Investors shrug off trade war fears as the US economy added more jobs than expected in May. The Dow Jones went up 214 points or 0.9%, the S&P 500 added 14 points or 0.5% and the Nasdaq traded 34 points or 0.5% higher around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2018-06-04T13:53:00
