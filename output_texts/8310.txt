Title: Dollar Trades Nearly Flat
Tag: Currency

Dollar Trades Nearly Flat United States CurrencyThe dollar index erased gains to trade around 100.1 on Tuesday after hitting a 2-week high of 100.445 earlier in the session as investors’ risk appetite dampened amid worries about a second wave of coronavirus infections as several countries reopen their economies. Both China and South Korea reported new local clusters of infections and the number of new cases also increased in Germany. 2020-05-12T07:16:31.13
