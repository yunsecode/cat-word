Title: US Jobless Claims at 5-Month High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims at 5-Month HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 17 thousand to 268 thousand in the week ended November 26th from the previous week's level of 251 thousand. It is the highest figure since the last week of June and above market expectations of 253 thousand. However, it is the 91st consecutive weeks of initial claims below 300,000, the longest streak since 1970.2016-12-01T13:33:43
