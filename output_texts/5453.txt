Title: Dollar Up for 2nd Day
Tag: Currency

Dollar Up for 2nd DayUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index gained for a 2nd day to 92.7 on Tuesday as investors continue to worry that weak data and the rapid spread of the coronavirus delta variant are pointing to a slowdown in global economic recovery. The growth in Chinese retail sales and industrial output slowed while US consumer sentiment cooled to a near decade-low. Meanwhile, investors await more clarity from the Fed on the tapering timeline with FOMC minutes due on Wednesday and a town hall meeting with Chair Jerome Powell on Tuesday.2021-08-17T07:08:55.333
