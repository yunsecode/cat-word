Title: US Jobless Claims at 5-Month Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims at 5-Month LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits was 259,000 in the week ended March 5th, a decrease of 18,000 from the previous week's revised level of 277,000 and hitting its lowest level since October 17th. The reading came in better than expected, pointing to sustained strength in the labor market. 2016-03-10T13:35:50
