Title: US Stocks Rise amid Strong Payrolls
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise amid Strong PayrollsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Friday, extending gains from the previous session after the jobs report showed the US economy added 313K jobs, the most since July of 2016. The Dow Jones was up 153 points or 0.6%, the S&P 500 added 15 points or 0.5% and the Nasdaq gained 48 points or 0.7% around 5 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-03-09T14:35:56
