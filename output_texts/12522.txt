Title: US Building Permits Fall Unexpectedly In December
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Fall Unexpectedly In December United States Building PermitsBuilding permits in the United States fell 0.2 percent month-over-month to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1210 thousand in December 2016 from 1212 thousand in November while markets were expecting a 1.1 percent gain to 1225 thousand. Authorization for 5 units or more declined 9 percent to 393 thousand while single-family permits rose 4.7 percent to 817 thousand. Decreases occurred in the South (-2.9 percent), while permits increased in the West (+3.3 percent), the Northeast (+2.7 percent) and the Midwest (+0.5 percent). Year-on-year, building permits increased 0.7 percent.2017-01-19T13:45:20.143
