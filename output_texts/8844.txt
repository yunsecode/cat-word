Title: US Employment Costs Rise 0.7% in Q4
Tag: Employment Cost Index

US Employment Costs Rise 0.7% in Q4United States Employment Cost IndexCompensation costs for civilian workers in the US increased 0.7 percent on quarter in the three months to December 2019, the same as in the previous period and in line with market expectations. Wages and salaries rose 0.7 percent, below a 0.9 percent gain in the third quarter; and benefit costs went up 0.5 percent, slower than 0.6 percent. Year-on-year, compensation costs for civilian workers increased 2.7 percent in the fourth quarter, easing from a 2.8 percent rise in the prior period.2020-01-31T13:32:00
