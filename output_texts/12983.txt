US Services Sector Growth Picks Up in September: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe flash Markit US Services PMI rose to 51.9 in September from 51 in August, and above market expectations of 51.1. It was the sharpest rise in activity since April mostly due to growth in ongoing new business while new orders, employment and inflationary pressures eased.2016-09-27T13:54:18
