Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Holds at 1-Month High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Holds at 1-Month HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note stood above 1.34% on Friday, remaining close to its highest since mid-July, as signs of continued labor market recovery and rising inflationary pressure continued to support prospects of early tapering by the Federal Reserve. Yields have been also bolstered by comments from two Fed officials on Tuesday that suggested the central bank could soon reduce or taper its asset purchases.2021-08-13T08:40:53.14
