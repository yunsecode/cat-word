Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Output Growth at 1-1/2-Year HighUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI increased to 54.7 in August 2020 from 50.3 in the previous month, signaling the sharpest upturn in private sector business activity since February 2019. Service sector activity expanded by the most for 17 months, while goods manufacturers recorded the fastest increase in production since January 2019.2020-08-21T13:57:00
