Title: US Treasury Yields Rise Ahead of Inflation Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Rise Ahead of Inflation DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury note was slightly higher at 1.51% on Friday as investors await the highly-anticipated CPI release later in the day which will likely show inflation was the highest since 1982 last month, strengthening the case for the Fed to double the pace of taper next week. Traders also weigh the risks to economic growth from omicron restrictions. 2021-12-10T09:49:45.217
