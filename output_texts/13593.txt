Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 3.3 percent in the week ended March 18th 2016 from the previous period, following a 3.3 percent drop last week while fixed 30-year mortgage rates edged down 1 bps to 3.93 percent, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home fell 1 percent and refinance applications shrank 4.9 percent.
