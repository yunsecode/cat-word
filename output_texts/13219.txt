US Stocks Fail to Sustain Gains on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened mostly lower on Monday as investors were cautious ahead of the biggest week for corporate earnings results, Fed and BoJ monetary policy decisions and falling oil prices. The Dow Jones was down 35 points or 0.2% as Verizon shares fell after the company confirmed it would buy Yahoo for $4.83 billion.
