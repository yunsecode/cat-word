Title: US Stocks Rebound on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green, as coronavirus vaccine news outweighed rising infections. On Monday, AstraZeneca and the University of Oxford said their vaccine candidate was up to 90% effective, becoming the third inoculation this month that was revealed to be effective in trial data. Also, stocks hit session highs amid news that President-elect Joe Biden plans to nominate former Federal Reserve Chair Janet Yellen for Treasury secretary. Meanwhile, the US reported a record-high spike of more than 195,500 new cases on Friday. On the macro side, Goldman Sachs became the second major bank to cut 4Q20 and 1Q21 GDP growth forecasts for the US. The Dow Jones gained 330 points or 1.1% to 29,594. The S&P 500 rose 20 points or 0.6% to 3578. The Nasdaq added 26 points or 0.2% to 11,881.
