Title: US Jobless Rate Falls to Lowest since 1969
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Falls to Lowest since 1969United States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate fell to 3.6 percent in April 2019 from 3.8 percent in the previous month, below market expectations of 3.8 percent. It was the lowest jobless rate since December 1969, as the number of unemployed persons went down by 387 thousand to 5.8 million while employment declined by 103 thousand to 156.6 million. The participation rate dropped to 62.8 percent in April from 63 percent in March and lower than market consensus of 62.9 percent.2019-05-03T12:31:00
