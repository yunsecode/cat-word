Title: US Stocks Surge on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Monday 26 November 2018, as Amazon (+5.3%) rose sharply on solid Black Friday sales and as the EU and the UK reached an agreement on the terms of Brexit on Sunday. The Dow Jones gained 354 points or 1.5% to 24640. The S&P 500 advanced 41 points or 1.6% to 2674. The Nasdaq surged 143 points or 2.1% to 7082.2018-11-26T21:11:56.32
