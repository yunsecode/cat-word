Title: US Service Sector Remains in Contraction Territory
Tag: Services PMI

US Service Sector Remains in Contraction TerritoryUnited States Services PMIThe S&P Global US Services PMI increased to 46.6 in January 2023, up from 44.7 at the end of 2022 and above market expectations of 45.0, a preliminary estimate showed. The latest reading signaled a solid fall in service sector output, but one that was the softest since last October, amid customer hesitancy and high inflation. The decrease in new business was only marginal overall, while employment rose only fractionally and backlogs of work declined further. On the price front, input cost inflation accelerated and output price inflation was unchanged from December. Finally, business confidence reached a four-month high amid hopes that domestic and external demand conditions improve.2023-01-24T15:00:45.893
