Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States declined 0.7 percent in the week ended December 2nd 2016 from the prior period, following a 9.4 percent slump in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went down 0.7 percent while applications to purchase a home increased 0.4 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates rose 4bps to 4.27 percent, the highest since October of 2014.2016-12-07T12:12:02.307
