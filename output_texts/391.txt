Title: DXY Up to 2-Month High
Tag: Currency

DXY Up to 2-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rose above 103 on Thursday, the strongest in 2 months, underpinned by growing optimism that the US government would strike a deal on the debt ceiling and avoid a default. President Joe Biden and House Speaker Kevin McCarthy both indicated confidence late on Wednesday that the US government will not default on its debt after a months-long standoff. On top of that, a pullback in jobless claims pointed to a still-tight labor market, dashing hopes for interest rate cuts soon. Also, Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee said it was “far too premature to be talking about rate cuts,” while Cleveland Fed President Loretta Mester said rates were not yet at a point where the central bank could hold steady due to stubborn inflation.2023-05-18T12:48:00
