Title: US Stocks Lower on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Lower on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Thursday amid higher oil prices and mixed economic data: GDP for the third quarter was revised up and durable goods fell slightly less than anticipated while jobless claims came above forecasts and personal income and spending disappointed. The Dow Jones fell 23 points or 0.1%, failing to top the 20,000 milestone, the S&P 500 lost 4 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq declined 24 points or 0.4%.    2016-12-22T21:14:40.307
