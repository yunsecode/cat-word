Title: Dollar Heads for Best Week Since October
Tag: Currency

Dollar Heads for Best Week Since OctoberUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index gained further ground on Friday, breaking through 91.9 for the first time in three months and heading for its best weekly gain since October, as soaring US Treasury yields lent some optimism to the greenback bulls. In recent remarks, Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell once again downplayed concerns about higher interest rates and inflation, which, in turn, prompted a massive selloff in the US bond markets. On the fiscal front, the Senate has started debating President Biden’s $1.9 trillion coronavirus relief package, setting the stage for its approval as soon as this weekend. February's nonfarm payrolls and the unemployment rate will be the key drivers later today. 2021-03-05T01:59:00
