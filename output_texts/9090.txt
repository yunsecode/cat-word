Title: US MBA Mortgage Applications Rebound
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US MBA Mortgage Applications ReboundUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 8.1 percent in the week ended September 27th 2019, rebounding from a 10.1 percent fall in the previous month, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications jumped 14.2 percent and applications to purchase a home increased 0.9 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went down by 3bps to 3.99 percent.2019-10-02T11:03:00
