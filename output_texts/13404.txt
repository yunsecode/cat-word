Title: European Stocks Trade Mostly Lower Ahead of Yellen

European Stocks Trade Mostly Lower Ahead of YellenUnited States Stock MarketEquities in Europe traded mainly in the red on Friday, mainly due to losses in auto and banking shares while oil prices retreated and investors await Fed’s Chair Yellen speech later in the day for further clues about Fed next steps. The FTSE 100 traded near the flat line at 6265 points, the DAX 30 lost 0.2% or 20 points, the CAC 40 fell 0.2% or 10 points, the IBEX 35 slid 0.3% or 27 points, and the FTSE MIB decreased 0.5% or 92 points around 10:20 AM London time.2016-05-27T10:20:02.783
