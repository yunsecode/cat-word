Title: US Goods Gap Narrows in July
Tag: Goods Trade Balance

US Goods Gap Narrows in JulyUnited States Goods Trade BalanceThe US goods trade deficit narrowed to USD 86.38 billion in July of 2021, the lowest in 3 months, from USD 92.05 billion in the previous month, the advance estimate showed. Exports advanced 1.5 percent to a record USD 147.6 billion, the fifth consecutive month of gains as sales of automotive vehicles (5.1 percent), consumer goods (4.7 percent) and capital goods (2.2 percent) more than offset declines in foods, feeds, & beverages (-0.1 percent) and capital goods (-0.3 percent). Meantime, imports declined 1.4 percent from a record high, due to consumer goods (-4.3 percent), industrial supplies (-3.4 percent), capital goods (-0.3 percent) and foods, feeds, & beverages (-0.5 percent). Meanwhile, purchases of vehicles were up 3.9 percent. 2021-08-27T12:37:00
