Title: Bitcoin Gains 60% in 2021
Tag: Currency

Bitcoin Gains 60% in 2021United States CurrencyBitcoin ended 2021 below 50K but booked a more than 60% yearly gain, benefiting from a rise in risk appetite, higher demand from at-home traders, growing acceptance by major corporations and institutional investors and as El Salvador became the first country to adopt it as an official currency. On February 19th, bitcoin hit $1 trillion in market value for the first time but has since pulled back to close to $900 billion. Volatility is likely to continue in 2022 amid rising regulatory risks and as investors continue to wonder if it can be an inflation hedge. Meanwhile, ethereum, which is the second-most popular coin, jumped more than 400% to 3.7K in 2021.2021-12-30T22:58:00
