Title: US 10-Year Yield Falls Below 1.7%
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Yield Falls Below 1.7%United States Government Bond 10YThe benchmark 10-year Treasury yield fell to below 1.7% on  Monday amid a general flight to safety after fights in Ukraine intensified and President Putin said the war in Ukraine would continue. The move comes despite a better than expected US labor report which brought forward the expectations of more aggressive action from the Federal Reserve. The Fed, the world's most powerful rate-setter, is seen starting its policy tightening cycle in March with a 25 basis point rate hike.2022-03-07T01:10:00
