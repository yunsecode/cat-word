Title: US September Manufacturing PMI Revised Up to 53.1
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US September Manufacturing PMI Revised Up to 53.1United States Manufacturing PMIThe seasonally adjusted IHS Markit final US Manufacturing PMI came in at 53.1 in September 2017, up slightly on the flash reading of 53 and compared with 52.8 in August. Employment expanded at the fastest pace since December 2016 and production continued to rise while new order growth softened.2017-10-02T13:50:26.967
