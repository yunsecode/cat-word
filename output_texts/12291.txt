Title: US Current Account Gap Narrows To 1-1/2-Year Low
Tag: Current Account

US Current Account Gap Narrows To 1-1/2-Year LowUnited States Current AccountThe US current account deficit decreased to USD 112.4 billion in the last three months of 2016 from an upwardly revised USD 116 billion gap in the previous period and better than market expectations of a USD 128 billion shortfall. It is the smallest gap since the second quarter of 2015, as the surplus widened for the primary income (by USD 19.9 billion to USD 61.5 billion) and the services account (by USD 1.4 billion to USD 63.8 billion). In contrast, the goods deficit widened by USD 17.5 billion to USD 196 billion due to a drop in soybean exports. In addition, the secondary income gap increased slightly by USD 0.16 billion to USD 41.5 billion. Considering full 2016, the US current account deficit went up to USD 481.2 billion from a USD 463 billion gap in 2015. It represents 2.6 percent of current-dollar GDP, the same percentage as in 2015.2017-03-21T12:45:44
