Title: US Stocks Open in Record Territory
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open in Record TerritoryUnited States Stock MarketWall Street continued to break record highs on Friday despite the jobs report showed the US economy added less jobs than expected and the trade deficit reached the highest in nearly 8 years. The Dow Jones added 64 points or 0.3% to 25,139; the S&P 500 rose 9 points or 0.3% to 2,732 and the Nasdaq went up 23 points or 0.3% to 7,101 around 18 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-01-05T14:30:05
