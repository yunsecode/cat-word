Title: Wall Street Rally Runs Out of Steam
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Rally Runs Out of SteamUnited States Stock MarketWall Street's main indexes began a holiday-shortened week lower amid lingering worries over the implications of soaring inflation and tighter monetary policy on the growth momentum. Fed Governor Christopher Waller highlighted earlier this week that the US central bank should be prepared to hike interest rates by a half percentage point at every meeting until there is clear and convincing evidence that inflation is dropping. Adding to concerns about runaway price growth were soaring crude oil prices after EU leaders reached an agreement to ban 90% of Russian crude by the end of 2022. At the same time, record-high inflation in the eurozone sparked bets that the ECB would have to move even more aggressively. The Dow and the S&P 500 indexes are only marginally higher on the month, while the Nasdaq is down almost 2%. 2022-05-31T13:34:00
