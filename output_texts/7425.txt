Title: US Crude Stocks Rise More than Forecast
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Rise More than ForecastUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 4.32 million barrels in the week ended October 23rd, 2020, following a 1.001 million decrease in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 1.23 million increase, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Also, crude production rose by 1.2 million barrels per day, the largest weekly gain on record. Gasoline inventories were down by 0.892 million barrels, while markets had forecast a bigger decline of 0.961 million.2020-10-28T14:37:00
