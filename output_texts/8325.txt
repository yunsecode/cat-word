Title: US Consumer Credit Drops in March
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Drops in MarchUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States went down by USD 12.1 billion in March after climbing an upwardly revised USD 20 billion in February and missing market expectations of USD 15 billion rise. It is the first decrease since August 2011 as revolving credit including credit card borrowing decreased USD 28.2 billion after advancing a revised USD 3.3 billion in the previous month and non-revolving credit including loans for education and automobiles jumped USD 16.1 billion after a revised USD 16.6 billion gain in the prior month. Year-on-year, credit grew 3.4 percent after rising 5.7 percent in February.
