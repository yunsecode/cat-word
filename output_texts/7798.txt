Title: US Stocks Trade in the Green
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade in the GreenUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded slightly higher on Tuesday amid better-than-expected earnings from Home Depot, Walmart and Kohl’s. US home construction starts and building permits also beat market forecasts. Investors try to shrug off a stalemate in new US fiscal stimulus bill and mounting US-China tensions. The Trump administration announced on Monday it will further tighten restrictions on Huawei Technologies aiming to prevent the company's access to commercially available chips. Meanwhile, Senate Republicans are planning to propose a narrow coronavirus relief bill, including $300 in weekly federal unemployment benefits, an extension of the Paycheck Protection Program and $10 billion for the U.S. Postal Service. On the political front, the Democratic National Convention kicked-off and the Republican one will start next week.2020-08-18T13:31:00
