Dollar Continues to RiseUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index strengthened slightly for the second straight session to near 93.1 on Tuesday, the strongest on a closing basis in a week, as coronavirus infections continue to spike worldwide. The US, Russia and France set daily records for virus infections, and deaths in the US surpassed 225K.
