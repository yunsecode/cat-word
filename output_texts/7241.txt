Title: Wall Street Set to Start Week Higher
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Set to Start Week Higher United States Stock MarketUS stock index futures pointed to a higher open on Monday, as investors have been cheering promising developments toward a coronavirus vaccine, which is due to drive the broader economy recover and revive cyclical and other hard-hit sectors. On the bullish side, US lawmakers were seeking to revive long-stalled negotiations over additional coronavirus relief. But markets are becoming concerned about the economic damage caused by the surge within coronavirus cases, and restrictions put in place to stop it. Last week, the Dow retreated 0.7%, and the S&P 500 dropped 0.8%, whereas the Nasdaq rose 0.2%. 2020-11-23T02:34:00
