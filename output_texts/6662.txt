Title: US Import Prices Rise the Most Since 2012
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Rise the Most Since 2012 United States Import Prices MoMImport prices in the US jumped 1.4 percent month-over-month in January of 2021, following a revised 1.0 percent rise in December and above market forecasts of 1.0 percent. It was the biggest increase since March of 2012, mainly due to an increase in both fuel and nonfuel prices. Year-on-year, import prices advanced 0.9 percent, the first yearly gain since January of 2020 and the largest since October of 2017, following a 0.3 percent drop in the prior month. 2021-02-18T13:37:00
