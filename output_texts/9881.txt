Title: US Small Business Optimism Lowest in 7 Months
Tag: NFIB Business Optimism Index

US Small Business Optimism Lowest in 7 MonthsUnited States NFIB Business Optimism IndexThe NFIB's Small Business Optimism Index in the US fell to 104.8 in November of 2018 from 107.4 in October, below market expectations of 107.3. It is the lowest reading in seven months although it continued its exceptionally strong two-year trend. Slightly more than half of the decline was attributable to Expected Business Conditions and Expected Real Sales. Increases in compensation tied a near 30-year high as owners seek to attract more qualified candidates. An increasing percentage of owners reported capital outlays and higher sales.2018-12-11T11:11:49.48
