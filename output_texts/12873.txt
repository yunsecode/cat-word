Title: US Stocks Close Lower on Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Lower on EarningsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks dropped on Tuesday after bearish outlooks from both 3M and Whirlpool offset positive results from Merck & Co. and P&G while US consumer confidence fell to a three-month low in October. The Dow Jones Industrial Average retreated 53 points, or 0.3%, to 18,170; the S&P 500 declined 8 points, or 0.4%, to 2,143; and the Nasdaq Composite Index decreased 26 points, or 0.5%, to 5,283.2016-10-25T20:20:02.113
