Title: US Construction Spending Unchanged in July
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Unchanged in JulyUnited States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the United States showed no growth in July 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.9 percent growth in June and missing market expectations of a 0.5 percent gain. Public construction spending dropped sharply by 3.1 percent, dragged down by educational construction (-8.3 percent) while highway sector grew slightly (+0.3 percent). In contrast, private construction rose 1 percent, as spending increased for: nonresidential construction (+1.7 percent) and residential construction (+0.3 percent). During the first seven months of this year, construction spending amounted to $647.7 billion, 5.6 percent above the $613.1 billion for the same period in 2015.2016-09-01T15:09:01
