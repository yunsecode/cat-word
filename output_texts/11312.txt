Title: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index Falls from Record High
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index Falls from Record HighUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index The Manufacturing Activity Index in the US fifth district including the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia fell to 20 in December of 2017 from 30 in November which was the highest on record. Figures came below market expectations of 22 amid a slowdown in shipments (24 from 33); new orders (16 from 35); capacity utilization (16 from 19) and average workweek (8 from 17). On the other hand, the number of employees (20 from 18) and inventories of both finished goods (17 from 9) and raw materials (24 from 20) increased at a faster pace. Also, price pressures slowed for both prices paid (1.79 from 2.04) and received (1.27 from 1.63).2017-12-26T15:10:16.877
