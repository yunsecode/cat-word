Title: US Personal Spending Rises Less Than Expected
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Rises Less Than ExpectedUnited States Personal SpendingPersonal spending in the United States went up 0.2 percent in January of 2017 from December, following a 0.5 percent gain in the previous period. Figures came in below market expectations of 0.3 percent mainly due to a fall in purchases of durable goods, namely autos while spending on services was flat. 2017-03-01T13:31:12
