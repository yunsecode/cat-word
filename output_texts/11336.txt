Title: US Stocks Book Fresh Records on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Book Fresh Records on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately in the green on Monday 18 December 2017 on rising expectations that Congress will pass this week the tax bill expected to cut the corporate tax rate to 21 percent from 35 percent. All three indices hit new records. The Dow Jones gained 140 points or 0.6% to 24792. The S&P 500 advanced 14 points or 0.5% to 2690. The Nasdaq jumped 58 points or 0.8% to 6995. 2017-12-18T21:09:21
