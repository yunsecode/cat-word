Title: 10-Year Treasury Yield Falls
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

10-Year Treasury Yield FallsUnited States Government Bond 10YThe 10-year US Treasury note yield consolidated around the 2.80% level, easing from a multi-week peak of 2.91% as investors rushed to the safety of bonds due to lingering concerns over a global economic slowdown. A batch of economic releases from China, including disappointing industrial production and retail sales figures, raised further concerns about the health of the world's second-larger economy. On top of that, several Federal Reserve policymakers have pointed out that a dovish pivot is unlikely despite signs that inflation could be peaking, added to concerns about a Fed-induced recession. 2022-08-15T10:52:00
