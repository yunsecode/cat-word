Title: US Stocks Extend Gains on Late Session Comeback
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend Gains on Late Session ComebackUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Thursday 27 December 2018 after falling as much as 3 percent early in the session, as trade tensions between the US and China weighed on investors' mood after news that President Trump is considering banning US companies from using telecommunications equipment made by Huawei and ZTE; oil prices retreated, and the partial shutdown of the US government continued. The Dow Jones climbed 260 points or 1.1% to 23139. The S&P 500 gained 21 points or 0.9% to 2489. The Nasdaq added 25 points or 0.4% to 6580.  2018-12-27T21:09:00
