Title: US Stocks Decline on Sticky Trade Policy Uncertainty
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Decline on Sticky Trade Policy UncertaintyUnited States Stock MarketWal Street closed moderately in the red on Thursday 9 May 2019, as fears that a deal may not be reached before tariffs’ rise on Chinese imports on Friday hit sentiment after US President Donald Trump said China “broke the deal” on trade. The Dow Jones lost 139 points or 0.5%. The S&P 500 retreated 9 points or 0.3%. The Nasdaq declined 33 points or 0.4%.2019-05-09T20:11:00
