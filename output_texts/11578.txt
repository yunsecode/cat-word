Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Fall More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 2.747 million barrels in the week ended October 6th, 2017, following a 6.023 million decline in the previous period and compared with market expectations of a 1.991 million drop. Gasoline inventories went up unexpectedly by 2.490 million, the biggest gain since the first week of August.2017-10-12T15:02:57.933
