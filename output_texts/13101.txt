Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Went Up by 2.5M in Latest Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Went Up by 2.5M in Latest WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 2.5 million barrels in the week ended August 19th, 2016, following a 2.5 million decline in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 0.45 million fall. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks increased slightly by 36 thousand barrels, the first gain in four weeks, after declining by 2724 thousand in the previous period.2016-08-24T15:35:07
