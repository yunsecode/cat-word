Title: US Pending Home Sales Fall 2.3% YoY in January
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Fall 2.3% YoY in JanuaryUnited States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned US homes fell 2.3 percent from a year earlier in January 2019, following an upwardly 9.9 percent decline in the previous month. It marks the 13th straight month of decreases in pending home sales. The sales dropped the most in the West (-10.1 percent), followed by the South (-3.1 percent), and the Midwest (-0.3 percent) while in the Northeast purchased advanced 7.6 percent. On a month-over-month basis, sales went up 4.6 percent, after an upwardly revised 2.3 percent fall and above market consensus of a 0.5 percent gain. 2019-02-27T15:06:00
