Title: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index Beats Expectations
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index Beats ExpectationsUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index The Manufacturing Activity Index in the US fifth district including the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia increased to 10 in July of 2016 from a downwardly revised -10 in the previous month. Figures came much better than market expectations of -4, reaching the highest in three months. New orders (15 from -17 in June) and shipments (7 from -8) rebounded while backlogs flattened (1 from -12). Employment rose modestly (6 from 1) and while firms continue to report wage increases (14 from 15). 2016-07-26T16:06:33.803
