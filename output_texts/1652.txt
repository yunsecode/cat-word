Title: DXY Jumps after Jobs Report
Tag: Currency

DXY Jumps after Jobs ReportUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index jumped to 105.5 on Friday, after a strong jobs report renewed expectations for higher interest rates. The US economy unexpectedly added 263K jobs in November of 2022, beating market forecasts of 200K, and following an upwardly revised 284K in October. For the week however, the greenback is still down more than 0.5%, as data released on Thursday showed US PCE inflation slowed to a ten-month low in October and the Fed Chair Jerome Powell said on Wednesday that the Fed could moderate the size of rate hikes as soon as December.2022-12-02T13:35:00
