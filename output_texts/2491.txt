Title: DXY Strengthens after Strong Payroll Number
Tag: Currency

DXY Strengthens after Strong Payroll NumberUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index jumped 0.8% to above 96.5 on Friday after a stronger-than-expected jobs report strengthened expectations the Federal Reserve will remain aggressively in the coming months to tame inflation. The US economy added 528K jobs in July, much better than market forecasts of 250K and above an upwardly revised 398K in June. For the week, the dollar is up 0.7%. 2022-08-05T12:40:00
