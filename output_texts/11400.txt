Title: US Stocks Surge on Tax Bill Prospects
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge on Tax Bill ProspectsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deep into the green on Thursday November 30th of 2017, as investors await a Senate vote on tax reform late in the day, with key Republican John McCain endorsing the Senate tax bill in a statement hours ahead of the vote. Excluding the Nasdaq, major indices jumped to new highs. The Dow Jones soared 332 points or 1.4% to 24272. The S&P 500 gained 22 points or 0.8% to 2648. The Nasdaq advanced 50 points or 0.7% to 6874.2017-11-30T21:06:15
