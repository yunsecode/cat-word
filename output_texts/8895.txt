Title: US Wages Growth Below Forecasts in December
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Growth Below Forecasts in DecemberUnited States Average Hourly EarningsUS average hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls rose by 3 cents, or 0.1 percent, to USD 28.32, after an upwardly revised 0.3 percent gain in November and missing market estimates of a 0.3 percent gain. Year-on-year, average hourly earnings have increased by 2.9 percent, the first below 3 percent reading since July 2018 and below market expectations of a 3.1 percent gain. In December, average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees were little changed at USD 23.79.
