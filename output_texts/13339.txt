Title: US Inflation Rate Slows to 1% in May
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Slows to 1% in MayUnited States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States went up 1 percent year-on-year in May of 2016, slowing slightly from a 1.1 percent rise in April. Figures came below market expectations of 1.1 percent as inflation for food and transportation services slowed while energy cost fell at a faster pace. Yet, core inflation accelerated slightly to 2.2 percent. 2016-06-16T13:36:07
