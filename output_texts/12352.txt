Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Rise Less Than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Rise Less Than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States went up by 1.501 million barrels in the week ended February 24th, 2017, following a 0.564 million increase in the previous week and below market expectations of a 3.079 million rise. Gasoline stocks fell by 0.546 million, compared to expectations of a 1.789 million drop.2017-03-01T15:34:21.433
