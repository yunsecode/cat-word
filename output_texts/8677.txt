Title: US Stocks Hold Gains after Trump Declares National Emergency
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Hold Gains after Trump Declares National EmergencyUnited States Stock MarketWall Street was keeping the gains on Friday after rising as much as 6% on the open, as investors monitor the rapid spread of the coronavirus worldwide and as President Trump declared a national emergency over the outbreak to open access to a $50 billion fund to assist Americans affected by the virus. Earlier, markets welcomed Nancy Pelosi announcement that US lawmakers and the administration were close to making a deal on economic stimulus although no more details have been provided yet.2020-03-13T19:37:00
