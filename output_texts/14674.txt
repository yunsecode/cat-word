Title: US Unemployment Rate at 7.6% in June
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Unemployment Rate at 7.6% in JuneUnited States Unemployment RateU.S. total nonfarm payroll employment increased by 195,000 in June, and the unemployment rate was unchanged at 7.6 percent, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on July 5th. Employment rose in  leisure and hospitality, professional and business services, retail trade, health care, and financial activities. 2013-07-05T13:38:37.52
