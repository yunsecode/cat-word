Title: US ISM NY Index Slows in December
Tag: Ism New York Index

US ISM NY Index Slows in DecemberUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions in the United States fell to 56.3 in December of 2017 from 58.1 in November. Still, it remained above the 12-month average of 54.9. Employment contracted (42.9 from 64.6), purchases slowed (55 from 56.3) and prices paid increased (66.7 from 62.5). Also, both current (55 from 64.3) and expected (63.6 from 71.4) revenues eased. On the other hand, the six-month outlook jumped to 85.7 from 69.7, reaching the highest since July of 2006.2018-01-03T14:59:26.65
