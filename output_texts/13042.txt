Title: US Consumer Credit Rises More Than Expected in July
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Rises More Than Expected in JulyUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States increased by $17.71 billion in July 2016 following an upwardly revised $14.53 billion rise in June and above market expectations of $16 billion. Non-revolving credit rose by $14.94 billion, after increasing by $5.36 billion in the previous month and revolving credit went up by $2.78 billion, compared with a $9.17 billion rise in June.2016-09-08T20:04:26.437
