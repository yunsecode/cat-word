Title: US Stocks Sink at the Open
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Sink at the OpenUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened sharply in the red on Tuesday after quarterly results from Caterpillar and 3M disappointed. Caterpillar costs increased due to higher steel prices and tariffs; 3M earnings missed forecasts and the company also lowered its outlook for 2018. The Dow Jones was down 1.2%, the S&P 500 fell 1.3% and the Nasdaq traded 1.6% lower around 8 minutes  after the opening bell.2018-10-23T13:45:51.817
