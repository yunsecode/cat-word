Title: US Service Sector Growth Slows to 15-Month Low
Tag: Services PMI

US Service Sector Growth Slows to 15-Month LowUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services PMI Business Activity Index fell to 52.4 in December 2017 from November's final reading of 54.5 and way below market expectations of 55, the flash estimate showed. The reading pointed to the weakest pace of expansion in the service sector since September last year, as incoming new business rose the least since April and the rate of job creation slowed to a seven-month low. Also, business optimism eased for the second month running to its weakest since June 2016.2017-12-14T14:49:53
