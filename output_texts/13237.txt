Title: US Housing Starts at 4-Month High of 1189K
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts at 4-Month High of 1189KUnited States Housing StartsUS housing starts jumped 4.8 percent to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1189 thousand in June from May of 2016, after falling an upwardly revised 1.7 percent in the previous period. Figures came much better than market expectations of a 0.5 percent gain, reaching the highest in four months, as groundbreaking on single-family homes rebounded.2016-07-19T13:39:53
