Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise for 1st Time in 4 Weeks
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise for 1st Time in 4 WeeksUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 7.1 percent in the week ended August 5th 2016 from the prior period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It was the first increase in four months as refinance applications went up 9.6 percent and applications to purchase a home grew 2.6 percent. Fixed 30-year mortgage rates fell 2bps to 3.65 percent.2016-08-10T12:03:30
