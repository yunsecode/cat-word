Title: US Futures Lack Direction as Protests Continue
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Lack Direction as Protests ContinueUnited States Stock MarketStock futures in the US traded mixed on Tuesday as investors assess how current protests will impact the economy as states try to reopen while making it more difficult to control the spread of the coronavirus. President Trump threatened to use military force if states cannot control the situation. Protests across several US cities over police brutality and the death of George Floyd entered the 7th day on Monday and many cities including NY, Atlanta, Seattle, Los Angeles, Chicago, Philadelphia and Minneapolis have imposed curfews. On Monday, the Dow Jones gained 92 points or 0.4% to 25,475. The S&P 500 added 11 points or 0.4% to 3056. The Nasdaq climbed 62 points or 0.7% to 9552.2020-06-02T06:48:00
