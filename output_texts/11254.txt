Title: US Government Budget Gap Narrows in December
Tag: Government Budget Value

US Government Budget Gap Narrows in DecemberUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US budget deficit narrowed to USD 23.2 billion in December 2017 from USD 27.3 billion in the same month of the previous year, and compared to market expectations of USD 40.0 billion. Outlays climbed 0.7 percent year-on-year (vs +3.1 percent in November) to USD 349.0 billion and receipts increased 2.1 percent (vs +4.3 percent) to USD 325.8 billion.2018-01-11T19:20:10
