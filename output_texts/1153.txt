Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Beat Estimates: API
Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Beat Estimates: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States jumped by 10.507 million barrels in the week ended February 10th, 2023, following a 2.184 million barrels drop in the previous week, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed. It was the highest weekly gain since the first week of January 2023, compared with market expectations of a 0.321 million barrels increase.
