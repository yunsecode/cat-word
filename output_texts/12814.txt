Title: US Stocks Rise Sharply After FBI Clears Clinton
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise Sharply After FBI Clears Clinton United States Stock MarketWall Street closed sharply higher on Monday after the FBI said would take no action against Hillary Clinton, raising expectations she will become the next US President. The Dow Jones added 371 points or 2.1%, the S&P 500 gained 46 points or 2.2%, ending a 9-day losing streak and the Nasdaq closed 120 points or 2.4% higher.2016-11-07T21:16:26
