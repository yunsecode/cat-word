Title: U.S. Nonfarm Payrolls up 80K in October, Unemployment Rate at 9%
Tag: Unemployment Rate

U.S. Nonfarm Payrolls up 80K in October, Unemployment Rate at 9%United States Unemployment RateU.S. nonfarm payroll employment continued to trend up in October (+80,000), and the unemployment rate was little changed at 9.0 percent, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on November 4. Employment in the private sector rose, with modest job growth continuing in professional and businesses services, leisure and hospitality, health care, and mining. Government employment continued to trend down.
