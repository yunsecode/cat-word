Title: US Manufacturing PMI Revised Slightly Lower: S&P Global
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing PMI Revised Slightly Lower: S&P GlobalUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe S&P Global US Manufacturing PMI was revised slightly lower to 48.4 in May of 2023 from a preliminary of 48.5 driven by a solid contraction in new orders amid muted demand conditions. Efforts to run down stocks were met by a steeper contraction in purchasing activity, hinting at lower production growth in the coming months. Sharply falling demand for inputs contributed to an unprecedented improvement in vendor performance. On the other hand, output and employment continued to increase, as firms expanded their capacity to fulfill existing backlogs of work amid improved supply conditions. On the price front, input costs fell for the first time since May 2020. The decrease in costs contributed to the slowest rise in selling prices for almost three years as firms also sought to become more competitive and boost new sales. Also, the degree of confidence was solid overall. 2023-06-01T13:50:00
