Title: US Futures Lower as Evergrande Concerns Mount
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Lower as Evergrande Concerns MountUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were lower on Friday amid mounting concerns over a potential default from Evergrande after the property group failed a payment to its foreign bondholders, leaving investors without any explanation whether the interest payment will be made. At the same time, traders continue to digest prospects the Fed will soon start cutting stimulus despite signs of a slowing recovery. So far on the week, the Dow Jones is up 0.5% and is on track to end a three-week losing streak. The S&P 500 gained 0.4% and the Nasdaq 0.1%.2021-09-24T08:16:00
