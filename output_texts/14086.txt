Dollar Falls, Stocks Largely Unchanged on FedUnited States Stock MarketAfter the Federal Reserve decision on interest rates, benchmark stock markets remained largely unchanged but the EURUSD rallied strongly to 1.14 as forex traders took the FOMC statement as more dovish than expected.
