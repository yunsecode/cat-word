Title: Dollar Strengthens as Optimism over a Fast Recovery Fades
Tag: Currency

Dollar Strengthens as Optimism over a Fast Recovery FadesUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index gained nearly 0.4% to trade above 96.3 on Thursday, recovering slightly from a 3-month low in the previous session as interest in safe haven assets increased after the Federal Reserve painted a gloomy and challenging economic outlook for the next few years. Moreover, the number of new coronavirus cases is rising in Texas, Florida and California, raising fears of a second wave of infections in the US. 2020-06-11T07:21:00
