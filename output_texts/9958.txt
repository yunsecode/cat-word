Title: US Stocks Fall

US Stocks Fall United States Stock MarketWall Street opened sharply in the red on Tuesday, with all 3 major stock indexes erasing 2018 gains. Apple stocks continued to decline and Target shares also fell after earnings came worse than expected. The Dow Jones declined 1.5%, the S&P 500 fell 1.2% and the Nasdaq traded 1.5% lower around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2018-11-20T14:46:00
