Title: US Crude Stocks Rise More than Expected: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Rise More than Expected: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 2.558 million barrels in the week ended October 21st, compared with market expectations of a 1.029-million-barrel increase, the latest US Energy Information Administration report showed. Also, crude stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, went up by 0.667 million barrels, following a 0.583 million build; and distillate stockpiles, which include diesel and heating oil, increased by 0.17 million, versus forecasts for a 1.138 million drop. On the other hand, gasoline stocks fell by 1.478 million, more than market expectations of a 0.805 million draw. 2022-10-26T14:38:34.74
