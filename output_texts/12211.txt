Title: US Initial Jobless Claims Edge Down To 234K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Initial Jobless Claims Edge Down To 234KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 1 thousand to 234 thousand in the week ended April 8th 2017 from the previous period, below expectations of 245 thousand. It is the lowest figure in six weeks. The 4-week moving average that removes week-to-week volatility fell by 3 thousand to 247.25 thousand.2017-04-13T12:29:15
