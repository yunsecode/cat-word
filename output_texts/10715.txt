Title: US Business Inventories Unchanged in March
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Unchanged in MarchUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the United States were unchanged at USD 1,929.6 billion in March 2018, following a 0.6 percent advance in February and missing market expectations of a 0.1 percent gain. A decline in retailers' stocks (-0.5 percent vs 0.4 percent in February) was offset by increases in inventories at both manufacturers (0.3 percent vs 0.4 percent) and wholesalers (0.3 percent vs 0.9 percent).2018-05-15T14:04:52
