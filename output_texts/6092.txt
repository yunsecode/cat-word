Title: US Jobless Rate Seen Falling to 5.8% in April
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Seen Falling to 5.8% in AprilUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate is expected to fall to 5.8 percent in April 2021, the lowest level since the COVID-19 pandemic first hit the US labor market in March 2020. The pace of job creation is seen gathering steam in the spring and through summer, as the country re-opens due to the acceleration in the pace of COVID-19 vaccinations and as the government's $1.9 trillion relief package boosts domestic demand.2021-05-07T09:42:23.607
