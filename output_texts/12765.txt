Title: US Housing Starts Rise to 9-Year High in October
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Rise to 9-Year High in OctoberUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States climbed 25.5 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1323 thousand in October 2016, way above market expectations of 1156 thousand. It was the highest reading since August 2007 and the biggest increase since July 1982, as single-family housing starts advanced 10.7 percent and those for the volatile multi-family segment soared 68.8 percent. Starts increased in all four regions. Meanwhile, building permits rose 0.3 percent to 1229 thousand while markets were expecting a 2.9 percent drop.2016-11-17T13:36:21
