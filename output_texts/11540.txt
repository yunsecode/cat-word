Title: Dow Jones Books New High After Strong Earnings

Dow Jones Books New High After Strong EarningsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed modestly in the green on Tuesday October 24th of 2017 propelled by strong earnings from manufacturers, as Caterpillar, 3M, General Motors and Fiat Chrysler delivered better-than-expected quarterly results. The Dow Jones gained 168 points or 0.7% to a new record of 23442. The S&P 500 edged up 4 points or 0.2% to 2569. The Nasdaq advanced 12 points or 0.2% to 6598. 2017-10-24T20:12:11
