Title: US GDP Growth Revised Up to 1%
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Revised Up to 1%United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy expanded an annualized 1 percent on quarter in the last three months of 2015, higher than a preliminary figure of 0.7 percent, according to the second estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. Business inventories were revised higher and the negative impact from trade came lower than expected while consumer spending was revised down.2016-02-26T13:34:40
