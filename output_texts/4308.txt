Title: US Trade Gap Hits Record in 2021
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Gap Hits Record in 2021United States Balance of TradeThe US trade gap in both goods and services rose 27% to hit $859 billion in 2021, an annual record as imports grew faster than exports. The imports surged 20.5% or $576.5 billion last year, as Americans purchased more foreign products and strong demand pushed up the prices. Meanwhile, exports were up 18.5 percent or $394.1 billion. In December alone, the deficit increased to $80.7 billion from a downwardly revised $79.3 billion gap in November and close to a record high of $80.8 billion in September, as imports surged to an all-time high as businesses were restocking the shelves. Also, exports hit the highest on record driven by shipments of consumer goods, such as pharmaceutical preparations, civilian aircraft engines, passenger cars and nonmonetary gold.
