Title: US Factory Orders Rise More than Expected in August
Tag: Factory Orders

US Factory Orders Rise More than Expected in AugustUnited States Factory OrdersNew orders for US manufactured goods rose 1.2 percent month-over-month in August 2017, following a 3.3 percent drop in July and beating market expectations of a 1 percent gain. Orders for transportation equipment advanced 5.1 percent, boosted by a 44.8 percent surge in civilian aircraft orders, and those for motor vehicles rose 0.7 percent. Also, orders for machinery gained 0.3 percent. Orders for non-defense capital goods excluding aircraft, seen as a measure of business spending plans, jumped 1.1 percent in August after a 0.9 percent increase last month.2017-10-05T14:06:29.577
