Title: Dollar Rebounds From 3-month Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rebounds From 3-month LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index remained steady at 92.50 on Tuesday, making a dramatic reversal after testing the main support level at 92.00 in the prior session. The rebound from an almost three-month low was backed by upbeat PMI data, which for now dampened the need for more stimulus to support the near-term economic recovery. Still, news that the Trump administration is making federal resources available for Joe Biden’s transition team and further progress towards an effective coronavirus boosted risk-appetite and consequently weighed on the safe-haven dollar. 2020-11-23T18:27:00
