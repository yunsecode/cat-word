Title: US Stocks Rebound As Fed Meeting Begins
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound As Fed Meeting BeginsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Tuesday, as technology stocks recovered moderately after two straights sessions in decline to book the worst drop in the year. A key FOMC kicked off on Tuesday, where policymakers are expected to lift interest rates by 25 bps. The Dow Jones gained 93 points or 0.4 percent to 21328, while the S&P 500 advanced 11 points or 0.5 percent to 2440. Both indices clinched fresh records. Meanwhile, the Nasdaq climbed 45 points or 0.7 percent to 6220. 2017-06-13T20:07:36
