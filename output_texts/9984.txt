Title: US Inflation Rate Rises to 2.5%, Matches Forecasts
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Rises to 2.5%, Matches ForecastsUnited States Inflation RateAnnual inflation rate in the US increased to 2.5 percent in October of 2018 from 2.3 percent in September. Figures match market expectations, mainly due to prices of fuel oil and gasoline. On a monthly basis, consumer prices increased 0.3 percent, higher than 0.1 percent in September and matching forecasts. It is the highest monthly gain in nine months, mainly due to gasoline.2018-11-14T13:32:00
