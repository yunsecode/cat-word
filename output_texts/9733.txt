Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States dropped 2.7 percent in the week ended January 18th 2019, following a 13.5 percent gain in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications declined 5.3 percent and applications to purchase a home went down 2.2 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged up by 1bps to 4.75 percent.2019-01-23T12:03:05.27
