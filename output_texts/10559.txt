Title: US Mortgage Applications Retreat: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Retreat: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 4.9 percent in the week ended June 22nd 2018, after a 5.1 percent jump in the previous period, which was the biggest increase since the first week of January, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications declined 3.5 percent and applications to purchase a home went down 5.9 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged up by 1bps to 4.84 percent.2018-06-27T11:06:25.267
