Title: US Manufacturing Growth at 3-1/2-Year High: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing Growth at 3-1/2-Year High: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI stood at 56.5 in April 2018, unchanged from the preliminary estimate and above March's final 55.6. The reading pointed to the strongest pace of expansion in the manufacturing sector since September 2014, supported by quicker rates of output and new order growth and a greater deterioration in vendor performance.2018-05-01T13:48:32
