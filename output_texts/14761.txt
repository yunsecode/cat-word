Title: U.S. GDP Up 2.2% in Q1
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

U.S. GDP Up 2.2% in Q1United States GDP Growth RateU.S. real gross domestic product increased at an annual rate of 2.2 percent in the first quarter of 2012 (that is, from the fourth quarter to the first quarter), according to the "advance" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.  In the fourth quarter of 2011, real GDP increased 3.0 percent.
