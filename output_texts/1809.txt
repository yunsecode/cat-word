Title: US Monthly Inflation Rate Steady at 0.4%
Tag: Inflation Rate MoM

US Monthly Inflation Rate Steady at 0.4%United States Inflation Rate MoMThe Consumer Price Index in the United States increased 0.4% month-over-month in October of 2022, the same as in September and below forecasts of 0.6%. The index for shelter contributed over half of the monthly all items increase (up by 0.8%). The energy index increased 1.8%, as gasoline rose 4%, following three consecutive declines and electricity (0.1%), while natural gas cost decreased 4.6%. Meanwhile, food prices rose 0.6%, below 0.8% in September, with cost of food at home rising 0.4%, the least since December. Other price increases were also seen for motor vehicle insurance (1.7%); recreation (0.7%); new vehicles (0.4%); personal care (0.5%). In contrast, medical care services (-0.6%) and commodities (0%) pushed the CPI down due to a fall in retained earnings by insurance companies this year due to high rates of health care utilization in 2021 and robust profit margins from 2020.2022-11-10T13:47:00
