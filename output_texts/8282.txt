Title: Dollar Ends Week Higher
Tag: Currency

Dollar Ends Week HigherUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index fell for the first time in 3 sessions to 100.37 on Friday after investors turned to riskier assets on encouraging signs of recovery from China. Official data showed industrial production increased for the first time this year and oil demand picked up. For the week, the dollar gained 0.6% amid concerns about the second wave of coronavirus infections and mounting tensions between the US and China.2020-05-15T21:20:00
