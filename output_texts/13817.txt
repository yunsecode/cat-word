Title: US Job Openings Match Expectations
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Match ExpectationsUnited States Job OffersJob openings in the United States increased to 5431 thousand in November from a downwardly revised 5349 thousand in October of 2015, in line with market expectations. The number of job openings was little changed for total private and government, increased in health care and social assistance (+57,000) and decreased in retail trade (-64,000). Among regions, job openings rose in the South and fell in the Midwest.2016-01-12T15:12:44.06
