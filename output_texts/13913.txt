Title: US Markit Manufacturing PMI At 25-Month Low
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Markit Manufacturing PMI At 25-Month LowUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe final Markit US Manufacturing PMI came in at 52.8 in November of 2015, slightly higher than 52.6 in the preliminary estimate, but below 54.1 reported in October. It is the lowest figure in 25 months as output, new orders and employment all expanded at slower rates while average cost burdens decreased for the third month.2015-12-01T14:49:13
