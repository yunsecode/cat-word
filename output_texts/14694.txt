Title: United States Economy Expands 2.5% in Q1
Tag: Gdp Growth Rate

United States Economy Expands 2.5% in Q1United States Gdp Growth RateReal gross domestic product increased at an annual rate of 2.5 percent in the first quarter of 2013 (that is, from the fourth quarter to the first quarter), according to the 'advance' estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.  In the fourth quarter, real GDP increased 0.4 percent.2013-05-21T19:36:03.957
