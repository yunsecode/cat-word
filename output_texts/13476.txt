Title: Wall Street Falls on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Falls on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketStocks in US traded lower on Tuesday as global growth fears increased after Manufacturing PMI data for China disappointed and the Dollar fell further against the Euro and the Yen. In contrast, better than expected earnings reports from Pfizer and CVS limited losses. The Dow Jones lost 0.94% or 168 points, the S&P 500 slid 1% or 21 points and the Nasdaq fell 0.1.1% or 53 points around 60 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-05-03T15:34:06
