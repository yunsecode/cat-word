Title: Wall Street Opens Higher on Oil, Jobs
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Higher on Oil, JobsUnited States Stock MarketMarkets in the United States opened higher on Wednesday as oil prices rebounded after Russia said it would be open to discussions with OPEC over production cuts and the ADP employment report showed the US private sector added more jobs than expected in January. The Dow Jones was up 81 points or 0.51%, the S&P 500 added 0.39% and the Nasdaq gained 0.29% around 9 minutes after the open. 2016-02-03T14:48:19.377
