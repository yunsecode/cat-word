Title: US Stocks Rise On Inauguration Day
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise On Inauguration DayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Friday ahead of Trump inauguration speech later in the morning. The Dow Jones went up 88 points or 0.4%, the S&P 500 rose 12 points or 0.5% and the Nasdaq gained 34 points or 0.6% around 8 minutes after the opening bell.
