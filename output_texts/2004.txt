Title: US Goods Gap Rises to 3-Month High
Tag: Goods Trade Balance

US Goods Gap Rises to 3-Month HighUnited States Goods Trade BalanceThe trade gap on goods in the US widened to a three-month high of $92.2 billion in September of 2022, compared to a $87.3 billion deficit in August. Exports went down 1.5% to $177.6 billion, with sales of food, feeds and beverages (-14%) and industrial supplies (-3.1%) recording the biggest decrease. Meanwhile, imports were up 0.8% to $269.85 billion, led by purchases of capital goods (4.4%), consumer goods (1.4%) and autos (1.9%). 2022-10-26T12:35:00
