Title: Bitcoin Extends Gains to Over 5-Month High
Tag: Currency

Bitcoin Extends Gains to Over 5-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe crypto bull run gained traction, with Bitcoin trading above $54,000, the highest since May 12th and not far from a record high of near $65,000. The virtual currency is up more than 30% in the past seven days, amidst stagflation fears and slowing economic recovery, after Securities and Exchange Commission Chair Gary Gensler said the US would not follow China’s lead in banning digital tokens.
