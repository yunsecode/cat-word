Title: US New Home Sales Up to 6-Year High
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Up to 6-Year HighUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses were at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 504,000 in May, 18.6 percent above the revised April rate of 425,000.
