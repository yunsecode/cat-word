Title: US Inflation Rate Lowest in 7 Months
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Lowest in 7 MonthsUnited States Inflation RateAnnual inflation in the US fell to 2.3 percent in September of 2018 from 2.7 percent in August and below market expectations of 2.4 percent. It is the lowest inflation rate since February, mainly due to a sharp slowdown in gas prices and smaller increases in fuel and shelter costs.2018-10-11T12:33:00
