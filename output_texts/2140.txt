Title: US Dollar Extends Rise
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Extends Rise United States CurrencyThe dollar index rose above the 113 mark on Wednesday, edging higher for the sixth session and approaching the 20-year high of 114.1 hit on September 26 amid expectations that the Federal Reserve will continue to aggressively raise interest rates. Ahead of FOMC minutes this afternoon and the CPI print tomorrow, fresh data showed that producer price growth rose more than expectations with a 0.4% monthly gain in September, rebounding from the decline in August and adding to the urgency for the Federal Reserve to tame inflation. Moreover, the dollar continued to benefit from strong demand of haven currencies due to a highly uncertain outlook amid geopolitical tension, with the IMF slashing its 2023 growth forecast for the global economy. The dollar extended gains against the euro, sterling, and surged to fresh 24-year highs against the yen amid the increasing discrepancy of monetary policy between the Fed and BoJ.2022-10-12T12:54:00
