Title: US Trade Deficit Higher Than Expected In April
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit Higher Than Expected In AprilUnited States Balance of TradeThe goods and services deficit in the United States widened to USD 47.6 billion in April of 2017 from an upwardly revised USD 45.6 billion gap a month earlier and higher than market expectations of a USD 46.1 billion shortfall. Exports fell 0.3 percent to USD 190.98 billion, driven by lower sales of consumer goods and vehicles. Imports rose 0.8 percent to USD 238.6 billion, boosted by cellphones and capital goods. 2017-06-02T12:35:55.56
