Title: DXY Resumes Upward Momentum
Tag: Currency

DXY Resumes Upward MomentumUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rose more than 0.5 percent on Tuesday recovering further from an over 2-1/12-year low of 90 reached in the previous week as investors again turned their attention to safe-haven assets because of the concerns about the new COVID-19 strain. Still, the greenback is expected to depreciate in the next few months as the approval of the new coronavirus relief bill will 
translate into higher debt and the monetary policy in the US will remain accommodative for a long time. 2020-12-22T15:55:00
