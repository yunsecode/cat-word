Title: Dollar Hovers at 4-Month High
Tag: currency

Dollar Hovers at 4-Month HighUnited States currencyThe US dollar traded near a 4-month high on Thursday as investors turned to safe haven currencies after a rise in the number of new coronavirus cases prompted fresh concerns about the impact of the flu-like virus in the global economy. Investors also await the release of CPI figures later in the day, and expect the inflation to pick up to 2.4% from 2.3% in December. The DXY traded nearly flat at 99.051 around 7:00 AM NY time.2020-02-13T11:58:00
