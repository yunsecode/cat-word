Title: US Corporate Profits Fall in Q4
Tag: Corporate Profits

US Corporate Profits Fall in Q4United States Corporate ProfitsCorporate profits in the United States decreased 3.3 percent to USD 1.95 trillion in the fourth quarter of 2020, down from a 27 percent jump in the previous period. Undistributed profits fell 14.5 percent to USD 0.58 trillion and net cash flow with inventory valuation adjustment, the internal funds available to corporations for investment, went down 2.9 percent to USD 2.54 trillion. Meanwhile, net dividends rose 2.3 percent to USD 1.37 trillion.2021-03-25T12:36:00
