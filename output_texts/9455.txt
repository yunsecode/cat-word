Title: US Construction Spending Climbs to 9-Month High
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Climbs to 9-Month HighUnited States Construction SpendingUS construction spending rose 1 percent from a month earlier to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of USD 1.32 billion in February 2019, the highest level in nine months, following an upwardly revised 2.5 percent surge in January and easily beating market expectations of a 0.2 percent drop. Investment in public construction grew 3.6 percent (vs 5.7 percent in January), led by federal government construction projects and state and local government construction outlays. In addition, spending on private construction rose 0.2 percent (vs -1.5 percent in January) on the back of private residential projects.2019-04-01T14:07:52.873
