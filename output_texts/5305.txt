Treasury Yields Retreat United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note remained below 1.3% on Wednesday, moving further away from a two-month peak of 1.38% hit last week, as inflation rate slowed but remained elevated, raising doubts whether inflationary pressure is transitory as supply chain issues and raw material shortages persist.
