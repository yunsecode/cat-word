U.S. Annual Inflation Up to 2.2 Percent in OctoberUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) increased 0.1 percent in October on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on November 15. Over the last 12 months, the all items index increased 2.2 percent before seasonal adjustment.2012-11-15T13:33:45
