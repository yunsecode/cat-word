Title: US Personal Spending Rebounds Less than Expected
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Rebounds Less than ExpectedUnited States Personal SpendingPersonal spending in the United States edged up 0.1 percent from a month earlier in January, after falling 0.6 percent in December, the most in a decade. The latest reading came in below market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain, as an increase in consumption of nondurables and services offset a sharp decline in durables spending. 2019-03-29T12:32:00
