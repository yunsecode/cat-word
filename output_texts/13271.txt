Title: US Economy Adds 287K Jobs in June
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds 287K Jobs in JuneUnited States Non Farm PayrollsTotal nonfarm payroll employment increased by 287,000 in June, and the unemployment 
rate rose to 4.9 percent, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported today. Job 
growth occurred in leisure and hospitality, health care and social assistance, and 
financial activities. Employment also increased in information, mostly reflecting 
the return of workers from a strike.2016-07-08T13:33:08.277
