Title: US Trade Deficit Shrinks In May
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit Shrinks In MayUnited States Balance of TradeThe goods and services deficit in the United States narrowed to USD 46.5 billion in May of 2017 from a USD 47.6 billion gap a month earlier and compared to market expectations of a USD 46.2 billion shortfall. Exports rose 0.4 percent to the highest value in nearly two years mainly boosted by sales of consumer goods and cell phones.2017-07-06T12:32:47
