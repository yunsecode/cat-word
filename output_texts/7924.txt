Title: U.S Futures Cautious Ahead of Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

U.S Futures Cautious Ahead of EarningsUnited States Stock MarketU.S. stock futures slipped in early Asian trade as investors braced for a big week of corporate earnings, while U.S lawmakers continued coronavirus stimulus negotiations. McDonald’s, Pfizer, Alphabet, Apple and AMD are among the companies due to release their latest quarterly figures this week. Meantime, White House economic advisor Larry Kudlow confirmed that the next round of coronavirus relief will include US$1,200 stimulus payments to Americans, while noting that the Trump administration will lengthen the federal eviction moratorium. The Dow Jones Industrial Average futures traded 60 points lower, or 0.2%, while S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 futures both fell 0.2%.2020-07-26T23:03:00
