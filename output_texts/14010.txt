Title: US Building Permits Drop 5% in September
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Drop 5% in SeptemberUnited States Building PermitsBuilding permits for privately-owned housing units were at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,103,000 in September of 2015, down 5 percent from the August rate of 1,161,000 but 4.7 percent above the September 2014 estimate of 1,053,000. Authorizations of units in buildings with five units or more (-14.6 percent) and single-family authorizations (-0.3 percent) were the main contributors to the monthly decline.2015-10-20T13:41:09
