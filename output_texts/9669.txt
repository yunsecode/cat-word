Title: US Stocks Open High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open HighUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Tuesday as investors await President Trump's State of the Union speech later in the day. Gains come despite Alphabet reporting pressure on advertising prices and decreasing margins although earnings beat forecasts. The Dow Jones went up 0.5%, the S&P 500 added 0.3% and the Nasdaq traded 0.6% higher around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2019-02-05T14:30:00
