Title: Fed Leaves Rates on Hold
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Leaves Rates on Hold United States Interest RateThe Federal Reserve left the target range for its federal funds rate unchanged at 0.25 percent to 0.5 percent for the fifth time during its July 2016 meeting. Policymakers said that the labor market strengthened and near-term risks to growth decreased, suggesting a rate hike is still possible this year although dependent on incoming economic data. 2016-07-27T19:00:49
