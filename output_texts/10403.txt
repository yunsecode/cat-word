Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Wednesday 1 August 2018, as trade fears persisted; the Fed left rates unchanged and upgraded its view on the economy; and Apple’s new high following solid earnings nudged tech stocks. The Dow Jones retreated 81 points or 0.3% to 25334. The S&P 500 lost 3 points or 0.1% to 2813. In contrast, the Nasdaq gained 36 points or 0.5% to 7707. 2018-08-01T20:08:47.277
