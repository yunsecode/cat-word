Title: US Jobless Claims Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits decreased by 1 thousand to 211 thousand in the week ended May 18th 2019 from the previous week’s unrevised level of 212 thousand and compared with market expectations of 215 thousand. According to unadjusted data, the largest declines were seen California (-1,662); Illinois (-1,351) and Georgia (-1,014) while the biggest rises were registered in Ohio (+4,228); Pennsylvania (+1,012) and Texas (+855).2019-05-23T12:32:00
