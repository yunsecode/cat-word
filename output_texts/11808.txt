Title: US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI at Near 1-Year Low
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US ISM Non Manufacturing PMI at Near 1-Year LowUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States fell to 53.9 in July of 2017, well below 57.4 in June and market expectations of 57. It is the lowest reading since August of 2016 as production, new orders and employment expansion eased and price pressures increased. However, the majority of respondents’ comments were mostly positive about business conditions and the state of the economy. 2017-08-03T14:06:14
