Title: US Existing Home Sales Fall 2.8% In December
Tag: Existing Home Sales

US Existing Home Sales Fall 2.8% In DecemberUnited States Existing Home SalesSales of previously owned houses in the United States slumped 2.8 percent month-over-month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 5490 thousand in December of 2016, below market expectations of 5520 thousand. Sales of condos shrank 10.3 percent to 610 thousand and those of single family homes fell 1.8 percent to 4880 thousand. The average price declined 0.9 percent, the months’ worth of supply went down to 3.6 from 3.9 and the supply of houses on the market decreased to 1.65 million, the lowest since 1999. The November figure was revised up to 5650 thousand from 5610 thousand. Considering full 2016, sales increased to 5450K, the highest since 2016, from 5250K in 2015.2017-01-24T15:09:35
