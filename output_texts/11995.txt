Title: US Inflation Rate Falls To 6-Month Low Of 1.9%
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Falls To 6-Month Low Of 1.9%United States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States increased 1.9 percent year-on-year in May of 2017, easing from a 2.2 percent rise in April and below market expectations of 2 percent. It is the lowest inflation rate since November last year although it was still above the 1.6 percent average over the past 10 years. Core inflation slowed further to a 2-year low of 1.7 percent. 2017-06-14T12:33:03
