Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Sharply Down
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Sharply Down United States Consumer ConfidenceThe final University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States came in at 92 in January of 2016, much lower than a preliminary estimate of 93.3 and below 92.6 in December. Both current and future expectations decreased and the 1 year inflation outlook eased.2016-01-29T15:07:35
