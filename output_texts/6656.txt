Title: US Crude Inventories Drop for 4th Straight Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Drop for 4th Straight WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 7.258 million barrels in the week ended February 12th, 2021, a fourth consecutive week of decline and compared to market forecasts of a 2.429 million drop, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories increased by 0.672 million barrels, while markets had forecast a larger 1.397 million advance.2021-02-18T16:02:00
