Title: US Stocks Slide Ahead of Busy Earnings Week
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Slide Ahead of Busy Earnings WeekUnited States Stock MarketUS Stocks declined moderately on Monday October 23rd of 2017, as investors monitored progress in tax legislation and President Trump’s pick for the Fed’s next chairman. Technology stocks underperformed ahead of a busy earnings week. The Dow Jones lost 55 points or 0.2% to 23274. The S&P 500 dipped 10 points or 0.4% to 2565. The Nasdaq retreated 42 points or 0.6% to 6587. 2017-10-23T20:09:31
