Title: Wall St Eases from Record High
Tag: Stock Market

Wall St Eases from Record HighUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks receded from record highs and turned negative in afternoon trading on doubts about whether Congress can get a deal on a new $916 billion coronavirus relief bill unveiled by Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin. At the same time, the threats posed by renewed restrictions on business activity drag sentiment. On the coronavirus front, FDA said Pfizer/BioNTech Covid-19 vaccine “met the prescribed success criteria” clearing it for emergency use approval.  The infection rate in the US remains elevated as more than 215K new cases were recorded on Tuesday, the third-highest on record. 2020-12-09T18:22:50.933
