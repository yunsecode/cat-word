Title: US Treasury Yields Fall
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields FallUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note went down to 0.6558% on Monday, the lowest in 2 weeks amid mounting concerns about the economic impact of the coronavirus as the number of cases in the country is increasing rapidly and President Trump extended the national social distancing guidelines to April 30th, backing off his hopes that the US economy will reopen by Easter. Meanwhile, the yield on the US 30-year Treasury bond declined to 1.2486%.2020-03-30T10:25:00
