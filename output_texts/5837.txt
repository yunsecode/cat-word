Title: US Stocks Set to Open in the Green
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Set to Open in the GreenUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures traded slightly higher on Tuesday, ahead of US Federal Reserve chair Jerome Powell’s testimony to Congress later in the day. Meantime, Fed Chair Jerome Powell in prepared remarks reiterated that the central bank will continue its supportive stance to the economy, and reiterated his view that inflation’s recent jump to a 13-year high would prove temporary. On Monday, Wall Street rebounded as investors continued to digest a sharp shift in the Fed’s approach to managing the US economic recovery after the central bank last week signaled that it may raise interest rates twice by late 2023. 2021-06-22T06:11:00
