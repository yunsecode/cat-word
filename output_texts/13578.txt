Title: US S&P Case-Shiller House Prices Up 5.7%
Tag: Case Shiller Home Price Index YoY

US S&P Case-Shiller House Prices Up 5.7%United States Case Shiller Home Price Index YoYHouse prices across 20 major metropolitan areas in the United States rose 5.7 percent year-on-year in January of 2016, the same as in December and below market expectations of a 5.9 percent increase. Portland (+11.8 percent), Seattle (10.7 percent) and San Francisco (+10.5 percent) recorded the highest gains while Phoenix ended its streak of 12 consecutive months of increasing annual gains (+6.1 percent). On a monthly basis, the S&P/Case-Shiller's 20-City Home Price Index was flat.2016-03-29T14:14:47
