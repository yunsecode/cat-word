Title: US Mortgage Rates Highest since November: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Rate

US Mortgage Rates Highest since November: MBAUnited States Mortgage RateThe average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($726,200 or less) in the United States jumped by 22bps to 6.91% in the week ended May 26th, reaching the highest level since early November. Mortgage rates have been on the rise in the last three weeks, mirroring the upward trend of the 10-year Treasury note yield, amid uncertainty regarding the US debt ceiling and expectations the Fed will keep interest rates higher for longer. 2023-05-31T11:10:00
