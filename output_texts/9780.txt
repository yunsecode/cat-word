Title: US Stocks Extend Rally on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend Rally on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street extended gains on Monday 7 January 2018 as sentiment continued to improve  after Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell said last week the central bank will be patient in raising rates, and after the US economy added 312,000 jobs in December, well above expectations. Also, the White House said on Monday that tax refunds will be sent out despite the ongoing government shutdown. The Dow Jones jumped 98 points or 0.4% to 23532. The S&P 500 climbed 18 points or 0.7% to 2550. The Nasdaq soared 85 points or 1.3% to 6824.2019-01-07T21:06:00
