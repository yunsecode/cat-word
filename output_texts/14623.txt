Title: U.S. Jobless Claims Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

U.S. Jobless Claims Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending October 26, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 340,000, a decrease of 10,000 from the previous week's unrevised figure of 350,000. The 4-week moving average was 356,250, an increase of 8,000 from the previous week's unrevised average of 348,250.
