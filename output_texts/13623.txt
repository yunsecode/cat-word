Title: US Stocks Fall on Lower Oil Prices
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on Lower Oil PricesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Monday as oil prices tumbled after some news reported Iran’s Oil minister the country would freeze production until its own output rose to 4 million barrels a day. The Dow Jones was down 35 points or 0.21%, the S&P 500 fell 0.27% and the Nasdaq lost 0.2% around 8 minutes after the open. 2016-03-14T13:51:05.937
