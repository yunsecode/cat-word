Title: US Stocks Gain on Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Gain on EarningsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Tuesday after better-than-expected earnings reports for Goldman Sachs, BlackRock, UnitedHealth, Johnson & Johnson, Philip Morris and Netflix boosted investors’ sentiment. The Dow Jones added 76 points or 0.4%, the S&P 500 gained 13 points or 0.6% and the Nasdaq finished 44 points or 0.9% higher.  2016-10-18T20:28:45.847
