Title: US Mortgage Applications Surge 9%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Surge 9%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 9 percent in the week ended January 15th 2016 from the previous week as fixed 30-year mortgage rates fell by 6 bps to 4.06 percent, the lowest in ten weeks, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications increased 18.7 percent while applications to purchase a home fell 1.6 percent.2016-01-20T12:06:16.923
