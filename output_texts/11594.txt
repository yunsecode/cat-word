Title: US Unemployment Rate Lowest in Over 16 Years
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Unemployment Rate Lowest in Over 16 YearsUnited States Unemployment RateUS unemployment rate unexpectedly fell to 4.2 percent in September 2017 from 4.4 percent in the previous month and below market consensus of 4.4 percent. It was the lowest jobless rate since February 2001, as the number of unemployed persons declined by 331 thousand to 6.8 million. Meanwhile, the labor force participation rate rose by 0.2 percentage points to 63.1 percent, its highest level since March 2014. 2017-10-06T12:31:38
