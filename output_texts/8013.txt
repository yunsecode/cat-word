Dollar Hovers at 96.7United States CurrencyThe dollar index traded around the flatline at 96.7 on Friday as investors' risk appetite dampened as rising coronavirus cases in the US and other countries including India, Mexico, Australia and Hong Kong raised further concerns about the global economic recovery.
