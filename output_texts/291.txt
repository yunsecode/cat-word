Title: Chicago Business Barometer Drops in May
Tag: Chicago PMI

Chicago Business Barometer Drops in MayUnited States Chicago PMIThe Chicago Business Barometer, also known as the Chicago PMI, fell to 40.4 points in May 2023, down from an eight-month high of 48.6 in April and well below market forecasts of 47. The reading marked the ninth consecutive month of contraction in business activity in the Chicago region, but one that was the softest since November last year.2023-05-31T13:55:00
