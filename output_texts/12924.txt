Title: US Stocks Fall Slightly Ahead of Fed Minutes
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall Slightly Ahead of Fed MinutesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Wednesday amid falling oil prices while investors await FOMC minutes release later in the day for further clues on the timing of next Fed's rate hike. The Dow Jones fell 38 points or 0.2%, the S&P 500 was down 4 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq traded 17 points or 0.3% lower around 11 minutes after the open.2016-10-12T13:48:27
