Title: US Stocks Fall on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened sharply lower on Friday after Federal Reserve Bank of Boston President Rosengren said gradual tightening of monetary policy is likely to be appropriate to avoid overheating the economy, raising expectations of a Fed rate-hike as soon as this month. The Dow Jones fell 149 points or 0.8%, the S&P 500 lost 18 points or 0.8% and the Nasdaq traded 35 points or 0.7% lower around 10 minutes after the open.2016-09-09T15:01:27.737
