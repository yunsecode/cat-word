Title: US Wholesale Inventories Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Unexpectedly FallUnited States Wholesale InventoriesUS wholesale inventories decreased 0.5 percent from a month earlier in February 2020, following an upwardly revised 0.5 percent fall in the previous month and missing market expectations of a flat reading, preliminary estimates showed. Stocks of non-durable goods declined further (-0.7 percent vs -0.5 percent in January) and those of non-durable goods fell 0.4 percent (vs -0.5 percent). Year-on-year, wholesale inventories dropped 1 percent in February.2020-03-26T12:42:00
