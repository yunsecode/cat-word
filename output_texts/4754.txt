Title: US 10-Year Bond Yield Eases on Weak Payroll Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Bond Yield Eases on Weak Payroll DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on 10-year Treasury notes edged lower to 1.41% on Friday, moving towards an over two-month low of 1.4% touched on December 1st, after US non-farm payrolls came in below market expectations. Investors have been weighing concerns over the emergence of a new coronavirus variant against prospects of a faster tapering by the US Federal Reserve. Fed Chief Powell said earlier this week the central bank will discuss whether to reduce their bond purchases faster than previously anticipated at its next meeting and the word "transitory" is no longer the most accurate term to describe current inflation.2021-12-03T13:48:00
