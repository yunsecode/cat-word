Title: US Manufacturing Payrolls Fall the Most in a Decade
Tag: Manufacturing Payrolls

US Manufacturing Payrolls Fall the Most in a DecadeUnited States Manufacturing PayrollsThe US manufacturing sector shed 36 thousand jobs in October 2019, following a revised 5 thousand fall in September and compared to market expectations of 50 thousand decline. It was the biggest decline in manufacturing employment since October 2009 following a strike at GM plants in Michigan and Kentucky, which ended last Friday. Striking workers who do not receive a paycheck during the payrolls survey period are treated as unemployed.2019-11-01T12:50:41.617
