Title: US Stocks Rebound On Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound On Tuesday United States Stock MarketWall Street recovered on Tuesday afternoon with the Nasdaq and the S&P 500 reaching record highs amid mixed earnings reports for Alibaba, 3M, Verizon, DuPont and J&J. In addition, materials led gains as copper prices jumped after news that a mine in Chile is potentially going on strike. The Dow Jones added 131 points or 0.7%, the S&P 500 went up 15 points or 0.7% and the Nasdaq gained 40 points or 0.7% around 1:40 PM NY time. 2017-01-24T18:53:20
