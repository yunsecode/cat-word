Title: US Stock Futures Trade at Record Highs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Trade at Record HighsUnited States Stock MarketUS Futures were flat in overnight trade Thursday, trading at all time high valuations after extending fresh record highs for the 6th consecutive session, as investors digest strong economic data and await the monthly payrolls report due tomorrow which will give an update on the labor market recovery and some clarity on the Fed tapering timeline. Among data, initial claims fell to a fresh 15-month low of 364K, below forecasts of 390K, job cuts were the lowest in 21 years and the ISM manufacturing PMI also pointed to robust factory growth. During Thursday’s regular session, the S&P 500 rose 0.5%, the Dow Jones Industrial Average was higher by 131 points to close at 34,633.53, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite ticked up about 0.1% to 14,522.38.2021-07-02T00:22:00
