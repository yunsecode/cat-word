Title: Core PCE Inflation Rises More than Expected
Tag: Core PCE Price Index MoM

Core PCE Inflation Rises More than ExpectedUnited States Core PCE Price Index MoMCore PCE prices in the US, which exclude food and energy, went up by 0.6 percent month-over-month in August of 2022, rising from the revised stall in the previous month and slightly above market expectations of a 0.5 percent uptick. The annual rate, the Federal Reserve’s preferred gauge of inflation, unexpectedly rose to 4.9 percent from the upwardly revised 4.7 percent in the prior month, surpassing expectations of 4.7 percent.
