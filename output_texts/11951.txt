Title: US Stocks Open Lower As Tech Shares Tumble
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Lower As Tech Shares TumbleUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Tuesday after Google has been hit with $2.7 billion fine by the European Union for breaking antitrust law. The Nasdaq slumped 29 points or 0.5% to 6219, the Dow Jones was down 24 points or 0.1% to 21386 and the S&P 500 declined 5 points or 0.2% to 2434 around 10 minutes after the opening bell. 2017-06-27T13:40:25
