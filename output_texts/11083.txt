Title: US Stocks Rise
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks RiseUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded in the green on Friday, attempting to hold gains for the 5th straight session. The Dow Jones was up 191 points or 0.8%; the S&P 500 added 17 points or 0.6% and the Nasdaq traded 42 points or 0.6% higher around 12:30 PM NY time. Last week, the Dow Jones plunged 5.5%, the S&P 500 5.4% and the Nasdaq 5.3%. It was the worst selloff in two years amid concerns over rising inflation and interest rates.2018-02-16T17:44:29.39
