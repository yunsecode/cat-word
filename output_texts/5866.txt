Title: US Crude Stocks Fall by Most Since September 2020
Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

US Crude Stocks Fall by Most Since September 2020United States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States plunged by 8.537 million barrels in the week ended June 11th of 2021, after a 2.108 million drop in the previous week, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed. It was the biggest draw since the week ended on September 11th, 2020.  2021-06-15T20:41:00
