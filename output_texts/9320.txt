Title: US Stocks Plummet on Tuesday on Renewed Trade Tensions
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Plummet on Tuesday on Renewed Trade TensionsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on Tuesday 7 May 2019 to extend Monday's losses, as renewed trade tensions raised concerns about global economic growth, after US Trade Representative Lighthizer said higher tariffs on $200 billion worth of Chinese goods will take effect on Friday. A new round of trade talks is still scheduled for May 9-10. The Dow Jones plummeted 473 points or 1.8%. The S&P 500 retreated 48 points 1.7%. The Nasdaq plunged 160 points or 2.0%.2019-05-07T20:05:00
