Title: U.S. Payrolls Rose 166,000 in October
Tag: Unemployment Rate

U.S. Payrolls Rose 166,000 in OctoberUnited States Unemployment RateEmployment in the U.S. rose more than forecast in October. Payrolls climbed by 166,000 after a 96,000 increase in September, the Labor Department said today in Washington. The jobless rate held at 4.7 percent.
