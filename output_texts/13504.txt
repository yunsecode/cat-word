Title: US New Home Sales Fall 1.5% in March
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Fall 1.5% in MarchUnited States New Home SalesNew US single-family home sales unexpectedly fell by 1.5 percent on the month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 511,000 in March of 2016, following a downwardly revised 0.4 percent drop in February and missing market expectations of 1 percent rise. The decline was led by a sharp drop in home sales in the West region. 2016-04-25T15:28:50.647
