Title: US Stocks Rally
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks RallyUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks climbed Monday following news that T-Mobile and Sprint are joining forces, and that Marathon Petroleum is buying oil refiner Andeavor. Also, better-than expected earnings reports from McDonald's and Allergan lifted sentiment. The Dow Jones rose 0.7% to 24,477; the S&P 500 gained 0.5% to 2,682; and Nasdaq added 0.7% to 7,167 around 10 AM New York time.2018-04-30T14:07:27.79
