Title: US Home Builder Sentiment Below Forecasts
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Home Builder Sentiment Below ForecastsUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States fell to 68 in June of 2018 from 70 in May, lower than market expectations of 70. The index measuring current single-family home sales edged down to 75 from 76 and the component gauging sales expectations in the next six months fell slightly to 76 from 77. Meanwhile, the prospective buyer index also decreased to 50 from 51. 2018-06-18T14:05:05
