Title: US Mortgage Applications Rebound In Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rebound In Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 2.4 percent in the week ended May 5th 2017, after edging down 0.1 percent in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went up 3.3 percent and applications to purchase a home increased 1.7 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates were steady at 4.23 percent.2017-05-10T11:06:58.153
