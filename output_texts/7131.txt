Title: US Wholesale Inventories Revised Higher
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Revised HigherUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the US grew 1.1 percent month-over-month in October of 2020, better than initial estimates of a 0.9 percent rise, and following an upwardly revised 0.9 percent increase in September. Inventories went up for durable goods (0.3 percent vs 0.2 percent in September) with hardware (4.3 percent) posting the highest gain. Nondurable goods stocks also increased (2.5 percent vs 1.9 percent) with farm products (15.8 percent) and drugs (3.9 percent) rising the most. Year-on-year, wholesale stocks declined 2.2 percent.
