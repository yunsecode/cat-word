Title: Dollar Under Renewed Pressure
Tag: Currency

Dollar Under Renewed Pressure United States CurrencyThe dollar weakened against a basket of major currencies on Wednesday, with the DXY softening towards 101, as investors reassessed the outlook for monetary policy and growth. Data on Tuesday showed US consumer confidence dropped to a nine-month low in April, exacerbating concerns that the world's largest economy would fall into recession later this year. On the monetary side, the Federal Reserve will likely deliver another 25bps rate hike next week as signs of persistent inflation suggest the tightening cycle is not over. Still, money markets are pricing in the peak for US interest rates in June and then a decline to end the year below 4.5%. Investors now look ahead to first-quarter GDP data for more clues into the state of the economy. The most pronounced selling activity was against the euro and the British pound. On the other hand, the greenback continued to appreciate against risk-sensitive currencies such as the Australian and New Zealand dollars.
