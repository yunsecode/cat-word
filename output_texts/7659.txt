Title: US Stocks Rally Led by Tech
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally Led by TechUnited States Stock MarketWall Street rallied on Monday, attempting to recover from first back-to-back weekly declines in months. The Dow Jones gained near 400 points, the S&P 500 more than 50 and the Nasdaq around 250. Tech shares were among the top performers after Nvidia has agreed to buy Arm Holdings from Softbank in a deal worth $40 billion while Bytedance said will not sell TikTok’s US operations to Microsoft but will pursuit a partnership with Oracle. Also, Gilead Sciences will buy Immunomedics in a $21 billion. Meanwhile, traders await the Fed monetary policy decision later in the week with the central bank expected to keep its dovish tone. 2020-09-14T13:34:00
