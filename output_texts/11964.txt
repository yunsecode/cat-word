Title: US Stocks End Mixed As Technology Shares Climb
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed As Technology Shares ClimbUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Wednesday, as crude oil fell over 2 percent to book its biggest drop in a month, falling deeper into a bear market on worries of a global supply glut. Technology stocks decoupled from losses and continue to rebound from its steepest selloff in the year. The Dow Jones lost 57 points or 0.3 percent to 21410. The S&P 500 edged down 1 point or 0.1 percent to 2436. In contrast, the Nasdaq eked out a 46-point gain, or 0.7 percent, to 6234.  2017-06-21T20:05:53
