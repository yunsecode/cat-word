Title: Dollar Back to 3-Month Highs
Tag: Currency

Dollar Back to 3-Month Highs United States CurrencyThe dollar index surged to 92.7 on Tuesday, back to over three-month highs and tracking a rise in Treasury yields after fresh data showed US inflation rate hit a new 2008-high of 5.4% in June, well above market expectations of 4.9%. The Fed has been reiterating that a spike in CPI is likely to be temporary but investors worry the inflation may rise even further, leading the Fed to tighten policy sooner than expected.2021-07-13T12:48:00
