Title: US Trade Deficit Narrows in March
Tag: Balance Of Trade

US Trade Deficit Narrows in MarchUnited States Balance Of TradeU.S. trade gap fell 3.6 percent to $40.4 billion in March from $41.9 billion in February as exports rebounded to the second highest level on record supported by increase in shipments of gas, oil, commercial aircraft, cars and farm goods.
