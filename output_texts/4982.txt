Title: US Stocks Hover at Records
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Hover at RecordsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks were trading around record levels on Tuesday with the Dow Jones and the S&P 500 hitting another intraday record amid strong corporate results and ahead of the key Federal Reserve decision. Earnings from Pfizer, Estée Lauder, DuPont, KKR & Co and Under Armour topped forecasts. So far, 55.8% of S&P 500 companies have reported quarterly financial results, with 82% beating earnings estimates, according to FactSet. On the other hand, Tesla shares sank around 3% after Elon Musk said the company has not signed contract with Hertz yet and after news that the carmaker will recall 11,700 vehicles. Meanwhile, the Fed's 2-day monetary policy meeting kicks off today and the central bank is expected to announce a reduction in its bond purchases. The payrolls report due Friday will also be in the spotlight.2021-11-02T13:37:00
