Title: US Stocks End November on a Positive Note
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End November on a Positive NoteUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Friday ahead of a meeting between US President Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping and after news reported that a Chinese official said "consensus is steadily increasing" between the two countries on trade talks. The Dow Jones added 200 points or 0.8% to 25538; the S&P 500 rose 22 points or 0.8% to 2760; and the Nasdaq closed 57 points or 0.8% higher at 7331. 2018-11-30T21:18:00
