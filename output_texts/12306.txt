Title: Dollar Retreats To 3-Week Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar Retreats To 3-Week Low United States CurrencyThe US dollar weakened on Wednesday after the Federal Reserve raised the key rate by 25bps as expected but left rate hike forecasts for this year unchanged. Policymakers expect another two rate increases while some investors were seeing three. The dollar index was down 0.9% to 100.9 around 2:45 PM NY time, the lowest since February 23rd. 2017-03-15T18:43:08
