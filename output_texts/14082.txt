Title: US House Prices Rise More than Expected
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US House Prices Rise More than ExpectedUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States increased 0.60 percent in July of 2015 over the previous month, above market expectations and the biggest rise in five months. Year-on-year, The FHFA house price index went up 5.8 percent. 2015-09-22T14:05:02
