Title: US Stocks Recover On Last Day Of Q2
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Recover On Last Day Of Q2United States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Friday, following losses in the previous session, amid a pause in the tech selloff. The banking sector was among the best performers and Nike shares also increased after better-than-expected earnings report. The Dow Jones added 114 points or 0.5%, the S&P 500 went up 11 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq gained 22 points or 0.4% around 20 minutes after the opening bell.2017-06-30T13:58:30
