Title: Dollar Index Struggles to Find Momentum
Tag: Currency

Dollar Index Struggles to Find MomentumUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar strengthened for the first time in six sessions on Thursday, breaking the 92.70 level spurred on by safe-haven demand stemming from a surprise increase in initial jobless claims as mounting lockdowns hit business activity. However, this upside momentum rapidly lost steam and the DXY bottomed around the 92.30 area. A worsening pandemic and a vulnerable job market raised speculation over further monetary easing, or additional government stimulus to support the economic recovery. But such prospects added to concerns that rising levels of debt could drag the US economy, and bring further dollar weakness. 2020-11-19T19:10:00
