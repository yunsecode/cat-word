Title: Fed Raises Benchmark Rate to 1%
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Raises Benchmark Rate to 1%United States Interest RateThe Federal Reserve raised the target range for its federal funds rate by 25bps to 0.75% to 1% during its March 2017 meeting. The hike came in line with market expectations as the US economy has almost met the Fed target of full employment and stable prices, and may get  additional support from President Trump promised fiscal stimulus.2017-03-15T17:59:12
