Title: US Chicago PMI Falls Less than Expected
Tag: Chicago Pmi

US Chicago PMI Falls Less than ExpectedUnited States Chicago PmiThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer for the US fell to 65.4 in December 2018 from an 11-month high of 66.4 in November, but above market expectations of 62.0. Demand fell after surging last month while output growth picked up to the highest since January. Meanwhile, the Supplier Deliveries indicator fell to its lowest level in a year this month and employment rose the least for three months.2018-12-28T15:10:04.467
