Title: US Stocks Extend Comeback Rally
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend Comeback RallyUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green for a fifth session on 15 February 2018, as a weaker dollar and mixed economic data were not enough to hamper investors’ morale. Technology shares led gains on Thursday, with Cisco Systems rising 5.0% and Apple 3.2%. The Dow Jones jumped 307 points or 1.2% to 25200. The S&P 500 climbed 33 points or 1.2% to 2731. The Nasdaq soared 113 points or 1.6% to 7256. 2018-02-15T21:13:44.06
