Title: US Stock Futures Extend Losses
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Extend LossesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street's main indexes looked set to start the week in the red, with the Dow Futures failing more than 100 points on growing concerns about the latest spike in coronavirus cases and its impact on the economy. In the US, the epicentre of the disease, the number of infections surpassed 2.5 million, with record numbers of cases reported in the states of Florida and Texas. US health secretary Alex Azar warned that “the window is closing” on the country’s chance to act effectively to control the spread of Covid-19. Last week, the Dow Jones lost 3.3%, the S&P 500 shed 2.9%, and the Nasdaq fell 1.9%. 2020-06-28T22:31:00
