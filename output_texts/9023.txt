Title: US Stocks Mostly Muted ahead of Trump Speech
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Mostly Muted ahead of Trump SpeechUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened flat to higher on Tuesday as investors await a speech from President Trump later in the day at the Economic Club of New York for further clues on trade with China. The Dow Jones traded around the flatline; the S&P 500 gained 5 points or 0.2%; and the Nasdaq was up 17 points or 0.2% around 5 minutes after the opening bell. 2019-11-12T14:42:00
