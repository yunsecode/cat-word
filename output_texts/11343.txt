Title: US Industrial Production Rises Less than Expected
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Production Rises Less than ExpectedUnited States Industrial Production MomUS industrial output went up 0.2 percent month-over-month in November of 2017, following an upwardly revised 1.2 percent rise in October which was the highest since May of 2010. Figures came slightly below market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. Manufacturing production recorded its third consecutive monthly gain and oil and gas extraction returned to normal levels after being held down in October by Hurricane Nate. Excluding the post-hurricane rebound in oil and gas extraction, total industrial production would have been unchanged in November. 2017-12-15T14:18:54
