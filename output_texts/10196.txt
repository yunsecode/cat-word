Title: US Durable Goods Orders Jump 4.5%
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Jump 4.5%United States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods surged 4.5 percent from a month earlier in August 2018, following a downwardly revised 1.2 percent drop in July and easily beating market expectations of a 2 percent growth. Transportation equipment drove the increase as volatile demand for civilian aircraft rose. 2018-09-27T12:32:00
