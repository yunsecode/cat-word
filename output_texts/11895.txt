Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall The Most Since November
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall The Most Since NovemberUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States shrank 7.4 percent in the week ended July 7th 2017, following a 1.4 percent rise in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the biggest decline since the week ended November 25th of 2016 as applications to purchase a home went down 2.5 percent and refinance applications slumped 13 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rate increased by 2bps to 4.22 percent.2017-07-12T11:06:52.073
