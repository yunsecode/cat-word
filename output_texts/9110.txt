Title: US Weekly Mortgage Applications Rebound
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Weekly Mortgage Applications ReboundUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsTotal mortgage application volume in the US rose 2 percent in the week ended September 6th, following three consecutive weeks of declines, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Mortgage applications to purchase a home rose 5 percent while applications to refinance a home loan were up just 0.4 percent. The average rate on the 30-year fixed decreased to 3.82 percent from 3.87 percent.2019-09-11T11:23:08.92
