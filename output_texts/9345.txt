Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most in Over 5 Months
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most in Over 5 MonthsUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 9.934 million barrels in the week ended April 26th 2019, after a 5.479 million increase in the previous week and well above market expectations of a 1.485 million gain. It is the biggest gain since the week ended November 9th 2018. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went up by 0.917 million, following a 2.129 million drop in the prior week and compared with market expectations of a 1.005 million decline. It is the first gain in stocks of gasoline since the week ended February 8th 2019.2019-05-01T14:32:00
