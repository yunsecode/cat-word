Title: US Mortgage Applications Rebound
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications ReboundUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 0.7 percent in the week ended February 2nd 2018, following a 2.6 percent drop in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went up 0.9 percent and applications to purchase a home were flat. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate rose by 9bps to 4.5 percent, the highest since April of 2014.2018-02-07T12:12:41.723
