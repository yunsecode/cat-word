Title: DXY Falls to Over 3-Month High
Tag: Currency

DXY Falls to Over 3-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index fell more than 1% toward 106, the lowest since mid-August, as traders raised bets that the Fed would only hike the rates by 50 bps in December. The latest Federal Reserve meeting minutes showed that a substantial majority of policymakers agreed it would likely soon be appropriate to slow the pace of interest rate hikes. During the last policy meeting, the Fed raised the rates by 75 bps to 3.75%-4%, the sixth consecutive rate hike and the fourth straight three-quarter point increase, pushing borrowing costs to a new high since 2008. But now it appears that the central bank needs more time to judge the impacts of the hikes.
