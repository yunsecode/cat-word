Title: US PCE Prices Rise Again in August
Tag: PCE Price Index Monthly Change

US PCE Prices Rise Again in AugustUnited States PCE Price Index Monthly ChangeThe personal consumption expenditure price index in the United States rose 0.4 percent month-over-month in August of 2021, the same as in the previous month, driven by increases in cost for goods (0.6 percent vs 0.4 percent in July) and services (0.3 percent vs 0.4 percent). Within goods, prices were up for durable (0.9 percent vs 0.2 percent) and non-durable goods (0.4 percent vs 0.6 percent).
Excluding food and energy, PCE prices edged up 0.3 percent, the same as in July and slightly above market expectations of 0.2 percent. Year-on-year, the PCE price index advanced 4.3 percent and the core index increased 3.6 percent.2021-10-01T12:33:00
