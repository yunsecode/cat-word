Title: US Consumer Sentiment Confirmed at 7-Year High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Confirmed at 7-Year HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe Thomson Reuters/University of Michigan's final reading on the overall index of consumer sentiment came in at 86.9 in October, compared with a preliminary reading of 86.4 and 84.6 in September. Consumers’ sentiment rose for the third consecutive month to its highest since July of 2007.2014-10-31T14:09:25.84
