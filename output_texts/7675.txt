Title: Nasdaq Books Best Session Since April
Tag: Stock Market

Nasdaq Books Best Session Since AprilUnited States Stock MarketWall Street rallied on Wednesday nudged by the tech sector’s biggest one-day gain since April. All six big tech stocks Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Facebook, Tesla, and Apple ended in positive territory. Investors appeared to shrug off news that AstraZeneca had paused trials of its experimental COVID-19 vaccine after an unexplained illness in one of the volunteers. The Dow Jones added 440 points or 1.6% to 27,941. The S&P 500 climbed 67 points or 2% to 3399. The Nasdaq jumped 294 points or 2.7% to 11,142.2020-09-09T20:04:00
