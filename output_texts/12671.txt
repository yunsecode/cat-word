Title: US Government Posts $137 Billion Deficit In November
Tag: Government Budget Value

US Government Posts $137 Billion Deficit In NovemberUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US government posted a $137 billion budget deficit in November 2016, a 112 percent increase from a $65 billion gap the same month of the previous year, as outlays rose 25 percent to $337 billion while receipts fell 2 percent to $200 billion. The fiscal 2017 year-to-date deficit was $181 billion compared with $201 billion in the same period of fiscal 2016.2016-12-12T19:04:41
