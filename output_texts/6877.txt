Title: Dollar Rebounds Ahead of Fed
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rebounds Ahead of FedUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index rebounded to 90.4 on Wednesday as investors await the Fed monetary policy decision later in the day. Chairman Jerome Powell is due to confirm the US central bank's pledge to keep its monetary policy accommodative for the foreseeable future. Still, any clue about its exit strategy could be a key driver for prices. Meanwhile, the Euro extended losses after an ECB official said the central bank has the necessary tools to act on Euro strength and could cut the deposit rate further to keep inflation target. 2021-01-27T11:48:00
