Title: US Exports Rebound in March
Tag: Exports

US Exports Rebound in MarchUnited States ExportsExports from the United States increased by USD 5.3 billion from a month earlier to USD 256.2 billion in March 2023. This surge, up from the revised figure of USD 250.9 billion the previous month, indicated a rebound in external demand as inflationary pressures across the globe started to ease. Exports of goods were up USD 5.2 billion mainly due to sales of industrial supplies, in particular crude and fuel oil, and natural gas. In addition, sales of vehicles also increased. Exports of services rose USD 0.1 billion, boosted by travel.2023-05-04T12:38:33.253
