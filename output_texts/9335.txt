Title: US Stocks Extend Losses
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend LossesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street extended losses on Thursday 2 May 2019, as stocks fell for a second day after Federal Reserve Chief Powell signaled no rate cut soon. The Dow Jones lost 122 points or 0.5%. The S&P 500 edged down 6 points or 0.2%. The Nasdaq retreated 13 points or 0.2%.2019-05-02T20:08:00
