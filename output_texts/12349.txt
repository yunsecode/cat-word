Title: ISM New York Index Falls For 2nd Month
Tag: Ism New York Index

ISM New York Index Falls For 2nd MonthUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM NY Current Business Conditions Index fell to 51.3 in February of 2017 from 57.7 in January, mainly due to a decline in employment (43.2 from 54.8 in January) and lower expectations for revenues (66.7 from 69). In contrast, purchases went up (53.3 from 46.9), prices paid eased (51.8 from 54.7) and current revenues increased (62.1 from 55). The six-month outlook also went down to 58.2 from 66.2.2017-03-02T15:01:08.343
