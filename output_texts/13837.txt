Title: US Private Sector Employment Rises The Most in a Year: ADP
Tag: ADP Employment Change

US Private Sector Employment Rises The Most in a Year: ADPUnited States ADP Employment ChangePrivate businesses in the United States hired 257 thousand workers in December of 2015 compared to a downwardly revised 211 thousand in November. It is the highest employment gain since December of 2014 and much better than market expectations of a 192 thousand rise. The service-providing sector added 234 thousand jobs and goods-producing added 23 thousand: professional/business activities added 66 thousand jobs, trade/transportation/utilities 38 thousand, construction 24 thousand, financial activities 13 thousand and manufacturing 2 thousand.2016-01-06T13:18:46
