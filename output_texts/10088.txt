Title: US Tech Stocks Decouple from Losses

US Tech Stocks Decouple from LossesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Monday 22 October 2018, with tech stocks decoupling from moderate losses across the board as earnings season continues. The Dow Jones lost 127 points or 0.5% to 25317. The S&P 500 retreated 12 points or 0.4% to 2756. In contrast, the Nasdaq gained 20 points or 0.3% to 7469.2018-10-22T20:50:18.807
