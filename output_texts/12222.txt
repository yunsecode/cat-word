Title: US Stocks End Mixed Friday To Book Weekly Loss
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed Friday To Book Weekly LossUnited States Stock MarketWall Street erased early gains to close mostly flattish on Friday, as investors scrutinized the US military strike in Syria and the loss of momentum in job creation for March. The Dow Jones lost 7 points, or stayed flattish, to 20656. The S&P 500 decreased by 2 points, or 0.1 percent, to 2356. The Nasdaq edged down 1 points, staying flattish, to 5878. During the week, all major indices booked losses, with the Dow Jones losing 0.03 percent; the S&P 500 0.30 percent; and the Nasdaq 0.57 percent. 2017-04-07T20:05:28
