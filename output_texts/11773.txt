Title: US Import Prices Inch Up 0.1% in July
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Inch Up 0.1% in JulyUnited States Import Prices MoMUS import prices went up 0.1 percent on the month in July of 2017, recovering from a 0.2 percent drop in June and in line with market expectations. It was the first gain in import prices since April as recovery in fuel prices (0.5 percent from -2.6 percent in June) more than offset a drop in non-fuel prices (-0.1 percent from 0.1 percent). While cost declined for non-fuel industrial supplies and materials and automotive vehicles; prices rose for foods, feeds, beverages and capital goods. Year-on-year, import price inflation was steady at 1.5 percent.2017-08-15T12:37:07
