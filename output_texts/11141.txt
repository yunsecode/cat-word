Title: US Job Openings Fall to 7-Month Low
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Fall to 7-Month LowUnited States Job OffersThe number of job openings in the United States fell to 5.811 million in December 2017 from an upwardly revised 5.978 million in the previous month and below market expectations of 5.900 million. It was the lowest level since May, as job openings decreased mainly in professional and business services (-119,000), retail trade (-85,000), and construction (-52,000). Across regions, the number of job openings dropped in the South (-117,000), the Midwest (-77,000) and the Northeast (-14,000).2018-02-06T15:06:49.163
