Title: NY Business Activity Contracts in September
Tag: Ism New York Index

NY Business Activity Contracts in SeptemberUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions Index decreased to 44.5 percent in September from 51.1 percent in August of 2015. New York City business activity contracted for the first time in eight months while the outlook for the next 6 months slowed to 62.8 from 68.5 in August.2015-10-02T14:50:44.833
