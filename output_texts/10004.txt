Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 5.783 million barrels in the week ended November 2nd 2018, following a 3.217 million increase in the previous week and above market expectations of a 2.433 million gain. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went up by 1.852 million barrels compared to market forecasts of a 2.276 million decline.2018-11-07T15:39:54.98
