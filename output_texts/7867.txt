Title: Dollar Falls Back to 2-Year Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar Falls Back to 2-Year LowUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar continued to depreciate against a basket of major currencies on Wednesday, as the United States faces the threat of new business closures due to the pandemic. Investors are also focused on new stimulus negotiations between Republicans and Democrats as any new measure has to be approved before the Senate leaves on an extended break on Friday.  On Monday, better-than-expected manufacturing data supported the currency after it suffered its worst performance in a decade in July.2020-08-04T09:24:00
