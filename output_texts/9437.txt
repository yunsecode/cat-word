Title: US Jobless Rate Holds Steady at 3.8%
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Holds Steady at 3.8%United States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate came in at 3.8 percent in March 2019, unchanged from the previous month's figure and in line with market expectations. The number of unemployed persons decreased by 24 thousand to 6.2 million while employment dropped by 201 thousand to 156.7 million. The participation rate fell to 63 percent in March from 63.2 in February.2019-04-05T12:31:00
