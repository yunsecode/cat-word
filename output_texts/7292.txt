Title: US Crude Inventories Rise Unexpectedly
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Rise UnexpectedlyUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 4.278 million barrels in the week ended November 6th, 2020, following a 7.998 million drop in the previous period and compared with market expectations of a 0.913 million decrease, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories were down by 2.309 million barrels, while markets had forecast a decline of 0.263 million.2020-11-12T16:03:13.44
