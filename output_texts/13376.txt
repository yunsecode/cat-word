Title: US Imports Rise 2.1% in April
Tag: Imports

US Imports Rise 2.1% in April United States ImportsTotal imports to the United States increased 2.1 percent to $220.2 billion in April from March of 2016. Imports of goods rose $4.3 billion to $178.9 billion, due to higher purchases of capital goods (+$2.5 billion); civilian aircraft (+$0.8 billion); computers (+$0.5 billion); electric apparatus (+$0.3 billion); industrial supplies and materials (+$1.1 billion); other petroleum products (+$0.3 billion) and crude oil (+$0.2 billion). Imports of services also went up by $0.3 billion to $41.4 billion, as transport increased $0.2 billion.2016-06-03T14:29:28
