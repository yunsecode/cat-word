Title: US Covid-19 Deaths Approach 300,000
Tag: Coronavirus Deaths

US Covid-19 Deaths Approach 300,000United States Coronavirus DeathsThe US reported 1,389 Covid-19-related deaths on Sunday, pushing up the total to almost 300,000. At the same time, more 190,920 people tested positive for the disease and hospitalizations remained above 100 thousand for the twelfth consecutive day. Meantime, US regulators authorized Pfizer/BioNTech vaccine for emergency use and the first shipments are set to arrive today. Healthcare workers and elderly residents of long-term care homes are first in line to receive the vaccine, and by the end of March more than 100 million people, or about 30% of the US population, are expected to be immunized. 2020-12-14T16:52:00
