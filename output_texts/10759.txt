Title: US Trade Gap Lower than Expected
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Gap Lower than ExpectedUnited States Balance of TradeThe US trade deficit narrowed to USD 49 billion in March of 2018 from a slightly upwardly revised USD 57.7 billion in the previous month which was the highest since October 2008. Figures came better than market expectations of a USD 50 billion gap as exports reached a record high boosted by sales of commercial aircraft and soybeans. Also, imports fell as payments for the rights to broadcast the 2018 Winter Olympic Games faded.2018-05-03T12:35:02
