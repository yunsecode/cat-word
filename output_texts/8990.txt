Title: US Mortgage Applications Rebound: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rebound: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States went up 1.5 percent in the week ended November 22nd 2019, after a 2.2 percent drop in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications increased 4.2 percent while applications to purchase a home dropped 1.2 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went down 2bps to 3.37 percent.2019-11-27T12:03:14.74
