Title: Dollar Extends Losses on Monday
Tag: currency

Dollar Extends Losses on MondayUnited States currencyThe dollar fell for the second session on Monday, extending Friday's losses after Fed Chair Yellen made no comments on monetary policy at the Jackson Hole Symposium. Concerns over the impact of Hurricane Harvey on the US economy also weighed on the currency. The DXY declined near 0.1% to 92.26 around 12:10 PM NY time, the lowest intraday since May 3rd 2016.2017-08-28T15:09:50
