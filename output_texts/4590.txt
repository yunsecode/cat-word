Title: US Wholesale Inventories Rise More than Initially Thought
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Rise More than Initially ThoughtUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the United States rose 1.4 percent from a month earlier to $771.09 billion in November of 2021, above a preliminary estimate of 1.2 percent. Increases were seen in inventories of durables (2.1 percent), namely machinery (1.2 percent), automotive (2.7 percent) and professional equipment (0.9 percent). Also, stocks of nondurable goods went up 0.3 percent, particularly groceries (2.6 percent), apparel (3.9 percent) and paper (1.5 percent).
