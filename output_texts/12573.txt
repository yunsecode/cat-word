Title: Most US Stocks Fall, Nasdaq Closes At Record High
Tag: Stock Market

Most US Stocks Fall, Nasdaq Closes At Record HighUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks closed mostly in the red on Thursday, after disappointing economic data showed the pace of private-sector job growth slowed in December while service sector expansion came in better than expected. The Dow Jones fell 43 points, or 0.2%, to finish at 19,899; and the S&P 500 retreated 2 points, or less than 0.1%, to 2,269. By contrast, the Nasdaq gained 11 points, or 0.2%, to hit a record 5,488, mainly boosted by rises in the share price of Baidu, Amazon and Facebook. 2017-01-05T21:22:11
