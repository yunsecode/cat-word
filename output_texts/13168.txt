Title: US Trade Deficit Rises to 10-Month High in June
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit Rises to 10-Month High in JuneUnited States Balance of TradeThe trade gap in the United States widened to USD 44.5 billion in June of 2016 from a downwardly revised USD 40.96 billion deficit in the previous month. Figures came above market expectations of a USD 43.1 billion shortfall as imports rose 1.9 percent due to higher oil prices and rising domestic demand while exports edged up only 0.3 percent. 2016-08-05T13:40:14.883
