Title: Upbeat Earnings Lift Stock Futures
Tag: Stock Market

Upbeat Earnings Lift Stock FuturesUnited States Stock MarketStock futures rose in overnight trading, amid better than expected corporate earnings while investors assessed encouraging data about a possible treatment for COVID-19. After the Close, both Facebook Stock and Microsoft stock surged after each reported promising revenue figures. On Wednesday, the Dow Jones climbed 532 points or 2.2% to 24,634.
