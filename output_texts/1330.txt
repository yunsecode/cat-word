Title: Dollar Steadies as Fed Decision Looms
Tag: Currency

Dollar Steadies as Fed Decision LoomsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index steadied near 102 on Monday as investors cautiously awaited a key interest rate decision from the Federal Reserve this week. The US central bank is widely expected to deliver a smaller quarter-point increase on Wednesday, while investors will be watching for clues on the path for interest rate rises. Data released on Friday showed that the Fed-preferred core PCE inflation measure in the US slowed to an over one-year low in December, supporting the case for a slower pace of policy tightening. US consumer spending also declined more than expected in December, indicating a slowing economy at the end of last year, with most analysts expecting a mild recession by the second half of 2023.
