Title: Wall Street Rally Gains Steam
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Rally Gains Steam United States Stock MarketAll three major US stock indexes added about 1% on Monday as investors welcomed upbeat earnings results from some big banks while scaling back forecasts for rate rises from the Federal Reserve. Goldman Sachs and Bank of America were the latest banks to beat Wall Street's expectations, with shares rising roughly 3% and 1%, respectively. In other corporate news, Boeing surged almost 3% on news that Delta Air Lines bought hundred 737 MAX 10 airplanes. On top of that, several Federal Reserve policymakers have supported raising interest rates another 75 basis points next week to curb inflation, rather than the full percentage point increase predicted by some analysts. 2022-07-18T13:31:00
