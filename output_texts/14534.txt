Title: US Jobless Claims Rise in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending March 15, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 320,000, an increase of 5,000 from the previous week's unrevised figure of 315,000. The 4-week moving average was 327,000, a decrease of 3,500 from the previous week's unrevised average of 330,500.2014-03-20T12:34:31.397
