Title: US Wholesale Inventories Fall for 3rd Month
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Fall for 3rd MonthUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale stocks in the United States decreased 0.1 percent to USD 582 billion in December from November of 2015, compared to an upwardly revised 0.4 percent drop in the previous period. Inventories of durable goods declined 0.3 percent, stocks of metals and minerals, except petroleum, were down 4.4 percent and inventories of petroleum and petroleum products shrank 7.8 percent In contrast, inventories of nondurable goods edged up 0.1 percent and those of chemicals and allied products were up 2.3 percent.2016-02-09T15:09:18.1
