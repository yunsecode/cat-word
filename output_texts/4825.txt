Title: US Futures Point to Losses at the Open
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Point to Losses at the OpenUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were flat to lower on Tuesday, following a big tech sell-off the day before prompted by a rally in Treasury yields and the dollar after President Biden picked Jerome Powell for a second term at the Fed. Markets now expect the Fed to continue its tightening plans with some officials even suggesting the central bank will need to reduce stimulus faster than initially anticipated. On Monday, the Nasdaq sank 1.3% to 15855, the S&P 500 fell 0.3% to 4683 and the Dow Jones ended little changed at 35619. Technology, communication services and healthcare sectors were the biggest draggers, while the energy and basic materials sectors gained. Meanwhile, investors await a slew of economic releases this week including the latest FOMC minutes and the Fed-preferred measure of inflation PCE.2021-11-23T08:02:00
