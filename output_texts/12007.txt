Title: US Stocks Reach Record High
Tag: stock market

US Stocks Reach Record HighUnited States stock marketWall Street traded in the green on Friday with the three major stock indexes reaching record highs. Financials led gains amid a rising dollar while investors shrug off the UK election results. The Dow Jones went up 83 points or 0.4% to 21265.6; the S&P 500 gained 10 points or 0.4% to 2443.4 and the Nasdaq added 18 points or 0.3% to 6339.3 around 10:20 AM NY time.2017-06-09T14:08:53
