Title: Wall Street Opens Higher On Lower Brexit Risks
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Higher On Lower Brexit RisksUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US soared at the opening on Monday led by financial shares, following earlier gains in Europe and Asia as fears of a 'Brexit' scenario eased. The Dow Jones added 1.4% or 248 points, the S&P 500 rose 1.3% or 27 points and the Nasdaq went up 1.4% or 66 points around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2016-06-20T14:55:06
