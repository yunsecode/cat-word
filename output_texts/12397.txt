Title: US Stocks Fall On Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall On Thursday United States Stock MarketWall Street traded in negative territory on Thursday morning after hitting record highs at the open and following 5 consecutive sessions of record closes, the longest streak in 25 years. The Dow Jones lost 19 points or 0.1%, the S&P 500 fell 6 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq traded 15 points or 0.3% lower around 12:20 PM NY time.
