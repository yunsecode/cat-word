Title: Dollar Extends Downside Momentum
Tag: Currency

Dollar Extends Downside MomentumUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index bottomed below the 105 mark, closing in on its lowest level since late June, as markets reassessed the Federal Reserve’s policy outlook following further evidence that inflation could be peaking. Data showed US producer prices declined in July for the first time in over two years, while analysts expected them to increase. The data followed a softer-than-expected US CPI reading on Wednesday, which suggested that the Federal Reserve could slow down its aggressive policy tightening plans. Markets are now pricing a 50 bps hike at the US central bank’s next policy meeting in September.2022-08-11T13:01:58.553
