Title: US Job Openings Fall More than Expected in February
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Fall More than Expected in FebruaryUnited States Job OffersThe number of job openings in the United States fell to 6.052 million in February 2018 from a downwardly revised 6.228 million in the previous month and below market expectations of 6.173 million. There were less open positions in accommodation and food services (-91,000), construction  (-56,000), and wholesale trade (-38,000). Meanwhile, job openings increased in finance and insurance (69,000) and state and local government education (31,000). The number of job openings decreased mostly in the West region (-148,000).2018-04-13T14:03:11
