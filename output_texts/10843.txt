Title: US Overseas Treasury Purchases Beat Expectations
Tag: Capital Flows

US Overseas Treasury Purchases Beat ExpectationsUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought USD 44.7 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments in February of 2018, after buying an upwardly revised USD 122.6 billion in the previous month. The figure contrasted sharply with market expectations of USD 26.1 billion of purchased assets. Meanwhile, foreigners bought USD 49.0 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying USD 61.4 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors bought USD 43.2 billion of Treasures in February after buying USD 8.4 billion in the previous month.2018-04-16T20:07:32
