Title: US Pending Home Sales Unexpectedly Fall In April
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Unexpectedly Fall In AprilUnited States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned homes in the United States shrank 3.3 percent year-on-year in April of 2017, following a 0.8 percent rise in March. It is the biggest decline since June of 2014. Compared to the previous month, pending home sales slumped 1.3 percent, following an upwardly revised 0.9 percent fall in March and compared to market expectations of a 0.5 percent rise. Across main four regions, contracts fell in the Northeast (-1.7 percent), Midwest (-4.7 percent) and the South (-2.7 percent) but rose in the West (5.8 percent). 2017-05-31T14:06:56.49
