Title: US Stocks Surge on Trade Deal
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge on Trade DealUnited States Stock MarketWall Street finished deeply in the green on Monday 27 August 2018, with the S&P and the Nasdaq closing at new record highs after the US and Mexico reached a deal that paved the way to replace NAFTA. The Dow Jones surged 259 points or 1.0% to 26050. The S&P 500 climbed 22 points or 0.8% to 2897. The Nasdaq jumped 72 points or 0.9% to 8018. 2018-08-27T20:12:00
