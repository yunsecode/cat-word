Title: US Job Openings Fall to Lowest in 8 Months
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Fall to Lowest in 8 MonthsUnited States Job OffersJob openings in the United States decreased by 388,000 to 5.44 million in August 2016 from a downwardly revised 5.83 million in the previous month, missing market expectations of 5.72 million. The number of positions waiting to be filled decreased mostly in professional and business services (-223,000), durable goods manufacturing (-29,000), and arts, entertainment, and recreation (-28,000). Among regions, job openings decreased in the South and the Midwest.2016-10-12T14:04:09
