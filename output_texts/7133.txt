Title: US Futures Point to Gains at the Open
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Point to Gains at the OpenUnited States Stock MarketUS futures traded mainly in the green on Wednesday, after the S&P and the Nasdaq closed at fresh record highs in the previous session, as optimism over Covid-19 vaccine rollout continues to lift investors' mood. US FDA said Pfizer/BioNTech Covid-19 vaccine “met the prescribed success criteria” clearing it for emergency use approval while the UK rolled out the first doses to the public on Tuesday. Meanwhile, Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin spoke with House Speaker Nancy Pelosi on Tuesday and presented a $916 billion coronavirus relief proposal, in another effort to reach a deal. On the pandemic front, more than 215K new cases were recorded on Tuesday in the US, the third highest on record. On Tuesday, the Dow Jones gained 104 points or 0.4% to 30,174. The S&P 500 climbed 10 points or 0.3% to 3702. The Nasdaq added 63 points or 0.5% to 12,5832020-12-09T10:13:58.197
