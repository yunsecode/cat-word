Title: US GDP Growth Revised Up to 3.7%
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Revised Up to 3.7%United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy expanded an annualized 3.7 percent on quarter in the three months to June, according to the second estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. Figures came better than a 2.3 percent gain initially reported and beat market expectations due to higher business spending and quicker build up of inventories.2015-08-27T14:51:38.827
