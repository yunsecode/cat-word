Title: US Home Prices Rise the Least in Over 2 Years: FHFA
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US Home Prices Rise the Least in Over 2 Years: FHFAUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States rose 0.1 percent from a month earlier in March 2019, easing from an upwardly revised 0.4 percent increase in February and below market expectations of a 0.2 percent gain. It was the smallest monthly increase in house prices since January 2017. Among Census divisions, rises in West North Central (1.1 percent); Middle Atlantic (0.8 percent); Mountain (0.7 percent); West South Central (0.3 percent) and South Atlantic (0.1 percent) were partly offset by declines in East North Central (-0.8 percent); East South Central (-0.4 percent) and New England (-0.6 percent). At the same time, prices were flat in the Pacific division. Year-on-year, house prices were up 4.9 percent, the same pace as in the previous month.
