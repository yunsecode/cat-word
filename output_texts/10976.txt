Title: US Export Prices Rise 0.2% in February
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise 0.2% in FebruaryUnited States Export Prices MoMPrices for US exports rose 0.2 percent month-over-month in February of 2018, easing from a revised 0.8 percent gain in January and below market expectations of a 0.3 percent increase. Prices of non-agricultural goods advanced 0.2 percent compared to a 0.8 percent rise in January, amid gains in materials, consumer and capital goods, and motor vehicles. Meantime, prices of agricultural exports went up 0.6 percent, the most since October last year, after a 0.1 percent increase in the previous month, mainly driven by higher prices of corn (7.2 percent) and soybeans (2.5 percent). Year-on-year, export prices jumped 3.3 percent in February.2018-03-15T12:37:28
