Title: US Treasury Yields Rise
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Rise United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note edged higher to 1.53% on Tuesday, getting closer to levels not seen since June, as worries over persistently high inflation and an early Fed tapering persist. Attention this week turns to the payrolls report due Friday which will provide an update on the labor market recovery. Traders will also follow negotiations in Washington to raise the US debt ceiling in order to avoid the first-ever default on the US debt. 2021-10-05T14:53:45.877
