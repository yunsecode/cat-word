Title: US Stocks Rise Slightly
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise Slightly United States Stock MarketWall Street erased most gains to close modestly higher on Monday after President Trump tweeted on Sunday that the US has made substantial progress in trade talks with China and the March 1st deadline to increase tariffs on Chinese imports will be delayed. The Dow Jones went up 60 points or 0.2% to 26092; the S&P 500 increased 3 points or 0.1% to 2796; and the Nasdaq closed 27 points or 0.4% higher at 7554.2019-02-25T21:14:18.82
