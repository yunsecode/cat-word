Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Edges Lower
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Edges LowerUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note edged lower to 1.64% on Monday, remaining below 14-month highs of 1.75% reached early in the month as the bond market seems to be stabilising. Investors digest mixed economic data and tame inflation, prospects of brighter economic outlook for the US and solid demand for recent Treasury auctions. Yields have been on an upward trajectory since August but the jump accelerated from mid-January as coronavirus vaccination and further fiscal stimulus support prospects of a strong economic recovery although traders worry that a rebound could lead to a spike in inflation and debt levels. Fed Chair has been reiterating any spike in inflation would likely be temporary and showed no concerns over the recent rise in bond yields.2021-03-29T07:49:00
