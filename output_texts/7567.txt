Title: Dollar Nearly Flat as Investors Await News on Trump's Health
Tag: Currency

Dollar Nearly Flat as Investors Await News on Trump's HealthUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded around the flatline near 93.8 on Monday as investors await more developments on the US presidential campaign after President Trump tested positive for covid-19 last week and went to the hospital for treatment. Despite contradictory and confusing information about the President recovery, Trump may be able to leave the hospital on Monday. Meanwhile, traders hope the new coronavirus bill could be approved soon after the House Speaker Nancy Pelosi said on Sunday that progress is being made on it. Last week, the dollar lost 0.9%, the biggest fall since the last week of August.2020-10-05T07:13:00
