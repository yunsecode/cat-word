Title: US Average Hourly Earnings Rise Less Than Expected
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Average Hourly Earnings Rise Less Than ExpectedUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls in the US rose by 4 cents, or 0.2 percent, to $26.25 in June 2017, following a 0.1 percent gain in May and missing market expectations of a 0.3 percent increase. Over the year, average hourly earnings have risen by 63 cents, or 2.5 percent.2017-07-07T12:52:08
