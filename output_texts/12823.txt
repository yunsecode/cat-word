Title: US Economy Adds Less Jobs Than Expected in October
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds Less Jobs Than Expected in OctoberUnited States Non Farm PayrollsNon Farm Payrolls in the United States increased by 161 thousand in October of 2016, lower than upwardly revised 191 thousand in September and below market expectation of 175 thousand. The change  for August was revised up from 167 Thousand to 176 Thousand. In October, employment continued to trend up in health care, professional and business services, and financial activities.2016-11-04T12:33:23
