Title: US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly FallUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 1.046 million barrels in the week ended January 28th, the first decline in three weeks, and compared to forecasts of a 1.525 million rise, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. Crude stocks at the Cushing, Oklahoma, delivery hub were down by 1.2 million barrels and distillate stockpiles which include diesel and heating oil, fell by 2.4 million barrels. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories increased by 2.119 million, above forecasts of 1.645 million.2022-02-02T15:37:00
