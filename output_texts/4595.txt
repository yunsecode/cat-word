Title: US Futures in the Red after Payrolls
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures in the Red after PayrollsUnited States Stock MarketUS futures cut early gains to trade in the red on Friday after jobs reports reinforced the view that Fed may accelerate its timeline for rate hikes. Although only 199K jobs were created in December, less than half of the market forecasts, the jobless rate fell to 3.9% and hourly wages surged 4.7% from a year ago. The Dow Jones lost 0.4% so far on the week and the S&P 500 1.7% while the Nasdaq plunged 4%, and is on track for its worst week since February 2021.2022-01-07T13:52:00
