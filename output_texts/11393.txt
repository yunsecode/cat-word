Title: US ISM NY Index at 4-Month High in November
Tag: Ism New York Index

US ISM NY Index at 4-Month High in NovemberUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions in the United States jumped to 58.1 in November of 2017 from 51.6 in October. Employment (64.6 from 54.8) and expected revenues (71.4 from 63.6) rose faster and a rebound was seen for purchases (56.3 from 45) and current revenues (64.3 from 45.5). In addition, the six-month outlook also improved to 69.7 from 62.6.
