Title: Dollar Rebounds Gains Momentum
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rebounds Gains Momentum United States CurrencyThe dollar index rallied to weekly tops at 90.80 on Wednesday, making a dramatic reversal after testing a key support level at 90.10 in the previous session. The rebound from an almost three-week low was backed by rising real yields, which, in turn, made the greenback a more attractive investment relative to other currencies. Still, signs of an economic recovery, fueled by large stimulus packages globally and a gradual rollout of vaccines continued to cap the upside in the USD. 2021-02-17T01:20:00
