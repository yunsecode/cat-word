Title: US Stocks Gain After Fed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Gain After FedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed sharply higher on Wednesday after the Federal Reserve left interest rates on hold but signaled a rate hike is on the table for this year. The Dow Jones went up 164 points or 0.9%, the S&P 500 added 23 points or 1.1% and the Nasdaq gained 54 points or 1% to 5295, an all-time high.  2016-09-21T20:32:20
