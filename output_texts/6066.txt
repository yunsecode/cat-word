Title: Treasury Yields Hold Gains After Three-Year Note Auction
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Treasury Yields Hold Gains After Three-Year Note AuctionUnited States Government Bond 10YUS Treasury yields remained slightly higher at 1.62% on Tuesday following a solid three-year note auction and ahead of a 10- and 30-year note offering later this week, testing investors’ appetite for government debt. The yield on the US 10-year Treasury note has been rising since last week on worries that soaring commodity prices and supply chain issues could create inflationary pressures that would erode the returns on such fixed-income securities. Investors now await key CPI and PPI data for the US scheduled for Wednesday and Thursday, respectively. A measure of US inflation expectations showed American consumers see inflation at 3.4%, the highest level since September 2013.
