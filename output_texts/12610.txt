Title: US Home Prices Rise 5.1% YoY in October: Case-Shiller
Tag: Case Shiller Home Price Index YoY

US Home Prices Rise 5.1% YoY in October: Case-ShillerUnited States Case Shiller Home Price Index YoYThe S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller 20-City Composite Home Price Index in the US rose 5.1 percent year-on-year in October of 2016, following a downwardly revised 5 percent increase in the previous month and matching market expectations. Prices in Seattle rose the most (+10.7 percent), followed by Portland (+10.3 percent) and Denver (+8.3 percent). Meanwhile, the national index, covering all nine US census divisions, rose by 5.6 percent year-on-year, up from 5.4 percent last month. On a monthly basis, the not seasonally adjusted S&P CoreLogic Case-Shiller 20-City Composite Home Price Index increased by 0.1 percent, the same as in October.2016-12-27T14:10:30.84
