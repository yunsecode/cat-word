Title: US Continuing Jobless Claims Rises for 6th Week
Tag: Continuing Jobless Claims

US Continuing Jobless Claims Rises for 6th WeekUnited States Continuing Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing continuing claims for unemployment benefits rose for the sixth straight week to 1.551 million on the week ending November 12th, well above market forecasts of 1.517 million. It was the largest amount of continued unemployment benefits recorded since the first week of March. The result signals that unemployed Americans are out of work for longer periods, in line with the slowdown of the economy brought by aggressive interest rate hikes by the Federal Reserve.2022-11-23T13:53:37.563
