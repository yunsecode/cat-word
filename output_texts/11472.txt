Title: US Mortgage Applications Flat in Latest Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Flat in Latest Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States stalled in the week ending November 3rd 2017, following a 2.6 percent drop in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went down 0.5 percent, offsetting a 0.5 percent gain in applications to purchase a home. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate declined by 4bps to 4.18 percent.2017-11-08T12:03:23.893
