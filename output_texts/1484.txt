Title: US Services PMI Revised Slightly Higher
Tag: Services PMI

US Services PMI Revised Slightly HigherUnited States Services PMIThe S&P Global US Services PMI was revised slightly higher to 44.7 in December of 2022 from a preliminary of 44.4 but continued to point to the biggest contraction in the services sector in four months and was the second-fastest since May of 2020. Lower business activity was commonly attributed to a further reduction in new orders, as client demand weakened due to the impact of higher interest rates and inflation on customer spending. New export orders also declined amid global economic uncertainty and high inflation in key export markets. Meanwhile, job creation was only marginal overall as cost saving initiatives and lay-offs weighed on hiring. On the price front, input inflation was the slowest since October of 2020 as higher supplier and wage bills were partly offset by reductions in some key input prices. Finally, service sector firms registered optimistic
