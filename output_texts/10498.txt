Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise in Latest Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise in Latest Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States went up 2.5 percent in the week ended July 6th 2018, after falling in the previous two weeks, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home rose 6.5 percent while refinance applications declined 3.8 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 3bps to 4.76 percent.2018-07-11T11:09:06.23
