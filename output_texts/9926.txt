Title: US Stocks Rally on Fed Powell Comments
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally on Fed Powell CommentsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street rallied on Tuesday 28 November 2018 after Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell said in a speech that interest rates are just below neutral, suggesting fewer rate hikes were coming in 2019. The Fed chairman's latest remarks compare to his October's observation on rates, in which he said “we’re a long way from neutral at this point, probably.” The Dow Jones surged 618 points or 2.5%, the sharpest climb in eight months, to 25366. The S&P 500 soared 62 points or 2.3% to 2744. The Nasdaq jumped 209 points or 3.0% to 7292.
