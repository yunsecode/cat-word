Title: US February Budget Gap Highest since 2012
Tag: Government Budget Value

US February Budget Gap Highest since 2012United States Government Budget ValueThe US government budget deficit widened to USD 215 billion in February of 2018 from USD 192 billion in the same month of the previous year, compared to market expectations of USD 216 billion. It is the biggest gap for a February month since 2012 as receipts declined 9.4 percent year-on-year to USD 155.6 billion while outlays increased 1.9 percent to USD 370.9 billion. 2018-03-12T18:06:45.69
