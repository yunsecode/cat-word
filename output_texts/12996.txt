Title: US House Prices Rise 0.5% MoM in July: FHFA
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US House Prices Rise 0.5% MoM in July: FHFAUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States rose 0.5 percent on the month in July 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.3 percent growth in June and beating market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. Year-on-year, the FHFA house price index went up 5.8 percent compared to a 5.6 percent increase in June. 2016-09-22T13:03:20.83
