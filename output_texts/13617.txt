Title: Foreigners Buy $118.4 Billion of US Assets in January
Tag: Capital Flows

Foreigners Buy $118.4 Billion of US Assets in January  United States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought $118.4 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments in January of 2016, after selling a downwardly revised $106.8 billion in December. However, foreigners sold $12 billion of long-term US securities in January, including government and corporate, after selling $29.4 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors also sold $50.4 billion of US Treasuries, after sales of $35.9 billion in December.2016-03-15T20:07:28
