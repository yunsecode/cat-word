Title: USD Falls after Payrolls Disappoint
Tag: Currency

USD Falls after Payrolls DisappointUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index lost 1.5% on Friday, the biggest one-day decline in six months after data showed nonfarm payrolls increased a meager 38 thousand in May, the lowest since 2010. The greenback lost around 1.6% against the Euro, 0.7% against the British pound, 1.5% against the Aussie and 1.8% against the yen around 11:40 AM NY time.2016-06-03T16:45:47
