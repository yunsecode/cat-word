Title: US IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index Falls in October
Tag: Economic Optimism Index

US IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index Falls in OctoberUnited States Economic Optimism IndexThe IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index in the United States fell 5.8 percent from the previous month to 50.3 in October 2017, below market expectations of 54.2. The gauge of the six-month economic outlook declined 4.7 percent to 48.7; and the personal financial outlook index dropped 4 percent to 59.5. Also, the measure of confidence in federal economic policies decreased 9.1 percent to 42.8.2017-10-10T14:11:44.96
