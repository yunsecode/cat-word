Title: U.S. Jobless Claims Up to 374K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

U.S. Jobless Claims Up to 374K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending October 5, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 374,000, an increase of 66,000 from the previous week's unrevised figure of 308,000.
