Title: US Wage Growth Accelerates in December
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wage Growth Accelerates in DecemberUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings in the US rose by 11 cents, or 0.4 percent, to $27.48 in December 2018 after gaining 0.2 percent in November and beating market expectations of a 0.3 percent advance. That lifted the annual increase in wages to 3.2 percent, matching the fastest pace since 2009 seen in October, from 3.1 percent in November. Meanwhile, average hourly earnings of private-sector production and non-supervisory employees increased by 9 cents, or 0.4 percent, to $23.05 in December.2019-01-04T13:34:00
