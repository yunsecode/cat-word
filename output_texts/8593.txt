Title: Dollar Depreciates Further
Tag: Currency

Dollar Depreciates FurtherUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar continued to ease from last week’s three-year highs, as money flowed into riskier investments after the US lawmakers agreed a $2tn stimulus deal to soften the economic impact from the coronavirus epidemic. The US currency has been appreciating since the beginning of March after a crash in the stock market and several lockdowns prompted funding and liquidity needs.
