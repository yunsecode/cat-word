Title: US Markets Fall at Open Ahead of Fed
Tag: Stock Market

US Markets Fall at Open Ahead of FedUnited States Stock MarketUS shares started the trading day lower on Wednesday ahead of a Fed policy decision later as lower-than-expected earnings from Apple and Boeing dragged investors’ confidence down while oil prices failed to sustain Tuesday’s recovery. The Dow Jones declined more than 130 points or 0.78%, the S&P 500 lost 0.28% and the Nasdaq was 0.51% lower around 8 minutes after the open. 2016-01-27T14:40:24
