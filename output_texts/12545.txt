Title: US Stocks Fall On Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall On ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded in the red on Thursday, mainly dragged down by financial shares after President-Elect Trump comments failed to provide details on planned growth policies. The Dow Jones was down 105 points or 0.5%, the S&P 500 fell 10 points or 0.5% and the Nasdaq lost 34 points or 0.6% around 1:00 PM NY time.
