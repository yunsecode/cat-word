Title: US Net Capital Inflow At 6-Month High
Tag: Capital Flows

US Net Capital Inflow At 6-Month HighUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought $110.4 billion of US assets in January of 2017, including short-dated instruments after selling an upwardly revised $65.3 billion in December. Meanwhile, foreigners sold $7 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after selling $21.9 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors bought $6.3 billion of US treasuries, after selling $12.9 billion in December.2017-03-15T20:09:39.493
