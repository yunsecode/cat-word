Title: US Goods Gap Widens in August
Tag: Goods Trade Balance

US Goods Gap Widens in AugustUnited States Goods Trade BalanceThe goods trade deficit in the US increased to $87.6 billion in August of 2021 from $86.8 billion in July. Imports rose 0.8% mom to $236.6 billion, led by consumer goods (4.6%) and industrial supplies (3%). Exports went up at a slightly slower 0.7% to $149 billion, pushed up by sales of industrial supplies (5.9%) while shipments of capital goods declined 1.5%. 2021-09-28T12:36:00
