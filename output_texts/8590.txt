Title: US Treasury Yields Fall ahead of Jobless Claims Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Fall ahead of Jobless Claims DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-Treasury note declined to 0.798% on Thursday as investors' risk apetite continues to decline ahead of the jobless claims release later in the day which is expected to show a record rise in unemployment due to layoffs and business closures as many states set lockdown. Meanwhile, the yield on the US 30-year Treasury bond also fell to 1.367%.2020-03-26T09:59:00
