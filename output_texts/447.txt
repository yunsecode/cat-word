Title: US Food Inflation Slows to 15-Month Low
Tag: Food Inflation

US Food Inflation Slows to 15-Month LowUnited States Food InflationFood inflation in the United States fell to a 15-month low of 7.7% year-on-year in April 2023, from 8.5% in the prior month and a peak of 11.4% in August 2022. Prices continued to slow down for food at home (7.1% vs 8.4% in March) and food away from home (8.6% vs 8.8%).
