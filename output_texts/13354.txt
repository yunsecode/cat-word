Title: Wall Street Opens Lower on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Lower on MondayUnited States Stock MarketStocks in the US started the week in the red led by losses in the tech sector, following earlier falls in Asia and Europe as investors remain cautious ahead of a Fed monetary policy meeting this week and rising concerns regarding the UK EU membership referendum. The Dow Jones lost 0.18% or 32 points, the Nasdaq went down 0.1% or 6 points and the S&P 500 traded near the flatline at 2095 points around 20 minutes after the opening bell.2016-06-13T15:04:58.067
