Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Unchanged after Fed
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Unchanged after FedUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury bond remained flat at 0.58% on Wednesday after the Federal Reserve kept the fed funds rate and its asset purchase program unchanged, in line with expectations. The 10-year Treasury yield has been around record low levels as the coronavirus pandemic seems far from controlled worldwide and specially in the US. 2020-07-29T18:35:14.28
