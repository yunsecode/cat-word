Title: US Futures Rebound
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures ReboundUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were in the green on Friday, rebounding from a sell-off in the previous session, as investors try to shrug off concerns over tax hikes and focus on strong corporate earnings and prospects for a strong economic recovery. Intel earnings and sales topped estimates but the company expects lower second-quarter earnings. American Express, Honeywell and Kimberly Clark and due to report today before the opening bell. Meanwhile, Senate Republicans unveiled a $568 billion infrastructure plan, much lower than President Biden's more than $2 trillion proposal, but the GOP plan is likely to be rejected by the Democrats. On the data front, preliminary Markit PMIs and new home sales will be in the spotlight. On Thursday, the Dow Jones sank 321 points or 0.9% to 33,816; the S&P 500 fell 38 points or 0.9% to 4,135 and the Nasdaq retreated 132 points or 0.9% to 13,818. So far on the week, the Dow Jones is down 1.1%, the S&P 500 1.2% and the Nasdaq 1.6%.2021-04-23T09:10:00
