Title: US Stock Futures Gain Ahead of Busy Week
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Gain Ahead of Busy WeekUnited States Stock MarketUS stock-index futures climbed modestly on Sunday night, with investors bracing for a busy week of earnings, economic data reports and the Federal Reserve’s monetary policy meeting. The earnings season will kick into higher gear, with Apple, Tesla, Facebook, and Microsoft set to be among the most highly anticipated.  Steady growth and resilience in the face of the coronavirus crisis made the tech sector an attractive investment. However, worries increase over frothy valuations as the coronavirus pandemic in the US is far from controlled. Last week, the Dow Jones added 0.6%, the S&P 500 rose 2.0% and the Nasdaq jumped 4.2%.
