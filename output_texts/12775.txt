Title: US Industrial Production Flat in October
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Production Flat in OctoberUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial output in the United States was unchanged in October from September of 2016, following a 0.2 percent drop in the previous period and lower than market expectations of a 0.2 percent gain. Warmer-than-normal temperatures reduced the demand for heating, hurting utilities output and offsetting gains in mining and manufacturing. 2016-11-16T14:21:27
