Title: US Imports Rise 1.6% to All-Time High
Tag: Imports

US Imports Rise 1.6% to All-Time HighUnited States ImportsImports of goods and services to the US increased 1.6 percent from the previous month to a record USD 244.6 billion in October 2017. Goods imports hit the highest level since May 2014, boosted by rising purchases of crude oil, food, cellphones and other goods. On a non-seasonally adjusted basis, imports from China and Mexico were the highest on record in October.2017-12-05T13:36:10
