Title: US Stock Futures Point to Lower Open
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Point to Lower OpenUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures edged lower on Wednesday after US President Donald Trump threatened not to sign a $900 billion COVID-19 relief bill, saying it should be amended to increase the amount of stimulus checks.   Risk appetite was also rattled by reports that US consumer sentiment dropped to the lowest in four months in December, while sales of previously owned US homes fell in November for the first time in six months. Meantime, the US  Justice Department sued the retail giant Walmart on Tuesday, accusing it of fueling the country's opioid crisis. The company claims the lawsuit is riddled with factual inaccuracies, according to BBC News. On Tuesday,  the Dow Jones shed 201 points or 0.7% to 30016 and the S&P 500 lost 8 points or 0.2% to 3687. In contrast, the Nasdaq added 65 points or 0.5% to 12,808.2020-12-23T06:17:00
