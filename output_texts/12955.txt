Title: NY Business Conditions Index Improves in September
Tag: Ism New York Index

NY Business Conditions Index Improves in SeptemberUnited States Ism New York IndexThe ISM NY Current Business Conditions Index rose to 49.6 in September of 2016, compared to a 47.5 in August. Volume of purchases increased (47.1 from 44.6 in August) and current revenues returned to an expansion territory (51.6 from 50). In contrast, employment dropped to the lowest level since the Great Recession of 2008 and its after effects in 2009 (33.9 from 54.9) and the growth of expected revenues eased (56.7 from 57.1).2016-10-04T13:57:14.21
