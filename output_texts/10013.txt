Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Monday 5 November 2018 ahead of US midterm elections on Tuesday, and as Berkshire Hathaway stocks rallied (+4.7%) after a SEC filing showed that the company bought nearly USD 1 billion of its own shares in an aggressive buyback program that raised investors’ confidence on current market conditions. The Dow Jones gained 191 points or 0.8% to 25462. The S&P 500 advanced 15 points or 0.6% to 2738. In contrast, the Nasdaq retreated 28 points or 0.4% to 7329, dragged by Apple (-2.8%). 2018-11-05T21:05:18.483
