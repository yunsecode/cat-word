Title: US Composite PMI Confirmed at 8-Month Low
Tag: Composite PMI

US Composite PMI Confirmed at 8-Month LowUnited States Composite PMIThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI was confirmed at 55.4 in August 2021, down from 59.9 in the previous month, to signal the slowest expansion in private sector output in 2021 to date. The slower overall upturn stemmed from weaker expansions in the manufacturing and service sectors. Overall new business and employment increased at softer rates, while backlogs of work rose markedly. Meanwhile, Inflationary pressures across the private sector remained elevated midway through the third quarter, as input costs rose substantially and the rate of charge inflation ticked higher.2021-09-03T13:59:45.383
