Title: U.S. Economy Grows 2.5% in Q3
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

U.S. Economy Grows 2.5% in Q3United States GDP Growth RateU.S. real gross domestic product increased at an annual rate of 2.5 percent in the third quarter of 2011 (that is, from the second quarter to the third quarter) according to the "advance" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.  In the second quarter, real GDP increased 1.3 percent.2011-10-27T13:38:02.063
