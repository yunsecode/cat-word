Title: US Futures Rise Sharply
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Rise SharplyUnited States Stock MarketStock futures in the US climbed on Thursday after a slump in Wall Street overnight, with all three major indexes falling over 2%, amid mounting concerns over a resurgent of coronavirus globally as well as fading hopes for imminent new fiscal stimulus. Traders now await the release of US Q3 GDP, with market expected the economy has grown by an annualized 31%, the strongest expansion ever,  after posting a record of 31.4 percent slump in Q2 when the COVID-19 forced many businesses to shut.2020-10-29T07:20:00
