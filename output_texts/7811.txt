Title: US Business Inventories Fall Less than Expected
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the US fell 1.1 percent from a month earlier in June 2020, following a record 2.3 percent drop in May and compared with market expectations of a 1.2 percent decrease, amid the coronavirus pandemic. Stocks declined at retailers (-2.6 percent vs -6.2 percent in May) and wholesalers (-1.4 percent vs -1.2 percent). Meanwhile, stocks at manufacturers increased 0.6 percent, following a 0.2 percent rise in the prior month. Year-on-year, business inventories went down 5.8 percent.2020-08-14T14:02:00
