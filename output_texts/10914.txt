Title: US Stocks Rise on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded higher on Thursday, mainly driven by a rise in tech shares namely Facebook, Alphabet, Apple and Microsoft. The Dow Jones jumped 297 points or 1.2%, the S&P 500 increased 31 points or 1.2% and the Nasdaq gained 80 points or 1.2% around 11:15 AM NY time. On Friday, markets will be closed in observance of Good Friday. 2018-03-29T15:34:04.3
