Title: US MBA Mortgages Applications Rise 1.6% in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US MBA Mortgages Applications Rise 1.6% in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States increased 1.6 percent in the week ended December 7th 2018, following a 2 percent gain in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went up 1.8 percent and applications to purchase a home rose 2.5 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 12bps to 4.96 percent.2018-12-12T12:02:44.537
