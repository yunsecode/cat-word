Title: US GDP Growth Revised Slightly Down to 2.5%
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Revised Slightly Down to 2.5%United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy advanced an annualized 2.5 percent on quarter in the last three months of 2017, below an advance reading of 2.6 percent and 3.2 percent in the previous period. Figures came in line with market expectations. Imports rose more than exports and at the fastest pace since Q3 2010, bringing the net trade contribution to negative. Also, private inventory investment fell more than initially expected. On the other hand, personal spending remained robust and fixed investment growth was revised higher. 2018-02-28T13:34:48
