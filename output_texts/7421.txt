Title: Dollar Posts Largest 1-Day Jump in a Month
Tag: Currency

Dollar Posts Largest 1-Day Jump in a MonthUnited States CurrencyThe dollar posted its largest one-day percentage rise against a basket of major currencies since mid-September on Wednesday amid a general risk aversion sentiment as coronavirus infections continue to spike worldwide. The greenback appreciated to as high as 93.65 this session, pushing the euro to one-week lows on news of renewed lockdowns in Germany and France. 2020-10-28T21:30:00
