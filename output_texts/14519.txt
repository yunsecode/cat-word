Title: US Jobless Claims Down to Nearly 7-Year Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Down to Nearly 7-Year LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending April 5, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 300,000, a decrease of 32,000 from the previous week's revised level. The last time initial claims were this low was May 12, 2007 when they were 297,000. There were no special factors impacting this week's initial claims.
