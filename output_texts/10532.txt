Title: US Stocks Trade Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade HigherUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened mostly higher on Tuesday led by a rally in energy shares after the price of crude oil broke above $75 per barrel for the first time since November 2014. The Down Jones jumped 123 points, or 0.5%, to 24,431; the S&P 500 gained 8 points, or 0.3%, to 2,734; while the Nasdaq traded near the flat line around 15 minutes after the opening bell. The NYSE stock market will close at 1 PM New York time today ahead of the July 4th holiday on Wednesday.2018-07-03T13:47:03.843
