Title: US Small Business Optimism Lowest in More than 1 Year
Tag: NFIB Business Optimism Index

US Small Business Optimism Lowest in More than 1 YearUnited States NFIB Business Optimism IndexThe NFIB's Small Business Optimism Index in the US fell to 104.4 in December of 2018 from 104.8 in November, compared to market expectations of 103.6. It is the lowest reading since October of 2017 as expected real sales growth and expected business conditions in the next six months dragged the Index lower. On the positive side, unfilled jobs and the lack of qualified applicants continue to be a primary driver, with job openings setting a record high and job creation plans strengthening. Also, reports of higher worker compensation remained near record levels and inventory investment plans surged. 2019-01-08T11:08:00
