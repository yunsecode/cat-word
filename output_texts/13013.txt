Title: US Consumer Sentiment Flat at 89.8 in September
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Flat at 89.8 in SeptemberUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe preliminary reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States was unchanged at 89.8 in September of 2016 from 89.8 in the previous month. Figures came below market expectations of 90.8, mainly due to less favorable current conditions while future expectations regarding the overall economy improved.2016-09-16T14:08:20.847
