Title: US Personal Spending Rises Solidly in July
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Rises Solidly in July United States Personal SpendingPersonal spending in the United States rose 0.4 percent from a month earlier in July 2018, the same pace as in June and in line with market expectations. There was an increase in spending for nondurables and services while consumption of durable goods fell for the second consecutive month.2018-08-30T12:39:00
