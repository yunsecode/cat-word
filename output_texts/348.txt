Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall the Most in Six Months: API
Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall the Most in Six Months: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the US fell by 6.799 million barrels in the week ended May 19th, 2023, the biggest decline in six months, after a 3.69 million barrels increase in the previous week, and compared to market expectation of a 0.525 million barrel increase, data from the API's Weekly Statistical Bulletin showed.
