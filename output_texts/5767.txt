Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Edges Down After Payroll Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Edges Down After Payroll DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note edged down to 1.43% on Friday, after the jobs report pointed to a continued rebound in the US economy, but the jobs gains were not strong enough to raise inflation and tightening concerns. The non-farm payrolls increased by 850K in June, well above forecasts of 700K; wages grew slightly less than expected while the unemployment rate surprisingly edged up to 5.9% from 5.8%. 2021-07-02T12:48:00
