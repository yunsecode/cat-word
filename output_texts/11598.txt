Title: US Exports Rise 0.4% to 2-1/2-Year High
Tag: Exports

US Exports Rise 0.4% to 2-1/2-Year HighUnited States ExportsExports of goods and services from the US increased by 0.4 percent month-over-month to USD 195.3 billion in August 2017. It was the highest level in exports since December 2014, as goods exports increased USD 0.6 billion to a near two-year high of USD 129.2 billion. Sales rose for consumer goods (up USD 1.0 billion), of which pharmaceutical preparations (up USD 0.6 billion); and capital goods (up USD 0.4 billion), of which telecommunications equipment (up USD 0.4 billion). On a non-seasonally adjusted basis, exports of goods rose to China (8.7 percent), Mexico (5.7 percent), Canada (12.5 percent) and the EU (9.1 percent), while sales to Japan dropped 5.5 percent.2017-10-05T12:38:08
