Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest Week United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 2.5 percent in the week ended September 11th, after rising 2.9 percent in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to refinance a home loan dropped 3.7 percent and homebuyer mortgage applications declined 0.5 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was unchanged at 3.07 percent.2020-09-16T11:02:00
