Title: Dollar Extends Decline
Tag: Currency

Dollar Extends DeclineUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar continued to fall on Monday after the Federal Reserve cut the target range for its federal funds rate by 100 bps to 0-0.25% during an emergency move the day before. The measure aims to protect the economy from the impact of the coronavirus and follows another emergency 50bps slash on March 3rd. The DXY was down near 1% to 97.77 around 5:55 AM NY time. 2020-03-16T09:55:00
