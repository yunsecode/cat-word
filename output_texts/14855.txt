Title: US Economy Sheds 131,000 Jobs in July
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Economy Sheds 131,000 Jobs in JulyUnited States Unemployment RateTotal nonfarm payroll employment declined by 131,000 in July, and the unemployment rate was unchanged at 9.5 percent, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported. Federal government employment fell, as 143,000 temporary workers hired for the decennial census completed their work. Private-sector payroll employment edged up by 71,000.2010-08-06T09:39:11.237
