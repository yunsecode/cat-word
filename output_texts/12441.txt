Title: US Economic Optimism At More Than 12-Year High
Tag: Economic Optimism Index

US Economic Optimism At More Than 12-Year High United States Economic Optimism IndexThe IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index rose to 56.4 in February of 2017 from 55.6 in January. It is the highest figure since October of 2004, as Americans are hopeful that President Trump will deliver on his promises to boost employment and growth. The personal financial outlook index gained 0.5 points to 62.8 and the measure of confidence in federal economic policies rose 2.4 point to 50.9. In contrast, the six-month economic outlook edged down 0.4 points to 55.6.2017-02-07T15:21:04
