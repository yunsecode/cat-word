Title: US Manufacturing PMI Revised Down: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing PMI Revised Down: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI edged down to 55.3 in July of 2018 from a preliminary of 55.5 and 55.4 in June. The reading pointed to the slowest expansion in factory activity in five months amid weaker rises in output and employment and higher inflationary pressures while export sales fell for the second month in a row.2018-08-01T13:51:00
