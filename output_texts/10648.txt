Title: US Wages Rise More than Expected in May
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Rise More than Expected in MayUnited States Average Hourly EarningsUS average hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls rose by 8 cents to USD 26.92, or 0.3 percent, in May 2018, following an unrevised 0.1 percent gain in April and above expectations of a 0.2 percent increase. Over the year, average hourly earnings have increased by 71 cents, or 2.7 percent. Average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees increased by 7 cents to USD 22.59 in May.
