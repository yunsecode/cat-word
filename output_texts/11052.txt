Fed Remains On Course For More Rate HikesUnited States Interest RateThe Federal Reserve remains on course for more interest rate hikes despite the stimulus of tax cuts and government spending and market volatility, new Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell said in a prepared remarks released early by the House Financial Service committee.
