Title: US Stock Futures Gain
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures GainUnited States Stock MarketUS stock index futures rose on Sunday night, as Wall Street looks to extend its record run amid growing prospects for a coronavirus relief package. Data on Friday showed the US economy added the fewest jobs in six months in November, dragged down by a fall in government and retail employment, and cementing expectations of more fiscal stimulus to support an already fragile economic recovery. On the coronavirus front, Pfizer and Moderna have already applied for emergency use authorization for their vaccines. Last week, the Dow Jones gained 1.0%, the S&P 500 1.6% and the Nasdaq 2.1%. 2020-12-07T01:08:00
