Title: US Q4 Labor Costs Revised Lower
Tag: Labour Costs

US Q4 Labor Costs Revised LowerUnited States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the US nonfarm business sector increased an annualized 0.9 percent in the fourth quarter of 2019, lower than a preliminary estimate of 1.4 percent and after a downwardly revised 0.2 percent rise in the prior period. Hourly compensation went up 2.1 percent (from an initial 2.8 percent), while productivity grew at a softer 1.2 percent (from an initial 1.4 percent). In 2019, labor costs rose 1.7 percent, reflecting increases of 3.6 percent in hourly compensation and 1.9 percent in productivity.2020-03-05T13:31:00
