Title: Fed Likely to Cut Rates in March
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Likely to Cut Rates in MarchUnited States Interest RateThe Federal Reserve is widely expected to cut the fed funds rate either by 50bps or 75bps during its next meeting ending on March 18th, in an attempt to curb the economic impact of the coronavirus. On February 28th, Fed Chair Powell issued an unexpected statement saying the fundamentals of the US economy remain strong although the coronavirus poses risks to economic activity and the Fed will use its tools and act as appropriate to support the economy. The statement aimed to calm markets after a big plunge in Wall Street and prompted other central banks all over the world to start loosening. In its latest FOMC decision on January 29th 2020, the Fed left the target range for its federal funds rate unchanged at 1.5-1.75 percent, raised the interest on excess reserves rate (IOER) by 5 basis points to 1.6% and said that overnight repo operations will continue at least through April 2020 to ensure that the supply of reserves remain ample.2020-03-03T11:19:02.807
