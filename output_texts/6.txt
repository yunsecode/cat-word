Title: US Export Prices Fall More than Expected
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Export Prices MoMExport prices in the US dropped 0.9 percent month-over-month in June 2023, following a 1.9 percent fall in May and more than market expectations of a 0.2 percent decrease. Prices for agricultural exports fell 1.6 percent due to lower prices for soybeans, fruit, and nuts more than offset higher meat prices. Also, nonagricultural cost decreased 0.9 percent as lower prices for nonagricultural industrial supplies and materials and nonagricultural foods more than offset higher prices for capital goods, consumer goods, and automotive vehicles. On a yearly basis, export prices tumbled 12 percent, the largest decline since the series was first published in September 1984.3 days ago
