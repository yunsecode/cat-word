Title: US Stocks At New High On Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks At New High On ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened slightly higher and at fresh highs on Thursday after booking the longest streak of record closing highs in 25 years in the previous day. Investors became more optimistic after President Trump promised a tax reform last week. The Dow Jones added 11 points or less than 0.1% at 20,622.6; the S&P 500 gained less than 1 point or 0.1% to 2,348.1 and the Nasdaq gained 11 points r 0.2% to 5,829.94 around 15 minutes after the opening bell.
