Title: Wall Street Falls Sharply
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Falls SharplyUnited States Stock MarketU.S. stocks traded sharply lower on Monday as uncertainty about the timing of a rate hike and concerns about growth in China continued to weigh on sentiment. The S&P 500 ended the session down 2.6 percent, below the key level of 1,900 for the first time since August 26. The Dow Jones retreated around 1.9 percent and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 3 percent. 2015-09-28T17:23:07
