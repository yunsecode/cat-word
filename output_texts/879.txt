Title: US Mortgage Rates Fall for 3rd Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Rate

US Mortgage Rates Fall for 3rd Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage RateThe average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($726,200 or less) fell by 3bps to 6.45% in the week ended March 24th 2023, declining for a third consecutive week and to the lowest level since mid-February, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. "While the 30-year fixed rate remained 1.65 percentage points higher than a year ago, homebuyers responded, leading to a fourth straight increase in purchase applications", MBA Vice President and Deputy Chief Economist Joel Kan said.2023-03-29T11:10:13.597
