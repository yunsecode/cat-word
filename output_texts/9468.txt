Title: US Personal Income Rises Less than Expected in February
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Rises Less than Expected in FebruaryUnited States Personal IncomeUS personal income rose 0.2 percent month-over-month in February 2019, rebounding from a 0.1 percent fall in the previous month but below market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. The increase in personal income primarily reflected gains in wages and salaries, government social benefits to persons, and proprietors’ income that were partially offset by a decline in personal interest income. Wages and salaries, the largest component of personal income, went up 0.3 percent in February, the same as in January.2019-03-29T12:31:00
