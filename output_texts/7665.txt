Title: US Futures Point to Big Gains at the Open
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Point to Big Gains at the OpenUnited States Stock MarketUS futures traded sharply higher on Friday, attempting to recover from the previous session losses, as investors try to shrug off tech valuation and a Senate rejection of new Republican stimulus bill. Futures contracts tied to the Dow Jones rose more than 200 points, the S&P 500 more than 30 and the Nasdaq near 100 points. So far on the week, both the Dow and the S&P 500 fell more than 2% and the Nasdaq plunged 3.5%. On Thursday, the Dow Jones lost 406 points or 1.5% to 27,535. The S&P 500 shed 60 points or 1.8% to 3339. The Nasdaq plunged 222 points or 2% to 10,920.2020-09-11T07:36:00
