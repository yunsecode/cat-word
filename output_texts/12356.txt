Title: US Stocks Reach Fresh Record Highs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Reach Fresh Record HighsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Wednesday, with the three major stock indexes reaching record highs amid rising expectations of a Fed rate hike as early as March following comments from a Fed official while President Trump speech on Tuesday failed to provide details on reforms. The Dow Jones gained 198 points or 1% to 21010.22; the S&P 500 added 20 points or 0.8% to 2,383.68 and the Nasdaq went up 48 points or 0.8% to 5,873.56 around 8 minutes after the opening bell.2017-03-01T14:45:58
