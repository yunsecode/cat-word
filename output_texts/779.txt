Title: US CPI Rises 0.1% MoM in March
Tag: Inflation Rate MoM

US CPI Rises 0.1% MoM in MarchUnited States Inflation Rate MoMThe CPI in the US increased 0.1 percent month-over-month in March of 2023, slowing from a 0.4 percent rise in February and below analysts' expectations of a 0.2 percent gain. The index for shelter was by far the most significant contributor (0.6 percent vs. 0.8 percent in February), offsetting a sharp decline in energy (-3.5 percent vs. -0.6 percent). 2023-04-12T12:36:13.36
