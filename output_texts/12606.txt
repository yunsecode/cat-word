Title: US Stocks Rise on Tuesday, Nasdaq At Record High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on Tuesday, Nasdaq At Record HighUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed slightly higher on Tuesday after being closed in the previous day for the Christmas holiday, after consumer confidence, Richmond Fed and Dallas Fed data came above expectations. The Dow Jones finished near the flatline, the S&P 500 gained 5 points or 0.2%, the Nasdaq rose 25 points or 0.5% and hit a new record high of 5,487.2016-12-27T21:13:37.02
