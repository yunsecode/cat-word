Title: US Mortgage Applications Drop 3.3%: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Drop 3.3%: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 3.3 percent in the week ended March 30th 2018, following a 4.8 percent rise in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went down 4.9 percent and applications to purchase a home dropped 2.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was unchanged at 4.69 percent.2018-04-04T11:02:34.887
