Title: US Industrial Production Falls More than Expected
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Production Falls More than ExpectedUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial production in the United States declined 0.5 percent in February of 2016 from the previous month, following a downwardly revised 0.8 percent gain in January. Figures came worse than market expectations of a 0.3 percent drop as sizable declines in utilities and mining outweighed a gain of 0.2 percent for manufacturing.2016-03-16T13:24:30
