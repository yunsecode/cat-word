Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Slightly Up
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Slightly UpUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States came in at 90 in July 2016, slightly up from a preliminary reading of 89.5 but well below 93.5 in June. It is the lowest reading since April as both current conditions and future expectations worsened, final figures showed.2016-07-29T15:13:56.283
