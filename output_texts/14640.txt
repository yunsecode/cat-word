Title: US Jobless Claims Up to 309K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Up to 309K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending September 14, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 309,000, an increase of 15,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 294,000. The 4-week moving average was 314,750, a decrease of 7,000 from the previous week's revised average of 321,750.2013-09-19T13:47:37.33
