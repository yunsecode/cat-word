Title: Dollar Surges on Inflation Data
Tag: Currency

Dollar Surges on Inflation DataUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar index jumped to 90.7 Wednesday, from the previous session's 10-week low, after a larger-than-expected rise in US consumer prices fueled expectations that the Federal Reserve might start tightening its ultra-loose monetary policy sooner than forecast. The consumer price inflation rate jumped to 4.2% in April, its highest level since September 2008 and well above the Federal Reserve's target of about 2%, as the economy reopens and both activity and demand rebound firmly.2021-05-12T12:42:00
