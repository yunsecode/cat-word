Title: Dow, S&P Hit New All-Time High
Tag: stock market

Dow, S&P Hit New All-Time HighUnited States stock marketUS stocks traded mostly higher on Thursday, after US Treasury Secretary Steve Mnuchin said the Trump administration is committed to passing a “very significant” tax reform before Congress' August recess. The Dow Jones rose 28 points, or 0.1%, to a new all-time high of 20,803, and the S&P 500 gained 3 points, or 0.1%, to a record 2,365 around 20 minutes after the opening bell. Meanwhile, the Nasdaq declined 11 points, or 0.2%, to 5,850.2017-02-23T14:30:07
