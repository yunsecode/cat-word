Title: US Fifth District Manufacturing Activity Index Falls to Record Low
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

US Fifth District Manufacturing Activity Index Falls to Record LowUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Manufacturing Activity Index in the US fifth district including the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia dropped to -53 in April 2020 from 2 in the previous month. It was the lowest reading on record, as all three main components of the composite index deteriorated: shipments (-70 vs 13 in March), new orders (-61 vs 0) and employment (-21 vs -7). In addition, firms reported a further decline in backlogs of orders (-42 vs -8). Meantime, respondents reported a rise in the growth rates of prices paid (1.48 vs 1.29) while that of prices received fell (0.92 vs 1.22). Finally, manufacturers were pessimistic regarding business conditions in the coming months.2020-04-28T14:02:00
