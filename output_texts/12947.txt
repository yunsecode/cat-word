Title: US Markit Services PMI Revised Up to 52.3
Tag: Services PMI

US Markit Services PMI Revised Up to 52.3United States Services PMIThe final Markit US Services PMI increased to 52.3 in September of 2016 from a preliminary reading of 51.9 and 51 in August. It is the highest figure since April. New work increased only moderately, new business growth eased and employment was the weakest since March of 2013 while backlogs of work increased for the third month. Input cost inflation eased. 2016-10-05T13:53:09.95
