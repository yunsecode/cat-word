Title: US Retail Sales Rise More than Expected
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States increased 0.8 percent month-over-month in November of 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.5 percent rise in October and beating market expectations of 0.3 percent. November was the month of the holiday shopping season, with sales rising for most categories except autos and at general merchandise stores.2017-12-14T13:38:25
