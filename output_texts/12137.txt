Title: US Services Sector Growth Beats Expectations In April: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Sector Growth Beats Expectations In April: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States went up to 57.5 in April of 2017 from 55.2 in March, beating market expectations of 55.8. Business activity increased and new orders reached the highest since August of 2005 as non-manufacturers were mostly positive about business conditions and the overall economy.2017-05-03T14:07:10
