Title: US Stock Futures Muted
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Muted United States Stock MarketStock futures contracts tied to the three major indices were mostly flat on Tuesday, as investors refrained from placing big bets ahead of crucial US inflation data, which could pave the way for the Fed's policy stance. The annual inflation rate in the US likely accelerated to 8.4% in March of 2022, the highest since January 1982. Runaway inflation and an extremely tight job market brought forward the expectations of sharper interest-rate hikes to cool an overheating economy, which, in turn, sparked a selloff among tech and other growth stocks. In regular trading on Monday, the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite dropped 2.18%, while the Dow and S&P 500 fell 1.19% and 1.69%, respectively.2022-04-12T12:03:56.403
