Title: US Futures Lack Direction
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Lack DirectionUnited States Stock MarketUS futures lack direction on Friday, after hitting record highs in the previous session, as investors continue to follow any developments on US stimulus. Senate Majority Leader Mitch McConnell said a bipartisan deal “appears to be close at hand” and House Speaker Nancy Pelosi also said Democrats were moving closer to a consensus. Meanwhile, FDA advisory panels endorsed the emergency use of Moderna’s coronavirus vaccine, paving the way for second vaccine approval. On Thursday, The Dow Jones added 149 points or 0.5% to 30,303. The S&P 500 climbed 21 points or 0.6% to 3722. The Nasdaq jumped 107 points or 0.8% to 12,765.2020-12-18T09:51:00
