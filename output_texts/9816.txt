Title: US Jobless Claims Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits decreased by 1 thousand to 216 thousand in the week ending December 22 from the previous week’s revised level of 217 thousand. It compares with market expectations of 217 thousand. According to unadjusted data, the largest declines were seen in Texas (-1,823); Michigan (-1,513); Florida (-1,007) and Louisiana (-488) while the biggest increases were reported in New Jersey (+6,155); Pennsylvania (+3,078); Missouri (+2,463) and Ohio (+2,385). 2018-12-27T13:31:00
