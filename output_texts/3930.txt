Greenback Gains Pace, New 2-Year HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index bounced back from early losses 100.3 on Tuesday, with its 8-day rally regaining momentum after Fed Governor Brainard’s hawkish comments overshadowed inflation figures. Earlier, core consumer prices in March rose less than analysts had forecasted, while headline inflation came hotter-than-expected.
