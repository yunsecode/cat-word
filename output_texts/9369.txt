Title: Dollar Retreats from 23-Month Highs
Tag: Currency

Dollar Retreats from 23-Month HighsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar fell slightly from 23-month highs on Friday as investors waited to see whether US GDP data due out later will reinforce signs of economic strength. The dollar index was down 0.0607 or 0.06% to 98.1423 on Friday April 26 from 98.2030 in the previous trading session.2019-04-26T10:13:00
