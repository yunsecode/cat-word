Title: US Stocks Rise Ahead of Yellen
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise Ahead of YellenUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Friday, minutes before the highly anticipated speech by Fed's Yellen and despite a downward revision to US GDP growth in the second quarter. The Dow Jones gained 63 points or 0.3%, the S&P 500 added 7 points or 0.3% and the Nasdaq traded 19 points or 0.3% higher around 5 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-08-26T14:43:41
