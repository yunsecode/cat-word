Title: US Business Inventories Rise More than Expected
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the US rose 2 percent from a month earlier in March of 2022, quickening from an upwardly revised 1.8 percent increase in the prior month and beating market expectations of a 1.9 percent advance. Stocks rose the most among merchant wholesalers (2.3 percent vs 2.8 percent in February), followed by retailers (2.3 percent vs 1.6 percent) and manufacturers (1.3 percent vs 0.9 percent). Year-on-year, business inventories surged by 14.7 percent in March.
