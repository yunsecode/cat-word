Title: US Budget Deficit Widens More than Expected in June
Tag: Government Budget Value

US Budget Deficit Widens More than Expected in JuneUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US government budget deficit came at USD 227.8 billion in June 2023, widening from a USD 88.8 billion gap in the same month of the previous year, and below market expectations of a USD 175 billion deficit as revenues continued to weaken and July benefit payments were accelerated into June. Revenues declined by 9.2% to USD 418 billion due to lower non-withheld individual income taxes. Outlays soared 17.5% to $646 billion led by higher individual tax refunds as the Internal Revenue Service cleared a backlog of unprocessed receipts. Considering the first nine months of the fiscal year, the budget deficit stood at USD 1.393 trillion compared to a USD 1.375 billion deficit a year earlier.
