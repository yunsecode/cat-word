Title: Dow Futures Drop Over 150 Points
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Futures Drop Over 150 Points United States Stock MarketWall Street's main indexes looked set to start Thursday in the red, as investors weighed a solemn address from the top-ranking Federal Reserve signalling economic risks from the virus were significant. The US Central Bank reassured investors of its support for the economy but forecasted a 6.5% decline in gross domestic product this year. On Wednesday, the Dow Jones shed 282 points or 1% to 26,990. The S&P 500 lost 17 points or 0.5% to 3190. In contrast, the Nasdaq gained 67 points or 0.7% to 10,020.2020-06-11T00:51:12.623
