Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 4th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise for 4th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 0.414 million barrels in the week ended February 14th 2020, following a 7.459 million gain in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 2.494 million gain, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went down by 1.971 million barrels after decreasing by 0.095 million in the previous week and compared with market consensus of a 0.435 million rise. 2020-02-20T16:04:37.67
