Title: Dollar Depreciates after Moderna's Coronavirus Vaccine Results
Tag: Currency

Dollar Depreciates after Moderna's Coronavirus Vaccine ResultsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index fell to below 99.7 on Monday, the lowest on a closing basis since May 4th amid a rise in investors’ optimism after news that Moderna's phase 1 Covid-19 vaccine trial produced positive antibody responses in all 45 human volunteers tested. Investors continue to welcome global reopening efforts despite concerns that the economic damage caused by the coronavirus may be bigger than anticipated. Meanwhile, a rise in oil prices helped to boost commodity-driven currencies 2020-05-18T13:04:00
