Title: US Dollar Index Steady
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Index SteadyUnited States CurrencyThe US Dollar Index held steady at 93.62 in early Thursday trade. Investors remain cautious regarding further fiscal stimulus measures as President Donald Trump urged Congress to pass a series of smaller, standalone bills after abruptly calling off negotiations on a comprehensive bill on Tuesday. Meantime, minutes from the Federal Open Market Committee on Wednesday showed that policy makers remained concerned that a lack of further fiscal stimulus would jeopardize the economic recovery and exacerbate economic hardships in minority and lower-income communities.2020-10-08T00:32:00.557
