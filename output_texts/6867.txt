Title: US Crude Inventories Post Biggest Drop in 6 Months
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Post Biggest Drop in 6 MonthsUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 9.91 million barrels in the week ended January 22nd 2021, the biggest decline since the week ended July 24th 2020 and compared to market forecasts of a 0.43 million rise, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Crude stockpiles at Cushing, Oklahoma fell to the lowest since July. Meantime, gasoline inventories rose by 2.47 million barrels, while markets had forecast a 1.764 million increase.2021-01-27T15:47:00
