Title: US Wholesale Inventories Revised Lower
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US Wholesale Inventories Revised LowerUnited States Wholesale InventoriesUS wholesale inventories rose 0.3 percent from a month earlier in April 2020, less than a preliminary estimate of a 0.4 percent gain but rebounding from March’s revised figure of a 1.1 percent fall. Stocks of nondurable goods increased 1.1 percent (vs 1.3 percent in a preliminary estimate), while durable goods inventories dropped 0.3 percent (vs -0.2 percent in a preliminary estimate). Year-on-year, wholesale inventories went down 2.8 percent in April.2020-06-09T14:02:00
