Title: US Services Sector Growth at 3-Month High: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Sector Growth at 3-Month High: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services PMI was revised higher to 58.7 in October of 2021 from a preliminary of 58.2. The latest expansion was sharp overall and the quickest since July. The upturn was faster than the series average, with firms linking the increase to greater client demand and a further rise in new business. The rise in output was the quickest for three months and was supported by a stronger expansion in new business. In line with greater new order inflows, firms signalled the fastest increase in backlogs of work since data collection began in October 2009, despite a faster pace of job creation. Nonetheless, concerns regarding labor shortages and unstable supply chains led business confidence to drop to an eight-month low. Meanwhile, the rate of cost inflation eased to an eight-month low, despite being quicker than any pace of increase seen before March 2021.
