Title: US Jobless Rate Falls More than Expected
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Falls More than ExpectedUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate declined to 3.6 percent in March of 2022 from 3.8 percent in the previous month, the lowest since February 2020 and below market expectations of 3.7 percent. The number of unemployed people declined by 318 thousand to 5.952 million, while employment levels rose by 736 thousand to 158.458 million. Meanwhile, the labor force participation rate edged up to 62.4 percent in March, the highest level since March 2020.2022-04-01T12:32:00
