Title: US ISM Non Manufacturing Lowest in 5 Months
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US ISM Non Manufacturing Lowest in 5 MonthsUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index registered 55.9 percent in November of 2015, down from 59.1 percent in October, well below forecasts. It is the lowest reading since May as new orders, employment and business activity fell while prices increased.2015-12-03T15:06:19
