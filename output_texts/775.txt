Title: Treasury Yields Retreat After CPI
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Treasury Yields Retreat After CPIUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the 10-year US Treasury note sank to below the 3.35% mark on Wednesday, as cooler-than-expected inflation data eased pressure on the Federal Reserve to increase interest rates. Consumer prices rose by 5% in March, below market expectations of 5.2% and slowing from the 6% increase in the previous month. On the other hand, the annual core inflation edged higher for the first time in six months. As a result, money markets pared bets of a 25bps interest rate increase by the Fed in May, extending the dovish momentum following a release of softer labor market data last week. Traders now await FOMC minutes for further clues on the Fed's next move.2023-04-12T12:47:00
