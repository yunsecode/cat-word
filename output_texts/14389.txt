Title: US Jobless Claims Rise Below Expectations
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise Below ExpectationsUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending September 20, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 293,000, an increase of 12,000 from the previous week's revised level.  The 4-week moving average was 298,500, a decrease of 1,250 from the previous week's revised average. 2014-09-25T13:36:05.91
