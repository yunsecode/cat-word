Title: US Construction Spending Unexpectedly Falls in June
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Unexpectedly Falls in JuneUnited States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the US dropped 1.3 percent month-on-month to USD 1.21 trillion in June 2017, the lowest level since September 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.3 percent gain in May and missing market expectations of a 0.4 percent increase. Investment in public projects shrank 5.4 percent, the biggest decline since March 2002, as outlays on educational projects fell 5.5 percent and highways construction spending plunged 6.6 percent. Also, private construction spending declined 0.1 percent.2017-08-01T14:04:49
