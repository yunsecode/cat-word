Title: US Non Manufacturing Sector Grows The Most in 11 Months: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Non Manufacturing Sector Grows The Most in 11 Months: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index jumped to 57.1 in September of 2016 after slumping to a 6-1/2-year low of 51.4 in August. It is the highest reading since October and well above market expectations of 53 as business activity, new orders and employment all expanded at a faster pace.
