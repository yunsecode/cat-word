Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Factory Growth At 4-Month High: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit Flash US Manufacturing PMI increased to 53.2 in July of 2017 from 52 in June, beating market expectations of 52.1. It is the highest value in four months as output, new orders, employment and stocks of inputs rose faster.
