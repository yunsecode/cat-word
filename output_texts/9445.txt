Title: US Services Growth Lowest since 2017: ISM
Tag: Non Manufacturing PMI

US Services Growth Lowest since 2017: ISMUnited States Non Manufacturing PMIThe ISM Non-Manufacturing PMI index for the United States slumped to 56.1 in March of 2019 from 59.7 in February, below market expectations of 58. The reading pointed to the weakest expansion in the services sector since August of 2017, amid a slowdown in production and new orders. Respondents remain mostly optimistic about overall business conditions and the economy but concerns remain about employment resources and capacity constraints.2019-04-03T14:05:37.193
