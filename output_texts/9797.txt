US Factory Activity Growth Slows to 2-Year Low: ISMUnited States Business ConfidenceThe ISM Manufacturing PMI in the US fell to 54.1 in December, the weakest since November 2016, from 59.3 in November and missing market expectations of 57.9. It was the largest monthly drop since October 2008 as growth in new orders, production and employment slowed sharply. 2019-01-03T15:03:00
