Title: US Jobless Rate Unchanged for 3rd Month
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Unchanged for 3rd MonthUnited States Unemployment RateThe US unemployment rate was unchanged at 3.6 percent in May of 2022, the same as in the previous two months, remaining the lowest since February 2020 and compared with market expectations of 3.5 percent. The number of unemployed people increased by 9 thousand to 5.950 million, while employment levels rose by 321 thousand to 158.426 million. Meanwhile, the labor force participation rate edged up to 62.3 percent in May from a 3-month low of 62.2 percent in April.2022-06-03T12:33:00
