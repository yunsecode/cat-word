Title: US Stocks Open Mixed on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Mixed on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened mixed on Tuesday amid better-than-expected retail sales data and a rebound in oil prices while the bond rout eased. The Dow Jones lost 32 points or 0.2%, struggling to hold its seventh day of gains. In contrast, the S&P 500 was up 5 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq gained 33 points or 0.6% around 11 minutes after the opening bell.2016-11-15T14:47:34
