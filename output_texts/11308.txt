Title: US Jobless Claims Above Expectations in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Above Expectations in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits was unchanged at 245 thousand in the week ended December 23rd 2017, the same as in the previous period and slightly above market expectations of 240 thousand. It remains the highest number since the week ended November 11th. Claims taking procedures continue to be disrupted in the Virgin Islands and in Puerto Rico, claims have still not returned to normal.2017-12-28T13:33:44
