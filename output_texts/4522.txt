Title: US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly Rise: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly Rise: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories increased by 0.515 million barrels in the week ending January 14th, the first increase since November and compared to market forecasts of a 0.938 million drop, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories rose by 5.873 million barrels, more than an expected 2.634 million increase.2022-01-20T16:06:00
