Title: US 10Y Treasury Note Yield Trims Early Gains
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Treasury Note Yield Trims Early GainsUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury note edged higher to 1.50% on Friday, losing some initial momentum as investors digested US inflation data. Consumer prices climbed 6.8% from a year earlier in November, the highest inflation rate since 1982 amid soaring energy prices, strengthening the case for the Fed to double the pace of tapering next week. A faster unwinding of monetary stimuli could bring forward interest rate hikes by the middle of 2022. Traders also weigh the risks to economic growth from omicron restrictions.
