Title: Fifth District Manufacturing Activity Contracts Further
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

Fifth District Manufacturing Activity Contracts FurtherUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Manufacturing Activity Index in the Richmond area sank to -10 in April of 2023, deteriorating from the -5 in the previous month as higher interest rates continue to pressure business activity for manufacturers. Among the survey’s main components, shipments swung to negative territory ( -7 vs 2 in March), while conditions deteriorated further for the volume of new orders (-20 vs -11) and the backlog of orders (-31 vs -14). At the same time, firms remained pessimistic about current sentiment (-19 vs -12) and future expectations (-5 vs -1) of local business conditions. On the price front, both prices paid and prices received edged higher, erasing the improved inflation rate seen in the previous month.2023-04-25T14:26:04.257
