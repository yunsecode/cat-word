Title: US Posts Smallest Trade Deficit in 8 Months
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Posts Smallest Trade Deficit in 8 MonthsUnited States Balance of TradeThe US trade deficit narrowed to USD 49.4 billion in February 2019, the smallest since June 2018, from USD 51.1 billion in the previous month and compared to market expectations of USD 53.5 billion. Exports surged 1.1 percent while imports rose at a softer 0.2 percent amid ongoing trade negotiations with China.2019-04-17T12:33:00
