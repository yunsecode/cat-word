Title: US 10-Year Yield Falls Below 1% for the 1st time
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Yield Falls Below 1% for the 1st timeUnited States Government Bond 10YYields on the US-10 years bonds fell more than 8bps to a fresh record low of 0.986% on Tuesday, after rising as high as 1.13 following the Federal Reserve’s emergency rate cut to offset the impact from the coronavirus in the economy. The yield on the 30-year bond also hit an all-time low of 1.601%, and that on the two-year note, which typically moves in step with interest rate expectations, dropped to its lowest since July 2016.2020-03-03T18:50:00
