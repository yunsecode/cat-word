Title: US Stocks Fall Sharply
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall SharplyUnited States Stock MarketWall Street slumped at the open on Friday amid mounting concerns over a global slowdown after China data for industrial production and retail sales and flash PMIs for France, Germany and the Euro Area came well below expectations. The Dow Jones fell 1.1%, the S&P 500 went down 1.1% and the Nasdaq traded 1.4% lower around 5 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-12-14T14:41:53.527
