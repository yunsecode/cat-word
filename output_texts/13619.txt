Title: US Stocks Fall on Lower Oil Prices
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on Lower Oil PricesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened lower on Tuesday, following earlier losses in Asia and Europe after the Bank of Japan left monetary policy unchanged while oil prices continue to slide. The Dow Jones was down 100 points or 0.58%, the S&P 500 fell 0.64% and the Nasdaq lost 0.58% around 9 minutes after the open. 2016-03-15T13:43:30.563
