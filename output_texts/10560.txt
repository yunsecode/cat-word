Title: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index Rises Further in June
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index Rises Further in JuneUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Manufacturing Activity Index in the US fifth district including the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia rose by 4 points to 20 in June 2018, beating market expectations of 15. The advance was buoyed by an increase in all main components: shipments (17 vs 15 in May); new orders (22 vs 16); and employment (22 vs 18). Firms also saw an increase in backlog of orders, as the index rose to its highest value of this year. Respondents were optimistic in June, expecting growth to continue across most indicators.2018-06-26T14:05:00
