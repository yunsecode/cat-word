Title: US Imports Hit Fresh Record High
Tag: Imports

US Imports Hit Fresh Record HighUnited States ImportsImports to the United States increased 0.6 percent from the previous month to a new all-time high USD 288.5 billion in September of 2021, led by higher purchases of capital goods (up USD 2.5 billion), in particular computers and electric apparatus. Imports also rose for organic chemicals (up USD 0.9 billion) and passenger cars (up USD 1.7 billion). On the other hand, purchases of consumer goods decreased by USD 0.1 billion and imports of services went down by USD 0.2 billion.2021-11-04T12:42:56.09
