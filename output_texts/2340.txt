Title: US Construction Spending Falls for 2nd Month
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Falls for 2nd MonthUnited States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the US fell by 0.4 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of USD 1.78 billion in July of 2022, in line with market expectations and easing from the downwardly revised 0.5 percent drop in June. Spending on private construction fell by 0.8 percent, dragged by residential (-1.5 percent) and healthcare (-1.3 percent) construction. On the other hand, public construction expanded by 1.5 percent, supported by residential (0.8 percent) and power (4.3 percent) construction. 2022-09-01T14:05:00
