Title: US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised Slightly Up
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised Slightly UpUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe final Markit US manufacturing PMI came in at 51.3 in February of 2016, higher than a preliminary reading of 51 but the second-lowest reading since October of 2012. Growth in production volumes slowed to a 28-month low, job growth moderated to a five-month low and producer prices recorded the biggest decline since June of 2012.2016-03-01T14:56:26
