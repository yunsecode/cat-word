Title: Job Quits in US Hit Record 4.4 Million
Tag: Job Quits

Job Quits in US Hit Record 4.4 MillionUnited States Job QuitsThe number of Americans who quit their jobs voluntarily rose by 4.4 million in September, a record since the data become available in 2000 as workers looked for higher wages and better working conditions. There was a record number of people quitting jobs in the leisure and hospitality, manufacturing and health care sectors. Also, quits went up in arts, entertainment and recreation, other services, and state and local government education. 2021-11-12T19:21:00
