Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall 0.2% in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall 0.2% in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States decreased 0.2 percent in the week ended April 20th 2018, following a 4.9 percent rise in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications declined 0.3 percent and applications to purchase a home were unchanged. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate rose to 4.73 percent from 4.66 percent, reaching the highest since September 2013.2018-04-25T11:09:00
