Title: US Stocks Rise On Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise On WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Wednesday, attempting to recover from losses in the previous session, despite a weaker dollar and a delay in the health care bill vote. The Dow Jones went up 100 points or 0.5%, the S&P 500 added 10 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq gained 12 points or 0.2% around 11 minutes after the opening bell.2017-06-28T13:51:54
