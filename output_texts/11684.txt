Title: US Stocks Rally on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Monday September 11th of 2017, after Hurricane Irma wrecked less damage than expected and North Korea did not exacerbate tensions ahead of the UN meeting. Technology shares outperformed the market on Monday, with Tesla climbing 5.9% and Netflix 3.0%. The Dow Jones gained 260 points or 1.2% to 22057. The S&P 500 booked a new record, rising 27 points or 1.1% to 2488. The Nasdaq jumped 72 points or 1.1% to 6432.  2017-09-11T20:03:24
