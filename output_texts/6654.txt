Title: Dollar Set for Small Weekly Gain
Tag: Currency

Dollar Set for Small Weekly Gain United States CurrencyThe dollar index gained some ground on Friday, consolidating above the 90.60 region and on track for a small weekly gain as higher US Treasury yields drove investors to the greenback. Still, signs of a struggling job market along with a dovish stance from the Federal Reserve capped much of the upside momentum. On top of that, prospects of an economic recovery fueled by large stimulus packages globally and a gradual rollout of vaccines spooked traders away from the safe-haven assets. Next week, aside from stimulus headlines, the focus will be on how the US recovery is doing. 2021-02-19T02:53:00
