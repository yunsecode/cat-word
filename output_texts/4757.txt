Title: US 10-Year Bond Yield Flat Ahead of Jobs Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Bond Yield Flat Ahead of Jobs DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on 10-year Treasury notes traded around 1.43% on Friday, not far from an over two-month low of 1.4% touched on December 1st, as investors remain cautious ahead of the release of the highly-anticipated US jobs report due later today, which will be scrutinized for clues on central bank policy path. A larger-than-expected increase in employment levels could boost expectations of an early rate hike by the Federal Reserve, days after Chair Jerome Powell indicated that he expected to discuss speeding up the taper of the bond-buying program at the central bank's next meeting. Investors also remained concerned about the emergence of a new coronavirus variant.2021-12-03T12:11:00
