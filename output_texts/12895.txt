Title: US Housing Starts Fall for 2nd Month
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Fall for 2nd MonthUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States tumbled 9 percent to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1047 thousand in September from August of 2016, below market expectations of 1175 thousand. It is the lowest figure since March of 2015, due to a fall in construction of multifamily homes. In contrast, building permits rose 6.3 percent to 1225 thousand, beating expectations of 1165 thousand.2016-10-19T12:33:58
