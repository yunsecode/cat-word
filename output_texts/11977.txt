Title: Overseas Investors Buy $65.8B Of US Assets In April
Tag: Capital Flows

Overseas Investors Buy $65.8B Of US Assets In AprilUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought USD 65.8 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments in April of 2017 after buying an upwardly revised USD 9.3 billion in March. Meanwhile, foreigners bought USD 1.8 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying USD 59.7 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors sold USD 22.5 billion of US treasuries, after buying USD 24.4 billion in March.2017-06-15T20:16:05.97
