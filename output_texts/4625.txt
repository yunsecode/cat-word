Title: Dollar Recovers as Treasury Yields Jump
Tag: Currency

Dollar Recovers as Treasury Yields JumpUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was around 96.2 on Tuesday after a recovery rally the previous session, lifted by a jump in Treasury yields as traders bet on an early Federal Reserve interest rate hike despite surging Covid cases. The benchmark 10-year US yield jumped above 1.6% on Monday, as the Fed is expected to begin raising interest rates as early as March amid a robust US economic rebound and surging inflation, with the Fed-preferred PCE index accelerating at its fastest pace since 1982 at 5.7% in November. The Fed announced that it would end its pandemic-era asset purchases in March and signaled three quarter point interest rate hikes this year.2022-01-04T05:45:00
