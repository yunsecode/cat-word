Title: Dollar Rises for First Time in 5 Days
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rises for First Time in 5 DaysUnited States CurrencyThe dollar appreciated for the first time in five sessions against a basket of currencies on Friday after the US House of Representatives passed a $2.2 trillion coronavirus stimulus plan. However, the bill faces considerable opposition from Republican-held Senate. In the last four sessions, the safe-haven currency was under pressure as hopes of an economic turnaround boosted risk appetite. 2020-10-02T01:37:00
