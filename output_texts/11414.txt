Title: US Stocks Reach New Highs on Tuesday amid Tax Debate
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Reach New Highs on Tuesday amid Tax DebateUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deep in the green on Tuesday November 28th of 2017, after the long-awaited fiscal reform passed an important hurdle in the Senate. Financial stocks gained the most, with Bank of America rising 4.0% and JPMorgan Chase 3.8%. The Dow Jones jumped 256 points or 1.1% to a new record of 23837. The S&P 500 gained 26 points or 1.0% to 2627. The Nasdaq advanced 34 points or 0.5% to a new high of 6912.2017-11-28T21:07:04
