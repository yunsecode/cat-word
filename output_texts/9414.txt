US Stocks Surge on Strong Corporate EarningsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks rallied on Friday on the back of stronger-than-expected quarterly earnings results from J.P. Morgan, Wells Fargo, First Republic Bank and PNC. The Dow Jones jumped 271 points, or 1%, to 26,414; the S&P 500 rose 21 points, or 0.7%, to 2,909; and the Nasdaq gained 41 points, or 0.5%, to 7,989.2019-04-12T13:44:39.727
