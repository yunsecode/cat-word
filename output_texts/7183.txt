Title: Dollar Set to Weaken Further
Tag: Currency

Dollar Set to Weaken FurtherUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar continues to face heavy selling pressure in December, testing the 91 area for the first time since April of 2018. Robust economic growth prospects outside the US, low-interest rates and concerns about rising levels of debt have dented the dollar’s status as the preferred safe-haven. A worsening coronavirus pandemic and new lockdowns across the country raised concerns about the extent of economic pain in the world’s largest economy before a vaccine is made available. Furthermore, hopes of extra fiscal stimulus have re-emerged along with further monetary easing from the Fed, leaving room for further downside momentum. 2020-12-01T18:17:00
