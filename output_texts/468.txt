Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Rebounds
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield ReboundsUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note has risen to 3.51% as investors look ahead to the US inflation report on Wednesday. The payrolls report for April showed a larger-than-expected job gain and rising wage inflation, which has tempered fears of a recession and made it more difficult for the Federal Reserve to justify cutting interest rates. Meanwhile, investors experienced temporary relief from the regional banking turmoil, but they will be closely watching the release of the Loan Officer Survey for clues on how the recent turmoil has affected lending.2023-05-08T13:30:00
