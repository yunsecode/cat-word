US Mortgage Applications Fall: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the US fell 4 percent in the week ended December 10th, after a 2 percent advance om the previous period. The refinancing rate tumbled 6.4 percent while applications to purchase a home went up 0.7 percent, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed.
