Title: US December Consumer Sentiment Weaker than Estimated
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US December Consumer Sentiment Weaker than EstimatedUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States fell to 95.9 in December of 2017 from 98.5 in November and below the preliminary estimate of 96.8. It was the lowest reading in three months, mainly due to a decline in consumer expectations.2017-12-22T15:09:28
