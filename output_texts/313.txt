Title: US PCE Inflation Picks Up in April
Tag: PCE Price Index Monthly Change

US PCE Inflation Picks Up in AprilUnited States PCE Price Index Monthly ChangeThe personal consumption expenditure price index in the United States went up 0.4 percent month-over-month in April 2023, following a 0.1 percent increase in March. Prices for goods increased 0.3 percent, rebounding from a 0.2 percent fall in the previous month and services inflation accelerated to 0.4 percent from 0.3 percent. Food prices decreased less than 0.1 percent (vs -0.2 percent), and energy prices increased 0.7 percent, after a 3.7 percent decline. Excluding food and energy, the PCE price index rose 0.4 percent, above market expectations of 0.3 percent.
