Title: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index Beats Forecasts
Tag: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index

Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index Beats ForecastsUnited States Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index in the United States rose to 17 in January of 2019 from a downwardly revised 9.1 in December, well above market expectations of 10. Faster increases were seen in new orders (21.3 from 13.3) and average workweek (6 from 4) and price pressures slowed for both prices paid (32.7 from 38.9) and received (24.8 from 29). On the other hand, a slowdown was seen for shipments (11.4 from 12.4) and employment (9.6 from 19.1) and inventories contracted (-7.6 from 2.6). Additionally, the 6-month outlook edged up to 31.2 from 29.9.2019-01-17T13:43:00.423
