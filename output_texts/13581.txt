Title: US Personal Income Edges Up 0.2% in February
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Edges Up 0.2% in February United States Personal IncomePersonal income in the United States rose 0.2 percent month-on-month in February of 2016, following a 0.5 percent growth in the previous month and beating market expectations of 0.1 percent. The increase was mainly due to a 1.0 percent growth in rental income and a 0.3 percent rise in current transfer receipts.2016-03-28T12:45:34
