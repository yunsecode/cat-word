Title: US Manufacturing Sector Accelerates in February
Tag: Business Confidence

US Manufacturing Sector Accelerates in FebruaryUnited States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management index of national factory activity rose to 53.2 in February after slumping to 51.3 in January, which was the weakest reading since May. New orders, supplier deliveries, inventories and order backlogs bounced back while production fell.
