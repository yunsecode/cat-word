Title: Dollar Little Changed at 1-Month High as Election Looms
Tag: Currency

Dollar Little Changed at 1-Month High as Election LoomsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded nearly flat around 94 on Tuesday, remaining close to levels not seen in a month, reinforcing its safe-haven status as investors are cautious ahead of the US presidential election. Biden leads the polls but anxiety and uncertainty mount amid the possibility of contested outcome and delayed results as many states may not have the polls counted on election night due to the increase in mail voting. Also, new lockdowns in France, Germany and more recently in the UK and a dovish ECB spooked investors away from the euro and sterling. Last week, the dollar was up around 1.3%, the biggest weekly gain since late September.2020-11-02T13:17:00
