Title: US Stocks Retreat on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Retreat on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Tuesday 13 March 2018 after inflation data (2.2 percent in February) came in line with expectations while President Trump announced Mike Pompeo, director of the CIA will replace Rex Tillerson as a Secretary of State, triggering a tech selloff that snapped the Nasdaq’s 7-day winning streak. The Dow Jones lost 172 points or 0.7% to 25007. The S&P 500 retreated 18 points or 0.6% to 2765. The Nasdaq plunged 77 points or 1.0% to 7511.2018-03-13T20:05:51
