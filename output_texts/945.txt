Title: US Capacity Utilization Below Forecasts
Tag: Capacity Utilization

US Capacity Utilization Below ForecastsUnited States Capacity UtilizationCapacity utilization in the United States was unchanged at two-year lows of 78% in February of 2023, the same as in January, and below market forecasts of 78.4%. It is also 1.6 percentage points below its long-run average. Capacity utilization for manufacturing slipped 0.1 percentage point to 77.6%, the operating rate for mining fell 0.6 percentage point to 87.3%, while the operating rate for utilities increased 0.2 percentage point to 68.9%.2023-03-17T13:28:13.343
