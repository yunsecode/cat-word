Title: US Retail Sales Rise for 2nd Month
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Rise for 2nd MonthUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States increased 0.8 percent month-over-month in October from September of 2016, following an upwardly revised 1 percent rise in the previous period. Figures beat market expectations of a 0.6 percent gain, boosted by sales of motor vehicles and a range of other goods.2016-11-15T13:35:13.937
