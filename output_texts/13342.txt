Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 4th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 4th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil stocks in the United States declined by 0.933 million barrels in the week ended June 10th, 2016, following a 3.226 million fall in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 2.27 million drop. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks went down by 2.625 million barrels after rising by 1.010 million barrels in the previous week.2016-06-15T15:39:54
