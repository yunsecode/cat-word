Title: Dollar Rally Loses Momentum
Tag: Currency

Dollar Rally Loses MomentumUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar index lost further ground on Monday, bottoming around 93 after gaining 1.1% in the previous week hit by flash Markit PMI data that showed US private sector growth slowed to an 8-month low. Still, the greenback is likely to remain strong during the week amid expectations the Fed could start to ease back on asset purchases this year. Investors' attention turns to the Jackson Hole Symposium later in the week when Fed Chair Powell is expected to announce some tips on the taper timeline.2021-08-23T20:30:00
