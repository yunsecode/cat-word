Title: US Manufacturing PMI Revised Down in February: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing PMI Revised Down in February: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI fell to 55.3 in February of 2018 from 55.5 in January and lower than 55.9 in the preliminary estimate. Although the reading pointed to a slower expansion in factory activity and below January’s 34-month high, the overall improvement in operating conditions across the manufacturing sector was one of the strongest recorded since late-2014.2018-03-01T14:52:04.647
