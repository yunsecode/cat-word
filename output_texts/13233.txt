Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall 1.3%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall 1.3%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 1.3 percent in the week ended July 15th of 2016 from the prior period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the first drop in three weeks. Refinance applications went down 0.9 percent and applications to purchase a home shrank 2 percent. Fixed 30-year mortgage rates rose 5bps to 3.65 percent.2016-07-20T12:08:46.963
