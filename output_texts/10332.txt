Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise for 1st Time in 6 Weeks
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise for 1st Time in 6 WeeksUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States went up 4.2 percent in the week ended August 17th 2018, after falling 2 percent in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the first increase in six weeks. Applications to purchase a home rose 2.9 percent and refinance applications jumped 6 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was steady at 4.81 percent.2018-08-22T11:05:56.847
