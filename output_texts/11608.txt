Title: US Stocks Edge Up to Fresh Records
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Up to Fresh RecordsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed modestly in the green on Tuesday October 3rd of 2017, as car sales surprised positively in September and nudged auto and industrial shares. US total vehicle sales surged to 18.57 million in September, up from 16.14 million in August and 17.72 in September of 2016. All three major indices booked new highs. The Dow Jones closed up 84 points or 0.4% to 22642. The S&P 500 edged up 6 points or 0.2% to 2535. The Nasdaq gained 15 points or 0.2% to 6532. 2017-10-03T20:17:01
