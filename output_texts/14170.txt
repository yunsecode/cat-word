Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 5-Month High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 5-Month HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's final reading on the overall index of consumer sentiment came in at 96.1 in June, higher than a preliminary reading of 94.6 and a final 90.7 in May.
