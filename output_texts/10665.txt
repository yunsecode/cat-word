Title: US GDP Growth Revised Lower to 2.2% in Q1
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Revised Lower to 2.2% in Q1United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy expanded an annualized 2.2 percent on quarter in the first quarter of 2018, below 2.3 percent in the advance estimate and market expectations of 2.3 percent. Downward revisions to private inventory investment, residential fixed investment, and exports were partly offset by an upward revision to nonresidential fixed investment, the second estimate showed.2018-05-30T12:33:55.677
