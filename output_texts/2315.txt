Title: Dollar Approaches 110
Tag: Currency

Dollar Approaches 110United States CurrencyThe dollar approached 110 on Thursday, as Fed Chair reiterated during his speech at the Cato Institute that the central bank is strongly committed to bring inflation down, and that longer inflation remains above target which poses a great risk. Markets are currently betting on another 75bps rate hike when the Fed meets later in the month. At the same time, the US economy is seen in a better shape to handle a recession than other major economies. The dollar strengthened mostly against Euro as investors get more increasingly concerned about the outlook in the Euro Area, even after the ECB delivered a historic 75bps rate hike in its September 2022 meeting.2022-09-08T13:47:00
