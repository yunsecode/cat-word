Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Up In February
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Up In FebruaryUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe final reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States came in at 96.3 in February 2017 compared to a preliminary figure of 95.7 and a final 98.5 in January. It was the weakest reading in three months, due to a drop in future expectations while the current economic conditions continued to improve.2017-02-24T15:09:52
