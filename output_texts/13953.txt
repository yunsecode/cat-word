Title: US Consumer Sentiment Up to 4-Month High

US Consumer Sentiment Up to 4-Month HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment increased to 93.1 in November from 90 in October of 2015, beating market expectations, preliminary figures showed.
