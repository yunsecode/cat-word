Title: Wall Street Retreats for 3rd Session
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Retreats for 3rd SessionUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks sank on Wednesday, extending losses for a 3rd consecutive session, as a drop in cryptocurrencies including bitcoin prompted a fall in cryptocurrency-linked stocks. Concerns over rising inflation and coronavirus infections in some Asian countries also weighed on investors' mood. Meanwhile, traders await the FOMC minutes release later in the day for an update on Fed's view over rising inflationary pressures. Policymakers are also expected to confirm that a rate hike is still in the distance. The Dow Jones fell more than 500 points, and both the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq lost around 1%.2021-05-19T13:33:00
