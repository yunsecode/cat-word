Title: US Business Inventories Flat in July
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Flat in JulyUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the United States were unchanged in July from June of 2016, following a 0.2 percent rise in the previous period and lower than market expectations of 0.1 percent gain. Retail inventories excluding autos, which go into the calculation of GDP fell 0.3 percent. Year-on-year, inventories were up 0.5 percent.2016-09-15T14:08:22
