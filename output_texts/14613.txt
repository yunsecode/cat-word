US Inflation Rate Slows to 4-Year Low in OctoberUnited States Inflation RateIn October of 2013, the US inflation rate eased for the third straight month to 1 percent, the lowest rate since October of 2009. From September, prices dropped 0.1 percent, mainly due to a fall in gasoline prices.
