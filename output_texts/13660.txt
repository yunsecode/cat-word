Title: US ISM Manufacturing PMI at 5-Month High
Tag: Business Confidence

US ISM Manufacturing PMI at 5-Month HighUnited States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management’s Manufacturing PMI came in at 49.5 in February of 2016, higher than 48.2 in January and above market expectations of 48.5. Manufacturing activity shrank for the fifth consecutive month although at a slower pace as production, employment and prices all increased. 2016-03-01T15:09:49
