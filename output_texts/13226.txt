Title: US House Prices Grow The Least in 11-Months: FHFA
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US House Prices Grow The Least in 11-Months: FHFAUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States edged up 0.2 percent on the month in May of 2016, lower than an upwardly revised 0.3 percent increase in April. Figures came in below market expectations of a 0.4 percent gain. Year-on-year, the FHFA house price index went up at a slower 5.6 percent compared with a 5.9 percent growth in the previous month.2016-07-21T14:21:40.777
