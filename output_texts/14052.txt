Title: US Corporate Layoffs Rise in September
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Corporate Layoffs Rise in SeptemberUnited States Challenger Job CutsThe number of announced corporate layoffs in the United States increased by 43 percent to 58877 in September from 41186 in August, led by job cuts in Hewlett-Packard. The reductions reported last month pushed the layoff count to 205,759 in the third quarter, making it the worst quarter in six years.
