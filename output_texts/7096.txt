Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise as Rates Fall to New Low
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise as Rates Fall to New LowUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 1.1 percent in the week ended December 11th, rebounding from two straight weeks of declines, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home increased 1.8 percent, up 26 percent year-on-year; while those for refinance a home loan advanced 1.4 percent, up 105 percent year-on-year. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was down 5 bps to 2.85 percent last week, a record low.2020-12-16T12:06:00
