Title: US GDP Growth at 11-Year High
Tag: Gdp Growth Rate

US GDP Growth at 11-Year HighUnited States Gdp Growth RateThe United States economy advanced an annualized 5.0 percent in the third quarter of 2014, according to final figures from the Bureau of Economic Analysis, beating previous estimates and market expectations. It is the highest pace since the third quarter of 2003, reflecting an upturn in consumer spending and investment and a downturn in imports. 2014-12-23T13:49:02.023
