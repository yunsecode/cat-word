Title: US Industrial Production Falls for 1st Time Since January
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Production Falls for 1st Time Since JanuaryUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial output in the US shrank 0.9 percent month-over-month in August of 2017, following an upwardly revised 0.4 percent gain in July and missing market expectations of a 0.1 percent increase. It is the first drop in industrial output in seven months as Hurricane Harvey, which hit the Gulf Coast of Texas in late August, is estimated to have reduced the rate of change in total output by roughly 3/4 percentage point.2017-09-15T13:24:42
