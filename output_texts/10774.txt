Title: US Manufacturing Growth Eases to 9-Month Low: ISM
Tag: Business Confidence

US Manufacturing Growth Eases to 9-Month Low: ISMUnited States Business ConfidenceThe Institute for Supply Management’s Manufacturing PMI in the US fell to 57.3 in April 2018 from the previous month's 59.3, and missing market expectations of 58.3. The reading pointed to the weakest pace of expansion in the manufacturing sector since last July, as new orders, production and employment rose at softer rates.2018-05-01T14:02:37
