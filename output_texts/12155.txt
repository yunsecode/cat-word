Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down In April
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Down In AprilUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States came in at 97 in April 2017 compared with a preliminary reading of 98 but slightly higher than 96.9 in March, a final estimate showed. The gauge of future expectations increased slightly while the barometer for current economic conditions fell.2017-04-28T14:02:06
