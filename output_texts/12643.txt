Title: US Stocks Close Higher As Financial Equities Rise
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher As Financial Equities RiseUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks closed in the green on Thursday, led by the financial sector, after the Federal Reserve raised rates and signaled a faster path of interest-rate increases in 2017. The Dow Jones gained 60 points, or 0.3%, to close at 19,852; the S&P 500 finished up 9 points, or 0.4%, at 2,262; and the Nasdaq added 20 points, or 0.4%, to finish at 5,457.2016-12-15T21:42:26
