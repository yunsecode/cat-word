US Homebuilder Sentiment Slips In February: NAHBUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States fell to 65 in February 2017 from 67 in the previous month and below market consensus of 67. Current single-family home sales decreased 1 point to 71; sales expectations over the next six months went down 3 points to 73; and buyer traffic declined 5 points to 46. 2017-02-15T15:03:55
