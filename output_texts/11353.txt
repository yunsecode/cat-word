Title: US Initial Claims Unexpectedly Drop for Fourth Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Initial Claims Unexpectedly Drop for Fourth WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 11 thousand to 225 thousand in the week ended December 9th, from the previous week's unrevised level of 236 thousand and below market expectations of 239 thousand. It is the fourth straight week of declines in initial claims, bringing it to the lowest since the week ended October 14th. Claims taking procedures continue to be disrupted in the Virgin Islands and those in Puerto Rico still have not returned to normal.2017-12-14T13:35:45
