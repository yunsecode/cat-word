Title: US Jobless Claims Rise To 241K In Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise To 241K In Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 3 thousand to 241 thousand in the week ended June 17th from the previous week's revised level of 238 thousand and above market expectations of 240 thousand. Claims have now been below 300,000 for 120 straight weeks, the longest such stretch since 1970. Meanwhile, the 4-week moving average, which removes week-to-week volatility, rose 1.5 thousand to 244.75 thousand last week, the highest since early April.2017-06-22T12:30:31
