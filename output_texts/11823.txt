Title: US Manufacturing PMI Revised Higher in July: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing PMI Revised Higher in July: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI rose to 53.3 in July of 2017 from a preliminary of 53.2 and 52 in June, final figures showed. It is the highest reading in four months as growth in output and new orders rebounded and inflationary pressures were muted. 2017-08-01T13:49:09.253
