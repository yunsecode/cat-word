Title: US Stocks Close Lower on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Lower on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Friday 10 August 2018, as the Turkish lira dropped as much as 20 percent against the USD after President Trump tweeted that the US will double steel and aluminum tariffs on Turkey. In addition, investors are worried about the country’s overheating economy and the government’s lack of willingness to address the problem. The Dow Jones lost 196 points or 0.8% to 25313. The S&P 500 retreated 20 points or 0.7% to 2833. The Nasdaq declined 53 points or 0.7% to 7839. 2018-08-10T22:07:31.327
