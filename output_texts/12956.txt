Title: US Stocks Open Slightly Higher on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Slightly Higher on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened slightly higher on Tuesday following gains in Europe and Asia, as concerns about the financial health of Deutsche Bank helped erase strong gains in premarket trading while investors await a slew of economic data due later this week. The Dow Jones Industrial Average increased 42 points, or 0.2%; the S&P 500 index was up 4 points, or 0.2%; and the Nasdaq Composite Index gained 19 points, or 0.4%, around 10 minutes after the open. 2016-10-04T13:43:23.3
