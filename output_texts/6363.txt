Title: Dollar Touches Three-Week Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar Touches Three-Week LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index bottomed around the 92.00 level on Tuesday, in a choppy session that saw the greenback dive from an intraday high of 92.30 to a three-week low of 91.90 after the US inflation figures were not enough to re-ignite another rally in Treasury yields. The US consumer price inflation jumped to 2.6% in March, the highest since August 2018 but only marginally above the market consensus of 2.5% and mainly because of the base effect. Aside from the inflation outlook, the Federal Reserve’s insistence that it will keep financial conditions as lenient as possible until the US economy heals from the effects of the coronavirus pandemic seems to be making it difficult for the US currency to attract new investors this month.  2021-04-13T15:14:15.037
