Title: US Stocks Fall Sharply on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall Sharply on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street finished sharply lower on Friday, following earlier losses in Europe and Asia after the UK voted to leave the European Union. The Dow Jones lost 610 points or 3.39%, erasing year-to-date gains; the S&P 500 fell 76 points or 3.6% and the Nasdaq ended 202 points or 4.12% lower.2016-06-24T21:24:20
