Title: 10-Year Treasury Note Yield Rises to 8-Month High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

10-Year Treasury Note Yield Rises to 8-Month HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note held above 4.05% on Friday, the highest since November 2022 as markets continued to assess the latest data for hints on the extent of remaining tightening by the Federal Reserve. Non-farm payrolls rose slightly less than markets expected in June, but the increase was robust enough to hold recent evidence that the US labor market remains tight, a favorable backdrop for the Federal Reserve to continue raising interest rates to curb stubborn inflation. The central bank is expected to raise its funds rate by 25bps in its upcoming meeting this month before holding the terminal rate of 5.5% until May of 2024. 10 days ago
