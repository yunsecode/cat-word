Title: US Markit Manufacturing PMI Rises Slightly
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Markit Manufacturing PMI Rises Slightly United States Manufacturing PMIAt 53.1 in September, the final headline seasonally adjusted Markit U.S. Manufacturing Purchasing Managers’ Index™ (PMI™) was up only fractionally from August’s 22-month low of 53.0. Although still above the neutral 50.0 threshold, the latest reading was one of the lowest recorded over the past two years, thereby indicating a relatively subdued improvement in overall business conditions in September.2015-10-01T14:54:02
