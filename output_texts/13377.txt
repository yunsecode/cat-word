Title: US Exports at 4-Month High in April
Tag: Exports

US Exports at 4-Month High in April United States ExportsTotal exports from the United States rose 1.5 percent $182.7 billion in April of 2016 from the previous month. It is the highest figure since December 2015, as sales of goods grew $2.8 billion to $120.1 billion, mainly driven by industrial supplies and materials (+$1.8 billion); fuel oil (+$0.3 billion); other petroleum products (+$0.2 billion); organic chemicals (+$0.2 billion); automotive vehicles (+$0.8 billion); other parts and accessories (+$0.4 billion). In contrast, exports of services decreased $0.3 billion to $62.7 billion, dragged down by lower travel sales (-$0.2 billion). 2016-06-03T14:16:22
