Title: US Stocks Pare Losses to Close Mixed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Pare Losses to Close MixedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street pared most of the sharp losses earlier in the session to close mixed on Friday 8 February 2019, amid renewed worries about the US and China trade dispute. A new report suggested there is still no draft on a trade deal and other news showed next week President Donald Trump could sign an order to ban Chinese telecommunication equipment from US wireless network. The Dow Jones retreated 63 points or 0.3% to 25106. In contrast, the S&P 500 edged up 2 points or 0.1% to 2708 and the Nasdaq gained 10 points or 0.1% to 7298.2019-02-08T21:07:00
