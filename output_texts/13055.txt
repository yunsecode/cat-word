Title: US Factory Orders Rise The Most in 9 Months
Tag: Factory Orders

US Factory Orders Rise The Most in 9 MonthsUnited States Factory OrdersNew orders for manufactured goods in the United States went up 1.9 percent month-over-month in July of 2016, following an upwardly revised 1.8 percent decline in June and compared to market expectations of a 2 percent gain. It is the first increase in three months and the highest since October. Orders for transport equipment recorded the biggest rise (10.6 percent), followed by computers and related (10.4 percent), durables (4.4 percent) and electrical equipment appliances (3.7 percent). In contrast, orders for motor vehicles and parts edged down 0.1 percent. Excluding transportation, factory orders rose 0.2 percent and excluding defense orders went up 1.5 percent. 2016-09-02T15:13:41
