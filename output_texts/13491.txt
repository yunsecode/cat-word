Title: US GDP Growth Slows to 0.5% in Q1
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Slows to 0.5% in Q1United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy expanded an annualized 0.5 percent on quarter in the first three months of 2016, lower than a 1.4 percent expansion in the previous period,  and below market expectations of 0.7 percent growth, according to the advanced estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis. It is the weakest performance since the first quarter of 2014 when the economy contracted 0.9 percent as consumer spending slowed, the drag from trade and business inventories worsened and business investment fell for the third straight quarter.2016-04-28T13:32:35
