Title: US Stocks Close At Fresh Records
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close At Fresh RecordsUnited States Stock MarketUS equities closed higher on Tuesday as investors awaited the Federal Reserve's December interest-rate announcement on Wednesday. The Fed is widely expected to raise interest rates for the first time this year. The Dow Jones gained 115 points, or 0.6%, to 19,911; the S&P 500 advanced 15 points, or 0.7%, to 2,272; and the Nasdaq rose 51 points, or 1%, to 5,464.2016-12-13T21:34:29.363
