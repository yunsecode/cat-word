Title: US Homebuilder Sentiment Rebounds in January
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Homebuilder Sentiment Rebounds in JanuaryUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index for the US edged up to 58 in January of 2019 from 56 in December which was the lowest reading since May of 2015 and beating market forecasts of 56. The subindex for current single-family home sales rose to 63 from 61; the gauge for home sales over the next six months went up to 64 from 61 and prospective buyers edged up to 44 from 43.2019-01-16T15:05:51.68
