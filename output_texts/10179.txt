Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Monday 1 October 2018, as Canada agreed to join a trade deal with the US and Mexico, replacing NAFTA with a new agreement (USMCA) that ends months of uncertainty and gives the US access to Canada’s dairy industry and established some regional content clauses to avoid tariffs in the auto industry. The Dow Jones closed up 193 points or 0.7% to 26651. The S&P 500 11 points or 0.4% to 2925. In contrast, the Nasdaq retreated 9 points or 0.1% to 8037.2018-10-01T20:16:01.607
