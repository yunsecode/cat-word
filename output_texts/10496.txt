Title: US Stocks Fall on New Tariffs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on New TariffsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Wednesday, tracking losses in Europe and in Asia after President Trump said he will impose new tariffs on $200 billion of Chinese imports. The Dow Jones fell 0.6%, the S&P 500 declined 0.5% and the Nasdaq dropped 0.5% around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2018-07-11T13:47:30.46
