Title: Foreign Investors Buy $114.5 Billion of US Assets
Tag: Capital Flows

Foreign Investors Buy $114.5 Billion of US AssetsUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought USD 114.5 billion of US assets in June of 2018, more than the USD 69.2 billion purchased in May while markets were expecting them to sell USD 80 billion. Meanwhile, foreigners sold USD 36.5 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after buying USD 45.6 billion in the previous month. Also, overseas investors sold USD 48.6 billion in Treasuries in June, compared with the USD 26.7 billion in purchases in May.2018-08-15T20:04:00
