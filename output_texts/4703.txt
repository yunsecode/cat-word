Title: US Crude Stocks Fall for 3rd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Fall for 3rd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 4.584 million barrels in the week ending December 10th, a third consecutive period of decline and compared with market forecasts of a 2.082 million drop, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories were down by 0.719 million barrels, compared with expectations of a 1.606 million increase.2021-12-15T15:33:10.553
