Title: US Futures Extend Losses on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Extend Losses on MondayUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures extended losses on Monday as investors continued to grapple with rising Covid cases and tighter central bank policies. Dow Jones, S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 futures each lost at least 1%. The major averages came off a losing week, with the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite dropping 2.95% as high valuation growth stocks sold off on the prospect of higher interest rates. Meanwhile, the omicron virus continued to spread as the winter holiday season approached, prompting fresh lockdowns in Europe. The WHO also said on Saturday that the number of cases with the new strain doubles in 1.5 to 3 days in areas with community transmission. Elsewhere, democratic senator Joe Manchin said on Sunday he won’t support his party’s $2 trillion social and environment bill, dealing a blow to the Biden administration heading into an election year. 2021-12-20T01:07:00
