Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise The Most In 8 Weeks
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise The Most In 8 WeeksUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 4.097 million barrels in the week ended January 6th, 2017, following a 7.051 million fall in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 1.162 million rise. It is the biggest increase in eight weeks. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks rose by 5.023 million, compared to expectations of a 1.638 million increase.2017-01-11T15:36:06.627
