Title: US Crude Stocks Rise for 3rd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Rise for 3rd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 1.001 million barrels in the week ending November 5th, following a 3.291 million advance in the previous period and compared with market forecasts of a 2.125 million increase, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. It was the third straight period of a higher than expected increase. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories were down by 1.555 million barrels, more than an expected 1.193 million drop.2021-11-10T15:35:00
