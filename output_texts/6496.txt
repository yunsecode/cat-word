US Crude Stocks Rise Less than ForecastUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 2.396 million barrels in the week ended March 12th, 2021, after increasing by 13.798 million in the previous week and compared with market forecasts of a 2.964 million advance, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report.
