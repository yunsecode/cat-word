Title: US Jobless Rate Remains at Lowest Since April 2008
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Remains at Lowest Since April 2008United States Unemployment RateUnemployment rate in the US was recorded at 5 percent in November 2015, the same as in the previous month and the lowest in more than seven years, while total nonfarm payroll employment increased by a higher-than-expected 211,000. Job gains occurred in construction, professional and technical services, and health care. Mining and information lost jobs.2015-12-04T13:35:26
