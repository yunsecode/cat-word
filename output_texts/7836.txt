Title: US 10-Year Bond Yield Rises on Strong Jobs Report
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Bond Yield Rises on Strong Jobs ReportUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year US Treasury note rose to 0.549% on Friday, after recent data showed the US economy added more than 1.7 million jobs in July, above expectations and pushing the jobless rate to 10.2 percent. At the same time, the yield on the 30-year Treasury bond climbed to 1.214%.2020-08-07T12:46:22.17
