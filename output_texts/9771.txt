Title: US Crude Inventories Fall Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude stocks fell by 1.680 million barrels in the week ended January 4th, 2019, following a 0.007 million increase in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 2.400 million decline. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories rose by 8.066 million barrels, the most in two years and much more than an expected 3.387 million increase.2019-01-09T15:34:00
