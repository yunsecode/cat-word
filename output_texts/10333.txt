Title: US Stocks Edge Higher on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Higher on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed modestly in the green on Tuesday 21 August 2018, as the dollar index against a basket of major currencies declined after President Trump said overnight he is not thrilled about the Fed’s decisions this year to raise interest rates and that he would criticize the Fed if it continues to raise rates. The Dow Jones gained 64 points or 0.3% to 25822. The S&P 500 advanced 6 points or 0.2% to 2863. The Nasdaq added 38 points or 0.5% 7859.2018-08-21T20:22:10.573
