Title: US Private Sector Output Expands Most Since 2014: Markit
Tag: Composite PMI

US Private Sector Output Expands Most Since 2014: MarkitUnited States Composite PMIThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI was revised higher to 59.7 in March 2021, from a preliminary estimate of 59.1 and above February's 59.5, signaling the fastest upturn in private sector business activity since August 2014. Service sector activity growth accelerated to an over six-and-a-half-year high, while the manufacturing production expansion slowed. Overall new business advanced the most since September 2014 and employment continued to rise at a solid pace. Price pressures remained elevated across the private sector, while business expectations regarding the outlook for output over the coming year were robust.2021-04-05T14:02:00
