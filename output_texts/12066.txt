Title: US Private Sector Growth Rebounds In May: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Growth Rebounds In May: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe seasonally adjusted IHS Markit Flash US Composite PMI Output Index rose to 53.9 in May 2017 from 53.2 in April, as growth accelerated in services (PMI at 54 from 53.1 in April) but slowed to an eight-month low in manufacturing (PMI at 52.5 from 52.8 in April). New business growth accelerated to its strongest since the start of 2017 and the rate of job creation edged up to a three-month peak. On the price front, average cost burdens continued to increase at a robust pace.2017-05-23T13:57:02
