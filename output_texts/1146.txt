Title: US Industrial Output Unexpectedly Unchanged
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Output Unexpectedly UnchangedUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial production in the United States was unchanged in January 2023 after falling by a revised 1.0 percent in December, missing market expectations of a 0.5 percent increase. The output of utilities fell 9.9 percent, as a swing from unseasonably cool weather in December to unseasonably warm weather in January depressed the demand for heating, while manufacturing output moved up 1.0 percent and mining output rose 2.0 percent. On a yearly basis, industrial output rose 0.8 percent in January, the least since March 2021.2023-02-15T14:18:00
