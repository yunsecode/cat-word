Title: US Personal Spending Flat, Income Up 0.3%
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Flat, Income Up 0.3%United States Personal SpendingConsumer spending in the United States remained unchanged in December of 2015, after rising an upwardly revised 0.5 percent in the previous month. The reading came below market expectations, mainly due to lower expenditure on autos and utilities. Income went up 0.3 percent, the same as in November and above forecasts. As a result, savings hit a three-year high.
