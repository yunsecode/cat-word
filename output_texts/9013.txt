Title: US Export Prices Fall 0.1% MoM
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Fall 0.1% MoMUnited States Export Prices MoMExport prices in the US edged down 0.1 percent month-over-month in October of 2019, following a 0.2 percent decline in the previous month and matching market expectations. Prices decreased for nonagricultural exports (-0.3 percent vs -0.1 percent in September) driven by industrial supplies and materials, capital goods, and consumer goods. On the other hand, prices for agricultural exports went up 1.9 percent, following a 1.7 percent drop in the previous month, mainly boosted by a 4.9 percent increase in soybean cost. Year-on-year, export prices slumped 2.2 percent, the largest 12-month decrease in the index since a 2.4 percent drop in August of 2016.2019-11-15T13:38:00
