Title: US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly Fall: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Unexpectedly Fall: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 3.831 million barrels in the week ending June 16, 2023, compared with market expectations of a 0.329 million build, data from the EIA Petroleum Status Report showed. Also, crude stocks at the Cushing, Oklahoma delivery hub went down by 98 thousand barrels, following a 1.554 million increase in the previous period. Meanwhile, distillate stockpiles, which include diesel and heating oil, rose by 0.433 million barrels, less than the consensus for a 0.705 million rise and gasoline inventories went up by 0.479 million, more than forecasts of a 0.113 million injection.25 days ago
