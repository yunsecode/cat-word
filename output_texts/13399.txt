Title: Fed Rate Hike Appropriate in Coming Months
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Rate Hike Appropriate in Coming MonthsUnited States Interest RateIt is appropriate for the Fed to gradually and cautiously increase interest rates and a rate hike probably would be appropriate in coming months if the economy continues to improve, Fed Chair Janet Yellen said in an event at Harvard University. Fed Chair comments came in line with hawkish FOMC minutes and earlier remarks from other Fed officials, raising expectations the central bank will raise rates as soon as in June. 2016-05-27T19:06:15.427
