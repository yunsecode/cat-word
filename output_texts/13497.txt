Title: US Stocks Open Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open HigherUnited States Stock MarketUS equities opened in the green on Tuesday as investors await earnings reports from Apple, AT&T, Ebay and Twitter and ahead of the Federal Reserve meeting where no action on interest rates is expected. The Dow Jones added 0.18% or 33 points, the S&P 500 gained 0.21% or 4 points and the Nasdaq went up 0.07% or 3 points around 12 minutes after the opening bell.2016-04-26T15:17:56.803
