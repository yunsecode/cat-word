Title: US Stocks Fall on Friday, Gain on the Week
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on Friday, Gain on the WeekUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed lower on Friday, ending a three-day winning streak amid a sharp drop in oil prices and lower-than-expected preliminary Markit manufacturing PMI data. The Dow Jones declined 131 points or 0.7%, the S&P 500 lost 12 points or 0.6% and the Nasdaq finished 34 points or 0.6% lower. Considering the full week, the Dow Jones gained 0.8%, the S&P 500 added 1.2% and the Nasdaq rose 1.2%.2016-09-23T20:21:47.36
