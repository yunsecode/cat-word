Title: Dollar Hovers at 3-Month High
Tag: Currency

Dollar Hovers at 3-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index against a basket of six major currencies stood at 92.06 on Monday at around 04:15 AM GMT, hovering around a three-month high, amid hopes for faster economic recovery from the COVID-19 pandemic after the US Senate has approved a sweeping pandemic relief package on Saturday and now heads back to the House for final passage, which could come as early as this week. On the data front, the February US payroll report came in stronger than expected while Federal Reserve Chair Powell reiterated the economic reopening could boost inflation temporarily but still made a very dovish pledge to maintaining its current ultra-accommodative policy until the economy reaches full employment and inflation is at 2%2021-03-08T04:32:00
