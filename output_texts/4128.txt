Title: Dollar Index Stabilizes Amid Ukraine Crisis
Tag: Currency

Dollar Index Stabilizes Amid Ukraine CrisisUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index stabilized near the 97 mark on Tuesday as markets took stock of geopolitical developments and reassessed risks arising from the Russia-Ukraine conflict. Investors were also concerned that inflationary risks and supply disruptions resulting from the conflict could dampen global growth, clouding the outlook for monetary policy. Meanwhile, investors look ahead to Federal Reserve chair Jerome Powell's appearance in Congress on Wednesday for fresh clues on the timing and magnitude of interest rate hikes.2022-03-01T06:34:00
