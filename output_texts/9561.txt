Title: US Stocks Extend Losses
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend LossesUnited States Stock MarketUS stock markets extended losses on Wednesday as investors remain cautious ahead of February's jobs report due Friday while there are no new developments on US-China trade negotiations. In addition, poor economic data showed the US posted the largest monthly trade deficit since 2008 in December and private sector businesses added 183,000 jobs in February, a smaller level than expected. The Dow Jones lost 0.4% to 25,694; the S&P 500 dropped 0.5% to 2,776; and the Nasdaq fell 0.6% to 7,529 around 12:30 PM NY time.2019-03-06T17:41:29.29
