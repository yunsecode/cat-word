Title: US Stocks Rebound
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks ReboundUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened higher on Thursday recovering from losses in the previous session as bonds yields declined after going up a day earlier after FOMC minutes suggested further rate hikes could be appropriate. The Dow Jones went up 199 points or 0.8%; the S&P 500 rose 21 points or 0.8% and the Nasdaq traded 50 points or 0.7% higher around 10 minutes after the opening bell.
