US Crude Oil Stocks Lower Than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeCrude oil stocks in the United States increased by 1.999 million barrels in the week ended April 22nd, 2016, below market expectations of 2.5 million rise. In the previous week, stocks went up by 2.08 million barrels. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks rose by 1.608 million barrels after falling by 0.11 million barrels in the previous week, above market expectations of 0.4 million fall.2016-04-27T15:36:31.917
