Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States increased 3.3 percent in the week ended March 3rd 2017, following a 5.8 percent jump in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications rose 5.2 percent and applications to purchase a home went up 1.7 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates increased by 6bps to 4.36 percent.2017-03-08T12:03:57.463
