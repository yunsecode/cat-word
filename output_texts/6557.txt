Title: Dow in the Green on Bright Economic Outlook
Tag: Stock Market

Dow in the Green on Bright Economic OutlookUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones gained almost 200 points on Monday as investors try to focus on prospects of a global economic recovery and progress towards fiscal stimulus and shrug off stubbornly high Treasury yields. The benchmark 10-year Treasury note retreated slightly to 1.59% from 1.6% early in the session. The Senate approved President Biden’s $1.9 trillion stimulus bill on Saturday and the House is seen passing it on Tuesday. Comments from David Tepper, founder of Appaloosa Management, also helped to lift investors' mood. Tepper said it’s very difficult to be bearish on stocks right now and thinks the sell-off in Treasuries that has driven rates higher is likely over. Meanwhile, the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq were in the red as rising rates are set to hurt companies relying on easy-borrowing and lead to less favourable valuation models for some hot technology shares. 2021-03-08T14:39:00
