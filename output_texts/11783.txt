Title: US Stocks Trade Lower on Geopolitical Tension
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Lower on Geopolitical TensionUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones dropped 143 points, or 0.7%, to 21,903 around 10:00 AM New York time on Thursday, as tensions between the US and North Korea hurt sentiment. Also, the S&P 500 retreated 20 points, or 0.8%, to 2,454; and the Nasdaq declined 77 points, or 1.2%, to 6,274.2017-08-10T14:12:29.43
