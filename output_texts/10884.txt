Title: US Stocks Extend Gains on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Extend Gains on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately higher on Thursday 5 April 2018, extending gains from the previous session amid a rise in tech shares led by Tesla (+6%) and Amazon (+3%) and Facebook (+3%), all recovering from recent negative news flow. The Dow Jones gained 241 points or 1.0% to 24505. The S&P 500 climbed 18 points or 0.7% to 2663. The Nasdaq jumped 34 points or 0.5% to 7077.  2018-04-05T20:05:26.5
