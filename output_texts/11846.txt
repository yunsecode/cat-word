Title: Fed Leaves Monetary Policy Steady
Tag: Interest Rate

Fed Leaves Monetary Policy SteadyUnited States Interest RateThe Federal Reserve left the target range for its federal funds rate unchanged at 1 percent to 1.25 percent during its July 2017 meeting and said it will start reducing its USD 4.5 trillion portfolio relatively soon. The committee considered near-term risks to the economic outlook as roughly balanced, but said it will closely monitor inflation.2017-07-26T17:56:44
