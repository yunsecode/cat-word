Title: US Stock Futures Gain
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures GainUnited States Stock MarketUS stock index futures gained in after-hours trading, as easing coronavirus restrictions and prospects of an economic recovery overshadowed escalating tensions between the US and China over the coronavirus crisis and a sharp fall in airlines stocks. On Monday, The Dow Jones added 26 points or 0.1% to 23,750. The S&P 500 climbed 12 points or 0.4% to 2843. The Nasdaq jumped 106 points or 1.2% to 8711. 2020-05-05T01:04:53.917
