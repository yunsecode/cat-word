Title: US Economy Adds More Jobs than Expected
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds More Jobs than ExpectedUnited States Non Farm PayrollsNon farm payrolls in the United States increased by 213 thousand in June of 2018, following an upwardly revised 244 thousand in May and well above market expectations of 195 thousand. Job gains occurred in professional and business services, manufacturing, and health care, while employment in retail trade declined.2018-07-06T12:31:00
