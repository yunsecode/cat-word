Title: US Export Prices Rise 0.6% MoM in May
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise 0.6% MoM in MayUnited States Export Prices MoMPrices for US exports increased 0.6 percent month-over-month in May of 2018, the same pace as in April and above market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. Cost of agricultural exports rose 1.6 percent compared to a 1.2 percent fall in the previous month, boosted by higher prices of wheat (10.1 percent); corn (4.4 percent) and soybean (1.8 percent). Meanwhile, non-agricultural export prices slowed to 0.5 percent from 0.7 percent in April. Year-on-year, export prices went up 4.9 percent, after increasing 3.8 percent in the prior month and above market consensus of a 4.4 percent rise.2018-06-14T12:33:00
