Title: US Mortgage Applications Jump 7.3%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Jump 7.3%United States Mortgage ApplicationsApplications for house mortgages in US rose 7.3 percent in the week ended December 18th, 2015, recovering from a 1.1 percent drop in the previous week. Fixed 30-year mortgage rates went up 2bps to 4.16 percent. Refinancing applications jumped 10.8 percent while purchase applications went up 4.1 percent.2015-12-23T12:06:43.273
