Title: US Mortgage Demand Hits 25-Year Low
Tag: MBA Mortgage Market Index

US Mortgage Demand Hits 25-Year LowUnited States MBA Mortgage Market IndexThe MBA Mortgage Market Index in the United States decreased by 4.5% to 204.6 points in the week ended October 14th, the lowest since 1997 as interest rates continue to rise. Applications to purchase a home loan went down 3.7% and the refinancing index dropped 6.8%, also the lowest since 1998.2022-10-19T14:09:00
