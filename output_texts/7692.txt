Dow Futures Rise 200 PointsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street looks set to begin a holiday-shortened week higher with US stocks attempting to rebound from a massive tech sell-off last week. However, investors remained worried that any rally could be short-lived amid persistent concerns about the disconnection between rising stock prices and a turbulent economy. Meanwhile, US-China tensions mount after the American Department of Defense said it is considering imposing export restrictions on China’s most advanced manufacturer of semiconductors.
