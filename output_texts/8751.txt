Title: US Stocks Open Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open HigherUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Monday, attempting to recover from its worst week since the 2008 financial crisis as investors hope that major central banks including the Fed, the ECB and the BoJ will introduce additional monetary stimulus to reduce the impact of coronavirus, while recession worries resurfaced after latest Markit PMI data showed the coronavirus hurt new export orders in China and disrupted supply chains in the Euro Area, Germany and France. The Dow was up 1.2%, the S&P 500 gained 0.6% and the Nasdaq went up 0.7% around 5 minutes after the opening bell. 2020-03-02T14:36:00
