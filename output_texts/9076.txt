Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise for 3rd Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise for 3rd Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States increased 0.5 percent in the week ended October 11th 2019, following a 5.4 percent gain in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications rose 3.6 percent while applications to purchase a home dropped 4.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went up by 2bps to 3.92 percent.2019-10-16T11:02:00
