Title: US Factory Growth Below Expectations: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Factory Growth Below Expectations: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI fell to 53.8 in November of 2017 from a nine-month high of 54.6 in October and below market expectations of 54.8. Stocks of finished goods declined for the first time since March amid capacity pressures and cost inflation was one of the highest in five years due to rising raw material costs and strong demand for inputs, flash figures showed.2017-11-24T14:48:42
