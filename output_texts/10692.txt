Title: Richmond Fed Index Rebounds in May
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

Richmond Fed Index Rebounds in MayUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Manufacturing Activity Index in the US fifth district including the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia jumped to 16 in May of 2018 from -3 in April which was the lowest reading since September of 2016. Figures beat market expectations of 9, boosted by shipments (15 from -8), new orders (16 from -9) and employment (18 from 12). Local business conditions also moved back into expansionary territory and firms remained optimistic that growth would continue in coming months. Meanwhile, wages (28 from 27), prices paid (2.63 from 2.43) and received (1.75 from 1.46) increased more.
