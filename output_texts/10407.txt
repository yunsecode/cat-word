Title: US Construction Spending Posts Biggest Drop in Over a Year
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Posts Biggest Drop in Over a YearUnited States Construction SpendingUS construction spending fell 1.1 percent month-over-month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of USD 1.32 trillion in June 2018, following an upwardly revised 1.3 percent growth in May and missing market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. It was the largest decline in construction spending since April 2017. Investment in public construction projects tumbled 3.5 percent in June (vs 3 percent in May), the steepest fall since March 2013, and spending on private residential projects fell 0.5 percent (vs 1.3 percent in May).2018-08-01T14:06:40.347
