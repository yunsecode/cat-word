Title: US Pending Home Sales Below Expectations
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Below ExpectationsUnited States Pending Home SalesThe National Association of Realtors' Pending Home Sales Index rose 3.9 percent year-on-year in October of 2015, following a 3 percent increase in the previous month. On a monthly basis, contracts to buy previously owned houses edged up 0.2%, rebounding from a downwardly revised 1.6 percent drop in the previous month, due to higher sales in the Northeast and the West. 2015-11-30T15:07:35.92
