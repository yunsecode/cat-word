Title: Dow and S&P Hit Fresh Records
Tag: Stock Market

Dow and S&P Hit Fresh RecordsUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones and the S&P 500 reached new record highs on Wednesday, extending gains from the previous session while the Nasdaq traded near the flatline as optimism over Covid-19 vaccine rollout continues to lift investors' mood. US FDA said Pfizer/BioNTech Covid-19 vaccine “met the prescribed success criteria” clearing it for emergency use approval while the UK rolled out the first doses to the public on Tuesday. Meanwhile, Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin spoke with House Speaker Nancy Pelosi on Tuesday and presented a $916 billion coronavirus relief proposal, in another effort to reach a deal after the Democrats rejected a previous offer. On the pandemic front, more than 215K new cases were recorded on Tuesday in the US, the third highest on record.2020-12-09T14:36:00
