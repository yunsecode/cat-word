Title: US Stocks Open Higher on Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Higher on EarningsUnited States Stock MarketUS stock markets opened in the green on Thursday despite ongoing trade concerns, buoyed by strong earnings results from Walmart and Cisco Systems. The Dow Jones gained 133 points, or 0.5%, to 25,781; the S&P 500 advanced 16 points, or 0.6%, to 2,867; and the Nasdaq rose 44 points, or 0.6%, to 7,866 around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2019-05-16T13:43:56.843
