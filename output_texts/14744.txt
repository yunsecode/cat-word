Title: U.S. Trade Deficit Widens in July
Tag: Balance of Trade

U.S. Trade Deficit Widens in JulyUnited States Balance of TradeU.S. July exports of $183.3 billion and imports of $225.3 billion resulted in a goods and services deficit of $42.0 billion, up from $41.9 billion in June, revised. July exports were $1.9 billion less than June exports of $185.2 billion. July imports were $1.8 billion less than June imports of $227.1 billion.2012-09-11T13:39:55
