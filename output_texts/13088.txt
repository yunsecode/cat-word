Title: US Personal Income Rises 0.4% in July
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Rises 0.4% in JulyUnited States Personal IncomePersonal income in the United States increased 0.4 percent month-on-month in July of 2016, following an upwardly revised 0.3 percent growth in the previous month and in line with market expectations. Wages and salaries rose 0.5 percent; proprietors' income went up 0.2 percent; rental income advanced 0.7 percent; personal dividend income rebounded 0.4 percent and personal interest income increased 0.4 percent.
