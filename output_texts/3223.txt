Title: Wall Street Set to End the Week Sharply Higher
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Set to End the Week Sharply HigherUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks extended gains for a 3rd straight session on Friday, with the Dow Jones adding almost 100 points while the S&P 500 jumped nearly 1% and the Nasdaq more than 1.5%. Traders welcomed fresh data showing consumer spending rose more than expected in April, signalling consumption remained robust at the beginning of Q2 and may support a GDP expansion this year while PCE inflation eased as price pressures seem to be slowing. At the same time, dip buyers returned to the market while FOMC minutes released early in the week pointed out that the Fed would not embark on more aggressive interest rate hikes. The earnings season continues with Ulta Beauty reporting better-than-expected quarterly results, while Gap cut its profit guidance. On the week, the three main averages are on track to gain nearly 4%, with the Dow snapping its longest losing streak since 1923.2022-05-27T13:39:00
