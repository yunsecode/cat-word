Title: US GDP Growth Revised Up to 1.3% in Q2
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Revised Up to 1.3% in Q2United States GDP Growth RateReal gross domestic product in the United States increased at an annual rate of 1.3 percent in the second quarter of 2011, (that is, from the first quarter to the second quarter), according to the "third" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.  In the first quarter, real GDP increased 0.4 percent.
