Title: US Personal Income Rises 0.4% MoM in June
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Rises 0.4% MoM in JuneUnited States Personal IncomePersonal income in the United States increased 0.4 percent month-over-month in June 2018, the same pace as in the previous month and in line with market expectations. Wages and salaries rose faster (0.4 percent from 0.3 percent in May) and disposable income advanced 0.4 percent (the same as in May). The saving rate was unchanged at 6.8 percent.2018-07-31T12:34:00
