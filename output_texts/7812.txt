Title: Dollar Trades Lower on US Retail Sales Data, Stimulus Talks
Tag: Currency

Dollar Trades Lower on US Retail Sales Data, Stimulus TalksUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index dropped against a basket of currencies on Friday, hovering around 93.1 and falling for eight strait weeks after data showed US retail trade in July rose by only 1.2 percent, below market expectations of a 1.9 percent increase. At the same time, the lack of progress in negotiations on a new coronavirus relief package worried investors, as Republicans and Democrats remain divided on key issues. Meanwhile, weaker than expected Chinese industry and sales data helped to curb the losses.2020-08-14T12:54:00
