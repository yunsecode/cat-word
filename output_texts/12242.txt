Title: US Exports Hit 2-Year High
Tag: Exports

US Exports Hit 2-Year HighUnited States ExportsTotal exports from the United States rose 0.2 percent month-over-month to USD 192.9 billion in February 2017. It was the highest value since December 2014, as sales of automobiles and parts hit their strongest level since July 2014 and exports of industrial supplies and materials were the highest since December 2015. Exports to the EU jumped 8 percent, with goods shipped to Germany growing 8.6 percent; and those to the UK soared 17.3 percent. However, exports to China fell 2.7 percent and to Mexico dropped 7.1 percent.2017-04-04T12:30:55
