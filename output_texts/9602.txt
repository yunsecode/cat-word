Title: US Housing Starts Lowest since 2016
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Lowest since 2016United States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States slumped 11.2 percent from a month earlier to an annualized rate of 1,078 thousand in December of 2018, compared to market forecasts of a 0.5 percent drop. It is the lowest reading since September of 2016. Considering full 2018, housing starts rose a non-seasonally adjusted 3.6 percent to 1246.6 thousand.2019-02-26T13:36:00
