Title: US Futures Slip Further After Weak Session
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Slip Further After Weak SessionUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures slipped further on Thursday after all three major averages ended lower in the last regular session, weighed down by disappointing earnings reports and the Federal Reserve’s commitment to fighting inflation. Futures contracts tied to the three major indexes were all trading in negative territory. In extended trading, Bed Bath & Beyond tumbled 18% after it was revealed that activist investor Ryan Cohen plans to exit his entire position in the retailer. Meanwhile, Dow component Cisco jumped more than 4% on better-than-expected earnings and revenue. In regular trading on Wednesday, the Dow fell 0.5%, the S&P 500 lost 0.7% and the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.25%. Those moves came as investors digested the latest FOMC minutes, where policymakers indicated they likely would not consider slowing the pace of rate hikes until inflation comes down toward the 2% target.2022-08-18T00:34:20.993
