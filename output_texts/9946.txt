Title: US Private Sector Activity at 2-Month Low: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Activity at 2-Month Low: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit Composite PMI for the US fell to 54.4 in November of 2018 from 54.9 in October and below market expectations of 56, the flash estimate showed. The reading pointed to the weakest growth in the US private sector activity in two months, as both services (54.4 from 54.8) and manufacturing (55.4 from 55.7) rose at a softer pace.2018-11-23T14:51:02.153
