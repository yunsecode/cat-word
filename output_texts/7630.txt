Dollar Jumps to Over 1-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index strengthened near 0.7% to trade above 93.5 on Monday, the highest on a closing basis since August 11th amid fears that mounting coronavirus infections in Europe could lead to another economic lockdown and more restrictions.
