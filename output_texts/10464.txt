Title: US Jobless Claims Fall Unexpectedly to Lowest Since 1969
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall Unexpectedly to Lowest Since 1969United States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits fell by 8 thousand to 207 thousand in the week ending in July 14 from the previous week's upwardly revised level of 215 thousand. It was the lowest reading since the week ending December 6, 1969 and surprising markets which expected a rise to 220 thousand. According to unadjusted data, the biggest decreases in claims were recorded in New York (-9,722), Michigan (-9,389), New Jersey (-4,847), Kentucky (-2,288) and Massachusetts (-2,279) while largest gains were seen in California +(7,015), Georgia (+3,750), Alabama (+2,598) and South Carolina (+1,127). Claims dropped in Puerto Rico (-866) and Virgin Islands (-22).2018-07-19T12:32:00
