Title: US Mortgage Applications Jump 21.3%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Jump 21.3%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States surged 21.3 percent in the week ended January 8th 2016 from the previous week as fixed 30-year mortgage rates fell by 8 bps to 4.12 percent, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications increased 23.8 percent and applications to purchase a home went up 17.8 percent.2016-01-13T12:05:04
