Title: US Stocks Hit New Records On Earnings
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Hit New Records On Earnings United States Stock MarketUS stocks hit fresh record highs on Tuesday, after stronger-than-expected earnings reports from some major US retailers such as Walmart, Home Depot and Macy's boosted sentiment. The Dow Jones advanced 72 points, or 0.4%, to 20,696; the S&P 500 gained 8 points, or 0.4%, to 2,359; and the Nasdaq added 16 points, or 0.3%, to 5,856 around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2017-02-21T14:43:10
