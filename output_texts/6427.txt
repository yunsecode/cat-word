Title: Dollar at Near 5-Month High
Tag: Currency

Dollar at Near 5-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was little changed around 93.2 on Thursday, remaining close to levels not seen since early November, as investors continue to bet on a strong economic recovery amid further fiscal stimulus and coronavirus vaccination. US President Biden announced a $2.25 trillion package, the second major stimulus plan after the $1.9 trillion bill. However, the new spending push is likely to get strong GOP opposition, specially over a corporate tax hike increase to 28%. 2021-04-01T09:37:00
