Title: Wall Street Opens Lower
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens LowerUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened in the red on Monday, dragged down by weak earnings reports and a slump in commodity prices while investors remain cautious ahead of the Federal Reserve and Bank of Japan’s meetings later in the week. The Dow Jones lost 0.26% or 47 points, the S&P 500 went down 0.22% or 5 points and the Nasdaq traded near the flat line, down 0.06% or 3 points around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2016-04-25T14:41:10
