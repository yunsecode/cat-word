Title: US Jobless Claims Below Expectations
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Below ExpectationsUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 1 thousand to 234 thousand in latest week, lower than market expectations of 238 thousand. It is 116th straight week that claims were below 300,000, a level associated with a healthy jobs market. In addition, the 4-week moving average which removes weekly volatility fell to 235.25 thousand, the lowest in 44 years.2017-05-25T12:35:21
