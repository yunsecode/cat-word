Title: US Jobless Claims at 5-Month High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims at 5-Month HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits was 282,000 in the week ended December 5th, a sharp increase of 13,000 from the previous week's unrevised level of 269,000 and staying way above market expectations of 269,000. It was the highest figure in more than twenty weeks, when jobless claims hit 296,000 in the week ended July 4th.2015-12-10T13:33:41
