Title: US Job Openings Rise Less Than Expected in September
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Rise Less Than Expected in SeptemberUnited States Job OffersThe number of job openings in the United States increased 5.49 million in September 2016 from an upwardly revised 5.45 million in the previous month, but missing market expectations of 5.51 million. The number of positions waiting to be filled increased mainly in professional and business services (+33,000) and construction (+29,000). Among regions, job openings increased in the South (+81,000) and the Northeast (+47,000).2016-11-08T15:11:39.15
