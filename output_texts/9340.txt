Title: US Jobless Claims Unchanged in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Unchanged in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits came in at 230 thousand in the week ending April 27th 2019, the same as the week before and compared with market expectations of 215 thousand. Jobless claims stood at its highest level since February 9th 2019. The volatility in recent weeks has been attributed to Easter holidays as well as school spring breaks. Also, a workers’ strike at the Stop & Shop supermarket chain likely influenced the recent surge. According to unadjusted data, the biggest rises were recorded in New York (+6,637), New Jersey (+2,964) and Delaware (+760) while the largest declines were seen in Massachusetts (-6,701), California (-2,731), Rhode Island (-2,461) and Connecticut (-1,763).
