Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 5th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 5th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 2.976 million barrels in the week ended September 30th, 2016, compared to market expectations of a 2.4 million gain. It is the fifth consecutive decline and follows a 1.88 million drop in the previous week. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks went up by 0.222 million barrels.2016-10-05T14:34:15.613
