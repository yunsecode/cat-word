Title: US Stocks End Mixed on Earnings Reports
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed on Earnings ReportsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Tuesday as Goldman Sachs and Johnson & Johnson earnings reports came better-than-expected while disappointing Netflix and Yahoo results weighed on investors' sentiment. The Dow Jones rose by 0.14% to an all-time high of 18,559.01 points. In contrast, the S&P 500 lost 0.14% or 3.11 points and the Nasdaq declined 0.38% or 19.41 points.2016-07-19T21:37:42
