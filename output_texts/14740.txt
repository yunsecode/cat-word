U.S. Unemployment Down to 7.8% in SeptemberUnited States Unemployment RateU.S. unemployment rate decreased to 7.8 percent in September, and total nonfarm payroll employment rose by 114,000, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on October 5th. Employment increased in health care and in transportation and warehousing but changed little in most other major industries.2012-10-05T13:45:51.043
