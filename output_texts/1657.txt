US Natural Gas Storage Draw Slightly Smaller-than-Expected: EIAUnited States Natural Gas Stocks ChangeWorking gas held in storage facilities in the United States decreased by 81 billion cubic feet in the week ending November 25 of 2022. It is the biggest draw since the first week of March, following a 80 billion cubic feet decline in the previous week.
