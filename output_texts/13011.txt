US Stocks Fall on Friday, Gain on the WeekUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the red on Friday amid lower oil prices and better-than-expected inflation data for August while investors await Fed monetary policy decision next week. The Dow Jones closed 89 points or 0.5% lower, the S&P 500 declined 8 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq lost 5 points or 0.1%.
