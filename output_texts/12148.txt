Title: US Manufacturing PMI Confirmed At 7-Month Low: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Manufacturing PMI Confirmed At 7-Month Low: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe seasonally adjusted Markit final US Manufacturing PMI fell to 52.8 in April 2017 from 53.3 in March, matching a flash estimate. It was the lowest reading since September 2016, as a slowdown in output and new business growth more than offset a slight rebound in job creation.2017-05-01T13:48:32
