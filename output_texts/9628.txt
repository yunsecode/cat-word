Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Friday 15 February 2019, as investors’ sentiment was boosted by news of progress on US-China trade dispute. Talks between the two countries are set to continue next week in Washington in an effort to achieve a trade deal before the March 1st deadline. Also, sentiment was nudged by US consumer sentiment, which  rose to 95.5 in February of 2019 from 91.2 in January. The Dow Jones gained 444 points or 1.8% to 25883, booking its eighth straight weekly gain. The S&P 500 climbed 30 points or 1.1% to 2776. The Nasdaq added 46 points or 0.6% to 7472.2019-02-15T21:08:00
