Title: US New Home Sales at 7-Month Low
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales at 7-Month LowUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States shrank 9.4 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 571 thousand in July of 2017 from an upwardly revised 630 thousand in June. It is the lowest reading so far this year and well below market expectations of 612 thousand. Sales fell in the Northeast, the West and the South.
