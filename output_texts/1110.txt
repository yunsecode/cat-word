Title: DXY Firms at 104.5
Tag: Currency

DXY Firms at 104.5United States CurrencyThe dollar index firmed up near 104.5 on Thursday, hovering near its strongest levels in seven weeks as minutes from the Federal Reserve’s last meeting showed that policymakers largely agreed to keep raising interest rates to bring inflation back down within target. The minutes stated that inflation “remained well above” the Fed’s 2% target and the labor market “remained very tight, contributing to continuing upward pressure on wages and prices.” Meanwhile, Q4 GDP growth was revised slightly lower while PCE prices rose faster than initially thought, second estimates showed. Another report showed weekly claims unexpectedly fell last week, pointing to a still tight labor market.2023-02-23T13:38:00
