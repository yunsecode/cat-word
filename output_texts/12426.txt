Title: US Consumer Sentiment At 3-Month Low
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment At 3-Month LowUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe preliminary reading of the University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States fell to 95.7 in February of 2017 from a 13-year high of 98.5 in the previous month and lower than market forecasts of 97.9. Future expectations declined and current conditions edged down.2017-02-10T15:05:26.69
