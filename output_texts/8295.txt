Title: US Treasury Yields Fall ahead of Jobless Claims Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Fall ahead of Jobless Claims DataUnited States Government Bond 10YUS government debt prices rose on Thursday, as investors' risk appetite continues to decline ahead of the jobless claims release later in the day which is expected to show that total unemployed since the coronavirus pandemic started in mid-March increased by about 36 million, nearly a quarter of the working-age population. On Wednesday, the Fed’s Powell advised that unemployment would reach a peak during the next month. The Chairman also warned for an extended period of weak growth due to the coronavirus pandemic and pledged for more stimulus but ruled out the possibility of negative interest rates. The US 10-year Treasury note decreased to 0.6202% and that on 30-year fell to 1.3157%.2020-05-14T11:47:00
