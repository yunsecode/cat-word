Title: US Consumer Sentiment Surges in September
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Surges in SeptemberUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US jumped to 100.8 in September of 2018 from 96.2 in August, beating market expectations of 96.7. It is the second highest level since 2004, only behind the March 2018 reading of 101.4, preliminary estimates showed. Expectations were the strongest since July of 2004, largely due to more favorable prospects for jobs and incomes and current conditions were also assessed more favorable.2018-09-14T14:07:50.603
