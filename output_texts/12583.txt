Title: US Stocks Close Higher After Fed Minutes
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher After Fed MinutesUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks closed in the green on Wednesday, after minutes from the Fed's December meeting showed policymakers expected a faster pace of rate hikes in 2017. The Dow Jones rose 60 points, or 0.3%, to finish at 19,942; the S&P 500 closed up 13 points, or 0.6%, at 2,271; and the Nasdaq gained 48 points, or 0.9%, to 5,477. 2017-01-04T21:19:58.243
