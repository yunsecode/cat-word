Title: US Jobless Claims Drop to Lowest in 3 Months
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Drop to Lowest in 3 MonthsUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits was 262,000 in the week ended February 13th, a decrease of 7,000 from the previous week's level of 269,000. It was the lowest figure since week ended November 21st and below market expectations of 275,000. Claims have been below 300,000 for 50 weeks, the level associated with a healthy jobs market.2016-02-18T13:35:09
