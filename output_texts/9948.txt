Title: US Stocks Close Mostly Higher on Wednesday

US Stocks Close Mostly Higher on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street rebounded moderately on Wednesday 21 November 2018 with the Dow Jones closing flat and paring gains on late session, as technology shares rebounded from previous sessions' sharp sell-off and oil prices rallied.
