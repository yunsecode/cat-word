Title: Dollar Weakens Below 111
Tag: Currency

Dollar Weakens Below 111United States CurrencyThe dollar index weakened below the 111 mark, a level not seen in almost two weeks, on speculation that weaker-than-expected US economic data would temper the Federal Reserve’s aggressive tightening. The number of job openings in the US fell more than expected in August, signaling that tighter financial conditions could be cooling the job market. This data came a day after a gauge of US manufacturing from ISM showed activity declined to the lowest since May 2020. Meanwhile, Fed officials continue to advocate for further rate hikes. New York Fed Bank President John Williams said that while there are signs of cooling inflation, underlying price pressures remain elevated, reinforcing the case for further monetary tightening.2022-10-04T14:28:54.927
