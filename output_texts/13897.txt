Title: US Economy Adds 211K Jobs in November
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds 211K Jobs in NovemberUnited States Non Farm PayrollsTotal nonfarm payroll employment increased by 211,000 in November, lower than an upwardly revised 298,000 in the previous month but beating market expectations of 200,000. Job growth occurred in construction, professional and technical services, and health care while employment in mining and information declined.2015-12-04T13:33:04
