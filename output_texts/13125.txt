Title: US Stocks Fall on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed lower on Tuesday, retreating from record highs in the previous day amid mixed economic data and a fall in the USD while investors await Fed minutes release on Wednesday for further clues on the timing of next rate hike. The Dow Jones lost 84 points or 0.45%, the S&P 50 declined 12 points or 0.55% and the Nasdaq finished 35 points or 0.66% lower. 2016-08-16T21:13:13.483
