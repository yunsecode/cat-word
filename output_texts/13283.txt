Title: US Exports Fall 0.2% in May
Tag: Exports

US Exports Fall 0.2% in MayUnited States ExportsTotal exports from the United States edged down 0.2 percent to $182. 35 billion in May of 2016, following a 1.4 percent rise in April. Exports of goods decreased $0.2 billion to $119.8 billion, led by capital goods, civilian aircraft; computer accessories; automotive vehicles, parts, and engines and other parts and accessories. Sales to the European Union fell 4.2 percent, with those to the UK down 15.6 percent. Exports to Mexico shrank 1.8 percent, China fell 1.7 percent and those to Canada declined 1.5 percent. In contrast, sales to Japan surged 11.5 percent.2016-07-06T14:15:44
