Title: US Treasury Yields Continue to Fall
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Continue to FallUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note fell for the fourth consecutive session to 1.57% on Tuesday, the lowest on a closing basis in near three weeks, as comments from Fed officials helped to ease inflation fears. Several officials, including Lael Brainard, Raphael Bostic and James Bullard, continued to reiterate that most price increases should be temporary and affirmed their support to keep monetary policy accommodative for some time. 2021-05-25T08:25:00
