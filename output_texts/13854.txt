Title: US Consumer Sentiment at 5-Month High
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment at 5-Month HighUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's final reading on the overall index of consumer sentiment came in at 92.6 in December of 2015, higher than a first reading of 91.8 and hitting its highest level since July this year. Consumers were more optimistic over their current conditions due to lower inflation, which bolstered real incomes, while they felt less confident about their future situation. Meanwhile, inflation expectations for the next year decreased slightly.2015-12-23T15:11:02
