Title: Dollar Hits 8-Month High
Tag: Currency

Dollar Hits 8-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index increased for the 4th day on Friday to the highest since February amid rising expectations of a Fed rate hike this year and after the ECB left the door open for more monetary stimulus on Thursday. The greenback gained around 0.5% against the Euro, 0.5% against the British pound, 0.3% against the Aussie and 0.8% against the Loonie around 10:00 AM NY time.2016-10-21T14:00:00
