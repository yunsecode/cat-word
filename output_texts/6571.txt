Title: US Q4 Labor Costs Revised Lower
Tag: Labour Costs

US Q4 Labor Costs Revised LowerUnited States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the US nonfarm business sector rose an annualized 6 percent in the fourth quarter of 2020, rebounding from an upwardly revised 9.6 percent drop in the previous period but less than a preliminary estimate of a 6.8 percent jump and below market expectations of a 6.6 percent rise. Hourly compensation climbed 1.5 percent (vs 1.7 percent previously reported) and productivity fell 4.2 percent (vs -4.8 percent previously reported). Year-on-year, unit labor costs in the nonfarm business sector rose 4.2 percent.2021-03-04T13:34:00
