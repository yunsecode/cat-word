Title: US Dollar Falls for 4th Day as Economies Reopen
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Falls for 4th Day as Economies ReopenUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded lower at 99.6 on Tuesday, extending declines for the 4th straight session amid an improvement in risk appetite, as investors  expect the global economy will reopen soon. Also, the Fed is seen leaving monetary policy on hold while the Bank of Japan already rolled out more stimulus measures and the ECB will likely increase its asset purchases on Thursday. 2020-04-28T08:08:00
