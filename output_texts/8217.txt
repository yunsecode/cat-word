Dollar Hits 8-week LowUnited States currencyThe dollar index extended losses to touch an 8-week low of 98.49 on Thursday after continuing claims fell for the first time since the coronavirus pandemic, raising hopes the US job market bottomed out. It more than offset an upwardly revised 5% GDP contraction for Q1 and mounting tensions between the US and China after China’s National People’s Congress officially approved the new national security law for Hong Kong.2020-05-28T15:59:00
