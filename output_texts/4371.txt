Title: Treasury Yields Steady, Payrolls Awaited
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

Treasury Yields Steady, Payrolls AwaitedUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury note hovered around 1.8% in the first week of February as investors remain cautious ahead of the payrolls report on Friday and wonder what the tightening path from the Fed will be. Fresh ADP data showed US private companies unexpectedly cut 301K jobs in January, a sign the impact of the omicron coronavirus variant may be bigger than initially anticipated. At the same time, St. Louis Fed president James Bullard, a noted hawk, said on Tuesday he would argue for rate rises in March, May and June, but did not favor a half-point move. The US central bank indicated last week that it would likely hike interest rates in March, but markets started to price in five quarter-point rate hikes this year and some investors even pointed to a half-point rate hike next month.2022-02-02T13:42:00
