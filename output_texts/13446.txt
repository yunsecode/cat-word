Title: US Jobless Claims at 15-Month High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims at 15-Month HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 20,000 to 294,000 in the week ended May 7th. Claims rose for the third straight week, reaching the highest since February last year and well above market expectations of 270,000. However, it is the 62nd consecutive week initial claims are below 300,000, the level associated with a healthy jobs market.2016-05-12T13:37:34.957
