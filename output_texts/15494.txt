Title: The U.S. trade deficit narrowed in July
Tag: Balance of Trade

The U.S. trade deficit narrowed in JulyUnited States Balance of TradeThe gap shrank 0.3 percent to $59.2 billion from a revised $59.4 billion in June that was bigger than previously estimated, the Commerce Department said today in Washington.
