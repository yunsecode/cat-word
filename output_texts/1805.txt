Title: US Continued Unemployment Claims Rise for 4th Week
Tag: Continuing Jobless Claims

US Continued Unemployment Claims Rise for 4th WeekUnited States Continuing Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing continuing claims for unemployment benefits rose by 5 thousand to 1.493 million on the week ending October 29th, increasing for the fourth consecutive week and well above forecasts of 1.475 million. It was the largest amount of continuing claims since March, in line with the slowdown of the economy brought by aggressive interest rate hikes by the Federal Reserve.2022-11-10T15:38:58.707
