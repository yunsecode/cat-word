US Equities Jump On Monday As Oil Rallies
United States Stock MarketWall Street closed in the green on Monday with the S&P and the Nasdaq hitting new highs amid a 2.5% jump in oil prices after energy ministers from Russia and Saudi Arabia said they would back a 9-month extension to global production cuts. Gains were broad, led by stocks of companies that sell software security services, as the global cyberattack continued to spread. The Dow Jones gained 85 points, or 0.4 percent, to 20982. The S&P 500 advanced 11 points, or 0.5 percent, to 2402. The Nasdaq rose 28 points, or 0.5 percent, to 6150. 2017-05-15T17:41:34
