Mortgage Applications Rise for 2nd Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States jumped 3.8 percent in the week ended October 30th, following a 1.7 percent increase in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the first time in 3 months applications rise for 2 consecutive weeks. Applications to refinance a home loan surged 6.4 percent while homebuyer mortgage applications went down 1.3 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged up 1bps bps to 3.01 percent, remaining at near record low levels. The rates for jumbo loans, FHA loans and 15-year fixed loans also set record lows.2020-11-04T12:08:00
