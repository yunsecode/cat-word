Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Higher in April
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Higher in AprilUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US was revised higher to 98.8 in April of 2018 from 97.8 in the preliminary estimate as expectations fell less than initially estimated. It compares with 101.4 in March which was the highest reading since January 2004. The final April figure was close to the 2018 average of 98.9 which was higher than any other yearly average since 107.6 in 2000.2018-04-27T14:17:42.457
