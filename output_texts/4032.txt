Title: Dollar Hits One-Week Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar Hits One-Week Low United States CurrencyThe dollar index depreciated to fresh weekly lows in the 98 neighbourhood, moving in tandem with lower Treasury yields, as investors digested the kickstart of the Federal Reserve tightening cycle, which brought no additional hawkish surprises. The US central bank has approved its first interest rate hike in more than three years, lifting its benchmark rate by 25bps while pointing to six more interest-rate increases by year’s end, moving more aggressively to slow inflation. The Fed has also downgraded its gross domestic product growth estimate for 2022 to 2.8%, from the 4% projected in December. Aside from policy headlines, investors continued to follow developments in the Russia-Ukraine war.
