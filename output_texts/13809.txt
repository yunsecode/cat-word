Title: Wall Street Closes Sharply Lower
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Closes Sharply LowerUnited States Stock MarketA selloff in US equities accelerated on Wednesday on concerns about global economic slowdown and ahead of major earnings reports. The S&P 500 sank 2.5%, below the key 1,900 level for the first time since September 29th; the Dow Jones fell 365 points or 2.2%; and the Nasdaq Composite declined 160 points or 3.4%. 2016-01-13T22:01:00.55
