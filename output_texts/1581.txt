Title: Wall Street Set to Open Sharply Lower
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Set to Open Sharply Lower  United States Stock MarketStock futures contracts tied to the blue-chip Dow fell roughly 1% on Thursday, and those linked to the S&P 500 and Nasdaq were down 1.3% and 1.5%, respectively, as a hawkish Federal Reserve and concerns about a looming recession rattled sentiment. The US central bank raised its federal-funds rate by a half percentage point to a range between 4.25% and 4.5%, a 15-year high, signaling that interest rates will go higher for longer while dashing hopes for a rate cut in 2023. On the data front, a Commerce Department report showed retail sales contracted more than expected in November, as stubbornly high inflation and tight financial conditions hit consumer demand. In regular trading on Wednesday, the Dow fell 0.4%, the S&P 500 lost 0.6%, and the Nasdaq Composite tumbled 0.8%.2022-12-15T09:00:00
