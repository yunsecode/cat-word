Title: US Weekly Earnings Post Biggest Rise in a Year
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Weekly Earnings Post Biggest Rise in a YearUnited States Average Hourly EarningsAverage hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls in the United States increased 0.5 percent or 12 cents to USD 25.39 in January of 2016. It is the highest increase since January last year and above market expectations of 0.3 percent. Over the year, average hourly earnings have risen by 2.5 percent. 2016-02-05T14:45:38
