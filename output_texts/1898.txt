Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Falls Below 4%
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Falls Below 4%United States Government Bond 10YThe US 10-year Treasury note yield decreased below 4% after the Federal Reserve hiked interest rates by 75bps but also signaled the aggressive campaign to curb inflation could be reaching its final phase. There is a possibility that the central bank slows down the pace of its rate-hiking path already in December amid mounting evidence that tighter financial conditions are already impacting the economy while fuelling concerns about a recession. Still, better-than-expected ISM Manufacturing PMI and JOLTs Job Openings figures throw a little cold water on expectations earlier this week.2022-11-02T18:11:00
