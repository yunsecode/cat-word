Title: US Consumer Sentiment Rises in December
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Rises in DecemberUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment increased to 91.8 in December from 91.3 in November of 2015 but slightly below market expectations.
