U.S. Retail Sales Rose 0.1% in JanuaryUnited States Retail Sales MomThe U.S. Census Bureau announced on February 13th that advance estimates of U.S. retail and food services sales for January, adjusted for seasonal variation and holiday and trading-day differences, but not for price changes, were $416.6 billion, an increase of 0.1 percent (±0.5%)* from the previous month and 4.4 percent (±0.7%) above January 2012.2013-02-13T13:37:31
