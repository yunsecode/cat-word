Title: US Stocks Make Sharp Comeback on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Make Sharp Comeback on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Wednesday 4 April 2018 after plunging at the opening bell on as trade war concerns escalated after China said would target 106 US products with a 25 percent tariff in response to President Trumps' list of Chinese imports to be targeted. Equities rebounded after led by tech shares. The Dow Jones jumped 231 points or 1.0% to 24264. The S&P 500 climbed 30 points or 1.2% to 2645. The Nasdaq soared 101 points or 1.5% to 7042. 2018-04-04T20:13:12.2
