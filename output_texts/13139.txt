Title: Wall Street Opens Lower on Weak Retail Trade
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Opens Lower on Weak Retail TradeUnited States Stock MarketUS equities open lower on Friday as weaker-than-expected retail sales data and an unexpected drop in producer prices weighed on sentiment. The Dow Jones Industrial Average lost 41 points, or 0.2%; the S&P 500 index fell 3.8 points, or 0.2%; and the Nasdaq Composite Index retreated 6 points, or 0.1% around 10 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-08-12T14:43:16
