Title: US Economy Adds More Jobs than Expected
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds More Jobs than ExpectedUnited States Non Farm PayrollsNon farm payrolls in the United States increased by 201 thousand in August of 2018, following a downwardly revised 147 thousand in July and above market expectations of 191 thousand. Employment increased in professional and business services, health care, wholesale trade, transportation and warehousing, and mining.
