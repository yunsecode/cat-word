Title: Dollar Supported by Elevated Yields
Tag: Currency

Dollar Supported by Elevated YieldsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded firmly above 95.5 on Wednesday, recovering further from a 2-month low of 94.63 hit on Jan. 14th, lifted by elevated US yields as traders braced for a more aggressive tightening by the Federal Reserve. The benchmark 10-year yield reached a 2-year high of 1.89% on Wednesday, while the 2-year Treasury yield leapt 15 basis points over two sessions to cross 1%. The US central bank is set to meet on Jan. 25-26, and although it is not expected to move rates, there has been strong hawkish commentary coming from policymakers. Last week, Fed chair Jerome Powell said that the US economy is ready for the rise in borrowing cost and that he sees a series of interest rate hikes this year.2022-01-19T06:26:21.543
