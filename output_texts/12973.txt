Title: US Jobless Claims Rise Less Than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise Less Than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 3,000 to 254,000 in the week ended September 24th compared with the previous week's revised level of 251,000 and below market expectations of 260,000. The four-week average, which smooths out week-to-week volatility in the claims data, dropped to 256,000, matching an April reading as the lowest average since 1973.2016-09-29T12:35:25
