Title: US Stocks Trade Mixed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade MixedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded mixed on Tuesday as investors digest global trade relations. The Dow Jones rose 198 points or 0.8% and the S&P 500 added 12 points or 0.4% while the Nasdaq fell 9 points or 0.1% around 12:20 PM NY time. On Monday, the 3 key stock indexes rallied, rebounding from the worst week since 2016 as trade fears eased after the US and Chinese authorities started talks to improve US access to Chinese markets and the US Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin said he is cautiously hopeful a deal could be reached.2018-03-27T16:29:53.177
