Title: Dollar Hits One-Week Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar Hits One-Week Low United States CurrencyThe dollar index depreciated to fresh weekly lows in the 92.40 neighbourhood Friday, in tandem with Treasury yields, as investors reacted to disappointing sentiment data. The University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US slumped to 70.2 in August and well below market expectations of 81.2. That was the lowest level since 2011 and one of the six sharpest drops in the past 50 years of the survey, signalling lingering concerns that the rapid spread of the contagious Delta variant would derail the global economic recovery. As a result, the greenback ended the week lower, despite a clear sign of a move in a hawkish direction by the Federal Reserve. 2021-08-13T16:51:00
