Title: US NAHB Housing Market Index Edges Up in October
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US NAHB Housing Market Index Edges Up in OctoberUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index for the US increased to 68 in October of 2018 from 67 in September, slightly above market expectations of 67. The subindex for current single-family home sales rose to 74 from 73 in September; the gauge for home sales over the next six months went up to 75 from 74 and prospective buyers increased to 53 from 49.
