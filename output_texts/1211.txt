Title: US Imports Rebound from 11-Month Low
Tag: Imports

US Imports Rebound from 11-Month LowUnited States ImportsImports to the United States increased by USD 4.2 billion to USD 317.6 billion in December 2022, up from the previous month's 11-month low of USD 313.4 billion, suggesting a rebound in domestic demand following a slowdown in inflation. Purchases of goods were up USD 4.5 billion, boosted by consumer goods (up USD 4.1 billion), in particular cell phones and other household goods, as well as automotive vehicles, parts, and engines (up USD 2.9 billion). Meanwhile, imports of industrial supplies and materials declined USD 2.7 billion, led by fuel oil and organic chemicals. Finally, imports of services decreased USD 0.3 billion, due to travel and transport. Considering 2022 full year, imports increased USD 556.1 billion from 2021 to USD 3.96 trillion.2023-02-07T13:45:00
