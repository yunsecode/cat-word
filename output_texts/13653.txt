Title: US Planned Layoffs Fall in February
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Planned Layoffs Fall in February United States Challenger Job CutsThe number of announced corporate layoffs in the United States fell 18 percent to 61599 in February of 2016 from a six-month high of 75114 in January. The energy sector announced plans to cut 25,051 jobs, the highest figure among industries. Year-on-year, planned job cuts rose 22 percent. Considering the first two months of 2016, job cuts totaled 136713, up 32 percent from the same period in 2015.2016-03-03T12:34:32
