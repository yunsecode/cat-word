Title: US Current Account Gap Lower than Expected
Tag: Current Account

US Current Account Gap Lower than ExpectedUnited States Current AccountThe United States recorded a current account deficit of USD 109.7 billion in the second quarter of 2015, lower than an upwardly revised USD 118.3 billion gap in the first three months of the year.
