Title: US Jobless Claims Rise to 339K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise to 339K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending February 8, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 339,000, an increase of 8,000 from the previous week's unrevised figure of 331,000. The 4-week moving average was 336,750, an increase of 3,500 from the previous week's revised average of 333,250.2014-02-13T13:35:06.083
