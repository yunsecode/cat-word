Title: US Economic Optimism Index Hits 10-Year High On Trump
Tag: Economic Optimism Index

US Economic Optimism Index Hits 10-Year High On TrumpUnited States Economic Optimism IndexThe IBD/TIPP Economic Optimism Index rose to 54.8 in December 2016 from 51.4 in November, better than market expectations of 52.3. It was the highest figure since November 2006. The six-month economic outlook jumped by 4.1 points to 56.3, despite signs that the Fed might soon raise interest rates, suggesting that Americans are more focused on Trump's promises to reform the tax code, deregulate government and to repeal and replace ObamaCare. The measure of confidence in federal economic policies got the biggest lift from the election, increasing 5.2 points to 48.3, the highest since August 2007. The personal financial outlook index rose a modest 0.9 point to 59.8, but remained the highest of the three subindexes.
