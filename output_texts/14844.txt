Title: US GDP Grows a Modest 2% in 3rd quarter
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Grows a Modest 2% in 3rd quarterUnited States GDP Growth RateUnited States real gross domestic product increased at an annual rate of 2.0 percent in the third quarter of 2010, (that is, from the second quarter to the third quarter), according to the "advance" estimate released by the Bureau of Economic Analysis.  In the second quarter, real GDP increased 1.7 percent.2010-10-30T17:21:59.72
