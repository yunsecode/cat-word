Title: US Stocks Fall on Retail Sales and Production Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on Retail Sales and Production DataUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded lower on Wednesday after recent data suggests the impact of the coronavirus on the US economy may be harder than anticipated. In March, retail sales plunged at a record 8.7% and industrial production fell 5.4%, the most in 74 years, with both figures coming much worse than market forecasts.
