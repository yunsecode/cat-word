Title: US Jobless Claims Hit 16-Week High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Hit 16-Week HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing new claims for unemployment benefits increased by 21 thousand to 218 thousand in the week ended May 14th, from a revised 197 thousand in the previous period and above the market estimate of 200 thousand. It is the highest reading since the week ended January 22nd. On a non-seasonally adjusted basis, initial claims rose by 12,811 from the previous week to 198,711, with notable increases in Kentucky (+6,728), California (+3,315), Pennsylvania (+2,102) and Illinois (+1,827). The 4-week moving average, which removes week-to-week volatility, was 199,500, an increase of 8,250 from the previous week's revised average.2022-05-19T12:37:00
