Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Earnings, PMI Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher on Earnings, PMI DataUnited States Stock MarketWall Street finished higher on Monday despite a rising dollar and falling oil prices, as upbeat earnings from T-Mobile and VF Corp boosted sentiment and PMI data for US showed manufacturing activity in October came in stronger-than-expected. The Dow Jones Industrial Average finished 77 points, or 0.4%, higher at 18,223, after rising as much as 130 points early in the session; the S&P 500 index advanced 10 points, or 0.5%, to 2,151; and the Nasdaq Composite Index climbed 52 points, or 1%, to 5,310.2016-10-24T20:13:16.397
