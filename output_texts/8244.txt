Title: US Dollar Holds Near 100
Tag: Currency

US Dollar Holds Near 100United States CurrencyThe dollar index erased earlier gains but hold around 99.8 on Monday, as mounting tensions between the US and China dulled risk appetite. The US said it will add 33 Chinese firms and institutions to an economic blacklist and the White House National Security Advisor Robert O’Brien warned about further sanctions if Beijing implements national security law in Hong Kong. The Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi said Americans should give up their “wishful thinking” of changing China and warned US actions are pushing the two countries to the brink of a new cold war.2020-05-25T13:29:00
