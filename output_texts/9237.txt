Title: US Personal Spending Rises More than Expected
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Rises More than ExpectedUnited States Personal SpendingPersonal spending in the United States increased 0.3 percent from a month earlier in April 2019, following an upwardly revised 1.1 percent gain in March. The latest reading came in above market expectations of a 0.2 percent rise, as an increase in consumption of nondurables and services offset a decline in durables spending.2019-05-31T12:31:00
