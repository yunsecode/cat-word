Title: US Private Sector Growth Hits 9-Month High: Markit
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Growth Hits 9-Month High: MarkitUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite Output Index rose to 55.7 in October 2017 from 54.8 in the previous month, a flash estimate showed. The reading pointed to the fastest pace of expansion in the private sector since January, as manufacturing growth hit a nine-month high (PMI at 54.5 from 53.1 in September) and service sector output rose the most in two months (PMI at 55.9 from 55.3 in September).2017-10-24T13:59:26
