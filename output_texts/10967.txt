Title: US Job Openings Hit All-Time High in January
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Hit All-Time High in JanuaryUnited States Job OffersThe number of job openings in the United States increased to an all time high of 6.312 million in January 2018 from a downwardly revised 5.667 million in the previous month and way above market expectations of 5.890 million. There were more open positions in professional and business services (215,000), transportation, warehousing, and utilities (113,000), construction (101,000), and several other industries. The number of job openings increased in the South, Midwest, and West regions.2018-03-16T14:03:59
