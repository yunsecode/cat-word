Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 8th Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 8th WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States declined by 2.546 million barrels in the week ended July 8th, 2016, following a 2.223 million fall in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 2.95 million drop. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks rose by 1.213 million barrels, after falling by 0.122 million in the previous week.2016-07-13T15:45:47
