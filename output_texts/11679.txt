Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most Since March
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most Since MarchUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States jumped by 5.888 million barrels in the week ended September 8th, 2017, following a 4.58 million rise in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 3.238 million increase. It is the biggest gain in inventories in six months. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks dropped by 8.428 million, compared to expectations of a 2.05 million fall. It is the biggest drop ever, much worse than market expectations of a 2050 thousand decline.2017-09-13T14:40:04
