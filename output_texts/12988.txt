Title: US New Home Sales Fall Less than Expected
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States fell 7.6 percent to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 609,000 in August of 2016, better than market expectations of an 8.8 percent decline. Figures for the previous month were revised up by 5,000 to 659,000, the highest since 2007.2016-09-26T14:07:04
