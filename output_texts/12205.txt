Title: US Retail Sales Drop 0.2% in March
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Drop 0.2% in MarchUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States decreased by 0.2 percent month-over-month in March 2017 after February figures were revised to a 0.3 percent drop which previously was reported as a 0.1 percent gain. The primary drivers of the decline were lower spending at auto dealerships and gas stations. It was the first consecutive two-month drop in more than two years. Sales was down in six of 13 major retail categories in March.2017-04-14T12:31:26
