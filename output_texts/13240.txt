Title: US Home Builder Optimism Falls in July
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Home Builder Optimism Falls in JulyUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexNAHB Housing Market Index in the United States decreased to 59 in July 2016 from 60 in June. The figure came in below market consensus, as sales expectations over the next six months dropped 3 points to 66 and current single-family home sales and buyer traffic each fell 1 point to 63 and 45, respectively. Regionally, on a three-month moving average, home builder confidence was unchanged in the Northeast, Midwest and South; while confidence in the West rose.2016-07-18T15:14:45
