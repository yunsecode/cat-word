Title: US Stock Futures Rise
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures RiseUnited States Stock MarketStock futures in the US were rising on Friday and pointed to gains at the open, after Wall Street suffered its worst session since the 1987 market crash the day before as NY Fed extraordinary funding actions and Trump's measures failed to ease coronavirus concerns. On Friday, Dow futures were up near 600 points, S&P 500 more than 70 and the Nasdaq ones more than 230 points around 5:00 AM NY time. On Thursday, the Dow Jones plummeted 2352 points or 10% to 21201; the S&P 500 lost 261 points or 9.5% to 2481; and the Nasdaq finished 750 points or 9.4% lower at 7202. All 3 key stock indexes have now entered bear market territory.2020-03-13T09:03:00
