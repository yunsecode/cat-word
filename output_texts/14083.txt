Title: US Existing Home Sales at 4-Month Low
Tag: Existing Home Sales

US Existing Home Sales at 4-Month LowUnited States Existing Home SalesSales of previously owned houses in the US shrank 4.8% to an annual rate of 5.31 million units in August, below market expectations and following a downwardly revised 5.58 million in July. The median sale price went up 4.7% yoy and the months’ worth of supply rose 0.3 to 5.2.
