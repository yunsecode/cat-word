Title: US MBA Mortgage Applications Rise 13.5% in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US MBA Mortgage Applications Rise 13.5% in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States increased 13.5 percent in the week ended January 11th 2019, following a 23.5 percent gain in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications rose 18.7 percent and applications to purchase a home went up 9.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was unchanged at 4.74 percent.2019-01-16T12:01:00
