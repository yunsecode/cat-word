Title: US Stocks Retreat from Records
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Retreat from Records United States Stock MarketUS stocks closed slightly lower on Monday with health care shares among the worst performers and despite a rise in oil prices while investors remained cautious ahead of retail sales data for July, due Friday. The Dow Jones fell 14 points, or less than 0.1%, to close at 18,529; the S&P 500 index finished down 2 points, or 0.1%, at 2,180; while the Nasdaq Composite dropped 8 points, or 0.2%, to end at 5,213.2016-08-08T21:13:10
