Title: US Stocks Surge on Earnings, Economy Reopening
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge on Earnings, Economy Reopening United States Stock MarketWall Street traded higher and the Dow Jones hit a 6-week high of 24,526 on Tuesday after earnings from 3M, Merck & Co, Pfizer and PepsiCo beat forecasts. Investors also welcomed news that more states including Ohio and Illinois plan to reopen. The number of coronavirus cases in the US surpassed 1 million and more than 56K people died although the rate of new infections and deaths has been slowing to levels not seen since March. Alphabet, Ford, Starbucks and Caterpillar are among companies still reporting earnings today. 2020-04-28T13:30:00
