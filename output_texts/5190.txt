Title: Wall Street Extends Losses in Afternoon Trading
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Extends Losses in Afternoon TradingUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks extended losses heading into the close on Monday, with the Dow Jones falling more than 170 points, the S&P 500 down 0.4% and the Nasdaq 0.2% as a rally in some commodity prices rekindled fears of persistent inflation and concerns over a global slowdown. WTI crude futures surged to a 7-year high, Chinese coal futures hit a fresh record and aluminum jumped to a new 13-year high. Also, market participants assessed a news report that China is expanding its crackdown to banks. At the same time, traders await the earnings season to kick-off with major banks set to report third-quarter results this week, while the FOMC minutes due Wednesday will be in the spotlight for any clues on the Fed's tapering timeline. Markets are anticipating some announcement next month.2021-10-11T19:39:00
