Title: US Inventories Growth Below Expectations
Tag: Business Inventories

US Inventories Growth Below ExpectationsUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the US rose 0.8 percent from a month earlier in August of 2022, picking up from the one-year low and downwardly revised 0.5 percent gain in the prior month but slightly below expectations of a 0.9 percent rise. Stock growth were seen higher for merchant wholesalers (1.3 percent vs 0.6 percent in July) and retailers (1.3 percent vs 1 percent), offsetting the decline for manufacturers (-0.1 percent vs 0 percent).
