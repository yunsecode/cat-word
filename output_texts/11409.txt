Title: US Pending Home Sales Decline 0.6% YoY
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Decline 0.6% YoYUnited States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned homes in the United States fell 0.6 percent year-on-year in October of 2017, following an upwardly revised 3.9 percent drop in September. Contracts went down in the West (-4.4 percent), the Northeast (-1.9 percent) and the Midwest (-0.9 percent) but rose 2 percent in the South. Compared to the previous month, pending home sales jumped 3.5 percent after a revised 0.4 percent fall in September and beating market expectations of a 1 percent rise due a recovery from hurricane-related effects.2017-11-29T15:10:50
