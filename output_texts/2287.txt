Title: Nasdaq 100 Hits 8-Week Low
Tag: Stock Market

Nasdaq 100 Hits 8-Week LowUnited States Stock MarketThe tech-heavy Nasdaq 100 tumbled below the 12,000 mark, a level not seen since July, as prospects of an even more aggressive tightening from the Federal Reserve sent Treasury yields higher, which, in turn, made tech and other growth stocks less attractive. In other corporate news, Adobe slid almost 20% after announcing that it will acquire Figma for $20 billion in a cash and shares deal.
