Title: US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised Higher
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised HigherUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI was revised slightly higher to 59.2 in January of 2021 from 59.1 int he preliminary estimate, pointing to record factory growth, due to accelerated expansions in output and new orders. Meanwhile, cost pressures intensified amid raw material shortages. Firms were able to partially pass on higher costs, however, with selling prices rising at the fastest pace since July 2008. Robust business confidence was reflected in the strongest rise in workforce numbers for two years, as pressure on capacity increased once again.2021-02-01T14:48:23.337
