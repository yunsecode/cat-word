Title: US Consumer Credit Growth Beats Expectations
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Growth Beats ExpectationsUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States increased by $16.21 billion in September 2020 after declining by $6.93 billion in the previous month, missing market expectations of a $9 billion increase. Total revolving credit increased by $2 billion after falling by $6.7 billion in August, whereas non-revolving credit increased by $12.2billion after rising by $2.8 billion. On an annual basis, consumer credit rose by 4.7 percent after declining 2.0 percent in the previous month.
