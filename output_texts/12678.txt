Title: US Stocks Close At Fresh Record High After ECB Decision
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close At Fresh Record High After ECB DecisionUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks rose on Thursday, with financials among the best performers after the European Central Bank kept interest rates unchanged and extended its quantitative easing programme until December 2017. The Dow Jones added 65 points, or 0.3%, to 19,615; the S&P 500 rose 5 point, or 0.2%, to 2,246; and the Nasdaq gained 24 points, or 0.4%, to 5,417.2016-12-08T21:13:21
