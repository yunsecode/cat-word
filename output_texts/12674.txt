Title: US Stocks Close At Record Highs, Best Week Since Election
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close At Record Highs, Best Week Since ElectionUnited States Stock MarketPost-election rally in US continued on Friday, led by consumer and health care stocks. The shares closed at fresh all-time highs: the Dow Jones added 142 points, or 0.7% to 19,757; the S&P 500 rose 13 points, or 0.6% to 2,259, the sixth straight day of gains and the best winning streak since June 2014; and the Nasdaq increased 27 points, or 0.5% to 5,444. On a week, the Dow Jones rose 3.1%; the S&P 500 went up 3% and the Nasdaq increased 3.6%.2016-12-09T21:39:33
