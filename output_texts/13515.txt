Wall Street Deeps in Negative Territory at the OpenUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened in the red on Wednesday amid a drop in oil prices after Kuwait workers ended a three-day strike and ahead of releases for existing home sales, crude oil inventories and earnings reports.
