Title: US New Home Sales Fall Less than Expected
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States shrank 1.5 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 662 thousand in April of 2018, compared to market expectations of a 2 percent drop. It follows a downwardly revised 2 percent jump in March.2018-05-23T14:12:08.46
