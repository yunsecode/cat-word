Title: US Stocks Open Higher on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Higher on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Monday amid rising oil prices while investors await Fed and Bank of Japan monetary policy decisions on Wednesday. The Dow Jones added 89 points or 0.5%, the S&P 500 gained 10 points or 0.5% and the Nasdaq rose 23 points or 0.4% around 7 minutes after the opening bell. 2016-09-19T13:44:27.107
