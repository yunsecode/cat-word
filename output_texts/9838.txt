Title: US Philadelphia Manufacturing Index Lowest Since 2016
Tag: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index

US Philadelphia Manufacturing Index Lowest Since 2016United States Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index in the United States fell to 9.4 in December of 2018 from 12.9 in November, well below market expectations of 15, and reaching the lowest level since August 2016. A slowdown was seen in shipments (10 from 21.6) and average workweek (0.5 from 6.3). Also, inventories dropped (-0.2 from 9.5) and prices paid eased (38 from 39.3). On the other hand, new orders (14.5 from 9.1), employment (18.3 from 16.3) and prices received (26.2 from 21.9) rose. Additionally, the 6-month outlook went up (31.7 from 27.2).2018-12-20T13:35:00
