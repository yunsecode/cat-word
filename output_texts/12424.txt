Title: US Stocks At Record High
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks At Record High United States Stock MarketThe three major stock indexes in the US finished at fresh record highs on Friday, as President Donald Trump’s promised to make an announcement on “phenomenal”  tax reform in the next few weeks. The Dow Jones added 97 points or 0.5% to 20,269; the S&P 500 gained 8 points or 0.4% to 2,316 and the Nasdaq increased 19 points or 0.3% to 5,734. On a week, the Dow Jones gained 1%, the S&P 500 went up 0.9% and the Nasdaq increased 1.2%.2017-02-10T21:19:15.223
