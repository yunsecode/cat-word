Title: Dollar Reverses Trend
Tag: Currency

Dollar Reverses TrendUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index reversed earlier gains to trade lower around 93.3 on Wednesday, as investors welcomed better-than-expected earnings from big US banks including Goldman Sachs and JP Morgan. Traders also digest a stalemate in US stimulus negotiations after Nancy Pelosi said the White House $1.8 trillion package proposal 'falls significantly short' of expectations. However, Joe Biden is seen beating President Trump in the November 3rd presidential election, raising hopes of a large stimulus plan. Still, mounting coronavirus infections and further restrictions in Europe, Brexit worries, and an attempt from the PBoC to depreciate the yuan are pressuring the dollar up.2020-10-14T13:00:00
