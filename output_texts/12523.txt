Title: US Housing Starts Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States jumped 11.3 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1226 thousand in December of 2016, beating market expectations of 1200 thousand. Multi-segment starts rebounded while single-family declined for the second month. Considering full 2016, housing starts rose 4.9 percent to 1166.4 thousand.2017-01-19T13:35:55
