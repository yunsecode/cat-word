Title: US MBA Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US MBA Mortgage Applications Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 5.6 percent in the week ended April 5th 2019, following a 18.6 percent gain in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance application dropped 11.4 percent while applications to purchase a home rose 0.5 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went up by 4bps to 4.40 percent.2019-04-10T11:01:52.457
