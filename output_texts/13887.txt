Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Post First Drop in 11 Weeks
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Post First Drop in 11 WeeksUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeInventories of crude oil in the United States decreased by 3.568 million barrels in the week ending December 4 of 2015, well below market expectations. It is the first fall since mid-September and the biggest in five months. Gasoline stocks grew by 786 thousand, also lower than expected.
