Title: US Fifth District Manufacturing Activity Index Rises
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

US Fifth District Manufacturing Activity Index RisesUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Manufacturing Activity Index in the US fifth district including the District of Columbia, Maryland, North Carolina, South Carolina, Virginia, and most of West Virginia increased to 0 in June of 2020 from -27 in May. Shipments were relatively flat (-1 vs -26 in May), more firms reported increases in new orders (5 vs -35), and firms generally reported continued declines in employment (-5 vs -16). The index for local business conditions rose notably in June, indicating optimism among firms after three months of some of the most negative readings on record for that series. Manufacturers were also optimistic, overall, that conditions would improve in the next six months.2020-06-23T14:03:00
