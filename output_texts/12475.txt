Title: US Stocks Fall on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red for the second day on Tuesday amid mixed earnings reports for Pfizer, Exxon Mobil, Under Armour and Xerox while worries over Trumps' immigration policies persist. The Dow Jones went down 67 points or 0.3%, the S&P 500 fell 5 points or 0.2% and the Nasdaq lost 17 points or 0.3% around 6 minutes after the opening bell. 2017-01-31T14:44:28.927
