Title: US Jobless Claims Fall to 278K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall to 278KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 16,000 to 278,000 in the week ended May 14th compared to a 15-month high of 294,000 in the previous period. Claims fell for the first time in four weeks, slightly higher than market expectations of 275,000. It is the 63rd consecutive week initial claims are below 300,000, the level associated with a healthy jobs market. 2016-05-19T13:35:51
