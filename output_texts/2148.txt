Title: Dollar Pares Gains, Still Holds Close to 20-Year Highs
Tag: Currency

Dollar Pares Gains, Still Holds Close to 20-Year HighsUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index paused a four-day rally to traded around 113 on Tuesday, after touching 113.5 early in the session, which was the highest level so far this month. Bond yields also retreated as traders take a wait-and-see approach ahead of FOMC minutes release tomorrow, the highly-anticipated CPI report Thursday and the kick-off of the earnings season which will provide more clarity on the Fed's hawkish stance. Still, the greenback remains close to levels not seen since mid-2002 as the Fed has been raising rates aggressively and is expected to deliver another 75bps rate hike next month. At the same time, the US economy remains more robust and resilient than others, despite high inflation and increasing borrowing costs.
