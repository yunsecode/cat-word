Title: US Jobless Claims Down to 12-Week Low
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Down to 12-Week LowUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending November 30, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 298,000, a decrease of 23,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 321,000. The 4-week moving average was 322,250, a decrease of 10,750 from the previous week's revised average of 333,000.2013-12-05T13:36:50.943
