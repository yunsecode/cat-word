Title: Foreigners Buy $68.9 Billion of US Assets
Tag: Capital Flows

Foreigners Buy $68.9 Billion of US AssetsUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought $68.9 billion of U.S. assets, including short-dated instruments in October of 2015, after selling a revised $172.7 billion in September. However, foreigners sold $16.6 billion of long-term U.S. securities, including government and corporate, from purchases of $33.6 billion in the previous month. Foreign investors also sold $55.2 billion of US Treasuries.2015-12-15T21:16:02.593
