Title: Chicago Business Barometer Below Forecasts
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago Business Barometer Below ForecastsUnited States Chicago PmiThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer edged up to 57.6 in April of 2018 from 57.4 in the previous month. It compares with market expectations of 58. Business activity continued to rise at a solid pace in April, with growth in firms’ operations up for the first time this year, albeit marginally. Three of the five Barometer components fell on the month, with only Production and Supplier Deliveries finding room to grow.2018-04-30T14:16:17.31
