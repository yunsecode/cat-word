Title: US Stock Futures Fall
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures FallUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures traded lower in pre-market on Monday, as investors focused back on the coronavirus outbreak after the US death toll climbed above 2,000 over the weekend. Markets are expected to remain volatile on doubts whether additional stimulus measures by the US would be enough to offset the economic impact of the coronavirus outbreak. Dow Jones futures dropped 344 points or 1.7%; The S&P 500 futures fell 42 points or 1.7%; The Nasdaq 100 futures went down 84 points or 1.1%.2020-03-29T22:07:00
