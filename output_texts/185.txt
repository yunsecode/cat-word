Title: US Export Prices Unexpectedly Fall in May
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Unexpectedly Fall in MayUnited States Export Prices MoMExport prices in the US dropped 1.9 percent month-over-month in May 2023, following a revised 0.1 percent decrease in April and defying the market expectation of a flat reading. It was the largest monthly decline in export prices since last December, led by a 2.1 percent fall in agricultural export prices and a 1.8 percent drop in the cost of nonagricultural exports. On a yearly basis, export prices tumbled 10.1 percent, the largest decline since the series was first published in September 1984.2023-06-15T12:39:55.58
