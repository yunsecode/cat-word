Title: US Weekly Jobless Claims Fall More than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Weekly Jobless Claims Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing new claims for unemployment benefits decreased by 18 thousand to 215 thousand in the week ended February 26th, from a revised 233 thousand in the previous period and compared with market expectations of 225. It was the lowest number since the start of the year amid strong demand for labor as Omicron restrictions eased. On a non-seasonally adjusted basis, initial claims fell by 21,285 from the previous week to 194,693, with notable decreases being recorded in Michigan (-9,200); California (-4,673); Florida (-2,388); Ohio (-2,147) and Illinois (-2,089). The 4-week moving average which removes week-to-week volatility was 230.5 thousand, a decrease of 6 thousand and continuing claims were at 1.476 million.2022-03-03T13:32:00
