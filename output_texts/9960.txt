Title: US Housing Starts Rise Slightly Less than Expected
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Rise Slightly Less than ExpectedUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the US increased 1.5 percent from a month earlier to an annualized rate of 1,228 thousand in October of 2018, following an upwardly revised 5.5 percent drop in September and compared with market expectations of a 1.6 percent rise. The multi-family segment led the rise while construction of single-family houses declined for a second month. Starts rebounded in the South after being hurt by Hurricane Florence in September but fell in the Northeast and the West. 2018-11-20T13:33:00
