Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 1.9 percent in the week ended April 6th 2018, following a 3.3 percent drop in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went down 1.7 percent and applications to purchase a home dropped 2 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate decreased by 3bps to 4.66 percent.2018-04-11T11:03:39.257
