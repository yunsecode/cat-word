Title: US International Capital Inflows Lose Steam
Tag: Capital Flows

US International Capital Inflows Lose SteamUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors bought USD 125.3 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments, in April 2020 after buying an upwardly revised USD 355.3 billion in March. Meanwhile, foreigners sold USD 176.7 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate after selling USD 298.9 billion in the previous month. Overseas investors sold USD 128.4 billion of Treasuries in April after dumping USD 111.6 billion in the previous month.
