Title: US Personal Income Rises More than Expected
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Rises More than ExpectedUnited States Personal IncomePersonal income in the United States increased 0.4 percent from a month earlier in November of 2022, following a 0.7 percent rise in October and above market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. The increase primarily reflected increases in compensation and personal income receipts on assets. The increase in compensation reflected increases in private wages and salaries in both services-producing industries and goods-producing industries.2022-12-23T13:32:00
