Title: US GDP Growth Slows to 1.5% in Q3
Tag: GDP Growth Rate

US GDP Growth Slows to 1.5% in Q3United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy expanded 1.50 percent in the third quarter of 2015 over the previous quarter, below market expectations of 1.6 percent growth. The increase reflected positive contributions from personal consumption expenditures (PCE), state and local government spending, nonresidential fixed investment, exports, and residential fixed investment that were partly offset by negative contributions from private inventory investment. Imports, which are a subtraction in the calculation of GDP, increased.2015-10-29T12:34:12
