Title: US Mortgage Applications Rise 8.2%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Rise 8.2%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States went up 8.2 percent in the week ended February 12th 2016 from the previous period, following a 9.3 percent surge last week as fixed 30-year mortgage rates declined further by 8 bps to 3.83 percent, the lowest since mid- April 2015, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications jumped 16 percent while applications to purchase a home fell 3.7 percent.2016-02-17T12:05:51.773
