Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Unexpectedly in Latest Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise Unexpectedly in Latest WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose unexpectedly by 1.643 million barrels in the week ended March 23rd 2018, after a 2.622 million drop in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 0.287 million decline. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories fell sharply by 3.472 million barrels after decreasing by 1.693 million in the previous week, while markets were expecting a smaller 1.954 million drop.2018-03-28T14:36:22.837
