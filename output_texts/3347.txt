Title: US Core CPI Rises More than Expected
Tag: Core Inflation Rate MoM

US Core CPI Rises More than ExpectedUnited States Core Inflation Rate MoMThe CPI index for all items less food and energy in the US rose 0.6% month-over-month in April 2022, accelerating from a 0.3% increase the prior month and above market expectations of 0.4%. Along with indexes for shelter (0.5%), airline fares (18.6%), and new vehicles (1.1%), prices also increased for medical care (0.4%), recreation (0.4%), and household furnishings and operations. The annual core inflation rate eased to 6.2% from 1982-highs of 6.5%, but above market forecasts of 6%.2022-05-11T12:33:00
