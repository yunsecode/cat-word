Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Rises Again
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Rises AgainUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury note held around 1.5% on Thursday, amid global coronavirus vaccine rollout and prospects of a swift economic recovery. President Biden recently said the US will have enough vaccines for every American by the end of May, earlier than July. Treasuries have been rallying since the end of January when the Democrats won control of the US Senate, amid inflationary concerns arising from strong economic activity and further fiscal support. The yield remains much lower than 1.61% touched later in February but is close to levels not seen in 1 year, although central banks around the world pledged not to tight monetary policy anytime soon. Fed Chair Powell recently reinforced the US economy needs support and the Fed is committed to the current policy stance as the economic recovery remains uneven while inflation is seen rising above 2% for some time.2021-03-03T14:10:00
