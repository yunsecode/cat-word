Title: Chicago PMI Below Expectations in August
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago PMI Below Expectations in AugustUnited States Chicago PmiThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer fell 4.3 points to 51.5 in August of 2016 from 55.8 in July. It is the lowest figure in three months, led by a decline in order backlogs and a deceleration in new orders and production. In contrast, the employment subindex hit a 16-month high. 2016-08-31T14:58:34
