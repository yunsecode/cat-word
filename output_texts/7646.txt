Title: US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Drop
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly DropUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil stocks fell by 4.389 million barrels in the week ended September 11th, 2020, following a 2.032 million increase in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 1.271 million rise, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories were down by 0.381 million barrels, while markets had forecast a smaller 0.160 million decline.2020-09-16T14:32:59.95
