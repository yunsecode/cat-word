Title: US Homebuilder Confidence At Near 12-Year High
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Homebuilder Confidence At Near 12-Year High United States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States rose to 71 in March 2017 from 65 in the previous month and above market consensus of 65. It was the highest reading since June 2005. Current single-family home sales increased 7 points to 78; sales expectations over the next six months went up 5 points to 78; and buyer traffic jumped 8 points to 54. 2017-03-15T14:02:51
