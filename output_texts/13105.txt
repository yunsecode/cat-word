Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 2.1 percent in the week ended August 19th 2016 from the prior period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It follows a 4 percent drop in the week before. Refinance applications declined 3.2 percent and applications to purchase a home edged down 0.3 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates increased 3bps to 3.67 percent.2016-08-24T12:08:18.553
