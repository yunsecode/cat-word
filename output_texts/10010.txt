Title: US Job Openings Fall from Record High
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Fall from Record HighUnited States Job OffersThe number of job openings dropped by 284,000 to 7.009 million in September 2018 from August's all-time high of 7.293 million and slightly below market expectations of 7.1 million. The job opening level declined by 188,000 for total private and by 96,000 in government. By industry, the largest decreases in job opening were recorded in professional and business services (-118,000), finance and insurance (-82,000), and state and local government, excluding education (-67,000); while openings in health care and social assistance increased by 71,000.
