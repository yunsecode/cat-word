US Stocks Rally on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed higher on the last trading day of the quarter amid a rebound in tech shares. The Dow Jones gained 255 points or 1.1% to 24103; the S&P 500 added 36 points or 1.4% to 2641; the Nasdaq closed 114 points or 1.6% higher at 7063. Considering the first quarter of the year, the Dow slumped 2.5% and the S&P 500 fell 1.2% while the Nasdaq gained 2.3%.2018-03-29T20:31:55.487
