Title: US Stocks Trade Higher After Jobs Report
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Higher After Jobs ReportUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Friday after the jobs report for January showed the economy added 227,000 payrolls, beating expectations of 175,000. The Dow Jones added 102 points or 0.5%, the S&P 500 gained 10 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq traded 19 points or 0.3% higher around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2017-02-03T14:47:16.747
