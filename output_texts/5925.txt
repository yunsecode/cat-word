Title: US Treasury Yields Inch Up
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Inch UpUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury yield rose further to 1.63% on Thursday after fresh economic data pointed to a strong economic recovery and persistent price pressures. Private payrolls rose more than expected by the most in 11 months, jobless claims fell more than expected to 385K and Services PMIs pointed to record growth in the services sector. Early in the week, manufacturing PMIs showed American factory growth beat expectations despite labour supply constraints and elevates price pressures. Last week, PCE inflation soared to levels not seen since the 1990s. The jobs report due Friday will provide a further update on the US labour market.2021-06-03T14:23:00
