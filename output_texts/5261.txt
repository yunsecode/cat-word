Title: Dollar Trades Near 1-Week Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar Trades Near 1-Week LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index edged down to a near one-week low of 93.2 on Monday, as traders continued to digest the latest FOMC statement and prospects of slower growth in the US. On the data front, initial claims rose for a 2nd week last week while flash Markit PMIs came below forecasts. Meanwhile, fears of widespread contagion from China Evergrande eased after the PBoC today injected a net of CNY 100 billion into the financial system, adding to the net CNY 320 billion last week, the most since January. At the same time, it is reported that several local governments in China have set up special custodian accounts for Evergrande property projects to protect funds earmarked for housing projects from being diverted.2021-09-27T03:37:00
