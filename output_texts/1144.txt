Title: US Capacity Utilization Rate Falls to Lowest since 2021
Tag: Capacity Utilization

US Capacity Utilization Rate Falls to Lowest since 2021United States Capacity UtilizationCapacity utilization in rate in the US fell to 78.3% in January of 2023 from a downwardly revised 78.4% in December and well below forecasts of 79%. It is the lowest rate since September of 2021 and 1.3 percentage points below its long-run average. Capacity utilization for utilities fell to a record low of 68.6% while the operating rate for mining rose 1.6 percentage points to 89% and the one for manufacturing increased 0.6 percentage point to 77.7%.2023-02-15T14:31:18.437
