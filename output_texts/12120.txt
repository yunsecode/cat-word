US Stocks Edge Higher To Book Weekly GainsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately in the green on Friday, as non farm payrolls  increased by 211K in April, higher than a downwardly revised 79K in March and beating expectations of 185K. Also, commodities gains boosted mining stocks. The Dow Jones closed up 55 points, or 0.3 percent, to 21007. The S&P 500 gained 10 points, or 0.4 percent, to 2399 and rose to a new high. The Nasdaq jumped 25 points, or 0.4 percent, to 6101.
