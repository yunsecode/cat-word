Title: Dollar Index Remains Near 97
Tag: Currency

Dollar Index Remains Near 97United States CurrencyThe dollar index remained near 97 on Monday closing on the strongest level since July 2020 as investors resumed flight to safety as fierce fighting continues in Ukraine. Ukrainian officials are holding talks with Russian counterparts at the Belarus border, but Ukraine voiced skepticism about the talks. During the weekend, G7 nations agreed to exclude some Russian banks from the SWIFT and today Biden’s administration banned U.S. people and companies from doing business with the Bank of Russia, the Russian National Wealth Fund and the Ministry of Finance.2022-02-28T13:01:00
