Title: US Imports Jump to 1-Year High
Tag: Imports

US Imports Jump to 1-Year High United States ImportsImports to the United States rose 1.5 percent from the previous month to USD 256.6 billion in December of 2020, the highest level in a year. Goods purchases advanced by USD 3.1 billion to USD 217.7 billion, led by imports of supplies and materials (USD 3.0 billion) and automotive vehicles, parts, and engines (USD 2.0 billion). Imports of services increased by USD 0.7 billion to USD 38.9 billion, boosted by travel (USD 0.5 billion) and transport (USD 0.2 billion). For 2020, imports were USD 2,810.6 billion, down USD 294.5 billion from 2019. 2021-02-05T13:37:00
