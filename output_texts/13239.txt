Title: US Net Capital Outflow $11B in May
Tag: Capital Flows

US Net Capital Outflow $11B in MayUnited States Capital FlowsOverseas investors sold $11 billion of US assets, including short-dated instruments in May of 2016, after buying an upwardly revised $93.1 billion in April. Foreign investors sold $18.3 billion of US Treasuries, after selling $74.6 billion in April. Foreigners bought $41.1 billion of long-term US securities, including government and corporate, after selling $67.4 billion in the previous month. 2016-07-18T21:18:07.973
