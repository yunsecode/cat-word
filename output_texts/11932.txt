Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Up In June
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised Up In JuneUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States was revised higher to 95.1 in June of 2017 from a preliminary reading of 94.5 as current conditions were assessed more positive. However, it is the lowest reading in seven months, on lower future expectations, final figures showed. 2017-06-30T14:11:43
