Title: US Home Builder Sentiment Steady in September
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Home Builder Sentiment Steady in SeptemberUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index for the US was unchanged at 67 in September of 2018, the same as in August and slightly above market expectations of 66.
The subindex for current single-family home sales edged up to 74 from 73 in August; the gauge for home sales over the next six months went up to 74 from 72 while prospective buyers was flat at 49.2018-09-18T14:07:52.363
