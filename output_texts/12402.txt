Title: US Jobless Claims Rise Less Than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise Less Than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 5 thousand to 239 thousand in the week ended February 11th 2017, from the previous week's unrevised level of 234 thousand and below market expectations of 245 thousand. Claims have been below 300 thousand for 102 straight weeks, the longest streak since 1970.2017-02-16T13:32:56
