Title: US Futures Little Changed to Start the Week
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Little Changed to Start the WeekUnited States Stock MarketUS futures were around the flatline on Monday as investors await the earnings season to gather pace this week after mixed earnings reports from big banks on Friday while Treasury yields continue to march higher amid prospects of a faster Fed tightening. Meanwhile, the PBoC accelerated its pace of policy easing after fresh figures showed the Chinese economy slowed in Q4 last year. Last week, both the Dow (-0.9%) and the S&P (-0.3%) extended losses for a 2nd consecutive week and the Nasdaq declined for a third week (-0.3%). US stock and bond markets are closed on Monday for the Martin Luther King Jr. holiday.2022-01-17T09:09:36.443
