Title: US Corporate Profits Down 8.4% in Q4
Tag: Corporate Profits

US Corporate Profits Down 8.4% in Q4United States Corporate ProfitsCorporate profits in the United States decreased by 8.4 percent to 1380.4 USD Billion in the fourth quarter of 2015 from 1507.7 USD Billion in the previous period, the lowest since the first quarter of 2011. For the whole 2015, corporate profits fell 5.1 percent after 0.6 drop in the previous year.2016-03-25T13:08:52.29
