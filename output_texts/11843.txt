Title: US Jobless Claims Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 10 thousand to 244 thousand in the week ended July 22nd from the previous week's revised level of 234 thousand and above market expectations of 241 thousand. Meanwhile, the 4-week moving average, which removes week-to-week volatility, was unchanged at 244 thousand. 2017-07-27T12:31:59.383
