Title: US Stocks Edge Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge HigherUnited States Stock MarketWall Street edged higher on Friday 24 2019, as investors’ nerves eased on US President Donald Trump prediction that ongoing trade disputes will end soon. The Dow Jones posted its longest streak of weekly losses since 2011, despite climbing on Friday 95 points or 0.4%. The S&P 500 gained 4 points or 0.1%. The Nasdaq inched up 9 points or 0.1%. 2019-05-24T20:24:31.02
