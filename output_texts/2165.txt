Title: Dollar Extends Upside Momentum
Tag: Currency

Dollar Extends Upside MomentumUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index moved toward the 112 mark, rebounding further from a two-week low of around 110 as investors adjusted their portfolios for an aggressive tightening of monetary policy. While signs that the job market is cooling sparked some hopes for a policy pivot, recent hawkish remarks from several policymakers suggest the opposite. Minneapolis Fed President Neel Kashkari was the latest policymaker to signal that further rate hikes are needed to bring down inflation to its 2% target. Now, monthly non-farm payrolls and unemployment rate figures, due on Friday, will be at the top of markets' radar for clues about the central bank rate path. 2022-10-06T15:07:23.087
