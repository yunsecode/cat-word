Title: US Inflation Rate Edges Down to 2.1%
Tag: Inflation Rate

US Inflation Rate Edges Down to 2.1%United States Inflation RateConsumer prices in the United States increased 2.1 percent year-on-year in December of 2017, easing from a 2.2 percent rise in November. Figures came below market expectations of 2.2 percent amid a slowdown in gasoline and fuel prices. Still, core inflation edged up to 1.8 percent and the monthly rate increased to 0.3 percent, the highest in eleven months amid rising cost of rents, healthcare and autos.2018-01-12T13:33:08
