Title: US Crude Inventories Fall Much Less than Expected: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Fall Much Less than Expected: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories dropped by 0.562 million barrels in the week ended December 18th, 2020, following a 3.135 million fall in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 3.186 million decline, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories were down by 1.125 million barrels, the first fall in six weeks, while markets had forecast a 1.21 million rise.2020-12-23T15:35:00
