Title: US Jobless Claims Fall to 336K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall to 336KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending February 15, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 336,000, a decrease of 3,000 from the previous week's unrevised figure of 339,000. The 4-week moving average was 338,500, an increase of 1,750 from the previous week's unrevised average of 336,750.2014-02-20T13:34:07.343
