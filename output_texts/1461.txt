Title: Mortgage Rates Fall in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Rate

Mortgage Rates Fall in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage RateThe average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($647,200 or less) in the US fell by 16bps to 6.42% in the first week of January 2023 from 6.58% in the last week of 2022,  closing in on the lowest level since mid-September, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association (MBA) showed. “Mortgage rates declined last week as markets reacted to data showing a weakening economy and slowing wage growth. All loan types in the survey saw a decline in rates,” said Joel Kan, an MBA economist. In January 2022, interest rates were much lower at 3.52% and in January 2020, before the covid pandemic, the rate was 3.91%.2023-01-11T12:13:00
