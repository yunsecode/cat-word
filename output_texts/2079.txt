Title: US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 1.725 million barrels in the week ended October 14th, compared with market expectations of a 1.38-million-barrel increase,  the latest US Energy Information Administration report showed. Also, gasoline stocks went down by 0.114 million, less than market expectations of a 1.114 million draw. On the other hand, distillate stockpiles, which include diesel and heating oil, increased by 0.124 million, versus forecasts for a 2.167 million drop. Crude stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, rose by 0.583 million barrels, after a 0.309 million decrease.2022-10-19T14:34:00
