Title: 10-Year Treasury Yield at 3-Month Low
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

10-Year Treasury Yield at 3-Month LowUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note was at 1.5% on Thursday, hovering at closing levels not seen in 3 months, as investors await the highly anticipated CPI data and claims report for further clues on the Fed's next steps and the possibility of an early tapering. Meanwhile, the $38 billion auction of 10-year notes saw strong demand on Wednesday,  with a bid-to-cover ratio of 2.58, the highest in a year. On Tuesday, an auction of $58 billion worth of three-year notes also showed good demand with a high yield of 0.325%.2021-06-10T07:09:00
