Title: Philadelphia Manufacturing Growth Slows Less than Expected
Tag: Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index

Philadelphia Manufacturing Growth Slows Less than ExpectedUnited States Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Philadelphia Fed Manufacturing Index in the US decreased to 23.1 in February of 2021 from 26.5 in January but above market expectations of 20. The survey’s current indicators for general activity, new orders, and shipments declined from January’s readings but remained elevated. Additionally, employment increases were more widespread this month. Most future indexes moderated this month but continue to indicate that firms expect growth over the next six months. 2021-02-18T13:42:00
