Title: US Productivity Falls 3% in Q4
Tag: Productivity

US Productivity Falls 3% in Q4United States ProductivityHourly output per worker in nonfarm sector fell at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 3 percent qoq in last three months of 2015, following a downwardly revised 2.1 percent gain in the previous period and much worse than market expectations, preliminary estimates showed. It is the fastest drop since the first quarter of 2014 as GDP growth slowed sharply and hiring accelerated. Year-on-year, productivity grew 0.3 percent. Considering full 2015, average productivity increased 0.6 percent from 2014.2016-02-04T13:38:28
