Title: US Stocks Trade Mostly Lower
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Mostly LowerUnited States Stock MarketWall Street swung between gains and losses in another volatile session on Thursday as investors digest strong corporate results and expectations for higher inflation, borrowing costs and resilient growth. The Dow Jones declined 159 points or 0.6%; the S&P 500 fell 10 points or 0.4% and the Nasdaq traded 18 points or 0.3% lower around 30 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-02-08T15:03:55.09
