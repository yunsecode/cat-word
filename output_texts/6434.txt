Title: Chicago Business Barometer Jumps to 2-1/2-Year High
Tag: Chicago PMI

Chicago Business Barometer Jumps to 2-1/2-Year HighUnited States Chicago PMIThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer in the US increased by 6.8 points to 66.3 in March 2021, the highest level since July 2018 and above market expectations of 60.7. Among the main five indicators, production saw the largest gain (up 10.1 points to a three-year high), followed by new orders (up 7.1 points) while order backlogs saw the biggest drop (down 6 points). Through the first quarter the index gained 4.4 points to 63.2, the strongest reading since Q3 2018.2021-03-31T13:56:00
