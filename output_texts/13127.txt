US Industrial Production Grows The Most in 20 MonthsUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial output in the United States went up 0.7 percent in July from June of 2016, following a downwardly revised 0.4 percent gain in the previous period and better than market expectations of a 0.3 percent increase. It is the biggest rise since November of 2014 boosted by a 0.5 percent growth in manufacturing.2016-08-16T14:24:11
