Wall Street Opens Lower On OilUnited States Stock MarketUS equities opened in the red led by losses in energy stocks due to a retreat in oil prices as OPEC meeting ended without any deal on production freeze. The Dow Jones lost 0.27% or 49 points, the S&P 500 slid 0.25% or 5 points and the Nasdaq fell 0.24% or 12 points around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2016-06-02T14:49:16.65
