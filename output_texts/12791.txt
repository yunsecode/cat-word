Title: Dow Closes at Record High, Best Week in 5 Years
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Closes at Record High, Best Week in 5 YearsUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones added 40 points or 0.2% to close at a record high of 18847.66 on Friday, recovering from earlier losses. On the week, the Dow Jones rallied 5.4%, the biggest gain since 2011 as Trump’s victory raised expectations of a fiscal boost. Meanwhile, the Nasdaq went up 28 points or 0.5% for a 3.8% rise on the week; the S&P 500 lost 3 points or 0.1%, dragged down by energy shares on lower oil prices. However, it finished the week 3.8% higher. 2016-11-11T21:27:40.36
