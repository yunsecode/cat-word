Title: US Producer Prices Fall 0.1% MoM in January
Tag: Producer Price Inflation MoM

US Producer Prices Fall 0.1% MoM in JanuaryUnited States Producer Price Inflation MoMProducer prices for final demand in the US fell by 0.1 percent in January 2019, the same as in the previous month and compared with market expectations of a 0.1 percent gain. Price of goods dropped 0.8 percent, the largest decrease since September 2015, driven by lower cost of energy (-3.8 percent) and food (-1.7 percent). Meanwhile, cost of services rose 0.3 percent, after being unchanged in the previous month. The core index, which excludes food and energy, increased 0.3 percent in January, rebounding from a 0.1 percent fall in December and above market consensus of 0.2 percent. On a yearly basis, producer prices went up 2.0 percent and the core index increased 2.6 percent.2019-02-14T13:34:00
