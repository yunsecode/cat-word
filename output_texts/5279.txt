Title: US Shares Poised for Higher Open
Tag: Stock Market

US Shares Poised for Higher OpenUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures were higher on Wednesday, following a volatile session the day before, as traders await the outcome of the FOMC meeting later in the day. Adding to the positive mood, Democrats in the US House of Representatives passed a bill on Tuesday to fund the government through December 3rd and suspend the nation's borrowing limit until the end of 2022. Meanwhile, China's Evergrande crisis was temporarily averted, after the company said it would pay the bond interest due Thursday.  On Tuesday, the Dow Jones fell 51 points or 0.15% to end at 33,920. The S&P 500 dropped 4 points or less than 0.1% to 4,354. The Nasdaq was up 33 points or 0.22% to 14,746.2021-09-22T06:47:00
