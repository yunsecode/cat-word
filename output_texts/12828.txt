Title: US Services PMI at 11-Month High: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services PMI at 11-Month High: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe final IHS/Markit US Services PMI came in at 54.8 in October of 2016, unchanged from preliminary estimates and higher than 52.3 in September. It is the best reading since November, boosted by a robust increase in new work while job creation remained subdued and input price inflation accelerated to a 15-month high.  2016-11-03T13:58:58.53
