Title: US Jobless Rate Falls to Over Five-Decade Low
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Falls to Over Five-Decade LowUnited States Unemployment RateThe unemployment rate in the United States edged down to 3.4 percent in April 2023, matching a 50-year low of 3.4 percent seen in January and below market expectations of 3.6 percent. The number of unemployed people decreased by 182  thousand to 5.657 million and employment levels rose by 139 thousand to 161.031 million. Meanwhile, the labor force participation rate was unchanged at 62.6 percent.2023-05-05T12:32:00
