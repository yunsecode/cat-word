Title: US Export Prices Dip 0.2% In June
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Dip 0.2% In JuneUnited States Export Prices MoMPrices for US exports fell 0.2 percent month-over-month in June 2017 after a downwardly revised 0.5 percent drop in May while markets were expecting prices to remain unchanged. It was the second straight month of decline in export prices, as cost of agricultural goods went down 1.5 percent (-1.6 percent in May), while that of non-agricultural products recorded no change (-0.4 percent in May). Year-on-year, export prices advanced 0.6 percent after rising 1.5 percent in May.2017-07-18T12:33:03
