Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise For 2nd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise For 2nd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 2.347 million barrels in the week ended January 13th, 2017, following a 4.097 million increase in the previous week and compared with market expectations of a 0.342 million decline. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks rose by 5.951 million, compared to expectations of a 2.023 million increase.
