Title: US Corporate Profits Revised Lower in Q3
Tag: Corporate Profits

US Corporate Profits Revised Lower in Q3United States Corporate ProfitsCorporate profits in the United States rose 3.4 percent to a fresh record high of USD 2.52 trillion in the third quarter of 2021, slowing from a 10.5 percent jump in the previous period and compared with preliminary estimates of 4.3 percent. Undistributed profits climbed 5.3	 percent to $1.08 trillion and net cash flow with inventory valuation adjustment, the internal funds available to corporations for investment, increased 1.8 percent to $3.14 trillion. Also, net dividends increased 2.0 percent to $1.44 trillion.2021-12-22T13:34:00
