Title: DXY Firms Above 102.5
Tag: Currency

DXY Firms Above 102.5United States CurrencyThe dollar index firmed up above 102.5 on Friday and was set for an over 1% weekly gain, the biggest since February, due to concerns over the US debt ceiling and regional banking stress, causing investors to seek safety in the currency. Additionally, there is speculation that the Federal Reserve may need to maintain higher interest rates for a longer period, following comments from Fed Governor Michelle Bowman that rates may need to be raised further if inflation persists. The latest economic data also showed that US consumer sentiment dropped unexpectedly to a six-month low in May.2023-05-12T16:28:00
