US Mortgage Applications Decrease for 2nd Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 4.9 percent in the week ending December 15th 2017, following a 2.3 percent drop in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to purchase a home shrank 5.5 percent and refinance applications declined 3.2 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went down by 4bps 4.16 percent, the lowest in two months.2017-12-20T12:05:08.28
