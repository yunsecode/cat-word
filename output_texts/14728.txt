U.S. Annual Inflation Down To 1.8 Percent in NovemberUnited States Inflation RateThe Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) declined 0.3 percent in November on a seasonally adjusted basis, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on December 14.
