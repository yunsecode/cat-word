Title: Dow Jones, S&P Bounce Back in Afternoon Trading
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Jones, S&P Bounce Back in Afternoon TradingUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones and the S&P 500 reversed earlier losses to trade higher on Friday, hovering near record levels helped by gains in defensive companies although traders remained cautious amid renewed concerns over slower global growth. A gauge of New York state manufacturing moderated in August after expanding at a record pace a month earlier, and China's economic activity slowed more than expected in July. Meantime, traders await more clarity from the Fed on the tapering timeline with FOMC minutes due on Wednesday and a town hall meeting with Chair Jerome Powell on Tuesday. Also, retail sales and industrial production due this week will provide an update on the American recovery.2021-08-16T18:07:00
