Title: ISM NY Index Highest Since 2015
Tag: Ism New York Index

ISM NY Index Highest Since 2015United States Ism New York IndexThe ISM New York Current Business Conditions in the United States increased to 62.8 in July of 2017 from 55.5 in June, reaching the highest value since October of 2015. Increases were seen for quantity of purchases (52.8 from 50) and expected revenues (69.4 from 60) while prices paid slowed (52.8 from 60.3). On the other hand, employment (37.8 from 49) and current revenues (44.4 from 46.9) fell more. The six-month outlook declined for the fourth straight time to 58.1 from 64.5.
