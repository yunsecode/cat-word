Title: US Employment Costs Rise the Least in 3 Years
Tag: Employment Cost Index

US Employment Costs Rise the Least in 3 YearsUnited States Employment Cost IndexCompensation costs for civilian workers in the US increased 0.5 percent on quarter in the three months to June of 2020, easing from a 0.8 percent gain in the previous period. It was the smallest gain since Q2 2017 and compares to market expectations of a 0.6 percent growth. Wages and salaries rose 0.4 percent, below 0.9 percent in the first quarter; while benefit costs went up 0.8 percent, faster than 0.4 percent. 2020-07-31T12:32:00
