Title: US Jobless Claims Rise by 16K in Latest Week
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise by 16K in Latest WeekUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsIn the week ending March 29, the advance figure for seasonally adjusted initial claims was 326,000, an increase of 16,000 from the previous week's revised figure of 310,000. The 4-week moving average was 319,500, an increase of 250 from the previous week's revised average of 319,250.2014-04-03T13:33:16.04
