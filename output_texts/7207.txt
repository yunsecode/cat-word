Title: US Crude Inventories Fall Unexpectedly
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Inventories Fall UnexpectedlyUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories dropped by 0.754 million barrels in the week ended November 20th, 2020, following a 0.768 million increase in the previous period and compared with market expectations of a 0.127 million advance, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories were up by 2.2 million barrels, while markets had forecast a smaller 0.6 million rise.2020-11-25T16:02:18.067
