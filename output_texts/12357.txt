Title: US Personal Income Rises More Than Expected In January
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Rises More Than Expected In JanuaryUnited States Personal IncomePersonal income in the United States rose by 0.4 percent month-over-month in January 2017, following a 0.3 percent increase in December and beating market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. Wages and salaries went up by 0.4 percent, the same pace as in December, and personal current transfer receipts rose by 0.8 percent after growing by 0.3 percent in the previous month. Meanwhile, contributions for government social insurance, a subtraction in the calculation of personal income, climbed 1.6 percent after an increase of 0.3 percent in December.2017-03-01T13:38:03.68
