Title: US Stocks Surge To Record Highs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge To Record HighsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed sharply higher and at new records on Wednesday following US president Trump's promises to cut taxes and spend $1 trillion on infrastructure in his first address to Congress. The Dow Jones surged 303 points, or 1.5% to 21,115.55. The S&P 500 rose 32 points, or 1.4% to 2,395.95. The Nasdaq increased 79 points, or 1.4% to 5,904.03. 2017-03-01T21:05:19
