Title: US Stocks Rise Ahead of Key Economic Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise Ahead of Key Economic DataUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow added almost 300 points on Monday, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 were up 0.9% and 1.3%, respectively, as investors were digesting the latest comments from Fed officials and awaited the US inflation report due out later this week for clues about the path of interest rates. On the one hand, Fed Governor Michelle Bowman was among the latest officials to warn that interest rates would need to keep rising. On the other, Tuesday's CPI report will likely show that annual inflation slowed to 6.2% in January, the lowest since October 2021, reigniting hopes for a policy pivot despite the persistently tight labor market. On the corporate side, Facebook-parent Meta rallied roughly 3.5% on reports of fresh layoffs. Other mega-cap companies, including Amazon.com, Apple, and Microsoft, added between 1.5% and 3.5%.2023-02-13T14:35:00
