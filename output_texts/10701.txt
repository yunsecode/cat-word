Title: US Stocks Edge Higher on Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Higher on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately in the green on Wednesday 16 May 2018, as Macy's shares rallied more than 10 percent on stronger-than-expected quarterly earnings, nudging the broader retail sector to its biggest one-day climb since April 10. The Dow Jones gained 63 points or 0.3% to 24769. The S&P 500 jumped 11 points or 0.4% to 2722. The Nasdaq climbed 47 points or 0.6% to 7398. 2018-05-16T20:37:56.05
