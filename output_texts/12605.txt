Title: US Stocks Open Higher On Wednesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Higher On WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks opened higher on Wednesday, with investors hoping that gains in technology and financial stocks would nudge the Dow Jones Industrial Average beyond 20,000, a level it has never breached. The Dow rose 31 points, or 0.2% to 19,976; the S&P 500 advanced 1 point, or 0.1%; and the Nasdaq gained 6 points, or 0.1%, around 15 minutes after the opening bell.2016-12-28T14:38:12
