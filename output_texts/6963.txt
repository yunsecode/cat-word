Title: Dollar Set for Further Weakness
Tag: Currency

Dollar Set for Further Weakness United States CurrencyThe dollar index managed to hold above the 90.00 marks on Friday, buoyed by higher Treasury yields and some safe-haven flow as the virus worries grow and political turmoil intensified. However, this upside momentum lacked any strong fundamental, with bearish signals emanating from the high debt levels. US President-elect Joe Biden has unveiled a $1.9 trillion rescue package, called the American Rescue Plan, that includes $1,400 checks for most Americans, a temporary boost in unemployment benefits and a rise in the federal minimum wage to $15 per hour. Furthermore, dovish-sounding remarks from the Chairman of the Federal Reserve put further pressure in the US currency.
