Title: US Treasury Yields Below Recent Highs
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Below Recent HighsUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury note was around 1.52% on Thursday, after rising to a near 4-month high of 1.596% the day before as investors digest higher than expected CPI data and the FOMC minutes. Inflation rate in the US came above forecasts in September, pointing to continuing upward price pressures on the economy and raising further concerns that a spike in commodity prices would weigh on growth and push inflation even higher. Meanwhile, the Federal Reserve meeting minutes showed the process of tapering could start in mid-November, as expected.2021-10-14T08:23:00
