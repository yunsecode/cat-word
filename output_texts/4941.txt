Title: US Layoffs at 5-Month High: Challenger
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Layoffs at 5-Month High: ChallengerUnited States Challenger Job CutsUS-based employers announced 22,822 job cuts in October of 2021, the highest reading in 5 months, with the majority of cuts (5,796) attributed to plant, store, and unit closing. For the third month in a row, companies in the Health Care/Products sector announced the most cuts with 6,694 and workers’ refusing to comply with vaccine mandates accounted for 5,071 cuts in October. So far this year, employers have announced plans to cut 288,043 jobs from their payrolls, down 87% from the 2,162,928 jobs eliminated through the same period last year. It is the lowest January-October total on record. 2021-11-04T11:58:00
