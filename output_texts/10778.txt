Title: US Stocks Dip on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Dip on MondayUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks declined moderately on Monday 30 April 2018 despite news that T-Mobile and Sprint are joining forces; Marathon Petroleum buying oil refiner Andeavor; and better-than expected earnings reports from McDonald's and Allergan lifted sentiment. The Dow Jones declined 147 points or 0.6% to 24164. The S&P 500 dropped 22 points or 0.8% to 2648. The Nasdaq lost 53 points or 0.8% to 7066.2018-04-30T20:02:28
