Dollar Strengthens on ThursdayUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index edged higher to 96.3 on Thursday after retreating in the previous session, as concerns over the impact of omicron on the global economy reemerge, despite optimism about the efficacy of vaccines and studies showing it is less severe than initially thought.
