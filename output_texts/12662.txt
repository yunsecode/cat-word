Title: U.S. Retail Sales Rise Less Than Expected
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

U.S. Retail Sales Rise Less Than ExpectedUnited States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States increased 0.1 percent in November from October of 2016, below market estimates of 0.3 percent and following an downwardly revised 0.6 percent rise in the previous period. Nine of 13 major retail categories showed gains in November, led by restaurants and furniture stores, the Commerce Department report showed. Retail sales excluding automobiles and service stations increased 0.2 percent, less than the projected gain of 0.4 percent and following a revised 0.5 percent advance the previous month.2016-12-14T13:33:54
