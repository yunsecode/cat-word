Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Jumps after CPI Data
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Jumps after CPI DataUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark US 10-year Treasury jumped to around 1.57% on Wednesday, rebounding from a 7-week low of 1.415% hit in the previous session, after fresh data showed the US consumer prices surged more than expected, pushing forward rate hike expectations. The annual inflation rate in the US surged to 6.2% in October, the highest since November of 1990 and above forecasts of 5.8%. Meanwhile, weekly claims fell to a new pre-pandemic low, broadly in line with expectations. The bond market will be closed on Thursday for the Veterans Day holiday.2021-11-10T14:02:00
