Title: US Weekly Jobless Claims Set to Decline Again
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Weekly Jobless Claims Set to Decline AgainUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsInitial jobless claims are likely to decrease to a fresh 14-month low of 450 thousand in the May 15th week, as the re-opening of the economy, coupled with growing consumer demand, prompted employers to hire more workers. The pace of the labor market's recovery gained further momentum in the spring, due to the ongoing government support and one of the world's most successful vaccination campaigns. Meanwhile, continuing claims are seen declining to 3.64 million in the May 8th week from 3.655 million the week before, but remaining well above February 2020 levels.2021-05-20T10:25:03.66
