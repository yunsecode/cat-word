Title: US Unemployment Rate Slightly Above Forecasts
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Unemployment Rate Slightly Above ForecastsUnited States Unemployment RateThe unemployment rate in the US increased by 0.2 percentage point to 3.7 percent in October 2022, up from September's 29-month low of 3.5 percent and slightly above market expectations of 3.6 percent. The jobless rate has been in a narrow range of 3.5 percent to 3.7 percent since March, suggesting that the labor market is already very tight, which, in turn, is likely to contribute significantly to inflationary pressure in the world's largest economy for some time to come. The number of unemployed persons rose by 306 thousand to 6.06 million in October, while the number of employed decreased by 328 thousand to 158.6 million. The labor force participation rate edged down to 62.2 percent from 62.3 percent.2022-11-04T12:33:00
