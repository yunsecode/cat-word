Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States declined by 2.342 million barrels in the week ended July 15th, 2016, following a 2.546 million fall in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 2.1 million drop. It is the 9th straight decrease in oil inventories. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks rose by 0.911 million barrels, after increasing by 1.213 million in the previous week.2016-07-20T15:38:44
