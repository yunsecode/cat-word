Title: US Jobless Rate Rises to 4.9% in June
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Rises to 4.9% in JuneUnited States Unemployment RateUS unemployment rate went up to 4.9 percent in June 2016 after falling by 0.3 p.p. to 4.7 percent in the previous month. The figure came in worse than market expectations, as more people entered the labor force. The number of unemployed persons increased by 347,000 to 7.8 million, offsetting declines in May and bringing both measures back in line with levels that had prevailed from August 2015 to April.2016-07-08T13:34:37
