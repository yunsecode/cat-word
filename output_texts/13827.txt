Title: US Planned Layoffs at 15-Year Low
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US Planned Layoffs at 15-Year LowUnited States Challenger Job CutsThe number of announced corporate layoffs in the United States fell 24 percent to 23622 in December from 30953 in November of 2015. It is the lowest figure since June of 2000 and the lowest ever for a December month. The December decline was significant enough to prevent 2015 job cuts from reaching a six-year high. In all, employers announced 598,510 job cuts in 2015, 24 percent more than the 483,171 planned layoffs in 2014 and the highest since 2011.2016-01-07T12:32:58
