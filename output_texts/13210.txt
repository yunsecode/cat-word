Title: US Mortgage Applications Slump 11.2%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Slump 11.2%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 11.2 percent in the week ended July 22nd of 2016 from the prior period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is second straight drop as refinance applications went down 15.1 percent and applications to purchase a home shrank 3.3 percent. Fixed 30-year mortgage rates rose 4bps to 3.69 percent.2016-07-27T12:02:27.09
