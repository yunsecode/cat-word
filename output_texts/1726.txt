Title: US Private Sector Business Activity Shrinks Further
Tag: Composite PMI

US Private Sector Business Activity Shrinks FurtherUnited States Composite PMIThe S&P Global US Composite PMI dropped to 46.3 in November 2022 from 48.2 in the previous month, signaling the sharpest pace of contraction in the private sector since August and among the quickest since 2009, a preliminary estimate showed. New orders fell the most since the initial pandemic wave in May 2020 as the impact of high inflation and rising borrowing costs weighed on demand, while the pace of job creation was only marginal. On the price front, input cost inflation softened for the sixth month running to the lowest since December 2020, but remained high overall, and selling price inflation eased to an over two-year low. Nonetheless, business expectations improved in November, amid improvements in supply chain stability and hopes of greater client demand following new product launches.2022-11-23T14:54:00
