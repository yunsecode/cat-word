Title: US Jobless Claims Fall to 240K
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall to 240KUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits dropped by 5 thousand to 240 thousand in the week ended July 29th from the previous week's revised level of 245 thousand and slightly below market expectations of 242 thousand. Meanwhile, the 4-week moving average, which removes week-to-week volatility, fell by 2.5 thousand to 241,750 thousand. 2017-08-03T12:33:59
