US Crude Oil Inventories Unexpectedly FallUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States dropped by 5.2 million barrels in the week ended October 14th, 2016, following a 4.9 million rise in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 2.75 million gain.
