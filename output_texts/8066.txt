Title: Dollar Nearly Flat on Wednesday
Tag: Currency

Dollar Nearly Flat on WednesdayUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded nearly flat at 97.3 on Wednesday as investors continue to digest a spike in new coronavirus cases and hopes for a coming economic recovery. ADP data showed the American economy added less jobs than expected in June although May figures were revised sharply higher to a job gain of 3.06 million. Traders also await ISM manufacturing PMI figures for an update on the pandemic impact.2020-07-01T09:39:00
