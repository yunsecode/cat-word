Title: US Building Permits Rise 3.5% MoM to 1170K
Tag: Building Permits

US Building Permits Rise 3.5% MoM to 1170KUnited States Building PermitsBuilding permits for privately-owned housing units were at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 1,170,000 in August. This is 3.5 percent above the revised July rate of 1,130,000 and is 12.5 percent above the August 2014 estimate of 1,040,000.2015-09-17T13:33:43
