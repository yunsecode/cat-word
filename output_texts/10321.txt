Title: US New Home Sales Unexpectedly Fall
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales Unexpectedly FallUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States shrank 1.7 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 627 thousand in July of 2018, following a downwardly revised 2.4 percent drop in June. It is the lowest rate since October, worse than market expectations of 645 thousand. Sales fell in the Northeast and in the South.2018-08-23T14:06:00
