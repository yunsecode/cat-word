US GDP Growth Beats Forecasts in Q1United States GDP Growth RateThe US economy expanded an annualized 2.3 percent on quarter in the first quarter of 2018, below 2.9 percent in the previous period but beating market expectations of 2 percent. Still, it s the lowest growth rate in a year, the advance estimate showed. The increase in real GDP reflected positive contributions from nonresidential fixed investment, personal consumption expenditures (PCE), exports, private inventory investment, federal government spending, and state and local government spending.2018-04-27T12:36:55.267
