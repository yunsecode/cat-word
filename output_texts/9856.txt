Title: US Homebuilder Sentiment Lowest since 2015
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Homebuilder Sentiment Lowest since 2015United States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index for the US slumped to 56 in December of 2018 from 60 in November and well below market expectations of 61. It is the lowest reading since May of 2015. The subindex for current single-family home sales fell to 61 from 67; the gauge for home sales over the next six months went down to 61 from 65 and prospective buyers edged down to 43 from 45.2018-12-17T15:07:23.127
