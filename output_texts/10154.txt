Title: US Trade Deficit Highest in 6 Months
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Deficit Highest in 6 MonthsUnited States Balance of TradeThe U.S. Census Bureau and the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis announced today that the goods and services deficit was $53.2 billion in August, up $3.2 billion from $50.0 billion in July, revised. August exports were $209.4 billion, $1.7 billion less than July exports. August imports were $262.7 billion, $1.5 billion more than July imports. The politically sensitive goods trade deficit with China surged 4.7 per cent to a record high of $38.6 billion.2018-10-05T12:36:00
