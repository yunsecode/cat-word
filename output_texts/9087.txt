Title: US Stocks Slump on Weak Economic Data
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Slump on Weak Economic DataUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks plunged on Thursday, following a steep sell-off in the previous session, after ISM data showed non-manufacturing growth slowed to a three-year low in September. The sentiment was also hurt by President Trump announcing tariffs on $75 billion of EU goods and Tuesday data showing second consecutive contraction in manufacturing sector. The Dow Jones lost 200 points or 0.8%; while the S&P 500 slid 16 points or 0.5%; and the Nasdaq retreated 40 points or 0.5% around 10:30 AM NY time. 2019-10-03T13:44:00
