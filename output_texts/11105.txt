Title: US Stocks Lower Amid Higher Inflation
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Lower Amid Higher InflationUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened slightly lower on Wednesday after data showed the US inflation was higher than expected in January, raising concerns over the pace of US interest rate rises. The Dow Jones was down 61 points or 0.3%; the S&P 500 fell 8 points or 0.3% and the Nasdaq declined 18 points or 0.3% around 5 minutes after the opening bell.
