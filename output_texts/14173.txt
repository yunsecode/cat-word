Title: US GDP Contracts Less Than Expected in Q1
Tag: Gdp Growth Rate

US GDP Contracts Less Than Expected in Q1United States Gdp Growth RateThe United States economy shrank at an annual rate of 0.2 percent in the first quarter of 2015, instead of a 0.7 percent drop reported in the second estimate. The exports decreased less than previously estimated, and personal expenditures and imports rose more.
