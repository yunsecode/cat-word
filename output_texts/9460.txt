US Retail Sales Unexpectedly Drop in FebruaryUnited States Retail Sales MoMUS retail trade fell by 0.2 percent from a month earlier in February 2019, following an upwardly revised 0.7 percent growth in January and missing market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain.
