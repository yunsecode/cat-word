Title: US Jobless Claims Lowest Since September
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Lowest Since SeptemberUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits decreased by 10 thousand to 203 thousand in the week ended November 30th from the previous week’s unrevised level of 213 thousand and compared with market expectations of 215 thousand. It is the lowest level since the week ended September 7th. According to unadjusted data, the largest declines were reported in California (-12,312), Texas (-5,915) and New York (-5,283). 2019-12-05T13:32:00
