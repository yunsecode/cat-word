Title: US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories declined by 0.446 million barrels in the week ended July 15th, following an injection of 3.254 million barrels in the previous period, and contrasting with market expectations for a 1.357 million build. Crude stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, rose by 1.143 million barrels, up from a 0.316-million-barrel injection in the previous period. Gasoline stocks rose by 3.498 million barrels, beating forecasts of a 0.071 million build. Distillate stockpiles, which include diesel and heating oil, shrank by 1.295 million barrels, against expectations for a 1.169 million increase.2022-07-20T14:53:14.4
