Title: US Markit Services PMI at 2-1/2-Year Low
Tag: Services PMI

US Markit Services PMI at 2-1/2-Year LowUnited States Services PMIThe final Markit US Services PMI came in at 49.7 in February of 2016, compared to a flash reading of 49.8 and 53.2 in the previous month, posting the first contraction in services activity since October of 2013. New business volumes expanded at the slowest rate since January 2015 and service providers were the least optimistic about their growth prospects for five-and-a-half years.2016-03-03T14:54:47.873
