Title: US June House Prices Rise Less Than Expected: FHFA
Tag: FHFA House Price Index MoM

US June House Prices Rise Less Than Expected: FHFAUnited States FHFA House Price Index MoMThe average prices of single-family houses with mortgages guaranteed by Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac in the United States edged up 0.1 percent month-over-month in June 2017, following a downwardly revised 0.3 percent rise in May and missing market expectations of 0.4 percent. Year-on-year, prices went up 6.5 percent after growing 6.9 percent in May.2017-08-22T13:02:49
