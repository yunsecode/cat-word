Title: Dollar Books First Weekly Loss Since August
Tag: Currency

Dollar Books First Weekly Loss Since AugustUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index fell below 93 on Monday as weaker-than-expected data on industrial production, retail sales, housing starts and initial jobless claims last week raised concerns about the US economic recovery. At the same time, a lack of progress in US fiscal stimulus negotiations, coupled with a resurgence in coronavirus cases, the US Federal Reserve's promise to keep rates at record lows and US-China tensions continued to weigh on the greenback.2020-09-18T20:11:00
