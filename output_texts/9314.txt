Title: US Producer Prices Rise 0.2% MoM in April
Tag: Producer Prices Change

US Producer Prices Rise 0.2% MoM in AprilUnited States Producer Prices ChangeProducer prices for final demand in the US increased 0.2 percent from a month earlier in April of 2019, easing from a 0.6 percent gain in March and in line with market expectations. Energy cost rose 1.8 percent, slowing from a 5.6 percent jump in March and services inflation eased to 0.1 percent in April from 0.3 percent. Meanwhile, food prices decreased 0.2 percent, down from a 0.3 percent advance in the prior month. The core index, which excludes food and energy, edged up 0.1 percent from the previous month, after a 0.3 percent gain in March and below consensus of 0.2 percent. On a yearly basis, producer prices rose 2.2 percent and the core index advanced 2.4 percent.2019-05-09T12:33:00
