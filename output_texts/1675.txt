Title: DXY Eases to Session Lows on Prospect of Smaller Rate Hikes
Tag: Currency

DXY Eases to Session Lows on Prospect of Smaller Rate HikesUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index pulled back to session lows of 106.3 in a roller coaster session Wednesday, having risen above 107 earlier, after Fed Chair Powell confirmed smaller rate hikes could start already in December. The greenback turned positive earlier in the session after labour data was not enough to dash concerns about another 75 bps hike in December. Employment increased by 127,000 from October to November, the least since January 2021, and job openings declined to 10.3 million in October, suggesting demand for labor was cooling amid tight financial conditions.2022-11-30T18:40:00
