Title: Wall Street Closes Lower on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

Wall Street Closes Lower on MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed lower on Monday 4 March 2019 after data showed construction spending unexpectedly fell 0.6% in December, compared to expectations of a 0.2% gain and few developments on US-China trade negotiations persisted. The Dow Jones retreated 207 points or 0.8% to 25820. The S&P 500 declined 11 points or 0.4% to 2793. The Nasdaq fell 18 points or 0.2% to 7578.2019-03-04T21:07:00
