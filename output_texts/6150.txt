Title: US Employment Costs Rise More than Expected
Tag: Employment Cost Index

US Employment Costs Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Employment Cost IndexCompensation costs for civilian workers in the US rose 0.9 percent on quarter in the three months to March 2021, accelerating from a 0.7 percent gain in the previous period and above market expectations of a 0.7 percent gain. Wages and salaries increased 1.0 percent and benefit costs went up 0.6 percent from December 2020.2021-04-30T12:31:00
