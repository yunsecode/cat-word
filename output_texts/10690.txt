Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall 2.6% in Latest Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall 2.6% in Latest WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States dropped 2.6 percent in the week ended May 18th 2018, after declining 2.7 percent in the previous period. It marks the fifth consecutive week of falls in mortgage applications, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications slumped 3.7 percent, with the index reaching the lowest since December 2000 and applications to purchase a home fell 2 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate jumped by 9bps to 4.86 percent.2018-05-23T11:02:44.947
