Title: US Budget Deficit Swells in May
Tag: Government Budget Value

US Budget Deficit Swells in MayUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US government budget deficit came at USD 240 billion in May 2023, widening from a USD 66 billion deficit in the corresponding period of the previous year, and below market expectations of a USD 236 billion gap led by a decline in revenue while Medicare spending surged. Revenues tumbled by 21% to USD 307 billion as non-withheld individual income taxes and higher tax return payments slashed revenues. Meantime, outlays jumped 20% to $548 billion with a tripling of spending on the government-run Medicare health program for seniors driving much of the increase. Considering the first eight months of the fiscal year, the budget deficit stood at USD 1.165 trillion compared to a USD 426 billion deficit a year earlier. 2023-06-12T18:12:00
