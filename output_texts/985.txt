Title: US Mortgage Rates Fall for 1st Time in 5 Weeks: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Rate

US Mortgage Rates Fall for 1st Time in 5 Weeks: MBAUnited States Mortgage RateThe average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($726,200 or less) fell 8bps to 6.71% in the week ended March 10th 2023, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association (MBA) showed. It is the first decline in five weeks after the collapse of SVB prompted a bond rally. Still, mortgage rates hold close to November highs.2023-03-15T11:18:37.133
