Title: US Job Openings Up to 5526K
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Up to 5526KUnited States Job OffersJob openings in the United States increased to 5526 thousand in September from an upwardly revised 5377 thousand in August of 2015, well above market expectations. The number of job openings was little changed in September for private and government. Job openings increased in professional and business services (+126,000) and retail trade (+64,000).2015-11-12T15:15:45.38
