Title: US Services Activity Rises at Faster Pace: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services Activity Rises at Faster Pace: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services Business Activity Index rose to 55.9 in October 2017 from 55.3 in the previous month and above market consensus of 55.6. The reading signalled the second-fastest rate of expansion since November 2015, due to improving domestic economic conditions and increased client spending. Also, the degree of optimism improved from September’s seven-month low, while employment rose at a weaker pace amid softer new business growth. On the price front, input cost inflation moderated to its least marked since March and average charges rose the least for six months.2017-10-24T13:52:34
