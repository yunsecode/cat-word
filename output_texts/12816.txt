Title: US Stocks Rise After FBI Clears Clinton
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise After FBI Clears ClintonUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Monday after the FBI said would take no action against Hillary Clinton, raising expectations she will become the next US President. The Dow Jones was up 262 points or 1.5%, the S&P 500 added 30 points or 1.5% and the Nasdaq gained 80 points or 1.6% around 7 minutes after the opening bell.2016-11-07T14:45:34
