Title: US Personal Income Rises 0.4% in February
Tag: Personal Income

US Personal Income Rises 0.4% in February United States Personal IncomePersonal income in the United States rose by 0.4 percent month-over-month in February 2018, the same pace as in January and in line with market expectations. The increase in personal income primarily reflected rises in wages and salaries (0.5 percent vs 0.6 percent in January) and nonfarm proprietors’ income (1.1 percent vs 0.3 percent).2018-03-29T12:32:08
