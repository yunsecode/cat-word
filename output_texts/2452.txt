Title: US Export Prices Post First Decline in Seven Months
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Post First Decline in Seven MonthsUnited States Export Prices MoMUS export prices dropped by 3.3 percent from a month earlier in July of 2022, following a 0.7 percent gain in June and compared with market expectations of a 1.1 percent drop. It was the first drop in export prices in seven months and the sharpest one-month decline since April 2020. Lower agricultural and nonagricultural prices each contributed to the decline. Compared to the previous year, export prices climbed 13.1 percent.2022-08-12T12:37:00
