Title: US Import Prices Fall More than Expected
Tag: Import Prices MoM

US Import Prices Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Import Prices MoMImport prices in the US shrank 0.5 percent in October from September, following a downwardly revised 0.6 percent drop in the previous month, due to a 2 percent decrease in fuel import cost and a 0.3 percent decline in nonfuel. Export prices fell 0.2 percent as agricultural cost edged down 0.1 percent and nonagricultural decreased 0.3 percent. Year-on-year, import prices fell 10.5 percent and export cost slumped 6.7 percent.2015-11-10T13:42:42
