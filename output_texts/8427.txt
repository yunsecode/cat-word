Title: US Treasury Yields Lower After GDP, Ahead Fed Decision
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Lower After GDP, Ahead Fed DecisionUnited States Government Bond 10YThe US 10-year Treasury yield edged lower to 0.59% on Wednesday, after recent data showed the US GDP contracted 4.8% in the first quarter, more than market expectations and the most since the Great Recession. Investors await now the Federal Reserve's monetary policy decision later in the day. Policymakers will probably leave interest rates near zero and pledge to do whatever it takes to support the world's largest economy.2020-04-29T12:55:00
