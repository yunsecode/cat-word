Title: US Stocks Finish Mostly Lower; Dow Closes At New Record
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Finish Mostly Lower; Dow Closes At New RecordUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks closed mostly in the red on Monday despite a rally in oil prices as Treasury yields rose ahead of the Fed meeting on Wednesday. The S&P 500 index shed 3 points, or 0.1%, to 2,257, amid a selloff in financials and consumer discretionary shares; and the Nasdaq Composite fell 32 points, or 0.6%, to 5,413. Meanwhile, the Dow Jones gained 39 points, or 0.2%, to 19,796, the sixth consecutive record close.2016-12-12T21:18:54
