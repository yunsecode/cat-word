Title: US Retail Sales Fall The Most In 16 Months
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Fall The Most In 16 Months United States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States decreased by 0.3 percent month-over-month in May of 2017, following a 0.4 percent rise in April and missing market expectations of a 0.1 percent gain. It was the biggest decline since January of 2016, driven by lower sales of motor vehicles, electronics & appliance stores, gasoline stations, sporting goods, hobby, book & music stores and general merchandise stores. On a year-on-year basis, retail sales rose 3.8 percent.2017-06-14T12:34:48.85
