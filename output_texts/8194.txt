Title: US Stock Futures Rise
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Rise United States Stock MarketMajor US stock index futures edged higher during the Asian session as Wall Street reflected investors optimism over a successful reopening of the global economy from coronavirus-induced shutdowns. Traders will continue to follow developments about the civil unrest around the country as states try to reopen the economy from the coronavirus pandemic. On Tuesday, the Dow Jones added 268 points or 1.1% to 25,743. The S&P 500 climbed 25 points or 0.8% to 2081. The Nasdaq gained 56 points or 0.6% to 9608.2020-06-03T01:34:11.127
