Title: US Mortgage Applications Plunge 13.3%
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Plunge 13.3%United States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the US plunged 13.3% in the week ended February 17th, 2023, following a 7.7% decline in the previous week, as mortgage rates continue to rise, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to refinance a home loan were down 2.2% and those to purchase a home loan sank 18.1%. Meanwhile, the average contract interest rate for 30-year fixed-rate mortgages with conforming loan balances ($726,200 or less) jumped 23bps to 6.62%, the highest since mid-November, and tracking a rise in Treasury yields.2023-02-22T12:05:00
