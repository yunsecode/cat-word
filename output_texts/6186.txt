Title: US 10-Year Treasury Yield Hits 2-Week High
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Treasury Yield Hits 2-Week HighUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note briefly touched a two-week high of 1.665% on Thursday ahead of GDP and jobless claims data. The Federal Reserve left rates and asset purchases unchanged, despite acknowledging an improving economic outlook and a rise in inflation. In addition, US President Biden proposed a $1.8 trillion stimulus package, pleading to raise taxes on wealthy Americans to help pay for his "American Families Plan." Auctions held in recent weeks were met with sufficient demand, easing fears that investors would struggle to soak up the flood of supply hitting the market.2021-04-29T08:16:00
