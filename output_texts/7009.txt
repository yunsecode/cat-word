Title: US Crude Stocks Fall For 4th Straight Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Fall For 4th Straight WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories dropped by 8.01 million barrels in the week ended January 1st, 2021, a fourth consecutive period of decline and compared with market expectations of a 2.133 million fall, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories were up by 4.519 million barrels, while markets had forecast a smaller 1.525 million increase.2021-01-06T15:33:00
