Title: US Labor Costs Rise More than Expected in Q4
Tag: Labour Costs

US Labor Costs Rise More than Expected in Q4United States Labour CostsUnit labor costs in the US nonfarm business sector went up an annualized 2 percent on quarter in the last three months of 2018, following an upwardly revised 1.6 percent rise in the previous period. It compares with market expectations of a 1.6 percent increase. It mainly reflects a 3.9 percent rise in hourly compensation and a 1.9 percent gain in labor productivity. Year-on-year, unit labor costs went up 1 percent, slightly lower than a 1.1 percent rise in the previous period. Considering full 2018, unit labor costs in the nonfarm business sector increased 1.4 percent, below 2.2 percent in 2017 and the lowest gain since 2016. It mainly reflects increases of 2.7 percent in hourly compensation and 1.3 percent in productivity. 2019-03-07T13:39:00
