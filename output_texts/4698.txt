Title: DXY Falls for 2nd Session
Tag: Currency

DXY Falls for 2nd SessionUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index touched 96.1 on Thursday morning, falling for a second consecutive session as its appeal weakened after the Federal Reserve avoided any drastic changes to its monetary policy. As expected, the Federal Reserve suggested it would end pandemic-era asset purchases earlier and hike interest rates three times next year amid low unemployment and high inflation. Meanwhile, the Bank of England unexpectedly hiked its bank rate to 0.25% citing mounting inflationary pressures. In contrast, today ECB is also likely to signal that it will keep buying bonds throughout the year, in an attempt to rule out any rate hike in 2022.2021-12-16T10:06:00
