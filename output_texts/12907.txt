Title: US Stocks Close Mixed on Friday, Fall on the Week
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Mixed on Friday, Fall on the WeekUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed flat to higher on Friday after earnings reports for JP Morgan, Wells Fargo and Citigroup all beat estimates and dovish comments from Fed Chair Yellen. The Dow Jones added 39 points or 0.2% while the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq ended near the flatline. Considering the full week, the Dow Jones lost 0.6%, the S&P 500 fell 1% and the Nasdaq finished 1.5% lower.
