Title: US Durable Goods Orders Fall Less than Forecast
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Fall Less than ForecastUnited States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods slid 17.2 percent month-over-month in April of 2020, following a downwardly revised 16.6 percent slump in March and better than market expectations of a 19 percent drop. Still, it is the biggest drop in durable goods orders since August of 2014. Demand for transportation equipment sank 47.3 percent. Excluding transportation, new orders decreased 7.4 percent and excluding defense new orders went down 16.2 percent. Core capital goods orders, which exclude aircraft and military hardware, fell 5.8 percent.2020-05-28T12:34:00
