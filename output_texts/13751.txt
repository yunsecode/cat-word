Title: US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised Down Slightly
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Markit Manufacturing PMI Revised Down SlightlyUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe final Markit US Manufacturing PMI came in at 52.4 in January of 2016, down from a preliminary reading of 52.7 but higher than 51.2 in the previous month. Output and new orders rose at faster pace while job creation slowed, reflecting manufacturer’s heightened uncertainty about the economic outlook. Some firms noted that the strong dollar continued to act as a growth headwind, especially in terms of export sales.2016-02-01T14:55:28
