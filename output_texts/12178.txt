Title: US Stocks Jump After French Vote
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Jump After French VoteUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened the week with gains on Monday as investors mood improved after Macron’s victory in the first round of the French presidential election. The Dow Jones went up 202 points or 1%, the S&P 500 added 25 points or 1.1% and the Nasdaq gained 65 points or 1.1% to hit an all-time high of 5976 around 4 minutes after the opening bell.
