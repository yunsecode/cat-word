US Construction Spending Rises Less Than Expected United States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the US rose by 0.8 percent to near 11-yeah high of $1.19 trillion in February 2017 compared with a downwardly revised 0.4 percent drop in January and below market expectations of a 1.1 percent increase.
