Title: US Stocks End Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End HigherUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks closed higher on Monday, but gave back most of their gains as an early rally in energy stocks faded after President Trump said he will be announcing his decision on whether to withdraw from the Iran nuclear deal on Tuesday. The Dow Jones rose 95 points, or 0.4%, to 24,357; the S&P 500 gained 9 points, or 0.4%, to 2,673; and the Nasdaq advanced 56 points, or 0.8%, to 7,265. 2018-05-07T20:20:32
