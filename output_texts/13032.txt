Title: US Stocks Close Sharply Lower as Oil Retreats
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Sharply Lower as Oil RetreatsUnited States Stock MarketUS stocks fell sharply on Tuesday, dragged lower by a fresh drop in oil prices as investors remained cautious ahead of next week's Federal Reserve meeting. The Dow Jones Industrial Average shed 258 points, or 1.4%, to 18,067; the S&P 500 index slid 32 points, or 1.5%, to 2,127; and the Nasdaq Composite Index sank 57 points, or 1.1%, to 5,155.2016-09-13T20:10:26.54
