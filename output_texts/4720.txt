Title: Dollar Little-Changed After US Inflation Data
Tag: Currency

Dollar Little-Changed After US Inflation DataUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index held at 96.3 against a basket of currencies on Friday, after data showed the US consumer price inflation jumped to 6.8% in November, the highest since 1982 and well above the Fed's target of 2%. The central bank is expected to announce that it will speed up the pace of its stimulus tapering at next week’s policy meeting, and to signal a more aggressive path for interest rate hikes in 2022. Elsewhere, the greenback benefited from lingering threats from the Omicron variant, as some nations introduced renewed restrictions amid a spike in global infection cases.
