Title: US Home Builder Sentiment Matches Forecasts
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Home Builder Sentiment Matches ForecastsUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States fell to 75 in January of 2020 from an over 20-year high of 76 in the previous month, matching market expectations. The current single-family sub-index dropped to 81 from 84 in December and the one for prospective buyers decreased to 57 from 58. Meantime, the gauge for home sales over the next six months was unchanged at 79.2020-01-16T15:03:00
