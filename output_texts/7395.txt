Title: Dollar Hovers Near 1-Month High
Tag: Currency

Dollar Hovers Near 1-Month HighUnited States CurrencyThe dollar held steady near one-month highs against the other major currencies on Friday, as traders rushed to the safety of the greenback amid rising coronavirus infections and a potentially contested election result in the US. In the absence of further fiscal stimulus in the US, a dovish ECB communique and fresh lockdowns in Germany and France the dollar is expected to appreciate further. 2020-10-30T01:30:09.367
