Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 3rd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 3rd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States dropped by 0.497 million barrels in the week ended December 14th 2018, following a 1.208 million decline in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 2.437 million fall. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories increased by 1.766 million barrels, above market consensus of a 1.2 million rise.2018-12-19T15:32:39.58
