Title: US Treasury Yields Continue to Rise
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Continue to RiseUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-year Treasury note went up to 1.12% on Wednesday, breaking the 1% level for the second straight session and the first time it happens in two weeks. Investors welcomed a $1 trillion stimulus package announced by the Trump administration to help offset the economic impact of the coronavirus. The 30-year Treasury bond yield also went up to 1.78%.2020-03-18T09:09:35.923
