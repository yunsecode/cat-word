Title: US Trade Gap Lowest In 5 Months In March
Tag: Balance of Trade

US Trade Gap Lowest In 5 Months In MarchUnited States Balance of TradeThe goods and services deficit in the United States narrowed slightly to USD 43.7 billion in March of 2017 from an upwardly revised USD 43.8 billion gap a month earlier and lower than market expectations of a USD 44.5 billion shortfall. Exports declined for the first time in four months mainly due to lower sales to China and imports fell for the second straight month.2017-05-04T12:34:30
