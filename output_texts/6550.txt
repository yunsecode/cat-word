Title: US Futures Remain Mixed
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures Remain MixedUnited States Stock MarketThe Dow Jones Industrial Average climbed on Monday, touching record highs in intraday trade as investors piled into economic comeback plays after Senate approval of a new Covid stimulus package, while a continuous sell-off in high-flying tech shares put pressure on the broader market. Meantime, US 10-year rates held near 13-month highs of 1.599% amid fears that the fresh stimulus programs could push the economy into overdrive and stoke inflation. President Biden’s $1.9 trillion stimulus bill was approved by the Senate on Saturday and the House is seen passing it on Tuesday. On Monday, the Dow Jones added 1%, the S&P 500 lost 0.5% and the Nasdaq sank 2.4%.2021-03-08T23:50:00
