Title: US Futures Recover
Tag: Stock Market

US Futures RecoverUnited States Stock MarketUS futures rebounded on Wednesday as investors digest rising coronavirus infections and their impact on the economic recovery and prospects of a working vaccine. The US recorded 161.9K new cases on Tuesday, the third highest daily rate since the pandemic started and there were 1.7K deaths, the most since May, according to Johns Hopkins. Meanwhile, Fed Chair Powell said on Tuesday that the US still has a long way to go to full recovery and that it's too soon to say with any confidence what impact vaccines would have. On the macro front, housing starts and building permits will be in the spotlight today. On the earnings front, several companies are due to report quarterly results, including Target, Lowe’s, Nvidia and TJX Companies.2020-11-18T10:30:00
