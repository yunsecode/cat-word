Title: US Stocks Trade Mixed
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade MixedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened mixed on Monday, with Boeing shares plunging more than 11% after China, Indonesia and Ethiopia suspended flights of Being 737 MAX-8 following the crash a of a Boeing 737 MAX plane on Sunday in Nairobi. On the other hand, Apple stocks were up after Bank of America Merrill Lynch upgraded the company's shares to buy from neutral. The Dow Jones fell 0.7% while the Nasdaq gained 0.4% and the S&P 500 added 0.8% around 8 minutes after the opening bell.
