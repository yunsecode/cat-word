Title: US Consumer Sentiment Beats Expectations
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Beats ExpectationsUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe Thomson Reuters/University of Michigan's final June reading on the overall index of consumer sentiment came in at 82.5, up from a preliminary 81.2 and above market forecasts. Consumers were more confident that first quarter GDP contraction was due to bad weather conditions. 2014-06-27T15:19:47.897
