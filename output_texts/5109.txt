Title: DXY Drifts Lower, Set for 2nd Weekly Loss
Tag: Currency

DXY Drifts Lower, Set for 2nd Weekly LossUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar index drifted lower on Friday, trading below 93.7 and is on track for its second weekly loss. The greenback has been in a downtrend for the past two weeks on mounting expectations of faster monetary policy tightening in other countries and as rallying equity and commodity markets sapped demand for safe-haven assets.. Meanwhile, US benchmark 10-year yields remained close to a 5-month high of 1.7%.2021-10-22T06:34:00
