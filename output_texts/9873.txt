Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall Less than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall Less than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States dropped by 1.208 million barrels in the week ended December 7th 2018, following a 7.323 million decline in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 2.99 million fall. This was the second period of decline after 10 weekly increases. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories increased by 2.087 million barrel, below market consensus of a 2.461 million rise.2018-12-12T15:31:00
