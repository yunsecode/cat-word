Title: US Stock Futures Rally
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures RallyUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures recovered ground on Thursday, a day after the Dow Jones plunged more than 700 index points to post its worst day since June 11 on the back of intensifying worries about a coronavirus resurgence. Florida, Oklahoma and South Carolina reported record increases in new cases, while the governors of New York, New Jersey and Connecticut announced a 14-day quarantine for travellers entering from hotspots. Investors were also worried about the possibility of trade tariffs and geopolitical tension between the United States, Canada, and the European Union. On Wednesday, the Dow Jones plunged 710 points or 2.7% to 25,446. The S&P 500 plummeted 81 points or 2.6% to 3050. The Dow Jones sank 222 points or 2.2% to 9909. 2020-06-25T00:44:00
