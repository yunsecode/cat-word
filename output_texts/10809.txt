Title: US Stocks Plunge on Tuesday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Plunge on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the red on Tuesday 24 April 2018, mainly dragged down by industrials, materials and tech shares. Losses came despite better-than-expected earnings reports for Caterpillar (-6.2%), Coca-Cola (-2.0%), United Technologies (-1.1%), and Verizon (+2.1%). The Dow Jones declined 425 points or 1.7% to 24024. The S&P 500 lost 36 points or 1.3% to 2635. The Nasdaq plummeted 121 points or 1.7% to 7007.2018-04-24T20:18:06
