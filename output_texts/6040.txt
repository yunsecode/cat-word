Title: US Treasury Yields Steady Ahead of Fed Minutes
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Steady Ahead of Fed Minutes United States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note was steady at 1.64% on Monday, as investors await the Federal Reserve's latest meeting minutes, due on Wednesday, for clues about the central bank's thought about rising prices pressures. Federal Reserve officials have consistently downplayed the risk of a sharp and sustained rise in inflation, though, and looking for how equities sold off on such inflation worries, investors are no longer buying the Fed's message. Meanwhile, the Treasury Department will auction $34 billion in 52-week bills, $27 billion in 20-year bonds and $13 billion in 10-year TIPS this week.2021-05-17T19:26:00
