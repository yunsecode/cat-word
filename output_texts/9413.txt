US April Consumer Sentiment Weaker than ExpectedUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US fell to 96.9 in April 2019 from 98.4 in the previous month and below market consensus of 98.0, a preliminary estimate showed.
