Title: US Services PMI Revised Lower: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services PMI Revised Lower: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services PMI was revised lower to 56 in July of 2018 from a preliminary reading of 56.2 and 56.5 in June. The rise in services output was steep overall, despite softening to the weakest since April. New business growth eased for the third successive month to the slowest since January, but remained well above the trend seen in 2017. On the price front, input cost inflation softened to a three-month low while output inflation was the strongest since September of 2014. Finally, business confidence fell to a six-month low amid concerns surrounding tariffs and their effects on client demand.2018-08-03T13:53:49.387
