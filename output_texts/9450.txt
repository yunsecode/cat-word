Title: US MBA Mortgage Applications Rise the Most in 12 Weeks
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US MBA Mortgage Applications Rise the Most in 12 WeeksUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 18.6 percent in the week ended March 29th 2019, following an 8.9 percent gain in the previous week, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the biggest rise since the week ended January 4th. Refinance application increased 38.5 percent and applications to purchase a home advanced 3.4 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate fell by 9bps to 4.36 percent.2019-04-03T11:02:43.893
