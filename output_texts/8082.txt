Title: US 10-Year Bond Yields Slightly Higher
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10-Year Bond Yields Slightly HigherUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on US government 10-year Treasury note was slightly higher at 0.6741% on Friday, as investors await data on personal income, consumer spending and core PCE prices later in the day. Yields fell on Thursday amid fears of a second wave of coronavirus infections in the US as several states including California, Texas and Florida posted record rises in the number of new cases. The yield on the 30-year Treasury bond was also higher at 1.4186%.2020-06-26T07:34:00
