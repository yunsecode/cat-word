Title: US 10Y Bond Yield Rises After US Payroll Miss
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US 10Y Bond Yield Rises After US Payroll MissUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the benchmark 10-year Treasury note rose to above 1.32% on Friday after disappointing payrolls number put in question Fed's pledge to wind down bonds purchases. The US economy added 235K jobs in August of 2021, the lowest in 7 months and well below forecasts of 750K as a surge in COVID-19 infections may have discouraged companies from hiring and workers from actively looking for a job. 2021-09-03T12:39:00
