Title: US Factory Activity Growth at 1-Year High: Markit
Tag: Manufacturing PMI

US Factory Activity Growth at 1-Year High: MarkitUnited States Manufacturing PMIThe final IHS Markit Manufacturing PMI for the US increased to 53.4 in October 2016 from 51.5 in September and slightly better than the earlier flash reading of 53.2. It was the highest figure since October last year, as both production and new orders expanded at a faster pace and employment rose for the fortieth month in a row. Meanwhile, cost inflation went up to highest level for two years.2016-11-01T13:49:30
