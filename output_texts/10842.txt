Title: US Stocks Close Higher on Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher on Monday United States Stock MarketWall Street closed deeply in the green on Monday 16 April 2018 after geopolitical concerns over Syria eased and the earnings season continues with Bank of America's earnings beating forecasts. The Dow Jones gained 213 points or 0.9% to 24573. The S&P 500 increased 22 points or 0.8% to 2678. The Nasdaq climbed 50 points or 0.7% to 7156.2018-04-16T20:13:55.453
