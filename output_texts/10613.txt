Title: US Mortgage Applications Drop in Latest Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Drop in Latest Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States dropped 1.5 percent in the week ended June 8th 2018, after a 4.1 percent surge in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications fell 1.5 percent and applications to purchase a home declined also 1.5 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate went up by 8bps to 4.83 percent.2018-06-13T11:05:20.843
