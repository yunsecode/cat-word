Title: US Stocks Open Mixed After Strong GDP Growth
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Mixed After Strong GDP GrowthUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened mixed on Friday amid falling oil prices, better-than-expected GDP growth figures for the third quarter and upbeat earnings for Chevron while Exxon Mobil profits disappointed. The Dow Jones added 53 points or 0.3%, the S&P 500 traded near the flatline while the Nasdaq lost 10 points or 0.2% around 10 minutes after the opening bell.2016-10-28T13:48:22.813
