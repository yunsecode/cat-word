Title: US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than Expected
Tag: Consumer Credit

US Consumer Credit Rises Less Than ExpectedUnited States Consumer CreditConsumer credit in the United States increased by USD 6.84 billion in January of 2022, the least since January of 2021, easing from an upwardly revised USD 23.38 billion gain in the previous month, and well below market expectations of a USD 23.8 billion rise. Revolving credit, which includes credit card debt, decreased by USD 0.22 billion while non-revolving credit, which includes auto and student loans went up by USD 7.06 billion. On an annual basis, consumer credit grew 1.9 percent, following an upwardly revised 6.1 percent increase in December 2021.
