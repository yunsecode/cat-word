Title: US 5th District Factory Index at 5-Month Low
Tag: Richmond Fed Manufacturing Index

US 5th District Factory Index at 5-Month LowUnited States Richmond Fed Manufacturing IndexThe Manufacturing Activity Index in the US fifth district decreased to a 5-month low of 1 in February of 2022 from 8 in the previous month, due to declines in the indexes for shipments (-11 vs 14 in January) and new orders (-3 vs 6). However, the third component in the composite index, employment, increased to 20 from 4 in January. Firms reported decreases in order backlogs, as the index became negative for the first time since June 2020. Vendor lead times increased for many firms as that index remained at near-historic highs. Firms’ perceptions about changes in local business conditions remained slightly negative; however, firms remained optimistic about future conditions. Firms continued to report increasing wages while also citing challenges finding workers with the necessary skills. Firms expect this challenge to last for at least the next six months as the expectations index remained in negative territory.2022-02-22T15:06:00
