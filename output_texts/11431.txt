Title: US Consumer Sentiment Revised Higher
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Revised HigherUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States was revised up to 98.5 in November of 2017 from a preliminary of 97.8. Still, it was lower than 100.7 in October which was the strongest since January 2004. Expected economic conditions deteriorated compared to the previous month although less than initially estimated.
