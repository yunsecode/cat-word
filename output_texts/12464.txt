Title: Most US Stocks Rise After Fed
Tag: Stock Market

Most US Stocks Rise After FedUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mostly in the green on Wednesday after the Federal Reserve left rates unchanged as expected and data for ADP payrolls and ISM manufacturing PMI came above forecasts. On the earnings front, upbeat reports for companies including ADP, Johnson Controls, Altria and Apple that reported after the close in the previous day lifted investors' mood. The Dow Jones gained 27 points or 0.1% and the Nasdaq increased 28 points or 0.5% while the S&P 500 finished nearly flat.2017-02-01T21:28:53
