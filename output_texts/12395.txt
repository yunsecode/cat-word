Title: US Stocks Close Higher to Notch Over 1.5% Weekly Gains
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Close Higher to Notch Over 1.5% Weekly GainsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street trimmed early losses with the three major indexes closing at new record highs on Friday as investors continue to assess the prospects for President Trump’s economic plans and the timing of Fed interest-rate hikes. The Dow Jones gained 4 points to 20624.05; the S&P 500 went up 4 points to 2351.16 and the Nasdaq increased 24 points to 5838.58. On the week, the Dow gained 1.7%; the S&P rose 1.5% and the Nasdaq went up 1.8%. 2017-02-17T21:09:24
