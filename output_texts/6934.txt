Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 1st Time in 3 Weeks
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 1st Time in 3 WeeksUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States fell 1.9 percent in the week ended January 15th, following two consecutive weeks of increases, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Applications to refinance a home loan dropped 4.7 percent, but were up 87 percent year-on-year; while those to purchase a home rose 2.7 percent, and were 15 percent higher than a year ago. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate was up 4 basis point to 2.92 percent last week, the highest level since late November.2021-01-20T12:08:00
