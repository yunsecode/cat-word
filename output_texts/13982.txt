Title: US Factory Orders Fall for 2nd Month
Tag: Factory Orders

US Factory Orders Fall for 2nd MonthUnited States Factory OrdersNew orders for manufactured goods in the United States shrank 1 percent in September from August of 2015, slightly more than markets expected and following a downwardly revised 2.1 percent drop in the previous period. Excluding transportation, orders fell 0.6 percent and excluding defense, they decreased 1.4 percent.2015-11-03T15:07:05
