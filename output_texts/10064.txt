Title: US Stocks Rebound Sharply on Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rebound Sharply on ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street rebounded sharply on Thursday 25 October 2018 from a big sell-off in the previous session amid rising global growth concerns, geopolitical tensions and disappointing earnings results. Tech stocks surged 9.1% as an unexpected profit for Q3 relieved recent concerns with the company and CEO Elon Musk. The Dow Jones jumped 401 points or 1.6% to 24985. The S&P 500 surged 50 points or 1.9% to 2706. The Nasdaq soared 210 points or 3.0% to 7318. 2018-10-25T20:15:00
