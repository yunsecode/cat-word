Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Unexpectedly Rise
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Unexpectedly Rise United States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 1.058 million barrels in the week ended September 13th 2019, after a 6.912 million drop in the previous week and against market expectations of a 2.496 million decline, according to EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories edged up 0.781 million barrels, recovering from a 0.682 million decrease.2019-09-18T14:32:00
