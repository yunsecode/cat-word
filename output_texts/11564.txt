Title: US Home Builder Confidence Stronger than Expected
Tag: Nahb Housing Market Index

US Home Builder Confidence Stronger than ExpectedUnited States Nahb Housing Market IndexThe NAHB Housing Market Index in the United States rose four points to 68 in October 2017, beating market expectations of 64. It was the highest reading since May, as sentiment among home builders recovered from the initial shock of the hurricanes. The component gauging current sales conditions rose five points to 75; the index charting sales expectations in the next six months increased five points to 78; and the component measuring buyer traffic ticked up a single point to 48.2017-10-17T14:07:55
