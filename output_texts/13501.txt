Title: US S&P Case-Shiller House Prices Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Case Shiller Home Price Index YoY

US S&P Case-Shiller House Prices Rise Less than ExpectedUnited States Case Shiller Home Price Index YoYHouse prices across 20 major metropolitan areas in the United States rose 5.4 percent year-on-year in February of 2016, easing from a 5.7 percent increase in January and lower than market expectations of a 5.5 percent rise.  Portland (+11.9 percent), Seattle (11 percent) and Denver (+9.7 percent) recorded the highest increases. On a monthly basis, the S&P/Case-Shiller's 20-City Home Price Index increased 0.2 percent, recovering from a flat reading in January.2016-04-26T14:12:41
