Title: US Jobless Claims Fall Less Than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Fall Less Than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits decreased by 3 thousand to 258 thousand in the week ended March 25th 2017, from the previous week's unrevised level of 261 thousand but above expectations of 248 thousand. The 4-week moving average, which removes week-to-week volatility, increased by 7.75 thousand to 254.25 thousand.2017-03-30T12:29:45
