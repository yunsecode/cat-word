Title: US Job Openings Fall to 5.4 Million
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Fall to 5.4 MillionUnited States Job OffersJob Offers in the United States decreased to 5370 thousand in August after reaching a record high of 5668 thousand in July of 2015 and missing market expectations of 5625 thousand. Job openings declined in state and local government (-33 thousand) and non-durable goods manufacturing (-25 thousand).2015-10-16T15:09:15
