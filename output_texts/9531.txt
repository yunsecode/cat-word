Title: US Durable Goods Orders Unexpectedly Rise
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Unexpectedly RiseUnited States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods increased 0.4 percent from a month earlier in January of 2019, following an upwardly revised 1.3 percent advance in December and beating market expectations of a 0.5 percent drop. Transportation equipment, up five of the last six months, jumped 1.2 percent and drove the increase. Orders for non-defense capital goods excluding aircraft, a closely watched proxy for business spending plans, went up 0.8 percent, the highest gain since July and rebounding from a 0.9 percent fall in December.2019-03-13T12:34:00
