Title: US Export Prices Rise 0.3 MoM in September
Tag: Export Prices MoM

US Export Prices Rise 0.3 MoM in SeptemberUnited States Export Prices MoMUS export prices rose 0.3 percent on month in September 2016, after decreasing by 0.8 percent in August. The figure came in better than expectations of a flat reading, as prices of non-agricultural exports went up 0.4 percent, recovering from a 0.6 percent drop in August; while agricultural exports' prices fell 1 percent, compared to a 3.5 percent drop in the previous month. Year-on-year, export prices declined 1.5 percent.2016-10-13T12:43:12.8
