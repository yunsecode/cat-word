Title: Dollar Little Changed as Traders Await Developments in Ukraine
Tag: Currency

Dollar Little Changed as Traders Await Developments in UkraineUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index was around 96 on Tuesday, reversing from early gains, as investors track the crisis in Ukraine and the risks to growth and inflation. Russian president Vladimir Putin recognized on Monday the independence of two breakaway regions in eastern Ukraine and later ordered forces into the area. US president Joe Biden responded by ordering sanctions on the two separatist regions, with the European Union taking additional measures. Moreover, Federal Reserve governor Michelle Bowman suggested Monday that a 50 basis point rate hike at the March meeting could happen if PCE inflation to be released later in the week comes in above expectations.
