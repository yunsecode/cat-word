Title: US ADP Payrolls at 5-Month High
Tag: ADP Employment Change

US ADP Payrolls at 5-Month HighUnited States ADP Employment ChangePrivate businesses in the United States hired 217 thousand workers in November of 2015 compared to an upwardly revised 196 thousand in October. It is the highest employment gain since June and much better than market expectations of a 190 thousand rise. Small companies added 81 thousand jobs, midsized 62 thousand and large 74 thousand. Private businesses in the United States hired 217 thousand workers in November of 2015 compared to an upwardly revised 196 thousand in October. It is the highest employment gain since June and much better than market expectations of a 190 thousand rise. Small companies added 81 thousand jobs, midsized 62 thousand and large 74 thousand. Goods-producing employment rose by 13 Thousand, down from 22 Thousand in the previous month. Service-providing employment rose by 204 Thousand,  a strong increase from an upwardly revised 174 Thousand in October. 2015-12-02T13:20:29
