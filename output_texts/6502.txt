Title: US Business Inventories in line with Forecasts
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories in line with ForecastsUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers’ and trade inventories in the US went up 0.3 percent from a month earlier in January of 2021, in line with market expectations and easing from an upwardly revised 0.8 percent growth in December. It was the seventh consecutive month of gains in business inventories. Stocks at manufacturers edged up 0.1 percent, slowing from a 0.3 percent advance in the previous month and inventories at retailers fell 0.5 percent, down from a 1.7 percent rise. Meantime, stocks at wholesalers rose faster (1.3 percent vs 0.6 percent). Year-on-year, business inventories dropped 1.8 percent. 2021-03-16T14:03:00
