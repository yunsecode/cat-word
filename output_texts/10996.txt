Title: US Stocks Surge on Friday amid Strong Payrolls
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Surge on Friday amid Strong PayrollsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed deep in the green on Friday 9 March 2018, extending gains from the previous session after the jobs report showed the US economy added 313K jobs in February, the most since July of 2016 and comfortably beating expectations of 200K, nudging the Nasdaq to a new record high. The Dow Jones soared 429 points or 1.7% to 25325. The S&P 500 jumped 47 points or 1.7% to 2786. The Nasdaq surged 129 points or 1.7% to 7557. During the week, all three major indices booked strong gains, with the Dow Jones climbing 3.2%; the S&P 500 3.4%; and the Nasdaq 4.1%. 2018-03-09T21:01:12.877
