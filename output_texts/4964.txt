Title: US Factory Orders Unexpectedly Rise
Tag: Factory Orders

US Factory Orders Unexpectedly RiseUnited States Factory OrdersNew orders for US manufactured goods inched up 0.2% September of 2021, following a downwardly revised 1.0% rise in August and beating market forecasts of a flat reading. Orders rose the most for machinery(1.2%), namely photographic equipment (7.7%) and industrial machinery (7.2%); non-durable goods industries (0.8%); fabricated metal products (0.7%); and primary metals (0.7%), notably ferrous metal foundries (4.7%). On the other hand, the steepest drop was seen in orders for transport equipment (-2.3%), weighed down mostly by nondefense aircraft and parts (-27.9%). Excluding transportation, factory orders edged up 0.7%, accelerating from a 0.5% gain in August.2021-11-03T14:08:14.727
