Title: US Business Inventories Rebound in April as Forecast
Tag: Business Inventories

US Business Inventories Rebound in April as ForecastUnited States Business InventoriesManufacturers and trade inventories in the US rose by 0.2% from a month earlier in April 2023, after a downwardly revised 0.2% fall in the prior month, matching market forecasts. Stocks increased at manufacturers (0.5% vs -0.8% in March) and retailers (0.1% vs 0.4%) but fell for merchant wholesalers (-0.1% vs -0.2%).
