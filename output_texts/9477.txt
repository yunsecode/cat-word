Title: US Imports Drop 2.6% to 7-Month Low
Tag: Imports

US Imports Drop 2.6% to 7-Month LowUnited States ImportsImports of goods and services to the US dropped 2.6% month over month to USD 6.8 billion, reaching the lowest level since last June. Goods imports declined USD 6.5 billion to USD 210.7 billion, as purchases of capital goods were down USD 3.0 billion on the back of computer accessories, semiconductors and civilian aircraft. Also imports of industrial supplies and materials fell USD 2.3 billion due to crude oil. On a non seasonally adjusted basis, imports fell from China (-9.6 percent), Japan (-6.7 percent), the EU (-3.8 percent) and Canada (-1.5 percent), but rose from Mexico (2 percent). 2019-03-27T12:35:00
