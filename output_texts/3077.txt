US Crude Oil Stock Slump Much More than Expected: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States slumped by 3.799 million barrels in the week ended June 24th, 2022, the largest withdrawal since mid-April, following a 5.607 million barrel surge in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 0.106 million barrel draw, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed.
