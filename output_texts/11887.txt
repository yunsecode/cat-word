Title: US June Budget Deficit Larger Than Expected
Tag: Government Budget Value

US June Budget Deficit Larger Than ExpectedUnited States Government Budget ValueThe US government posted a USD 90 billion budget deficit in June 2017, larger than market expectations of a USD 35 billion gap and compared with a USD 6 billion surplus in the same month of the previous year. Outlays jumped 33 percent to USD 429 billion while receipts increased at a much slower 3 percent to USD 339 billion. 2017-07-13T18:02:34
