Title: US Stocks Gain Amid Trade Truce
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Gain Amid Trade TruceUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened sharply in the green on Monday, following gains in Europe and in Asia after US President Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping agreed on a 90-day trade tariff truce at the G20 meeting during the weekend. The Dow Jones increased 1.7%, the S&P 500 rose 1.3% and the Nasdaq traded 1.8% higher around 8 minutes after the opening bell. 2018-12-03T14:40:11.99
