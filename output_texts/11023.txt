Title: US Private Sector Grows the Most in Over 2 Years
Tag: Composite Pmi

US Private Sector Grows the Most in Over 2 YearsUnited States Composite PmiThe IHS Markit US Composite PMI came in at 55.8 in February 2018, little-changed from the preliminary estimate of 55.9 and above January's final reading of 53.8. The figure pointed to the strongest pace of expansion in the private sector since November 2015, as service sector growth hit a six-month high (PMI at 55.9 vs 53.3 in January) while manufacturing output rose at a slower, but solid pace (55.3 vs 55.5 in January).2018-03-05T14:50:25.08
