Title: US Stocks Set to Start Week Higher
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Set to Start Week HigherUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures rose on Monday as investors are anticipating higher volatility and are monitoring developments around the fast-spreading omicron variant. Dow Jones futures rose by about 90 points to the highest level since mid-November, while S&P 500 was up 0.4% and Nasdaq 100 futures gained 0.5%. Still, airline stocks and cruise line shares headed for sharp losses after thousands of US flights were canceled due to the pandemic and amid reports of Covid outbreaks on ships. The major averages recovered last week as concerns that the omicron variant would derail economic growth receded, with president Biden saying that the US will not go back to lockdown. Also, the sentiment was lifted on hopes the massive democratic spending bill will be revived as president Biden insisted that he and senator Manchin will “get something done.” 2021-12-27T13:54:00
