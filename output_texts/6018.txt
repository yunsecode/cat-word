Title: US Crude Oil Stocks Rise Most in 2 Months
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Stocks Rise Most in 2 MonthsUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories rose by 1.321 million barrels in the May 14th week, the largest increase in two months but below market forecasts of a 1.623 million advance, according to the EIA Petroleum Status Report. Meantime, gasoline inventories dropped by 1.963 million barrels, the most since the March 5th week and compared with consensus of a 0.886 million decline.2021-05-19T14:39:14.917
