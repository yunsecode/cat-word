Title: US Stock Futures Fall
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Fall United States Stock MarketStock futures contracts tied to the blue-chip Dow fell 0.7% on Friday, while those linked to the S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100 were down 0.6% and 0.3%, respectively, as investors remain cautious ahead of the crucial non-farm payrolls report. A tight labor market has been a headache for markets and policymakers, with Federal Reserve Chair Powell reaffirming his message of likely sharper interest rate hikes should economic data warrant. Hints of stress in the US banking system exacerbated this equity rout after US tech-industry lender SVB Financial Group launched a share sale to shore up its balance sheet amid losses from bond sales. Shares of the US bank are down more than 20% in premarket trading, following a 60% decline in the previous session.
