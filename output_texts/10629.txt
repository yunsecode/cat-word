Title: US Labour Costs Rise More than Estimated
Tag: Labour Costs

US Labour Costs Rise More than EstimatedUnited States Labour CostsUS unit labor costs in the nonfarm business sector increased 2.9 percent in the first quarter of 2018, compared to an earlier estimate of 2.7 percent gain and following a 2.5 percent rise in the previous-three month period. Figures came above market expectations of a 2.8 percent gain. The rise reflected a 3.3 percent increase in hourly compensation and a 0.4 percent gain in productivity. On a yearly basis, unit labor costs increased 1.3 percent.2018-06-06T12:35:00
