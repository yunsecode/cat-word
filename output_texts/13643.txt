Title: US Economy Adds 242K Jobs in February
Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

US Economy Adds 242K Jobs in FebruaryUnited States Non Farm PayrollsTotal nonfarm payroll employment increased by 242,000 in February of 2016, higher than an upwardly revised 172,000 in the previous month and beating market expectations of 190,000. Employment gains occurred in health care and social assistance, retail trade, food services and drinking places, and private educational services. Job losses continued in mining.2016-03-04T13:32:33
