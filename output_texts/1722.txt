Title: US Crude Stocks Drops More than Expected: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Drops More than Expected: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories fell by 3.691 million barrels in the week ended November 18th, compared with market expectations of a smaller 1.055-million-barrel decrease, the latest US Energy Information Administration report showed. Crude stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, went down by 0.887 million barrels, following a 1.624 million draw. In the meantime, gasoline stocks rose by 3.058 million, more than market expectations of a 0.383 million build, and distillate stockpiles, including diesel and heating oil, went up by 1.718 million, versus forecasts for a 0.55 million drop.  2022-11-23T15:33:03.917
