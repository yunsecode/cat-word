Title: US Job Openings Beat Forecasts in June
Tag: Job Offers

US Job Openings Beat Forecasts in JuneUnited States Job OffersThe number of job openings in the US rose to 6.662 million in June of 2018 from an upwardly revised 6.659 million in May, above market expectations of 6.646 million. The number of job openings was little changed for total private and for government. Job openings increased for educational services (+20,000) while fell for transportation, warehousing, and utilities (-84,000). Meantime, the number of hires was little changed at 5.7 million.
