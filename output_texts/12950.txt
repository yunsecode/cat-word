Title: US Exports at 13-Month High in August
Tag: Exports

US Exports at 13-Month High in AugustUnited States ExportsTotal exports from the United States increased 0.8 percent mom to $187.9 billion in August of 2016, reaching the highest value since July of 2015. Exports of goods went up $1.2 billion to $125.3 billion, boosted by industrial supplies and materials (up $1.4 billion); nonmonetary gold (up $0.9 billion) and automotive vehicles, parts, and engines (up $0.4 billion). Exports of services increased $0.3 billion to $62.5 billion: travel (for all purposes including education) increased $0.2 billion; transport, which includes freight and port services and passenger fares, rose $0.1 billion. Shipments to Brazil (+14.8 percent) recorded the highest increase, followed by Mexico (+10.8 percent); Canada (+10.7 percent); OPEC (+8.8 percent); Japan (+5.7 percent); China (+2.6 percent) and the European Union (+1.2 percent).2016-10-05T13:03:49
