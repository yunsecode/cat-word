Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd Week: MBA
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 3rd Week: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the US dropped by 3.7% in the week ended August 26th, a third consecutive decline, prompted by a 7.8% plunge in the refinancing index and a 1.8% drop in applications to purchase a home. Meanwhile, the average rate on a 30-year fixed-rate mortgage went up by 15bps to 5.8%, the highest level since mid-July, and tracking a rise in Treasury yields, after Fed officials reiterated interest rates would stay higher for longer. Compared to a year ago, mortgage applications are down 63%, with refinancing plunging 83% and the purchase index 23%.2022-08-31T11:09:00
