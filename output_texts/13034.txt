US Stocks Reverse Gains on Tuesday United States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the red on Tuesday amid a fall in oil prices while investors await Fed monetary policy decision next week. Brainard comments on Monday were the last ones before the committee meets as Fed officials enter in a quiet period.
