Title: Dollar Extends the Decline Heading into September
Tag: Currency

Dollar Extends the Decline Heading into September    United States CurrencyThe dollar index extended the decline for the 6th straight session at the start of September, trading below 92 and remaining close to levels not seen since May of 2018. In August, the dollar lost near 1.3%, the fifth straight month of declines and the the longest monthly losses since 2017. A new Fed policy framework in which interest rates are expected to stay low for a longer period together with a dovish outlook for the US have been weighing on investors' mood. Also, concerns about the US economic recovery and whether a new coronavirus relief package will be unveiled dragged down the greenback. 2020-09-01T09:45:00
