Title: US Retail Sales Fall for 3rd Straight Month
Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Fall for 3rd Straight MonthUnited States Retail Sales MoMUS retail trade fell unexpectedly by 0.1 percent month-over-month in February 2018, following a downwardly revised 0.1 drop in January and missing market expectations of a 0.3 percent gain. It was the third consecutive month of decline in retail trade, mainly due to lower sales at motor vehicle & parts dealers and gasoline stations. Excluding automobiles, gasoline, building materials and food services, retail sales edged up 0.1 percent last month after being unchanged in January.2018-03-14T12:33:34
