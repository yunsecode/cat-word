Title: Dollar Regains Momentum
Tag: Currency

Dollar Regains MomentumUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index gradually rose back towards the 90.50 level from an over 2-1/12-year low of 89.80 touched in the previous week as investors turned their attention to safe-haven assets because of persistent concerns about the new coronavirus strain. Still, another major rescue package for the US economy along with prospects of a vaccine-driven global economic recovery next year left room for further dollar weakness. 2020-12-23T00:02:08.83
