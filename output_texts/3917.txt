Title: Dollar Eases as Treasury Yields Retreat
Tag: Currency

Dollar Eases as Treasury Yields RetreatUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index weakened below 99.8 on Thursday after retreating from a near 2-year high of 100.5 hit in the previous session, tracking declines in Treasury yields as traders scaled back aggressive bets on faster Federal Reserve interest rate hikes. Investors also shrugged off the latest US CPI report at least momentarily amid signs that inflation may be peaking, with US core consumer prices showing slower increases. Still, the dollar remained at elevated levels against major peers on prospects of a more aggressive US monetary policy tightening to tame surging inflation. Fed Governor Lael Brainard also said the central bank will raise rates expeditiously to reduce soaring inflation and expressed confidence in its ability to moderate price pressures without triggering an economic downturn.2022-04-14T06:13:50.69
