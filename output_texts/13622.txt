Tag: Retail Sales MoM

US Retail Sales Down 0.1%United States Retail Sales MoMRetail sales in the United States edged down 0.1 percent in February from the previous month, in line with market expectations and following a revised 0.4 percent drop in the previous month. Receipts at gasoline stations shrank 4.4 percent and auto sales declined 0.2 percent. 2016-03-15T12:35:12
