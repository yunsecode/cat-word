Title: US Stock Futures Rise as Earnings Season Kicks Off
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Rise as Earnings Season Kicks OffUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures pointed to a positive start on Monday, as investors shrug off a spike in coronavirus cases and turn their attention to corporate earnings season to grasp the impact of the pandemic on companies. The companies reporting earnings this week include JPMorgan, Citigroup, Wells Fargo, Goldman Sachs, Pepsi, Netflix, IBM, Johnson & Johnson, UnitedHealth, and Blackrock. According to Johns Hopkins University, Florida reported a record increase of more than 15,000 new coronavirus cases on Sunday, the highest single-day total for any US state since the pandemic began. Last week, the Dow Jones gained 0.9%, the S&P 500 added 1.7% and the Nasdaq rose 4%.2020-07-13T08:42:00
