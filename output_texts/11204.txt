Title: US Jobless Claims Rise Less than Expected
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise Less than ExpectedUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits rose by 17 thousand to 233 thousand in the week ended January 20th, from the previous week's 45-year low of 216 thousand and below market expectations of 236 thousand. The claims taking procedures in Puerto Rico and in the Virgin Islands have still not returned to normal.
