Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall More than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 4.948 million barrels in the week ended January 5th 2018, following a 7.419 million drop in the previous period and compared with market expectations of a 3.89 million decline. It marks the eighth consecutive week of falls. Meanwhile, gasoline inventories went up by 4.135 million, following a 4.813 million rise in the previous week and above market expectations of a 2.625 million increase.2018-01-10T15:33:47
