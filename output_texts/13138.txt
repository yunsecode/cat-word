Title: US Consumer Sentiment Improves Slightly in August
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Improves Slightly in AugustUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the United States came in at 90.4 in August 2016 from 90 in July but well below market expectations of 91.5, preliminary figures showed. Consumer expectations improved firmly while current conditions deteriorated.2016-08-12T15:03:27
