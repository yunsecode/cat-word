Title: US Stocks Fall on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Fall on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street slumped at the open on Friday amid a jump in yields after the jobs report showed the US economy added a better-than-expected 200 thousand jobs in January and the annual rise in wages was the strongest in more than 8 years. The Dow Jones was down 212 points or 0.8%, the S&P 500 fell 16 points or 0.6% and the Nasdaq traded 24 points or 0.3% lower around 5 minutes after the opening bell.2018-02-02T14:41:40.97
