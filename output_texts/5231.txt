Title: US Construction Spending Unexpectedly Stalls
Tag: Construction Spending

US Construction Spending Unexpectedly StallsUnited States Construction SpendingConstruction spending in the US was virtually unchanged at a seasonally adjusted annual rate of USD 1.584 trillion in August of 2021, after increasing 0.3 percent in the previous month and defying market expectations of a 0.3 percent rise. Spending on private construction decreased 0.1 percent (vs 0.2 percent in July), mostly transportation (-2.4%) and lodging (-2.3%), while public construction outlays rose 0.5 percent (the same as in July).
