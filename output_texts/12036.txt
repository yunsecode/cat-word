Title: US Jobless Claims Rise To 5-Week High
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Rise To 5-Week HighUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filing for unemployment benefits increased by 13 thousand to 248 thousand in the week ended May 27th from the previous week's revised level of 235 thousand and way above market expectations of 239 thousand.  Still, it was 117th straight week that claims were below 300 thousand, a level associated with a healthy jobs market.2017-06-01T12:32:52
