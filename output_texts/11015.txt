Title: US Stocks Edge Up on Tuesday

US Stocks Edge Up on TuesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed moderately in the green on 6 March 2018, as uncertainty coming from recent events in US trade policy ameliorated. Tech stocks led by Mercado Libre (+5.0%) and Netflix (3.2%) outperformed on Tuesday. The Dow Jones inched up 9 points or less than 0.1% to 24884. The S&P 500 gained 7 points or 0.3% to 2728.
