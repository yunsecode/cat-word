Title: US Wages Increase 0.2% MoM in October
Tag: Average Hourly Earnings

US Wages Increase 0.2% MoM in OctoberUnited States Average Hourly EarningsUS average hourly earnings for all employees on private nonfarm payrolls rose by 5 cents or 0.2 percent month-over-month to $27.30 in October of 2018, following a 0.3 percent rise in the previous month and matching market expectations. Over the year, average hourly earnings have increased by 85 cents, or 3.1 percent, the most since 2009 and higher than a 2.8 percent rise in September. Average hourly earnings of private-sector production and nonsupervisory employees increased by 7 cents to $22.89 in October.2018-11-02T12:42:00
