Title: US Employment Costs Rise More than Expected
Tag: Employment Cost Index

US Employment Costs Rise More than ExpectedUnited States Employment Cost IndexCompensation costs for civilian workers in the US rose 0.7 percent on quarter in the three months to December 2020, accelerating from a 0.5 percent gain in the previous period and above market expectations of a 0.5 percent increase. Wages and salaries climbed 0.9 percent, following a 0.4 percent rise in Q3; and benefit costs went up 0.7 percent, after a 0.5 percent increase.2021-01-29T13:34:00
