Title: US August Budget Deficit Smaller than Expected
Tag: Government Budget Value

US August Budget Deficit Smaller than Expected United States Government Budget ValueThe US government posted a USD 108 billion budget deficit in August 2017, compared with a USD 107 billion gap in the same month of the previous year and below market expectations of a USD 119.5 billion deficit. Outlays fell 1 percent year-on-year to USD 334 billion while receipts decreased 2 percent to USD 226 billion.2017-09-13T18:09:21
