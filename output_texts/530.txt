Title: US Job Quits Fall in March
Tag: Job Quits

US Job Quits Fall in MarchUnited States Job QuitsThe number of job quits in the United States decreased by 129,000 from the previous month to 3.85 million in March 2023, the lowest level since May 2021 and below the record peak of roughly 4.5 million quits reached in November 2021. Accommodation and food services saw the most significant decline in the number of quits, with a decrease of 178,000. The quits rate, which measures the proportion of voluntary job leavers among total employment, fell to 2.5% from 2.6% in February, matching the lowest in two years. 2023-05-02T14:06:00
