Title: US Durable Goods Orders Fall More than Expected
Tag: Durable Goods Orders

US Durable Goods Orders Fall More than ExpectedUnited States Durable Goods OrdersNew orders for US manufactured durable goods slumped 1.7 percent month-over-month in April of 2018, following an upwardly revised 2.7 percent rise in March. It compares with market expectations of a 1.4 percent drop as orders for transportation equipment tumbled 6.1 percent. However, orders for non-defense capital goods excluding aircraft, a proxy for business spending, jumped 1 percent, reversing from a 0.9 percent decrease in March.2018-05-25T12:34:54
