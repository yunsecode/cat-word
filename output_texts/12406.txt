Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More Than Expected
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise More Than ExpectedUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States went up by 9.527 million barrels in the week ended February 10th, 2017, following a 13.83 million increase in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 3.513 million rise. Also, gasoline stocks rose by 2.846 million, compared to expectations of a 0.752 million drop.2017-02-15T15:35:02
