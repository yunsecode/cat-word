US Manufacturing PMI Revised Down: Markit United States Manufacturing PMIThe IHS Markit US Manufacturing PMI was revised down to 55.7 in October of 2018 from a preliminary of 55.9, but slightly higher than 55.6 in September. Figures still pointed to the strongest increase in factory activity in five months, mainly boosted by a sharp rise in new business which was the strongest in five months. 2018-11-01T13:52:02.277
