Title: Dollar Gains on Safe-Haven Buying
Tag: Currency

Dollar Gains on Safe-Haven BuyingUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index remained steady at 90.40 on Tuesday, as investors sought shelter in the safe-haven assets amid surging coronavirus cases and a slower-than-expected COVID-19 vaccine rollout in the United States. Despite the recent rebound, the dollar’s weakness is likely to continue well into 2021. Biden and his Democrat-controlled Congress are due to roll out massive spending plans that include extra $1,400 checks for most Americans, a temporary boost in unemployment benefits and a rise in the federal minimum wage to $15 per hour. Large budget and current account deficits have made the greenback an increasingly unattractive investment. 2021-01-25T18:36:00
