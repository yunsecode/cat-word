Title: Most US Stocks Rise On Thursday
Tag: Stock Market

Most US Stocks Rise On ThursdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened flat to higher on Thursday amid a stronger dollar and mixed economic data for jobless claims, building permits and housing starts while investors await the inauguration of President-elect Trump on Friday. The Dow Jones traded nearly flat while the S&P 500 added 2 points or 0.1% and the Nasdaq gained 12 points or 0.2% around 6 minutes after the opening bell.
