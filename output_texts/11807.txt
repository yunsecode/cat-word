Title: US Unemployment Rate Falls to 4.3%
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Unemployment Rate Falls to 4.3%United States Unemployment RateUS unemployment rate fell one-tenth of a percentage point to 4.3 percent in July 2017, matching a 16-year low touched in May. The number of unemployed persons was little changed at 7.0 million while the labor force participation rate edged up to 62.9 percent. 2017-08-04T12:30:27
