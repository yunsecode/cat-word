Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise The Most In Nearly 3 Months
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Rise The Most In Nearly 3 MonthsUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States rose by 3.295 million barrels in the week ended June 2nd, 2017, following a 6.428 million decrease in the previous period and compared to market expectations of a 3.464 million decline. It was the first increase following 8 consecutive weeks of falls and the biggest in almost 3 months. Gasoline stocks went up by 3324 thousand, compared to expectations of a 580 thousand increase.
