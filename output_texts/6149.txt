Title: US Personal Spending Rises Most in 9 Months
Tag: Personal Spending

US Personal Spending Rises Most in 9 MonthsUnited States Personal SpendingPersonal spending in the United States jumped 4.2 percent from a month earlier in March 2021, following a 1.0 percent fall in February and slightly beating market consensus of a 4.1 percent growth. It was the largest increase in consumption since June 2020, as households received an additional round of direct economic impact payments from the government. Spending on goods was supported by both nondurable, led by “other” nondurable goods, which includes recreational items like games, toys, and hobbies, and durable goods, in particular motor vehicles and parts.
