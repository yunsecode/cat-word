Title: Dallas Fed Services Index Improves Further in February
Tag: Dallas Fed Services Index

Dallas Fed Services Index Improves Further in FebruaryUnited States Dallas Fed Services IndexThe Dallas Fed General Business Activity index for services in Texas went up to -9.3 in February of 2023 from -15 in the previous month. Although perceptions of business conditions continued to worsen in during the period, the result pointed to the most moderate deterioration in five months. The company outlook index booked a considerable improvement, increasing to -1.7 from -8.3 in the previous month, while the index measuring the outlook on uncertainty fell to 12.9 from 20 in January.2023-02-28T15:44:00
