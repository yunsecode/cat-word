US Mortgage Applications Rebound: MBAUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States rose 4.8 percent in the week ended March 23rd 2018, following a 1.1 percent fall in the previous period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. Refinance applications went up 7.3 percent and applications to purchase a home increased 3.1 percent. The average fixed 30-year mortgage rate edged up by 1bps 4.69 percent.2018-03-28T11:02:47.45
