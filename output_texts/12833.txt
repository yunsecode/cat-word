Title: US October Corporate Layoffs at 17-Year Low
Tag: Challenger Job Cuts

US October Corporate Layoffs at 17-Year LowUnited States Challenger Job CutsThe number of announced corporate layoffs in the United States fell 30.6 percent month over month to 30,470 in October of 2016. It was the second lowest value for the year and the smallest for an October month since 1999. Employers in the computer industry saw the heaviest job cuts, announcing 4,792 planned layoffs, mainly due to newly formed HP Inc. The firm cut another 4,000 jobs, adding to the 30,000 job cuts the company announced in 2015. The energy sector announced 4,414 layoffs and telecommunication announced 4,135. Year-on-year, layoffs fell 39 percent in October. Since the beginning of the year, companies announced 66,352 job cuts, down 14 percent from the 543,935 cuts announced a year earlier.2016-11-03T11:41:18
