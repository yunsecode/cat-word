Title: Dow Futures Drop Over 200 Points
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Futures Drop Over 200 Points United States Stock MarketWall Street's main indexes looked set to extend losses on Monday, as worries over a resurgence in the pandemic's spread persisted. Nevada, Florida, California, and Arizona have reported record-high single-day infections. The concerns outweighed expected economic stimulus and the sustained economic recovery from many businesses re-opening. Last week, all three major stock indexes recorded the fourth weekly gain in five. The Dow Jones increased 1%, the S&P 500 climbed 1.9%, and the Nasdaq jumped 3.7%.2020-06-21T23:04:00
