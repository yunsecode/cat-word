Title: US Stocks Edge Higher on Friday, S&P and Nasdaq at Fresh Highs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Higher on Friday, S&P and Nasdaq at Fresh HighsUnited States Stock MarketWall Street edged higher on Friday 26 April 2019, as US GDP grew by an annualized 3.2 percent in the first quarter of 2019, easily beating market expectations of 2 percent and following a 2.2 percent expansion in the previous three-month period. The Dow Jones gained 81 points or 0.3% to 26543. The S&P 500 climbed 14 points or 0.5% to a new record of 2940. The Nasdaq jumped 28 points or 0.3% to a new high of 8146.2019-04-26T20:19:36.56
