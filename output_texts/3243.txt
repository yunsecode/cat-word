Title: US Corporate Profits Fall from Record High
Tag: Corporate Profits

US Corporate Profits Fall from Record HighUnited States Corporate ProfitsCorporate profits in the United States fell 4.3 percent to USD 2.42 trillion in the first quarter of 2022, from a record USD 2.53 trillion reached in the previous period. Net cash flow with inventory valuation adjustment, the internal funds available to corporations for investment, fell 1.6 percent to $3.18 trillion, while net dividends rose 0.5 percent to $1.47 trillion. Meanwhile, undistributed profits slumped 11.0 percent to $0.95 trillion.
