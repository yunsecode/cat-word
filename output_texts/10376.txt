Title: US June Wholesale Inventories Revised Higher
Tag: Wholesale Inventories

US June Wholesale Inventories Revised HigherUnited States Wholesale InventoriesWholesale inventories in the United States edged up 0.1 percent from a month earlier in June 2018 instead of being unchanged as previously estimated, following a downwardly revised 0.3 percent gain in May. Machinery stocks increased 0.8 percent (vs 1.7 percent in May), while auto inventories fell 1.2 percent (vs -1.5 percent in May) and farm products stocks tumbled 6.5 percent (vs -2.6 percent in May) Wholesale stocks excluding autos, which goes into the calculation of GDP, advanced 0.2 percent in June. Year-on-year, inventories increased 5.1 percent.2018-08-09T14:09:00
