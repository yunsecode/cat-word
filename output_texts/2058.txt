US Stocks Turn Positive in Rollercoaster Session United States Stock MarketWall Street recovered from a muted start on Friday, with the Dow adding more than 200 points and the S&P 500 and Nasdaq crossing into positive territory as investors reassessed the outlook for monetary policy. The trigger for the abrupt upside move was a report from the WSJ that showed that some officials are signaling a desire to slow down the pace of the increase soon to gauge the impact of such tightening on growth. The market movement came in tandem with easing Treasury yields, which brought some respite to growth-oriented stocks. Still, volatility showed no signs of abating amid Friday’s $2 trillion options expiration. Meanwhile, a slew of negative quarterly results from corporate America capped gains.
