Title: Dow Notches 10th Straight Record Close
Tag: Stock Market

Dow Notches 10th Straight Record Close United States Stock MarketThe Dow booked its 10th consecutive day of record highs Thursday, after US Treasury Secretary Steve Mnuchin said the Trump administration is committed to passing a “very significant” tax reform before Congress' August recess.  The Dow closed 35 points higher, or 0.2%, to 20,810; and the S&P rose 1 point, or less than 0.1%, to 2,364; while the Nasdaq declined 25 points, or 0.4%, to 5,836.2017-02-23T21:11:22
