Title: NY Factory Activity Expands Modestly in June
Tag: NY Empire State Manufacturing Index

NY Factory Activity Expands Modestly in JuneUnited States NY Empire State Manufacturing IndexThe NY Empire State Manufacturing Index came in at 6.01 in June of 2016, compared to -9.02 in the previous month and beating market expectations of -4. New orders (10.9 from -5.54 in May) and shipments (9.32 from -1.94 in May) came back to growth and inventories were lower (-15.31 from -7.29). Employment index was flat (0 from 2.08 in May) while the average workweek decline slowed (-5.1 from -8.33 in May). Prices received decreased less than in the previous month (-1.02 from -3.13 in May) and prices paid rose at a faster pace (18.37 from 16.67). Firms were more optimistic about the six-month outlook this month, and capital spending plans picked up.
