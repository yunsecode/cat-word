Title: NY Factory Activity Shrinks for 3rd Month
Tag: NY Empire State Manufacturing Index

NY Factory Activity Shrinks for 3rd MonthUnited States NY Empire State Manufacturing IndexThe NY Empire State Manufacturing Index fell to -6.8 in October of 2016 from -1.99 in September and much worse than market expectations of 1. It is the lowest reading in five months. The new orders index edged up but remained in negative territory (-5.6 from -7.5 in September); shipments were nearly flat (-0.6 from -9.4) and labor market conditions remained weak, with both employment levels (-4.7 from -14.3) and the average workweek (-10.4 from -11.6) negative. In contrast, prices paid (22.6 from 17) and received (4.7 from 1.8) increased. Indexes for the six-month outlook suggested that manufacturing firms expect conditions to improve in the months ahead.
