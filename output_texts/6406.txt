Title: US Stock Futures Trade at Record Highs
Tag: Stock Market

US Stock Futures Trade at Record HighsUnited States Stock MarketUS stock futures held near record valuations in early Asian trade, following a strong rally in recent sessions as widespread vaccinations and an unprecedented government stimulus boosted investor confidence in an economic rebound. On the data front, the ISM Services PMI jumped to a record 63.7 in March, well above forecasts of 59, due to the lifting of coronavirus pandemic-related restrictions, while US nonfarm payrolls surged by 916,000 jobs in March, raising hopes of a faster-than-expected economic recovery. Meanwhile, market participants will follow negotiations between Democrats and Republicans on President Biden's $2.25 trillion infrastructure plan. On Monday, the Dow Jones added 374 points or 1.1% to 33,527. The S&P 500 gained 58 points or 1.4% to 4,078. The tech-heavy Nasdaq outperformed its peers, surging 226 points or 1.7% to 13,706.2021-04-06T00:21:00
