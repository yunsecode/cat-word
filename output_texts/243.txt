Title: US Exports Fall to 13-Month Low
Tag: Exports

US Exports Fall to 13-Month LowUnited States ExportsExports from the United States decreased by USD 9.2 billion from a month earlier to USD 249 billion in April 2023, the lowest since March 2022. Exports of goods declined by USD 9.4 billion, due to lower sales of crude oil (down USD 2.1 billion), fuel oil (down 1.3 billion), pharmaceutical preparations (down 0.5 billion), gem diamonds (down 0.4 billion) and jewelry (down 0.4 billion). On the other hand, exports of services increased by USD 0.2 billion mainly due to travel (up 0.5 billion) and other business services (up 0.3 billion).2023-06-07T12:37:00
