US Books Capital Inflow in MayUnited States Capital FlowsThe United States recorded a capital and financial account surplus of USD 105.3 billion in May of 2021, up from a revised USD 100.1 billion in the prior month. Foreign investors sold USD 93.4 billion in Treasuries in May, compared with an inflow of USD 49.6 billion the previous month.
