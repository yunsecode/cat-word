Title: US Housing Starts Fall More Than Expected
Tag: Housing Starts

US Housing Starts Fall More Than ExpectedUnited States Housing StartsHousing starts in the United States slumped 6.8 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annualized rate of 1215 thousand in March of 2017, following an upwardly revised 1303 thousand in the previous month and much worse than market expectations of a 3 percent drop. It is the lowest rate in four months, led by drops in the Midwest.2017-04-18T12:29:31
