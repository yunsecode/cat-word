Title: US Industrial Output Posts Biggest Gain in 1 Year
Tag: Industrial Production Mom

US Industrial Output Posts Biggest Gain in 1 YearUnited States Industrial Production MomIndustrial production in the United States increased 0.6 percent in June from May 2016, recovering from a downwardly revised 0.3 percent drop in the previous period and better than market expectations of a 0.2 percent gain. It was the biggest gain since July last year, as output of manufacturing, mining and utilities moved up. For the second quarter as a whole, however, industrial production fell at an annual rate of 1 percent, its third consecutive quarterly decline.2016-07-15T14:23:24
