Title: US Stocks Trade Mainly Flat After GDP
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Trade Mainly Flat After GDPUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened around the flatline on Friday after data showed the economy expanded less than expected last quarter and mixed earnings reports for companies including Chevron, Honeywell, American Airlines and Colgate-Palmolive. The Dow Jones, the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq traded nearly flat around 5 minutes after the opening bell. 2017-01-27T14:44:48.643
