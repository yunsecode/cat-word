Title: US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 2nd Week
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Oil Inventories Fall for 2nd WeekUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States fell by 0.559 million barrels in the week ended September 9th, 2016, compared to market expectations of a 4 million gain. It follows a 14.5 million drop in the previous week, after Tropical Storm Hermine, which threatened the Gulf Coast refining region, led to the loss of some oil production. Meanwhile, gasoline stocks rose by 0.567 million barrels after declining by 4.2 million in the previous week.2016-09-14T14:36:42.52
