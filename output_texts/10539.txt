Title: US Stocks Edge Higher on Friday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Edge Higher on FridayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street edged up on Friday 29 June 2018, led by a rally for financial shares after the country's biggest banks cleared the second part of the Federal Reserve's annual stress tests. Also, stronger-than-expected quarterly results from Nike lifted sentiment. The Dow Jones gained 55 points or 0.2% to 24271.The S&P 500 advanced 2 points or 0.1% to 2718. The Nasdaq inched up 7 points or 0.1% to 7510.2018-06-29T20:18:26.807
