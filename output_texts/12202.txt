Title: US Stocks Open Higher On Monday
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Open Higher On MondayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened in the green on Monday amid geopolitical tensions with North Korea, a weaker dollar and better-than-expected GDP data for China. The Dow Jones added 76 points or 0.4%, the S&P 500 went up 6 points or 0.3% and the Nasdaq gained 22 points or 0.4% around 5 minutes after the opening bell.2017-04-17T13:36:58.37
