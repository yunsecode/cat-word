Title: US Stocks Rise on Trade Optimism
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rise on Trade OptimismUnited States Stock MarketWall Street traded higher on Friday as investors were optimistic regarding trade negotiations between China and the US. President Trump is expected to meet Chinese Vice Premier Liu He later in the day. The Dow Jones was up 0.7%, the S&P 500 added 0.7% and the Nasdaq traded 0.9% higher around 12:00 PM NY time.2019-02-22T17:07:30.563
