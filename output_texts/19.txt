Title: US Core PPI Rises Less than Expected
Tag: Core Producer Prices MoM

US Core PPI Rises Less than ExpectedUnited States Core Producer Prices MoMCore producer prices in the United States increased by 0.1% over a month in June 2023, matching May's revised reading and slightly below market estimates of a 0.2% rise. On a yearly basis, core consumer prices advanced by 2.4% in June, slowing from a downwardly revised 2.6% increase in May and below market expectations of a 2.6% rise.4 days ago
