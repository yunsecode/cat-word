Title: US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall the Most in 8 Weeks: EIA
Tag: Crude Oil Stocks Change

US Crude Stocks Unexpectedly Fall the Most in 8 Weeks: EIAUnited States Crude Oil Stocks ChangeUS crude oil inventories declined by 4.523 million barrels in the week ended July 22nd, the most in 8 weeks and four times more than forecasts for a 1.037-million-barrel fall. Crude stocks at Cushing, Oklahoma, rose by 0.751 million barrels, up from a 1.143-million-barrel injection in the previous period. Gasoline stocks dropped by 3.304 million barrels, compared to expectations of a 0.857 million decrease. Distillate stockpiles, which include diesel and heating oil, rose by 0.784 million barrels, above expectations for a 0.5 million increase.2022-07-27T14:47:21.22
