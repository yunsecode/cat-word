Title: US New Home Sales Rebound
Tag: New Home Sales

US New Home Sales ReboundUnited States New Home SalesSales of new single-family houses in the United States jumped 3.5 percent from the previous month to a seasonally adjusted annual rate of 629 thousand in August of 2018, following a downwardly revised 1.6 percent drop in July. It compares with market expectations of a 0.5 percent rise to 630 thousand. Sales jumped in the Northeast but fell in the South. 2018-09-26T14:03:34.477
