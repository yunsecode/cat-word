Title: Dollar at 1-Week Low
Tag: Currency

Dollar at 1-Week LowUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index traded slightly lower at 96.9 on Tuesday, the weakest on a closing basis in a week, amid a slight improvement in investors' risk appetite after President Trump clarified via Twitter the trade deal with China is fully intact. Earlier, the trade adviser Peter Navarro confused markets by saying it was over. Navarro later added his comments “have been taken wildly out of context”. Also, PMIs for Europe came much better-than-expected, suggesting the economic recovery from the pandemic is on the way.2020-06-23T10:12:41.56
