Title: US Consumer Sentiment Lower than Expected
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Sentiment Lower than ExpectedUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US fell to 99 in October of 2018 from 100.1 in September and below market expectations of 100.4, preliminary estimates showed. The decline was due to less favorable assessments by consumers of their personal finances as upward revisions in the year-ahead expected inflation rate weakened real income expectations.2018-10-12T14:08:22.417
