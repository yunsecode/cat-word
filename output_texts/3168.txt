US Crude Oil Stocks Rise the Most in 6 Weeks: APIUnited States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States increased by 1.845 million barrels in the week ended June 3rd, after a 1.181 million barrels decline in the previous week and compared to market expectations of a 1.800 million fall.  It was the largest injection of crude oil US inventories in six weeks, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed.2022-06-07T20:37:28.32
