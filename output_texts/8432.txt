Title: Dollar Weakens Ahead of Fed Statement
Tag: Currency

Dollar Weakens Ahead of Fed StatementUnited States CurrencyThe dollar index fell to 99.6 against a basket of currencies on Wednesday ahead of the Federal Reserve's monetary policy statement later in the day at which policymakers will probably leave interest rates near zero and pledge to do whatever it takes to support the world's largest economy. Investors will be looking at any clues on the central bank's likely future path as well as a detailed forecast for the economy.2020-04-29T08:24:14.223
