Title: US Pending Home Sales Fall in May
Tag: Pending Home Sales

US Pending Home Sales Fall in MayUnited States Pending Home SalesContracts to buy previously owned homes in the United States shrank 2.2 percent year-on-year in May of 2018, following a 2.1 percent drop in April. It is the fifth consecutive month of annual declines in pending home sales. Sales fell in the Northeast (-4.8 percent), the West (-4.1 percent) and the Midwest (-2.5 percent) but were flat in the South. On a month-over-month basis, sales went down 0.5 percent, after 1.3 percent drop in April and compared to market expectations of a 0.5 percent gain. 2018-06-27T14:06:45.867
