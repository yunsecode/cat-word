US Stocks Trade Mixed on WednesdayUnited States Stock MarketWall Street opened mixed on Wednesday amid rising oil prices, better-than-expected earnings results for Morgan Stanley, Abbott Labs and BB&T and mixed economic data for building permits and housing starts.
