Title: API: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most Since 2017
Tag: API Crude Oil Stock Change

API: US Crude Oil Inventories Rise the Most Since 2017United States API Crude Oil Stock ChangeStocks of crude oil in the United States jumped by 13.143 million barrels in the week ended April 10th, 2020, following an 11.938 million rise in the previous week, data from the American Petroleum Institute showed. It was the biggest increase in oil inventories since the week ended February 3rd, 2017.  2020-04-14T20:52:00
