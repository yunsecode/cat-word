Title: Dollar Volatility Continues
Tag: Currency

Dollar Volatility ContinuesUnited States CurrencyThe US dollar swung between small gains and losses on Tuesday as investors digest latest Fed moves and try to assess what the real impact of the coronavirus outbreak in the global economy will be. The DXY fell to a 2-month low of 96.92 right after the Fed cut its federal funds rate by 50bps in an emergency move, aiming to offset the economic impact of the coronavirus outbreak. However, the dollar index recovered some of its losses after Fed Chair Powell during the press conference reiterated that the US economy remains strong and the Fed will do its part to keep the economy strong. 2020-03-03T16:28:00
