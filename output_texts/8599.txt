Title: US Treasury Yields Fall on Wednesday
Tag: Government Bond 10Y

US Treasury Yields Fall on WednesdayUnited States Government Bond 10YThe yield on the US 10-Year Treasury note fell to 0.827% on Wednesday morning as enthusiasm about the $2 trillion stimulus plan agreed by the US administration and the Congress faded and investors remain very concerned about the worldwide spread of the coronavirus. Meanwhile, the yield on the US 30-year bond was down to 1.38%.2020-03-25T12:19:51.473
