Title: US Jobless Rate Steady at 5%
Tag: Unemployment Rate

US Jobless Rate Steady at 5%United States Unemployment RateUnemployment rate in the United States was recorded at 5 percent in April 2016, unchanged from the March rate, as more people dropped out of the labor force while the number of unemployed persons was little changed at 7.9 million. The labor force participation rate fell for the first time since September last year.2016-05-06T13:32:44
