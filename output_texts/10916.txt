Title: Chicago Business Barometer Falls to 1-Year Low
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago Business Barometer Falls to 1-Year LowUnited States Chicago PmiThe MNI Chicago Business Barometer fell to 57.4 in March 2018 from 61.9 in the previous month, well below market expectations of 62.1. It was the lowest reading since March last year, as the production sub-index declined to the lowest level since October 2016, while the new orders indicator decreased to a level last seen lower just over a year ago. Also, the order
backlogs indicator slowed for a third consecutive month to an eleven-month low.2018-03-29T13:53:36
