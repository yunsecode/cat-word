Tag: Non Farm Payrolls

U.S. Economy Adds 155K Jobs in DecemberUnited States Non Farm PayrollsTotal nonfarm payroll employment increased by 155,000 in December. In 2012, employment growth averaged 153,000 per month, the same as the average monthly gain for 2011. In December, employment increased in health care, food services and drinking places, construction, and manufacturing.
