Title: US Consumer Morale Revised Higher
Tag: Consumer Confidence

US Consumer Morale Revised HigherUnited States Consumer ConfidenceThe University of Michigan's consumer sentiment for the US was revised higher to 98.4 in March 2019 from a preliminary 97.8, as the current economic conditions sub-index came in stronger than initially thought. The latest reading was slightly above the average of 97.2 recorded in the past 26 months.2019-03-29T14:05:41.967
