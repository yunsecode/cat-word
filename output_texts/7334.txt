Title: US Stocks Rally Continues
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks Rally ContinuesUnited States Stock MarketWall Street extended gains for the 4th session on Thursday, with the Dow Jones rising near 600 points and the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq adding near 2%. Investors await the final outcome of US presidential election as ballots continued to be counted in battleground states Nevada, Arizona, Pennsylvania, North Carolina and Georgia. Biden continues to lead with 264 electoral votes while Trump swept up 214 votes and his campaign already filed lawsuits and demanded a recount. Investors also assess the likelihood of gridlock in Congress which will make the passage of the stimulus package more difficult but major policy changes more unlikely. 2020-11-05T14:35:00
