Title: US Services PMI at 6-Month High: Markit
Tag: Services PMI

US Services PMI at 6-Month High: MarkitUnited States Services PMIThe IHS Markit US Services PMI rose to 54.7 in July of 2017 from a preliminary of 54.2 and 54.2 in June. It is the highest reading since January as business activity increased markedly, new orders rose the most in two years and payrolls increased at the fastest pace so far this year. In addition, input cost inflation eased. Business confidence fell from a five-month high in June but remained robust amid improvements in overall activity, new business and strengthening client demand. 2017-08-03T13:52:40.48
