Title: US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd Week
Tag: Mortgage Applications

US Mortgage Applications Fall for 2nd WeekUnited States Mortgage ApplicationsMortgage applications in the United States declined 0.7 percent in the week ended September 23rd 2016 from the prior period, data from the Mortgage Bankers Association showed. It is the second consecutive fall, following a 7.3 percent drop in the previous period. Refinance applications declined 1.6 percent while applications to purchase a home were up 0.8 percent. Average fixed 30-year mortgage rates fell 4bps to 3.66 percent. 2016-09-28T11:10:56.62
