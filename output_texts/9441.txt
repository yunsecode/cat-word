Title: US Jobless Claims Lowest since 1969
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Lowest since 1969United States Initial Jobless ClaimsThe number of Americans filling for unemployment benefits fell by 10 thousand to 202 thousand in the week ending March 30th from the previous week’s revised level of 212 thousand and compared with market expectations of 216 thousand. It is the  lowest reading since the week ended December 6th 1969. According to unadjusted data, the largest decreases were seen in Texas (-2,027), California (-1,755), Pennsylvania (-1,390) and Arkansas (-1,152) while the biggest rises were recorded in Illinois (1,362) and New York (465).
