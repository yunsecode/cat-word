Title: US Exports Drop 0.4% in January
Tag: Exports

US Exports Drop 0.4% in JanuaryUnited States ExportsExports from the United States fell USD 0.9 billion from the previous month to USD 208.6 billion in January 2020, due to decreases in sales of capital goods, in particular civilian aircraft, and industrial supplies and materials, such as crude oil and fuel oil. Meanwhile, motor vehicle and parts exports rose. On a non seasonally adjusted basis, exports to China tumbled 18.7 percent and those to Japan plunged 19.1 percent. On the other hand, there were increases in sales to Mexico (9.3 percent), the EU (1.5 percent) and Canada (1.2 percent).2020-03-06T13:37:00
