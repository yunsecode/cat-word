Title: US Stocks End Mixed to Book Weekly Loss
Tag: Stock Market

US Stocks End Mixed to Book Weekly LossUnited States Stock MarketWall Street closed mixed on Friday November 10th of 2017, as the US Senate revealed that its fiscal plan would delay tax cuts to the corporate rate until 2019. The Dow Jones retreated 40 points or 0.2% to 23422. The S&P 500 edged down 2 points or 0.1% to 2582. The Nasdaq inched up 1 point or less than 0.1% to 6751. During the week, all three major indices booked losses, with the Dow Jones declining 0.5%; the S&P 500 0.2%; and the Nasdaq 0.2%. 2017-11-10T21:11:12
