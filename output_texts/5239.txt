Title: US Jobless Claims Seen Falling for 1st Time in 3 Weeks
Tag: Initial Jobless Claims

US Jobless Claims Seen Falling for 1st Time in 3 WeeksUnited States Initial Jobless ClaimsInitial jobless claims likely fell last week to 335 thousand, ending a two-week period of increases and moving closer to a pandemic low of 312 thousand reached in early September, as the pace of hiring picks up steam amid companies' efforts to fill widespread vacancies and as more workers go back into the labor force following the end of federal enhanced unemployment benefits. Still, factors related to the pandemic, such as caregiving needs and ongoing fears of the virus, continue to curb job gains. Meanwhile, continuing claims likely dropped to 2.80 million, after a slight increase the week before.2021-09-30T10:57:20.72
