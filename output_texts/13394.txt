Title: Chicago PMI Down to 3-Month Low
Tag: Chicago Pmi

Chicago PMI Down to 3-Month LowUnited States Chicago PmiThe Chicago PMI in the United States fell 1.1 to 49.3 in May of 2016, the lowest in three months and compared to market expectations of 50.9. It is the sixth time it has been in contraction over the past 12 months, led by falls in production, new orders and inventories.2016-05-31T14:56:48.577
